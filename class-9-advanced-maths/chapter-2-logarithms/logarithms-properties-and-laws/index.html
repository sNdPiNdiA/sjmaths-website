Topic Overview

What You'll Learn in This Section

Welcome to the core of logarithms! In this section, we will study and prove the algebraic laws of logarithms that simplify exponent operations. We will also learn about base-10 common logs used in Richter, decibel, and pH scales.

Key Concepts Covered

- Product Rule: $\log_a(MN) = \log_a(M) + \log_a(N)$ (turns multiplication into addition).
- Quotient Rule: $\log_a(M/N) = \log_a(M) - \log_a(N)$ (turns division into subtraction).
- Power Rule: $\log_a(M^k) = k \log_a(M)$ (exponent comes down).
- Change of Base: Converting base $a$ to any base $b$.
- Common Logarithms (Base 10): Real-world pH and decibel scale scales.
