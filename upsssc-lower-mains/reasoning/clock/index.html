2. Overview of Clock Mechanics

A standard analog clock represents a circle of 360 degrees. The dial is divided into 12 hour-markings, each representing 30 degrees (360/12).एक मानक एनालॉग घड़ी 360 डिग्री के वृत्त का प्रतिनिधित्व करती है। डायल 12 घंटे के चिह्नों में विभाजित है, जिनमें से प्रत्येक 30 डिग्री (360/12) का प्रतिनिधित्व करता है।

The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes (6° per minute), while the hour hand moves 360 degrees in 12 hours (0.5° per minute). This difference is the core of all calculations.मिनट की सुई 60 मिनट में 360 डिग्री (6° प्रति मिनट) चलती है, जबकि घंटे की सुई 12 घंटे में 360 डिग्री (0.5° प्रति मिनट) चलती है। यह अंतर सभी गणनाओं का आधार है।

4. Angle Calculation Formula

To calculate the angle (θ) between hands at time H:M, use the formula: θ = |30H - 5.5M|. This formula is derived from the relative positions of both hands from the 12 o'clock position.

Example: At 4:20, H=4, M=20. Angle = |30(4) - 5.5(20)| = |120 - 110| = 10°. This is a standard examination query format in UPSSSC exams.

5. Overlapping & Right Angles

Note that the overlap does not happen exactly 12 times in 12 hours because the hands only overlap 11 times. The "missing" overlap occurs between 11:00 and 1:00.

6. Mirror Images (Horizontal & Vertical)

For Mirror Images (Vertical), subtract the given time from 11:60 or 12:00. If the time is 8:40, the mirror image is 11:60 - 8:40 = 3:20.

For Water Images, subtract the time from 18:30. If the time is 4:40, subtract from 17:90 (to handle minutes > 30), resulting in 13:50 or 1:50.

7. Gain and Loss Concepts

If a clock gains T1 minutes in time H1 and loses T2 in H2, the net error is proportional to the elapsed time.

Use the ratio method: (Gain/Total Time) * Time Elapsed = Total Gain. This is essential for questions involving faulty clocks in UPSSSC mains.

8. Tips, Tricks, and Mnemonics

Mnemonic: "11-1-11". In 12 hours, hands overlap 11 times, are opposite 11 times, and at 90 degrees 22 times.

Shortcut for 0° (Overlap): Time = (60/11) * H. For example, at 3 o'clock, overlap is at (60/11)*3 = 180/11 = 16 4/11 minutes past 3.

9. Advanced Problem Solving

When hands are given to be at a specific angle, use θ = |30H - 5.5M|. If the question asks for the time when hands are at 90°, set θ=90 and solve for M.

Remember: There are always two possible times for any angle except 0° and 180°, as the minute hand passes the hour hand twice in most hours.

How many times do the hands of a clock coincide in a day?

एक दिन में घड़ी की सुइयां कितनी बार एक साथ (संपाती) होती हैं?

Show Answer उत्तर देखें

✔ Correct: C

✔ सही उत्तर: C

The hands of a clock coincide 11 times in every 12 hours. Therefore, in 24 hours, they coincide 11 * 2 = 22 times. The overlap does not happen between 11 and 1 o'clock (only at 12:00).

घड़ी की सुइयां हर 12 घंटे में 11 बार मिलती हैं। इसलिए, 24 घंटे में वे 11 * 2 = 22 बार मिलती हैं। 11 से 1 बजे के बीच केवल 12 बजे ही वे मिलती हैं।

✔ सही उत्तर: A

Formula: |30H - 5.5M|. Here H=3, M=15. |30*3 - 5.5*15| = |90 - 82.5| = 7.5 degrees. The minute hand moves, so the hour hand also shifts.

At what time between 4 and 5 o'clock are the hands at 90 degrees?

Show Answer उत्तर देखें

✔ Correct: B

✔ सही उत्तर: B

सूत्र: T = (2/11) * (30H +/- कोण)। 90 डिग्री के लिए: (2/11) * (120 - 90) = 5 5/11 और (2/11)*(120+90) = 38 2/11।
