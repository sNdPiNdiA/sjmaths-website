1. Overview of Indian Climate

India exhibits a 'Tropical Monsoon' climate, primarily influenced by the Tropic of Cancer passing through its center. भारत की जलवायु मुख्य रूप से 'उष्णकटिबंधीय मानसूनी' है, जो कर्क रेखा के मध्य से गुजरने के कारण प्रभावित होती है।

The climatic diversity is dictated by the Himalayan range in the north and the Indian Ocean in the south. उत्तर में हिमालय और दक्षिण में हिंद महासागर भारत की जलवायु विविधता को निर्धारित करते हैं।

2. Major Controlling Factors

The Indian climate is a complex interplay of physical and atmospheric factors. Key drivers include the Tibetan Plateau heating, the Sub-Tropical Westerly Jet Stream (STWJ), and the Tropical Easterly Jet (TEJ). The Himalayas act as a climatic barrier, preventing cold Central Asian winds from entering India.

Key Fact: The shift of the Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ) is the primary engine of the monsoon onset.

4. The Monsoon Mechanism

The Southwest Monsoon enters via the Arabian Sea and Bay of Bengal branches. The Arabian Sea branch is stronger due to the larger expanse of water. It strikes the Western Ghats first, causing heavy orographic rainfall.

The Bay of Bengal branch is deflected by the Arakans and enters the Ganga plains. Interaction between these branches creates the 'Monsoon Trough'.

5. Climate Phenomena: El Nino & IOD

El Nino refers to the warming of the Eastern Pacific off the coast of Peru, which negatively impacts the Indian Monsoon. Conversely, a positive Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD) enhances rainfall by creating a temperature gradient in the Indian Ocean.

Impact: Strong El Nino years often lead to drought conditions in India.

7. Seasonal Variations

Winter months see Western Disturbances originating from the Mediterranean Sea, bringing winter rains to North-West India, crucial for the Rabi crop (Wheat).

The Retreating Monsoon (Oct-Nov) brings cyclonic rainfall to the Coromandel Coast (Tamil Nadu), unlike the rest of India which experiences dry conditions.

Mnemonic for Monsoon States: "K-M-K-G-O" (Kerala, Maharashtra, Karnataka, Gujarat, Odisha) - The order of arrival for the Arabian Sea branch.
