Vitamins: Comprehensive Overviewविटामिन: विस्तृत अवलोकन

Vitamins are organic compounds required in small quantities for normal metabolic processes. They act as biocatalysts or co-enzymes.विटामिन कार्बनिक यौगिक हैं जिनकी आवश्यकता सामान्य चयापचय प्रक्रियाओं के लिए कम मात्रा में होती है। वे जैव-उत्प्रेरक या सह-एंजाइम के रूप में कार्य करते हैं।

Classification of Vitamins

Vitamins are classified based on solubility. Fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, E, K) are stored in adipose tissue and liver. Water-soluble vitamins (B-complex and C) are excreted in urine and must be replaced daily.

Water-SolubleB, CNot stored

Vitamin A (Retinol)

Vitamin A is essential for vision, immune function, and skin health. Deficiency leads to Xerophthalmia and Night Blindness. It is found in carrots, liver, and spinach.

Key Fact: Beta-carotene is the precursor to Vitamin A, often found in yellow-orange vegetables.

Vitamin B1 (Thiamine)

Vitamin B1 is crucial for carbohydrate metabolism. Deficiency causes Beriberi, characterized by nerve damage and muscle wasting. It is abundant in whole grains and legumes.

Vitamin B2 (Riboflavin)

Riboflavin acts as a precursor to FAD and FMN, vital for energy production. Deficiency leads to Ariboflavinosis, causing mouth sores and cracked lips (Cheilosis).

Vitamin B3 (Niacin)

Niacin is essential for cellular respiration. Deficiency causes Pellagra, which is characterized by the 3 Ds: Dermatitis, Diarrhea, and Dementia.

Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin)

Vitamin B12 contains Cobalt. It is necessary for red blood cell formation and nervous system function. Deficiency leads to Pernicious Anemia.

Vitamin C (Ascorbic Acid)

Vitamin C is a powerful antioxidant and aids in collagen synthesis. Deficiency leads to Scurvy, characterized by bleeding gums and poor wound healing.

Vitamin D (Calciferol)

Vitamin D is synthesized in the skin under UV light. It regulates calcium absorption. Deficiency causes Rickets in children and Osteomalacia in adults.

Vitamin E (Tocopherol)

Vitamin E protects cell membranes from oxidative damage. It is often called the 'Beauty Vitamin' and is vital for reproductive health.

Vitamin K (Phylloquinone)

Vitamin K is essential for blood clotting proteins. It is synthesized by gut bacteria. Deficiency leads to prolonged bleeding and hemorrhaging.

Tips, Tricks, and Mnemonics

To remember water-soluble vitamins: "BC are water-soluble." Fat-soluble vitamins: "KADE" (K, A, D, E). Remember chemical names with the mnemonic: "Rats The Right Nut, Please Put Clear Pyramids Over Everything" (Retinol, Thiamine, Riboflavin, Niacin, Pantothenic acid, Pyridoxine, Cyanocobalamin).

Exam Note: Always remember that Vitamin B12 is the only vitamin containing a metallic element (Cobalt).

Statement 1: Vitamin K is fat-soluble. Statement 2: Vitamin K is produced by intestinal bacteria. True/False?

Show Answer उत्तर देखें

✔ Correct: A
