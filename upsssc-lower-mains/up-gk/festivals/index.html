Kumbh Mela: The Spiritual Pinnacle | कुंभ मेला: आध्यात्मिक शिखर

The Kumbh Mela in Prayagraj is inscribed on UNESCO's Representative List of Intangible Cultural Heritage of Humanity. It occurs at the 'Sangam' (confluence) of Ganga, Yamuna, and the mythical Saraswati. प्रयागराज का कुंभ मेला यूनेस्को की अमूर्त सांस्कृतिक विरासत सूची में शामिल है।

  • Maha Kumbh: Every 12 years.
  • Ardh Kumbh: Every 6 years.
  • Magh Mela: Annual event in the month of Magh.
Braj Region Festivals | ब्रज क्षेत्र के त्यौहार

Mathura and Vrindavan are the heart of Krishna Bhakti. मथुरा और वृंदावन कृष्ण भक्ति के केंद्र हैं।

  • Lathmar Holi: Celebrated in Barsana and Nandgaon. Women beat men with sticks (lathis) in a playful manner.
  • Janmashtami: Birth of Lord Krishna, celebrated with immense fervor in Mathura.
  • Jhula Utsav: Celebrated in temples of Vrindavan during the month of Shravan.
    • Tourism & Cultural Fairs | पर्यटन और सांस्कृतिक मेले

    The UP Tourism Department organizes several festivals to promote heritage. उत्तर प्रदेश पर्यटन विभाग विरासत को बढ़ावा देने के लिए कई उत्सव आयोजित करता है।

  • Taj Mahotsav (Agra): 10-day festival in February showcasing Mughal era culture.
  • Lucknow Mahotsav: Displays the 'Tehzeeb' and crafts of Awadh.
  • Deepotsav (Ayodhya): Famous for lighting millions of diyas on the banks of Saryu river.
  • Kabir Utsav (Maghar): Dedicated to Saint Kabir Das.
    • Important Religious Fairs | महत्वपूर्ण धार्मिक मेले

    UP hosts the highest number of fairs in India (approx 2250+). उत्तर प्रदेश में भारत में सबसे अधिक मेले (लगभग 2250+) आयोजित होते हैं।

  • Nauchandi Mela (Meerut): A symbol of Hindu-Muslim unity.
  • Bateshwar Mela (Agra): Famous animal/camel fair on the banks of Yamuna.
  • Shakumbhari Devi Mela: Saharanpur district.
  • Dauji Mela: Hathras.
    • Tribal Festivals | जनजातीय त्यौहार

    The Tharu tribe celebrates 'Bajhar' and considers Deepawali as a day of mourning (Shok Parv). थारू जनजाति 'बझर' मनाती है और दीपावली को शोक पर्व के रूप में मानती है।

  • Karma: Celebrated by Kharwar and Baiga tribes (Sonbhadra/Mirzapur).
  • Govardhan Puja: Widely celebrated by the agrarian and pastoral communities.
