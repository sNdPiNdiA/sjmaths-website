Why Subject-Wise PYQ Analysis is Crucial for UPSSSC PET

The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) is the mandatory qualifying gateway for recruitment across state departments (including Lekhpal, Junior Assistant, VDO, and Tubewell Operator). With high competition and normalizing shifts, scoring 80+ percentile marks requires targeted mastery over high-scoring clusters.

1. High-Yield Data Interpretation (Table & Graph Analysis = 20 Marks)

A full 20% of the entire 100-mark paper is devoted to Table Analysis (10 questions) and Graph Analysis (10 questions). In the 2023 examination, these questions featured sophisticated ratio-of-ratio calculations, speed-time distance systems, and multi-variable financial tables. Solving all 12 table sets and 12 graph sets in this module prepares you for any difficulty tier.

2. Reading Comprehension & Grammar (Hindi Unseen Passages = 10 Marks)

Hindi unseen passages test philosophical, sociological, and grammatical themes. By practicing all 12 actual exam passages from 2022 and 2023, you gain immediate familiarity with the question styles, synonym/antonym inference, and contextual sandhi questions.

3. Factual General Awareness & Static GK (10 Marks)

Questions consistently test Indian neighbours, capital currencies, folk art (such as Sanjhi, Nautanki, Koothu), Lok Sabha powers, and world days. Subject 8 consolidates all 56 questions from these shifts into focused drills.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is there negative marking in UPSSSC PET?

Yes, UPSSSC PET has a negative marking penalty of 0.25 marks (1/4th) for every incorrect response. Each correct answer carries 1 mark.

How can I practice these PYQs in Hindi?

Every subject drill page features an instant Language Toggle button (EN / HI) in the top bar. Clicking it toggles question stems, options, and full explanations to Hindi immediately.

Are solutions provided for mathematical and DI calculations?

Yes, all 60 Table Analysis questions, 60 Graph Analysis questions, and 30 Elementary Arithmetic questions include complete, step-by-step arithmetic equations, formula derivations, and verification notes for any typographical errors present in the original test papers.
