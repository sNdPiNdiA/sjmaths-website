Exam insights

Key ideas & Voronoi Construction Method

  • Midpoint and Negative Reciprocal Gradient: Every Voronoi edge separating two sites $A(x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x_2, y_2)$ is the perpendicular bisector. It passes through midpoint $M = \left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$ with gradient $m_{\perp} = -\frac{1}{m_{AB}}$.
  • Voronoi Vertices are Circumcenters: A vertex where three cells meet is equidistant to all three sites. It forms the center of the unique circumcircle passing through the three points.
  • Largest Empty Circle: The center of the largest empty circle that contains no sites in its interior must lie either at a Voronoi vertex or on the boundary of the region.
