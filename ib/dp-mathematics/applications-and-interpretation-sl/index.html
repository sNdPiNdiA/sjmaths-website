AI SL Assessment Structure

Paper 1 (External)

40% · 90 Minutes · 80 Marks

Compulsory short-response questions covering all five topics. Graphing Calculator (GDC) required throughout.

Paper 2 (External)

40% · 90 Minutes · 80 Marks

Extended response questions emphasizing multi-step real-world modeling and interpretation. GDC required throughout.

Internal Assessment (IA)

20% · Mathematical Exploration

An individual 12–20 page investigation assessed internally against criteria A–E. Read the IA guide →
