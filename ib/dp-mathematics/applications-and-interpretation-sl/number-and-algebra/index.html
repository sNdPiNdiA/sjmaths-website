Exam insights

Key ideas & Common GDC Pitfalls

  • Compounding Periods: When interest compounds quarterly ($k=4$) or monthly ($k=12$), remember that the nominal annual rate must be divided by $k$, and the total periods $n$ is multiplied by $k$.
  • Percentage Error Denominator: The official IB formula is $\varepsilon = \left|\frac{v_A - v_E}{v_E}\right| \times 100\%$. Always divide by the exact value ($v_E$), never the approximation.
  • TVM Sign Convention on GDC: Money leaving your pocket (investment, down payment) is negative; money received (loan principal, withdrawal) is positive.
