Exam insights

Key calculus concepts in Math AI

  • Distinguishing Average vs Instantaneous Rate: Average rate of change is the secant slope $\frac{f(b) - f(a)}{b - a}$; instantaneous rate of change is the derivative $f'(x)$.
  • GDC Numerical Derivative: On Paper 2, find gradient values using nDeriv(f(x), x, c) to check your analytical differentiation.
  • Trapezoidal Rule Over/Under Estimation:
    • If the curve is concave down ($\cap$, $f''(x) < 0$), straight trapezoid chords lie below the curve $\implies$ underestimation.
    • If the curve is concave up ($\cup$, $f''(x) > 0$), straight chords lie above the curve $\implies$ overestimation.
