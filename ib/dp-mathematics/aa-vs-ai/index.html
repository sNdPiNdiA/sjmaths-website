IB Math AA vs AI

IB Diploma Programme Mathematics has two courses, Analysis and Approaches (AA) and Applications and Interpretation (AI), each offered at Standard Level (SL) and Higher Level (HL). They develop related mathematics through different emphases. Neither course is universally easier or the right choice for every student.

Compare the courses

What is the difference between IB Math AA and AI?

Both courses build mathematical understanding, but their emphasis and exam technology rules differ. The current IB curriculum offers each course at SL and HL.

Make a considered choice

How should I choose?

  • Start with the mathematics you enjoy and the way you prefer to solve problems. A course label alone does not predict the grade you will earn.
  • Check entry requirements for the specific university programmes you may apply to. Requirements vary by country, institution, course and year; do not rely on a blanket rule that AA or AI is always accepted or required.
  • Discuss your choice with your IB mathematics teacher or school coordinator, especially if you are deciding between SL and HL or balancing your other Diploma Programme subjects.
  • Do not choose based only on which course is said to be easier. AA and AI emphasize different kinds of reasoning and both require consistent practice.

Assessment snapshot

How do the exams differ?

In the current syllabus, SL students sit two external papers and complete the Mathematical Exploration. HL students sit three external papers and complete the exploration. Paper formats and technology rules differ between AA and AI; check the subject guide for the examination session you will take. The current syllabus has its final assessment in November 2028, before the new curriculum is first assessed in 2029.

Common questions

AA vs AI FAQs

Is IB Math AA harder than AI?

There is no universal answer. AA puts more weight on algebraic reasoning and analysis; AI puts more weight on modelling, statistics, technology and interpretation. Difficulty depends on a student's strengths and preparation.

Can AI lead to a STEM degree?

Some STEM programmes accept AI and others specify a course or level. Requirements vary, so check the current admissions page for each programme you are considering.

Do AA and AI both have SL and HL?

Yes. Both courses are offered at Standard Level and Higher Level. Students take one DP Mathematics course.

Check the official course information

Course descriptions and assessment rules can change by syllabus session. Confirm your course choice and examination requirements with your school and the official IB materials.
