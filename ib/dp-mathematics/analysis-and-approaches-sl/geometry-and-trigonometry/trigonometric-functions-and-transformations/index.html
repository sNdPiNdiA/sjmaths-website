  1. 3.7Functions and transformations of sine, cosine and tangent

Learn the ideas

Key ideas and methods

  • For y=a sin(b(x−c))+d or the cosine form, amplitude is |a|, period is 2π/|b|, horizontal shift is c, and midline is y=d.
  • A negative a reflects the graph vertically; a negative b reverses its horizontal orientation, though the periodic graph may be represented equivalently with a phase shift.
  • The tangent graph has period π/|b| and vertical asymptotes; it has no amplitude because it is unbounded.

Follow the reasoning

Worked example

This original example shows the method step by step; it is not copied from an IB examination paper.

Worked example 1

Read a cosine transformation

Question. For y=2cos(3x), state the amplitude and period.

  1. The coefficient outside cosine is 2, so amplitude is |2|=2.
  2. The period is 2π/|3|.

Answer: Amplitude 2; period 2π/3.

Try it yourself

Practice with worked answers

  1. State the midline and period of y=−3sin(2x)+4.

    Show worked answer

    Midline y=4; period 2π/2=π.

  2. What is the period of y=tan(4x)?

    Show worked answer

    π/4.

Avoid these slips

Common mistakes

  • Using π/|b| for sine or cosine; their period is 2π/|b|.
  • Calling the vertical shift the amplitude.
  • Trying to assign a finite amplitude to tangent.
