  1. 4.3Measures of central tendency and dispersion

Learn the ideas

Key ideas and methods

  • The mean uses every value but can move substantially because of an extreme observation; the median is more resistant.
  • Range is maximum minus minimum. The interquartile range describes the middle half; standard deviation measures spread around the mean.
  • When comparing groups, state the units and connect the numerical summary to the context. Use the same quartile convention or calculator settings for a fair comparison.

Follow the reasoning

Worked example

This original example shows the method step by step; it is not copied from an IB examination paper.

Worked example 1

Summarize a small data set

Question. For 2, 3, 5, 5, 10 find the mean, median and range.

  1. The sum is 25 across 5 values, so mean=5.
  2. The middle ordered value is 5, so median=5.
  3. Range=10−2=8.

Answer: Mean 5, median 5, range 8.

Try it yourself

Practice with worked answers

  1. Find the median and range of 4, 7, 8, 12.

    Show worked answer

    Median=(7+8)/2=7.5; range=12−4=8.

  2. Which is more resistant to a very large outlier: mean or median?

    Show worked answer

    The median is generally more resistant to extreme values.

Avoid these slips

Common mistakes

  • Mixing population and sample standard deviation settings without checking the question.
  • Reporting a spread measure without its units.
  • Using a mean alone when a strong outlier makes it unrepresentative.
