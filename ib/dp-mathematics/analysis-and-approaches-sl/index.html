FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary difference between AA SL and AA HL?
AA HL includes all topics from AA SL, plus additional advanced topics and deeper exploration of core concepts, requiring a higher level of mathematical maturity and problem-solving skill. HL often prepares students for highly mathematical university degrees.
Is a graphic display calculator (GDC) allowed in AA SL exams?
Yes, GDCs are integral to both AA SL papers. Students are expected to be proficient in using their GDC for calculations, graphing, solving equations, and statistical analysis.
How much calculus is in AA SL?
Calculus is a significant component of AA SL, covering both differentiation and integration with applications, including kinematics, optimization, and area/volume calculations. It forms a substantial portion of the curriculum.
