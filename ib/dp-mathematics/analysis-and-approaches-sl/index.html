FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between AA SL and AI SL?
AA focuses on algebra, proof, and calculus. AI focuses on modelling, statistics, and technology use.
Is a graphic display calculator (GDC) allowed in all DP AA SL exam papers?
No. Paper 1 (90 min) is non-calculator. Paper 2 (90 min) requires a GDC.
How much calculus is covered in AA SL compared to AA HL?
SL covers differentiation rules, optimization, tangents/normals, and definite integrals. HL adds integration techniques, differential equations, Maclaurin series, and L'Hôpital's rule.
