Topic 5: Calculus (HL)

The AHL component of Calculus represents the pinnacle of the AA HL pure mathematics curriculum: evaluating indeterminate limits with L'Hôpital's rule, implicit curves, geometric related rates, advanced integration techniques (parts and partial fractions), first-order differential equations (Euler's method & integrating factor), 3D volumes of revolution, and power series via Maclaurin expansions.

Syllabus Map

AA HL Calculus Syllabus Points

The official IB syllabus content covering AHL points 5.9 through 5.16:

  1. 5.9–5.10Higher derivatives, continuity and differentiability, derivatives of reciprocal and inverse trigonometric functions ($\sec x, \arcsin x, \arctan x$)
  2. 5.11Implicit differentiation and related rates of change
  3. 5.12L'Hôpital's rule for indeterminate forms $\frac{0}{0}$ and $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$
  4. 5.13Advanced integration techniques: integration by parts ($\int u\,dv = uv - \int v\,du$), integration by partial fractions, trigonometric substitution
  5. 5.14First-order differential equations: separable variables, linear differential equations with integrating factor $I(x) = e^{\int P(x)dx}$, and Euler's numerical step method
  6. 5.15Volumes of revolution about the $x$-axis ($V = \pi \int y^2 dx$) and $y$-axis ($V = \pi \int x^2 dy$)
  7. 5.16Maclaurin series expansions: definition $f(x) = \sum_{n=0}^\infty \frac{f^{(n)}(0)}{n!} x^n$, standard expansions ($e^x, \sin x, \cos x, \ln(1+x), (1+x)^p$), and Taylor series approximations
