IB Assessment Model

AA HL Examination Structure

Higher Level includes three external written examinations plus the internally assessed Mathematical Exploration.

Paper 1 (30%)

120 Minutes · Non-Calculator

Section A (short response, ~50 marks) and Section B (extended response, ~60 marks). Pure algebraic manipulation, analytic proofs, and exact values without technology.

Paper 2 (30%)

120 Minutes · Graphic Display Calculator

Section A and Section B. Questions require efficient calculator use for polynomial root finding, numerical differential equation solving, and continuous probability integrals.

Paper 3 (20%)

60 Minutes · Two Extended Investigations

Two compulsory multi-part sustained inquiry questions (27 marks each). Tests mathematical generalization, pattern discovery, conjecture formation, and proof.

Internal Assessment (20%)

Mathematical Exploration

Individual 12–20 page written report on a topic chosen by the student, assessed against five IB criteria: Communication, Mathematical Presentation, Personal Engagement, Reflection, and Use of Mathematics.

Questions & Advice

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Paper 3 different from Papers 1 & 2?
Paper 3 does not test routine textbook problems. Instead, each of the two questions introduces an unfamiliar mathematical scenario, guiding the candidate from a basic initial example through scaffolded parts toward a general proof or formula. Practicing sustained problem-solving and conjecture verification is critical.
Can I take AA HL if I didn't take extended mathematics in MYP or IGCSE?
AA HL moves rapidly through abstract algebraic manipulation and proofs. While possible, students coming from standard-level prerequisites should thoroughly review the 30 AA SL foundation units, especially advanced trigonometry, functions, and calculus.
Which university courses require IB Math AA HL?
Leading global universities typically require or strongly recommend AA HL for Engineering, Computer Science, Pure Mathematics, Physics, and highly quantitative Economics or Data Science degree programmes.
