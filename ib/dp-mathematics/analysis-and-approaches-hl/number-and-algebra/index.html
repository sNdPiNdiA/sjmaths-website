Topic 1: Number and Algebra (HL)

The Additional Higher Level (AHL) component of Number and Algebra introduces rigorous formal proof by induction, the algebra and geometry of complex numbers in Cartesian, polar and Euler forms, De Moivre's theorem, roots of unity, 3-variable linear systems, and Vieta's relations.

Syllabus Map

AA HL Number and Algebra Syllabus Points

The official IB syllabus content covering AHL points 1.10 through 1.16:

  1. 1.10Extension of the binomial theorem to fractional and negative indices $(1+x)^n$ where $n \in \mathbb{Q}$ and $|x| < 1$
  2. 1.11Partial fractions: linear factors, repeated linear factors, and irreducible quadratic factors
  3. 1.12Complex numbers: Cartesian form $z = a+bi$, complex conjugates, modulus-argument form $r(\cos\theta+i\sin\theta)$, Euler form $z = r e^{i\theta}$
  4. 1.13Powers and roots of complex numbers: De Moivre's theorem for integer and rational powers; $n$th roots of complex numbers and roots of unity
  5. 1.14Proof by mathematical induction (sums of series, divisibility, matrix powers, inequalities); proof by contradiction and counterexample
  6. 1.15Solutions of systems of linear equations in up to 3 variables; row operations, Gaussian elimination, unique/infinite/inconsistent planes
  7. 1.16Polynomials: Factor and remainder theorems, Fundamental Theorem of Algebra, sum and product of roots (Vieta's formulas)
