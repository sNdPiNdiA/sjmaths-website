Topic 3: Geometry & Trigonometry (HL)

The AHL component of Geometry and Trigonometry covers reciprocal ($\sec, \csc, \cot$) and inverse ($\arcsin, \arccos, \arctan$) trigonometric functions, compound angle identities, and the complete 3D vector syllabus: scalar products, vector equations of lines, skew lines, vector cross product, and equations of planes.

Syllabus Map

AA HL Geometry & Trigonometry Syllabus Points

The official IB syllabus content covering AHL points 3.9 through 3.14:

  1. 3.9Compound angle identities: $\sin(A \pm B), \cos(A \pm B), \tan(A \pm B)$; double angle formulas for tan
  2. 3.10Reciprocal functions $\sec\theta, \csc\theta, \cot\theta$ and identities ($1+\tan^2\theta=\sec^2\theta$); inverse trigonometric functions $\arcsin x, \arccos x, \arctan x$, domains, ranges, graphs
  3. 3.113D Vectors: component form $\mathbf{v} = v_1\mathbf{i} + v_2\mathbf{j} + v_3\mathbf{k}$, unit vectors, magnitude, scalar product $\mathbf{v}\cdot\mathbf{w}$, angle between vectors, perpendicularity
  4. 3.12Vector equations of a line in 3D: $\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{a} + \lambda\mathbf{b}$; parametric and Cartesian forms; coincident, parallel, intersecting, and skew lines
  5. 3.13Vector cross product $\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{w}$, geometric interpretation, area of parallelograms and triangles
  6. 3.14Equations of planes in 3D: $\mathbf{r}\cdot\mathbf{n} = \mathbf{a}\cdot\mathbf{n}$, Cartesian $ax+by+cz=d$; intersections of lines and planes, intersection of planes, angles
