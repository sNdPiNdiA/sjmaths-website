Topic 4: Statistics & Probability (HL)

The AHL component of Statistics and Probability bridges calculus and probability through continuous random variables defined by probability density functions (PDF) and cumulative distribution functions (CDF), determination of median, mode, mean $E(X)$, variance $\text{Var}(X)$, and algebraic linear combinations of multiple random variables.

Syllabus Map

AA HL Statistics & Probability Syllabus Points

The official IB syllabus content covering AHL points 4.13 through 4.15:

  1. 4.13Continuous random variables and their probability density functions (PDF) $f(x)$ with $\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x)\,dx = 1$; cumulative distribution functions (CDF) $F(x) = P(X \le x) = \int_{-\infty}^x f(t)\,dt$
  2. 4.14Expected value (mean) $E(X) = \int x f(x)\,dx$, variance $\text{Var}(X) = E(X^2) - [E(X)]^2$, median ($F(m) = 0.5$), and mode for continuous probability distributions
  3. 4.15Linear combinations of random variables: $E(aX+b) = aE(X)+b$, $\text{Var}(aX+b) = a^2\text{Var}(X)$; for independent random variables $E(aX+bY) = aE(X)+bE(Y)$ and $\text{Var}(aX \pm bY) = a^2\text{Var}(X) + b^2\text{Var}(Y)$
