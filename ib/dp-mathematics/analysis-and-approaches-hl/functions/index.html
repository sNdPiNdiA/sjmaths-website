Topic 2: Functions (HL)

The AHL component of Functions extends foundational graphing into advanced modulus curves and inequalities, rational functions with oblique/slant asymptotes found via polynomial division, and complex composite transformations including reciprocal graphs $\frac{1}{f(x)}$ and squared graphs $[f(x)]^2$.

Syllabus Map

AA HL Functions Syllabus Points

The official IB syllabus content covering AHL points 2.12 through 2.15:

  1. 2.12Polynomial functions, rational functions of form $f(x) = \frac{ax^2+bx+c}{dx+e}$, polynomial division, oblique (slant) asymptotes; graphs of $y = |f(x)|$ and $y = f(|x|)$
  2. 2.13Composite transformations; transformations yielding $y = \frac{1}{f(x)}$ and $y = [f(x)]^2$; odd and even functions symmetry
  3. 2.14Solving inequalities graphically and analytically: polynomial, rational, and modulus inequalities ($|f(x)| \le g(x)$)
  4. 2.15Composite functions and inverse functions with restricted domains
