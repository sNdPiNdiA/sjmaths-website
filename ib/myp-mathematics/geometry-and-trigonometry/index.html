IB MYP Framework

Inquiry Concepts in MYP Geometry & Trigonometry

MYP Mathematics uses key and related concepts to promote deep conceptual understanding and transfer learning.

Key Concept: Space

Understanding geometric properties, measurements, and transformations in 2D and 3D space, and the use of coordinate systems to describe position.

Key Concept: Relationships

Exploring connections between angles, sides, and trigonometric ratios within triangles and circles, and how these relationships define geometric structures.

Real-World Context

Architecture and engineering design, navigation and surveying, cartography, computer graphics, and physics (force vectors).

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the focus of the MYP Geometry & Trigonometry branch?
This branch focuses on developing spatial reasoning, understanding properties of shapes, applying trigonometric principles to solve problems involving angles and distances, and using vectors for magnitude and direction.
How does MYP Geometry & Trigonometry transition to DP Mathematics?
Mastery of the Sine/Cosine rules, circle theorems, and vectors is foundational for DP AA/AI, especially in advanced trigonometry, complex numbers, and vector calculus.
Are geometric proofs emphasized in MYP Geometry?
While formal two-column proofs are less emphasized than in traditional geometry, students are expected to justify their reasoning and demonstrate understanding of geometric properties and theorems through problem-solving.
