IB MYP Framework

Inquiry Concepts in MYP Number

MYP Mathematics uses key and related concepts to promote deep conceptual understanding and transfer learning.

Key Concept: Form

Understanding how numbers can be represented in different forms (fractions, decimals, scientific notation, logarithms) to reveal different structural properties.

Key Concept: Logic

Applying deductive reasoning through set operations, exponential laws, and exact numerical representations (surds and radicals).

Real-World Context

Financial decision making, exponential growth in biological systems, radioactive decay, and logarithmic scales (pH, Richter, decibels).

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the focus of the MYP Number branch?
The Number branch focuses on numerical operational fluency, exact representations (surds/radicals), scaling, index laws, exponential relationships, and logical grouping through set theory.
How does MYP Number transition to DP Mathematics?
Mastery of laws of indices and logarithms in MYP Extended is essential for DP AA HL and SL calculus and functions. Concepts like geometric series directly form the core of DP Financial Math and Sequences.
Are calculators permitted for all MYP Number topics?
MYP assessments feature both non-calculator (testing exact surd simplification, index laws, and mental estimation) and GDC (Graphic Display Calculator) papers for financial growth and logarithmic modeling.
