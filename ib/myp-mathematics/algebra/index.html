IB MYP Framework

Inquiry Concepts in MYP Algebra

MYP Mathematics uses key and related concepts to promote deep conceptual understanding and transfer learning.

Key Concept: Relationships

Understanding how dependent and independent variables are connected, and how these relationships are represented graphically and algebraically.

Key Concept: Logic

Applying algebraic equivalence, inverse operations, and logical deductions to solve complex systems and higher-degree functions.

Real-World Context

Optimising resources, predicting population trajectories, assessing cost functions in engineering, and modeling infectious disease curves.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the focus of the MYP Algebra branch?
The Algebra branch focuses on transitioning from numerical operations to abstract variable notation, solving multi-step equations, understanding functional relationships, and modeling change.
Why is MYP Algebra critical for DP Mathematics?
Algebra is the absolute backbone of DP Math. In particular, function transformations, quadratics, and polynomial operations are heavily relied upon in both Calculus and Algebra units of DP SL/HL.
Which topics are Extended-only in MYP Algebra?
Extended-only topics include factorising cubic/higher-degree polynomials via the Remainder & Factor theorems, composite & inverse functions, and complex graph transformations.
