Drafting Committee

UPSC Study Notes - Brief and Comprehensive

ENGLISH

Formation and Significance

  • Date of Establishment: Set up on August 29, 1947.
  • Purpose: Tasked with the responsibility of scrutinizing the draft of the Constitution prepared by the Constitutional Advisor (B.N. Rau) and preparing a comprehensive draft for the Constituent Assembly.
  • Status: It was the most important committee among all the committees of the Constituent Assembly.

Composition

The Drafting Committee consisted of seven members:

  • Dr. B.R. Ambedkar: Chairman
  • N. Gopalaswami Ayyangar
  • Alladi Krishnaswami Ayyar
  • Dr. K.M. Munshi
  • Syed Mohammad Saadullah
  • N. Madhava Rau: Replaced B.L. Mitter (who resigned due to ill health).
  • T.T. Krishnamachari: Replaced D.P. Khaitan (who died in 1948).

Key Facts and Process

  • Drafting Timeline: The committee took less than six months to prepare its draft, sitting for a total of 141 days.
  • First Draft: Published in February 1948. The people of India were given eight months to discuss the draft and propose amendments.
  • Second Draft: Published in October 1948 after considering public feedback and criticisms.
  • Final Draft: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar introduced the final draft of the Constitution in the Assembly on November 4, 1948 (first reading).

UPSC Significance

  • Architect of the Constitution: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar is famously known as the 'Father of the Constitution of India' and the 'Chief Architect' due to his pivotal role in this committee.
  • Nature of Work: The committee functioned by incorporating suggestions from various other committees and the general public, balancing diverse viewpoints to create a consensus-based document.
  • Constitutional Morality: The committee's work reflects the effort to blend democratic principles with the socio-political realities of post-independent India.
