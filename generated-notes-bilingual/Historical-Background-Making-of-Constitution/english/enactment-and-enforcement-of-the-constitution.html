Enactment and enforcement of the Constitution

UPSC Study Notes - Brief and Comprehensive

ENGLISH

Adoption of the Constitution

  • Date of Adoption: November 26, 1949.
  • Constitutional Status: The Preamble states that the people of India "do hereby adopt, enact and give to ourselves this Constitution."
  • Provisions that came into force on Nov 26, 1949: Citizenship, Elections, Provisional Parliament, temporary and transitional provisions (Articles 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 60, 324, 366, 367, 379, 380, 388, 391, 392, and 393).

Commencement of the Constitution

  • Date of Enforcement: January 26, 1950.
  • Significance of the Date: Chosen to commemorate the 'Purna Swaraj' declaration of 1930 (Lahore Session of INC).
  • Repeal of Previous Acts: With the commencement, the Indian Independence Act, 1947 and the Government of India Act, 1935 were repealed.
  • Continuance of Laws: The Abolition of Privy Council Jurisdiction Act, 1949 remained in force, ensuring legal continuity.

Key Facts for Prelims

  • Time Taken: 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days to complete the drafting.
  • Sessions: The Constituent Assembly held 11 sessions over 165 days.
  • Drafting: The Constitution was based on the Draft Constitution published in February 1948, which underwent three readings.
  • Final Signing: On January 24, 1950, the members of the Constituent Assembly appended their signatures to the Constitution.
  • Constituent Assembly as Provisional Parliament: The Constituent Assembly continued as the provisional Parliament of India from January 26, 1950, until the formation of the new Parliament in 1952.

Constitutional Provisions on Enforcement

Article 394: This article specifies that the provisions relating to citizenship, elections, and the provisional Parliament came into force on November 26, 1949, while the remaining provisions came into force on January 26, 1950 (referred to as the "commencement of this Constitution").

Article 395: Formally repealed the Indian Independence Act, 1947, and the Government of India Act, 1935, ensuring the Constitution became the supreme law of the land.
