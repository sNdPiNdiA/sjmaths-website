Indian Sociological Thinkers

Examines the foundational pioneers of Indian sociology: G.S. Ghurye’s Indological approach to caste and culture, Radhakamal Mukerjee’s Theory of Social Values and Regional Sociology, D.P. Mukerji’s Marxian-Indological synthesis, and A.R. Desai’s materialist critique of nationalism and agrarian class structure.
