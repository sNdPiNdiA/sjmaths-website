Constitutional Provision

  • Article 368 in Part XX of the Constitution deals with the power of Parliament to amend the Constitution and its procedure.
  • It lays down two types of amendments: by a special majority of Parliament, and by a special majority of Parliament with the ratification by half of the state legislatures.
