Enactment of the Constitution

  • **Constituent Assembly (CA)**:
    • Formed in **November 1946** under the **Cabinet Mission Plan**.
    • First meeting: **December 9, 1946**. **Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha** was elected temporary President.
    • Permanent President: **Dr. Rajendra Prasad** (elected **December 11, 1946**).
    • Vice-Presidents: **H.C. Mukherjee** and **V.T. Krishnamachari**.
    • Constitutional Adviser: **B.N. Rau**.
    • Total members: Initially 389. Post-partition, reduced to 299 (229 from British provinces, 70 from princely states).
    • **Objective Resolution**: Moved by **Jawaharlal Nehru** on **December 13, 1946**; adopted on **January 22, 1947**. It laid down the fundamental philosophy and guiding principles for the Constitution.
  • **Key Committees**:
    • **Drafting Committee**: Most important committee, set up on **August 29, 1947**.
    • Chairman: **Dr. B.R. Ambedkar** (recognized as the chief architect of the Indian Constitution).
    • Members: N. Gopalaswamy Ayyangar, Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar, K.M. Munshi, Syed Mohammad Saadullah, N. Madhava Rau (replaced B.L. Mitter), T.T. Krishnamachari (replaced D.P. Khaitan).
