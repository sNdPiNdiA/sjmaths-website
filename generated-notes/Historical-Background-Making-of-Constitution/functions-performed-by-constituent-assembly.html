Functions Performed by Constituent Assembly

UPSC Study Notes - Brief and Comprehensive

Primary Function: Constitution Making

  • The Constituent Assembly's foremost task was to draft and enact the Constitution of India.
  • It took 2 years, 11 months, and 18 days to complete this monumental task, holding 11 sessions over this period.
  • The final draft of the Constitution was adopted on November 26, 1949.
  • The Constitution came into full force on January 26, 1950, marking India's Republic Day.

Legislative Function: Provisional Parliament

  • The Constituent Assembly also performed the function of the provisional Parliament of India.
  • This dual role began on November 17, 1947, when it first met as the Dominion Legislature, and continued until the formation of the first elected Parliament in 1952.
  • When functioning as the Constituent Assembly (making the constitution), it was presided over by Dr. Rajendra Prasad.
  • When functioning as the legislative body (making ordinary laws), it was presided over by G.V. Mavalankar.

Other Significant Functions

  • Ratified India's membership of the Commonwealth in May 1949.
  • Adopted the National Flag on July 22, 1947.
  • Adopted the National Anthem on January 24, 1950.
  • Adopted the National Song on January 24, 1950.
  • Elected Dr. Rajendra Prasad as the first President of India on January 24, 1950.

Key Dates and Facts

  • The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly was held on December 9, 1946.
  • The last session of the Constituent Assembly (as a constitution-making body) was on January 24, 1950. On this day, members signed the Constitution.
  • After January 26, 1950, the Constituent Assembly ceased to exist as a constitution-making body and was transformed into the Provisional Parliament of India, continuing its legislative functions until the first general elections of 1951-52.
