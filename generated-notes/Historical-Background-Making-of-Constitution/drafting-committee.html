UPSC Study Notes - Brief and Comprehensive

Drafting Committee: Key Aspects

  • Appointment Date: The Drafting Committee was appointed by the Constituent Assembly on August 29, 1947.
  • Primary Objective: To prepare a draft of the new Constitution for India.

Composition of the Committee

The Drafting Committee consisted of seven members, including the Chairman:

  • Chairman: Dr. B.R. Ambedkar (often referred to as the 'Father of the Indian Constitution').
  • Members:
    • N. Gopalaswamy Ayyangar
    • Alladi Krishnaswamy Ayyar
    • Dr. K.M. Munshi
    • Syed Mohammad Saadullah
    • N. Madhava Rau (replaced B.L. Mitter who resigned due to ill health)
    • T.T. Krishnamachari (replaced D.P. Khaitan who died in 1948)

Role and Function

  • The committee was entrusted with the task of scrutinizing the draft prepared by the Constitutional Adviser (B.N. Rau) and incorporating the decisions of the Constituent Assembly.
  • Its main function was to give a concrete shape and final form to the principles and provisions agreed upon by the Assembly.
  • It ensured legal precision, clarity, and coherence in the constitutional document.

Key Contributions and Process

  • First Draft: Prepared and submitted its first draft of the Constitution to the President of the Constituent Assembly on February 21, 1948. This draft was published for public discussion and comments.
  • Second Draft: After considering public comments, criticisms, and suggestions, the committee prepared a second draft, which was published in October 1948.
  • Debate and Readings: The Constituent Assembly then extensively debated and discussed the Draft Constitution clause by clause. This process involved three readings:
    • First Reading: (November 4-9, 1948) - General discussion on the draft.
    • Second Reading: (November 15, 1948 - October 17, 1949) - Clause-by-clause consideration and amendments.
    • Third Reading: (November 14-26, 1949) - Final passage of the Constitution.
  • The committee sat for a total of 141 days during its drafting period.

Significance

  • The Drafting Committee, under the dynamic leadership of Dr. B.R. Ambedkar, played a crucial role in giving a definite shape and structure to the Indian Constitution.
  • Its meticulous work ensured that the Constitution was a comprehensive, robust, and legally sound document, capable of guiding the newly independent nation.
  • The committee's efforts were instrumental in synthesizing diverse ideas and principles into a coherent constitutional framework.
