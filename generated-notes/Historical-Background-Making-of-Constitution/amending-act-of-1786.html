Amending Act of 1786

Background and Purpose

  • Date: 1786

Passed primarily to accommodate Lord Cornwallis, who was appointed as the new Governor-General of Bengal. Cornwallis agreed to accept the position only on two specific conditions, and this Act was passed to fulfill these demands.

Key Provisions

  • Overriding Power: The Act empowered the Governor-General to override the decision of his Council in special cases.
