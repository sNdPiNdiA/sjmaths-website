Government of India Act 1935

UPSC Study Notes - Brief and Comprehensive

Background and Context

  • Result of the recommendations of the Simon Commission (1930), deliberations at the Round Table Conferences (1930-32), and the White Paper (1933) issued by the British government.
  • Longest and most detailed Act passed by the British Parliament before India's independence.

Key Provisions and Features

1. All-India Federation

  • Provided for the establishment of an All-India Federation comprising British Indian Provinces and Princely States as units.
  • The federation was conditional on a certain number of Princely States acceding to it.
  • Never came into being as Princely States did not join.

2. Provincial Autonomy

  • Abolished Dyarchy in the provinces and introduced Provincial Autonomy in its place.
  • Provinces were granted autonomy within their defined sphere.
  • Ministers were made responsible to the provincial legislature.
  • However, the Governor retained discretionary powers and special responsibilities.
