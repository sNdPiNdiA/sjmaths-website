Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026 • Effective Date: January 1, 2024

SJ Maths ("we", "us", or "our") operates the educational website https://sjmaths.com. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect, how it is handled, and how your privacy is protected when you browse our study notes, practice questions, and educational materials.

1. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in collecting minimal information strictly necessary to provide an exceptional learning experience:

  • Direct Contact Data: When you send a message through our Contact form or email, we receive your name, email address, and message content to assist you.
  • User Authentication: If you sign in via Google Authentication / Firebase, your profile name, email, and authentication UID are stored to keep track of your practice scores and bookmarks.
  • Local Learning State: Test submissions, dark/light theme choices, and reading history are saved in your browser's local storage and not transmitted to third parties.

3. Log Files

SJ Maths follows standard industry practice by using server log files. The logged information includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These data points are not linked to personally identifiable information and are analyzed purely for site health and traffic metrics.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policy

Google is a third-party vendor on SJ Maths. It uses cookies, including DART cookies, to serve ads based on prior visits to www.sjmaths.com and other websites across the Internet.

We partner with third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense to sustain free access to educational content for students. Please take note of the following policy requirements:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visits to our site and/or other sites across the internet.
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party cookie-based interest advertising via www.aboutads.info/choices/ or the Network Advertising Initiative Opt-Out Page.

5. Cookies & Web Beacons

Like almost all modern web platforms, SJ Maths uses cookies to record user preferences and optimize page rendering according to your device. You can configure or disable cookies via your browser settings at any time.

6. CCPA / CPRA & GDPR Rights

We respect international privacy frameworks. Consumers and students in California (CCPA/CPRA) and the European Economic Area (GDPR) have the right to request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of any stored personal information, as well as the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. To make an inquiry or request, contact us at support@sjmaths.com.

7. Children's Online Privacy Protection (COPPA)

SJ Maths provides curriculum resources for Class 9 through 12 and competitive exams. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If you believe your child has submitted personal details, contact us immediately for prompt deletion.
