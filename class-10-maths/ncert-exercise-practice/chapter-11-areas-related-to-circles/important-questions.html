Top High-Yield Board Questions: Areas Related to Circles

The following recurring questions have been selected by board examiners as essential practice for Class 10 CBSE examinations:

Standard Problem • 2 Marks Question 1

Find the area of a sector of a circle with radius 6 cm if the angle of the sector is 60°. (Take π = 22/7)

Solution: Radius \(r = 6\text{ cm}\), central angle \(\theta = 60^\circ\).
\(\text{Area of Sector} = \frac{\theta}{360^\circ} \times \pi r^2 = \frac{60^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 6^2 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 36 = \frac{132}{7}\text{ cm}^2 \approx 18.86\text{ cm}^2\).
Clock Hand Sweep • 3 Marks Question 2

The length of the minute hand of a clock is 14 cm. Find the area swept by the minute hand in 5 minutes.

Solution: In 60 minutes, the minute hand completes a full rotation of \(360^\circ\).
Angle swept in 5 minutes: \(\theta = \frac{360^\circ}{60} \times 5 = 30^\circ\).
\(\text{Area swept} = \frac{30^\circ}{360^\circ} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (14)^2 = \frac{1}{12} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 196 = \frac{154}{3}\text{ cm}^2 \approx 51.33\text{ cm}^2\).
Segment Area • 3 Marks Question 3

A chord of a circle of radius 10 cm subtends a right angle at the center. Find the area of the corresponding minor segment. (Take π = 3.14)

Solution: Radius \(r = 10\text{ cm}\), \(\theta = 90^\circ\).
\(\text{Area of Sector } OAB = \frac{90^\circ}{360^\circ} \times 3.14 \times 10^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times 314 = 78.5\text{ cm}^2\).
Since \(\angle AOB = 90^\circ\), \(\triangle OAB\) is a right triangle: \(\text{Area of } \triangle OAB = \frac{1}{2} \times 10 \times 10 = 50\text{ cm}^2\).
\(\text{Area of minor segment} = \text{Area of sector} - \text{Area of triangle} = 78.5 - 50 = 28.5\text{ cm}^2\).
