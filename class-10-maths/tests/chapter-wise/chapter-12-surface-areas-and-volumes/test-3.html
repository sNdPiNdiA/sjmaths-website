Section B (2 Marks each)

Q5. 500 persons are taking a dip into a cuboidal pond which is 80 m long and 50 m broad. What is the rise of water level in the pond, if the average displacement of the water by a person is $0.04\text{ m}^3$?

Section C (3 Marks each)

Q8. Water flows at the rate of 2.52 km/h through a cylindrical pipe into a cylindrical tank, the radius of whose base is 40 cm. If the increase in the level of water in the tank in half an hour is 3.15 m, find the internal diameter of the pipe.

Q9. A wooden toy is in the shape of a cone mounted on a hemisphere with the same base radius. The radius of the base of the cone is 1.75 cm and total height of the toy is 5 cm. Find the total surface area of the toy to be painted. (Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)

Section D (5 Marks)

Section E (Case Study - 4 Marks)

(ii) Find the actual capacity of the glass.
