How many decimal places will the expansion of $\frac{23}{2^2 \times 5}$ have?
The denominator is $2^2 \times 5^1$.
The maximum power of 2 or 5 in the denominator is 2 (from $2^2$).
Therefore, the decimal expansion will terminate after 2 decimal places.
Calculation: $\frac{23}{2^2 \times 5} = \frac{23 \times 5}{2^2 \times 5^2} = \frac{115}{100} = 1.15$
Final Answer: 2 decimal places
Q12 2020
00:00
The decimal expansion of $\frac{17}{8}$ will terminate after how many decimal places?
Denominator $8 = 2^3$.
The maximum power is 3.
So it will terminate after 3 decimal places.
Calculation: $\frac{17}{8} = \frac{17 \times 5^3}{2^3 \times 5^3} = \frac{17 \times 125}{1000} = \frac{2125}{1000} = 2.125$
Final Answer: 3 decimal places
Q16 2017
00:00
Does $\frac{2\sqrt{45} + 3\sqrt{20}}{2\sqrt{5}}$ simplify to a rational or irrational number?
Simplify the numerators:
$\sqrt{45} = \sqrt{9 \times 5} = 3\sqrt{5}$
$\sqrt{20} = \sqrt{4 \times 5} = 2\sqrt{5}$
Numerator $= 2(3\sqrt{5}) + 3(2\sqrt{5}) = 6\sqrt{5} + 6\sqrt{5} = 12\sqrt{5}$
Expression $= \frac{12\sqrt{5}}{2\sqrt{5}} = \frac{12}{2} = 6$
6 is a rational number.
Final Answer: Rational number
Q25 2017
00:00
Without long division, state whether $\frac{17}{3125}$ is terminating or non-terminating repeating. Give reasons.
Factorize the denominator: $3125 = 5^5$.
The denominator is of the form $2^n \times 5^m$ (where $n=0, m=5$).
Since it has no prime factors other than 2 or 5, the decimal expansion is terminating.
Final Answer: Terminating
