In a right-angled triangle $ABC$, right-angled at $B$, if $AB = 5\text{ cm}$ and $BC = 12\text{ cm}$, find the length of hypotenuse $AC$.
(a)13 cm
(b)17 cm
(c)7 cm
(d)15 cm
By Pythagoras Theorem in $\triangle ABC$: $AC^2 = AB^2 + BC^2$
$AC^2 = 5^2 + 12^2 = 25 + 144 = 169$
$AC = \sqrt{169} = 13\text{ cm}$
Final Answer: (A) 13 cm

A ladder $10\text{ m}$ long reaches a window $8\text{ m}$ above the ground. Find the distance of the foot of the ladder from the base of the wall.
Let the height of window be $h = 8\text{ m}$ and ladder length $L = 10\text{ m}$.
Let distance of foot of ladder from wall base be $x\text{ m}$.
By Pythagoras Theorem: $x^2 + 8^2 = 10^2 \implies x^2 + 64 = 100 \implies x^2 = 36 \implies x = 6\text{ m}$.
Final Answer: 6 m
