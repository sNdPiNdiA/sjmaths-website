## Slide 01 — Start with the story
Suggested filename: `slide-01.mp3`

Chaliye, ek real-life situation se shuru karte hain. Sochiye, class ke students roz kitne minutes padhte hain, uska record rakhna hai. Har student ka alag time likhne ke bajay, hum data ko intervals mein group karte hain, aur har interval ke saath frequency likhte hain. Ab in observations ko ek representative number mein summarize karna hai. Grouped data ka mean yahi representative average deta hai. Aaj hum class mark se shuru karke ise teen board methods se nikaalenge.

## Slide 02 — The central idea
Suggested filename: `slide-02.mp3`

Individual observations ka mean nikalne ke liye sab values ko jodkar, unki total count se divide karte hain. Frequency table mein koi value jitni baar aati hai, use utni baar count karna hota hai. Isliye frequency ek weight ki tarah kaam karti hai. Grouped data mein har interval ko uske class mark se represent karte hain. Formula hai: x bar equals, sabhi f i x i ka sum, divided by N. Mean ki unit original data jaisi hi rehti hai, aur koi bahut badi ya chhoti value mean ko kaafi badal sakti hai.

## Slide 03 — Notation and class marks
Suggested filename: `slide-03.mp3`

Har class interval ka midpoint uska class mark hota hai. Lower limit aur upper limit ko jodkar two se divide kijiye; isse x i milta hai. f i us class ki frequency hai, N total frequency yani sabhi f i ka sum hai, aur x bar mean ko show karta hai. Jaise ten se twenty ka midpoint fifteen hai, isliye us interval ka class mark fifteen hoga.

## Slide 04 — Prepare the class intervals
Suggested filename: `slide-04.mp3`

Calculation se pehle class intervals check kijiye. Zero to ten aur ten to twenty jaise continuous intervals mein boundaries pehle se milti hain. Lekin inclusive classes ten to nineteen aur twenty to twenty-nine ke beech gap one hai. Iska half, zero point five hota hai. Lower limit se zero point five ghataiye aur upper limit mein zero point five jodiye. Classes nine point five to nineteen point five, aur nineteen point five to twenty-nine point five banengi. Ab in boundaries ka midpoint, class mark hoga.

## Slide 05 — Direct method
Suggested filename: `slide-05.mp3`

Direct method mein pehle har interval ka class mark x i nikaaliye. Phir use frequency f i se multiply karke f i x i paaiye. Sabhi products jodiye aur total frequency N se divide kijiye. Yani x bar equals, sabhi f i x i ka sum, divided by N. Jab class marks aur products aasaan hon, direct method sabse seedha hai. Board answer mein f i, x i aur f i x i ke columns saaf dikhaiye.

## Slide 06 — Direct method example
Suggested filename: `slide-06.mp3`

Ab opening table solve karte hain. Class intervals zero to ten, ten to twenty, twenty to thirty, aur thirty to forty hain. Inke class marks five, fifteen, twenty-five, thirty-five hain. Frequencies three, five, seven, five hain. Total frequency twenty hai, aur sabhi f i x i ka sum four hundred forty. Direct formula se mean, four hundred forty divided by twenty, yani twenty-two minutes. Grouped data mein yeh class marks se mila estimated mean hai.

## Slide 07 — Assumed mean method
Suggested filename: `slide-07.mp3`

Assumed mean method mein beech ke paas ek convenient class mark a choose kijiye. Har class mark x i mein se a ghata kar signed deviation d i milta hai. Phir har deviation ko frequency se multiply karke products jodiye. Formula hai: x bar equals a plus, sabhi f i d i ka sum divided by N. Centre ke paas a lene se deviations chhote rehte hain. Negative sign mat hataiye: a se neeche ka class mark negative deviation deta hai.

## Slide 08 — Assumed mean example
Suggested filename: `slide-08.mp3`

Reading-time example mein assumed mean twenty-five lete hain. Class marks five, fifteen, twenty-five, thirty-five hain. Deviations minus twenty, minus ten, zero, aur ten milte hain. Inhe frequencies se multiply karne par weighted deviations ka total minus sixty aata hai. Minus sixty ko twenty se divide karne par minus three milta hai; twenty-five mein se three ghataane par mean twenty-two minutes hai. Negative correction batata hai ki mean, assumed mean se neeche hai. Answer direct method se match karta hai.

## Slide 09 — Step-deviation method
Suggested filename: `slide-09.mp3`

Agar sabhi class intervals ki width same ho, step-deviation method calculation ko chhota kar deta hai. Pehle d i equals x i minus a nikaaliye. Phir d i ko common class width h se divide karke u i paaiye. Formula hai: x bar equals a plus h into, sabhi f i u i ka sum divided by N. Dhyaan rahe, correction ko h se multiply karna hai. Unequal class widths ke liye yeh shortcut use na karein.

## Slide 10 — Step-deviation example
Suggested filename: `slide-10.mp3`

Yahan assumed mean a twenty-five aur class width h ten hai. Deviations minus twenty, minus ten, zero, ten ko ten se divide karne par step values minus two, minus one, zero, one milti hain. Frequency se weight karne ke baad inka total minus six hai. Ab minus six ko total frequency twenty se divide karke ten se multiply karte hain; correction minus three hai. Twenty-five mein se three ghataane par mean twenty-two minutes milta hai. Direct, assumed mean aur step-deviation, teeno methods ka answer same hai.

## Slide 11 — Choosing a method
Suggested filename: `slide-11.mp3`

Method chunne ka simple rule yaad rakhiye. Class marks aur products aasaan hon, toh direct method. Class marks bade hon, lekin assumed mean se deviations chhote hon, toh assumed mean method. Sab intervals ki width equal ho, toh step-deviation calculation ko fast karta hai. Teeno methods ka final mean same aana chahiye. Agar question mein koi method specifically maanga ho, toh wahi method use karke poora working dikhaiye.

## Slide 12 — One missing frequency
Suggested filename: `slide-12.mp3`

Board exam mein ek missing frequency m ho, toh use total frequency aur weighted sum, dono mein include kijiye. Is example mein class marks five, fifteen, twenty-five, thirty-five hain; frequencies four, six, m, five hain, aur mean twenty-two diya hai. Isliye N equals fifteen plus m, aur sabhi f i x i ka sum equals two hundred eighty-five plus twenty-five m. Mean ki condition lagane par twenty-two into fifteen plus m, equals two hundred eighty-five plus twenty-five m. Solve karne par m equals fifteen. Check: total frequency thirty, weighted sum six hundred sixty, aur mean twenty-two.

## Slide 13 — Two missing frequencies
Suggested filename: `slide-13.mp3`

Do missing frequencies p aur q hon, toh mean ke saath total frequency ki condition bhi chahiye. Is example mein total thirty hai aur known frequencies four aur five hain, isliye p plus q equals twenty-one. Mean twenty-two hone par weighted sum six hundred sixty hoga. Known classes ka weighted sum ghataane par fifteen p plus twenty-five q equals four hundred sixty-five milta hai. Dono equations solve karne par p equals six aur q equals fifteen. Sirf ek equation se do unknown frequencies alag-alag nahi mil sakti.

## Slide 14 — Board-answer checklist
Suggested filename: `slide-14.mp3`

Board-ready answer mein pehle class marks likhiye, phir required product ya deviation columns banaiye. N equals sabhi f i ka sum, aur zaroori weighted sum clearly dikhaiye. Sahi formula mein values substitute karke signs aur unit ke saath final answer dijiye. Step-deviation mein h se multiply karna na bhoolein. Quick check: mean, sabse chhote aur sabse bade class mark ke beech hona chahiye. Mean ki unit data jaisi hoti hai, aur extreme values se mean badal sakta hai. Class limits ko class marks samajhna, classes ki count se divide karna, ya missing frequency ko total se chhod dena common mistakes hain.

## Slide 15 — Quick revision
Suggested filename: `slide-15.mp3`

Quick revision. Class mark equals lower limit plus upper limit, divided by two. Direct method mein x bar equals sabhi f i x i ka sum divided by N. Assumed mean method mein d i equals x i minus a, aur x bar equals a plus sabhi f i d i ka sum divided by N. Equal class widths ke liye u i equals x i minus a divided by h; phir correction ko h se multiply karte hain. Aakhri check: N forty hai aur weighted sum nine hundred twenty. Mean kya hoga? Nine hundred twenty divided by forty, yani twenty-three.
