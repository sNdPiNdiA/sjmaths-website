CBSE Class 10 Mathematics Question Paper Structure (80 Marks)

Every full-length practice paper is aligned with the official 38-question CBSE Board Exam design divided into five sections:

  • Section A (Q1 to Q20): 20 Multiple Choice Questions (including 2 Assertion-Reasoning questions) carrying 1 mark each.
  • Section B (Q21 to Q25): 5 Very Short Answer (VSA) questions carrying 2 marks each, requiring concise mathematical reasoning.
  • Section C (Q26 to Q31): 6 Short Answer (SA) questions carrying 3 marks each, covering standard textbook proofs and algebraic solutions.
  • Section D (Q32 to Q35): 4 Long Answer (LA) questions carrying 5 marks each with internal choices in geometry theorems, surface areas, and trigonometry word problems.
  • Section E (Q36 to Q38): 3 Case-Based / Integrated Units of Assessment carrying 4 marks each with sub-parts of 1, 1, and 2 marks.
