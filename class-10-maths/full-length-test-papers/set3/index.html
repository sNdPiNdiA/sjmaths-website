SECTION E (Case Study)

QQ36
Case Study: Probability (Cards)
Two friends, Rahul and Ravi, are playing a game with a standard deck of 52 playing cards. Rahul draws a card at random.
i. What is the probability that the card drawn is a face card?
ii. What is the probability that the card drawn is a Red Queen?
iii. If all the Kings are removed from the deck, what is the probability of drawing an Ace?
QQ37
Case Study: Statistics (Sports)
A coach is analyzing the performance of a batsman in 10 matches. The runs scored are: 12, 25, 38, 56, 12, 45, 78, 12, 34, 60.
i. Find the mean runs scored by the batsman.
ii. Find the mode of the data.
iii. Find the median score.
QQ38
Case Study: Trigonometry
A lighthouse is a tower, building, or other type of structure designed to emit light from a system of lamps and lenses and to serve as a navigational aid. A guard on top of a 100m high lighthouse observes a ship coming towards it. The angle of depression changes from $30^\circ$ to $45^\circ$.
i. Draw a labeled diagram to represent the situation.
ii. Find the distance of the ship from the lighthouse when the angle of depression is $45^\circ$.
iii. Find the distance traveled by the ship during the period of observation.
