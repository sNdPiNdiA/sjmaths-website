The 11th Raisina Dialogue was held in New Delhi (March 5-7), bringing together ~2,700 participants from 110 countries. The 4th India-Africa Forum Summit (IAFS-IV) was scheduled to be held in New Delhi from May 28–31, 2026 under the theme: "IA SPIRIT: India Africa Strategic Partnership for Innovation, Resilience, and Inclusive Transformation."
