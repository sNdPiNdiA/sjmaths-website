UPSSSC PET Syllabus Tip:

Awards carry 3–5 marks in GK. Focus heavily on: Padma Awards, Gallantry Awards, Jnanpith/Sahitya Akademi, Film Awards, PM Modi's international honours, Sportsखेल (टेनिस) Awards (Laureus, Khel Ratna), and UN prizes. Make a single-page cheat sheet with award → winner → field before the exam.
