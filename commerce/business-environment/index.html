Business Environment

Explore chapters and topics under Business Environment.

Understand the external forces that influence business decisions, planning, and performance. The linked chapter introduces the dimensions of the business environment, including economic, social, technological, political, legal, and ecological factors, so learners can examine how organisations respond to change and opportunity. Use the concepts with examples and revision practice for senior-secondary commerce and business studies learners preparing for examinations and future professional contexts in changing markets and organisations.
