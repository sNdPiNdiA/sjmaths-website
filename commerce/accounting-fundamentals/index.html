Accounting Fundamentals

Explore chapters and topics under Accounting Fundamentals.

Build accounting foundations by learning concepts and conventions, the double-entry system, books of original entry, ledger posting, and Indian Accounting Standards. These chapters introduce the language and recording logic needed to classify transactions, maintain reliable accounts, and prepare for later financial statements with examples, practice questions, and revision guidance for commerce learners at school and higher-secondary level.
