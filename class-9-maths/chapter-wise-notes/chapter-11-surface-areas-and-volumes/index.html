Q1: Find the curved surface area of a cone with radius 7 cm and slant height 10 cm.

Formula: CSA = πrl

= (22/7) × 7 × 10

= 220 cm²

Q2: A hemisphere has radius 3.5 cm. Find its total surface area.

Formula: TSA of hemisphere = 3πr²

= 3 × (22/7) × (3.5)²

= 3 × (22/7) × 12.25 = 115.5 cm²

Q3: A cylindrical tank has radius 1.4 m and height 2 m. Find its volume in litres.

Formula: V = πr²h

= (22/7) × (1.4)² × 2

= (22/7) × 1.96 × 2 = 12.32 m³

1 m³ = 1000 litres → 12320 litres

Q4: If the radius of a sphere is doubled, by what factor does its volume increase?

Volume: V = (4/3)πr³

New V = (4/3)π(2r)³ = (4/3)π × 8r³ = 8V

∴ Volume becomes 8 times

Key: Volume scales as the cube of the linear dimension.
