Class 9 Geometry Unit Tests

Euclid and constructions, lines and angles, congruence and converses, quadrilaterals and symmetry, and circle theorems.

69 teaching periods in the supplied syllabus; this is not examination mark weightage.

Scope

  • Geometry in ancient India, Egypt and Greece
  • Definitions, axioms and five Euclidean postulates
  • Square construction and Sulbasutra reasoning
  • Rays and angle classification
  • Parallel lines, transitivity and transversal angles
  • Linear pairs, their converse and vertical angles
  • Triangle rigidity and practical uses
  • SAS, SSS, ASA, AAS, RHS and vertex correspondence
  • Isosceles triangle properties
  • Propositions, converses, counterexamples and contradiction
  • SSA ambiguity and valid special cases
  • Precise quadrilateral definition and parallelogram characterisations
  • Midpoint theorem and converse
  • Median concurrency and 2:1 division
  • Midpoint quadrilateral theorem
  • Reflection, rotation and central symmetry
  • Quadrilateral tiling and manipulation
  • Circle terms, applications and cultural motifs
  • Circumcircle, uniqueness and circumcentre location
  • Chords, bisectors and distance from centre
  • Central and inscribed angles, segments and semicircles
  • Cyclic quadrilaterals and converse
