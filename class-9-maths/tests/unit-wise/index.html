Twelve practice papers across the six units in the supplied syllabus, with multiple-choice, written, proof, construction, graphing and contextual questions and step-wise model answers.

The counts below are teaching periods from the supplied syllabus, not official examination marks. Practice-paper marks are specified on each test.
