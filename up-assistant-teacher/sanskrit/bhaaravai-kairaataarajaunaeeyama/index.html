भारवि - किरातार्जुनीयम् Bharavi – Kiratarjuniya (भारवि - किरातार्जुनीयम्)

Bharavi — Sanskrit poet, Kiratarjuniya (the hunter and Arjuna), celebrated for its meaning (Arthagaurav), poetic magnificence, and the Shiva-Arjuna episode.
