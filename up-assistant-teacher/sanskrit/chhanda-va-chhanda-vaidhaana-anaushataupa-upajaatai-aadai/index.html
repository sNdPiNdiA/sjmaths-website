छंद एवं छंद विधान - अनुष्टुप, उपजाति आदि Chhanda evam Chhanda Vidhan (छंद एवं छंद विधान)

Sanskrit Chhanda (meters) — classification of Vedic and Classical meters including Anushtup, Upajati, Indravajra, Upendravajra, Vasantatilaka etc., with rules of gana and matra.
