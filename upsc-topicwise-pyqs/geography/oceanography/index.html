UPSC CSE Prelims 2010 · Q.73 Freshwater Distribution
Question: Consider the following statements:

2. Of the total fresh water found on the planet Earth 95% is bound up in polar ice caps and glaciers.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct?
प्रश्न: निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए:
1. पृथ्वी ग्रह पर उपयोग के लिए उपलब्ध अलवण जल (ताजा पानी) कुल पाए जाने वाले जल का 1% से भी कम है।
2. पृथ्वी ग्रह पर पाए जाने वाले कुल अलवण जल का 95% ध्रुवीय बर्फ की चोटियों और हिमनदों (ग्लेशियरों) में आबद्ध है।

(B) 2 only केवल 2
(C) Both 1 and 2 1 और 2 दोनों
(D) Neither 1 nor 2 न तो 1 और न ही 2
Correct Answer: Option A
Explanation:
Correct Answer: Option A (1 only)

Total water on Earth is approximately 1.386 billion cubic kilometers, of which ~97.5% is saline ocean water and only ~2.5% to 2.8% is freshwater.

Statement Analysis

  • Statement 1 is CORRECT: Out of the ~2.5% freshwater on Earth, nearly 68.7% is locked in glaciers and ice caps, and ~30.1% is stored deep underground. The readily accessible freshwater found in surface water bodies (lakes, rivers, swamps) and atmospheric moisture amounts to only about 0.3% to 0.4% of total freshwater, which corresponds to less than 0.01% of all water on Earth. Even when adding accessible shallow groundwater, total freshwater available for human use is well below 1% of the total water found.
  • Statement 2 is INCORRECT: Glaciers and permanent ice caps contain approximately 68.7% (or about 69%) of the Earth's freshwater, not 95%. Groundwater comprises about 30.1%, and other surface/atmospheric reservoirs constitute the remaining ~1.2%.

Correct Answer: Option B
Explanation:
Correct Answer: Option B (1, 2 and 3)

Ocean currents are continuous, directed movements of seawater generated by a combination of primary and secondary forces acting upon the oceans.

Analysis of Driving Factors

  • 1. Rotation of the Earth (INFLUENCES): The Coriolis force arising from Earth's axial rotation deflects currents to the right in the Northern Hemisphere and to the left in the Southern Hemisphere, creating large circular loops known as gyres.
  • 2. Air pressure and wind (INFLUENCES): Planetary winds (trade winds, westerlies, polar easterlies) are the primary driving force of surface ocean currents through frictional drag on the water surface. Variations in atmospheric pressure also produce slight sea surface slope gradients.
  • 3. Density of ocean water (INFLUENCES): Differences in water density, driven by temperature (thermal) and salinity (haline) variations, drive the vertical and deep horizontal movement of water known as thermohaline circulation (or the global ocean conveyor belt). Dense, cold, saline water sinks at polar regions and flows toward equatorial latitudes.
  • 4. Revolution of the Earth (DOES NOT INFLUENCE): Earth's annual orbital revolution around the Sun causes seasonal changes, but is not an active force initiating or influencing the physical flow and directional dynamics of ocean currents.
Key Takeaway: Primary forces that drive and influence ocean currents are planetary wind friction, atmospheric pressure, Earth's rotation (Coriolis deflection), and seawater density differences (temperature and salinity). Revolution around the Sun is not a direct factor.

The world's richest commercial fishing grounds are concentrated along shallow continental shelves where warm and cold oceanic currents converge.

Ecological & Oceanographic Mechanism

  • Nutrient Upwelling: When cold, dense, nutrient-laden currents collide with warm, lighter currents, intense vertical mixing and upwelling occur, bringing nitrates, phosphates, and silica from the ocean depths to the sunlit photic zone.
  • Plankton Explosion: The optimal moderate water temperatures and abundant dissolved nutrients trigger explosive blooms of phytoplankton and zooplankton, which serve as the base of the marine food web.
  • Dense Fish Populations: Huge schools of pelagic and demersal fish (cod, herring, mackerel, salmon, sardines) congregate to feed on the plankton.

Prominent Global Examples

  • Grand Banks of Newfoundland (Northwest Atlantic): Convergence of the warm Gulf Stream and the cold Labrador Current (one of the historically richest cod fisheries in the world).
  • Waters off Northeast Japan (Northwest Pacific): Convergence of the warm Kuroshio Current and the cold Oyashio Current.
  • Note on Navigation: The collision of warm, humid air above warm currents with chilled air above cold currents frequently creates dense, hazardous sea fog.
Key Takeaway: Convergence of warm and cold ocean currents induces vertical mixing and nutrient enrichment, fostering massive plankton blooms that support the world's most productive commercial fisheries.

Explanation:
Correct Answer: Option B (Convergence of the two equatorial currents)

The Equatorial Counter-Current (ECC) is an anomalous eastward-flowing surface current located in the equatorial belt between the westward-flowing North Equatorial Current (NEC) and South Equatorial Current (SEC).

Physical Mechanism of Flow

  • Westward Water Piling: The steady Northeast and Southeast Trade Winds continuously drive warm surface water westward across the Atlantic and Pacific oceans as the North and South Equatorial Currents.
  • Hydraulic Gradient (Pressure Head): As these two vast currents converge in the western margins of the ocean basins (near the western Pacific/Indonesia and Caribbean), water literally piles up. The sea level in the western Pacific, for instance, is 40 to 50 cm higher than in the eastern Pacific.
  • Gravity-Driven Return Flow: This convergence and piling of water creates an eastward-sloping pressure gradient (hydraulic head). In the calm, narrow zone of the Intertropical Convergence Zone (ITCZ / Doldrums) where trade winds are light or absent and Coriolis force is virtually zero at the equator, this accumulated water flows back 'downhill' toward the east as the Equatorial Counter-Current.
Key Takeaway: The convergence and westward piling of water by the North and South Equatorial Currents creates a sea-level slope (hydraulic head) that forces an eastward gravity return flow—the Equatorial Counter-Current.

Correct Answer: Option D
Explanation:
Correct Answer: Option D (1, 2 and 3)

Tides are the periodic rise and fall of ocean waters caused by the net tide-generating force (differential gravitational force) resulting from the gravitational attraction of extraterrestrial bodies balanced against inertial/centrifugal forces.

Analysis of All Three Forces

  • 1. Gravitational force of the Moon: The Moon is the principal driver of tides. Despite its smaller mass compared to the Sun, its extreme proximity to Earth makes its tidal pulling power roughly 2.17 times greater than that of the Sun. On the side of Earth facing the Moon, lunar gravitation pulls the water upward, forming a direct tidal bulge.
  • 2. Centrifugal force of the Earth: Earth and Moon revolve around a common center of mass (the barycenter, located ~1,700 km beneath Earth's surface). On the side of Earth opposite the Moon, lunar gravity is weakest and is exceeded by the outward centrifugal force resulting from this orbital motion, creating a second (antipodal) tidal bulge.
  • 3. Gravitational force of the Sun: Although the Sun is ~27 million times more massive than the Moon, its immense distance (~390 times farther) dilutes its tidal force (tidal force drops off with the cube of distance, $1/d^3$). Still, solar gravity contributes about 46% of the Moon's tide-generating power, determining the amplitude difference between spring tides and neap tides.
Key Takeaway: Ocean tides are produced by the interaction of three distinct forces: the Moon's gravitational pull (direct bulge), Earth's orbital centrifugal force (antipodal bulge), and the Sun's gravitational pull (modulating spring/neap range).

  • कथन 1 सही है: UNCLOS के अनुच्छेद 3 के अनुसार, प्रत्येक तटीय राज्य को आधार-रेखा (Baseline) से अधिकतम 12 समुद्री मील (Nautical Miles) तक अपने प्रादेशिक समुद्र (Territorial Sea) की सीमा निर्धारित करने का अधिकार है। इस क्षेत्र में तटीय राज्य की पूर्ण संप्रभुता होती है।
  • कथन 2 सही है: UNCLOS के अनुच्छेद 17 के अनुसार, सभी राज्यों के पोत—चाहे वे तटीय राज्य हों या भू-आबद्ध (Land-locked)—प्रादेशिक समुद्र से होकर निर्दोष आवागमन (Innocent Passage) के अधिकार का उपयोग कर सकते हैं। आवागमन तब तक निर्दोष माना जाता है जब तक कि वह तटीय राज्य की शांति, सुव्यवस्था या सुरक्षा के लिए हानिकारक न हो।
  • कथन 3 सही है: UNCLOS के अनुच्छेद 57 के अनुसार, अनन्य आर्थिक क्षेत्र (EEZ) आधार-रेखा से अधिकतम 200 समुद्री मील तक विस्तारित हो सकता है। इस क्षेत्र में तटीय राज्य को समुद्री जल, समुद्र-नितल और उसके उपमृदा के सभी प्राकृतिक संसाधनों (मत्स्य, खनिज आदि) के दोहन व संरक्षण का संप्रभु अधिकार होता है।
मुख्य निष्कर्ष: UNCLOS 1982 के तहत प्रादेशिक समुद्र 12 नॉटिकल मील (जिसमें सभी देशों को निर्दोष आवागमन का अधिकार है) और EEZ आधार-रेखा से 200 नॉटिकल मील तक होता है। अतः तीनों कथन सही हैं।

UPSC CSE Prelims 2024 · Q.89 Red Sea
Question: Consider the following statements :
1. The Red Sea receives very little precipitation in any form.
2. No water enters the Red Sea from rivers.
Which of the statements given above is/are correct ?
(a) 1 only
(b) 2 only
(c) Both 1 and 2
(d) Neither 1 nor 2
प्रश्न: निम्नलिखित कथनों पर विचार कीजिए :
1. लाल सागर (Red Sea) किसी भी रूप में बहुत कम वर्षण (precipitation) प्राप्त करता है।
2. नदियों से कोई जल लाल सागर में प्रवेश नहीं करता है।
उपर्युक्त कथनों में से कौन-सा/से सही है/हैं?
(a) केवल 1
(b) केवल 2
(c) 1 और 2 दोनों
(d) न तो 1 और न ही 2
(A) 1 only केवल 1
(B) 2 only केवल 2
(C) Both 1 and 2 1 और 2 दोनों ✓ [Official Key]
(D) Neither 1 nor 2 न तो 1 और न ही 2
Correct Answer: Option C
Explanation:
Correct Answer: Option C (Both 1 and 2)

The Red Sea is a narrow, semi-enclosed inland sea separating northeast Africa from the Arabian Peninsula, connected to the Indian Ocean via the narrow Bab-el-Mandeb strait.

Statement Analysis

  • Statement 1 is CORRECT: The Red Sea lies in the hyper-arid subtropical desert belt (sandwiched between the Sahara and Eastern Desert of Africa to the west and the Arabian Desert to the east). Annual precipitation over the Red Sea is extremely meager, averaging less than 60 mm (2.4 inches) per year, mostly falling as brief, infrequent showers during winter months.
  • Statement 2 is CORRECT: Remarkably, not a single permanent river or perennial stream enters or drains into the Red Sea. Only temporary wadis (dry ephemeral channels) occasionally carry flash flood runoff during rare torrential cloudbursts, but there are zero permanent riverine inflows.

Consequences on Oceanographic Characteristics

Because the Red Sea has virtually zero river discharge, extremely low rainfall, and intense evaporation (~2,000 mm/year driven by relentless solar heating), it is one of the hottest and saltiest marine bodies on Earth. Its surface salinity ranges between 36‰ to over 41‰ in the northern Gulf of Suez/Aqaba (significantly higher than the global ocean average of 35‰). The only significant freshwater input is water exchange with the Gulf of Aden through the Strait of Bab-el-Mandeb.

Key Takeaway: The Red Sea receives under 60 mm of annual rainfall and has NO permanent rivers discharging into it. Combined with extreme evaporation, this makes it one of the saltiest open seas in the world.
व्याख्या:
सही उत्तर: विकल्प C (1 और 2 दोनों)

लाल सागर (Red Sea) अफ्रीका और अरब प्रायद्वीप के बीच स्थित एक संकरा आंतरिक समुद्र है, जो दक्षिण में बाब-अल-मंदेब जलडमरूमध्य द्वारा हिंद महासागर (अदन की खाड़ी) से जुड़ता है।

कथनों का विश्लेषण

  • कथन 1 सही है: लाल सागर उपोष्णकटिबंधीय अत्यधिक शुष्क मरुस्थलीय कटिबंध (अफ्रीका के सहारा/पूर्वी मरुस्थल और अरब मरुस्थल के मध्य) में स्थित है। यहाँ वार्षिक औसत वर्षा अत्यंत कम (60 मिमी से भी कम प्रति वर्ष) होती है।
  • कथन 2 सही है: लाल सागर की सबसे अनूठी भौगोलिक विशेषता यह है कि कोई भी स्थायी (सदानीरा) नदी इसमें अपना जल नहीं गिराती है। केवल कभी-कभार वर्षा होने पर शुष्क मौसमी घाटियों (वादी - Wadis) से क्षणिक अपवाह आता है, किंतु कोई वास्तविक नदी इसमें प्रवेश नहीं करती।

समुद्र वैज्ञानिक प्रभाव

शून्य नदीय जल आवक, नगण्य वर्षा और अत्यधिक वाष्पीकरण (~2000 मिमी/वर्ष) के कारण लाल सागर विश्व के सर्वाधिक लवणीय खुले समुद्रों में से एक है। इसकी लवणता 36‰ से 41‰ तक पहुँच जाती है (विश्व महासागरों का औसत 35‰ है)।

मुख्य निष्कर्ष: लाल सागर में न तो कोई नदी गिरती है और न ही पर्याप्त वर्षा होती है। इस कारण यह अत्यधिक वाष्पीकरण और उच्चतम लवणता (36‰-41‰) वाला समुद्र है।
