  4. The coordinates of the orthocentre of the triangle formed by the lines $x = 0, y = 0$ and $x + y = 1$ is
  5. The area of the triangle formed by the lines $y = x, y = 2x$ and $y = 3x + 4$ is
  6. Find the equation of the line passing through the point $(2, 2)$ such that the sum of its intercepts on the axes is 9.
  7. Find the equation of the line mid-parallel to the lines $2x - 5y + 7 = 0$ and $2x - 5y - 3 = 0$.

  10. Find the equation of the line passing through the intersection of the lines $4x + 7y - 3 = 0$ and $2x - 3y + 1 = 0$ that has equal intercepts on the axes.

  12. Case Study: A person is standing at the junction (crossing) of two straight paths represented by the equations $2x - 3y + 4 = 0$ and $3x + 4y - 5 = 0$. He wants to reach the path whose equation is $6x - 7y + 8 = 0$ in the least time.
    (i) Find the position of the person (intersection point).
    (ii) Find the equation of the path he should follow.
    (iii) Find the minimum distance he has to walk.
