Questions covered in Standard

Review the question prompts below before starting the timed test. Try each problem independently, then use the interactive test to record your answers and review the worked solutions.

  1. The point $(-4, 2, -5)$ lies in which octant?
  2. The distance of the point $P(3, -2, 5)$ from the y-axis is
  3. If the distance between the points $(x, 0, 1)$ and $(2, 0, 1)$ is 4, then $x$ is
  4. The ratio in which the YZ-plane divides the line segment formed by joining the points $(-2, 4, 7)$ and $(3, -5, 8)$ is
  5. The centroid of the triangle with vertices $(3, -5, 7)$, $(-1, 7, -6)$ and $(1, 1, 2)$ is
  6. Find the coordinates of the point which divides the line segment joining the points $(1, -2, 3)$ and $(3, 4, -5)$ in the ratio 2:3 internally.
  7. Find the point on the y-axis which is equidistant from the points $P(3, 1, 2)$ and $Q(5, 5, 2)$.
  8. Show that the points $A(1, 2, 3)$, $B(-1, -2, -1)$, $C(2, 3, 2)$ and $D(4, 7, 6)$ are the vertices of a parallelogram.
  9. Find the equation of the set of points P, the sum of whose distances from $A(4, 0, 0)$ and $B(-4, 0, 0)$ is equal to 10.
  10. Find the coordinates of a point on the y-axis which is at a distance of $5\sqrt{2}$ from the point $P(3, -2, 5)$.
  11. Find the coordinates of the points which trisect the line segment joining the points $P(4, 2, -6)$ and $Q(10, -16, 6)$.
  12. Case Study: A cricket match is being played. A camera is suspended at a point $C(10, 10, 8)$ in the stadium (coordinates in meters). The pitch center is at origin $O(0,0,0)$.
    (i) Find the height of the camera from the ground (XY-plane).
    (ii) Find the distance of the camera from the pitch center.
    (iii) If the camera moves to a new position $D$ such that $D$ is the reflection of $C$ in the XY-plane, find coordinates of $D$.
