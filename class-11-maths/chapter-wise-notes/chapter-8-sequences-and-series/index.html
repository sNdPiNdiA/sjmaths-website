1. Introduction

Sequence: An arrangement of numbers in a definite order according to some rule.

Series: If \( a_1, a_2, ..., a_n \) is a sequence, then the sum \( a_1 + a_2 + ... + a_n \) is called the series associated with it.

3. Arithmetic vs Geometric Mean

For two positive numbers \( a \) and \( b \):

  • AM: \( \frac{a+b}{2} \)
  • GM: \( \sqrt{ab} \)
  • Relation: \( AM \ge GM \)

5.1 Arithmetico-Geometric Progression (AGP)

A sequence which is the product of corresponding terms of an AP and a GP.

Form: \( a, (a+d)r, (a+2d)r^2, \dots \)

Sum of \( n \) terms:

\( S_n = \frac{a}{1-r} + d r \frac{1-r^{n-1}}{(1-r)^2} - \frac{[a+(n-1)d]r^n}{1-r} \)

Sum to Infinity (\( |r| < 1 \)):

\( S_\infty = \frac{a}{1-r} + \frac{dr}{(1-r)^2} \)

5.2 Harmonic Progression (HP)

A sequence is in HP if the reciprocals of its terms form an Arithmetic Progression (AP).

Note: No term of an HP can be zero.

  • If \( a, b, c \) are in HP, then \( \frac{1}{a}, \frac{1}{b}, \frac{1}{c} \) are in AP.
  • Harmonic Mean (HM): The HM between two positive numbers \( a \) and \( b \) is \( \frac{2ab}{a+b} \).

Real-Life Word Problems (AP & GP)

Example 1 (AP):

The monthly salary of a worker increases by ?500 every year. If his first salary is ?15,000, find his salary after 10 years.

Example 2 (GP):

A ball rebounds to half its height every time. If initial height is 10 m, find total distance travelled.

Exam Insight: These questions test your ability to **identify a, d/r, n** correctly.

?? Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using AP formula for GP problems
  • Confusing \( n^{th} \) term with sum of n terms
  • Forgetting that infinite GP exists only when |r| < 1
  • Applying AM = GM for negative numbers
  • Missing number of terms in word problems
