Multiple Choice Questions

Q1
The distance of point P(3, 4, 5) from the yz-plane is
The distance of a point $(x, y, z)$ from the yz-plane is given by $|x|$. Here, the point is $(3, 4, 5)$, so the distance is $|3| = 3$ units.
$3 units$
Q2
What is the length of foot of perpendicular drawn from the point P (3, 4, 5) on y-axis
The length of the perpendicular from $P(x, y, z)$ to the y-axis is $\sqrt{x^2 + z^2}$. Here, it is $\sqrt{3^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{9 + 25} = \sqrt{34}$. The options provided (41, 34, 5) seem to represent the square of the distance. Since $34$ is the square of the actual length, option (B) is the intended answer in this context.
$34$
Q3
Distance of the point (3, 4, 5) from the origin (0, 0, 0) is
The distance from the origin is $\sqrt{3^2 + 4^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{9 + 16 + 25} = \sqrt{50}$. Similar to the previous question, the options represent the square of the distance. Option (A) 50 is the square of $\sqrt{50}$.
$50$
Q4
If the distance between the points (a, 0, 1) and (0, 1, 2) is 27 , then the value of a is
Let the distance be $\sqrt{27}$ (assuming the square root symbol was omitted in the question text, consistent with the pattern of squared values). Then distance$^2 = 27$. Using the distance formula: $(a-0)^2 + (0-1)^2 + (1-2)^2 = 27 \Rightarrow a^2 + 1 + 1 = 27 \Rightarrow a^2 = 25 \Rightarrow a = \pm 5$.
$± 5$
Q5
x-axis is the intersection of two planes
The x-axis is the line where $y=0$ and $z=0$. The xy-plane is $z=0$ and the xz-plane is $y=0$. Thus, their intersection is the x-axis.
$xy and xz$
Q6
Equation of y-axis is considered as
On the y-axis, the coordinates are of the form $(0, y, 0)$. Thus, both $x$ and $z$ coordinates are zero. The equations are $x=0$ and $z=0$.
$z = 0, x = 0$
Q7
The point (–2, –3, –4) lies in the
The signs of the coordinates are $(-,-,-)$. This corresponds to the 7th octant (VII).
$Seventh octant$
Q8
A plane is parallel to yz-plane so it is perpendicular to :
The yz-plane is perpendicular to the x-axis. Any plane parallel to the yz-plane will also be perpendicular to the x-axis.
$x-axis$
Q9
The locus of a point for which y = 0, z = 0 is
The condition $y=0$ and $z=0$ defines the x-axis.
$equation of x-axis$
Q10
The locus of a point for which x = 0 is
The equation $x=0$ represents the yz-plane.
$yz-plane$
Q11
If a parallelopiped is formed by planes drawn through the points (5, 8, 10) and (3, 6, 8) parallel to the coordinate planes, then the length of diagonal of the parallelopiped is
The length of the diagonal is the distance between the two points: $\sqrt{(5-3)^2 + (8-6)^2 + (10-8)^2} = \sqrt{2^2 + 2^2 + 2^2} = \sqrt{4+4+4} = \sqrt{12} = 2\sqrt{3}$.
$2 √3$
Q12
L is the foot of the perpendicular drawn from a point P (3, 4, 5) on the xy-plane. The coordinates of point L are
The foot of the perpendicular from $(x, y, z)$ on the xy-plane is $(x, y, 0)$. For $P(3, 4, 5)$, it is $(3, 4, 0)$. This is not in the options (A), (B), or (C).
$none of these$
Q13
L is the foot of the perpendicular drawn from a point (3, 4, 5) on x-axis. The coordinates of L are
The foot of the perpendicular from $(x, y, z)$ on the x-axis is $(x, 0, 0)$. For $(3, 4, 5)$, it is $(3, 0, 0)$.
$(3, 0, 0)$

The length of the longest piece of a string that can be stretched straight in a rectangular room whose dimensions are 10, 13 and 8 units are ______.

Q27
If the distance between the points (a, 2, 1) and (1, –1, 1) is 5, then a _______.
$\sqrt{(a-1)^2 + (2-(-1))^2 + (1-1)^2} = 5 \Rightarrow (a-1)^2 + 9 = 25 \Rightarrow (a-1)^2 = 16 \Rightarrow a-1 = \pm 4$. So $a=5$ or $a=-3$.
$5 or -3$
Q28
If the mid-points of the sides of a triangle AB; BC; CA are D (1, 2, – 3), E (3, 0, 1) and F (–1, 1, – 4), then the centriod of the triangle ABC is ________
The centroid of $\triangle ABC$ is the same as the centroid of the triangle formed by the midpoints of its sides. $G = (\frac{1+3-1}{3}, \frac{2+0+1}{3}, \frac{-3+1-4}{3}) = (1, 1, -2)$.
$(1, 1, -2)$
