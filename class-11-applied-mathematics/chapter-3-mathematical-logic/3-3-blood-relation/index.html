Exercise 3.3: Blood Relation

Construct genealogical family trees, resolve paternal/maternal branches, and decode symbol-based relationship ciphers.

Core Theoretical Principles

Systematic principles and analytical strategies prescribed by the CBSE curriculum for Blood Relation.

Standard Family Tree Notations:
• Male: $[+]$ or $\square$
• Female: $[-]$ or $\bigcirc$
• Married Couple: Double horizontal line $[A] = [B]$
• Siblings: Single horizontal line $A - B$
• Generational Shift: Vertical descent line $\downarrow$
Key Generational Levels:
• Grandparents generation: $+2$
• Parents, uncles, aunts: $+1$
• Own generation (siblings, cousins, spouse): $0$
• Children, nephews, nieces: $-1$
• Grandchildren: $-2$
Coded Relations Decoding Algorithm:
In expressions like $G @ T \# P$, decode by substituting definitions from left to right or right to left, tracking gender and generation step-by-step.

Solved Illustrative Examples

Example 1 (Direct Verbal Clue)
Introducing a woman, a man said, 'Her husband is the only son of my father.' How is the woman related to the man?
Step 1: 'Only son of my father' $\implies$ the speaker himself (since the speaker is a man).
Step 2: 'Her husband is [the speaker himself]'.
Step 3: Therefore, the woman is the man's wife.
Answer: (c) Wife.
Example 2 (Coded Blood Relation)
If 'A#B' means A is father of B, 'A*B' means A is brother of B, 'A@B' means A is mother of B. Which is correct about G@T#P?
Step 1: Decode $G @ T$: G is the mother of T (G is female, T is child).
Step 2: Decode $T \# P$: T is the father of P (T is male).
Step 3: Since T is male and G is mother of T, T is the son of G.
Answer: (c) T is son of G.
Example 3 (Multi-Generation Pointer)
Pointing towards a person, a man said to a woman, 'His father is the son of my mother's only son.' How is the man related to that person?
Step 1: 'My mother's only son' $\implies$ the speaker himself.
Step 2: 'The son of my mother's only son' $\implies$ speaker's son.
Step 3: 'His father is [the speaker's son]'.
Step 4: The person's father is the speaker's son $\implies$ The speaker is the grandfather of the person.
Answer: (d) Grandfather.

CYP 3.3.1 CBSE Textbook Question
R is the son of A's father's sister. S is son of D, who is the mother of G and grandmother of A. H is the father of T and grandfather of R. D is wife of H. How is R related to D?
1. D is grandmother of A and wife of H (grandfather of R).
2. Thus D and H are grandparents of R.
3. Since R is a male child of their daughter, R is the grandson of D.
Answer: (c) Grandson.
CYP 3.3.2 CBSE Textbook Question
A, B, C, D, E, F and G are members of a family of 4 adults and 3 children (F and G are boys). A and D are brothers; A is engineer. E is a doctor married to one brother and has 2 children. B is married to D and G is their child. Who is C?
1. Brothers are A and D.
2. B is married to D $\implies$ E is married to A.
3. E has 2 children (out of 3 children C, F, G). Since G is child of B & D, the other two children F and C belong to A & E.
4. Since two children are boys (F and G), C must be the female child (daughter).
Answer: (c) E's daughter.
CYP 3.3.3 CBSE Textbook Question
A and B are brothers. M is father of A and brother of S. F is sister of B and K is mother of S. What is the relationship between B and K?
1. M and S are children of K (mother of S).
2. A, B, and F are children of M.
3. Therefore, K is the grandmother of B, and B is the grandson of K.
Answer: (b) Grandson and Grandmother.

Question 1 1 Mark

Pointing to a photograph, a woman says, 'He is the son of the only son of my grandfather.' How is the man in the photograph related to the woman?

(a) Brother
(b) Cousin
(c) Uncle
(d) Father
Correct Answer: (A)
The only son of grandfather is her father. The son of her father is her brother.
Question 2 1 Mark

A's mother is sister of B and daughter of C. D is the daughter of B and sister of E. How is C related to E?

(a) Grandmother
(b) Grandfather
(c) Maternal Grandfather/Grandmother
(d) Mother
Correct Answer: (C)
C is the parent of B, who is parent of E. So C is maternal grandparent of E.
Question 3 1 Mark

If P + Q means P is husband of Q, P / Q means P is sister of Q, and P * Q means P is son of Q, what does A * B + C mean?

(a) C is mother of A
(b) A is nephew of C
(c) C is daughter of A
(d) B is mother of A
Correct Answer: (A)
A * B: A is son of B. B + C: B is husband of C. Hence C is the mother of A.
