The time taken/ distance covered from the given data

Comprehensive study of time, speed, and distance calculations for CBSE Class 11 Applied Mathematics, covering uniform motion, unit conversions, and step-by-step problem solving.

Fundamental Relationship of Motion

In Applied Mathematics, the relationship between distance (d), speed (v), and time (t) forms the bedrock of kinematics problems. Assuming constant speed, the distance traveled is directly proportional to the time taken. It is essential to ensure unit consistency (e.g., converting km/h to m/s) before performing calculations.

d = v × t; v = d/t; t = d/v
Example 1: A car travels at a constant speed of 72 km/h. How much distance will it cover in 20 minutes?
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Step 1: Convert speed from km/h to km/min: 72 km/h = 72/60 km/min = 1.2 km/min.
• Step 2: Use the formula Distance = Speed × Time.
• Step 3: Multiply: 1.2 km/min × 20 min = 24 km.

Answer: The car covers a distance of 24 km.

Unit Conversion and Average Speed

Often, data provided in exam questions involve mixed units. To convert km/h to m/s, multiply by 5/18. Conversely, to convert m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. When calculating time for a journey with varying speeds, one must calculate total distance and total time separately before finding the average.

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time
Example 1: A train covers 150 meters in 10 seconds. Calculate its speed in km/h and the time taken to cover 5 km.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Step 1: Calculate speed in m/s: 150m / 10s = 15 m/s.
• Step 2: Convert to km/h: 15 × (18/5) = 54 km/h.
• Step 3: Calculate time for 5 km: Time = Distance / Speed = 5 km / 54 km/h = 5/54 hours.
• Step 4: Convert to minutes: (5/54) × 60 = 5.55 minutes.

Answer: The speed is 54 km/h, and the time taken to cover 5 km is approximately 5.55 minutes.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time = 100 / 2 = 50 km/h.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Distance = Speed × Time = 4 km/h × (30/60) h = 4 × 0.5 = 2 km.

Section C: Short Answer Questions

1

A cyclist travels at 12 km/h. How many seconds will it take to cover 100 meters?

Show Answer & Solution

Speed = 12 km/h = 12 * (1000m / 3600s) = 120/36 = 10/3 m/s. Time = Distance / Speed = 100 / (10/3) = 100 * 3 / 10 = 30 seconds.

2

An athlete completes a race of 400m in 50 seconds. Find the speed in km/h.

Show Answer & Solution

Speed = Distance / Time = 400m / 50s = 8 m/s. To convert m/s to km/h, multiply by 18/5. Speed = 8 * 18/5 = 144/5 = 28.8 km/h.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

A bus leaves city A for city B at 8:00 AM at 50 km/h. Another bus leaves city B for city A at 9:00 AM at 60 km/h. If the distance between A and B is 330 km, at what time will they meet?

Show Answer & Solution

1. Distance covered by first bus in 1 hour (from 8 AM to 9 AM) = 50 km. 2. Remaining distance at 9 AM = 330 - 50 = 280 km. 3. Relative speed = 50 + 60 = 110 km/h. 4. Time taken to meet = 280 / 110 = 28/11 hours = 2 hours and (6/11 * 60) minutes approx 32.7 mins. 5. Meeting time = 9:00 AM + 2 hours 33 mins = 11:33 AM.

2

Two trains start at the same time from two stations 400 km apart and move towards each other. The speed of one train is 10 km/h more than the other. If they meet after 4 hours, find the speed of each train.

Show Answer & Solution

1. Let the speed of the slower train be x km/h. 2. Speed of faster train = x + 10 km/h. 3. Relative speed = x + (x + 10) = 2x + 10. 4. Distance = Relative Speed × Time => 400 = (2x + 10) * 4. 5. 100 = 2x + 10 => 2x = 90 => x = 45. 6. Slower train = 45 km/h, Faster train = 55 km/h.

Section E: Case-Based Questions

1

Case Study: A delivery drone is programmed to transport medical supplies between two hospitals located 24 km apart. The drone flies at a constant speed of 36 km/h. Due to wind conditions, the effective speed changes.

Sub-question 1: How much time in minutes does the drone take to cover the distance under normal conditions?

Show Solution

Time = Distance / Speed = 24 / 36 hours = 2/3 hours = 40 minutes.

Sub-question 2: If a headwind reduces the speed by 6 km/h, what is the new time taken to cover the same distance?

Show Solution

New Speed = 36 - 6 = 30 km/h. New Time = 24 / 30 hours = 0.8 hours = 48 minutes.
