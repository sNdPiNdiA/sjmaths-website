Calculation of the time for which hands of clock meet

A comprehensive guide on calculating the time when the hour and minute hands of a clock coincide, covering relative angular velocity and the mathematical derivation for CBSE Class 11 Applied Mathematics.

Relative Angular Velocity of Clock Hands

To find when the hands of a clock meet, we analyze the angular speeds of both hands. The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes, which is 6 degrees per minute. The hour hand moves 360 degrees in 12 hours (720 minutes), which is 0.5 degrees per minute. The relative speed of the minute hand with respect to the hour hand is 6 - 0.5 = 5.5 degrees per minute (or 11/2 degrees per minute).

Relative Speed = 5.5 degrees/min = 11/2 degrees/min
Example 1: At what time between 3 o'clock and 4 o'clock do the hands of a clock coincide?
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• At 3 o'clock, the minute hand is at 12 and the hour hand is at 3, creating a gap of 15 minutes (or 90 degrees).
• To coincide, the minute hand must cover this 90-degree gap relative to the hour hand.
• Using the formula Time = Distance / Relative Speed: Time = 90 / (11/2).
• Calculating: Time = (90 * 2) / 11 = 180 / 11 minutes.
• Converting to mixed fraction: 16 and 4/11 minutes.

Answer: The hands will coincide at 16 and 4/11 minutes past 3.

General Formula for Coincidence

The hands of a clock coincide every (60 * 12 / 11) minutes, which is approximately 65 minutes and 5 seconds. To find the exact time for any hour 'H' between 1 and 11, we use the position of the hour hand as the starting reference point.

Minutes past H = (60/11) * H
Example 1: Find the time between 8 and 9 o'clock when the hands meet.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Identify H = 8.
• Apply the formula: Minutes = (60/11) * 8.
• Calculate: 480 / 11 minutes.
• Perform division: 480 / 11 = 43 and 7/11 minutes.

Answer: The hands will meet at 43 and 7/11 minutes past 8.

Section C: Short Answer Questions

1

Calculate the time between 2 and 3 o'clock when the hands are at right angles.

Show Answer & Solution

At 2 o'clock, the gap is 10 min spaces. For right angle, gap must be 15 or 25 min spaces. Case 1: 10 + x = 15 => x = 5 min. Time = 5 * 12/11 = 5 5/11 min past 2. Case 2: 10 + x = 25 => x = 15 min. Time = 15 * 12/11 = 16 4/11 min past 2.

2

Find the angle traced by the minute hand in 15 minutes.

Show Answer & Solution

The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes. Therefore, it moves 360/60 = 6 degrees per minute. In 15 minutes, angle = 15 * 6 = 90 degrees.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

Derive the formula for the time at which the hands of a clock coincide between H and (H+1) o'clock.

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: At H o'clock, the minute hand is at 12 and hour hand is at H. The distance between them is 5H minute spaces. Step 2: To coincide, the minute hand must gain 5H spaces. Step 3: Relative speed is 55/60 = 11/12 spaces/min. Step 4: Time T = Distance / Relative Speed = 5H / (11/12) = 60H/11 minutes past H.

2

Calculate the exact time between 8 and 9 o'clock when the hands of a clock are opposite to each other (180 degrees).

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: For the hands to be opposite, the difference between their positions must be 30 minute spaces. Step 2: At 8 o'clock, the minute hand is at 0 and the hour hand is at 40 (measured from 12). Step 3: To be opposite, the minute hand must cover the 40-minute gap plus 30 more to reach the 10 position, totaling 10 minutes past 12 position relative to the hour hand. Calculation: M = (60/11) * (H + 6) for H < 6, or (60/11) * (H-6) for H >= 6. Here H=8, M = (60/11) * (8-6) = 120/11 = 10 10/11. Time is 10 10/11 minutes past 8.

Section E: Case-Based Questions

1

Case Study: A clock is a circular dial with 60 minute marks. The minute hand moves 6 degrees per minute, and the hour hand moves 0.5 degrees per minute. The hands of a clock meet at regular intervals, known as the gain time period.

Sub-question 1: What is the relative speed of the minute hand with respect to the hour hand?

Show Solution

Relative speed = 6 degrees/min - 0.5 degrees/min = 5.5 degrees/min.

Sub-question 2: How many minutes does it take for the hands to meet again once they have coincided?

Show Solution

The hands coincide when the minute hand covers 360 degrees relative to the hour hand. Time = 360 / 5.5 = 720/11 = 65 5/11 minutes.
