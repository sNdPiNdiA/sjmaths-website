Understanding Angular Units: Degrees and Minutes

In the sexagesimal system, an angle is measured in degrees, minutes, and seconds. A complete rotation is divided into 360 degrees (°). To achieve higher precision, each degree is further subdivided into 60 equal parts called minutes ('). Therefore, one degree contains 60 minutes. The angular value of one minute is defined as 1/60th of a degree.

1° = 60' (minutes) => 1' = (1/60)°
Example 1: Express 1 minute in terms of degrees and subsequently in radians.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Step 1: Since 60' = 1°, then 1' = 1/60 degrees.
• Step 2: To convert degrees to radians, multiply by (π/180).
• Step 3: Calculate: (1/60) * (π/180) = π / 10800 radians.

Answer: 1 minute is equal to (1/60)° or π/10800 radians.

Example 2: Convert 15 minutes into degrees.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Step 1: Use the relation 1' = (1/60)°.
• Step 2: Multiply the given value by the conversion factor: 15 * (1/60).
• Step 3: Simplify the fraction: 15/60 = 1/4 = 0.25°.

Answer: 15 minutes is equal to 0.25°.

What is the value of 1 minute in radians?

1 radian = (180/pi) degrees approx 57.296 degrees. Since 1 degree = 60 minutes, 57.296 * 60 = 3437.75, which rounds to 3438 minutes.

Section C: Short Answer Questions

1

Convert 25 minutes into degrees.

Show Answer & Solution

Since 60 minutes = 1 degree, then 1 minute = 1/60 degrees. Therefore, 25 minutes = 25/60 degrees = 5/12 degrees, which is approx 0.4167 degrees.

Express 10 minutes in radian measure.

Show Answer & Solution

10 minutes = 10/60 degrees = 1/6 degrees. Converting to radians: (1/6) * (pi/180) = pi/1080 radians.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

Calculate the number of radians in 1 degree, 1 minute, and 1 second.

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: 1 degree = pi/180 rad = 0.01745 rad. Step 2: 1 minute = (1/60) degree = (1/60) * (pi/180) = pi/10800 rad approx 0.0002908 rad. Step 3: 1 second = (1/60) minute = (1/60) * (pi/10800) = pi/648000 rad approx 0.000004848 rad.

2

If an angle is given as 2 degrees and 30 minutes, convert this entire angle into radians.

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: Convert minutes to degrees: 30 minutes = 30/60 = 0.5 degrees. Step 2: Total degrees = 2 + 0.5 = 2.5 degrees. Step 3: Convert to radians: 2.5 * (pi/180) = (5/2) * (pi/180) = 5pi/360 = pi/72 radians.

Case Study: In surveying and astronomy, precise angular measurement is critical. Angles are often measured in degrees, minutes, and seconds, where 1 degree = 60 minutes and 1 minute = 60 seconds. Sub-question 1: If a telescope detects an object moving at a rate of 12 minutes per second, express this movement in degrees per second.

Show Solution

12 minutes per second = 12/60 degrees per second = 0.2 degrees per second.

Sub-question 2: Convert the value 0.2 degrees per second into radians per second.

Show Solution

0.2 * (pi/180) radians per second = 0.2pi/180 = pi/900 radians per second.
