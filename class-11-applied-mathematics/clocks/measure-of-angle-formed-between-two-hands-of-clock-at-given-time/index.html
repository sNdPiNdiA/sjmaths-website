Measure of angle formed between two hands of clock at given time

Comprehensive guide to calculating the angle between the hour and minute hands of a clock for CBSE Class 11 Applied Mathematics, covering angular speeds, relative movement, and step-by-step problem-solving techniques.

Understanding Angular Movement of Clock Hands

A clock is a circular dial of 360 degrees. The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes, which means its speed is 6 degrees per minute. The hour hand moves 360 degrees in 12 hours (720 minutes), which means its speed is 0.5 degrees per minute. The angle between the hands depends on the relative position of both hands at any given time (H:M).

Angle = |(30 * H) - (5.5 * M)|
Example 1: Calculate the angle between the hands of a clock at 4:20.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Identify the values: H = 4 and M = 20.
• Apply the formula: Angle = |(30 * 4) - (5.5 * 20)|.
• Calculate values: 30 * 4 = 120 and 5.5 * 20 = 110.
• Find the absolute difference: |120 - 110| = 10 degrees.

Answer: The angle between the hands at 4:20 is 10 degrees.

Example 2: Find the angle between the hour and minute hands at 8:15.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Identify the values: H = 8 and M = 15.
• Apply the formula: Angle = |(30 * 8) - (5.5 * 15)|.
• Calculate values: 30 * 8 = 240 and 5.5 * 15 = 82.5.
• Find the absolute difference: |240 - 82.5| = 157.5 degrees.

Answer: The angle between the hands at 8:15 is 157.5 degrees.

Reflex Angle Calculation

The formula |(30 * H) - (5.5 * M)| provides the smaller interior angle. If the question specifically asks for the reflex angle (the angle greater than 180 degrees), subtract the calculated angle from 360 degrees.

Reflex Angle = 360 - (Angle between hands)
Example 1: Find the reflex angle between the clock hands at 3:00.
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Calculate the interior angle: |(30 * 3) - (5.5 * 0)| = |90 - 0| = 90 degrees.
• Calculate the reflex angle by subtracting from 360 degrees.
• 360 - 90 = 270 degrees.

Answer: The reflex angle between the hands at 3:00 is 270 degrees.

The angle traced by the minute hand of a clock in 10 minutes is:

For the hands to be opposite, the angle must be 180 degrees. Using 180 = |30H - 5.5M| where H=4, 180 = |120 - 5.5M|. Solving 5.5M = 300, M = 300/5.5 = 600/11 = 54 6/11 minutes.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

Derive the general formula for the angle between hands of a clock at H hours and M minutes.

Show Answer & Solution

1. The hour hand moves 360 degrees in 12 hours, so 30 degrees per hour. In H hours and M minutes, it is at 30H + 0.5M degrees. 2. The minute hand moves 360 degrees in 60 minutes, so 6 degrees per minute. In M minutes, it is at 6M degrees. 3. The difference is |(30H + 0.5M) - 6M| = |30H - 5.5M|.

2

Find the times between 2:00 and 3:00 when the hands of the clock are at right angles (90 degrees).

Show Answer & Solution

Set |30(2) - 5.5M| = 90. Case 1: 60 - 5.5M = 90 (impossible as M > 0). Case 2: 5.5M - 60 = 90 => 5.5M = 150 => M = 150/5.5 = 300/11 = 27 3/11 minutes. Case 3: 60 - 5.5M = -90 => 5.5M = 150 (same). Actually 5.5M = 60 - 90 (negative) or 5.5M = 60 + 90. The two times are 2:27 3/11 min and earlier when 30H - 5.5M = -90, so 60 - 5.5M = -90 => 5.5M = 150, already solved. Wait, 5.5M = |60 ± 90|. 5.5M = 150 => 27 3/11. 5.5M = -30 (ignore). So 2:27 3/11.

Case Study: A mechanical wall clock displays time using an hour hand and a minute hand. The movement of these hands is governed by circular geometry where the dial represents a 360-degree circle divided into 12 hour-sectors of 30 degrees each.

Sub-question 1: What is the angle covered by the hour hand in 1 hour?

Show Solution

The hour hand covers 360 degrees in 12 hours, so 360/12 = 30 degrees in 1 hour.

Sub-question 2: If the time is 9:00, what is the angle between the hands?

Show Solution

At 9:00, the minute hand is at 12 and the hour hand is at 9. The difference is 3 hour-sectors. 3 * 30 = 90 degrees.
