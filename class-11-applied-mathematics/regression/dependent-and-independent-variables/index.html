Dependent and Independent Variables in Regression

Master Dependent and Independent variables in Regression for CBSE Class 11 Applied Mathematics. Learn with solved examples, MCQs, and practice problems.

Concept of Independent Variable

The independent variable, often denoted by x, is the predictor variable that is manipulated or controlled to observe its effect on another variable. In regression analysis, it serves as the input that explains the variation in the dependent variable. For example, in studying the relationship between study hours and exam scores, study hours act as the independent variable.

x = Independent Variable (Predictor)
Example: Solved Example: Identifying the Predictor
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Identify the variable that influences the outcome. In a study of fuel consumption (y) based on distance traveled (x), distance is the cause.
Step 2: Assign x to distance.
Answer: x = distance traveled.

Concept of Dependent Variable

The dependent variable, denoted by y, is the outcome variable whose value depends on the changes in the independent variable. It is the variable being predicted or explained by the regression model. In the linear regression equation y = a + bx, y represents the dependent variable.

y = a + bx
Example: Solved Example: Identifying the Outcome
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Identify the variable being measured as the result. In a study of salary (y) based on years of experience (x), salary is the outcome.
Step 2: Assign y to salary.
Answer: y = salary.

Regression Relationship

Regression analysis quantifies the relationship between the independent variable x and the dependent variable y. The slope coefficient b indicates the change in y for every unit change in x. This mathematical dependency allows for forecasting future values based on observed data.

y = a + bx
Example: Solved Example: Calculate predicted y
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Given y = 10 + 2x and x = 5.
Step 2: Substitute x = 5 into the equation: y = 10 + 2(5).
Step 3: Calculate: y = 10 + 10 = 20.
Answer: y = 20.

mediumGiven the regression equation y = 2x + 5, if the independent variable x increases by 3 units, by how much does y increase?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: C

Change in y = slope × change in x = 2 × 3 = 6.

4

mediumIf y = 15 + 0.8x, what is the value of the independent variable x when y = 23?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

23 = 15 + 0.8x => 8 = 0.8x => x = 10.

5

hardFor a regression model y = a + bx, if x = 10, y = 30 and x = 20, y = 50, find the slope b.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

b = (y2 - y1) / (x2 - x1) = (50 - 30) / (20 - 10) = 20 / 10 = 2.

y = 4 + 2.5(4) = 4 + 10 = 14.

Section C: Short Answer Questions

1

mediumGiven the regression equation y = 5 + 1.2x. Find: (i) Value of y when x = 0 (ii) Value of y when x = 10 (iii) Change in y when x increases from 10 to 20.

Show Answer & Solution

(i) y = 5 + 1.2(0) = 5.
(ii) y = 5 + 1.2(10) = 17.
(iii) Change = 1.2(20 - 10) = 12.

2

mediumGiven the regression equation y = 20 - 0.4x. Find: (i) Intercept (ii) Value of y when x = 5 (iii) Value of x when y = 16.

Show Answer & Solution

(i) Intercept = 20.
(ii) y = 20 - 0.4(5) = 18.
(iii) 16 = 20 - 0.4x => 0.4x = 4 => x = 10.

3

hardFor a model y = a + bx, given (x=2, y=10) and (x=4, y=14). Find: (i) Slope b (ii) Intercept a (iii) Predicted y when x = 6.

Show Answer & Solution

(i) b = (14-10)/(4-2) = 2.
(ii) 10 = a + 2(2) => a = 6.
(iii) y = 6 + 2(6) = 18.

hardA company models its cost y (in thousands) based on units produced x (in hundreds). The equation is y = 10 + 2.5x. (i) Identify independent and dependent variables. Show Answer & Solution

Section E: Case-Based Question

Case Study: A taxi service charges a fixed base fare and a variable rate per kilometer. The total cost y (in ₹) for a trip of x kilometers is given by y = 50 + 15x.

Sub-question 1: (i) Identify the independent variable and the fixed cost. (ii) Calculate the total cost for a 10 km trip.

Show Solution

(i) Independent variable is x (distance). Fixed cost is the intercept, which is ₹50.
(ii) y = 50 + 15(10) = 50 + 150 = ₹200.

Sub-question 2: (i) If a customer paid ₹350, how many kilometers did they travel? (ii) If the rate per km increases by ₹5, what is the new equation?

Show Solution

(i) 350 = 50 + 15x => 300 = 15x => x = 20 km.
(ii) New slope = 15 + 5 = 20. New equation: y = 50 + 20x.
