Locating the Position of a Person in a Seating Arrangement

A high-frequency topic in CLAT, MAT, and UPSC CSAT. Learn to find exact positions using left/right counting, rank-based formulas, interchange logic, and circular position tracking.

Core Formulas — Linear Arrangement

These formulas are directly tested in CLAT and MAT. Memorise them and know when to apply each.

Formula 1 — Convert Left to Right Position
Position from Right = (Total − Position from Left) + 1
Example: In a row of 10, if Raj is 3rd from left → Position from right = (10−3)+1 = 8th
Formula 2 — Total Count from Both Ends
Total = (Position from Left) + (Position from Right) − 1
Example: A person is 6th from left and 4th from right → Total = 6+4−1 = 9 people
Formula 3 — People Between Two Persons
People between X and Y = |Position of X − Position of Y| − 1
Example: X at 4th, Y at 9th → Between them = |4−9|−1 = 5−1 = 4 people
Formula 4 — After Interchange
If A (at pos L) and B interchange, and A's new position = New_L
Then Total = New_L + (B's original position from right) − 1
Used when someone shifts position after a swap
Formula 5 — Position After Moving
New Position from Left = Old Position ± Steps moved (+ if right, − if left)
New Position from Right = Total − New Position from Left + 1
Example: A is 5th from left, moves 3 places right → New position from left = 5+3 = 8th

Common Question Types & Tricks

Type 1 — Direct Position
"A is 5th from left in a row of 12. What is A's position from the right?"
Apply Formula 1: (12−5)+1 = 8th
Type 2 — Find Total
"B is 7th from left and 5th from right. How many people are in the row?"
Formula 2: 7+5−1 = 11
Type 3 — Interchange Trick
"A and B swap. A's new position from left is known; B's old position from right is known. Find total."
Formula 4: Total = New_A_left + B_right − 1
Type 4 — Gap / Between
"How many people sit between X and Y?"
Formula 3: |PosX − PosY| − 1
Type 5 — Circular Position
"Who is 3rd to the right of A in a circle of 6?"
Move 3 seats clockwise. Use modular counting if needed.
Type 6 — Rank Puzzle
"A's rank from top is 8, from bottom is 5. How many students?"
Same as Formula 2: 8+5−1 = 12
⚠️ Common Mistake — Overlap: "A is 5th from left and B is 5th from right in a row of 8." Do A and B overlap? Position of A from right = (8−5)+1 = 4. Position of B from left = (8−5)+1 = 4. A is at left-position 5 and B is at left-position 4. They do NOT overlap — there is 1 gap between them.

Locating Position in Circular Arrangements

In circular seating, "position" is always described relative to another person — not from an absolute end. The key questions are:

  • Who is Kth to the left/right of X? — Count K seats in that direction
  • Who is opposite X? — For n people, opposite = n/2 seats away (only valid for even n)
  • How many people between X and Y? — Count the shorter arc (clockwise or anti-clockwise)
Circular Counting Rule
For n people in a circle, numbered 1 to n clockwise:
K seats to the right of position P = Position (P + K − 1) mod n + 1
K seats to the left of position P = Position (P − K − 1 + n) mod n + 1
Example: 8 people sit in a circle. A is at position 1. Who is 5th to the right of A?
Show Solution

5th to the right of position 1 = position 1 + 5 = position 6.
Whoever is assigned position 6 in the clockwise arrangement is the answer.
Shortcut: Move 5 seats clockwise from A → count A, B, C, D, E, F — the 5th person after A (not counting A) is at position 6.

Example (CLAT Style): In a group of 6 people sitting in a circle (facing centre), P is 3rd to the left of Q. R is 2nd to the right of P. What is the position of R relative to Q?
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Fix Q at position 1 (12 o'clock).
Step 2: P is 3rd to the left of Q → moving anti-clockwise 3 from position 1. Anti-clockwise from 1: 6, 5, 4. So P is at position 4.
Step 3: R is 2nd to the right of P → clockwise 2 from position 4: positions 5, 6. R is at position 6.
Step 4: Q is at position 1, R is at position 6. From Q going anti-clockwise: 1→6 is 1 step. So R is immediately to the left of Q.

Facing Direction — A CLAT Favourite

Questions often specify which direction people face. This completely changes what "left" and "right" mean:

Facing North (row)
  • Left = West direction
  • Right = East direction
  • "Immediate left" = 1 seat to west
Facing South (row)
  • Left = East direction
  • Right = West direction
  • Everything is reversed vs. facing north
Circular — Facing Centre
  • Right = clockwise
  • Left = anti-clockwise
  • Most common in CLAT
Circular — Facing Outward
  • Right = anti-clockwise
  • Left = clockwise
  • Opposite of facing centre!
💡 CLAT Shortcut: When two rows face each other (as in Panel/Interview puzzles), your left and the opposite person's left are mirror images. Use a diagram — draw both rows and connect facing pairs.

EasyCLAT

In a row of 20 people, Priya is 9th from the left. She moves 4 places to the right. What is her new position from the right?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

New position from left = 9 + 4 = 13. New position from right = (20 − 13) + 1 = 8th. Wait: 20−13+1 = 8. Answer: C (8th). Verify: 13th from left in 20 → from right = 20−13+1 = 8. ✔ Correct: C.

4
EasyMAT

Amit is 11th from the left in a row. Sumit is 5th from the right in the same row. If there are 3 people between Amit and Sumit, how many people are in the row?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: C

Amit is at position 11 from left. There are 3 people between Amit and Sumit → Sumit is at position 11+3+1 = 15 from left (assuming Sumit is to the right of Amit). Sumit is 5th from right → Total = 15+5−1 = 19. Hmm, let's check: 19th from left = Sumit, 5th from right → 19+5−1=23≠19. Correct approach: Sumit from left = Total−5+1. Amit at 11, between = 3, Sumit at 15. 15+5−1 = 19. Total = 19. Verify: 19−15+1=5 ✔. Answer: B (19). Note: if Sumit is left of Amit, Sumit is at 11−3−1=7, Total=7+5−1=11 (too small). So Total = 19.

🟡 Level 2 — Intermediate (Interchange & Multi-Step)

5
MediumCLAT

In a row, Kavya is 15th from the left and Riya is 10th from the right. They interchange their positions. Kavya now becomes 20th from the left. How many students are there in the row?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

After interchange, Kavya is at Riya's old position (20th from left). Kavya's new position from left = 20 = Riya's old position from left. Riya was 10th from right, so Riya's position from left = Total−10+1. Total−10+1 = 20 → Total = 29. Verify: Total = 29. Riya from left = 20th. 20th from right = 29−20+1 = 10th ✔. Answer: 29.

6
MediumMAT

In a row of 40 students, Arjun is 13th from the left. Deepa is 3 places to the left of Arjun. Sneha is exactly between Deepa and the right end. What is Sneha's position from the left?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

Arjun at position 13. Deepa is 3 places left of Arjun → Deepa at 13−3 = 10. Right end = position 40. Sneha is exactly between Deepa(10) and right end(40): midpoint = (10+40)/2 = 25. Sneha is at position 25th from left.

7
MediumCLAT Style

Ten people sit in a circle (facing centre). A is at position 1 (north). Going clockwise: B is 2nd, C is 3rd… up to J at 10th. If E moves 4 places clockwise, what is E's new position relative to B?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: C

E is originally at position 5. Moving 4 clockwise: new position = 5+4 = 9. B is at position 2. From B(2) to E's new position(9): clockwise distance = 9−2 = 7, or anti-clockwise = 10−7 = 3. Going clockwise from B: 3rd position after B = position 5, not 9. Going anti-clockwise from B by 3: 2,1,10,9 → 9 is 3 anti-clockwise from B = 3 places to the LEFT of B. Answer: 3 places to the left of B.

8
MediumMAT

Directions (Q8–Q10): In a row of 50 students, Ram is 20th from the left. Shyam is 15th from the right. Geeta is exactly between Ram and Shyam. Mohan is 5 places to the right of Geeta.

Q8: What is Geeta's position from the left?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

Ram at position 20 (from left). Shyam is 15th from right → from left = 50−15+1 = 36. Geeta is exactly between 20 and 36: (20+36)/2 = 28. Geeta is at position 28th from left. (Correction: midpoint of 20 and 36 = 28, not 23. Option D = 28 would be correct. Let's re-examine: if Shyam is at left position 36 and Ram at 20, midpoint = (20+36)/2 = 28. Answer: 28th from left.)

MediumMAT

(Same puzzle as Q8) Q9: What is Mohan's position from the right?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

Geeta is at position 28 (from Q8). Mohan is 5 places right of Geeta → Mohan at 28+5 = 33. Position from right = 50−33+1 = 18th from right. Answer: B.

🔴 Level 3 — Advanced (CLAT Hard / Paragraph-Based)

10
HardCLAT 2023 Style

Directions (Q10–Q12): Twelve lawyers are seated in two rows of 6 each. Row 1 (north side, facing south): A, B, C, D, E, F. Row 2 (south side, facing north): P, Q, R, S, T, U. Each person faces one person directly in the opposite row. B faces Q. D is to the immediate right of B. R is to the immediate left of Q. A is at the extreme left of Row 1. T faces C. U is at the extreme right of Row 2. S is between R and T. E faces S.

Q10: Who is at the extreme right of Row 1?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Row 1 sits facing south (left-right as seen from the north = their actual left-right).
A is at extreme left of Row 1 (position 1). B faces Q — find B's position. D is immediately right of B. Since A=1, B could be at 2 → D at 3.
Row 2 faces north. Mirror: Row 2's left from their own perspective = right when viewed from Row 1. Q faces B(Row1, pos 2). R is to the left of Q (from Row 2's perspective). S is between R and T. T faces C. U is at extreme right of Row 2.
Working through: Row 1 = A–B–D–C–E–F. Row 2 (viewed from Row 1) = P–Q–R–S–T–U. Facing pairs: A↔P, B↔Q ✔, D↔R, C↔S? But T faces C → T↔C. Contradiction. Re-arrange: Row 1 = A–B–D–?–C–F or A–B–D–E–C–F with T facing C at position 5, U at Row 2 extreme right. E faces S. Final Row 1: A–B–D–E–C–F. F is at extreme right.

11
HardCLAT Style

(Same puzzle as Q10) Q11: Who sits 3rd to the right of the person who faces R?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

From the arrangement: Row 1 = A(1)–B(2)–D(3)–E(4)–C(5)–F(6). R faces D (position 3 in Row 1). 3rd to the right of D = position 3+3 = position 6 = C. Wait: 3+3=6 = F. Let's recheck: 3rd right of position 3 → positions 4,5,6 → 3rd is position 6 = F? Or counting: 1st right of D = E(4), 2nd = C(5), 3rd = F(6). So 3rd to the right of D = F. Answer: C.

12
HardMAT Hard

In a circular arrangement of 12 people, Anil is seated at position 7 (numbered 1–12 clockwise). Bina is 4 positions anti-clockwise from Anil. Chetan is exactly between Bina and Anil going clockwise (the longer arc). What position number is Chetan at?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Anil at position 7. Bina is 4 anti-clockwise from Anil: 7−4 = position 3. Going clockwise from Bina(3) to Anil(7): shorter arc passes through 4,5,6 — that's 3 steps (not the longer arc). Longer arc from Bina(3) to Anil(7) going clockwise = the 12−4=8-step path: 3→4→5→6→7 (shorter) or 3→2→1→12→11→10→9→8→7 (longer, 8 steps). Midpoint of 8-step longer arc (8/2=4 steps from each end): 3 + 4 anti-clockwise = 3−4+12 = 11. Chetan is at position 11.

Section B: Assertion–Reason Questions

1

Assertion (A): If a person is Xth from the left and Yth from the right in a row, the total number of people is X + Y − 1.
Reason (R): The person is counted once from the left and once from the right, so the sum X + Y counts that person twice, requiring subtraction of 1.

Choose the correct option:
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(C) A is true but R is false.
(D) A is false but R is true.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

Both the assertion and the reasoning are correct. The total = X + Y − 1 because the person themselves is counted in both X and Y, so we subtract 1 to avoid double-counting.

2

Assertion (A): In a row, if person A is 10th from the left and person B is 10th from the right, they necessarily occupy the same seat if there are 19 people in the row.
Reason (R): When two people are at position X from opposite ends in a row of (2X−1) people, they must be at the same central position.

Choose the correct option:
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(C) A is true but R is false.
(D) A is false but R is true.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

A is 10th from left = position 10. B is 10th from right in a 19-person row = position 19−10+1 = 10. Both are at position 10. They are the same seat (same person, or if different people, the problem would be impossible). R correctly explains this using the formula for the center position in a row of 2X−1 people.
