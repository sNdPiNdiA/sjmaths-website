Arithmetic Progression in Finance

Arithmetic Progressions (AP) are used to model scenarios with constant periodic changes, such as simple interest or fixed annual salary increments. If a value changes by a fixed amount d each period, the value at time n is given by the formula for the nth term. This linear growth is fundamental for calculating total savings over time.

aₙ = a + (n − 1)d
Example: Solved Example: Salary Increment
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Identify a = 20000, d = 1500, n = 5.
Step 2: Apply a₅ = 20000 + (5 − 1)1500.
Step 3: Calculate 20000 + 6000 = 26000.
Answer: The salary in the 5th year is ₹26000.

Geometric Progression in Growth Models

Geometric Progressions (GP) model exponential growth or decay, such as compound interest, population growth, or depreciation of assets. When a quantity increases by a fixed percentage rate r, the value follows a GP where the common ratio is (1 + r/100). These models are essential for long-term financial planning.

aₙ = a·rⁿ⁻¹
Example: Solved Example: Population Growth
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Given a = 1000, r = 1.1 (10% growth), n = 3.
Step 2: Apply a₃ = 1000·(1.1)³⁻¹.
Step 3: Calculate 1000·1.21 = 1210.
Answer: The population after 2 years is 1210.

Summation in Financial Applications

Summation formulas allow us to calculate the total accumulation of funds over a specific duration. For AP, we use the sum of n terms to find total deposits, while for GP, we use the sum formula to find the future value of an annuity or total investment growth. These tools are vital for evaluating financial instruments.

Sₙ = a(rⁿ − 1)/(r − 1)
Example: Solved Example: Total Investment
Show Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1: Given a = 1000, r = 2, n = 4.
Step 2: Apply S₄ = 1000(2⁴ − 1)/(2 − 1).
Step 3: Calculate 1000(15)/1 = 15000.
Answer: The total sum is 15000.

easyA person saves ₹500 in the first month and increases savings by ₹50 every month. What is the saving in the 10th month?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: C

a = 500, d = 50, n = 10. a₁₀ = 500 + 9(50) = 500 + 450 = 950.

2

easyA machine depreciates at 10% per year. If its initial value is ₹10000, what is its value after 1 year?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Value = 10000 - (10% of 10000) = 10000 - 1000 = 9000.

3

mediumA company produces 1000 units in year 1 and production increases by 20% annually. What is the production in year 3?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

a = 1000, r = 1.2. a₃ = 1000(1.2)² = 1000(1.44) = 1440.

4

mediumThe sum of the first 3 terms of an AP is 15 and the common difference is 2. Find the first term.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

a + (a+2) + (a+4) = 15 => 3a + 6 = 15 => 3a = 9 => a = 3.

5

hardA ball is dropped from 100m. Each time it hits the ground, it rebounds to 1/2 of its previous height. What is the height after the 4th bounce?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

a = 100, r = 0.5. Height after 4th bounce = 100(0.5)⁴ = 100/16 = 6.25.

6

hardIf the 3rd term of a GP is 12 and the 5th term is 48, find the 1st term (assuming r > 0).

Show Answer

✔ Correct: C

ar² = 12, ar⁴ = 48. r² = 48/12 = 4 => r = 2. a(2)² = 12 => 4a = 12 => a = 3.

7

very hardA person invests ₹1000 at the start of each year at 10% compound interest. What is the total value after 3 years?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

1000(1.1)³ + 1000(1.1)² + 1000(1.1) = 1331 + 1210 + 1100 = 3641.

Reason (R): The sum of an AP is given by (n/2)(2a + (n-1)d).

mediumGiven an AP with a = 10 and d = 5, find: (i) The 10th term (ii) The sum of first 10 terms (iii) The 20th term.

mediumGiven a GP with a = 2 and r = 3, find: (i) The 4th term (ii) The sum of first 4 terms (iii) The 5th term.

Show Answer & Solution

(i) a₄ = 2(3)³ = 54.
(ii) S₄ = 2(3⁴ - 1)/(3 - 1) = 2(80)/2 = 80.
(iii) a₅ = 2(3)⁴ = 162.

3

hardA company's profit follows an AP where a = 5000 and d = 1000. Find: (i) Profit in year 6 (ii) Total profit over 6 years (iii) Year in which profit reaches 15000.

Show Answer & Solution

(i) a₆ = 5000 + 5(1000) = 10000.
(ii) S₆ = (6/2)(10000 + 5000) = 3(15000) = 45000.
(iii) 15000 = 5000 + (n-1)1000 => 10000 = (n-1)1000 => n = 11.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

hardA person starts a business with an initial investment of ₹50000. The business grows at a rate of 10% per annum. Calculate the total value of the business after 5 years.

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: Identify a = 50000, r = 1.1, n = 5.
Step 2: The value after 5 years is the 6th term of the GP (a, ar, ar²... where a is year 0).
Step 3: Use formula a₆ = 50000(1.1)⁵.
Step 4: Calculate 1.1⁵ = 1.61051.
Step 5: 50000 * 1.61051 = 80525.5. The total value is ₹80525.5.

2

very hardAn employee is offered two salary plans for 10 years: Plan A: Starting salary ₹20000 with annual increment of ₹2000. Plan B: Starting salary ₹15000 with annual increment of 10%. Which plan is better in terms of total earnings?

Show Answer & Solution

Step 1: Plan A is AP: a=20000, d=2000, n=10. S₁₀ = 5(40000 + 9*2000) = 5(58000) = 290000.
Step 2: Plan B is GP: a=15000, r=1.1, n=10. S₁₀ = 15000(1.1¹⁰ - 1)/(1.1 - 1).
Step 3: 1.1¹⁰ ≈ 2.5937.
Step 4: S₁₀ = 15000(1.5937)/0.1 = 150000 * 1.5937 = 239055.
Step 5: Plan A total = 290000, Plan B total = 239055. Plan A is better.

Case Study: A retail store tracks its daily sales for a week. On Day 1, sales were ₹2000. Each subsequent day, sales increased by ₹500. Additionally, the store owner invests ₹1000 on Day 1, and this investment doubles every day.

Sub-question 1: (i) Calculate the sales on Day 7. (ii) Calculate the total investment by the end of Day 5.

Show Solution

(i) AP: a=2000, d=500. a₇ = 2000 + 6(500) = 5000. (ii) GP: a=1000, r=2. S₅ = 1000(2⁵ - 1)/(2 - 1) = 1000(31) = 31000.

Sub-question 2: If the sales growth becomes a GP with r=1.2 from Day 2 onwards, what would be the sales on Day 3?

Show Solution

Day 1 = 2000. Day 2 = 2000 * 1.2 = 2400. Day 3 = 2400 * 1.2 = 2880.
