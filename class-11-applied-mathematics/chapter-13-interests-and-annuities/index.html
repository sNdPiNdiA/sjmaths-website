Chapter 13 Core Formula Sheet

Quick-glance formula revision for examination and numerical problem-solving:

  • $$A = P\left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^{mt}, \quad A = P e^{rt} \text{ (Continuous)}$$
  • $$EAR = \left(1 + \frac{r}{m}\right)^m - 1, \quad r_{\text{effective}} = e^r - 1$$
  • $$\text{Ordinary Annuity: Cash flow at period END}$$
  • $$\text{Annuity Due: Cash flow at period BEGINNING}$$
  • $$\text{Perpetuity: Infinite stream of periodic payments}$$
  • $$FV = R \left[\frac{(1+i)^n - 1}{i}\right], \quad PV = R \left[\frac{1 - (1+i)^{-n}}{i}\right]$$
  • $$PV_{\text{due}} = PV_{\text{ordinary}} \times (1+i), \quad PV_{\text{perpetuity}} = \frac{R}{i}$$
  • $$EMI = \frac{P \times r \times (1+r)^n}{(1+r)^n - 1}$$
  • $$\text{Sinking Fund Payment: } R = \frac{A \times i}{(1+i)^n - 1}$$
