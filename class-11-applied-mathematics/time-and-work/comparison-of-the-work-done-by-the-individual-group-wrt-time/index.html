Comparison of the work done by the individual / group w.r.t. time

An in-depth guide on comparing work done by individuals or groups relative to time for CBSE Class 11 Applied Mathematics, covering unitary methods, work rates, and efficiency problems.

Fundamental Concept of Work and Time

Work done is directly proportional to the number of people working and the time taken. If a person takes 'n' days to complete a task, their work rate is 1/n of the task per day. When comparing two entities, the ratio of work done is inversely proportional to the ratio of time taken, assuming the effort remains constant.

Work = Rate × Time; If 1 unit of work is completed in 'n' days, then Work Rate = 1/n per day.
Example 1: A can finish a task in 10 days and B can finish the same task in 15 days. How long will they take if they work together?
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Calculate A's daily work rate: 1/10.
• Calculate B's daily work rate: 1/15.
• Sum the rates: 1/10 + 1/15 = (3+2)/30 = 5/30 = 1/6.
• The time taken is the reciprocal of the combined rate: 6 days.

Answer: They will finish the task together in 6 days.

The Unitary Method for Groups

When dealing with groups of people, we use the principle that the product of workers (M), days (D), and hours (H) per unit of work (W) is constant, provided the efficiency remains the same. This allows us to equate scenarios using the M1D1/W1 = M2D2/W2 formula.

M1 × D1 / W1 = M2 × D2 / W2
Example 1: If 12 men can complete a project in 20 days, how many men are required to complete the same project in 15 days?
Show Step-by-Step Solution

• Identify variables: M1=12, D1=20, D2=15.
• Set up the equation: 12 * 20 = M2 * 15.
• Solve for M2: M2 = (12 * 20) / 15.
• Calculate: M2 = 240 / 15 = 16.

Answer: 16 men are required to complete the project in 15 days.

Section A: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

1

If A can do a work in 10 days and B can do the same in 15 days, how much work do they complete together in one day?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

A's 1-day work = 1/10, B's 1-day work = 1/15. Together = 1/10 + 1/15 = (3+2)/30 = 5/30 = 1/6.

2

If 10 men can complete a task in 5 days, how many days will 25 men take to finish the same task?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

Using M1D1 = M2D2: 10 * 5 = 25 * D2 => 50 = 25 * D2 => D2 = 2 days.

3

The efficiency of A is twice that of B. If B can complete a work in 20 days, in how many days can A complete the same work?

Show Answer

✔ Correct: B

If B takes 20 days, A (being twice as efficient) will take 20/2 = 10 days.

Section B: Assertion-Reason Questions

1

Assertion (A): If A takes 4 days to do a job and B takes 6 days, together they take 2.4 days.
Reason (R): The formula for time taken by two people working together is (A*B)/(A+B).

Directions: Choose the correct option:
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(C) A is true but R is false.
(D) A is false but R is true.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: A

Both Assertion and Reason are true. (4*6)/(4+6) = 24/10 = 2.4 days.

2

Assertion (A): If the number of workers is increased, the time taken to complete the work increases.
Reason (R): Number of workers and time taken are inversely proportional.

Directions: Choose the correct option:
(A) Both A and R are true and R is the correct explanation of A.
(B) Both A and R are true but R is not the correct explanation of A.
(C) A is true but R is false.
(D) A is false but R is true.

Show Answer

✔ Correct: D

Assertion is false because increasing workers decreases the time taken. Reason is true.

Section C: Short Answer Questions

1

A can finish a piece of work in 12 days and B can finish it in 18 days. If they work together, how long will it take?

Show Answer & Solution

A's rate = 1/12, B's rate = 1/18. Combined rate = (3+2)/36 = 5/36. Time taken = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

2

If 6 men or 8 women can complete a work in 12 days, how long will 3 men and 4 women take to complete it?

Show Answer & Solution

6 men = 8 women => 3 men = 4 women. Total work = 3 men + 4 women = 4 women + 4 women = 8 women. Since 8 women take 12 days, they take 12 days.

Section D: Long Answer Questions

1

A, B, and C can complete a task in 10, 12, and 15 days respectively. They start working together, but A leaves 2 days before the work is completed. Find the total time taken to finish the work.

Show Answer & Solution

Let the total time be 'x'. A worked for (x-2) days, while B and C worked for x days. Rate A=1/10, B=1/12, C=1/15. Equation: (x-2)/10 + x/12 + x/15 = 1. Multiply by 60: 6(x-2) + 5x + 4x = 60. 6x - 12 + 9x = 60. 15x = 72. x = 4.8 days.

2

Two workers P and Q are hired to complete a job. P can do it in 20 days and Q in 30 days. If they work on alternate days starting with P, how many days will the work take?

Show Answer & Solution

P's 1-day work = 1/20, Q's 1-day work = 1/30. Work in 2 days (pair) = 1/20 + 1/30 = 5/60 = 1/12. Since 1/12 of the work is done in 2 days, the whole work is done in 12 * 2 = 24 days.

Section E: Case-Based Questions

1

Case Study: A construction company employs a group of workers. It is observed that 12 workers can build a wall in 20 days. After 5 days, 3 workers leave the project.

Sub-question 1: How much work was completed in the first 5 days?

Show Solution

12 workers in 20 days is 240 man-days. In 5 days, 12 * 5 = 60 man-days of work were done. 60/240 = 1/4 of the work.

Sub-question 2: How many additional days will the remaining workers take to finish the rest of the wall?

Show Solution

Remaining work = 3/4 of 240 = 180 man-days. Remaining workers = 12 - 3 = 9. Time = 180 / 9 = 20 days.
