20.1 Chords & Arc Angle Theorems (जीवा एवं चाप प्रमेय)

Circle Master Theorems: Central Angle & Cyclic Quadrilateral
(Left) Angle at Center is twice the inscribed angle: $\mathbf{\angle AOB = 2\angle APB}$.
(Right) Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary: $\mathbf{\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ}$.
Theorem Name Mathematical Statement Core Exam Shortcut
Perpendicular to Chord The perpendicular from center $O$ to chord $AB$ bisects $AB$ at $M$. $\mathbf{r^2 = d^2 + \left(\frac{c}{2}\right)^2}$ (Right triangle $\triangle OMA$)
Angle at Center $\angle AOB = 2 \angle APB$ Center angle is double the angle at the remaining arc.
Angles in Same Segment $\angle APB = \angle AQB$ Angles subtended by the same chord in the same segment are equal.
Angle in a Semicircle If $AB$ is diameter, $\angle APB = 90^\circ$ Any triangle inscribed with a diameter as hypotenuse is right-angled.

20.2 Cyclic Quadrilateral Theorems (चक्रीय चतुर्भुज प्रमेय)

A cyclic quadrilateral (चक्रीय चतुर्भुज) is a quadrilateral whose all four vertices lie on the circumference of a circle.

Property Mathematical Expression Top-Rank Speed Deduction
Opposite Angles Sum $\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ$
$\angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ$		 Opposite angles are strictly supplementary.
Exterior Angle Property $\angle \text{Ext} = \angle \text{Interior Opposite}$ If side $AB$ is produced to $E$, then $\angle CBE = \angle D$.
Cyclic Parallelogram $ABCD$ is parallelogram & cyclic Must be a RECTANGLE ($\angle A = \angle C$ and $\angle A+\angle C=180^\circ \implies 90^\circ$).
Cyclic Rhombus $ABCD$ is rhombus & cyclic Must be a SQUARE.
Cyclic Trapezium $ABCD$ is trapezium & cyclic Must be an ISOSCELES TRAPEZIUM (non-parallel sides equal).

20.3 Tangent Theorems & Power of a Point (स्पर्श रेखाएँ)

Theorem Formula / Statement Exam Application
Tangent-Radius Perpendicularity $OP \perp PT \implies \angle OPT = 90^\circ$ Right triangle $\triangle OPT \implies PT = \sqrt{OP^2 - r^2}$.
Tangents from External Point $PA = PB$ $\angle APB + \angle AOB = 180^\circ$ (Supplementary kite).
Alternate Segment Theorem $\angle BAT = \angle BCA$ Angle between tangent and chord equals angle in alternate segment.
Tangent-Secant Theorem $\mathbf{PT^2 = PA \cdot PB}$ Length of tangent segment squared equals product of secant segments.
Intersecting Chords Theorem $\mathbf{PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD}$ Holds true whether chords intersect inside or outside the circle.

Type 1: Perpendicular from Center to Chord Archetype 1
Q1
A chord of length $16\text{ cm}$ is drawn in a circle of radius $10\text{ cm}$. What is the perpendicular distance of the chord from the center of the circle?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $6\text{ cm}$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord: $$\text{Half-chord } AM = \frac{16}{2} = 8\text{ cm}$$
  2. In right triangle $\triangle OMA$, by Pythagoras theorem: $$OM^2 + AM^2 = OA^2 \implies d^2 + 8^2 = 10^2 \implies d^2 + 64 = 100 \implies d^2 = 36 \implies d = 6\text{ cm}$$

Top-Rank Speed Hack: Recognize the classic $(6, 8, 10)$ Pythagorean triplet immediately!

Type 2: Angle Subtended at Center vs Circumference Archetype 2
Q2
In a circle with center $O$, an arc subtends an angle of $130^\circ$ at the center. What is the measure of the angle subtended by this arc at any point on the remaining part of the circle?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $65^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended at any point on the remaining part:

$$\angle AOB = 2\angle APB \implies 130^\circ = 2\angle APB \implies \angle APB = \frac{130^\circ}{2} = 65^\circ$$
Type 3: Angle in a Semicircle ($90^\circ$) Archetype 3
Q3
$AB$ is a diameter of a circle, and $C$ is any point on the circle. If $\angle CAB = 42^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle CBA$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $48^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Since $AB$ is diameter, the angle in a semicircle is a right angle: $\angle ACB = 90^\circ$.
  2. In $\triangle ABC$: $$\angle CAB + \angle CBA + \angle ACB = 180^\circ \implies 42^\circ + \angle CBA + 90^\circ = 180^\circ$$ $$\angle CBA = 90^\circ - 42^\circ = 48^\circ$$
Type 4: Cyclic Quadrilateral Opposite Angles Archetype 4
Q4
In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, if $\angle A = (2x + 4)^\circ$ and $\angle C = (3x - 14)^\circ$, find the measure of $\angle C$.
Correct Answer: Option (C) $100^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral are supplementary: $$\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ \implies (2x + 4) + (3x - 14) = 180$$ $$5x - 10 = 180 \implies 5x = 190 \implies x = 38$$
  2. Now calculate $\angle C = 3(38) - 14 = 114 - 14 = 100^\circ$.
  3. Check: $\angle A = 2(38) + 4 = 80^\circ$. $80^\circ + 100^\circ = 180^\circ$.

Trap Warning: Don't pick $x = 38^\circ$ (Option A)! The question asks for $\angle C$.

Type 5: Exterior Angle of a Cyclic Quadrilateral Archetype 5
Q5
In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, side $AB$ is extended to point $E$. If $\angle ADC = 78^\circ$, what is the measure of the exterior angle $\angle CBE$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $78^\circ$

Universal Cyclic Quadrilateral Theorem:

The exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral is directly equal to the interior opposite angle:

$$\angle CBE = \angle ADC = 78^\circ$$

Proof: $\angle ABC + \angle ADC = 180^\circ \implies \angle ABC = 180^\circ - 78^\circ = 102^\circ$. Since $ABE$ is a straight line, $\angle CBE = 180^\circ - 102^\circ = 78^\circ$. Solved in 2 seconds!

Type 6: Tangents from External Point & Central Angle Archetype 6
Q6
From an external point $P$, two tangents $PA$ and $PB$ are drawn to a circle with center $O$. If the angle between the tangents is $\angle APB = 50^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle AOB$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $130^\circ$

Top-Rank Speed Hack:

In quadrilateral $PAOB$, the angles at contact points $A$ and $B$ are both $90^\circ$. Therefore, the angle between tangents $\angle APB$ and central angle $\angle AOB$ are supplementary:

$$\angle AOB = 180^\circ - \angle APB = 180^\circ - 50^\circ = 130^\circ$$
Type 7: Alternate Segment Theorem Archetype 7
Q7
$PT$ is a tangent at point $A$ to a circle. $AB$ is a chord such that $\angle BAT = 64^\circ$. If $C$ is a point on the major arc of the circle, what is the measure of $\angle ACB$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $64^\circ$

Alternate Segment Theorem (एकांतर वृत्तखंड प्रमेय):

The angle formed between a tangent and a chord through the point of contact equals the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment:

$$\angle ACB = \angle BAT = 64^\circ$$
Type 8: Tangent-Secant Theorem ($PT^2 = PA \cdot PB$) Archetype 8
Q8
From an external point $P$, a tangent $PT$ of length $6\text{ cm}$ is drawn to a circle. A secant $PAB$ passes through the circle such that $PA = 4\text{ cm}$. What is the length of chord $AB$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $5\text{ cm}$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. By the Tangent-Secant Theorem: $$PT^2 = PA \cdot PB$$ $$6^2 = 4 \cdot PB \implies 36 = 4 \cdot PB \implies PB = 9\text{ cm}$$
  2. Chord $AB = PB - PA = 9 - 4 = 5\text{ cm}$.

Examiner Trap: Many candidates mark $PB = 9\text{ cm}$ (Option A)! The question asks for chord $AB$, which requires subtracting $PA$.

Type 9: Intersecting Chords Inside Circle Archetype 9
Q9
Two chords $AB$ and $CD$ intersect inside a circle at point $P$. If $AP = 6\text{ cm}$, $PB = 4\text{ cm}$, and $CP = 3\text{ cm}$, find the length of $PD$.
Correct Answer: Option (B) $8\text{ cm}$

Intersecting Chords Theorem:

$$PA \cdot PB = PC \cdot PD$$ $$6 \cdot 4 = 3 \cdot PD \implies 24 = 3 \cdot PD \implies PD = \frac{24}{3} = 8\text{ cm}$$
Type 10: Cyclic Parallelogram Identification Archetype 10
Q10
A parallelogram inscribed in a circle is ALWAYS a:
Correct Answer: Option (B) Rectangle

Proof:

  1. Let $ABCD$ be a cyclic parallelogram.
  2. In a parallelogram, opposite angles are equal: $\angle A = \angle C$.
  3. In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are supplementary: $\angle A + \angle C = 180^\circ$.
  4. Therefore: $\angle A + \angle A = 180^\circ \implies 2\angle A = 180^\circ \implies \angle A = 90^\circ$.
  5. A parallelogram having one angle equal to $90^\circ$ is by definition a Rectangle.
