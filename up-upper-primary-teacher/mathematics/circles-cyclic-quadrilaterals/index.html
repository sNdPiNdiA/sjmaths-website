1. Fundamental Axioms, Definitions & Foundational Properties

Definition of a Circle: The locus of all points in a plane that are equidistant from a fixed point called the center $O$. The fixed distance is the radius $r$.
Perpendicular from Center to Chord: The perpendicular drawn from the center of a circle to a chord bisects the chord ($ ext{If } OM perp AB$, then $AM = MB$). Conversely, the line joining the center to the midpoint of a chord is perpendicular to the chord.
Equal Chords and Distance: Equal chords of a circle are equidistant from the center ($AB = CD implies OM = ON$). Conversely, chords equidistant from the center are equal in length.
Angle Subtended by Arc at Center: The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle ($angle AOB = 2 angle APB$).
Exam Pro-Tip: Always draw a rough sketch including radii to endpoints of chords; this instantly reveals congruent right-angled triangles where Pythagoras theorem ($r^2 = ( rac{chord}{2})^2 + d^2$) can be applied.
Memory Mnemonic: CPA — Center Perpendicular Actively bisects chord.

Angles in the Same Segment: Angles in the same segment of a circle are equal ($angle ACB = angle ADB$). An angle in a semicircle is a right angle ($90^circ$).
Cyclic Quadrilateral Property: If all four vertices of a quadrilateral lie on the circumference of a circle, it is cyclic. The exterior angle is equal to the interior opposite angle.
Tangents from an External Point: Lengths of tangents drawn from an external point to a circle are equal ($PT_1 = PT_2$). The line joining the external point to the center bisects the angle between the tangents.
15-Second Speed-Math Trick: For a circle of radius $r$ with external point distance $d$ from center, tangent length $t = sqrt{d^2 - r^2}$. Memorize common Pythagorean triples like $(3, 4, 5)$, $(5, 12, 13)$, $(8, 15, 17)$ to bypass calculating squares.

3. Word Problem Modeling & Practical Applications

Chord and Distance Problems: Given radius $r = 5 ext{ cm}$ and chord length $8 ext{ cm}$, find distance from center. Model as right triangle with hypotenuse $5$, base $ rac{8}{2} = 4$, yielding perpendicular distance $d = sqrt{5^2 - 4^2} = 3 ext{ cm}$.
Cyclic Quadrilateral Angle Chasing: Given cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$ with $angle A = 70^circ$, find exterior angle at $C$. Since exterior angle equals interior opposite angle $angle A$, exterior angle $= 70^circ$.
Tangent Geometry Modeling: A point $P$ is $13 ext{ cm}$ away from the center of a circle of radius $5 ext{ cm}$. Find the length of the tangent drawn from $P$. Using $PT = sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = sqrt{169 - 25} = sqrt{144} = 12 ext{ cm}$.
Alternate Segment Theorem Application: If a tangent is drawn at point $P$ and a chord $AB$ passes through $P$, the angle between tangent and chord equals the angle subtended by the chord in the alternate segment ($angle TPB = angle APB$).
UP Exam High-Yield Tip: In multi-step geometry questions, annotate given values directly on the examination question paper diagram to avoid sign and position reversal errors.

4. Mathematical Edge Cases, Sign Conventions & Common Traps

Critical Distinction: A secant intersects a circle at two distinct points, whereas a tangent intersects the circle at exactly one point (the point of contact).
Edge Case / Exception: A tangent is never perpendicular to a chord unless the chord passes through the point of contact as a diameter.
Common Calculation Trap: Forgetting that opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to $180^circ$, not $360^circ$ (which is the sum of all four interior angles).
Trap Prevention Trick: When solving tangent problems, always verify that the triangle formed by center, point of contact, and external point is right-angled at the point of contact before applying Pythagoras theorem.
Memory Mnemonic: T-90 — Tangent makes 90 degrees with the radius.

Question 1 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

1. If $AB$ is a chord of a circle with center $O$ and radius $10\text{ cm}$, and the length of chord $AB$ is $16\text{ cm}$, what is the perpendicular distance of the chord from the center?

Correct Answer: Option A

The perpendicular from the center bisects the chord, so half the chord length is $\frac{16}{2} = 8\text{ cm}$. The radius forms the hypotenuse ($10\text{ cm}$) of a right-angled triangle. By Pythagoras theorem, distance $d = \sqrt{10^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{100 - 64} = \sqrt{36} = 6\text{ cm}$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 2 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

2. In a cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, if $\angle A = 65^\circ$ and $\angle B = 80^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle D$?

Correct Answer: Option A

In a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$. Since $\angle B$ and $\angle D$ are opposite angles, $\angle B + \angle D = 180^\circ \implies 80^\circ + \angle D = 180^\circ \implies \angle D = 100^\circ$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 3 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

3. From a point $P$, $10\text{ cm}$ away from the center of a circle, a tangent $PT$ of length $8\text{ cm}$ is drawn. What is the radius of the circle?

Correct Answer: Option A

The radius $r$, tangent $t$, and distance $d$ form a right-angled triangle with hypotenuse $d = 10\text{ cm}$ and base $t = 8\text{ cm}$. By Pythagoras theorem, $r = \sqrt{d^2 - t^2} = \sqrt{10^2 - 8^2} = \sqrt{100 - 64} = \sqrt{36} = 6\text{ cm}$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 4 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

4. If angle $\angle AOB = 110^\circ$ subtended by an arc $AB$ at the center $O$ of a circle, what is the angle $\angle ACB$ subtended by the same arc at any point $C$ on the remaining circumference?

Correct Answer: Option A

The angle subtended by an arc at the center is double the angle subtended by it at any point on the remaining part of the circle. Therefore, $\angle ACB = \frac{1}{2} \angle AOB = \frac{110^\circ}{2} = 55^\circ$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 5 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

5. Two concentric circles are of radii $5\text{ cm}$ and $3\text{ cm}$. What is the length of the chord of the larger circle which touches the smaller circle?

Correct Answer: Option A

The radius of the smaller circle ($3\text{ cm}$) acts as the perpendicular distance from the center to the chord of the larger circle. The radius of the larger circle ($5\text{ cm}$) is the hypotenuse. Half the chord length is $\sqrt{5^2 - 3^2} = \sqrt{25 - 9} = \sqrt{16} = 4\text{ cm}$. Total chord length = $2 \times 4 = 8\text{ cm}$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 6 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

6. If quadrilateral $PQRS$ is cyclic and its exterior angle at vertex $S$ measures $85^\circ$, what is the measure of the interior opposite angle $\angle Q$?

Correct Answer: Option A

By the exterior angle property of a cyclic quadrilateral, the exterior angle is equal to the interior opposite angle. Thus, interior opposite angle $\angle Q = 85^\circ$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 7 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

7. If tangents $PA$ and $PB$ from a point $P$ to a circle with center $O$ are inclined to each other at an angle of $70^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle POA$?

Correct Answer: Option A

The line joining the external point to the center bisects the angle between the tangents, so $\angle APO = \frac{70^\circ}{2} = 35^\circ$. In right triangle $\triangle PAO$, $\angle PAO = 90^\circ$. Thus, $\angle POA = 90^\circ - 35^\circ = 55^\circ$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 8 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

8. What is the maximum number of common tangents that can be drawn to two non-intersecting circles lying outside each other?

Correct Answer: Option A

Two non-intersecting external circles admit 4 common tangents: 2 direct common tangents and 2 transverse (internal) common tangents. Option 0 is correct.

Question 9 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

9. In a circle of radius $13\text{ cm}$, a chord is at a distance of $5\text{ cm}$ from the center. What is the length of the chord?

Correct Answer: Option A

Using the right-angled triangle formed by radius ($13$), distance ($5$), and half-chord ($x$): $x = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = 12\text{ cm}$. Total chord length = $2 \times 12 = 24\text{ cm}$. Option 0 is correct.

Question 10 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

10. If $\text{quadrilateral } ABCD$ is cyclic with $\angle A = 3x$ and $\angle C = x$, what is the value of $x$?

Correct Answer: Option A

Opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral sum to $180^\circ$. Therefore, $\angle A + \angle C = 3x + x = 4x = 180^\circ \implies x = \frac{180^\circ}{4} = 45^\circ$. Option 0 is correct.
