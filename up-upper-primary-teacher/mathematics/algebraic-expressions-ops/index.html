Algebraic Expressions & Polynomial Operations

1. Fundamental Axioms, Definitions & Foundational Properties

Algebraic Expression Definition: A finite combination of constants and variables connected by fundamental mathematical operations ($+, -, \times, \div$). Example: $3x^2 - 5xy + 7$.
Terms, Factors, and Coefficients: Terms are parts of an expression separated by $+$ or $-$ signs. Each term is a product of factors, and the numerical multiplier of a term's variable part is its coefficient. For $-4x^2y$, the coefficient is $-4$.
Polynomial Classification: Classified by the number of terms (Monomial, Binomial, Trinomial, Multinomial) and by degree ($0^{\text{th}}$ degree = constant, $1^{\text{st}}$ degree = linear, $2^{\text{nd}}$ degree = quadratic, $3^{\text{rd}}$ degree = cubic).
Degree of a Polynomial: The highest power of the variable in a single-variable polynomial. For a multivariable polynomial, it is the maximum sum of the powers of variables in any single term. Example: The degree of $5x^3y^2 - 2xy^4 + 7$ is $\max(3+2, 1+4, 0) = 5$.
Exam Pro-Tip: Always check whether an expression is a valid polynomial. Expressions with fractional powers of variables (e.g., $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$) or variables in denominators (e.g., $\frac{1}{x} = x^{-1}$) are NOT polynomials.
Memory Mnemonic: L-Q-C-B — Linear (1), Quadratic (2), Cubic (3), Biquadratic (4) degrees in ascending order.

2. Formulas, Standard Theorems & Algorithmic Procedures

Category / CaseFormula / Mathematical RuleTopper Calculation Shortcut
Addition / SubtractionCombine only like terms by adding or subtracting their numerical coefficients. $ax^n \pm bx^n = (a \pm b)x^n$.Group terms by degree vertically or use column alignment to avoid sign omission.
MultiplicationDistributive law: $(ax^m)(bx^n) = abx^{m+n}$. Every term of polynomial A multiplies every term of polynomial B.Use FOIL for binomials: $(a+b)(c+d) = ac + ad + bc + bd$.
DivisionPolynomial Long Division: $\frac{P(x)}{D(x)} = Q(x) + \frac{R(x)}{D(x)}$, where $\deg(R) < \deg(D)$.Use Synthetic Division or Remainder Theorem when dividing by $(x - c)$.
Remainder Theorem: If a polynomial $P(x)$ is divided by $(x - c)$, the remainder is equal to $P(c)$.
Factor Theorem: $(x - c)$ is a factor of polynomial $P(x)$ if and only if $P(c) = 0$.
Standard Algebraic Identities: $(a+b)^2 = a^2+2ab+b^2$, $(a-b)^2 = a^2-2ab+b^2$, $(a+b)(a-b) = a^2-b^2$, $(x+a)(x+b) = x^2+(a+b)x+ab$.
15-Second Speed-Math Trick: When multiplying $(x+a)(x+b)$, immediately write $x^2 + [\text{sum}]x + [\text{product}]$. For $(x+3)(x-5)$, it is $x^2 + (-2)x - 15 = x^2 - 2x - 15$.

3. Word Problem Modeling & Practical Applications

Age & Perimeter Modeling: Translating statements like 'The length of a rectangular park is $3$ meters more than twice its breadth' into algebraic equations $L = 2B + 3$ and solving perimeter $P = 2(L+B)$.
Financial & Cost Accounting: Modeling total cost functions $C(x) = F + vx$, where $F$ is fixed cost, $v$ is variable cost per unit, and $x$ is the number of units produced.
SCERT Textbook Problem Patterns: Combining algebraic expressions representing lengths of sides of geometric figures to find total perimeter or area expressions in terms of $x$.
Simplification of Nested Brackets: Order of operation precedence for brackets: Parentheses $( )$, Braces ${ }$, and Square Brackets $[ ]$. Always resolve from inside out: innermost to outermost.
UP Exam High-Yield Tip: In word problems asking for the 'excess of A over B', always compute $A - B$. Reversing this to $B - A$ is a favorite trap setter in Super TET.

4. Mathematical Edge Cases, Sign Conventions & Common Traps

Critical Distinction: Subtracting an expression means changing the sign of every term inside the parentheses. Example: Subtracting $(3x - 4)$ from $(5x + 2)$ yields $(5x + 2) - (3x - 4) = 5x + 2 - 3x + 4 = 2x + 6$.
Edge Case / Division by Zero: Division of a polynomial by zero is undefined. Furthermore, division by an expression containing a variable (e.g., $\frac{x^2-4}{x-2}$) requires the domain restriction $x \neq 2$.
Common Calculation Trap: Confusing $(a+b)^2$ with $a^2 + b^2$. The correct expansion includes the cross-product term $2ab$.
Trap Prevention Trick: Whenever a minus sign precedes a parenthesis, place a giant bracket and distribute the negative sign to every single term inside before simplifying.
Memory Mnemonic: L-I-K-E — Like terms Keep Identical Literal Exponents.

Question 1 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

1. What is the degree of the polynomial $5x^4y^2 - 3x^6 + 2xy^7 - 9$?

Correct Answer: Option B

The degree of a multivariable polynomial is the maximum sum of the powers of variables in any single term. Let us find the sum of powers for each term: Term 1 ($5x^4y^2$) has degree $4+2=6$. Term 2 ($-3x^6$) has degree $6$. Term 3 ($2xy^7$) has degree $1+7=8$. Term 4 ($-9$) has degree $0$. The maximum degree among all terms is $8$. Therefore, the correct option is $8$.

Question 2 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

2. If $P(x) = 2x^3 - 5x^2 + ax + 3$ is divided by $(x - 2)$ and leaves a remainder of $5$, what is the value of $a$?

Correct Answer: Option B

By the Remainder Theorem, when $P(x)$ is divided by $(x - 2)$, the remainder is $P(2)$. Given remainder = $5$, so $P(2) = 5$. Substitute $x = 2$ into $P(x)$: $2(2)^3 - 5(2)^2 + a(2) + 3 = 5 \implies 2(8) - 5(4) + 2a + 3 = 5 \implies 16 - 20 + 2a + 3 = 5 \implies -1 + 2a = 5 \implies 2a = 6 \implies a = 3$. Therefore, the correct option is $3$.

Question 4 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

4. What must be added to $x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 7$ to obtain $x^3 + 2x^2 - 4x + 1$?

Correct Answer: Option A

Let the required expression be $E$. Then $(x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 7) + E = x^3 + 2x^2 - 4x + 1$. Therefore, $E = (x^3 + 2x^2 - 4x + 1) - (x^3 - 3x^2 + 5x - 7) = (1 - 1)x^3 + (2 - (-3))x^2 + (-4 - 5)x + (1 - (-7)) = 0x^3 + 5x^2 - 9x + 8 = 5x^2 - 9x + 8$. Therefore, the correct option is $5x^2 - 9x + 8$.

Question 5 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

5. If $x + \frac{1}{x} = 5$, what is the value of $x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2}$?

Correct Answer: Option A

Square both sides of the given equation: $\left(x + \frac{1}{x}\right)^2 = 5^2 \implies x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2(x)\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) = 25 \implies x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} + 2 = 25 \implies x^2 + \frac{1}{x^2} = 25 - 2 = 23$. Therefore, the correct option is $23$.

Question 6 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

6. Find the product $(2x - 3)(3x^2 + 4x - 5)$

Correct Answer: Option A

Multiply each term of $(2x - 3)$ with $(3x^2 + 4x - 5)$: $2x(3x^2 + 4x - 5) - 3(3x^2 + 4x - 5) = 6x^3 + 8x^2 - 10x - 9x^2 - 12x + 15$. Group like terms: $6x^3 + (8x^2 - 9x^2) + (-10x - 12x) + 15 = 6x^3 - x^2 - 22x + 15$. Therefore, the correct option is $6x^3 - x^2 - 22x + 15$.

Question 7 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

7. Which of the following expressions is a polynomial in one variable $x$?

Correct Answer: Option C

A polynomial in one variable must have only non-negative integer exponents for the variable $x$. Option 1 has $\sqrt{x} = x^{1/2}$ (fractional exponent). Option 2 has $\frac{1}{x} = x^{-1}$ (negative exponent). Option 4 has two variables ($x$ and $y$). Option 3 ($x^4 - 3x^2 + 7$) has only variable $x$ with integer exponents $4, 2, 0$. Therefore, the correct option is $x^4 - 3x^2 + 7$.

Question 8 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

8. If $x - 2$ is a factor of $x^3 - 4x^2 + kx - 6$, what is the value of $k$?

Correct Answer: Option B

By the Factor Theorem, if $(x - 2)$ is a factor of $P(x)$, then $P(2) = 0$. Substitute $x = 2$: $(2)^3 - 4(2)^2 + k(2) - 6 = 0 \implies 8 - 4(4) + 2k - 6 = 0 \implies 8 - 16 + 2k - 6 = 0 \implies -14 + 2k = 0 \implies 2k = 14 \implies k = 7$. Therefore, the correct option is $7$.

Question 10 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

10. Simplify: $\frac{x^3 - 8}{x^2 + 2x + 4}$

Correct Answer: Option A

Use the sum/difference of cubes formula: $a^3 - b^3 = (a - b)(a^2 + ab + b^2)$. Here, $x^3 - 8 = x^3 - 2^3 = (x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)$. Dividing by $(x^2 + 2x + 4)$ gives: $\frac{(x - 2)(x^2 + 2x + 4)}{x^2 + 2x + 4} = x - 2$. Therefore, the correct option is $x - 2$.
