19.1 The Quadrilateral Family & Diagonal Properties

Quadrilateral Hierarchy & Property Inheritance Tree
A Square inherits ALL properties: equal sides, equal angles ($90^\circ$), equal diagonals, and perpendicular bisecting diagonals.

A closed four-sided polygon has an angle sum of $360^\circ$. The nature of its sides, angles, and diagonals defines its specific type:

Quadrilateral Sides & Angles Diagonal Behavior Key Formulas
Parallelogram (समांतर चतुर्भुज) Opposite sides parallel & equal;
Opposite angles equal		 Bisect each other (Not equal, not perpendicular) $\mathbf{d_1^2 + d_2^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2)}$
$\text{Area} = \text{base} \times \text{height}$
Rhombus (समचतुर्भुज) All 4 sides equal;
Opposite angles equal		 Bisect each other perpendicularly at $90^\circ$ (Not equal) $\mathbf{4a^2 = d_1^2 + d_2^2}$
$\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}d_1 d_2$
Rectangle (आयत) Opposite sides equal;
All 4 angles $= 90^\circ$		 Equal ($d_1 = d_2$) and bisect each other (Not perpendicular) $d = \sqrt{l^2 + b^2}$
$\text{Area} = l \times b$
Square (वर्ग) All sides equal;
All angles $= 90^\circ$		 Both EQUAL and PERPENDICULAR ($90^\circ$) $d = a\sqrt{2}$
$\text{Area} = a^2 = \frac{d^2}{2}$
Trapezium (समलंब) One pair of opposite sides parallel Diagonals divide each other proportionally $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h$
Kite (पतंग) Two pairs of equal adjacent sides Intersect perpendicularly ($90^\circ$); longer bisects shorter $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}d_1 d_2$
The #1 Exam Master Formula: Rhombus Side & Diagonals
$$4a^2 = d_1^2 + d_2^2 \iff a = \sqrt{\left(\frac{d_1}{2}\right)^2 + \left(\frac{d_2}{2}\right)^2}$$

In every rhombus, half-diagonals and the side form a right-angled triangle. If $d_1 = 16$ and $d_2 = 12$, half-diagonals are $8$ and $6$. The side is immediately the hypotenuse: $\sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = 10\text{ cm}$. Perimeter $= 4 \times 10 = 40\text{ cm}$. Solved in 5 seconds!

19.2 Varignon's Midpoint Theorem (मध्य-बिंदु प्रमेय)

The figure formed by joining the consecutive midpoints of the sides of any quadrilateral has special geometric properties:

Original Quadrilateral Shape Formed by Joining Midpoints Geometric Reason
Any General Quadrilateral Parallelogram (Varignon's Theorem) Opposite segments are parallel to diagonals and equal to $\frac{1}{2}d$.
Rectangle Rhombus Original diagonals are equal $\implies$ Midpoint sides are all equal.
Rhombus Rectangle Original diagonals are perpendicular ($90^\circ$) $\implies$ Midpoint angles are $90^\circ$.
Square Square Original diagonals are both equal and perpendicular.
Isosceles Trapezium Rhombus Diagonals are equal.

19.3 Minimum Elements for Unique Geometric Construction

A frequent theoretical question in UP Teacher exams tests the minimum number of independent measurements needed to construct a polygon uniquely:

Polygon Type Minimum Independent Elements Needed Valid Combination Example
General Quadrilateral 5 elements 4 sides & 1 diagonal, OR 3 sides & 2 included angles
Parallelogram 3 elements 2 adjacent sides & 1 included angle
Rectangle 2 elements Length & breadth, OR 1 side & 1 diagonal
Rhombus 2 elements 1 side & 1 diagonal, OR both diagonals ($d_1, d_2$)
Square 1 element Only side length $a$, OR diagonal length $d$

10 Questions • 10 Archetypes
Type 1: Quadrilateral Angle Ratio & Angle Sum Archetype 1
Q1
The angles of a quadrilateral are in the ratio $3 : 5 : 9 : 13$. What is the measure of the smallest angle?
Correct Answer: Option (A) $36^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Sum of angles in a quadrilateral is $360^\circ$.
  2. Sum of ratio parts $= 3 + 5 + 9 + 13 = 30\text{ units}$.
  3. $30\text{ units} = 360^\circ \implies 1\text{ unit} = 12^\circ$.
  4. Smallest angle $= 3\text{ units} = 3 \times 12^\circ = 36^\circ$.
  5. Largest angle $= 13 \times 12^\circ = 156^\circ$.
Type 2: Parallelogram Consecutive & Opposite Angles Archetype 2
Q2
In a parallelogram $ABCD$, two adjacent angles are $(3x - 20)^\circ$ and $(x + 40)^\circ$. What is the measure of the smaller angle?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $80^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are supplementary (sum $= 180^\circ$): $$(3x - 20) + (x + 40) = 180 \implies 4x + 20 = 180 \implies 4x = 160 \implies x = 40^\circ$$
  2. First angle $= 3(40) - 20 = 120 - 20 = 100^\circ$.
  3. Second angle $= 40 + 40 = 80^\circ$.
  4. The smaller angle is $80^\circ$.

Trap Warning: Don't mark $x = 40^\circ$ (Option A)! The question asks for the angle measure, not variable $x$.

Type 3: Rhombus Diagonals & Perimeter Relation Archetype 3
Q3
The diagonals of a rhombus are $16\text{ cm}$ and $12\text{ cm}$. What is the perimeter of the rhombus?
Correct Answer: Option (C) $40\text{ cm}$

Top-Rank Speed Hack:

  1. Diagonals of a rhombus bisect each other at $90^\circ$.
  2. Half-diagonals are $\frac{16}{2} = 8\text{ cm}$ and $\frac{12}{2} = 6\text{ cm}$.
  3. Side $a = \sqrt{8^2 + 6^2} = \sqrt{64 + 36} = \sqrt{100} = 10\text{ cm}$.
  4. $\text{Perimeter} = 4a = 4 \times 10 = 40\text{ cm}$.
Type 4: Trapezium Parallel Sides & Area Formula Archetype 4
Q4
The area of a trapezium is $180\text{ cm}^2$ and its height is $12\text{ cm}$. If one of the parallel sides is longer than the other by $6\text{ cm}$, find the length of the longer parallel side.
Correct Answer: Option (B) $18\text{ cm}$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. $\text{Area} = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)h \implies 180 = \frac{1}{2}(a + b)(12) \implies 6(a + b) = 180 \implies a + b = 30$.
  2. Given difference $a - b = 6$.
  3. Add the equations: $2a = 36 \implies a = 18\text{ cm}$ (longer side).
  4. Shorter side $b = 30 - 18 = 12\text{ cm}$.
Type 5: Varignon's Midpoint Theorem Shape Identification Archetype 5
Q5
The quadrilateral formed by joining the midpoints of the consecutive sides of a RECTANGLE is always a:
Correct Answer: Option (B) Rhombus

Geometric Theorem:

The diagonals of a rectangle are equal in length ($d_1 = d_2$). By the Mid-Point Theorem, each side of the inscribed quadrilateral is equal to half of a diagonal ($\frac{1}{2}d$). Since both diagonals are equal, all four sides of the inscribed quadrilateral are equal. A parallelogram with four equal sides is a Rhombus.

Type 6: Rectangle Diagonal Intersection Angle Archetype 6
Q6
In a rectangle $ABCD$, the diagonals intersect at point $O$. If $\angle BOC = 68^\circ$, what is the measure of $\angle OAD$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $56^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. $\angle AOD$ and $\angle BOC$ are vertically opposite angles, so $\angle AOD = 68^\circ$.
  2. In a rectangle, diagonals are equal and bisect each other $\implies OA = OD$.
  3. Therefore, $\triangle AOD$ is an isosceles triangle with $\angle OAD = \angle ODA$.
  4. In $\triangle AOD$: $$\angle OAD + \angle ODA + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \implies 2\angle OAD + 68^\circ = 180^\circ$$ $$2\angle OAD = 112^\circ \implies \angle OAD = 56^\circ$$
Type 7: Parallelogram Diagonal Law ($d_1^2 + d_2^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2)$) Archetype 7
Q7
The sides of a parallelogram are $10\text{ cm}$ and $8\text{ cm}$. If the length of one diagonal is $12\text{ cm}$, what is the length of the other diagonal?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $\sqrt{184}\text{ cm}$ (or $2\sqrt{46}\text{ cm}$)

Parallelogram Diagonal Law:

$$d_1^2 + d_2^2 = 2(a^2 + b^2)$$ $$12^2 + d_2^2 = 2(10^2 + 8^2)$$ $$144 + d_2^2 = 2(100 + 64) = 2(164) = 328$$ $$d_2^2 = 328 - 144 = 184 \implies d_2 = \sqrt{184}\text{ cm}$$
Type 8: Minimum Conditions for Unique Construction Archetype 8
Q8
What is the minimum number of independent measurements required to construct a unique parallelogram?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $3$

Construction Principles:

  • A general quadrilateral requires $5$ independent measurements.
  • A parallelogram requires only $3$ measurements (e.g., two adjacent sides and the included angle, or two sides and one diagonal), because opposite sides and opposite angles are automatically equal.
  • A rectangle or rhombus requires $2$.
  • A square requires only $1$.
Type 9: Angle Bisectors of a Parallelogram Archetype 9
Q9
The internal bisectors of the four angles of a parallelogram enclose a:
Correct Answer: Option (B) Rectangle

Proof:

  1. In a parallelogram, adjacent angles are supplementary ($\angle A + \angle B = 180^\circ$).
  2. The bisectors create half-angles whose sum is $\frac{1}{2}(\angle A + \angle B) = 90^\circ$.
  3. Therefore, the triangle formed by the side and two adjacent bisectors has its third angle equal to $180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$.
  4. All four interior angles of the enclosed quadrilateral are $90^\circ$, making it a Rectangle.
Type 10: Diagonal Properties Identification Archetype 10
Q10
Which quadrilateral has diagonals that are BOTH equal in length and perpendicular to each other?
Correct Answer: Option (C) Square

Diagonal Comparison:

  • Rhombus: Diagonals are perpendicular ($90^\circ$), but NOT equal ($d_1 \ne d_2$).
  • Rectangle: Diagonals are equal ($d_1 = d_2$), but NOT perpendicular.
  • Square: Diagonals are BOTH equal and perpendicular!
