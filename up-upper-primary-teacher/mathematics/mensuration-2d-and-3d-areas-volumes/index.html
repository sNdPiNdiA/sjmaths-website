22.2 Three-Dimensional (3D) Solids: Surface Areas & Volumes

Hemisphere $\mathbf{2\pi r^2}$ $\mathbf{3\pi r^2}$ $\frac{2}{3}\pi r^3$ $\text{Flat base area} = \pi r^2$
The Universal Melting & Recasting Law:
$$\text{Number of small solids } N = \frac{\text{Volume of original solid}}{\text{Volume of each recast small solid}}$$

Example: A metallic sphere of radius $R = 6\text{ cm}$ is melted to form small lead balls of radius $r = 1\text{ cm}$.
$$N = \frac{\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3}{\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3} = \left(\frac{R}{r}\right)^3 = \left(\frac{6}{1}\right)^3 = 216\text{ balls}$$ Solved in 5 seconds flat without calculating $\pi$!

Type 1: Heron's Formula for Scalene Triangle Area Archetype 1
Q1
The sides of a triangular park are $13\text{ m}, 14\text{ m},$ and $15\text{ m}$. What is the area of the park?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $84\text{ m}^2$

Step-by-Step Heron's Calculation:

  1. Semi-perimeter: $s = \frac{13 + 14 + 15}{2} = \frac{42}{2} = 21\text{ m}$.
  2. $s - a = 21 - 13 = 8$, $s - b = 21 - 14 = 7$, $s - c = 21 - 15 = 6$.
  3. $\text{Area} = \sqrt{21 \times 8 \times 7 \times 6} = \sqrt{(7 \times 3) \times (8) \times 7 \times (2 \times 3)} = \sqrt{7^2 \times 3^2 \times 16} = 7 \times 3 \times 4 = 84\text{ m}^2$.

Top-Rank Memory Tip: The triangle $(13, 14, 15)$ is the most repeated standard triangle in Indian competitive examinations. Its area is always $84$!

Type 2: Equilateral Triangle Inradius to Circumradius Ratio Archetype 2
Q2
What is the ratio of the area of the incircle to the area of the circumcircle of an equilateral triangle?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $1 : 4$

Step-by-Step Derivation:

  1. In an equilateral triangle of side $a$: $$\text{Inradius } r = \frac{a}{2\sqrt{3}}, \qquad \text{Circumradius } R = \frac{a}{\sqrt{3}}$$
  2. The ratio of radii is $\frac{r}{R} = \frac{1}{2}$.
  3. The ratio of areas is $\frac{\pi r^2}{\pi R^2} = \left(\frac{r}{R}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 = 1 : 4$.
Type 3: Path Around a Rectangular Field Archetype 3
Q3
A rectangular garden is $50\text{ m}$ long and $40\text{ m}$ wide. A path $2.5\text{ m}$ wide is constructed all around the OUTSIDE of the garden. What is the area of the path?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $475\text{ m}^2$

Top-Rank Speed Hack Formula:

$$\text{Area of Outer Path} = 2w(l + b + 2w)$$

Here $l = 50, b = 40, w = 2.5 \implies 2w = 5$:

$$\text{Area} = 5(50 + 40 + 5) = 5(95) = 475\text{ m}^2$$

Standard method: Outer dimensions $= 55 \times 45 = 2475\text{ m}^2$. Inner garden $= 50 \times 40 = 2000\text{ m}^2$. Path $= 2475 - 2000 = 475\text{ m}^2$.

Type 4: Wheel Revolutions & Distance Traveled Archetype 4
Q4
The diameter of a circular wheel is $70\text{ cm}$. How many complete revolutions will it make to travel a distance of $1.1\text{ km}$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $500$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Diameter $d = 70\text{ cm} \implies$ Circumference $C = \pi d = \frac{22}{7} \times 70 = 220\text{ cm} = 2.2\text{ m}$.
  2. Total distance $= 1.1\text{ km} = 1.1 \times 1000 = 1100\text{ m}$.
  3. $\text{Number of revolutions } N = \frac{\text{Total Distance}}{\text{Circumference}} = \frac{1100}{2.2} = 500$.
Type 5: Longest Rod / Body Diagonal of a Room Archetype 5
Q5
What is the length of the longest rod that can be placed in a rectangular room of dimensions $12\text{ m} \times 9\text{ m} \times 8\text{ m}$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $17\text{ m}$

Body Diagonal Formula:

$$d = \sqrt{l^2 + b^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + 9^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{144 + 81 + 64} = \sqrt{289} = 17\text{ m}$$

Speed Hack: $12$ and $9$ give floor diagonal $\sqrt{12^2 + 9^2} = 15$. Then $(15, 8, 17)$ is a standard Pythagorean triplet!

Type 6: Melting & Recasting of Solids Archetype 6
Q6
A solid metallic cylinder of base radius $3\text{ cm}$ and height $5\text{ cm}$ is melted to form solid cones, each of radius $1\text{ cm}$ and height $1\text{ cm}$. How many such cones can be formed?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $135$

Step-by-Step Conservation of Volume:

  1. Volume of cylinder $= \pi R^2 H = \pi (3^2)(5) = 45\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
  2. Volume of each cone $= \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (1^2)(1) = \frac{\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.
  3. Number of cones: $$N = \frac{\text{Volume of Cylinder}}{\text{Volume of One Cone}} = \frac{45\pi}{\pi / 3} = 45 \times 3 = 135$$
Type 7: Right Circular Cone Surface Area Archetype 7
Q7
The radius of the base and the vertical height of a solid cone are in the ratio $3 : 4$. If its curved surface area is $240\pi\text{ cm}^2$, what is its volume?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $768\pi\text{ cm}^3$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Let $r = 3k$ and $h = 4k$. Slant height $l = \sqrt{(3k)^2 + (4k)^2} = 5k$.
  2. Curved surface area: $\text{CSA} = \pi r l = \pi (3k)(5k) = 15\pi k^2$.
  3. Given: $15\pi k^2 = 240\pi \implies 15k^2 = 240 \implies k^2 = 16 \implies k = 4$.
  4. Radius $r = 3(4) = 12\text{ cm}$, Height $h = 4(4) = 16\text{ cm}$.
  5. $\text{Volume} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (12^2)(16) = \frac{1}{3}\pi (144)(16) = 48 \times 16 \pi = 768\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
Type 8: Total vs Curved Surface Area of Hemisphere Archetype 8
Q8
What is the total surface area of a solid hemisphere of radius $7\text{ cm}$? (Use $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)
Correct Answer: Option (B) $462\text{ cm}^2$

Top-Rank Speed Hack:

For a solid hemisphere, total surface area includes the curved surface ($2\pi r^2$) PLUS the flat circular base ($\pi r^2$):

$$\text{TSA} = 3\pi r^2 = 3 \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7^2 = 3 \times 22 \times 7 = 462\text{ cm}^2$$

Common Trap: Calculating CSA $= 2\pi r^2 = 308\text{ cm}^2$ (Option A)! Never omit the base circle for a solid hemisphere.

Type 9: Percentage Change in Area / Volume Archetype 9
Q9
If the radius of a circle is increased by $20\%$, by what percentage does its area increase?
Correct Answer: Option (C) $44\%$

Successive Percentage Shortcut:

$$\text{Net Change} = 2x + \frac{x^2}{100} = 2(20) + \frac{20 \times 20}{100} = 40 + 4 = 44\%$$

Solved in 2 seconds in your head!

Type 10: Wire Bent from Circle to Square Archetype 10
Q10
A copper wire bent in the form of a square encloses an area of $121\text{ cm}^2$. If the same wire is rebent into the form of a circle, what is the area enclosed by the circle? (Use $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)
Correct Answer: Option (B) $154\text{ cm}^2$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Area of square $= a^2 = 121 \implies a = 11\text{ cm}$.
  2. Perimeter of square (wire length) $= 4a = 4 \times 11 = 44\text{ cm}$.
  3. Circumference of circle $= 2\pi r = 44 \implies 2 \times \frac{22}{7} \times r = 44 \implies r = 7\text{ cm}$.
  4. Area of circle $= \pi r^2 = \frac{22}{7} \times 7^2 = 154\text{ cm}^2$.

Top-Rank Speed Hack:

$$\text{Area of Circle} = \frac{14}{11} \times \text{Area of Square} = \frac{14}{11} \times 121 = 14 \times 11 = 154\text{ cm}^2$$
