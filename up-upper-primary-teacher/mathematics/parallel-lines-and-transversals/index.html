17.1 Angle Classifications & Angle Pairs (कोणों के प्रकार एवं युग्म)

An angle is formed when two rays originate from the same initial end point (vertex).

Angle Type Measure Interval Key Characteristic
Acute Angle (न्यून कोण) $0^\circ < \theta < 90^\circ$ Sharp corner
Right Angle (समकोण) $\theta = 90^\circ$ Perpendicular lines
Obtuse Angle (अधिक कोण) $90^\circ < \theta < 180^\circ$ Wider than right angle
Straight Angle (ऋजु कोण) $\theta = 180^\circ$ Forms a continuous straight line
Reflex Angle (प्रतिवर्ती कोण) $180^\circ < \theta < 360^\circ$ Outer angle of acute/obtuse corner
Complete Angle (सम्पूर्ण कोण) $\theta = 360^\circ$ One complete circular rotation

Essential Angle Pair Theorems

  • Complementary Angles (पूरक / कोटिपूरक कोण): Two angles whose sum is $90^\circ$. Complement of $x$ is $90^\circ - x$.
  • Supplementary Angles (संपूरक कोण): Two angles whose sum is $180^\circ$. Supplement of $x$ is $180^\circ - x$.
  • Linear Pair Axiom (रैखिक युग्म): If a ray stands on a straight line, the sum of two adjacent angles formed is $180^\circ$.
  • Vertically Opposite Angles (शीर्षाभिमुख कोण): When two straight lines intersect, the vertically opposite angles are always equal.
Angle Bisector Linear Pair Theorem:

The bisectors of a linear pair of angles are mutually perpendicular ($90^\circ$). If $\angle AOB + \angle BOC = 180^\circ$, and rays $OP, OQ$ bisect them, then $\angle POQ = \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ) = 90^\circ$.

17.2 Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal (समांतर रेखाएँ एवं तिर्यक रेखा)

Geometry Master Diagram: Parallel Lines & Transversal Angle Pairs
Alternate Interior: $\angle 3 = \angle 6$, $\angle 4 = \angle 5$ Corresponding: $\angle 1=\angle 5, \angle 2=\angle 6, \angle 3=\angle 7, \angle 4=\angle 8$ Co-Interior Sum: $\angle 4+\angle 6 = 180^\circ$, $\angle 3+\angle 5 = 180^\circ$

When two parallel lines $l \parallel m$ are intersected by a transversal line $t$, eight distinct angles are formed:

Angle Relationship Geometric Property Visual Recognition Guide
Corresponding Angles (संगत कोण) Equal ($\angle 1 = \angle 5, \angle 2 = \angle 6$) F-shaped positions (same relative side and level)
Alternate Interior Angles (एकांतर अंतः कोण) Equal ($\angle 3 = \angle 5, \angle 4 = \angle 6$) Z-shaped corners inside parallel strips
Alternate Exterior Angles (एकांतर बाह्य कोण) Equal ($\angle 1 = \angle 7, \angle 2 = \angle 8$) Opposite sides outside parallel strips
Consecutive Interior Angles (क्रमागत अंतः कोण) Supplementary ($\angle 4 + \angle 5 = 180^\circ$) C-shaped or U-shaped allied angles on same side of transversal
Interior Bisector Rectangle Theorem:

The angle bisectors of all four interior angles formed by a transversal intersecting two parallel lines enclose a Rectangle!

17.3 Zigzag Vertex Angles & Clock Angle Speed Formula

The Zigzag / Arrowhead Theorem

When parallel lines have a bent vertex in between (e.g. $\angle AOC$ between $AB \parallel CD$):

$$\text{Draw an auxiliary line through the vertex } O \parallel AB \parallel CD$$ $$\angle AOC = \angle \text{Upper Part} + \angle \text{Lower Part}$$

Clock Angle Master Formula (घड़ी की सुइयों के बीच का कोण)

At time $H\text{ hours}$ and $M\text{ minutes}$, the acute angle $\theta$ between the hour hand and minute hand is given by:

$$\theta = \left|30H - \frac{11}{2}M\right|^\circ$$

If $\theta > 180^\circ$, the reflex angle is $\theta$, and the acute/internal angle is $360^\circ - \theta$.

Type 1: Complementary & Supplementary Angle Formulations Archetype 1
Q1
An angle is $24^\circ$ more than its complement. What is the measure of the angle?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $57^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Let the angle be $x$. Its complement is $90^\circ - x$.
  2. Given condition: $x = (90^\circ - x) + 24^\circ$.
  3. $2x = 114^\circ \implies x = 57^\circ$.
  4. Its complement is $90^\circ - 57^\circ = 33^\circ$. Indeed, $57^\circ - 33^\circ = 24^\circ$.

Top-Rank Speed Hack:

$$\text{Angle} = \frac{90^\circ + \text{Difference}}{2} = \frac{90^\circ + 24^\circ}{2} = \frac{114^\circ}{2} = 57^\circ$$
Type 2: Linear Pair with Algebraic Angle Expressions Archetype 2
Q2
In a straight line $AOB$, rays $OC$ and $OD$ stand such that $\angle AOC = (2x - 10)^\circ$, $\angle COD = (3x + 20)^\circ$, and $\angle DOB = (x + 20)^\circ$. What is the value of $\angle COD$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $95^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Angles on a straight line add up to $180^\circ$: $$(2x - 10) + (3x + 20) + (x + 20) = 180$$ $$6x + 30 = 180 \implies 6x = 150 \implies x = 25$$
  2. Now calculate $\angle COD = 3x + 20 = 3(25) + 20 = 75 + 20 = 95^\circ$.

Trap Warning: Don't mark $x = 25^\circ$ (Option A)! The question asks for the angle measure $\angle COD$, not the variable $x$.

Type 3: Vertically Opposite Angles Archetype 3
Q3
Two straight lines $AB$ and $CD$ intersect at point $O$. If $\angle AOC + \angle BOD = 130^\circ$, find the measure of $\angle AOD$.
Correct Answer: Option (B) $115^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. $\angle AOC$ and $\angle BOD$ are vertically opposite angles, so $\angle AOC = \angle BOD$.
  2. Given: $\angle AOC + \angle BOD = 130^\circ \implies 2\angle AOC = 130^\circ \implies \angle AOC = 65^\circ$.
  3. $\angle AOC$ and $\angle AOD$ form a linear pair on straight line $CD$: $$\angle AOC + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \implies 65^\circ + \angle AOD = 180^\circ \implies \angle AOD = 115^\circ$$
Type 4: Alternate Interior Angles (Z-angles) Archetype 4
Q4
In the figure where $AB \parallel CD$, a transversal intersects $AB$ and $CD$ such that alternate interior angles are $(3x - 15)^\circ$ and $(2x + 25)^\circ$. What is the value of $x$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $40^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

When lines are parallel, alternate interior angles are equal:

$$3x - 15 = 2x + 25 \implies 3x - 2x = 25 + 15 \implies x = 40^\circ$$

Each angle measure is $3(40) - 15 = 105^\circ$.

Type 5: Consecutive Interior Angles (Allied Angles) Archetype 5
Q5
Two parallel lines are cut by a transversal. If the ratio of two consecutive interior angles on the same side of the transversal is $2 : 3$, find the measure of the smaller angle.
Correct Answer: Option (B) $72^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Consecutive interior angles on the same side of a transversal are supplementary (sum $= 180^\circ$).
  2. Let the angles be $2x$ and $3x$. $$2x + 3x = 180^\circ \implies 5x = 180^\circ \implies x = 36^\circ$$
  3. Smaller angle $= 2x = 2 \times 36^\circ = 72^\circ$.
  4. Larger angle $= 3x = 3 \times 36^\circ = 108^\circ$.
Type 6: Auxiliary Parallel Line at Zigzag Vertex Archetype 6
Q6
In the figure, $AB \parallel CD$. If $\angle BAO = 108^\circ$ and $\angle OCD = 120^\circ$, what is the measure of the reflex angle $\angle AOC$?
Correct Answer: Option (B) $228^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Draw an auxiliary line $OX$ through $O$ parallel to both $AB$ and $CD$ directed to the left.
  2. Since $AB \parallel OX$, consecutive interior angles are supplementary: $$\angle A + \angle AOX = 180^\circ \implies 108^\circ + \angle AOX = 180^\circ \implies \angle AOX = 72^\circ$$
  3. Since $CD \parallel OX$, consecutive interior angles are supplementary: $$\angle C + \angle COX = 180^\circ \implies 120^\circ + \angle COX = 180^\circ \implies \angle COX = 60^\circ$$
  4. The internal angle $\angle AOC = \angle AOX + \angle COX = 72^\circ + 60^\circ = 132^\circ$.
  5. The question explicitly asks for the reflex angle $\angle AOC$: $$\text{Reflex } \angle AOC = 360^\circ - 132^\circ = 228^\circ$$

Examiner Trap: Many candidates stop at $132^\circ$ (Option A) and miss the word "reflex"! Always double-check whether the question demands internal or reflex angle.

Type 7: Interior Angle Bisector Intersection Theorem Archetype 7
Q7
If two parallel lines are intersected by a transversal, then the bisectors of the two interior angles on the same side of the transversal intersect at an angle of:
Correct Answer: Option (C) $90^\circ$

Proof:

  1. Let the two consecutive interior angles be $\angle 1$ and $\angle 2$.
  2. Since lines are parallel: $\angle 1 + \angle 2 = 180^\circ$.
  3. The angle bisectors create half-angles: $\frac{1}{2}\angle 1$ and $\frac{1}{2}\angle 2$.
  4. In the triangle formed by the transversal and the two bisectors: $$\text{Angle of Intersection} = 180^\circ - \left(\frac{1}{2}\angle 1 + \frac{1}{2}\angle 2\right)$$ $$= 180^\circ - \frac{1}{2}(\angle 1 + \angle 2) = 180^\circ - \frac{1}{2}(180^\circ) = 180^\circ - 90^\circ = 90^\circ$$
Type 8: Clock Angle Speed Hack Archetype 8
Q8
What is the acute angle between the hour hand and the minute hand of a clock at $7:20$?
Correct Answer: Option (A) $100^\circ$

Top-Rank Speed Hack Formula:

$$\theta = \left|30H - \frac{11}{2}M\right|^\circ$$

Substitute $H = 7$ and $M = 20$:

$$\theta = \left|30(7) - \frac{11}{2}(20)\right| = |210 - 110| = 100^\circ$$

Solved in 5 seconds without drawing a clock dial!

Type 9: Arrowhead / Reflex Corner Angles with Supplementary Rules Archetype 9
Q9
In the given figure, $AB \parallel CD \parallel EF$. If $y : z = 3 : 7$, find the value of $x$.
Correct Answer: Option (B) $126^\circ$

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Since $AB \parallel CD$, $x$ and $y$ are consecutive interior angles: $$x + y = 180^\circ \quad \text{--- (1)}$$
  2. Since $AB \parallel EF$, $x$ and $z$ are alternate interior angles: $$x = z \quad \text{--- (2)}$$
  3. Substitute (2) into (1): $z + y = 180^\circ$.
  4. Given $y : z = 3 : 7 \implies y = 3k, z = 7k$: $$3k + 7k = 180^\circ \implies 10k = 180^\circ \implies k = 18^\circ$$
  5. $z = 7k = 7 \times 18^\circ = 126^\circ$.
  6. Since $x = z$, we have $x = 126^\circ$.
Type 10: Converse Theorems for Parallelism Archetype 10
Q10
Which of the following conditions is SUFFICIENT to prove that two coplanar straight lines $l$ and $m$ cut by transversal $t$ are parallel?
Correct Answer: Option (B) Consecutive interior angles on the same side are supplementary

Theoretical Analysis:

  • Option A: Vertically opposite angles are always equal whenever any two lines intersect, regardless of whether lines are parallel!
  • Option C: Any linear pair sums to $180^\circ$ on any straight line, whether parallel or not!
  • Option D: Common vertex is a general definition of adjacent angles.
  • Option B is Euclid's 5th Postulate / Converse of Allied Angles Theorem: If consecutive interior angles on the same side sum to $180^\circ$, the lines can never meet and are strictly parallel.
