1. Fundamental Axioms, Definitions & Foundational Properties

Set of Integers: $\mathbb{Z} = \{..., -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, ...\}$, consisting of positive integers $\mathbb{Z}^+$, negative integers $\mathbb{Z}^-$, and the neutral integer zero ($0$).
Closure Property: For any $a, b \in \mathbb{Z}$, addition ($a+b$), subtraction ($a-b$), and multiplication ($a \times b$) always yield integers, but division ($a \div b$) does not guarantee an integer.
Commutative & Associative Laws: Addition and multiplication are commutative ($a+b = b+a$; $ab = ba$) and associative ($(a+b)+c = a+(b+c)$; $(ab)c = a(bc)$), whereas subtraction and division are neither.
Distributive Property: Multiplication distributes over addition and subtraction: $a \times (b \pm c) = (a \times b) \pm (a \times c)$.
Exam Pro-Tip: Remember that zero ($0$) is neither positive nor negative, but it is an even integer as it is divisible by $2$ without remainder.
Memory Mnemonic: CAD — Closure, Associative, Distributive are core ring axioms obeyed by integers under addition and multiplication.

Absolute Value Axiom: For any integer $x$, $|x| \ge 0$, and $|x| = 0 \iff x = 0$. Furthermore, $|xy| = |x| \cdot |y|$ and $|x+y| \le |x| + |y|$ (Triangle Inequality).
Additive Inverse & Identity: The additive identity is $0$ ($a + 0 = a$), and the unique additive inverse of $a$ is $-a$ such that $a + (-a) = 0$.
Multiplicative Identity: The multiplicative identity is $1$ ($a \times 1 = a$). Integers do not possess multiplicative inverses within $\mathbb{Z}$ except for $1$ and $-1$.
15-Second Speed-Math Trick: When summing a consecutive sequence of integers from $-n$ to $n$, instantly evaluate the total sum as $0$ by symmetry around the origin $0$.

3. Word Problem Modeling & Practical Applications

Temperature Variation Problems: Modeled using the vertical number line where above zero is positive and below zero is negative. Temperature difference = $\text{Maximum} - \text{Minimum}$.
Elevation & Depth Problems: Sea level represents $0$. Altitudes above sea level are positive integers ($+h$ meters), and submarine depths below sea level are negative integers ($-d$ meters).
Banking & Financial Transactions: Deposits represent positive additions ($+\text{Rs. } x$), while withdrawals and debits represent negative subtractions ($-\text{Rs. } y$).
Elevator Movement Modeling: Ground floor is $0$, floors above are positive, basement levels are negative. Net displacement = $\sum \text{Displacements}$.
UP Exam High-Yield Tip: In SCERT textbook word problems, always translate phrases like 'loss of', 'below', 'withdraw', or 'ago' into negative integers and 'profit', 'above', 'deposit', or 'hence' into positive integers before calculation.

4. Mathematical Edge Cases, Sign Conventions & Common Traps

Critical Distinction: Subtracting a negative integer is equivalent to adding its positive counterpart: $a - (-b) = a + b$. Neglecting this sign change is a primary distractor in exam questions.
Edge Case / Division by Zero: Division of any integer $a$ by zero ($a \div 0$) is undefined. Division of zero by any non-zero integer is strictly zero ($0 \div a = 0$).
Common Calculation Trap: Evaluating $-a^2$ versus $(-a)^2$. Note that $-3^2 = -(3 \times 3) = -9$, whereas $(-3)^2 = (-3) \times (-3) = +9$.
Trap Prevention Trick: Always enclose negative substitute values in parentheses during algebraic evaluation to prevent exponentiation and sign precedence errors.
Memory Mnemonic: PEVS — Parentheses, Exponent, Value, Sign rule check in BODMAS evaluations.

Question 1 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

1. What is the value of the expression $(-20) + (-8) \div (-2) \times 3 - (-6)$?

Correct Answer: Option B

Division and multiplication are evaluated from left to right: (-8)/(-2)=4, then 4 x 3=12. The expression becomes -20+12-(-6)=-20+12+6=-2. The answer is Option B.

Question 2 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

2. If $a = -5$, $b = 3$, and $c = -2$, what is the value of $a^2 - 2ab + c^3$?

Correct Answer: Option A

Substitute the values: a^2=25, 2ab=2(-5)(3)=-30, and c^3=-8. Therefore, a^2-2ab+c^3=25-(-30)-8=47. The answer is Option A.

Question 3 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

3. Which of the following statements is mathematically incorrect regarding integers?

Correct Answer: Option B

Subtraction of integers is not commutative because $a - b \neq b - a$ in general (e.g., $5 - 3 = 2$ while $3 - 5 = -2$). All other statements are standard field/ring axioms of integers.

Question 4 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

4. The temperature at 12:00 noon was $12^\circ\text{C}$ above zero. If it decreases at the rate of $2^\circ\text{C}$ per hour until midnight, at what time would the temperature be $8^\circ\text{C}$ below zero?

Correct Answer: Option B

Initial temperature at noon = $+12^\circ\text{C}$. Final target temperature = $-8^\circ\text{C}$. Total temperature change required = Final - Initial = $-8 - 12 = -20^\circ\text{C}$. Rate of decrease = $2^\circ\text{C}$ per hour. Total hours required = $(-20) / (-2) = 10$ hours. Adding 10 hours to 12:00 noon gives 10:00 PM.

Question 5 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

5. What is the absolute value of the result when the sum of $-14$ and $22$ is multiplied by the difference between $-5$ and $-9$?

Correct Answer: Option A

Sum of $-14$ and $22$ is $(-14) + 22 = 8$. Difference between $-5$ and $-9$ is $(-5) - (-9) = -5 + 9 = 4$. Product = $8 \times 4 = 32$. Absolute value $|32| = 32$. Distractor -32 ignores the absolute value operation.

Question 6 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

6. If $*$ is an operation on integers defined as $a * b = a^2 + b^2 - ab$, find the value of $(-3) * 4$.

Correct Answer: Option A

Substitute $a = -3$ and $b = 4$ into the definition: $(-3)^2 + 4^2 - (-3)(4)$. Calculate components: $(-3)^2 = 9$, $4^2 = 16$, $(-3)(4) = -12$. Thus, $9 + 16 - (-12) = 25 + 12 = 37$.

Question 7 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

7. An elevator descends into a mine shaft at the rate of $6\text{ m/min}$. If the descent starts from $10\text{ m}$ above ground level, what will be its position after $45\text{ minutes}$?

Correct Answer: Option C

Initial position = $+10\text{ m}$. Rate of descent = $-6\text{ m/min}$. Time = $45\text{ min}$. Displacement = Rate $\times$ Time = $-6 \times 45 = -270\text{ m}$. Final position = Initial Position $+$ Displacement = $10 + (-270) = -260\text{ m}$ (i.e., $260\text{ m}$ below ground).

Question 8 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

8. Which of the following integers is closest to $-100$ on the number line?

Correct Answer: Option A

Compare distances from -100: -105 is 5 units away, -94 is 6 units away, -91 is 9 units away, and -108 is 8 units away. The closest integer is -105, so the answer is Option A.

Question 9 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

9. Simplify the algebraic expression: $-[a - {b - (a - b - c) + c} - c]$

Correct Answer: Option A

Inside the braces, b-(a-b-c)+c=-a+2b+2c. The square bracket becomes a-(-a+2b+2c)-c=2a-2b-3c. Applying the outer negative gives -2a+2b+3c. The answer is Option A.

Question 10 of 10 Super TET Junior Mathematics

10. If the product of five non-zero integers is negative, what is the maximum possible number of positive integers among them?

Correct Answer: Option D

The product of integers is negative if and only if the number of negative factors is odd. Since there are 5 integers total, the possible counts of negative integers are 1, 3, or 5. To maximize the number of positive integers, we must minimize the number of negative integers, which is 1. Thus, maximum positive integers = $5 - 1 = 4$.

High-Yield Rapid Recall Formulas & Laws (Must-Remember Pointers)

#1
The set of integers $\mathbb{Z}$ is closed under addition, subtraction, and multiplication, but not under division.
#2
Absolute value $|x|$ represents the non-negative distance of an integer $x$ from zero on the number line.
#3
The product of an even number of negative integers is positive; an odd number of negative integers yields a negative product.
#4
Subtraction of an integer is identical to the addition of its additive inverse: $a - b = a + (-b)$.
#5
Division by zero is undefined, and zero divided by any non-zero integer equals zero.
#6
Multiplication distributes over addition: $a(b + c) = ab + ac$, which holds for all integers.
#7
The additive identity is $0$ and the multiplicative identity is $1$ within the domain of integers.
#8
In order of operations (BODMAS), parentheses and exponents take precedence over multiplication, division, addition, and subtraction.
