About these Class 9 Maths resources

Which chapters are in Ganita Manjari Part 2?

This collection covers Chapters 9–14: Propositions and Their Converses; How Quantities Combine: Understanding Data; The World of Algorithms; Quadrilaterals; Two Variables, One Line; and Math of Space: Surface Area and Volume.

How do I find an exercise solution?

Open a chapter above, then use its exercise links to reach the required set or end-of-chapter exercises. Each chapter also includes study notes and a ten-question mini test.

Are these the NCERT textbook PDF?

No. These are SJMaths study notes and worked solutions in English, not a textbook download. Keep your textbook alongside the notes and attempt a question before checking its solution.
