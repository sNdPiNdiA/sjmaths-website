Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Slope-Intercept Interpretation

Linear models represent constant rates of change where the slope is the unit rate and the y-intercept is the starting value.

  • Slope $m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}$ represents the rate of change.
  • Y-intercept $b$ represents the value when $x = 0$.
  • Equation form: $y = mx + b$.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the rate of change from the context and the initial value to construct the equation.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos to plot points $(x_1, y_1)$ and $(x_2, y_2)$ and type $y_1 \sim mx_1 + b$ to find parameters instantly.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A plumber charges a flat fee of $50 plus $75 per hour. Which equation models the total cost $C$ for $h$ hours?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify the constant fee as the y-intercept: $b = 50$
Step 2
Identify the hourly rate as the slope: $m = 75$
Step 3
Substitute into the linear form:
Calc
$C = 75h + 50$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Type '50 + 75x' into Desmos
Step 2
Observe the y-intercept at (0, 50)
Step 3
Verify the slope by checking the value at x=1
Concept 2

Concept 2: Point-Slope Modeling

Used when a specific point $(x_1, y_1)$ and a rate $m$ are provided, rather than the y-intercept.

  • Formula: $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$
  • Rearrange to $y = mx + b$ to find the intercept.
  • Slope $m$ must be consistent throughout the domain.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Substitute the point and slope into the formula and solve for y.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Type the equation $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ directly into Desmos to see the line pass through the given point.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A line has a slope of 3 and passes through (2, 10). Find the equation.

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Calc
$y - 10 = 3(x - 2)$
Calc
$y - 10 = 3x - 6$
Calc
$y = 3x + 4$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Type 'y - 10 = 3(x - 2)' into Desmos
Step 2
Click the line to verify it passes through (2, 10)
Step 3
Read the y-intercept from the graph
Concept 3

Concept 3: Parallel and Perpendicular Lines

Linear models with specific geometric relationships based on their slopes.

  • Parallel lines have equal slopes: $m_1 = m_2$
  • Perpendicular lines have negative reciprocal slopes: $m_1 = -\frac{1}{m_2}$
  • Vertical lines have undefined slopes.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Determine the slope of the original line, apply the slope rule, and solve for the new intercept.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Graph the original line and the potential answer choices in Desmos to visually confirm parallelism or perpendicularity.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Find the equation of a line perpendicular to $y = 2x + 5$ passing through (0, 0).

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify original slope: $m = 2$
Step 2
Calculate perpendicular slope: $m_{\perp} = -\frac{1}{2}$
Step 3
Use point (0,0) in $y = mx + b$:
Calc
$0 = -\frac{1}{2}(0) + b$
Calc
$b = 0$
Calc
$y = -0.5x$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Graph 'y = 2x + 5'
Step 2
Test answer choices in Desmos
Step 3
Select the line that forms a 90-degree angle at the intersection
Concept 4

Concept 4: Interpreting Intercepts in Context

Understanding what the x-intercept and y-intercept represent in real-world scenarios.

  • Y-intercept: The value of the dependent variable when the independent variable is zero.
  • X-intercept: The value of the independent variable when the dependent variable is zero.
  • Units must match the context of the problem.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set $x=0$ to find the y-intercept and $y=0$ to find the x-intercept.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the Desmos 'Table' feature or click the intercepts on the graph to identify coordinates.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A car's fuel $F$ in gallons is modeled by $F = 15 - 0.05d$, where $d$ is distance. What does 15 represent?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Set $d = 0$
Calc
$F = 15 - 0.05(0)$
Calc
$F = 15$
Step 2
15 is the initial fuel in the tank.
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Graph 'F = 15 - 0.05d'
Step 2
Click the y-intercept (0, 15)
Step 3
Interpret as the starting value when distance is zero

Question 1 Interpreting Slope and Intercept
Easy

A plumber charges a flat fee of $50 plus $75 per hour of work. If $C$ is the total cost in dollars and $h$ is the number of hours worked, which equation models this situation?

Hint: The flat fee is the y-intercept (constant), and the hourly rate is the slope (coefficient of h).
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Identify the constant fee as the y-intercept: $b = 50$.
Step 2
Identify the rate of change as the slope: $m = 75$.
Step 3
Substitute into the slope-intercept form $C = mh + b$.
Step 4
The resulting equation is $C = 75h + 50$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Look for the 'per' keyword to identify the slope.
Step 2
Look for the 'flat' or 'starting' value to identify the intercept.
Step 3
Match $75$ with $h$ and $50$ as the constant.

Revision, Traps & Desmos Syntax

Slope-Intercept Form

$y = mx + b$

Standard form for identifying rate ($m$) and start ($b$).

Point-Slope Form

$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$

Best for building equations from a point and a rate.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: Confusing Slope and Intercept
Always check the units. Slope is 'per unit', intercept is 'flat amount'.
⚠️ SAT Trap: Negative Slope Misinterpretation
A negative slope indicates a decrease; ensure the sign matches the context (e.g., fuel consumption).

Question 21 Level 3: 800 Mastery

The linear model $y = mx + b$ represents the relationship between $x$ and $y$. If the x-intercept is $k$ and the y-intercept is $p$, what is the slope $m$ in terms of $k$ and $p$?

Explanation:
Step 1
The points are $(k, 0)$ and $(0, p)$.
Step 2
$m = (p - 0) / (0 - k) = p / -k$
Step 3
$m = -p/k$, confirming option B.

A line $L$ is perpendicular to $y = 2x + 5$ and passes through $(4, 1)$. What is the equation of $L$?

Explanation:
Step 1
Perpendicular slope is the negative reciprocal: $m = -1/2 = -0.5$.

Step 3
$y = -0.5x + 3$, confirming option A.
Question 24 Level 3: 800 Mastery

The cost $C$ of producing $x$ items is $C = 5x + 1000$. The revenue $R$ from selling $x$ items is $R = 15x$. How many items must be sold to break even?

Explanation:
Step 1
Break even occurs when $R = C$.
Step 2
$15x = 5x + 1000 \implies 10x = 1000$
Step 3
$x = 100$, confirming option B.
Question 25 Level 3: 800 Mastery

If $f(x) = mx + b$ and $f(f(x)) = 4x + 9$, what is a possible value for $m$?

Explanation:
Step 1
$f(f(x)) = m(mx + b) + b = m^2x + mb + b$.
Step 2
$m^2 = 4 \implies m = 2$ or $m = -2$.
Step 3
$m = 2$ is an option, confirming B.
Question 26 Level 3: 800 Mastery

A line passes through $(a, b)$ and $(b, a)$ where $a \neq b$. What is the slope of the line?

Explanation:
Step 1
$m = (a - b) / (b - a)$.
Step 2
$m = (a - b) / -(a - b) = -1$.
Step 3
The slope is -1, confirming option B.
