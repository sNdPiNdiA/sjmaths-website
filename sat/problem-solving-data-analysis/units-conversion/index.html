Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Dimensional Analysis (Chain-Link Method)

The process of converting a quantity from one unit to another by multiplying by conversion factors equal to 1.

  • Ensure units cancel out diagonally: $\frac{A}{B} \times \frac{B}{C} = \frac{A}{C}$
  • Always write units explicitly to avoid numerator/denominator inversion errors.
  • For squared or cubed units, square or cube the entire conversion factor: $(1 \text{ ft} = 12 \text{ in})^2 \implies 1 \text{ ft}^2 = 144 \text{ in}^2$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the target unit, set up a chain of fractions where unwanted units appear in the opposite position (numerator vs denominator), and multiply across.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the Desmos calculator to perform the entire chain multiplication in one line to avoid intermediate rounding errors.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A car travels at 60 miles per hour. How many feet per second is this? (1 mile = 5280 feet)

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify the starting rate: $60 \text{ miles} / 1 \text{ hour}$
Step 2
Convert miles to feet: $(60 \text{ miles} / 1 \text{ hour}) \times (5280 \text{ feet} / 1 \text{ mile})$
Step 3
Convert hours to seconds: $(60 \times 5280 \text{ feet} / 1 \text{ hour}) \times (1 \text{ hour} / 3600 \text{ seconds})$
Step 4
Calculate final value: $316800 / 3600 = 88 \text{ feet/second}$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Type the entire expression into Desmos: $60 * 5280 / 3600$
Step 2
Observe the output: $88$
Concept 2

Concept 2: Currency and Rate Ratios

Solving problems involving exchange rates or unit prices where the relationship is defined by a constant ratio.

  • Set up a proportion: $\frac{A_1}{B_1} = \frac{A_2}{B_2}$
  • Cross-multiply to solve for the unknown: $A_1 \times B_2 = A_2 \times B_1$
  • Always verify the direction of the conversion (e.g., USD to EUR vs EUR to USD).
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set up a proportion equation and solve for the variable using cross-multiplication.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Define the conversion rate as a variable in Desmos (e.g., $r = 0.92$) and multiply the given value by $r$.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: If 5 US dollars are equivalent to 4.5 Euros, how many Euros are 120 US dollars?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Set up the proportion: $5 / 4.5 = 120 / x$
Step 2
Cross-multiply: $5x = 4.5 \times 120$
Step 3
Calculate product: $5x = 540$
Step 4
Divide by 5: $x = 108$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define rate: $r = 4.5 / 5$
Step 2
Multiply: $120 * r$
Step 3
Result: $108$

Revision, Traps & Desmos Syntax

Conversion Factor

$x \text{ units}_1 \times (\text{units}_2 / \text{units}_1) = y \text{ units}_2$

Standard method to change units while maintaining value.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: The 'Squared/Cubed' Trap
Students often forget to square/cube the conversion factor when converting area or volume (e.g., $1 \text{ yd}^3 \neq 3 \text{ ft}^3$, it is $27 \text{ ft}^3$).
⚠️ SAT Trap: Rounding Mid-Calculation
Rounding decimals too early in a multi-step conversion leads to incorrect answers. Keep exact values in Desmos.
