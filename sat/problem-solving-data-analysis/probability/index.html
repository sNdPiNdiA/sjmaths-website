Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Basic Probability

The probability of an event is the ratio of favorable outcomes to the total number of possible outcomes in a sample space.

  • Probability range: $0 \le P(E) \le 1$
  • Complement rule: $P(E') = 1 - P(E)$
  • Total probability: $\sum P(E_i) = 1$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the total count of items in the set. Count the specific items that satisfy the condition. Divide the favorable count by the total count.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the Desmos fraction bar immediately. Type the count of favorable items divided by the total count to get the decimal or fraction.

Concept 2

Concept 2: Conditional Probability

Probability of an event occurring given that another event has already occurred, restricted to a specific subset of the data.

  • Conditional formula: $P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$
  • Subset rule: $P(A|B) = \frac{\text{Number of A and B}}{\text{Total number of B}}$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Ignore the entire table. Focus only on the row or column specified by the 'given' condition. Use that row/column total as your new denominator.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Highlight the specific row or column in the table provided in the question. Only sum the values within that highlighted region.

Concept 3

Concept 3: Independent Events

Two events are independent if the occurrence of one does not change the probability of the other.

  • Independence condition: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$
  • Conditional check: $P(A|B) = P(A)$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Calculate the probability of event A. Calculate the probability of event B. Multiply them to see if they match the joint probability provided.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos to calculate P(A) * P(B) and compare it to the joint probability value given in the table.

Question 1 Basic Probability
Easy

A bag contains 5 red marbles, 3 blue marbles, and 2 green marbles. If one marble is chosen at random, what is the probability that the marble is blue?

Hint: Probability is the number of favorable outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Calculate the total number of marbles: $5 + 3 + 2 = 10$.
Step 2
Identify the number of favorable outcomes (blue marbles): $3$.
Step 3
Apply the probability formula: $P(\text{blue}) = \frac{\text{favorable}}{\text{total}}$.
Step 4
Calculate the final probability: $P(\text{blue}) = \frac{3}{10}$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack

Step 2
Place the target count in the numerator: $3$.
Step 3
Result is $3/10$.

Question 5 Basic Probability
Hard

A bag contains $x$ red marbles and $y$ blue marbles. If a marble is chosen at random, the probability of choosing a red marble is $2/5$. If 4 red marbles are added to the bag, the probability of choosing a red marble becomes $1/2$. What is the value of $y$?

Hint: Set up a system of equations: $x/(x+y) = 2/5$ and $(x+4)/(x+y+4) = 1/2$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
$5x = 2x + 2y \Rightarrow 3x = 2y \Rightarrow x = 2y/3$.
Step 2
$2(x+4) = x+y+4 \Rightarrow 2x + 8 = x + y + 4 \Rightarrow x + 4 = y$.
Step 3
Substitute $x$: $2y/3 + 4 = y$.
Step 4
$4 = y/3 \Rightarrow y = 12$. Wait, check math: $x=8, y=12$. $8/20 = 2/5$. $(8+4)/(20+4) = 12/24 = 1/2$. Correct.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Use Desmos to solve system: $x/(x+y)=0.4$ and $(x+4)/(x+y+4)=0.5$.
Step 2
Intersection point gives $y=12$.

Question 8 Conditional Probability
Medium

A company has two factories, A and B. Factory A produces 60% of the output, and Factory B produces 40%. 2% of A's output is defective, and 3% of B's output is defective. If an item is chosen at random and is found to be defective, what is the probability it came from Factory A?

Hint: Use Bayes' Theorem: $P(A|D) = P(D|A)P(A) / [P(D|A)P(A) + P(D|B)P(B)]$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
$P(D|A)P(A) = 0.02 \times 0.60 = 0.012$.
Step 2
$P(D|B)P(B) = 0.03 \times 0.40 = 0.012$.
Step 3
Total defective: $0.012 + 0.012 = 0.024$.
Step 4
$P(A|D) = 0.012 / 0.024 = 0.5$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Calculate weighted defects: $60 \times 2 = 120$ and $40 \times 3 = 120$.
Step 2
Ratio is $120 / (120 + 120) = 0.5$.

Question 20 Geometric Probability
Hard

Two people agree to meet between 1:00 PM and 2:00 PM. Each person arrives at a random time and waits for 15 minutes before leaving. What is the probability they meet?

Hint: Use a 60x60 square area. The condition is $|x-y| \le 15$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Total area = $60 \times 60 = 3600$.
Step 2
Area where they don't meet: two triangles of size $45 \times 45$.
Step 3
Area of non-meeting = $2 \times (0.5 \times 45 \times 45) = 2025$.
Step 4
Meeting area = $3600 - 2025 = 1575$. $1575/3600 = 7/16$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
$1 - (45/60)^2 = 1 - 9/16 = 7/16$.

Question 2 Level 3: 800 Mastery

A test for a disease is 99% accurate. If 1% of the population has the disease, what is the probability that a person who tests positive actually has the disease?

Explanation:
Step 1
Let $D$ be disease, $T$ be positive test. $P(D) = 0.01, P(T|D) = 0.99, P(T|\text{no } D) = 0.01$.
Step 2
$P(T) = P(T|D)P(D) + P(T|\text{no } D)P(\text{no } D) = (0.99)(0.01) + (0.01)(0.99) = 0.0099 + 0.0099 = 0.0198$.
Step 3
$P(D|T) = P(T|D)P(D) / P(T) = 0.0099 / 0.0198 = 0.5$
