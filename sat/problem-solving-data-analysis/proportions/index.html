Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Basic Proportionality

A proportion is an equation stating that two ratios are equivalent, often used to scale quantities while maintaining a constant relationship.

  • Cross-multiplication property: If $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d}$, then $ad = bc$
  • Unit rate identification: $k = \frac{y}{x}$ where $k$ is the constant of proportionality
  • Direct variation: $y = kx$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set up the ratio as a fraction, equate it to the target ratio, and solve for the unknown variable using cross-multiplication.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Type the equation into Desmos as 'y/x = k' or use the slider feature to find the intersection point instantly.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: If 5 apples cost $3.00, how much do 12 apples cost?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Set up the ratio: $\frac{5}{3} = \frac{12}{x}$
Step 2
Cross-multiply: $5x = 36$
Step 3
Divide by 5: $x = \frac{36}{5}$
Final Answer
$x = 7.2$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Type 'y/5 = 3/x' into Desmos
Step 2
Add a slider for x or look for the intersection with x=12
Step 3
Read the value of y as 7.2
Concept 2

Concept 2: Scale Factors & Geometry

Scale factors represent the ratio of corresponding sides between similar figures or maps.

  • Length ratio: $k = \frac{L_2}{L_1}$
  • Area ratio: $k^2 = \frac{A_2}{A_1}$
  • Volume ratio: $k^3 = \frac{V_2}{V_1}$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the scale factor $k$ by comparing known corresponding sides, then apply the power of $k$ to the target dimension.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos to calculate the ratio $k$ first, then store it as a variable 'k' to perform subsequent calculations.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A map has a scale of 1 inch to 5 miles. If two cities are 4 inches apart on the map, what is the actual distance?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify the ratio: $\frac{1}{5} = \frac{4}{x}$
Step 2
Cross-multiply: $1 \cdot x = 5 \cdot 4$
Step 3
Solve: $x = 20$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define the ratio in Desmos: $r = 5/1$
Step 2
Multiply the map distance by the ratio: $4 * r$
Step 3
Result: 20

A machine produces 120 widgets in 4 hours. At this constant rate, how many widgets can the machine produce in 10 hours?

Revision, Traps & Desmos Syntax

Constant of Proportionality

$k = \frac{y}{x}$

Used to find the unit rate in direct variation problems.

Percent Proportion

$\frac{\text{part}}{\text{whole}} = \frac{\text{percent}}{100}$

Essential for solving complex percentage-based proportion problems.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: Units Mismatch
The SAT often provides values in different units (e.g., feet vs. inches). Always convert to a single unit before setting up the proportion.
⚠️ SAT Trap: Inverse vs Direct
Watch for 'inversely proportional' keywords. If $y$ is inversely proportional to $x$, use $y = \frac{k}{x}$ instead of $y = kx$.

Question 9 Level 3: 800 Mastery

If $a/(b+c) = b/(a+c) = c/(a+b) = k$, what is the value of $k$?

Explanation:
Step 1
Sum the numerators and denominators: $k = (a+b+c) / (2(a+b+c))$.
Step 2
If $a+b+c \neq 0$, $k = 1/2$. Wait, check $a+b+c=0$.
Step 3
If $a+b+c=0$, $b+c = -a$, so $k = a/(-a) = -1$. Correcting: $1/2$ or $-1$.
Question 10 Level 3: 800 Mastery

A sum of money is divided among A, B, and C in the ratio 2:3:5. If C receives $40 more than A, what is the total amount?

Explanation:
Step 1
Let parts be $2x, 3x, 5x$.
Step 2
$5x - 2x = 40 \implies 3x = 40 \implies x = 40/3$.
Step 3
Total $= 10x = 10(40/3) = 400/3$. Re-evaluating: If C is $40 more than A, $5x-2x=40$, $3x=40$. Total $10x = 400/3$. If the question meant $60 more, total is 200.
