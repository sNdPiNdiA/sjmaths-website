Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Range and Interquartile Range (IQR)

Range measures the total spread of a dataset, while IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data.

  • Range = $Max - Min$
  • IQR = $Q_3 - Q_1$
  • Outliers do not affect IQR as much as they affect Range.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Sort the data in ascending order. Identify the minimum and maximum values. Calculate the median of the lower half (Q1) and upper half (Q3). Subtract the values.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Input the list into Desmos as a variable, e.g., $L = [x_1, x_2, ...]$. Use the functions $max(L) - min(L)$ or $quartile(L, 3) - quartile(L, 1)$.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Given the dataset $S = \{12, 15, 18, 20, 22, 25, 30\}$, find the IQR.

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify the median of the set: $20$
Step 2
Identify the lower half: $\{12, 15, 18\}$
Step 3
Identify the upper half: $\{22, 25, 30\}$
Step 4
Calculate $Q_1 = 15$ and $Q_3 = 25$
Step 5
Calculate $IQR = 25 - 15 = 10$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define $L = [12, 15, 18, 20, 22, 25, 30]$
Step 2
Type $quartile(L, 3) - quartile(L, 1)$
Step 3
Read result $10$
Concept 2

Concept 2: Standard Deviation Intuition

Standard deviation measures the average distance of data points from the mean.

  • Low standard deviation = data points are clustered near the mean.
  • High standard deviation = data points are spread far from the mean.
  • Adding a constant to every value does not change the standard deviation.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Calculate the mean. Subtract the mean from each value and square the result. Find the average of these squared differences (variance). Take the square root.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the Desmos function $stdev(L)$ to calculate the value instantly without manual arithmetic.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Which set has a larger standard deviation: $A = \{1, 2, 3\}$ or $B = \{1, 5, 9\}$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Mean of A = $2$
Step 2
Mean of B = $5$
Step 3
Spread of A is $1$ unit from mean
Step 4
Spread of B is $4$ units from mean
Step 5
Set B has a larger standard deviation
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define $L_A = [1, 2, 3]$
Step 2
Define $L_B = [1, 5, 9]$
Step 3
Compare $stdev(L_A)$ and $stdev(L_B)$
Step 4
Observe $stdev(L_B) > stdev(L_A)$

If every value in a data set is increased by 5, how does the range change?

Step 2
New range $R' = (Max + 5) - (Min + 5)$.
Step 3

Question 10 Standard Deviation Concepts
Hard

Data set X has a standard deviation of $\sigma$. If every value in X is multiplied by $-2$, what is the new standard deviation?

Hint: Standard deviation is always non-negative and scales by the absolute value of the multiplier.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Standard deviation is defined as $\sqrt{\frac{\sum(x_i - \mu)^2}{n}}$.
Step 2
If $x_i$ becomes $-2x_i$, the new mean $\mu'$ is $-2\mu$.
Step 3
The new deviation is $\sqrt{\frac{\sum(-2x_i - (-2\mu))^2}{n}} = \sqrt{\frac{\sum(-2(x_i - \mu))^2}{n}}$.
Step 4
$\sqrt{4 \cdot \sum(x_i - \mu)^2 / n} = 2\sigma$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Remember that standard deviation is a distance.
Step 2
Distances are always positive.
Step 3
Multiply by the absolute value: $|-2| \times \sigma = 2\sigma$.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: Outlier Sensitivity
Assuming mean and range are robust. Always remember: Mean and Range change significantly with outliers; Median and IQR do not.
