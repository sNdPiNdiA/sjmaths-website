Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Relative Frequency & Proportions

Data tables often require calculating the ratio of a specific subset to a larger group or total.

  • Relative Frequency = $\frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Total}}$
  • Conditional Probability = $\frac{\text{Target Category}}{\text{Given Condition Total}}$
  • Percentage = $\left(\frac{\text{Part}}{\text{Total}}\right) \times 100$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the specific row or column total requested in the denominator, then divide the specific cell value by that total.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the Desmos table feature to input the data; define variables for row/column sums to calculate ratios instantly.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: A table shows 50 cats and 50 dogs. 20 cats like tuna, 10 dogs like tuna. What is the probability a pet that likes tuna is a cat?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify total pets that like tuna: $20 + 10 = 30$
Step 2
Identify target group (cats that like tuna): $20$
Step 3
Calculate ratio: $\frac{20}{30}$
Final Answer
$\frac{2}{3}$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Input data into Desmos table: $C = [20, 30], D = [10, 40]$
Step 2
Calculate: $20 / (20 + 10)$
Step 3
Result: $0.666...$
Concept 2

Concept 2: Weighted Averages from Tables

Calculating the mean of a dataset presented in a frequency table where values are grouped.

  • Mean = $\frac{\sum (Value \times Frequency)}{\sum Frequency}$
  • Total Sum = $\sum (Value \times Frequency)$
  • Total Count = $\sum Frequency$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Multiply each value by its frequency, sum these products, and divide by the total number of observations.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Create two lists in Desmos: $L_1$ for values and $L_2$ for frequencies. Use the command $total(L_1 \cdot L_2) / total(L_2)$.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Values 1, 2, 3 have frequencies 4, 3, 3 respectively. Find the mean.

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Sum of products: $(1 \times 4) + (2 \times 3) + (3 \times 3)$
Step 2
Calculation: $4 + 6 + 9 = 19$
Step 3
Total frequency: $4 + 3 + 3 = 10$
Step 4
Mean: $\frac{19}{10} = 1.9$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define $L_1 = [1, 2, 3]$
Step 2
Define $L_2 = [4, 3, 3]$
Step 3
Compute: $total(L_1 \cdot L_2) / total(L_2)$
Concept 3

Concept 3: Missing Values in Two-Way Tables

Using row and column totals to solve for unknown variables within a table.

  • Row Sum = $\sum_{j=1}^{n} x_{ij}$
  • Column Sum = $\sum_{i=1}^{m} x_{ij}$
  • Grand Total = $\sum Row Sums = \sum Column Sums$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set up an algebraic equation using the property that the sum of parts equals the total provided in the margin.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Assign a variable $x$ to the missing cell and use the 'Solve' feature or simple arithmetic in Desmos to balance the row/column.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Row total is 100. Cells are 40, 30, and $x$. Find $x$.

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Set equation: $40 + 30 + x = 100$
Step 2
Simplify: $70 + x = 100$
Step 3
Solve: $x = 100 - 70$
Step 4
Result: $x = 30$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Input: $100 - (40 + 30)$
Step 2
Result: $30$
Concept 4

Concept 4: Median from Frequency Tables

Finding the middle value when data is organized by frequency.

  • Position of Median = $\frac{N + 1}{2}$
  • Cumulative Frequency = $\sum_{i=1}^{k} f_i$
  • Median is the value where cumulative frequency reaches or exceeds the median position.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Calculate cumulative frequencies, find the middle position, and identify the corresponding value.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use the 'cumsum' function in Desmos on the frequency list to identify the index of the median.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Values 10, 20, 30 have frequencies 2, 5, 3. Find the median.

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Total frequency: $2 + 5 + 3 = 10$
Step 2
Median position: $\frac{10+1}{2} = 5.5$
Step 3
Cumulative frequencies: $2, 7, 10$
Result
Since 5.5 falls in the second group, median is 20.
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define $L_1 = [10, 20, 30]$
Step 2
Define $L_2 = [2, 5, 3]$
Step 3
Compute $cumsum(L_2)$ to see where 5.5 lands.

Revision, Traps & Desmos Syntax

Weighted Mean

$\frac{\sum x_i f_i}{\sum f_i}$

Used when data is grouped by frequency.

Conditional Probability

$P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$

Crucial for 'Given that...' questions in two-way tables.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: The 'Total' Trap
Students often divide by the Grand Total instead of the specific row/column total requested.
⚠️ SAT Trap: The 'Median' Trap
Assuming the median is the average of the values rather than the middle position in the ordered list.
