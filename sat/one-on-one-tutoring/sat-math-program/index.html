6-Week SAT Math Syllabus

A rigorous, structured roadmap engineered by PGT Maths Sandeep Jaiswal to build conceptual mastery, speed shortcuts, and test-day endurance for an 800.

Week 01 · 35% Weightage Module 1 & 2 Algebra

Linear Equations, Systems & Inequalities

  • Linear equations in one & two variables
  • Systems of linear equations (elimination & substitution)
  • Parallel and perpendicular line criteria ($m_1 m_2 = -1$)
  • Linear inequalities & graphical solution regions
  • Interpreting slopes, constants, and intercepts in context
  • Desmos intersection hack for linear systems
🎯 Week 1 Milestone: Zero errors on foundational Algebra questions; under 40-second solve times on linear equations.
Week 02 · 35% Weightage Advanced Math Foundation

Quadratics, Polynomials & Exponents

  • Standard, vertex, and factored forms of quadratic equations
  • The discriminant ($b^2 - 4ac$) & number of real solutions
  • Polynomial division, remainder theorem, and factor theorem
  • Exponent rules, radicals, and rational exponents
  • Nonlinear systems of equations (line + parabola)
  • Desmos slider hacks for unknown constants ($a$, $b$, $k$)
🎯 Week 2 Milestone: Mastering vertex conversions and identifying extraneous radical roots instantly.
Week 03 · High Speed Calculator Optimization

Desmos Calculator Mastery & Regression Solves

  • Table regressions ($y_1 \sim mx_1 + b$ and $y_1 \sim ax_1^2 + bx_1 + c$)
  • Clickable extrema points for min/max word problems
  • Graphing shaded inequality regions
  • Identifying vertical and horizontal asymptotes
  • Student-Produced Response (SPR) numeric entry shortcuts
  • Timing audit: saving 60–90 seconds per hard question
🎯 Week 3 Milestone: Solving 60%+ of exam questions directly on Desmos with mathematical confidence.
Week 04 · 15% Weightage Geometry & Trig

Circles, Trigonometry & 3D Geometry

  • Standard circle equations: $(x-h)^2 + (y-k)^2 = r^2$
  • Completing the square to find circle center and radius
  • Right triangle trigonometry: $\sin, \cos, \tan$ and cofunction identities
  • Arc lengths, sector areas, and radian-to-degree conversions
  • Similar triangles, scale factors, and surface area vs. volume ratios
  • Special right triangles ($30^\circ-60^\circ-90^\circ$ and $45^\circ-45^\circ-90^\circ$)
🎯 Week 4 Milestone: Perfect scores on geometry questions with zero formula lookup required.
Week 05 · 15% Weightage Data & Statistics

Percentages, Probability & Statistical Inference

  • Percent increase/decrease and successive percentage changes
  • Ratios, unit conversions, and proportional relationships
  • Mean, median, mode, and standard deviation comparisons
  • Two-way contingency tables and conditional probability
  • Scatterplots, lines of best fit, and residual analysis
  • Margin of error, sample bias, and statistical generalizability
🎯 Week 5 Milestone: Eliminating College Board distractors in multi-step percentages and conditional probability.
Week 06 · Test Day Readiness 800-Mastery Sprint

Adaptive Mocks, Error Logs & Test-Day Execution

  • Full-length 70-minute timed Digital SAT adaptive mock
  • Hard Module 2 question prioritization and triage strategies
  • Personal error-log audit: categorizing silly vs conceptual mistakes
  • Negative answer and fraction formatting check for SPR questions
  • Time-management benchmarks (first 10 questions in under 12 minutes)
  • Psychological calm and mental endurance tactics
🎯 Week 6 Milestone: Hitting 750–800 score consistently under realistic Bluebook exam conditions.
