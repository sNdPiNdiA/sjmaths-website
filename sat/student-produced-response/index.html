DIGITAL SAT GRID-IN GUIDE · ~25% OF TOTAL MATH QUESTIONS

Student-Produced Response (SPR) Rules.
Never lose a point on syntax.

Student-Produced Response (grid-in) questions have no answer choices. Avoid score deductions by understanding character limits, negative sign handling, improper fraction formatting, and repeating decimal truncation.

Read the 6 Golden Rules → Practice SPR Drills

The 6 Official SPR Formatting Rules

Tested directly on the Bluebook Digital SAT Math testing interface.

RULE 01 · CHARACTER LIMIT

Maximum Character Limits

Positive answers allow up to 5 characters (including `/` and `.`). Negative answers allow up to 6 characters (including `-`).

Valid: -12.5, 23/50, 0.875
RULE 02 · FRACTIONS

No Mixed Numbers Allowed

Never enter mixed numbers like $3\frac{1}{2}$ (it will be scored as $31/2 = 15.5$). Always convert to improper fraction or decimal.

Enter: 7/2 or 3.5 (Never 3 1/2)
RULE 03 · REPEATING DECIMALS

Fill All Available Spaces

For repeating decimals like $2/3$, you MUST enter all characters that fit: `2/3`, `0.666`, or `0.667`. `0.66` is scored WRONG.

Correct: 2/3, 0.666, 0.667
RULE 04 · NEGATIVES

Negative Numbers are Permitted

Unlike the old paper SAT, negative values CAN be entered on the Digital SAT using the leading minus sign `-`.

Valid: -4, -2/3, -1.25
RULE 05 · MULTIPLE ROOTS

Multiple Valid Solutions

If a quadratic has solutions $x = 2$ and $x = 5$, enter either 2 OR 5 into the box. Both are graded as correct.

Enter: 2 (or enter 5)
RULE 06 · LEADING ZEROS

Leading Zeros are Optional

Decimals less than 1 can be entered with or without the leading zero (e.g. `.75` and `0.75` are both completely valid).

Valid: .75 or 0.75
