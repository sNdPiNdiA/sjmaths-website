SAMPLE SAT QUESTION · ADVANCED MATH Difficulty: Hard

The function $f(x) = -2(x - 3)^2 + 18$ models the height of a trajectory in meters, where $x$ is time in seconds. What is the maximum height achieved, and at what time $x$?

A) 3 meters at x = 18 seconds
B) 18 meters at x = 3 seconds (Correct)
C) -2 meters at x = 3 seconds
D) 18 meters at x = -3 seconds
💡 Desmos Method: Type $f(x) = -2(x - 3)^2 + 18$ into Row 1. Click the crest peak point directly to read $(3, 18)$, meaning maximum height is $18\text{m}$ at time $x = 3\text{s}$.
