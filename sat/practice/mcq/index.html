Question 1 · Algebra Easy

If $5(x - 2) + 3x = 38$, what is the value of $2x - 1$?

Hint: Expand the left side, combine like terms to solve for $x$, then evaluate $2x - 1$.

Algebraic Solution

Step 1
Expand: $5x - 10 + 3x = 38 \implies 8x - 10 = 38$.
Step 2
Add 10: $8x = 48 \implies x = 6$.
Step 3
Evaluate: $2(6) - 1 = 12 - 1 = 11$. Correct option is C.
Question 2 · Algebra Medium

A system of equations consists of $y = 3x - 5$ and $2x + 3y = 29$. What is the value of $xy$?

Hint: Substitute $y = 3x - 5$ into the second equation, solve for $x$, find $y$, then multiply.

Algebraic Solution

Step 1
Substitute: $2x + 3(3x - 5) = 29 \implies 2x + 9x - 15 = 29$.
Step 2
$11x = 44 \implies x = 4$.
Step 3
$y = 3(4) - 5 = 12 - 5 = 7$.
Step 4
$xy = 4 \times 7 = 28$. Correct option is B.
Question 3 · Advanced Math Medium

What are all real values of $x$ that satisfy the equation $2x^2 - 8x - 24 = 0$?

Hint: Divide the entire equation by 2 first, then factor.

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1
Divide by 2: $x^2 - 4x - 12 = 0$.
Step 2
Factor: $(x - 6)(x + 2) = 0$.
Step 3
Solutions: $x = 6$ or $x = -2$. Option D is correct.
Question 4 · Advanced Math Hard

If $f(x) = a(x - 3)(x + 7)$ and the graph of $f$ has a y-intercept of $(0, -42)$, what is the maximum or minimum value of $f(x)$?

Hint: Find $a$ using $f(0) = -42$. The axis of symmetry is the midpoint of the zeros.

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1
$f(0) = a(-3)(7) = -21a = -42 \implies a = 2$. Since $a > 0$, parabola opens up and has a minimum.
Step 2
Vertex x-coordinate: $(3 + -7)/2 = -4/2 = -2$.
Step 3
$f(-2) = 2(-2 - 3)(-2 + 7) = 2(-5)(5) = -50$. Minimum value is -50. Option A is correct.
Question 5 · Data Analysis Medium

A sample of 400 students found that 68% plan to attend a campus tour. If the margin of error is 4.5% at a 95% confidence interval, which range is the most plausible percentage of all students who plan to attend?

Hint: Confidence interval = point estimate $\pm$ margin of error.

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1
Lower bound: $68\% - 4.5\% = 63.5\%$.
Step 2
Upper bound: $68\% + 4.5\% = 72.5\%$. Plausible interval is $63.5\%$ to $72.5\%$. Option C is correct.
Question 6 · Geometry & Trig Hard

A circle in the xy-plane has equation $x^2 - 8x + y^2 + 6y = 24$. What is the radius of the circle?

Hint: Complete the square for both $x$ and $y$ terms.

Step-by-Step Solution

Step 1
Complete the square: $(x - 4)^2 - 16 + (y + 3)^2 - 9 = 24$.
Step 2
$(x - 4)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 24 + 16 + 9 = 49$.
Step 3
$r^2 = 49 \implies r = 7$. Correct option is B.
