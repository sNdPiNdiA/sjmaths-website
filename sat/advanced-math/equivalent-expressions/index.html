Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Polynomial Expansion and Combining Like Terms

Equivalent expressions yield identical outputs for all valid inputs, which can be verified by expanding products and collecting like terms.

  • Distributive property: $a(b + c) = ab + ac$
  • Binomial multiplication (FOIL): $(x + a)(x + b) = x^2 + (a + b)x + ab$
  • Combining like terms requires matching exact variable exponents: $ax^n + bx^n = (a + b)x^n$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Expand all parentheses systematically using distribution and group terms by descending powers of the variable.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Type the original expression as $f(x)$ and the options as $g(x), h(x),$ etc., in Desmos, then check which option shares the exact same table or graph.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Which expression is equivalent to $(3x^2 - 5x + 2) - 2(x^2 - 4x - 3)$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Write out the original expression: $(3x^2 - 5x + 2) - 2(x^2 - 4x - 3)$
Step 2
Distribute the $-2$ through the second parentheses: $3x^2 - 5x + 2 - 2x^2 + 8x + 6$
Step 3
Group the like terms together: $(3x^2 - 2x^2) + (-5x + 8x) + (2 + 6)$
Step 4
Simplify to find the final expression: $x^2 + 3x + 8$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Enter expression into Desmos: $f(x) = (3x^2 - 5x + 2) - 2(x^2 - 4x - 3)$
Step 2
Enter each multiple-choice option as $g(x)$, $h(x)$, etc.
Step 3
Compare tables or look for overlapping graphs to identify the matching function.
Concept 2

Concept 2: Factoring Quadratic Expressions

Rewriting standard quadratic forms into factored forms reveals roots and structural properties of expressions.

  • Difference of squares: $a^2 - b^2 = (a - b)(a + b)$
  • Perfect square trinomials: $a^2 \pm 2ab + b^2 = (a \pm b)^2$
  • Quadratic factoring: $x^2 + (p+q)x + pq = (x + p)(x + q)$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify two numbers that multiply to $c$ and add up to $b$ for trinomials of the form $x^2 + bx + c$.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Evaluate the given expression and an option at a random non-zero value like $x = 3$. If values match, test $x = 4$ to confirm equivalence.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Which of the following is equivalent to $2x^2 - 8x - 24$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Factor out the greatest common factor (GCF) of $2$: $2(x^2 - 4x - 12)$
Step 2
Find two numbers that multiply to $-12$ and add up to $-4$: $-6$ and $2$
Step 3
Rewrite the trinomial in factored form: $2(x - 6)(x + 2)$
Step 4
Final equivalent expression obtained: $2(x - 6)(x + 2)$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Define $f(x) = 2x^2 - 8x - 24$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Graph the options and check which curve perfectly coincides with $f(x)$.
Concept 3

Concept 3: Rational Expression Simplification

Equivalent rational expressions are obtained by factoring numerators and denominators and canceling common non-zero binomial factors.

  • Factor completely before canceling: $\frac{ac}{bc} = \frac{a}{b}$ where $c \neq 0$
  • Division of polynomials often uses polynomial long division or synthetic division.
  • Excluded values occur where the denominator equals zero.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Factor numerator and denominator entirely, then cancel common factors while noting domain restrictions.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Store a numerical value for $x$ (e.g., $x = 4.123$) in Desmos to avoid integer coincidence, then evaluate the expression and options.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Which expression is equivalent to $\frac{x^2 - 9}{x^2 - 5x + 6}$ for $x > 3$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Factor the numerator as a difference of squares: $(x - 3)(x + 3)$
Step 2
Factor the denominator: $(x - 3)(x - 2)$
Step 3
Express the rational function with factored parts: $\frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{(x - 3)(x - 2)}$
Step 4
Cancel the common factor $(x - 3)$ to get: $\frac{x + 3}{x - 2}$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Assign $x = 4.5$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate $\frac{4.5^2 - 9}{4.5^2 - 5(4.5) + 6}$ to get a target decimal.
Step 3
Substitute $x = 4.5$ into the choices to find the matching decimal.
Concept 4

Concept 4: Structure and Parameter Matching

Advanced SAT questions require matching coefficients of equivalent polynomial forms to solve for unknown constants.

  • Identity property: If $Ax^2 + Bx + C = dx^2 + ex + f$ for all $x$, then $A=d$, $B=e$, and $C=f$.
  • Substitution method: Plug in strategic values of $x$ (like $x = 0$ or $x = 1$) to quickly eliminate constants.
  • Polynomial degree matching ensures equivalent terms.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Expand both sides of an equation completely and equate coefficients of corresponding powers of $x$.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos sliders for unknown constants ($a, b, c$) and adjust them until the option graph matches the target expression.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: If $2x^2 + 5x - 12 = (ax + 4)(2x - 3)$, what is the value of $a$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Expand the right side of the equation: $(ax + 4)(2x - 3) = 2ax^2 - 3ax + 8x - 12$
Step 2
Group the linear terms: $2ax^2 + (8 - 3a)x - 12$
Step 3
Equate the coefficient of $x^2$ or $x$ to the left side: $2a = 2$
Step 4
Solve for $a$: $a = 1$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1
Type the equation in Desmos with an equalizer: $2x^2 + 5x - 12 = (ax + 4)(2x - 3)$
Step 2
Add a slider for $a$ when prompted by Desmos.
Step 3
Adjust slider value until the statement is identically true (or check where residual is zero).

Question 1 Polynomial Expansion and Combination
Easy

Which of the following expressions is equivalent to $(3x^2 - 5x + 2) + (2x^2 + 7x - 4)$?

Hint: Group like terms by matching the powers of $x$: combine $x^2$ terms, $x$ terms, and constant terms.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Rewrite the expression without parentheses by aligning like terms: $(3x^2 + 2x^2) + (-5x + 7x) + (2 - 4)$.
Step 2
Combine the coefficients of $x^2$: $3 + 2 = 5$, giving $5x^2$.
Step 3
Combine the coefficients of $x$: $-5 + 7 = 2$, giving $+2x$.
Step 4
Combine the constant terms: $2 - 4 = -2$, yielding the final expression: $5x^2 + 2x - 2$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Type the original expression into Desmos: $y = (3x^2 - 5x + 2) + (2x^2 + 7x - 4)$.
Step 2
Type each option into Desmos as a new function, e.g., $y_1 = 5x^2 + 2x - 2$.
Step 3
Notice that the graph of option A perfectly coincides with the original expression graph.
Question 2 Polynomial Expansion and Combination
Easy

What is the product $(2x - 3)(x + 4)$ expressed in standard form?

Hint: Use the distributive property (FOIL method): First, Outer, Inner, Last.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Apply the FOIL method to the binomials: $(2x)(x) + (2x)(4) + (-3)(x) + (-3)(4)$.
Step 2
Multiply each term: $2x^2 + 8x - 3x - 12$.
Step 3
Combine the middle like terms ($8x - 3x$): $5x$.
Step 4
Write the final combined expression: $2x^2 + 5x - 12$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Enter the product $f(x) = (2x - 3)(x + 4)$ into Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate both $f(x)$ and candidate expressions at a simple test value, such as $x = 2$.
Step 3
$f(2) = (4 - 3)(2 + 4) = 6$, and checking option B gives $2(2)^2 + 5(2) - 12 = 8 + 10 - 12 = 6$.
Question 3 Polynomial Expansion and Combination
Medium

Which of the following is equivalent to $(x^2 - 4x + 3) - 2(3x^2 + x - 5)$?

Hint: Distribute the $-2$ through the second set of parentheses before combining like terms.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Distribute $-2$ into the second parentheses: $x^2 - 4x + 3 - 6x^2 - 2x + 10$.
Step 2
Group the like terms together: $(x^2 - 6x^2) + (-4x - 2x) + (3 + 10)$.
Step 3
Combine the $x^2$ terms: $1 - 6 = -5$, resulting in $-5x^2$.
Step 4
Combine the $x$ terms and constants to get $-5x^2 - 6x + 13$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define $f(x) = (x^2 - 4x + 3) - 2(3x^2 + x - 5)$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Substitute $x = 3$ into $f(x)$ to get a numerical target value: $(9 - 12 + 3) - 2(27 + 3 - 5) = 0 - 2(25) = -50$.
Step 3
Test $x = 3$ in option A: $-5(9) - 6(3) + 13 = -45 - 18 + 13 = -50$, which matches.
Question 4 Polynomial Expansion and Combination
Medium

If $(ax + 3)(2x - 5) = 10x^2 - 19x - 15$ for all values of $x$, what is the value of $a$?

Hint: Multiply the left side polynomial and match coefficients of corresponding powers of $x$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Expand the left side: $(ax)(2x) + (ax)(-5) + (3)(2x) + (3)(-5)$.
Step 2
Simplify the expanded expression: $2ax^2 - 5ax + 6x - 15 = 2ax^2 + (6 - 5a)x - 15$.
Step 3
Match the $x^2$ coefficient to the right side: $2a = 10$.
Step 4
Solve for $a$: $a = 5$ (you can verify with the $x$ term coefficient: $6 - 5(5) = -19$).
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Graph the right side in Desmos: $y = 10x^2 - 19x - 15$.
Step 2
Graph the left side with test values for $a$ (e.g., $y = (ax + 3)(2x - 5)$ with sliders).
Step 3
Adjust slider $a$ until the graphs overlap completely, which happens at $a = 5$.
Question 5 Polynomial Expansion and Combination
Hard

Which of the following is equivalent to the product $(x^2 - 2x + 1)(x^2 + 2x + 1)$?

Hint: Notice that each factor can be written as a squared binomial: $x^2 - 2x + 1 = (x-1)^2$ and $x^2 + 2x + 1 = (x+1)^2$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Rewrite each factor as a squared binomial: $((x - 1)^2)((x + 1)^2)$.
Step 2
Use exponent rules to combine them: $((x - 1)(x + 1))^2$.
Step 3
Simplify the inner product using difference of squares: $(x^2 - 1)^2$.
Step 4
Expand the squared binomial: $(x^2)^2 - 2(x^2)(1) + (1)^2 = x^4 - 2x^2 + 1$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define $f(x) = (x^2 - 2x + 1)(x^2 + 2x + 1)$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(2) = (4 - 4 + 1)(4 + 4 + 1) = (1)(9) = 9$.
Step 3
Test $x = 2$ in option B: $(2)^4 - 2(2)^2 + 1 = 16 - 8 + 1 = 9$, confirming it matches.
Question 6 Rational Expressions and Simplification
Easy

Which of the following is equivalent to $\frac{6x^3 + 9x^2}{3x^2}$ for $x \neq 0$?

Hint: Divide each term in the numerator separately by the monomial denominator.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Split the fraction into two separate terms: $\frac{6x^3}{3x^2} + \frac{9x^2}{3x^2}$.
Step 2
Simplify the first term: $\frac{6}{3} \cdot \frac{x^3}{x^2} = 2x$.
Step 3
Simplify the second term: $\frac{9}{3} \cdot \frac{x^2}{x^2} = 3$.
Step 4
Combine the simplified terms to get $2x + 3$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Enter the original expression $f(x) = \frac{6x^3 + 9x^2}{3x^2}$ into Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(4) = \frac{6(64) + 9(16)}{3(16)} = \frac{384 + 144}{48} = \frac{528}{48} = 11$.
Step 3
Evaluate option A at $x = 4$: $2(4) + 3 = 11$, which matches.
Question 7 Rational Expressions and Simplification
Easy

Which expression is equivalent to $\frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3}$ for $x \neq 3$?

Hint: Factor the numerator using the difference of squares formula: $a^2 - b^2 = (a-b)(a+b)$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Recognize that the numerator is a difference of squares: $x^2 - 9 = (x - 3)(x + 3)$.
Step 2
Rewrite the expression as $\frac{(x - 3)(x + 3)}{x - 3}$.
Step 3
Cancel out the common factor $(x - 3)$ from the numerator and denominator.
Step 4
The remaining expression is $x + 3$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Graph $y = \frac{x^2 - 9}{x - 3}$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Notice it forms a straight line with a removable discontinuity (hole) at $x = 3$.
Step 3
Type option B ($y = x + 3$) and observe that it matches the line exactly.
Question 8 Rational Expressions and Simplification
Medium

Which of the following is equivalent to $\frac{2}{x+1} + \frac{3}{x-2}$ for $x \neq -1$ and $x \neq 2$?

Hint: Find a common denominator, which is $(x+1)(x-2)$, and combine the fractions.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Find the common denominator: $(x + 1)(x - 2) = x^2 - x - 2$.
Step 2
Rewrite each fraction with this common denominator: $\frac{2(x - 2)}{(x + 1)(x - 2)} + \frac{3(x + 1)}{(x + 1)(x - 2)}$.
Step 3
Expand the numerators: $\frac{2x - 4 + 3x + 3}{x^2 - x - 2}$.
Step 4
Combine like terms in the numerator: $\frac{5x - 1}{x^2 - x - 2}$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define $f(x) = \frac{2}{x+1} + \frac{3}{x-2}$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(4) = \frac{2}{5} + \frac{3}{2} = \frac{4}{10} + \frac{15}{10} = \frac{19}{10} = 1.9$.
Step 3
Test option B at $x = 4$: $\frac{5(4) - 1}{4^2 - 4 - 2} = \frac{19}{16 - 6} = \frac{19}{10} = 1.9$.
Question 9 Rational Expressions and Simplification
Medium

If $\frac{5x - 2}{x^2 - 4} = \frac{A}{x - 2} + \frac{B}{x + 2}$ for all $x$ such that $x \neq \pm 2$, what is the value of $A$?

Hint: Multiply both sides by the common denominator $x^2 - 4$ to clear the fractions, then use strategic substitution.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Multiply the entire equation by $(x - 2)(x + 2)$: $5x - 2 = A(x + 2) + B(x - 2)$.
Step 2
To find $A$, substitute $x = 2$ to eliminate $B$: $5(2) - 2 = A(2 + 2) + B(0)$.
Step 3
Simplify the equation: $10 - 2 = 4A$, which gives $8 = 4A$.
Step 4
Solve for $A$: $A = 2$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Graph the left-hand side in Desmos: $y = \frac{5x - 2}{x^2 - 4}$.
Step 2
Add the right-hand side using sliders for $A$ and $B$: $y = \frac{A}{x - 2} + \frac{B}{x + 2}$.
Step 3
Adjust slider $A$ until the graphs overlap, revealing $A = 2$.
Question 10 Rational Expressions and Simplification
Hard

Which of the following is equivalent to $\frac{x^2 - 4x + 4}{x^2 - 4} \div \frac{x - 2}{x^2 + 2x}$ for $x > 2$?

Hint: Factor every numerator and denominator completely, then change division to multiplication by the reciprocal.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Factor all components: $x^2 - 4x + 4 = (x-2)^2$, $x^2 - 4 = (x-2)(x+2)$, and $x^2 + 2x = x(x+2)$.
Step 2
Rewrite the expression: $\frac{(x-2)^2}{(x-2)(x+2)} \cdot \frac{x(x+2)}{x-2}$.
Step 3
Cancel common factors: the term $(x-2)^2$ in the numerator cancels with $(x-2)$ and $(x-2)$ in the denominators, and $(x+2)$ cancels out.
Step 4
The only remaining factor is $x$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define the original complex expression in Desmos as $f(x)$.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(5) = \frac{25 - 20 + 4}{25 - 4} \div \frac{5 - 2}{25 + 10} = \frac{9}{21} \div \frac{3}{35} = \frac{3}{7} \cdot \frac{35}{3} = 5$.
Step 3
Since $f(5) = 5$, and option A is $x$, option A is the correct match.
Question 11 Radical and Exponent Transformations
Easy

Which of the following is equivalent to $\sqrt[3]{x^6 y^3}$ for all positive numbers $x$ and $y$?

Hint: Rewrite the radical expression using fractional exponents: $\sqrt[3]{A} = A^{1/3}$.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Convert the radical to a fractional exponent: $(x^6 y^3)^{\frac{1}{3}}$.
Step 2
Distribute the exponent to each factor using power rules: $(x^6)^{\frac{1}{3}} \cdot (y^3)^{\frac{1}{3}}$.
Step 3
Multiply the exponents: $x^{6 \cdot \frac{1}{3}} y^{3 \cdot \frac{1}{3}}$.
Step 4
Simplify to obtain $x^2 y^1$, which is $x^2 y$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Assign test values to variables in Desmos, e.g., let $x = 2$ and $y = 3$.
Step 2
Compute the original expression value: $\sqrt[3]{2^6 \cdot 3^3} = \sqrt[3]{64 \cdot 27} = 4 \cdot 3 = 12$.
Step 3
Test option A with $x = 2, y = 3$: $(2^2)(3) = 4(3) = 12$, which matches.

Question 13 Radical and Exponent Transformations
Medium

Which expression is equivalent to $\frac{x^{\frac{3}{2}} \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}}}{x^{\frac{1}{4}}}$ for $x > 0$?

Hint: Add exponents in the numerator when multiplying, then subtract the exponent in the denominator when dividing.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Combine the numerator by adding exponents: $\frac{3}{2} + \frac{1}{2} = \frac{4}{2} = 2$.
Step 2
Rewrite the fraction with this combined numerator: $\frac{x^2}{x^{\frac{1}{4}}}$.
Step 3
Apply the quotient rule for exponents by subtracting: $2 - \frac{1}{4}$.
Step 4
Convert to a common denominator: $\frac{8}{4} - \frac{1}{4} = \frac{7}{4}$, resulting in $x^{\frac{7}{4}}$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define the original expression in Desmos as $f(x)$.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(16) = \frac{16^{\frac{3}{2}} \cdot 16^{\frac{1}{2}}}{16^{\frac{1}{4}}} = \frac{64 \cdot 4}{2} = \frac{256}{2} = 128$.
Step 3
Test option A at $x = 16$: $16^{\frac{7}{4}} = (16^{\frac{1}{4}})^7 = 2^7 = 128$, which matches.

Step 2
Apply the power of a power rule: $3^{2x} = 3^{3y}$.
Step 3
Equate the exponents since the bases are equal: $2x = 3y$.
Step 4
Divide both sides by $2y$ to find the ratio $\frac{x}{y} = \frac{3}{2}$.

Question 15 Radical and Exponent Transformations
Hard

Which of the following is equivalent to $\sqrt{x \sqrt{x \sqrt{x}}}$ for $x > 0$?

Hint: Convert each radical layer progressively from the inside out into fractional exponents.
📘 Step-by-Step Algebraic Solution
Step 1
Write the innermost $x$ with exponent 1, multiplying by the inner square root: $(x \cdot x^{\frac{1}{2}})^{\frac{1}{2}}$.
Step 2
Combine the inner terms by adding exponents: $(x^{\frac{3}{2}})^{\frac{1}{2}} = x^{\frac{3}{4}}$.
Step 3
Multiply by the next $x$ and apply the outermost square root: $(x \cdot x^{\frac{3}{4}})^{\frac{1}{2}} = (x^{\frac{7}{4}})^{\frac{1}{2}}$.
Step 4
Multiply the exponents to get the final result: $x^{\frac{7}{8}}$.
⚡ Desmos Shortcut / Speed Hack
Step 1
Define $f(x) = \sqrt{x \sqrt{x \sqrt{x}}}$ in Desmos.
Step 2
Evaluate $f(16) = \sqrt{16 \sqrt{16 \sqrt{16}}} = \sqrt{16 \sqrt{16(4)}} = \sqrt{16 \sqrt{64}} = \sqrt{16(8)} = \sqrt{128} = 8\sqrt{2} \approx 11.3137$.
Step 3
Test option A at $x = 16$: $16^{\frac{7}{8}} = (2^4)^{\frac{7}{8}} = 2^{\frac{7}{2}} = \sqrt{128} \approx 11.3137$, which matches.

🚨 Top SAT Traps & Misconceptions

⚠️ SAT Trap: Negative Distribution Errors
Failing to distribute a negative sign across all terms inside parentheses, e.g., $-(2x - 3)$ becoming $-2x - 3$ instead of $-2x + 3$.
⚠️ SAT Trap: Canceling Terms Across Addition
Incorrectly canceling terms in fractions like $\frac{x + 3}{x}$ into just $3$. Terms can only be canceled if they are common factors.
