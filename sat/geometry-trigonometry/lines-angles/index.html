Key Concepts & Worked Archetypes

Concept 1

Concept 1: Parallel Lines Cut by a Transversal

When parallel lines are intersected by a transversal line, special angle relationships such as corresponding, alternate interior, and consecutive interior angles are formed.

  • Alternate interior angles are equal: $\angle 3 = \angle 5$
  • Corresponding angles are equal: $\angle 1 = \angle 5$
  • Consecutive interior angles are supplementary: $\angle 3 + \angle 6 = 180^{\circ}$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Identify the angle relationship, set up an algebraic equation equating or summing the expressions to $180^{\circ}$, and solve for the variable.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos by defining the variable as $x$, typing both angle expressions as functions, and using intersection or table features to find the matching value.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Problem: Line $m$ is parallel to line $n$. A transversal intersects both lines. If one alternate interior angle is given by $3x + 15$ and the other by $5x - 25$, what is the value of $x$?

📘 Step-by-Step Textbook Solution:
Step 1
Identify that alternate interior angles are equal for parallel lines.
Step 2
Set up the equation: $3x + 15 = 5x - 25$
Step 3
Subtract $3x$ from both sides: $15 = 2x - 25$
Step 4
Add $25$ to both sides: $40 = 2x$
Step 5
Divide by $2$: $x = 20$
⚡ Speed / Desmos Tactic:
Step 1

Step 2
Find where the two lines intersect on the graph.
Step 3
Read the x-coordinate of the intersection point as $x = 20$.
Concept 2

Concept 2: Angles on a Straight Line & Vertically Opposite Angles

Angles lying on a straight line always sum to $180^{\circ}$, and angles directly across from each other at an intersection are vertically opposite and equal.

  • Linear pair sum rule: $\angle A + \angle B = 180^{\circ}$
  • Vertically opposite angles rule: $\angle A = \angle C$
  • Full circle sum rule: $\sum \text{angles around a point} = 360^{\circ}$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Sum adjacent angles to $180^{\circ}$ when forming a linear pair, or equate expressions for vertically opposite angles.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Define the unknown angle as $x$ in Desmos, enter the sum equation equal to $180$, and evaluate.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example

Concept 3

Concept 3: Triangle Angle Sum & Exterior Angle Theorem

The interior angles of any triangle always sum to $180^{\circ}$, and the measure of an exterior angle equals the sum of the two remote interior angles.

  • Triangle sum theorem: $\angle A + \angle B + \angle C = 180^{\circ}$
  • Exterior angle theorem: $\text{Exterior Angle} = \text{Remote Interior } A + \text{Remote Interior } B$
  • Isosceles triangle base angles theorem: If sides are equal, opposite angles are equal.
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set up the sum of interior angles to $180^{\circ}$ or equate the exterior angle to the sum of the two non-adjacent interior angles, then solve algebraically.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Enter the exterior angle equation into Desmos as a function of $x$ and find the intersection with the known constant sum.

Concept 4

Concept 4: Perpendicular Lines & Right Angles

Perpendicular lines intersect at a right angle ($90^{\circ}$), forming the basis for coordinate geometry slope relationships and orthogonal components.

  • Perpendicular lines form: $90^{\circ}$ angles
  • Perpendicular slopes product: $m_1 \cdot m_2 = -1$
  • Complementary angles sum: $\angle A + \angle B = 90^{\circ}$
📘 Traditional Algebraic Method

Set complementary angles equal to $90^{\circ}$ or use negative reciprocal slopes for linear equations.

⚡ SAT Speed Trick & Desmos Hack

Use Desmos to graph perpendicular lines and verify intersection coordinates or angle measurements.

💡 Worked SAT Archetype Example
