Detailed Topic Order

The tables below are arranged in the order you should learn each subject. P1–P3 entries have a related question in at least one supplied paper; P4 entries are additional concepts suggested by those questions.

18 = 2018, 23 = 2023. Question references show the most relevant examples, not every overlap.

Module 1 topic numbers remain identifiers; the recommended learning sequence may differ from numerical order. After the foundations, prioritize Topic 1.11 Aircraft Performance and Stability, Topic 1.10 Helicopters and Rotorcraft, then Topic 1.16 Load Factor, Manoeuvres and Flight Envelope.

Module Theme Content
Module 1
Elements of Aeronautics and Flight Principles		 Aircraft basics, flight forces and aerodynamic behaviour
  • Types of aircraft: aerodynes, aerostats, rotorcraft — Topic 1.1
  • Aircraft parts: wing, fuselage, tail, empennage — Topic 1.2
  • Four forces and flight equilibrium: lift, weight, thrust, drag — Topic 1.3
  • Flight axes: pitch, roll, yaw and coupled motion — Topic 1.4
  • Primary and secondary flight controls — Topic 1.5
  • Airfoil geometry: chord, camber, thickness
  • Lift coefficient, drag coefficient and L/D
  • Aerodynamic centre and pitching moment
  • Wing incidence and loading
  • Helicopters and rotary-wing aircraft
  • Aircraft performance and stability basics
  • Aspect ratio, sweepback, dihedral and anhedral
  • Stalls, stall speed and stall warning
  • Static and dynamic stability
  • Centre of gravity and centre of pressure
  • Load factor, manoeuvres and flight envelope
Module 2
Fluid Mechanics and Aerodynamics		 Fluid properties, flow regimes and aerodynamic forces
  • Fluid properties: density, viscosity, pressure
  • Static, dynamic and stagnation pressure
  • Manometers and hydrostatic pressure
  • Streamlines, pathlines and stream functions
  • Viscous and inviscid flows
  • Boundary layer, wall shear and skin friction
  • Mach number: subsonic, sonic, supersonic
  • Bernoulli equation and stagnation principles
  • Hydrostatic force and centre of pressure
  • Continuity and momentum equations
  • Surface tension and pressure in droplets
  • Rotating fluids and pressure distributions
  • Vortex theory and Biot–Savart law
  • Fluid machinery: pumps and turbines
  • Compressible flow, shocks and Fanno flow
  • High-lift devices and flow separation
  • Reynolds number and transition to turbulence
  • Prandtl number and similarity parameters
  • Oblique shocks and expansion waves
  • Pitot-static systems and measurement errors
  • Wingtip vortices and induced drag
  • Dynamic stall and aerodynamic icing effects
  • Buoyancy, metacentre and floating-body stability
Module 3
Heat Transfer		 Thermal energy transfer and aircraft thermal systems
  • Heat transfer modes: conduction, convection, radiation
  • Fourier's law and temperature gradients
  • Thermal resistance and composite walls
  • Newton's law of cooling
  • Convective heat transfer coefficient
  • Heat exchangers: effectiveness and temperature difference
  • Insulation and critical radius concepts
  • Fins and extended surfaces
  • Lumped heat capacity and Biot number
  • Thermal boundary layers
  • Thermal radiation and irradiation
  • Nusselt number and convective correlations
  • Transient heat conduction and Fourier number
  • Aircraft thermal protection and aerodynamic heating
  • Heat exchanger LMTD and NTU methods
  • Conduction with internal heat generation
  • Aircraft thermal management systems
Module 4
Aircraft Structures, Stress Analysis, Plates and Shells		 Loads, strength, materials and structural integrity
  • Aircraft structural loads
  • Limit load, proof load and ultimate load
  • Normal stress, shear stress and strain
  • Hooke's law, Young's modulus, Poisson's ratio
  • Thin pressure vessels: hoop and longitudinal stress
  • Fuselage stiffeners and buckling
  • Shear strain and angular distortion
  • Plane stress and plane elasticity
  • Beams, supports and load distribution
  • Plate bending and stretching
  • Finite element method: elements and meshes
  • Plates on elastic foundations
  • Circular and skew plates
  • Stress concentration around holes
  • Bredt–Batho torsion theory
  • Flutter, buffeting and aeroelasticity
  • Strain gauges and adhesives
  • Mohr's circle and principal stresses
  • Bending moment and shear force diagrams
  • Fatigue life, cracks and fracture mechanics
  • Fail-safe, safe-life and damage-tolerant design
  • Composite aircraft structures and delamination
  • Strain rosettes and Wheatstone bridges
  • Structural symmetry and symmetric plate loading
Module 5

  • Turbo-rocket engines

  • Turbofan engines and bypass ratio

How to use this 100-day plan

  • Normal study day: 90-120 min concepts, 30-40 min worked examples, 35-50 min MCQs, 15-20 min revision/error log.
  • Error codes after every test: C = concept missing, F = fact/formula forgotten, N = numerical error, G = guessing error, S = silly/careless error.
  • Running books: Formula Book, Aircraft Systems and Components Fact Book, Regulations/ICAO/ATC Fact Book, Error Book.
  • Weekly rule: revise previous day 15-20 min daily; reattempt 10-15 Error Book questions every 7th session; revise all formulas every 14 days.
  • From Day 40: add 15-20 mixed old-topic MCQs when time permits. From Day 80: 20 min daily mixed recall.
  • Exam passes (2023 RT had negative marking): Pass 1 certain, Pass 2 solvable, Pass 3 risk-controlled. Avoid blind guesses.
  • Supporting topics strengthened in v2: performance, stability/control, V-n diagram, maintenance philosophies, damage tolerance, autopilot/FMS/EFIS, ADS-B, declared distances, wake-turbulence separation, SID/STAR/holding, ICAO Annex 13/19, SMS and human factors.
  • Not given dedicated blocks unless a future notification restores them: standalone Mathematics, Physics, Electrical/Electronics fundamentals, General Studies, English, Reasoning and Current Affairs. Rebalance the plan if UPSC restores them.
