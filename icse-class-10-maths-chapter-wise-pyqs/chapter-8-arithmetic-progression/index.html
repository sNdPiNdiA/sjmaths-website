08. Arithmetic Progression (AP) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: T_n = a + (n−1)d, S_n = (n/2)[2a + (n−1)d]

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Check whether common difference d is positive or negative when terms decrease. Double-check sign handling in S_n.

ICSE 2026 Board Q2(i) AP Terms & Sum
4 Marks
In an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.), the 4th term is 18 and the 10th term is 42. Find the:
(a) first term and common difference.
(b) sum of the first 20 terms (S₂₀).
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) T₄ = a + 3d = 18 ... (1)
T₁₀ = a + 9d = 42 ... (2) [1 Mark]
Subtracting (1) from (2): 6d = 24 ⇒ d = 4.
Substitute d into (1): a + 3(4) = 18 ⇒ a + 12 = 18 ⇒ a = 6. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Sum formula: S_n = (n/2)[2a + (n−1)d].
S₂₀ = (20/2)[2(6) + 19(4)] = 10 × [12 + 76] = 10 × 88 = 880. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q4(ii) Arithmetic Progression
3 Marks
The first term of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is 5, the last term is 50 and their sum is 440. Find: (a) the number of terms, (b) common difference.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
a = 5, l = 50, Sn = 440.
(a) Sn = n/2 (a + l) ⇒ 440 = n/2 (5 + 50) = 55n/2 ⇒ n = 16 terms. [1.5 Marks]
(b) l = a + (n − 1)d ⇒ 50 = 5 + 15d ⇒ 15d = 45 ⇒ d = 3. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2024 Board Q1(viii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The 7th term of the given Arithmetic Progression (A.P.): 1/a, (1/a + 1), (1/a + 2) … is:
(a) (1/a + 6)
(b) (1/a + 7)
(c) (1/a + 8)
(d) (1/a + 77)
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Correct Option: (a) (1/a + 6)
First term a1 = 1/a, d = 1.
T7 = a1 + 6d = 1/a + 6. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q6(ii) Algebra • Arithmetic Progression
3 Marks
The nth term of an A.P. is given by Tn = 6(7 − n). Find:
(a) its first term and common difference
(b) sum of its first 25 terms.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Tn = 42 − 6n.
(a) First term a = T1 = 42 − 6 = 36. T2 = 30 ⇒ common difference d = 30 − 36 = −6. [1.5 Marks]
(b) S25 = (25/2)[2(36) + 24(−6)] = (25/2)[72 − 144] = 25 × (−36) = −900. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2023 Board Q1(x) Multiple Choice Question

The $n^\text{th}$ term of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is $2n + 5$. The $10^\text{th}$ term is:
(a) 7
(b) 15
(c) 25
(d) 45
View Verified Solution & Marking
Given $T_n = 2n + 5$. For $n = 10$:
$T_{10} = 2(10) + 5 = 20 + 5 = 25$.
Correct Option: (c) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q6(ii) Arithmetic Progression (Terms & Sum of 16 Terms)
3 Marks
The $5^\text{th}$ term and the $9^\text{th}$ term of an Arithmetic Progression are 4 and −12 respectively. Find:
  1. the first term
  2. common difference
  3. sum of 16 terms of the AP.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Let the first term be $a$ and common difference be $d$.
$T_5 = a + 4d = 4$ — (Eq. 1)
$T_9 = a + 8d = -12$ — (Eq. 2)

Subtract Eq. 1 from Eq. 2
$(a + 8d) - (a + 4d) = -12 - 4 \implies 4d = -16 \implies \mathbf{d = -4}$. (Common Difference)1 Mark

Find First Term
Substitute $d = -4$ into Eq. 1:
$a + 4(-4) = 4 \implies a - 16 = 4 \implies \mathbf{a = 20}$.1 Mark

Sum of 16 Terms
$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$
$S_{16} = \frac{16}{2}[2(20) + (16 - 1)(-4)] = 8[40 + 15(-4)] = 8[40 - 60] = 8(-20) = \mathbf{-160}$.1 Mark
ICSE 2023 Board Q9(ii) Arithmetic Progression (Term Number & Series Sum)
3 Marks
Which term of the Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) $15, 30, 45, 60, \dots$ is 300?
Hence find the sum of all the terms of the Arithmetic Progression (A.P.).
View Verified Solution & Marking
First term $a = 15$. Common difference $d = 30 - 15 = 15$.

Part 1 $n^\text{th}$ term formula: $T_n = a + (n - 1)d = 300$
$15 + (n - 1)15 = 300 \implies 15n = 300 \implies \mathbf{n = 20}$.
Therefore, the $20^\text{th}$ term is 300.1.5 Marks

Part 2 Sum of $n$ terms $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$ where last term $l = 300$:
$S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}(15 + 300) = 10 \times 315 = \mathbf{3,150}$.1.5 Marks
