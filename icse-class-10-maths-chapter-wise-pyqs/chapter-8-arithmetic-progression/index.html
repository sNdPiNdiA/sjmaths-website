08. Arithmetic Progression (AP) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: T_n = a + (n−1)d, S_n = (n/2)[2a + (n−1)d]

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Check whether common difference d is positive or negative when terms decrease. Double-check sign handling in S_n.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 AP Terms & Sum
4 Marks
In an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.), the 4th term is 18 and the 10th term is 42. Find the:
(a) first term and common difference.
(b) sum of the first 20 terms (S₂₀).
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) T₄ = a + 3d = 18 ... (1)
T₁₀ = a + 9d = 42 ... (2) [1 Mark]
Subtracting (1) from (2): 6d = 24 ⇒ d = 4.
Substitute d into (1): a + 3(4) = 18 ⇒ a + 12 = 18 ⇒ a = 6. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Sum formula: S_n = (n/2)[2a + (n−1)d].
S₂₀ = (20/2)[2(6) + 19(4)] = 10 × [12 + 76] = 10 × 88 = 880. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q2 Arithmetic Progression
3 Marks
The first term of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is 5, the last term is 50 and their sum is 440. Find: (a) the number of terms, (b) common difference.
View Verified Solution & Marking
a = 5, l = 50, Sn = 440.
(a) Sn = n/2 (a + l) ⇒ 440 = n/2 (5 + 50) = 55n/2 ⇒ n = 16 terms. [1.5 Marks]
(b) l = a + (n − 1)d ⇒ 50 = 5 + 15d ⇒ 15d = 45 ⇒ d = 3. [1.5 Marks]

ICSE 2024 Board Q4 Algebra • Arithmetic Progression
3 Marks
The nth term of an A.P. is given by Tn = 6(7 − n). Find:
(a) its first term and common difference
(b) sum of its first 25 terms.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Tn = 42 − 6n.
(a) First term a = T1 = 42 − 6 = 36. T2 = 30 ⇒ common difference d = 30 − 36 = −6. [1.5 Marks]
(b) S25 = (25/2)[2(36) + 24(−6)] = (25/2)[72 − 144] = 25 × (−36) = −900. [1.5 Marks]

(a) 7

ICSE 2023 Board Q6 Arithmetic Progression (Terms & Sum of 16 Terms)
3 Marks
The $5^\text{th}$ term and the $9^\text{th}$ term of an Arithmetic Progression are 4 and −12 respectively. Find:
  1. the first term
  2. common difference
  3. sum of 16 terms of the AP.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Let the first term be $a$ and common difference be $d$.
$T_5 = a + 4d = 4$ — (Eq. 1)
$T_9 = a + 8d = -12$ — (Eq. 2)

Subtract Eq. 1 from Eq. 2
$(a + 8d) - (a + 4d) = -12 - 4 \implies 4d = -16 \implies \mathbf{d = -4}$. (Common Difference)1 Mark

Find First Term
Substitute $d = -4$ into Eq. 1:
$a + 4(-4) = 4 \implies a - 16 = 4 \implies \mathbf{a = 20}$.1 Mark

Sum of 16 Terms
$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$
$S_{16} = \frac{16}{2}[2(20) + (16 - 1)(-4)] = 8[40 + 15(-4)] = 8[40 - 60] = 8(-20) = \mathbf{-160}$.1 Mark
ICSE 2023 Board Q7 Arithmetic Progression (Term Number & Series Sum)
3 Marks
Which term of the Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) $15, 30, 45, 60, \dots$ is 300?
Hence find the sum of all the terms of the Arithmetic Progression (A.P.).
View Verified Solution & Marking
First term $a = 15$. Common difference $d = 30 - 15 = 15$.

Part 1 $n^\text{th}$ term formula: $T_n = a + (n - 1)d = 300$
$15 + (n - 1)15 = 300 \implies 15n = 300 \implies \mathbf{n = 20}$.
Therefore, the $20^\text{th}$ term is 300.1.5 Marks

Part 2 Sum of $n$ terms $S_n = \frac{n}{2}(a + l)$ where last term $l = 300$:
$S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}(15 + 300) = 10 \times 315 = \mathbf{3,150}$.1.5 Marks
ICSE 2020 Board Q8 Arithmetic Progression (Terms & Sum of Series)
3 Marks
If the 6th term of an A.P. is equal to four times its first term and the sum of first six terms is 75, find the first term and the common difference.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Let the first term be $a$ and common difference be $d$.
$T_6 = a + 5d$. Given $T_6 = 4a$:
$$a + 5d = 4a \implies 5d = 3a \implies a = \frac{5}{3}d$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Given $S_6 = 75$:
$$S_6 = \frac{6}{2}[2a + (6 - 1)d] = 3(2a + 5d) = 75 \implies 2a + 5d = 25$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Substitute $5d = 3a$:
$$2a + 3a = 25 \implies 5a = 25 \implies \mathbf{a = 5}$$
Then $5d = 3(5) = 15 \implies \mathbf{d = 3}$.
Answer: First term $a = 5$, common difference $d = 3$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q9 Arithmetic Progression (Terms & Sum of 20 Terms)
4 Marks
In an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) the fourth and sixth terms are 8 and 14 respectively. Find the:
  1. first term
  2. common difference
  3. sum of the first 20 terms.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Set up linear equations in $a$ and $d$:
$T_4 = a + 3d = 8$   ...(i)
$T_6 = a + 5d = 14$   ...(ii) [1 Mark]
Step 2 Subtract (i) from (ii):
$(a + 5d) - (a + 3d) = 14 - 8 \implies 2d = 6 \implies \mathbf{d = 3}$.
$\therefore \text{Common difference } d = \mathbf{3}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Substitute $d = 3$ into (i):
$a + 3(3) = 8 \implies a + 9 = 8 \implies \mathbf{a = -1}$.
$\therefore \text{First term } a = \mathbf{-1}$. [1 Mark]
Step 4 Find sum of the first 20 terms ($S_{20}$):
$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$
$S_{20} = \frac{20}{2}[2(-1) + (20 - 1)(3)] = 10[-2 + 19(3)] = 10[-2 + 57] = 10(55) = \mathbf{550}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q10 Arithmetic Progression: Sum & Product of 3 Terms
3 Marks
The sum of the first three terms of an Arithmetic Progression (A.P.) is 42 and the product of the first and third term is 52. Find the first term and the common difference.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Let the three consecutive terms of the A.P. be $(a - d)$, $a$, and $(a + d)$.
Sum of the three terms $= (a - d) + a + (a + d) = 3a = 42 \implies \mathbf{a = 14}$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Product of the first and third terms $= 52$:
$(a - d)(a + d) = a^2 - d^2 = 52$.
Substitute $a = 14$:
$14^2 - d^2 = 52 \implies 196 - d^2 = 52 \implies d^2 = 196 - 52 = 144 \implies \mathbf{d = \pm 12}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Determine the terms and solutions:
Case 1 ($d = 12$):
First term $T_1 = a - d = 14 - 12 = \mathbf{2}$.
Common difference $d = \mathbf{12}$. (Terms: $2, 14, 26$)
Case 2 ($d = -12$):
First term $T_1 = a - d = 14 - (-12) = \mathbf{26}$.
Common difference $d = \mathbf{-12}$. (Terms: $26, 14, 2$)
Both solutions are completely valid. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q11 Surd Arithmetic Progression & Sum of 10 Terms
4 Marks
$\sqrt{2}, \sqrt{8}, \sqrt{18}, \dots$ forms a progression.
  1. Identify the type of progression.
  2. The sum of its first 10 terms is $p\sqrt{2}$. Find the value of $p$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 (a) Simplify surd terms:
$T_1 = \sqrt{2}$, $T_2 = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2}$, $T_3 = \sqrt{18} = 3\sqrt{2}$.
$T_2 - T_1 = 2\sqrt{2} - \sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2}$, and $T_3 - T_2 = 3\sqrt{2} - 2\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2}$.
Since the difference between consecutive terms is constant, it is an **Arithmetic Progression (A.P.)** with $a = \sqrt{2}$ and $d = \sqrt{2}$. [2 Marks]
Step 2 (b) Calculate sum of first 10 terms ($S_{10}$):
$S_{10} = \frac{10}{2}[2a + (10 - 1)d] = 5[2\sqrt{2} + 9\sqrt{2}] = 5(11\sqrt{2}) = 55\sqrt{2}$.
Given $S_{10} = p\sqrt{2} \implies \mathbf{p = 55}$. [2 Marks]
ICSE 2018 Board Q12 Consecutive Terms of an A.P.
3 Marks
If $(k - 3)$, $(2k + 1)$ and $(4k + 3)$ are three consecutive terms of an A.P., find the value of $k$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Condition for consecutive terms $a, b, c$ in A.P.:
$$b - a = c - b \iff 2b = a + c$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Substitute the given expressions:
$$2(2k + 1) = (k - 3) + (4k + 3)$$ $$4k + 2 = 5k$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Solve for $k$:
$$5k - 4k = 2 \implies \mathbf{k = 2}$$ [1 Mark]
Check: Terms are $2 - 3 = -1$, $2(2) + 1 = 5$, and $4(2) + 3 = 11$. Common difference $d = 6$.
Answer: $k = 2$.
ICSE 2018 Board Q13 Arithmetic Progression
4 Marks
The 4th term of an A.P. is 22 and 15th term is 66. Find the first term and the common difference. Hence find the sum of the series to 8 terms.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Let first term be $a$ and common difference be $d$.
$$T_4 = a + 3d = 22 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$ [1 Mark]
$$T_{15} = a + 14d = 66 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Subtract (1) from (2):
$$(a + 14d) - (a + 3d) = 66 - 22 \implies 11d = 44 \implies \mathbf{d = 4}$$ [0.5 Mark]
Substitute $d = 4$ into (1):
$$a + 3(4) = 22 \implies a + 12 = 22 \implies \mathbf{a = 10}$$ [0.5 Mark]
Step 3 Sum of first 8 terms ($S_8$):
$$S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n - 1)d]$$ $$S_8 = \frac{8}{2}[2(10) + (8 - 1)4] = 4[20 + 28] = 4 \times 48 = \mathbf{192}$$ [1 Mark]
Answers: First term $a = 10$, Common difference $d = 4$, Sum of 8 terms $S_8 = 192$.
