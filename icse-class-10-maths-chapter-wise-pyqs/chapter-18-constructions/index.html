18. Constructions (Circles & Tangents) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: Ruler & Compasses only; preserve all construction arcs

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Do not use protractor for constructing 60°, 90°, 120°, 45°. Construct angles using compass only.

ICSE 2026 Board Q3(iii-b) Geometric Construction
3 Marks
Using ruler and compasses only, construct a triangle ABC with AB = 6 cm, BC = 7 cm and ∠ABC = 60°. Construct its incircle and measure its radius.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Triangle ABC Draw base BC = 7 cm. At B, construct ∠B = 60° using compass. Cut off AB = 6 cm. Join AC to complete ΔABC. [1 Mark]

Step 2: Incentre I Bisect ∠B and ∠C using compass arcs. The point of intersection of the angle bisectors is the incentre I. [1 Mark]

Step 3: Incircle From I, draw a perpendicular to BC meeting at D. With centre I and radius ID, draw the incircle touching all three sides. Measured inradius r = 1.8 cm (± 0.1 cm). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q3(i) Geometric Construction
4 Marks
Construct ΔABC with AB = 6 cm, AC = 4.5 cm and ∠BAC = 120°. Construct circle circumscribing ΔABC. Measure and write down length of radius.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
1. Draw line AB = 6 cm. Construct angle 120° at A using compass. Cut off AC = 4.5 cm. Join BC. [2 Marks]
2. Draw perpendicular bisectors of AB and AC intersecting at circumcentre O. [1 Mark]
3. With centre O, draw circle passing through A, B, C. Measured radius = 5.3 cm (±0.1 cm). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q10(iii) Geometry • Geometric Constructions
4 Marks
Use ruler and compass to answer this question. Construct ∠ABC = 90°, where AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm.
(a) Construct the locus of points equidistant from B and C.
(b) Construct the locus of points equidistant from A and B.
(c) Mark intersection as O. Construct locus of points at distance OA from O.
(d) Construct locus of points equidistant from BA and BC.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
1. Draw BC = 8 cm, ∠B = 90°, BA = 6 cm, join AC (10 cm). [1 Mark]
2. Perpendicular bisector of BC. [1 Mark]
3. Perpendicular bisector of AB meeting at O (midpoint of AC). Draw circumcircle radius 5 cm. [1 Mark]
4. Angle bisector of ∠ABC. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q8(iii) Geometric Construction: Triangle & Circumscribed Circle
4 Marks
Using ruler and compass, construct a triangle $ABC$ in which $AB = 6\text{ cm}, \angle BAC = 120^\circ$ and $AC = 5\text{ cm}$. Construct a circle passing through $A, B$ and $C$. Measure and write down the radius of the circle.
Figure: Obtuse $\triangle ABC$ with circumcentre $O$ outside the triangle and circumcircle
View Verified Solution & Marking
Steps of Construction
1. Draw line segment $AB = 6\text{ cm}$.
2. At point $A$, construct an angle $\angle BAX = 120^\circ$ using a pair of compasses.
3. Cut off an arc of length $5\text{ cm}$ along the ray $AX$ to locate vertex $C$. Join $BC$ to complete $\triangle ABC$.2 Marks
4. Draw the perpendicular bisectors of sides $AB$ and $AC$. Produce them to intersect at point $O$ (circumcentre lies outside because $\angle A$ is obtuse).
5. With $O$ as centre and radius $OA = OB = OC$, draw the circumscribing circle.1 Mark

Radius Measurement
Measuring with ruler: $\mathbf{\text{Radius } R \approx 5.5\text{ cm}}$ (acceptable examination tolerance: $5.3\text{ to } 5.7\text{ cm}$).1 Mark
