Board Structure

Exam Pattern & Weightage Blueprint

80 Marks Written Paper (2 hours 30 min) + 20 Marks Internal School Assessment.

Section A • 40 Marks

Mandatory
  • Q1 (15 Marks): 15 single-correct MCQs testing formulas and core terms.
  • Q2 (12 Marks): 3 sub-parts (4+4+4M) on Banking, Factor Theorem, and Inequations.
  • Q3 (13 Marks): 3 sub-parts (4+4+5M) on GST, Reflection, and Trig Identities.

Section B • 40 Marks

Attempt Any 4 of 7
  • Statistics (Ogive / Histogram): High scoring 6-mark graph + 4-mark sub-part.
  • Quadratic Equations & AP: Word problems, nature of roots, and nth terms.
  • Geometry & Circles: Angle & tangent properties with mandatory proof reasons.
  • Mensuration & Coordinate: Combined solid volumes and line equations.

Examiner Feedback

Common Deduction Pitfalls to Avoid

Direct observations from official CISCE Pupil Performance Reports.

Geometric Reasons in ()

1 mark is deducted per step in Circles and Similarity if the theorem is not stated in parentheses (e.g. "angles in same segment are equal").

Ogive Scale & Baseline

State your scale explicitly at top-right (e.g. 2 cm = 10 units). Plot at upper class limits and join smoothly to the lowest lower limit on the x-axis.

Units & Rounding

Omitting cm² or cm³ in Mensuration loses 1 mark. For answers "correct to 2 decimal places", compute to 3 decimals first before rounding.

Board FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Are calculators allowed in ICSE Class 10 Maths?

No. Calculators are strictly prohibited in the ICSE Class 10 board exam. All calculations must be worked out manually in the rough margin.

Which questions in Section B are the most scoring?

Statistics (Ogive/Histogram + Mean) offers a clean 10 marks. Quadratic equations, AP, Matrices, and Coordinate Geometry are also algorithmic and high scoring.

Do examiners award step marks if my final answer is wrong?

Yes. CISCE marks stepwise: marks are awarded for formula citation, numerical substitution, and method steps, even if an arithmetic error happens at the end.

What are the best reference books for ICSE Maths?

Concise Mathematics Class 10 by R.K. Bansal (Selina) and Understanding ICSE Mathematics by M.L. Aggarwal are the standard gold references.
