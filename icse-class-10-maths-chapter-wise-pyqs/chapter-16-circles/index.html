ICSE 2026 Board Q1(viii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
In the given diagram, O is the centre of the circle and ABCD is a cyclic quadrilateral. If ∠CDE = 65°, then the value of central angle x is:
(a) 32.5°
(b) 65°
(c) 115°
(d) 130°
Cyclic Quad ABCD with ray ADE extended to E and ∠CDE = 65°
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (d) 130°
Exterior Angle Theorem The exterior angle of a cyclic quadrilateral equals the opposite interior angle.
⇒ ∠ABC = ∠CDE = 65°.
The angle subtended by arc AC at the centre is twice the angle subtended at the remaining circumference:
x = ∠AOC = 2 × ∠ABC = 2 × 65° = 130°.
ICSE 2026 Board Q2(iii) Circle Angle & Tangent Properties
4 Marks
In the given diagram, O is the centre of the circle and the tangent DE touches the circle at B. If ∠ADB = 32°. Find the values of x and y.
Circle with diameter AC, tangent DBE touching at B, and ∠ADB = 32°
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Radius & Tangent Diameter AC passes through center O ⇒ ∠ABC = 90° (angle in a semicircle). [1 Mark]
Step 2: Triangle ABD Radius OB ⊥ tangent DE ⇒ ∠OBD = 90°.
In right-angled ΔOBD: ∠BOD = 180° − (90° + 32°) = 58°. [1 Mark]
Since OB = OA (radii), in ΔOAB, ∠OAB = ∠OBA = x.
Exterior ∠BOD = ∠OAB + ∠OBA ⇒ 58° = 2x ⇒ x = 29°. [1 Mark]

Step 3: Alternate Segment Theorem By Alternate Segment Theorem, angle y between tangent BE and chord AB equals the angle in the alternate segment subtended by chord AB:
y = ∠ACB = 90° − x = 90° − 29° = 61°. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q1(vi) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
In the given diagram, chords AC and BC are equal. If ∠ACD = 120°, then ∠AEC is:
(a) 30°
(b) 60°
(c) 90°
(d) 120°
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
∠ACB = 180° − 120° = 60°. In ΔABC, AC = BC ⇒ ∠ABC = 60°.
In cyclic quadrilateral ABCE, opposite angles are supplementary: ∠ABC + ∠AEC = 180° ⇒ ∠AEC = 180° − 60° = 120°. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q3(ii) Geometry • Circles & Cyclic Quadrilaterals
4 Marks
In the given circle, O is centre, PR and PT are tangents touching at Q and S. MN is a diameter. ∠PQM = 42° and ∠PSM = 25°. Find: (a) ∠OQM   (b) ∠QNS   (c) ∠QOS   (d) ∠QMS.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) ∠OQM: 90° − 42° = 48°. [1 Mark]
(b) ∠QNS: In ΔOMS, ∠OMS = 90° − 25° = 65°. ∠QMS = 48° + 65° = 113°. Cyclic quad MQNS ⇒ ∠QNS = 180° − 113° = 67°. [1 Mark]
(c) ∠QOS: 2 × ∠QNS = 2 × 67° = 134°. [1 Mark]
(d) ∠QMS: 113°. [1 Mark]
