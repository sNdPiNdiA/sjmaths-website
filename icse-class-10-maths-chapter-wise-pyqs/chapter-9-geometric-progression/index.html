09. Geometric Progression (GP) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: T_n = a × r^{n−1}, 3 terms: a/r, a, ar

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

When solving for r from r² = k, consider both positive and negative roots unless the question specifies positive terms.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The 5th term of the Geometric Progression (G.P.) 2, 6, 18, 54, ... is:
(a) 162
(b) 486
(c) 144
(d) 324
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) 162
GP Term a = 2, common ratio r = 6/2 = 3.
T₅ = a × r⁴ = 2 × (3⁴) = 2 × 81 = 162.
ICSE 2026 Board Q2 GP Consecutive Terms
3 Marks
The fourth term of a Geometric Progression (G.P.) is 16 and its seventh term is 128. Find its:
(a) common ratio.
(b) first term.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Setup T₄ = ar³ = 16 ... (1)
T₇ = ar⁶ = 128 ... (2) [1 Mark]
Ratio Dividing (2) by (1): ar⁶ / ar³ = 128 / 16 ⇒ r³ = 8 ⇒ r = 2. [1 Mark]
First Term From (1): a(2)³ = 16 ⇒ 8a = 16 ⇒ a = 2. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q3 Geometric Progression
3 Marks
If 1701 is the nth term of the Geometric Progression (G.P.) 7, 21, 63 ..., find: (a) the value of 'n', (b) hence find the sum of the 'n' terms of the G.P.
View Verified Solution & Marking
a = 7, r = 21 / 7 = 3.
(a) Tn = a r^(n−1) ⇒ 1701 = 7 × 3^(n−1) ⇒ 3^(n−1) = 243 = 3&sup5 ⇒ n − 1 = 5 ⇒ n = 6. [1.5 Marks]
(b) S6 = a(r&sup6 − 1)/(r − 1) = 7(729 − 1)/2 = 7(728)/2 = 7 × 364 = 2548. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2024 Board Q4 Algebra • Geometric Progression
4 Marks
15, 30, 60, 120… are in G.P. (Geometric Progression).
(a) Find the nth term of this G.P. in terms of n.
(b) How many terms of the above G.P. will give the sum 945?
View Verified Solution & Marking
a = 15, r = 30/15 = 2.
(a) Tn = a × rn−1 = 15 × 2n−1. [1.5 Marks]
(b) Sn = 15(2n − 1) / (2 − 1) = 945 ⇒ 2n − 1 = 63 ⇒ 2n = 64 ⇒ n = 6 terms. [2.5 Marks]
ICSE 2020 Board Q5 Geometric Progression (Terms & Common Ratio)
4 Marks
The 4th, 6th and the last term of a geometric progression are 10, 40 and 640 respectively. If the common ratio is positive, find the first term, common ratio and the number of terms of the series.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Let the first term be $a$ and common ratio be $r$ ($r > 0$).
$T_4 = a r^3 = 10$
$T_6 = a r^5 = 40$
Dividing: $\frac{T_6}{T_4} = \frac{a r^5}{a r^3} = r^2 = \frac{40}{10} = 4 \implies \mathbf{r = 2}$ (since $r > 0$). [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 Find first term $a$:
$a r^3 = 10 \implies a (2)^3 = 10 \implies 8a = 10 \implies \mathbf{a = \frac{5}{4} = 1.25}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Find number of terms $n$:
Last term $T_n = a r^{n-1} = 640 \implies \frac{5}{4} \times 2^{n-1} = 640$
$$2^{n-1} = \frac{640 \times 4}{5} = 128 \times 4 = 512 = 2^9 \implies n - 1 = 9 \implies \mathbf{n = 10}$$
Answer: First term $a = \frac{5}{4}$, common ratio $r = 2$, number of terms $n = 10$. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2019 Board Q6 Geometric Progression: Number of Terms & Sum
3 Marks
The first and last term of a Geometrical Progression (G.P.) are 3 and 96 respectively. If the common ratio is 2, find:
  1. '$n$' the number of terms of the G.P.
  2. Sum of the $n$ terms.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 State parameters: $a = 3$, $l = T_n = 96$, $r = 2$.
Formula for $n\text{th}$ term: $T_n = a r^{n-1}$.
$96 = 3(2^{n-1}) \implies 2^{n-1} = \frac{96}{3} = 32$.
Since $32 = 2^5$: $n - 1 = 5 \implies \mathbf{n = 6}$.
$\therefore \text{Number of terms } n = \mathbf{6}$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 Find sum of the $n$ terms ($S_6$):
Formula: $S_n = \frac{a(r^n - 1)}{r - 1}$ for $r > 1$.
$S_6 = \frac{3(2^6 - 1)}{2 - 1} = \frac{3(64 - 1)}{1} = 3 \times 63 = \mathbf{189}$.
$\therefore \text{Sum of } 6 \text{ terms} = \mathbf{189}$. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q7 Geometric Progression: Sum and Product of 3 Terms
3 Marks
The sum of first three terms of a Geometric Progression (G.P.) is $\frac{21}{2}$ and their product is 27. Given $r < 1$, find its:
  1. common ratio.
  2. first three terms.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Let the three terms of G.P. be $\frac{a}{r}, a, ar$.
Product $= \left(\frac{a}{r}\right)(a)(ar) = a^3 = 27 \implies \mathbf{a = 3}$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Sum of three terms $= \frac{21}{2}$:
$\frac{3}{r} + 3 + 3r = \frac{21}{2} \implies 3\left(\frac{1}{r} + 1 + r\right) = \frac{21}{2} \implies \frac{r^2 + r + 1}{r} = \frac{7}{2}$
$2r^2 + 2r + 2 = 7r \implies 2r^2 - 5r + 2 = 0 \implies (2r - 1)(r - 2) = 0$.
$\implies r = \frac{1}{2}$ or $r = 2$.
Since given $r < 1$, choose $\mathbf{r = \frac{1}{2}}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Determine the three terms:
$T_1 = \frac{a}{r} = \frac{3}{1/2} = \mathbf{6}$
$T_2 = a = \mathbf{3}$
$T_3 = ar = 3 \times \frac{1}{2} = \mathbf{\frac{3}{2}}$ (or $1.5$).
Answer: Common ratio $r = \frac{1}{2}$, terms are $6, 3, \frac{3}{2}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2018 Board Q8 Geometric Progression
3 Marks
The 4th term of a G.P. is 16 and the 7th term is 128. Find the first term and common ratio of the series.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 General term $T_n = a r^{n-1}$:
$$T_4 = a r^3 = 16 \quad \text{--- (1)}$$ [1 Mark]
$$T_7 = a r^6 = 128 \quad \text{--- (2)}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Divide (2) by (1):
$$\frac{a r^6}{a r^3} = \frac{128}{16} \implies r^3 = 8 \implies \mathbf{r = 2}$$ Step 3 Substitute $r = 2$ in (1):
$$a(2^3) = 16 \implies 8a = 16 \implies \mathbf{a = 2}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: First term $a = 2$, Common ratio $r = 2$.
