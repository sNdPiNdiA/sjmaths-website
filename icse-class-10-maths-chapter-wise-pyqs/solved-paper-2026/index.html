The polynomial kx³ + 3x² − 11x − 6 when divided by (x + 1), leaves a remainder of 6.
(a) Find the value of k.
(b) Using the value of k factorise completely the polynomial kx³ + 3x² − 11x − 6.

k(−1)³ + 3(−1)² − 11(−1) − 6 = 6 ⇒ −k + 3 + 11 − 6 = 6 ⇒ −k + 8 = 6 ⇒ k = 2 [2M].

Part (b) [2M] Polynomial P(x) = 2x³ + 3x² − 11x − 6.

Factorising quadratic: 2x² + 6x + x + 3 = (2x + 1)(x + 3).
Complete factors = (x − 2)(2x + 1)(x + 3) [2M].

Prove that: (sec θ − cos θ)(cosec θ − sin θ) = sin θ cos θ
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Proof LHS = (sec θ − cos θ)(cosec θ − sin θ)
= (1/cos θ − cos θ)(1/sin θ − sin θ) [1M]
= [(1 − cos²θ) / cos θ] × [(1 − sin²θ) / sin θ]
= (sin²θ / cos θ) × (cos²θ / sin θ) [1M]
= sin θ cos θ = RHS. Hence proved. [1M]

(iii) [4 Marks] Ch 5: Quadratic Word Problem
The difference of two natural numbers is 5 and sum of their reciprocals is 3/10. Find the two numbers.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric

1/x + 1/(x + 5) = 3/10 ⇒ (2x + 5) / (x² + 5x) = 3/10

First number = 5, Second number = 5 + 5 = 10.
The two numbers are 5 and 10.
