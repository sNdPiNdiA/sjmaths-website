11. Reflection (Coordinate Geometry) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: M_x(x, y) = (x, -y), M_y(x, y) = (-x, y), M_O(x, y) = (-x, -y)

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Candidates lose marks by not stating scale at top-right of graph sheet and forgetting to write coordinates of reflected vertices on the plot.

ICSE 2026 Board Q8(iii) Graph & Reflection
4 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take 2 cm = 1 unit along both axes. Graphically represent parallelogram OABC, where O(0, 0), A(2, 3), B(5, 3) and C(3, 0).
Reflect OABC:
(a) on the x-axis and name its image as ODEC.
(b) through the origin and name its image as OIJH.
(c) on the y-axis and name its image as OFGA.
Parallelogram OABC plotted on grid with x-axis reflection ODEC
View Verified Solution & Marking
Plotting OABC Plot O(0,0), A(2,3), B(5,3), C(3,0) and join to form parallelogram OABC. [1 Mark]
Part (a) Reflection on x-axis Image of A(2,3) is D(2, −3); Image of B(5,3) is E(5, −3). O(0,0) and C(3,0) are invariant. Parallelogram: ODEC. [1 Mark]
Part (b) Reflection through origin O(0,0) is invariant. I(−2, −3), J(−5, −3), H(−3, 0). Image: OIJH. [1 Mark]
Part (c) Reflection on y-axis O(0,0) invariant, F(−2, 3), G(−5, 3), A invariant in y-coord. Image: OFGA. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q6(i) Coordinate Reflection & Geometry
5 Marks
Use a graph paper taking 2 cm = 1 unit along both axes:
(a) Plot A(1, 3), B(1, 2) and C(3, 0).
(b) Reflect A and B on the x-axis and name their images as E and D respectively.
(c) Reflect A and B through the origin and name their images as F and G respectively.
(d) Reflect A, B and C on the y-axis and name their images as J, I and H respectively.
(e) Join all points A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I and J in order and name the closed figure so formed.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) Plotted A(1, 3), B(1, 2), C(3, 0). [1 Mark]
(b) Reflections in x-axis: D(1, −2), E(1, −3). [1 Mark]
(c) Reflections in origin: F(−1, −3), G(−1, −2). [1 Mark]
(d) Reflections in y-axis: H(−3, 0), I(−1, 2), J(−1, 3). [1 Mark]
(e) Joining in order gives a symmetrical 10-sided polygon: Decagon (or arrowhead-star decagon). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q3(iii) Coordinate Geometry • Reflection on Graph
5 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take 2 cm = 1 unit.
(a) Plot A(0, 3), B(2, 1) and C(4, −1).
(b) Reflect point B and C in y-axis to get B' and C'.
(c) Reflect point A in line BB' to get A'.
(d) Plot and write coordinates of A'.
(e) Join ABA'B' and give the geometrical name of the figure.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) Points plotted. [1 Mark]
(b) B'(−2, 1), C'(−4, −1). [1 Mark]
(c) & (d) Line BB' is y = 1. Reflection of A(0, 3) across y = 1 is A'(0, −1). [1 Mark]
(e) Diagonals AA'=4 and BB'=4 are equal and perpendicular bisectors of each other. Figure is a Square (or Rhombus). [2 Marks]
ICSE 2023 Board Q3(iii) Coordinate Geometry & Reflection on Graph
5 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take 2 cm = 1 unit along both axes.
  1. Plot $A(0, 4)$, $B(1, 1)$ and $C(4, 0)$.
  2. Reflect $A$ and $B$ on the $x$-axis and name their images as $E$ and $D$ respectively. Write down coordinates of $E$ and $D$.
  3. Reflect $B$ through the origin and name it $F$. Write down coordinates of $F$.
  4. Reflect $B$ and $C$ on the $y$-axis and name their images as $H$ and $G$ respectively. Write down coordinates of $H$ and $G$.
  5. Join points $A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H$ and $A$ in order and name the closed figure so formed.
Figure: 8-sided symmetrical star polygon (Octagon) formed by reflections
View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) Correctly plotting points $A(0, 4)$, $B(1, 1)$, $C(4, 0)$ on graph scale.1 Mark

(b) Reflection in x-axis: $(x, y) \to (x, -y)$
Image of $A(0, 4)$ is $\mathbf{E(0, -4)}$.
Image of $B(1, 1)$ is $\mathbf{D(1, -1)}$.1 Mark

(c) Reflection through Origin: $(x, y) \to (-x, -y)$
Image of $B(1, 1)$ through origin is $\mathbf{F(-1, -1)}$.1 Mark

(d) Reflection in y-axis: $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$
Image of $B(1, 1)$ is $\mathbf{H(-1, 1)}$.
Image of $C(4, 0)$ is $\mathbf{G(-4, 0)}$.1 Mark

(e) Geometrical Name Joining $A \to B \to C \to D \to E \to F \to G \to H \to A$ forms an 8-sided closed figure with symmetrical diagonal axes.
Geometrical name: Octagon (or 8-pointed / 8-sided star polygon / Star Octagon).1 Mark
