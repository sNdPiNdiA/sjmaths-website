11. Reflection (Coordinate Geometry) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: M_x(x, y) = (x, -y), M_y(x, y) = (-x, y), M_O(x, y) = (-x, -y)

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Candidates lose marks by not stating scale at top-right of graph sheet and forgetting to write coordinates of reflected vertices on the plot.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Graph & Reflection
4 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take 2 cm = 1 unit along both axes. Graphically represent parallelogram OABC, where O(0, 0), A(2, 3), B(5, 3) and C(3, 0).
Reflect OABC:
(a) on the x-axis and name its image as ODEC.
(b) through the origin and name its image as OIJH.
(c) on the y-axis and name its image as OFGA.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Parallelogram OABC plotted on grid with x-axis reflection ODEC
Plotting OABC Plot O(0,0), A(2,3), B(5,3), C(3,0) and join to form parallelogram OABC. [1 Mark]
Part (a) Reflection on x-axis Image of A(2,3) is D(2, −3); Image of B(5,3) is E(5, −3). O(0,0) and C(3,0) are invariant. Parallelogram: ODEC. [1 Mark]
Part (b) Reflection through origin O(0,0) is invariant. I(−2, −3), J(−5, −3), H(−3, 0). Image: OIJH. [1 Mark]
Part (c) Reflection on y-axis O(0,0) invariant, F(−2, 3), G(−5, 3), A invariant in y-coord. Image: OFGA. [1 Mark]

(a) Plot A(1, 3), B(1, 2) and C(3, 0).
(b) Reflect A and B on the x-axis and name their images as E and D respectively.
(c) Reflect A and B through the origin and name their images as F and G respectively.
(d) Reflect A, B and C on the y-axis and name their images as J, I and H respectively.
(e) Join all points A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H, I and J in order and name the closed figure so formed.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) Plotted A(1, 3), B(1, 2), C(3, 0). [1 Mark]
(b) Reflections in x-axis: D(1, −2), E(1, −3). [1 Mark]
(c) Reflections in origin: F(−1, −3), G(−1, −2). [1 Mark]
(d) Reflections in y-axis: H(−3, 0), I(−1, 2), J(−1, 3). [1 Mark]

(b) Reflect point B and C in y-axis to get B' and C'.
(c) Reflect point A in line BB' to get A'.

View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) Points plotted. [1 Mark]
(b) B'(−2, 1), C'(−4, −1). [1 Mark]

ICSE 2023 Board Q4 Coordinate Geometry & Reflection on Graph
5 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take 2 cm = 1 unit along both axes.
  1. Plot $A(0, 4)$, $B(1, 1)$ and $C(4, 0)$.
  2. Reflect $A$ and $B$ on the $x$-axis and name their images as $E$ and $D$ respectively. Write down coordinates of $E$ and $D$.
  3. Reflect $B$ through the origin and name it $F$. Write down coordinates of $F$.
  4. Reflect $B$ and $C$ on the $y$-axis and name their images as $H$ and $G$ respectively. Write down coordinates of $H$ and $G$.
  5. Join points $A, B, C, D, E, F, G, H$ and $A$ in order and name the closed figure so formed.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Figure: 8-sided symmetrical star polygon (Octagon) formed by reflections
(a) Correctly plotting points $A(0, 4)$, $B(1, 1)$, $C(4, 0)$ on graph scale.1 Mark

(b) Reflection in x-axis: $(x, y) \to (x, -y)$
Image of $A(0, 4)$ is $\mathbf{E(0, -4)}$.
Image of $B(1, 1)$ is $\mathbf{D(1, -1)}$.1 Mark

(c) Reflection through Origin: $(x, y) \to (-x, -y)$
Image of $B(1, 1)$ through origin is $\mathbf{F(-1, -1)}$.1 Mark

(d) Reflection in y-axis: $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$
Image of $B(1, 1)$ is $\mathbf{H(-1, 1)}$.
Image of $C(4, 0)$ is $\mathbf{G(-4, 0)}$.1 Mark

(e) Geometrical Name Joining $A \to B \to C \to D \to E \to F \to G \to H \to A$ forms an 8-sided closed figure with symmetrical diagonal axes.
Geometrical name: Octagon (or 8-pointed / 8-sided star polygon / Star Octagon).1 Mark
ICSE 2020 Board Q5 Reflection on X-Axis & Polygon Identification
4 Marks
Use graph paper for this question. Take $1\text{ cm} = 1\text{ unit}$ on both x and y axes.
(i) Plot the following points: $A(-4, 0), B(-3, 2), C(0, 4), D(4, 1)$ and $E(7, 3)$.
(ii) Reflect the points $B, C, D$ and $E$ on the x-axis and name them as $B', C', D'$ and $E'$ respectively.
(iii) Join the points $A, B, C, D, E, E', D', C', B'$ and $A$ in order.
(iv) Name the closed figure formed.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Figure: 9-sided symmetrical polygon (Nonagon) formed by points and their x-axis reflections
(i) Points $A(-4, 0), B(-3, 2), C(0, 4), D(4, 1), E(7, 3)$ plotted accurately on graph sheet. [1 Mark]
(ii) Coordinates of reflected points across the x-axis ($M_x(x, y) = (x, -y)$):
$B'(-3, -2), \quad C'(0, -4), \quad D'(4, -1), \quad E'(7, -3)$. [1 Mark]
(iii) Join in order: $A \to B \to C \to D \to E \to E' \to D' \to C' \to B' \to A$. [1 Mark]
(iv) The figure has 9 sides and 9 vertices.
Name of closed figure: Nonagon (or Arrowhead Nonagon / 9-sided polygon). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q6 Coordinate Reflection: Heptagon Polygon on Graph
4 Marks
Use a graph sheet for this question. Take $1\text{ cm} = 1\text{ unit}$ along both $x$ and $y$ axis.
  1. Plot the following points: $A(0, 5), B(3, 0), C(1, 0) \text{ and } D(1, -5)$.
  2. Reflect the points $B, C \text{ and } D$ on the $y$ axis and name them as $B', C' \text{ and } D'$ respectively.
  3. Write down the coordinates of $B', C' \text{ and } D'$.
  4. Join the points $A, B, C, D, D', C', B', A$ in order and give a name to the closed figure $ABCDD'C'B'$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Vector Diagram: Reflection of points in the Y-axis forming an arrowhead heptagon
Step 1 (i) Plot original vertices on Cartesian grid: $A(0, 5), B(3, 0), C(1, 0), D(1, -5)$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 (ii) & (iii) Reflection in the Y-axis has coordinate mapping $(x, y) \xrightarrow{M_y} (-x, y)$:
$B(3, 0) \xrightarrow{M_y} \mathbf{B'(-3, 0)}$
$C(1, 0) \xrightarrow{M_y} \mathbf{C'(-1, 0)}$
$D(1, -5) \xrightarrow{M_y} \mathbf{D'(-1, -5)}$. [2 Marks]
Step 3 (iv) Join points $A \to B \to C \to D \to D' \to C' \to B' \to A$ in order.
The resulting closed polygon has 7 sides and 7 vertices ($A, B, C, D, D', C', B'$).
Name of closed figure: Heptagon (also accepted: Arrowhead or concave heptagon). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q7 Coordinate Geometry: Reflection & Origin Mapping
5 Marks
Use graph sheet for this question. Take $2\text{ cm} = 1\text{ unit}$ along the axes. Plot and write coordinates of:
  1. $A(2, 3)$ and $B(4, 5)$.
  2. image of $A$ and $B$ in the x-axis as $A'$ and $B'$.
  3. image of $A'$ in the y-axis as $A''$.
  4. name the single transformation that maps $A$ to $A''$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Vector Diagram: Reflection of A and B in x-axis followed by reflection of A' in y-axis to A''
Step 1 (a) Plot points: $A(2, 3)$ and $B(4, 5)$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 (b) Reflection in the x-axis has mapping $(x, y) \xrightarrow{M_x} (x, -y)$:
$\mathbf{A'(2, -3)}$ and $\mathbf{B'(4, -5)}$. [2 Marks]
Step 3 (c) Reflection of $A'(2, -3)$ in the y-axis has mapping $(x, y) \xrightarrow{M_y} (-x, y)$:
$\mathbf{A''(-2, -3)}$. [1 Mark]
Step 4 (d) Transformation mapping $A(2, 3) \to A''(-2, -3)$:
$(x, y) \to (-x, -y)$ represents a **Reflection in the Origin $(0, 0)$** (or rotation through $180^\circ$ about origin). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2018 Board Q8 Reflection on the y-axis
4 Marks
Use graph paper for this question (Take 2cm = 1unit along both x and y axis).
$ABCD$ is a quadrilateral whose vertices are $A(2,2)$, $B(2,-2)$, $C(0,-1)$ and $D(0,1)$.
(i) Reflect quadrilateral $ABCD$ on the y-axis and name it as $A'B'CD$.
(ii) Write down the coordinates of $A'$ and $B'$.
(iii) Name two points which are invariant under the above reflection.
(iv) Name the polygon $A'B'CD$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(i) Plotting Reflection in the y-axis maps $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$. Plot vertices $A(2,2), B(2,-2), C(0,-1), D(0,1)$ and connect to form $ABCD$. [1 Mark]
(ii) Coordinates
$$A'( -2, 2 ) \quad \text{and} \quad B'( -2, -2 )$$ [1 Mark]
(iii) Invariant Points Points that lie on the axis of reflection ($y$-axis, where $x = 0$) remain unchanged under reflection.
Hence, $C(0, -1)$ and $D(0, 1)$ are invariant points. [1 Mark]
(iv) Name of Polygon In $A'B'CD$:
$A'B'$ is vertical (from $(-2, -2)$ to $(-2, 2)$, length 4 units) and $DC$ is vertical along the y-axis (from $(0, -1)$ to $(0, 1)$, length 2 units).
Since $A'B' \parallel DC$, and non-parallel sides $A'D = B'C = \sqrt{2^2 + 1^2} = \sqrt{5}$, polygon $A'B'CD$ is an Isosceles Trapezium (or Trapezium). [1 Mark]
Answers: (ii) $A'(-2,2), B'(-2,-2)$, (iii) $C(0,-1)$ and $D(0,1)$, (iv) Isosceles Trapezium.
ICSE 2017 Board Q9 Coordinate Geometry - Reflection & Symmetry
5 Marks
Use a graph paper for this question (Take 2 cms = 1 unit on both x and y axis):
(i) Plot the following points: $A(0,4), B(2,3), C(1,1)$ and $D(2,0)$.
(ii) Reflect points $B, C, D$ on the y-axis and write down their coordinates. Name the images as $B', C', D'$ respectively.
(iii) Join the points $A, B, C, D, D', C', B'$ and $A$ in order, so as to form a closed figure. Write down the equation of the line of symmetry of the figure formed.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(i) Plotting Plot points $A(0,4), B(2,3), C(1,1), D(2,0)$ on the graph. [1.5 Marks]
(ii) Coordinates of Images Reflection across y-axis maps $(x, y) \to (-x, y)$:
$$B' = \mathbf{(-2, 3)}, \quad C' = \mathbf{(-1, 1)}, \quad D' = \mathbf{(-2, 0)}$$ [1.5 Marks]
(iii) Closed Figure & Symmetry Connecting $A - B - C - D - D' - C' - B' - A$ yields a symmetrical arrowhead figure.
The line of symmetry is the y-axis, whose equation is: $$\mathbf{x = 0}$$ [2 Marks]
Answers: (ii) $B'(-2,3), C'(-1,1), D'(-2,0)$; (iii) Equation of line of symmetry is $x = 0$.
