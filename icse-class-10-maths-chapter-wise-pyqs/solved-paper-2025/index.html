(iii) Trigonometric Identity Proof Ch 20: Trig Identities
Prove that: [(cot A + tan A − 1)(sin A + cos A)] / (sin³A + cos³A) = sec A · csc A.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Denominator: sin³A + cos³A = (sin A + cos A)(sin²A − sin A cos A + cos²A) = (sin A + cos A)(1 − sin A cos A).1.5 Marks

Cancelling common terms gives 1 / (sin A cos A) = sec A · csc A = RHS. Hence Proved.1 Mark
