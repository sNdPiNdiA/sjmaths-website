02. Banking (Recurring Deposit Accounts) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (R/100), MV = (P × n) + I

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Common error: Substituting time n in years instead of months in the RD formula. If time is 2 years, n must be 24.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
A man deposits ₹800 per month in a recurring deposit account for 1 year at 9% p.a. simple interest. The total interest earned at maturity is calculated using:
(a) ₹468
(b) ₹470
(c) ₹480
(d) ₹500
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) ₹468
Calculation P = ₹800, n = 12 months, R = 9%.
I = 800 × [12 × 13 / 24] × (9 / 100) = 800 × 6.5 × 0.09 = ₹468.
ICSE 2026 Board Q2 Short Answer
3 Marks
Rohit and Vinay both opened a recurring deposit account in a bank for 2 years at 8% simple interest. Vinay deposited ₹300 per month. On maturity, Rohit’s interest was ₹800 more than Vinay’s interest. Find the:
(a) interest earned by Vinay.
(b) sum deposited by Rohit every month.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Time n = 2 years = 24 months, R = 8%.
Vinay’s Interest: IV = 300 × [24 × 25 / 24] × (8 / 100) = 300 × 25 × 0.08 = ₹600. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Rohit’s Interest: IR = IV + 800 = 600 + 800 = ₹1,400.
Let Rohit’s monthly deposit be P.
⇒ 1,400 = P × [24 × 25 / 24] × (8 / 100) = P × 25 × 0.08 = 2P.
⇒ P = 1,400 / 2 = ₹700 per month. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q3 Multiple Choice Question

Mr. Anuj deposits ₹500 per month for 18 months in a recurring deposit account at a certain rate. If he earns ₹570 as interest at the time of maturity, then his matured amount is:
(a) ₹(500 × 18 + 570)
(b) ₹(500 × 19 + 570)
(c) ₹(500 × 18 × 19 + 570)
(d) ₹(500 × 9 × 19 + 570)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Maturity Value MV = Total Principal Deposited + Total Interest = (P × n) + I = ₹(500 × 18 + 570). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q4 Recurring Deposit Account
4 Marks
Mrs. Rao deposited ₹250 per month in a recurring deposit account for a period of 3 years. She received ₹10,110 at the time of maturity. Find:
(a) the rate of interest.
(b) how much more interest Mrs. Rao will receive if she had deposited ₹50 more per month at the same rate of interest and for the same time.
View Verified Solution & Marking
P = ₹250, n = 3 × 12 = 36 months, MV = ₹10,110.
Total deposited = P × n = 250 × 36 = ₹9,000.
Interest earned I = MV − (P × n) = 10,110 − 9,000 = ₹1,110. [1 Mark]

(a) I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (r / 100)
1110 = 250 × (36 × 37 / 24) × (r / 100) = 2.5 × 55.5 × r = 138.75 r ⇒ r = 8% per annum. [2 Marks]

(b) If deposit is ₹50 more ⇒ Extra interest = (50 / 250) × ₹1110 = ₹222. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q5 Commercial Mathematics • Recurring Deposit
3 Marks
Suresh has a recurring deposit account in a bank. He deposits ₹2000 per month and the bank pays interest at the rate of 8% per annum. If he gets ₹1040 as interest at the time of maturity, find in years total time for which the account was held.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Monthly deposit P = ₹2000, R = 8% p.a., Interest I = ₹1040.
Formula: I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (R/100) [1 Mark]
1040 = 2000 × [n(n+1)/24] × (8/100) = (20/3) × n(n+1)
n(n+1) = (1040 × 3)/20 = 156 ⇒ n2 + n − 156 = 0 [1 Mark]
(n + 13)(n − 12) = 0 ⇒ n = 12 months.
Total time in years = 12/12 = 1 year. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q6 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Naveen deposits ₹800 every month in a recurring deposit account for 6 months. If he receives ₹4,884 at the time of maturity, then the interest he earns is:
(a) ₹84
(b) ₹42
(c) ₹24
(d) ₹284
View Verified Solution & Marking
Total principal deposited $P \times n = 800 \times 6 = ₹4,800$.
Maturity Value $(MV) = ₹4,884$.
Interest earned = $MV - (P \times n) = 4884 - 4800 = ₹84$.
Correct Option: (a) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q7 Recurring Deposit (Maturity & Rate of Interest)
4 Marks
Salman deposits ₹1,000 every month in a recurring deposit account for 2 years. If he receives ₹26,000 on maturity, find:
  1. the total interest Salman earns.
  2. the rate of interest.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Monthly deposit $P = ₹1,000$. Time period $n = 2\text{ years} = 24\text{ months}$.
Maturity value $MV = ₹26,000$.

Part (a) Total deposited amount $= P \times n = 1000 \times 24 = ₹24,000$.
Total Interest earned $I = MV - (P \times n) = 26,000 - 24,000 = \mathbf{₹2,000}$.2 Marks

Part (b) Recurring deposit interest formula:
$I = \frac{P \times n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{r}{100}$
$2000 = \frac{1000 \times 24 \times 25}{24} \times \frac{r}{100}$1 Mark
$2000 = 10 \times 25 \times r \implies 2000 = 250r \implies r = \frac{2000}{250} = \mathbf{8\%}$.
Rate of interest = 8% per annum.1 Mark
ICSE 2020 Board Q8 Recurring Deposit Account (Find Rate of Interest)
3 Marks
Mr. Sonu has a recurring deposit account and deposits ₹750 per month for 2 years. If he gets ₹19,125 at the time of maturity, find the rate of interest.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Monthly deposit $P = ₹750$, Time $n = 2 \text{ years} = 24 \text{ months}$.
Step 1 Total deposit $= P \times n = 750 \times 24 = ₹18,000$.
Maturity Value $= ₹19,125 \implies$ Total Interest $I = 19,125 - 18,000 = \mathbf{₹1,125}$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Apply RD interest formula:
$$I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{R}{100} \implies 1125 = 750 \times \frac{24 \times 25}{24} \times \frac{R}{100}$$ [1 Mark]
$$1125 = 750 \times 25 \times \frac{R}{100} = 187.5 R \implies R = \frac{1125}{187.5} = \mathbf{6\% \text{ p.a.}}$$
Answer: Rate of interest = 6% per annum. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q9 Recurring Deposit Account: Finding Monthly Deposit
3 Marks
Rekha opened a recurring deposit account for 20 months. The rate of interest is $9\%$ per annum and Rekha receives ₹ 441 as interest at the time of maturity. Find the amount Rekha deposited each month.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 State parameters and formula:
Number of months $n = 20$.
Rate of interest $r = 9\% \text{ p.a.}$, Interest $I = \text{₹ } 441$.
Formula: $I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{r}{100}$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Substitute given values into equation:
$441 = P \times \frac{20(21)}{24} \times \frac{9}{100}$
$441 = P \times \frac{420}{24} \times \frac{9}{100} = P \times 17.5 \times 0.09 = P \times 1.575$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Solve for monthly deposit $P$:
$P = \frac{441}{1.575} = \frac{441000}{1575} = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 280}$.
Answer: Rekha deposited ₹ 280 each month. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q10 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
A person deposited ₹ 500 per month in a recurring deposit account for one year at $r\%$ per annum. The rate of interest is revised to $2r\%$. The interest earned by the person compared to the original amount of interest will be:
(a) one-fourth
(b) unchanged
(c) half
(d) double
View Verified Solution & Marking
Solution Formula for RD interest: $I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{24} \times \frac{r}{100}$.
Since $I \propto r$ when $P$ and $n$ remain constant, doubling the rate from $r\%$ to $2r\%$ doubles the interest earned.
Correct Answer: (d) double [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q11 Banking: Recurring Deposit Comparison
4 Marks
Anamika deposited ₹ 300 per month in a cumulative deposit account with a bank for 3 years. Her brother Sanjeev started depositing ₹ 500 per month for 2 years in the same scheme. The bank paid $10\%$ simple interest per annum to both. At the time of maturity, find:
  1. the interest earned by Anamika.
  2. who got more money as interest and how much?
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 (a) Interest earned by Anamika:
$P = \text{₹ } 300, n = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ months}, r = 10\%$.
$I_{\text{Anamika}} = 300 \times \frac{36 \times 37}{24} \times \frac{10}{100} = 300 \times 55.5 \times 0.1 = \mathbf{\text{₹ } 1665}$. [2 Marks]
Step 2 Interest earned by Sanjeev:
$P = \text{₹ } 500, n = 2 \times 12 = 24\text{ months}, r = 10\%$.
$I_{\text{Sanjeev}} = 500 \times \frac{24 \times 25}{24} \times \frac{10}{100} = 500 \times 25 \times 0.1 = \text{₹ } 1250$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 (b) Compare interest earned:
$I_{\text{Anamika}} - I_{\text{Sanjeev}} = 1665 - 1250 = \text{₹ } 415$.
$\mathbf{\text{Anamika got more interest by ₹ 415}}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2018 Board Q12 Recurring Deposit Account
3 Marks
Sonia had a recurring deposit account in a bank and deposited ₹600 per month for $2\frac{1}{2}$ years. If the rate of interest was $10\%$ p.a., find the maturity value of this account.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Given parameters:
Monthly deposit $P = ₹600$
Time $n = 2\frac{1}{2}\text{ years} = 2.5 \times 12 = 30\text{ months}$
Rate of interest $R = 10\%$ p.a.
Step 2 Calculate total simple interest earned:
$$I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{R}{100}$$ $$I = 600 \times \frac{30 \times 31}{24} \times \frac{10}{100} = 600 \times \frac{930}{24} \times \frac{1}{10} = 25 \times 93 = ₹2,325$$ [1.5 Marks]
Step 3 Total sum deposited and maturity value:
Total principal deposited $= P \times n = 600 \times 30 = ₹18,000$ [0.5 Mark]
$$\text{Maturity Value } (MV) = (P \times n) + I = 18,000 + 2,325 = \mathbf{₹20,325}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: The maturity value of the account is ₹20,325.
ICSE 2018 Board Q13 Recurring Deposit Duration
3 Marks
Priyanka has a recurring deposit account of ₹1000 per month at $10\%$ per annum. If she gets ₹5550 as interest at the time of maturity, find the total time for which the account was held.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 RD Formula for interest: $I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{R}{100}$.
Here $P = 1000, R = 10\%, I = ₹5550$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Substitute values:
$$5550 = 1000 \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{24} \times \frac{10}{100} = \frac{100 \times n(n + 1)}{24}$$ $$5550 \times 24 = 100(n^2 + n) \implies 133200 = 100(n^2 + n)$$ $$n^2 + n - 1332 = 0$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Factorise the quadratic equation ($36 \times 37 = 1332$):
$$(n + 37)(n - 36) = 0$$ Since time cannot be negative, $n = 36\text{ months}$.
$$\text{Time} = \frac{36}{12} = \mathbf{3\text{ years}}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: 36 months (or 3 years).
ICSE 2017 Board Q14 Compound Interest and Loan Repayment
3 Marks
Jaya borrowed ₹50,000 for 2 years. The rates of interest for two successive years are 12% and 15% respectively. She repays ₹33,000 at the end of the first year. Find the amount she must pay at the end of the second year to clear her debt.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Year 1 Principal $P_1 = ₹50,000$, Rate $R_1 = 12\%$.
$$\text{Interest for Year 1 } (I_1) = \frac{50,000 \times 12 \times 1}{100} = ₹6,000$$ $$\text{Amount at end of Year 1} = 50,000 + 6,000 = ₹56,000$$ [1 Mark]
Repayment Repaid at end of Year 1 $= ₹33,000$.
$$\text{Principal for Year 2 } (P_2) = 56,000 - 33,000 = ₹23,000$$ [1 Mark]
Year 2 Rate $R_2 = 15\%$.
$$\text{Interest for Year 2 } (I_2) = \frac{23,000 \times 15 \times 1}{100} = ₹3,450$$ $$\text{Amount to clear debt} = 23,000 + 3,450 = \mathbf{₹26,450}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: Jaya must pay ₹26,450 at the end of the second year.

ICSE 2017 Board Q16 Recurring Deposit Monthly Deposit & Maturity
3 Marks
Mr. Richard has a recurring deposit account in a bank for 3 years at 7.5% p. a. simple interest. If he gets ₹8325 as interest at the time of maturity, find:
(i) The monthly deposit
(ii) The maturity value.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Time $n = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ months}$, Rate $R = 7.5\%$, Interest $I = ₹8,325$.
$$I = P \times \frac{n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{R}{100}$$ $$8325 = P \times \frac{36 \times 37}{24} \times \frac{7.5}{100} = P \times 55.5 \times 0.075 = 4.1625 P$$ [1 Mark]
(i) Monthly Deposit P
$$P = \frac{8325}{4.1625} = \mathbf{₹2,000}$$ [1 Mark]
(ii) Maturity Value MV Total deposited $= 2000 \times 36 = ₹72,000$.
$$MV = (P \times n) + I = 72,000 + 8,325 = \mathbf{₹80,325}$$ [1 Mark]
Answers: (i) Monthly deposit $= ₹2,000$, (ii) Maturity value $= ₹80,325$.
