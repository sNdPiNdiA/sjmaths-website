02. Banking (Recurring Deposit Accounts) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (R/100), MV = (P × n) + I

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Common error: Substituting time n in years instead of months in the RD formula. If time is 2 years, n must be 24.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1(iv) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
A man deposits ₹800 per month in a recurring deposit account for 1 year at 9% p.a. simple interest. The total interest earned at maturity is calculated using:
(a) ₹468
(b) ₹470
(c) ₹480
(d) ₹500
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) ₹468
Calculation P = ₹800, n = 12 months, R = 9%.
I = 800 × [12 × 13 / 24] × (9 / 100) = 800 × 6.5 × 0.09 = ₹468.
ICSE 2026 Board Q8(i) Short Answer
3 Marks
Rohit and Vinay both opened a recurring deposit account in a bank for 2 years at 8% simple interest. Vinay deposited ₹300 per month. On maturity, Rohit’s interest was ₹800 more than Vinay’s interest. Find the:
(a) interest earned by Vinay.
(b) sum deposited by Rohit every month.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Time n = 2 years = 24 months, R = 8%.
Vinay’s Interest: IV = 300 × [24 × 25 / 24] × (8 / 100) = 300 × 25 × 0.08 = ₹600. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Rohit’s Interest: IR = IV + 800 = 600 + 800 = ₹1,400.
Let Rohit’s monthly deposit be P.
⇒ 1,400 = P × [24 × 25 / 24] × (8 / 100) = P × 25 × 0.08 = 2P.
⇒ P = 1,400 / 2 = ₹700 per month. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q1(ii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Mr. Anuj deposits ₹500 per month for 18 months in a recurring deposit account at a certain rate. If he earns ₹570 as interest at the time of maturity, then his matured amount is:
(a) ₹(500 × 18 + 570)
(b) ₹(500 × 19 + 570)
(c) ₹(500 × 18 × 19 + 570)
(d) ₹(500 × 9 × 19 + 570)
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Maturity Value MV = Total Principal Deposited + Total Interest = (P × n) + I = ₹(500 × 18 + 570). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q2(ii) Recurring Deposit Account
4 Marks
Mrs. Rao deposited ₹250 per month in a recurring deposit account for a period of 3 years. She received ₹10,110 at the time of maturity. Find:
(a) the rate of interest.
(b) how much more interest Mrs. Rao will receive if she had deposited ₹50 more per month at the same rate of interest and for the same time.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
P = ₹250, n = 3 × 12 = 36 months, MV = ₹10,110.
Total deposited = P × n = 250 × 36 = ₹9,000.
Interest earned I = MV − (P × n) = 10,110 − 9,000 = ₹1,110. [1 Mark]

(a) I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (r / 100)
1110 = 250 × (36 × 37 / 24) × (r / 100) = 2.5 × 55.5 × r = 138.75 r ⇒ r = 8% per annum. [2 Marks]

(b) If deposit is ₹50 more ⇒ Extra interest = (50 / 250) × ₹1110 = ₹222. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q4(i) Commercial Mathematics • Recurring Deposit
3 Marks
Suresh has a recurring deposit account in a bank. He deposits ₹2000 per month and the bank pays interest at the rate of 8% per annum. If he gets ₹1040 as interest at the time of maturity, find in years total time for which the account was held.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Monthly deposit P = ₹2000, R = 8% p.a., Interest I = ₹1040.
Formula: I = P × [n(n+1)/(2 × 12)] × (R/100) [1 Mark]
1040 = 2000 × [n(n+1)/24] × (8/100) = (20/3) × n(n+1)
n(n+1) = (1040 × 3)/20 = 156 ⇒ n2 + n − 156 = 0 [1 Mark]
(n + 13)(n − 12) = 0 ⇒ n = 12 months.
Total time in years = 12/12 = 1 year. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q1(xiv) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Naveen deposits ₹800 every month in a recurring deposit account for 6 months. If he receives ₹4,884 at the time of maturity, then the interest he earns is:
(a) ₹84
(b) ₹42
(c) ₹24
(d) ₹284
View Verified Solution & Marking
Total principal deposited $P \times n = 800 \times 6 = ₹4,800$.
Maturity Value $(MV) = ₹4,884$.
Interest earned = $MV - (P \times n) = 4884 - 4800 = ₹84$.
Correct Option: (a) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q2(ii) Recurring Deposit (Maturity & Rate of Interest)
4 Marks
Salman deposits ₹1,000 every month in a recurring deposit account for 2 years. If he receives ₹26,000 on maturity, find:
  1. the total interest Salman earns.
  2. the rate of interest.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Monthly deposit $P = ₹1,000$. Time period $n = 2\text{ years} = 24\text{ months}$.
Maturity value $MV = ₹26,000$.

Part (a) Total deposited amount $= P \times n = 1000 \times 24 = ₹24,000$.
Total Interest earned $I = MV - (P \times n) = 26,000 - 24,000 = \mathbf{₹2,000}$.2 Marks

Part (b) Recurring deposit interest formula:
$I = \frac{P \times n(n + 1)}{2 \times 12} \times \frac{r}{100}$
$2000 = \frac{1000 \times 24 \times 25}{24} \times \frac{r}{100}$1 Mark
$2000 = 10 \times 25 \times r \implies 2000 = 250r \implies r = \frac{2000}{250} = \mathbf{8\%}$.
Rate of interest = 8% per annum.1 Mark
