22. Measures of Central Tendency (Mean, Median, Mode) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: Step-Deviation: Mean = A + [(∑fd / ∑f) × i], Empirical: Mode = 3 Median − 2 Mean

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Check if class intervals are continuous (e.g. 10–19 vs 10–20). If discrete, subtract 0.5 from lower limit and add 0.5 to upper limit.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1(iii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
If the mean and mode of a moderately asymmetrical distribution are 24 and 12 respectively, then its median is:
(a) 18
(b) 20
(c) 22
(d) 16
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (b) 20
Empirical Formula Mode = 3 × Median − 2 × Mean
⇒ 12 = 3(Median) − 2(24) = 3(Median) − 48
⇒ 3(Median) = 60 ⇒ Median = 20.
ICSE 2026 Board Q6(ii) Mean by Step-Deviation
3 Marks
Calculate the arithmetic mean of the following distribution using the Step-Deviation method:
Class: 0–10 (f=5), 10–20 (f=8), 20–30 (f=15), 30–40 (f=12), 40–50 (f=10)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Table Setup Assumed Mean A = 25, class width i = 10.
• 0–10: x=5, d=(5−25)/10 = −2, f=5, fd=−10
• 10–20: x=15, d=−1, f=8, fd=−8
• 20–30: x=25, d=0, f=15, fd=0
• 30–40: x=35, d=1, f=12, fd=12
• 40–50: x=45, d=2, f=10, fd=20 [1.5 Marks]
Summation ∑f = 50, ∑fd = (−18 + 32) = 14.
Mean = A + [(∑fd / ∑f) × i] = 25 + [(14 / 50) × 10] = 25 + 2.8 = 27.8. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q1(xv) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Assertion (A): The mean of first 9 natural numbers is 4.5.
Reason (R): Mean = (Sum of observations) / (Total number of observations).
(a) (A) is true, (R) is false
(b) (A) is false, (R) is true
(c) Both true, (R) is correct reason
(d) Both true, (R) is incorrect reason
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Sum of first 9 natural numbers (1 to 9) = 9(10)/2 = 45. Mean = 45 / 9 = 5 ≠ 4.5. Hence Assertion (A) is false and Reason (R) is true. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q8(ii) Measures of Central Tendency (Mean & Median)
3 Marks
For the frequency distribution (x: 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16; f: 3, 2, 2, 6, 3, 5, 3), find: (a) mean to nearest whole number, (b) median.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
∑f = 24. ∑fx = 30 + 22 + 24 + 78 + 42 + 75 + 48 = 319.
(a) Mean = 319 / 24 ≈ 13.29 ⇒ 13 (nearest whole number). [1.5 Marks]
(b) N = 24 (even) ⇒ Median = (12th + 13th value)/2. Looking at cumulative frequencies: cf for 10=3, 11=5, 12=7, 13=13. Both 12th and 13th values lie in x = 13. Median = 13. [1.5 Marks]

The median of observations arranged in ascending order is 64. Find x:
27, 31, 46, 52, x, x + 4, 71, 79, 85, 90
(a) 60
(b) 61
(c) 62
(d) 66
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Correct Option: (c) 62
n = 10 ⇒ Median = ½ [x + (x + 4)] = x + 2.
x + 2 = 64 ⇒ x = 62. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q4(ii) Statistics • Step-Deviation Mean
3 Marks
Duration (min): 100–110 (5), 110–120 (10), 120–130 (17), 130–140 (8), 140–150 (6), 150–160 (4). Using step-deviation method, find the mean duration.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Assumed Mean A = 125, i = 10. Σf = 50, Σfu = −10 − 10 + 0 + 8 + 12 + 12 = 12. [1.5 Marks]
Mean = A + [(Σfu/Σf) × i] = 125 + [(12/50) × 10] = 125 + 2.4 = 127.4 minutes. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2023 Board Q1(xii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Which of the following cannot be determined graphically for a grouped frequency distribution?
(a) Median
(b) Mode
(c) Quartiles
(d) Mean
View Verified Solution & Marking
Median and Quartiles can be obtained from an Ogive (cumulative frequency curve). Mode is determined from a Histogram. The Mean requires arithmetic computation and cannot be read directly from standard curves. Correct Option: (d) [1 Mark]
