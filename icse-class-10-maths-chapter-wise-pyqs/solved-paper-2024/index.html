(ii) Factorisation (Remainder Theorem) Chapter 6
What must be subtracted from the polynomial x3 + x2 − 2x + 1, so that the result is exactly divisible by (x − 3)?
(a) −31
(b) −30
(c) 30
(d) 31
View Marking Scheme Solution
Correct Option: (d) 31
Let f(x) = x3 + x2 − 2x + 1.
By the Remainder Theorem, the remainder when f(x) is divided by (x − 3) is f(3):

(i) Trigonometrical Identities • Algebraic Factoring 4 Marks
Factorize: sin3θ + cos3θ.
Hence, prove the following identity:
[ (sin3θ + cos3θ) / (sinθ + cosθ) ] + sinθ cosθ = 1
Official Marking Scheme Solution
Step 1: Factorize sin3θ + cos3θ
Using algebraic identity a3 + b3 = (a + b)(a2 − ab + b2):
sin3θ + cos3θ = (sinθ + cosθ)(sin2θ − sinθ cosθ + cos2θ)
Since sin2θ + cos2θ = 1:
sin3θ + cos3θ = (sinθ + cosθ)(1 − sinθ cosθ). 2 Marks

Step 2: Prove the given identity
L.H.S. = [ (sin3θ + cos3θ) / (sinθ + cosθ) ] + sinθ cosθ
Substitute the factorized expression into L.H.S.:
L.H.S. = [ (sinθ + cosθ)(1 − sinθ cosθ) / (sinθ + cosθ) ] + sinθ cosθ 1 Mark
Cancelling (sinθ + cosθ):
= (1 − sinθ cosθ) + sinθ cosθ
= 1 = R.H.S. (Hence proved). 1 Mark

(d) Area ratio ΔAPD : ΔBPC:
Ratio of areas of two similar triangles is equal to the square of the ratio of their corresponding sides:
Area(ΔAPD) / Area(ΔBPC) = (AD / BC)2 = (6 / 2.4)2 = (2.5)2 = 6.25 = 25/4.

In the given diagram, an isosceles ΔABC is inscribed in a circle with centre O. PQ is a tangent to the circle at C. OM is perpendicular to chord AC and ∠COM = 65°.

In ΔAOC, OA = OC (radii of the circle).
Since OM ⊥ AC, ΔOMA ≅ ΔOMC (RHS criterion), so ∠AOM = ∠COM = 65°.
Central angle ∠AOC = 65° + 65° = 130°.

(a) Find ∠ABC:
The angle subtended by an arc at the circumference is half the angle subtended by it at the centre:
∠ABC = ½ ∠AOC = ½ × 130° = 65°. 1 Mark

(b) Find ∠BAC:
Since ΔABC is isosceles with AB = AC:
∠ACB = ∠ABC = 65° (angles opposite to equal sides).
In ΔABC: ∠BAC = 180° − (∠ABC + ∠ACB) = 180° − (65° + 65°) = 50°. 1 Mark

(c) Find ∠BCQ:
By the Alternate Segment Theorem, the angle between chord BC and tangent CQ is equal to the angle subtended by chord BC in the alternate segment:
∠BCQ = ∠BAC = 50°. 1 Mark

(i) Factorisation • Factor Theorem & Complete Factorisation 3 Marks
The polynomial 3x3 + 8x2 − 15x + k has (x − 1) as a factor. Find the value of k. Hence factorize the resulting polynomial completely.
Official Marking Scheme Solution
Let f(x) = 3x3 + 8x2 − 15x + k.
Since (x − 1) is a factor, f(1) = 0:
3(1)3 + 8(1)2 − 15(1) + k = 0
3 + 8 − 15 + k = 0 ⇒ −4 + k = 0 ⇒ k = 4. 1 Mark

Polynomial is 3x3 + 8x2 − 15x + 4.
Dividing f(x) by (x − 1):
3x3 + 8x2 − 15x + 4 = (x − 1)(3x2 + 11x − 4). 1 Mark
Factoring the quadratic quotient: 3x2 + 12x − x − 4 = 3x(x + 4) − 1(x + 4) = (3x − 1)(x + 4).
Complete factorisation: (x − 1)(3x − 1)(x + 4). 1 Mark
(ii) Probability • Word Letter Cards 3 Marks
The following letters A, D, M, N, O, S, U, Y of the English alphabet are written on separate cards and put in a box. The cards are well shuffled and one card is drawn at random. What is the probability that the card drawn is a letter of the word:
(a) MONDAY?
(b) which does not appear in MONDAY?
(c) which appears both in SUNDAY and MONDAY?
Official Marking Scheme Solution
Sample space S = {A, D, M, N, O, S, U, Y} ⇒ Total outcomes n(S) = 8.

Part (a) Letter of the word MONDAY:
Letters of MONDAY present in sample space: {A, D, M, N, O, Y} ⇒ 6 cards.
P(letter of MONDAY) = 6/8 = 3/4. 1 Mark

Part (b) Letter which does NOT appear in MONDAY:
Remaining letters in S: {S, U} ⇒ 2 cards.
P(not in MONDAY) = 2/8 = 1/4. 1 Mark

Part (c) Letter appearing in BOTH SUNDAY and MONDAY:
Common letters between SUNDAY and MONDAY: {A, D, N, Y}.
All 4 letters are present in S: {A, D, N, Y} ⇒ 4 cards.
P(in both words) = 4/8 = 1/2. 1 Mark
(iii) Mensuration • Sphere Capacity Transferred to Cylinder 4 Marks
Oil is stored in a spherical vessel occupying 3/4 of its full capacity. Radius of this spherical vessel is 28 cm. This oil is then poured into a cylindrical vessel with a radius of 21 cm. Find the height of the oil in the cylindrical vessel (correct to the nearest cm). (Take π = 22/7)
Official Marking Scheme Solution
Radius of sphere R = 28 cm. Radius of cylinder r = 21 cm.
Full capacity of spherical vessel = 4/3 π R3.
Volume of oil = 3/4 × (4/3 π R3) = π R3. 1.5 Marks
Volume of oil in cylindrical vessel = π r2 h.
Equating volumes:
π r2 h = π R3 ⇒ r2 h = R3 1 Mark
(21)2 × h = (28)3
