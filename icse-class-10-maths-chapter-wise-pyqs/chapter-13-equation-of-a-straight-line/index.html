13. Equation of a Straight Line Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: y − y₁ = m(x − x₁), m = −A/B, Perpendicular: m₁ × m₂ = −1

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

For perpendicular lines, remember m₂ = −1 / m₁. Omitting the negative sign is a very frequent error.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1(v) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The equation of a line is 3x − 4y + 12 = 0. Its slope (m) and y-intercept (c) are:
(a) m = 3/4, c = 3
(b) m = −3/4, c = −3
(c) m = 4/3, c = 12
(d) m = 3/4, c = −3
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) m = 3/4, c = 3
Slope-Intercept Form 4y = 3x + 12 ⇒ y = (3/4)x + 3.
Comparing with y = mx + c: slope m = 3/4 and y-intercept c = 3.
ICSE 2026 Board Q9(ii) Graph & Line Equation
3 Marks
Use the given graph to answer the questions:
(a) Write the co-ordinates of points A, B and C.
(b) Find the equation of a line passing through the mid-point of AC and parallel to AB.
ΔABC on grid with midpoint M(5, 5) of AC and parallel line 6x − 5y = 5
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) From the graph:
Point A = (4, 8), Point B = (−1, 2), Point C = (6, 2). [1 Mark]

Part (b) Slope of line AB: m = (2 − 8) / (−1 − 4) = −6 / −5 = 6/5.
Midpoint of AC: M = [ (4 + 6)/2, (8 + 2)/2 ] = (5, 5). [1 Mark]
Since the line is parallel to AB, its slope is also 6/5.
Equation using point-slope form: y − 5 = (6/5)(x − 5)
⇒ 5(y − 5) = 6(x − 5) ⇒ 5y − 25 = 6x − 30
⇒ 6x − 5y − 5 = 0 (or 6x − 5y = 5). [1 Mark]

ICSE 2025 Board Q3(iii)(c,d) Straight Lines & Parallelogram
3 Marks
In parallelogram ABCD with vertices A(3, 3), B(0, −3), C(−4, −3), D(−1, 3):
(c) find slope of sides CB and DA and verify that they represent parallel lines.
(d) find the equation of diagonal AC.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(c) Slope CB = (−3 − (−3)) / (0 − (−4)) = 0. Slope DA = (3 − 3) / (3 − (−1)) = 0. Since m_CB = m_DA = 0, sides CB and DA are parallel horizontal lines. [1.5 Marks]
(d) Slope of AC = (3 − (−3)) / (3 − (−4)) = 6/7. Equation: y − 3 = 6/7(x − 3) ⇒ 7y − 21 = 6x − 18 ⇒ 6x − 7y + 3 = 0. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q7(ii) Straight Line Intercepts & Perpendicular Lines
3 Marks
Line PQ cuts off intercepts of 5 units and 3 units along x-axis and y-axis respectively (P on positive x-axis, Q on negative y-axis). Line RS is perpendicular to PQ and passes through origin. Find: (a) coordinates of P and Q, (b) equation of line RS.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) P lies on positive x-axis ⇒ P(5, 0); Q lies on negative y-axis ⇒ Q(0, −3). [1 Mark]
(b) Slope of PQ = (−3 − 0)/(0 − 5) = 3/5.
Since RS ⊥ PQ, slope of RS = −5/3. Line RS passes through (0, 0): y = −5/3 x ⇒ 5x + 3y = 0. [2 Marks]

Points A(x, y), B(3, −2) and C(4, −5) are collinear. The value of y in terms of x is:
(a) 3x − 11
(b) 11 − 3x
(c) 3x − 7
(d) 7 − 3x
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Correct Option: (d) 7 − 3x
Slope BC = (−5 − (−2))/(4 − 3) = −3.
Slope AB = (y + 2)/(x − 3) = −3 ⇒ y + 2 = −3x + 9 ⇒ y = 7 − 3x. [1 Mark]

(b) Find the equation of a line parallel to 2y = 6x + 7 and passing through (−1, 1).
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) m1 = k, m2 = 3. Since perpendicular: k × 3 = −1 ⇒ k = −1/3. [1.5 Marks]
(b) Slope = 3, point (−1, 1): y − 1 = 3(x + 1) ⇒ y = 3x + 4. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2023 Board Q4(ii) Midpoint & Equation of a Median Line
3 Marks
$ABC$ is a triangle whose vertices are $A(1, -1)$, $B(0, 4)$ and $C(-6, 4)$. $D$ is the midpoint of $BC$. Find the:
  1. coordinates of $D$.
  2. equation of the median $AD$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Midpoint Formula: $D(x, y) = \left(\frac{x_B + x_C}{2}, \frac{y_B + y_C}{2}\right)$.
$D = \left(\frac{0 + (-6)}{2}, \frac{4 + 4}{2}\right) = \left(\frac{-6}{2}, \frac{8}{2}\right) = \mathbf{(-3, 4)}$.1.5 Marks

Part (b) Slope of median $AD$ passing through $A(1, -1)$ and $D(-3, 4)$:
$m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{4 - (-1)}{-3 - 1} = \frac{5}{-4} = -\frac{5}{4}$.
Equation using point-slope form $y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)$ with $A(1, -1)$:
$y - (-1) = -\frac{5}{4}(x - 1) \implies 4(y + 1) = -5(x - 1) \implies 4y + 4 = -5x + 5$.
Equation of median $AD$: $5x + 4y - 1 = 0$ (or $5x + 4y = 1$).1.5 Marks
ICSE 2023 Board Q6(iii) Coordinate Intercepts, Section Formula & Perpendicular Line
4 Marks
$A$ and $B$ are two points on the $x$-axis and $y$-axis respectively.
  1. Write down the coordinates of $A$ and $B$.
  2. $P$ is a point on $AB$ such that $AP : PB = 3 : 1$. Using section formula, find the coordinates of point $P$.
  3. Find the equation of a line passing through $P$ and perpendicular to $AB$.
Figure: Segment $AB$ with $P(1, 3)$ dividing in ratio $3:1$ and perpendicular line $x - y + 2 = 0$
View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) Coordinates of A and B From the graph, $A$ lies on the $x$-axis at 4 units and $B$ lies on the $y$-axis at 4 units:
$A(4, 0)$ and $B(0, 4)$.1 Mark

(b) Coordinates of P Point $P$ divides $AB$ in the ratio $m_1 : m_2 = 3 : 1$ from $A(4, 0)$ to $B(0, 4)$:
$x = \frac{m_1 x_2 + m_2 x_1}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{3(0) + 1(4)}{3 + 1} = \frac{4}{4} = 1$
$y = \frac{m_1 y_2 + m_2 y_1}{m_1 + m_2} = \frac{3(4) + 1(0)}{3 + 1} = \frac{12}{4} = 3$
Coordinates of $P = (1, 3)$.1.5 Marks

(c) Perpendicular Line Equation Slope of line segment $AB$:
$m_{AB} = \frac{4 - 0}{0 - 4} = \frac{4}{-4} = -1$.
Since the required line is perpendicular to $AB$, its slope $m_\perp = -\frac{1}{m_{AB}} = -\frac{1}{-1} = 1$.
Equation of line with slope $m = 1$ passing through $P(1, 3)$:
$y - y_1 = m(x - x_1) \implies y - 3 = 1(x - 1) \implies y - 3 = x - 1$.
Equation of required line: $x - y + 2 = 0$ (or $y = x + 2$).1.5 Marks
