14. Similarity (Triangles) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: Area(Δ1) / Area(Δ2) = (Side1 / Side2)²

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Candidates write side ratios without first explicitly proving the two triangles similar with AA, SAS, or SSS criteria.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 3 : 5. The ratio of their areas is:
(a) 9 : 25
(b) 3 : 5
(c) √3 : √5
(d) 27 : 125
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) 9 : 25
Area Theorem Ratio of areas of two similar triangles = (ratio of corresponding sides)² = (3/5)² = 9 : 25.
ICSE 2026 Board Q2 Geometry Proof & Area Ratio
4 Marks
In the given diagram, DE ∥ BC and AD : DB = 2 : 3.
(a) Prove that ΔADE ~ ΔABC and hence find DE : BC.
(b) Prove that ΔDFE ~ ΔCFB.
(c) Given area of ΔDFE = 16 square units, find the area of ΔCFB.
ΔABC with DE ∥ BC, AD : DB = 2 : 3, and diagonals intersecting at F
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) In ΔADE and ΔABC:
∠ADE = ∠ABC (corresponding angles, DE ∥ BC)
∠A = ∠A (common angle)
∴ ΔADE ~ ΔABC (by AA similarity). [1 Mark]
Since AD/DB = 2/3 ⇒ AD/AB = 2/(2+3) = 2/5.
DE/BC = AD/AB = 2 : 5. [1 Mark]

Part (b) In ΔDFE and ΔCFB:
∠FDE = ∠FCB (alternate interior angles, DE ∥ BC)
∠DFE = ∠BFC (vertically opposite angles)
∴ ΔDFE ~ ΔCFB (by AA similarity). [1 Mark]

Part (c) Area(ΔDFE) / Area(ΔCFB) = (DE / BC)² = (2/5)² = 4/25.
16 / Area(ΔCFB) = 4/25 ⇒ Area(ΔCFB) = (16 × 25) / 4 = 100 sq units. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2025 Board Q4 Triangle Similarity & Map Scale Factor
4 Marks
In ΔABC, ∠ABC = 90°, AB = 20 cm, AC = 25 cm, DE is perpendicular to AC such that ∠DEA = 90° and DE = 3 cm.
(a) Prove ΔABC ∼ ΔAED.
(b) Find lengths of BC, AD and AE.
(c) If BCED represents a plot of land whose actual ground area is 576 m², find scale factor of the map.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) In ΔABC and ΔAED: ∠A = ∠A (Common), ∠ABC = ∠DEA = 90°. By AA criterion, ΔABC ∼ ΔAED. [1 Mark]
(b) BC = √(25² − 20²) = 15 cm. Similarity ratio: AB/AE = BC/ED = AC/AD ⇒ 15/3 = 5.
AE = 20 / 5 = 4 cm; AD = 25 / 5 = 5 cm. [2 Marks]
(c) Area(BCED) = Area(ΔABC) − Area(ΔAED) = (1/2 × 20 × 15) − (1/2 × 4 × 3) = 150 − 6 = 144 cm².
Actual area = 576 m² = 5,760,000 cm². Scale factor k = √(144 / 5,760,000) = 12 / 2400 = 1 : 200. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2023 Board Q7 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
In the given figure, $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ$, $PC = 6\text{ cm}$ and $CA = 4\text{ cm}$, then $PD : DB$ is:
Figure: $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ \implies CD \parallel AB$
(a) 5 : 3
(b) 3 : 5
(c) 3 : 2
(d) 2 : 3
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Since corresponding angles $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ$, line $CD$ is parallel to line $AB$ ($CD \parallel AB$).
Step 2 In $\triangle PAB$, by the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem):
$\frac{PD}{DB} = \frac{PC}{CA} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$.
Therefore, $PD : DB = 3 : 2$. Correct Option: (c) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q8 Parallel Transversals & Similarity Proof
4 Marks
In the given figure, $AC \parallel DE \parallel BF$. If $AC = 24\text{ cm}, EG = 8\text{ cm}, GB = 16\text{ cm}, BF = 30\text{ cm}$:
  1. Prove $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$.
  2. Find $DE$.
  3. Find the ratio $DB : AB$.
Figure: $AC \parallel DE \parallel BF$ with intersecting segments $CB$ and $DF$ at $G$
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Similarity Proof In $\triangle GED$ and $\triangle GBF$:
1. $\angle EGD = \angle BGF$ (Vertically opposite angles)
2. $\angle GED = \angle GBF$ (Alternate interior angles, since $DE \parallel BF$ and $CB$ is transversal)
3. $\angle GDE = \angle GFB$ (Alternate interior angles, since $DE \parallel BF$ and $DF$ is transversal)
Therefore, by AA similarity criterion: $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$.1 Mark

Part (b) Find DE Since $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$, corresponding sides are proportional:
$\frac{DE}{BF} = \frac{EG}{GB} \implies \frac{DE}{30} = \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}$
$DE = \frac{1}{2} \times 30 = \mathbf{15\text{ cm}}$.1.5 Marks

Part (c) Find Ratio DB : AB In $\triangle BAC$, $DE \parallel AC$. By Basic Proportionality Theorem / similarity $\triangle BDE \sim \triangle BAC$:
$\frac{DB}{AB} = \frac{DE}{AC}$
Given $DE = 15\text{ cm}$ and $AC = 24\text{ cm}$:
$\frac{DB}{AB} = \frac{15}{24} = \frac{5}{8}$.
Ratio $DB : AB = 5 : 8$.1.5 Marks
ICSE 2020 Board Q9 Similar Triangles in Circle Intersecting Chords
3 Marks
In the given figure $AB = 9\text{ cm}, PA = 7.5\text{ cm}$ and $PC = 5\text{ cm}$. Chords $AD$ and $BC$ intersect at $P$.
(i) Prove that $\triangle PAB \sim \triangle PCD$
(ii) Find the length of $CD$.
(iii) Find $\text{area of } \triangle PAB : \text{area of } \triangle PCD$.
Figure: Chords $AD$ and $BC$ intersecting at $P$ inside the circle
View Verified Solution & Marking
(i) In $\triangle PAB$ and $\triangle PCD$:
$\angle APB = \angle CPD$ (Vertically opposite angles)
$\angle PAB = \angle PCD$ (Angles in the same segment subtended by chord $BD$ are equal)
Therefore, by AA similarity criterion, $\triangle PAB \sim \triangle PCD$. [1 Mark]
(ii) Since $\triangle PAB \sim \triangle PCD$, corresponding sides are in proportion:
$$\frac{AB}{CD} = \frac{PA}{PC} \implies \frac{9}{CD} = \frac{7.5}{5} = \frac{3}{2} \implies CD = \frac{9 \times 2}{3} = \mathbf{6\text{ cm}}$$ [1 Mark]
(iii) Ratio of areas of similar triangles equals square of ratio of corresponding sides:
$$\frac{\text{Area}(\triangle PAB)}{\text{Area}(\triangle PCD)} = \left(\frac{PA}{PC}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{7.5}{5}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{3}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{9}{4} = \mathbf{9 : 4}$$ [1 Mark]
ICSE 2020 Board Q10 Maps & Scale Models (Similarity & Area Factor)
3 Marks
A model of a high rise building is made to a scale of $1 : 50$.
(i) If the height of the model is $0.8\text{ m}$, find the height of the actual building.
(ii) If the floor area of a flat in the building is $20\text{ m}^2$, find the floor area of that in the model.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Scale factor $k = \frac{\text{Model dimension}}{\text{Actual dimension}} = \frac{1}{50}$.
(i) Actual height $= \text{Model height} \times 50 = 0.8\text{ m} \times 50 = \mathbf{40\text{ m}}$. [1.5 Marks]
(ii) Area scale factor $= k^2 = \left(\frac{1}{50}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2500}$.
$$\text{Floor area of model} = \frac{\text{Actual Area}}{2500} = \frac{20\text{ m}^2}{2500} = \frac{1}{125}\text{ m}^2 = \mathbf{0.008\text{ m}^2}$$
In $\text{cm}^2$: $0.008 \times 10,000 = \mathbf{80\text{ cm}^2}$. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2019 Board Q11 Similar Triangles: Proof & Area Ratio
3 Marks
In the given figure, $\angle PQR = \angle PST = 90^\circ$, $PQ = 5\text{ cm}$ and $PS = 2\text{ cm}$.
  1. Prove that $\Delta PQR \sim \Delta PST$.
  2. Find $\text{Area of } \Delta PQR : \text{Area of quadrilateral } SRQT$.
Vector Diagram: Similar right triangles ΔPQR and ΔPST sharing common angle P
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 (i) Prove $\Delta PQR \sim \Delta PST$:
In $\Delta PQR$ and $\Delta PST$:
$\angle P = \angle P$ (Common angle)
$\angle PQR = \angle PST = 90^\circ$ (Given)
$\therefore \mathbf{\Delta PQR \sim \Delta PST}$ by **AA (Angle-Angle) Similarity Criterion**. [1 Mark]
Step 2 (ii) Apply ratio of areas of similar triangles:
The ratio of areas of two similar triangles is equal to the ratio of the squares of their corresponding sides:
$\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta PQR)}{\text{Area}(\Delta PST)} = \left(\frac{PQ}{PS}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 = \frac{25}{4}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Relate to quadrilateral $SRQT$:
Let $\text{Area}(\Delta PST) = 4k$, then $\text{Area}(\Delta PQR) = 25k$.
$\text{Area of quadrilateral } SRQT = \text{Area}(\Delta PQR) - \text{Area}(\Delta PST) = 25k - 4k = 21k$.
$\therefore \mathbf{\frac{\text{Area}(\Delta PQR)}{\text{Area}(SRQT)} = \frac{25k}{21k} = 25 : 21}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q12 Maps and Models: Scale Factor & Volume Scaling
3 Marks
The model of a building is constructed with the scale factor $1 : 30$.
  1. If the height of the model is $80\text{ cm}$, find the actual height of the building in meters.
  2. If the actual volume of a tank at the top of the building is $27\text{ m}^3$, find the volume of the tank on the top of the model.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Linear scale factor $k = \frac{\text{Model Length}}{\text{Actual Length}} = \frac{1}{30}$.
(i) Find actual height of building:
$\text{Actual Height} = \text{Model Height} \times 30 = 80\text{ cm} \times 30 = 2400\text{ cm}$.
Converting to meters: $\frac{2400}{100} = \mathbf{24\text{ m}}$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 (ii) Volume scaling factor $k^3 = \left(\frac{1}{30}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{27000}$.
$\text{Volume of model tank} = \text{Actual Volume} \times k^3 = 27\text{ m}^3 \times \frac{1}{27000} = \frac{1}{1000}\text{ m}^3 = \mathbf{0.001\text{ m}^3}$.
In $\text{cm}^3$: $0.001 \times 10^6\text{ cm}^3 = \mathbf{1000\text{ cm}^3}$ (or $1\text{ litre}$). [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q13 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
In the given diagram, $PQ \parallel BC$ and $\text{ar}(\Delta APQ) = \text{ar}(\text{quad } BPQC)$. The value of $AP : PB$ is:
Vector Diagram: Transversal PQ parallel to BC dividing ΔABC into equal area regions
(a) 1 : 2
(b) 2 : 1
(c) 1 : √2
(d) (√2 + 1) : 1
View Verified Solution & Marking
Solution $\text{ar}(\Delta ABC) = \text{ar}(\Delta APQ) + \text{ar}(BPQC) = 2 \times \text{ar}(\Delta APQ)$.
Since $\Delta APQ \sim \Delta ABC$, $\frac{\text{ar}(\Delta APQ)}{\text{ar}(\Delta ABC)} = \left(\frac{AP}{AB}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{2} \implies \frac{AP}{AB} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$.
$AB = \sqrt{2} AP \implies PB = AB - AP = (\sqrt{2} - 1) AP$.
$\therefore \frac{AP}{PB} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} - 1} = \frac{\sqrt{2} + 1}{(\sqrt{2} - 1)(\sqrt{2} + 1)} = \mathbf{(\sqrt{2} + 1) : 1}$.
Correct Answer: (d) (√2 + 1) : 1 [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q14 Similar Triangles: Vertically Opposite Intersection
4 Marks
In the given diagram, lines $AE$ and $BD$ intersect each other at $C$ such that $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEC$.
  1. Complete the equation with corresponding sides of $\Delta ABC$ and $\Delta DEC$: $\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{?} = \frac{?}{EC}$.
  2. $\angle ABC$ is equal to which angle of $\Delta DEC$?
  3. If $AB : DE = 2 : 3$, find the ratio of $\text{ar}(\Delta ABC) : \text{ar}(\Delta DEC)$.
Vector Diagram: Vertically opposite similar triangles ΔABC and ΔDEC
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 (a) Corresponding sides in similar triangles $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEC$:
$\mathbf{\frac{AB}{DE} = \frac{BC}{EC} = \frac{AC}{DC}}$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 (b) Angle correspondence:
Since $\Delta ABC \sim \Delta DEC$, corresponding angles are equal:
$\mathbf{\angle ABC = \angle DEC}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 (c) Ratio of areas of similar triangles:
$\frac{\text{ar}(\Delta ABC)}{\text{ar}(\Delta DEC)} = \left(\frac{AB}{DE}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{2}{3}\right)^2 = \mathbf{\frac{4}{9}}$ (or $4 : 9$). [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2018 Board Q15 Maps and Models Scale Factor
3 Marks
On a map drawn to a scale of $1 : 50,000$, a rectangular plot of land $ABCD$ has the following dimensions: $AB = 6\text{ cm}$; $BC = 8\text{ cm}$ and all angles are right angles. Find:
(i) the actual length of the diagonal distance $AC$ of the plot in km.
(ii) the actual area of the plot in sq km.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Scale Factor $k = 50,000$. Hence $1\text{ cm on map} = 50,000\text{ cm} = 0.5\text{ km}$ in reality.
(i) Diagonal AC In right $\triangle ABC$ on the map:
$$AC = \sqrt{AB^2 + BC^2} = \sqrt{6^2 + 8^2} = \sqrt{36 + 64} = 10\text{ cm}$$ [0.5 Mark]
$$\text{Actual length of } AC = 10\text{ cm} \times 0.5\text{ km/cm} = \mathbf{5\text{ km}}$$ [1 Mark]
(ii) Actual Area
$$\text{Area on map} = 6\text{ cm} \times 8\text{ cm} = 48\text{ cm}^2$$ [0.5 Mark]
$$\text{Actual area} = 48 \times (0.5\text{ km})^2 = 48 \times 0.25 = \mathbf{12\text{ sq km}}$$ [1 Mark]
Alternative: Actual $AB = 3\text{ km}$, Actual $BC = 4\text{ km} \implies \text{Area} = 3 \times 4 = 12\text{ km}^2$.
Answers: (i) Diagonal $AC = 5\text{ km}$, (ii) Actual area $= 12\text{ sq km}$.
ICSE 2018 Board Q16 Triangle Similarity and Area Ratio
3 Marks
In $\triangle PQR$, $MN$ is parallel to $QR$ and $\frac{PM}{MQ} = \frac{2}{3}$.
(i) Find $\frac{MN}{QR}$
(ii) Prove that $\triangle OMN$ and $\triangle ORQ$ are similar.
(iii) Find, $\text{Area of } \triangle OMN : \text{Area of } \triangle ORQ$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(i) Find MN / QR Given $\frac{PM}{MQ} = \frac{2}{3} \implies \frac{PM}{PQ} = \frac{2}{2 + 3} = \frac{2}{5}$.
In $\triangle PMN$ and $\triangle PQR$, $MN \parallel QR \implies \triangle PMN \sim \triangle PQR$ by AA similarity.
$$\frac{MN}{QR} = \frac{PM}{PQ} = \mathbf{\frac{2}{5}}$$ [1 Mark]
(ii) Prove Similarity In $\triangle OMN$ and $\triangle ORQ$:
• $\angle OMN = \angle ORQ$ (alternate interior angles, since $MN \parallel QR$)
• $\angle ONM = \angle OQR$ (alternate interior angles)
• $\angle MON = \angle ROQ$ (vertically opposite angles)
$\therefore \mathbf{\triangle OMN \sim \triangle ORQ}$ by AA similarity. [1 Mark]
(iii) Ratio of Areas Ratio of areas of similar triangles is equal to the square of their corresponding side ratio:
$$\frac{\text{Area}(\triangle OMN)}{\text{Area}(\triangle ORQ)} = \left(\frac{MN}{QR}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{2}{5}\right)^2 = \mathbf{\frac{4}{25}}$$ [1 Mark]
Answers: (i) $\frac{MN}{QR} = \frac{2}{5}$, (ii) Proved, (iii) $4 : 25$.
ICSE 2017 Board Q17 Triangle Similarity and Lengths
3 Marks
$PQR$ is a triangle. $S$ is a point on the side $QR$ of $\triangle PQR$ such that $\angle PSR = \angle QPR$. Given $QP = 8\text{ cm}$, $PR = 6\text{ cm}$ and $SR = 3\text{ cm}$.
(i) Prove $\triangle PQR \sim \triangle SPR$
(ii) Find the length of $QR$ and $PS$
(iii) $\frac{\text{area of } \triangle PQR}{\text{area of } \triangle SPR}$
View Verified Solution & Marking
(i) Prove Similarity In $\triangle PQR$ and $\triangle SPR$:
• $\angle QPR = \angle PSR$ (given)
• $\angle PRQ = \angle SRP$ (common angle $\angle R$)
$\therefore \mathbf{\triangle PQR \sim \triangle SPR}$ by AA similarity criterion. [1 Mark]
(ii) Find QR and PS Corresponding sides are proportional:
$$\frac{PQ}{SP} = \frac{QR}{PR} = \frac{PR}{SR} \implies \frac{8}{PS} = \frac{QR}{6} = \frac{6}{3} = 2$$ 1) $\frac{QR}{6} = 2 \implies \mathbf{QR = 12\text{ cm}}$
2) $\frac{8}{PS} = 2 \implies \mathbf{PS = 4\text{ cm}}$ [1 Mark]
(iii) Ratio of Areas
$$\frac{\text{Area}(\triangle PQR)}{\text{Area}(\triangle SPR)} = \left(\frac{PR}{SR}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{6}{3}\right)^2 = 2^2 = \mathbf{\frac{4}{1}} = \mathbf{4 : 1}$$ [1 Mark]
Answers: (i) Proved, (ii) $QR = 12\text{ cm}, PS = 4\text{ cm}$, (iii) $4 : 1$.
