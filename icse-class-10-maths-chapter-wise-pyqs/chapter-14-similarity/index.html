14. Similarity (Triangles) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: Area(Δ1) / Area(Δ2) = (Side1 / Side2)²

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Candidates write side ratios without first explicitly proving the two triangles similar with AA, SAS, or SSS criteria.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1(xi) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Two similar triangles have corresponding sides in the ratio 3 : 5. The ratio of their areas is:
(a) 9 : 25
(b) 3 : 5
(c) √3 : √5
(d) 27 : 125
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (a) 9 : 25
Area Theorem Ratio of areas of two similar triangles = (ratio of corresponding sides)² = (3/5)² = 9 : 25.
ICSE 2026 Board Q4(iii) Geometry Proof & Area Ratio
4 Marks
In the given diagram, DE ∥ BC and AD : DB = 2 : 3.
(a) Prove that ΔADE ~ ΔABC and hence find DE : BC.
(b) Prove that ΔDFE ~ ΔCFB.
(c) Given area of ΔDFE = 16 square units, find the area of ΔCFB.
ΔABC with DE ∥ BC, AD : DB = 2 : 3, and diagonals intersecting at F
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) In ΔADE and ΔABC:
∠ADE = ∠ABC (corresponding angles, DE ∥ BC)
∠A = ∠A (common angle)
∴ ΔADE ~ ΔABC (by AA similarity). [1 Mark]
Since AD/DB = 2/3 ⇒ AD/AB = 2/(2+3) = 2/5.
DE/BC = AD/AB = 2 : 5. [1 Mark]

Part (b) In ΔDFE and ΔCFB:
∠FDE = ∠FCB (alternate interior angles, DE ∥ BC)
∠DFE = ∠BFC (vertically opposite angles)
∴ ΔDFE ~ ΔCFB (by AA similarity). [1 Mark]

Part (c) Area(ΔDFE) / Area(ΔCFB) = (DE / BC)² = (2/5)² = 4/25.
16 / Area(ΔCFB) = 4/25 ⇒ Area(ΔCFB) = (16 × 25) / 4 = 100 sq units. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2025 Board Q2(iii) Triangle Similarity & Map Scale Factor
4 Marks
In ΔABC, ∠ABC = 90°, AB = 20 cm, AC = 25 cm, DE is perpendicular to AC such that ∠DEA = 90° and DE = 3 cm.
(a) Prove ΔABC ∼ ΔAED.
(b) Find lengths of BC, AD and AE.
(c) If BCED represents a plot of land whose actual ground area is 576 m², find scale factor of the map.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) In ΔABC and ΔAED: ∠A = ∠A (Common), ∠ABC = ∠DEA = 90°. By AA criterion, ΔABC ∼ ΔAED. [1 Mark]
(b) BC = √(25² − 20²) = 15 cm. Similarity ratio: AB/AE = BC/ED = AC/AD ⇒ 15/3 = 5.
AE = 20 / 5 = 4 cm; AD = 25 / 5 = 5 cm. [2 Marks]
(c) Area(BCED) = Area(ΔABC) − Area(ΔAED) = (1/2 × 20 × 15) − (1/2 × 4 × 3) = 150 − 6 = 144 cm².
Actual area = 576 m² = 5,760,000 cm². Scale factor k = √(144 / 5,760,000) = 12 / 2400 = 1 : 200. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2024 Board Q6(iii) Geometry • Similarity of Triangles
4 Marks
In right triangles ΔADB and ΔACB, ∠ADB = ∠BCA = 90°, AB = 10 cm, AD = 6 cm, BC = 2.4 cm, DP = 4.5 cm.
(a) Prove that ΔAPD ∼ ΔBPC.
(b) Find length of BD and PB.
(c) Hence, find length of PA.
(d) Find area ΔAPD : area ΔBPC.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
(a) ∠D = ∠C = 90°, ∠APD = ∠BPC (vert opp). By AA, ΔAPD ∼ ΔBPC. [1 Mark]
(b) In right ΔADB: BD = √(102 − 62) = 8 cm. PB = 8 − 4.5 = 3.5 cm. [1 Mark]
(c) PA/3.5 = 6/2.4 = 2.5 ⇒ PA = 8.75 cm. [1 Mark]
(d) Area ratio = (6/2.4)2 = (2.5)2 = 25 : 4. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q1(vi) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
In the given figure, $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ$, $PC = 6\text{ cm}$ and $CA = 4\text{ cm}$, then $PD : DB$ is:
Figure: $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ \implies CD \parallel AB$
(a) 5 : 3
(b) 3 : 5
(c) 3 : 2
(d) 2 : 3
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Since corresponding angles $\angle BAP = \angle DCP = 70^\circ$, line $CD$ is parallel to line $AB$ ($CD \parallel AB$).
Step 2 In $\triangle PAB$, by the Basic Proportionality Theorem (Thales Theorem):
$\frac{PD}{DB} = \frac{PC}{CA} = \frac{6}{4} = \frac{3}{2}$.
Therefore, $PD : DB = 3 : 2$. Correct Option: (c) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q5(iii) Parallel Transversals & Similarity Proof
4 Marks
In the given figure, $AC \parallel DE \parallel BF$. If $AC = 24\text{ cm}, EG = 8\text{ cm}, GB = 16\text{ cm}, BF = 30\text{ cm}$:
  1. Prove $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$.
  2. Find $DE$.
  3. Find the ratio $DB : AB$.
Figure: $AC \parallel DE \parallel BF$ with intersecting segments $CB$ and $DF$ at $G$
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Similarity Proof In $\triangle GED$ and $\triangle GBF$:
1. $\angle EGD = \angle BGF$ (Vertically opposite angles)
2. $\angle GED = \angle GBF$ (Alternate interior angles, since $DE \parallel BF$ and $CB$ is transversal)
3. $\angle GDE = \angle GFB$ (Alternate interior angles, since $DE \parallel BF$ and $DF$ is transversal)
Therefore, by AA similarity criterion: $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$.1 Mark

Part (b) Find DE Since $\triangle GED \sim \triangle GBF$, corresponding sides are proportional:
$\frac{DE}{BF} = \frac{EG}{GB} \implies \frac{DE}{30} = \frac{8}{16} = \frac{1}{2}$
$DE = \frac{1}{2} \times 30 = \mathbf{15\text{ cm}}$.1.5 Marks

Part (c) Find Ratio DB : AB In $\triangle BAC$, $DE \parallel AC$. By Basic Proportionality Theorem / similarity $\triangle BDE \sim \triangle BAC$:
$\frac{DB}{AB} = \frac{DE}{AC}$
Given $DE = 15\text{ cm}$ and $AC = 24\text{ cm}$:
$\frac{DB}{AB} = \frac{15}{24} = \frac{5}{8}$.
Ratio $DB : AB = 5 : 8$.1.5 Marks
