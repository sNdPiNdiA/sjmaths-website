(a) Mean Proportion Proof [3 Marks] Ch 10: Ratio & Proportion
If $b$ is the mean proportion between $a$ and $c$, show that: $$\frac{a^4 + a^2 b^2 + b^4}{b^4 + b^2 c^2 + c^4} = \frac{a^2}{c^2}$$
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Since $b$ is the mean proportional between $a$ and $c$:
$$b^2 = ac \implies b^4 = a^2 c^2$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Substitute $b^2 = ac$ and $b^4 = a^2 c^2$ into LHS:
$$\text{Numerator} = a^4 + a^2(ac) + a^2 c^2 = a^4 + a^3 c + a^2 c^2 = a^2(a^2 + ac + c^2)$$ [1 Mark]
$$\text{Denominator} = a^2 c^2 + (ac)c^2 + c^4 = a^2 c^2 + ac^3 + c^4 = c^2(a^2 + ac + c^2)$$
Step 3 Cancel common terms:
$$\text{LHS} = \frac{a^2(a^2 + ac + c^2)}{c^2(a^2 + ac + c^2)} = \mathbf{\frac{a^2}{c^2}} = \text{RHS}$$ [1 Mark]
Hence Proved.
(b) Quadratic Equation to 2 Decimal Places [4 Marks] Ch 5: Quadratics
Solve the equation $4x^2 - 5x - 3 = 0$ and give your answer correct to two decimal places.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Compare with $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$: $a = 4, b = -5, c = -3$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Apply quadratic formula:
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a} = \frac{-(-5) \pm \sqrt{(-5)^2 - 4(4)(-3)}}{2(4)}$$ $$x = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{25 + 48}}{8} = \frac{5 \pm \sqrt{73}}{8}$$ [1.5 Marks]

(c) Distance Between Parallel Chords [3 Marks] Ch 16: Circles
$AB$ and $CD$ are two parallel chords of a circle such that $AB = 24\text{ cm}$ and $CD = 10\text{ cm}$. If the radius of the circle is $13\text{ cm}$, find the distance between the two chords.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 The perpendicular from the centre of a circle to a chord bisects the chord.
For chord $AB = 24\text{ cm}$: $AN = \frac{24}{2} = 12\text{ cm}$.
In right $\triangle ONA$ ($OA = 13\text{ cm}$):
$$ON = \sqrt{OA^2 - AN^2} = \sqrt{13^2 - 12^2} = \sqrt{169 - 144} = \sqrt{25} = \mathbf{5\text{ cm}}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 For chord $CD = 10\text{ cm}$: $CM = \frac{10}{2} = 5\text{ cm}$.
In right $\triangle OMC$ ($OC = 13\text{ cm}$):
$$OM = \sqrt{OC^2 - CM^2} = \sqrt{13^2 - 5^2} = \sqrt{169 - 25} = \sqrt{144} = \mathbf{12\text{ cm}}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Since the chords lie on opposite sides of the centre $O$:
$$\text{Distance between chords } NM = ON + OM = 5 + 12 = \mathbf{17\text{ cm}}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: Distance between the two chords $= 17\text{ cm}$.

(a) Trigonometric Evaluation without Tables [3 Marks] Ch 20: Trig Identities
Evaluate without using trigonometric tables: $$\sin^2 28^\circ + \sin^2 62^\circ + \tan^2 38^\circ - \cot^2 52^\circ + \frac{1}{4} \sec^2 30^\circ$$
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Complementary angles for $\sin$:
$$\sin 62^\circ = \sin(90^\circ - 28^\circ) = \cos 28^\circ$$ $$\sin^2 28^\circ + \sin^2 62^\circ = \sin^2 28^\circ + \cos^2 28^\circ = 1$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Complementary angles for $\cot$:
$$\cot 52^\circ = \cot(90^\circ - 38^\circ) = \tan 38^\circ$$ $$\tan^2 38^\circ - \cot^2 52^\circ = \tan^2 38^\circ - \tan^2 38^\circ = 0$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Standard angle $\sec 30^\circ = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}$:
$$\frac{1}{4} \sec^2 30^\circ = \frac{1}{4} \times \left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{4} \times \frac{4}{3} = \frac{1}{3}$$ $$\text{Total Value} = 1 + 0 + \frac{1}{3} = \mathbf{\frac{4}{3}} = \mathbf{1\frac{1}{3}}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: $\frac{4}{3}$ (or $1\frac{1}{3}$).
(b) Matrix Multiplication and Equation [4 Marks] Ch 7: Matrices
If $A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix}$ and $B = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix}$ and $A^2 - 5B^2 = 5C$. Find matrix $C$ where $C$ is a 2 by 2 matrix.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1: Compute A²
$$A^2 = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 3 \\ 3 & 4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1(1)+3(3) & 1(3)+3(4) \\ 3(1)+4(3) & 3(3)+4(4) \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 10 & 15 \\ 15 & 25 \end{bmatrix}$$ [1 Mark]

$$B^2 = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 1 \\ -3 & 2 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 4-3 & -2+2 \\ 6-6 & -3+4 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = I$$ [1 Mark]

(c) Compound Interest and Loan Repayment [3 Marks] Ch 2: Banking
Jaya borrowed ₹50,000 for 2 years. The rates of interest for two successive years are 12% and 15% respectively. She repays ₹33,000 at the end of the first year. Find the amount she must pay at the end of the second year to clear her debt.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Year 1 Principal $P_1 = ₹50,000$, Rate $R_1 = 12\%$.
$$\text{Interest for Year 1 } (I_1) = \frac{50,000 \times 12 \times 1}{100} = ₹6,000$$ $$\text{Amount at end of Year 1} = 50,000 + 6,000 = ₹56,000$$ [1 Mark]
Repayment Repaid at end of Year 1 $= ₹33,000$.
$$\text{Principal for Year 2 } (P_2) = 56,000 - 33,000 = ₹23,000$$ [1 Mark]
Year 2 Rate $R_2 = 15\%$.
$$\text{Interest for Year 2 } (I_2) = \frac{23,000 \times 15 \times 1}{100} = ₹3,450$$ $$\text{Amount to clear debt} = 23,000 + 3,450 = \mathbf{₹26,450}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: Jaya must pay ₹26,450 at the end of the second year.

(b) Section Formula - Finding End Point [4 Marks] Ch 12: Section Formula
$P(1, -2)$ is a point on the line segment $A(3, -6)$ and $B(x, y)$ such that $AP : PB$ is equal to $2 : 3$. Find the coordinates of $B$.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Let $A(3, -6) = (x_1, y_1)$ and $B(x, y) = (x_2, y_2)$ with ratio $m_1 : m_2 = 2 : 3$.
Step 2: Solve for x
$$x_P = \frac{m_1 x_2 + m_2 x_1}{m_1 + m_2} \implies 1 = \frac{2x + 3(3)}{2 + 3}$$ $$1 = \frac{2x + 9}{5} \implies 5 = 2x + 9 \implies 2x = -4 \implies \mathbf{x = -2}$$ [2 Marks]

The marks of 10 students of a class in an examination arranged in ascending order is as follows: $$13, 35, 43, 46, x, x + 4, 55, 61, 71, 80$$ If the median marks is 48, find the value of $x$. Hence find the mode of the given data.

What must be subtracted from $16x^3 - 8x^2 + 4x + 7$ so that the resulting expression has $2x + 1$ as a factor?
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Let $k$ be subtracted. The resulting polynomial is $f(x) = 16x^3 - 8x^2 + 4x + 7 - k$.
By Factor Theorem, for $(2x + 1)$ to be a factor, $f\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) = 0$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Substitute $x = -\frac{1}{2}$:
$$16\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^3 - 8\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + 4\left(-\frac{1}{2}\right) + 7 - k = 0$$ $$16\left(-\frac{1}{8}\right) - 8\left(\frac{1}{4}\right) - 2 + 7 - k = 0$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Simplify:
$$-2 - 2 - 2 + 7 - k = 0 \implies 1 - k = 0 \implies \mathbf{k = 1}$$ [1 Mark]
Answer: 1 must be subtracted.
(b) Area of Shaded Region [4 Marks] Ch 19: Mensuration
In the given figure $ABCD$ is a rectangle. It consists of a circle and two semi circles each of which are of radius 5 cm. Find the area of the shaded region. Give your answer correct to three significant figures.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric

Width $AB = 2r = 2 \times 5 = 10\text{ cm}$.
Length $BC = r + 2r + r = 4r = 4 \times 5 = 20\text{ cm}$.

(c) Probability with Letter Cards [3 Marks] Ch 24: Probability
Sixteen cards are labelled as $a,b,c\dots m,n,o,p$. They are put in a box and shuffled. A boy is asked to draw a card from the box. What is the probability that the card drawn is:
(i) a vowel.
(ii) a consonant.
(iii) none of the letters of the word median.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Sample Space $S = \{a, b, c, d, e, f, g, h, i, j, k, l, m, n, o, p\}$, $n(S) = 16$.
(i) Vowel Vowels in $S$: $\{a, e, i, o\} \implies 4$ outcomes.
$$P(\text{vowel}) = \frac{4}{16} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$$ [1 Mark]
(ii) Consonant Consonants in $S$: $16 - 4 = 12$ outcomes.
$$P(\text{consonant}) = \frac{12}{16} = \mathbf{\frac{3}{4}}$$ [1 Mark]
(iii) None of letters in 'median' Letters $\{m, e, d, i, a, n\} = 6$ cards.
Cards containing none of these letters $= 16 - 6 = 10$.
$$P(\text{none of the letters of median}) = \frac{10}{16} = \mathbf{\frac{5}{8}}$$ [1 Mark]
Answers: (i) $\frac{1}{4}$, (ii) $\frac{3}{4}$, (iii) $\frac{5}{8}$.

$$\bar{x} = A + \left(\frac{\sum f_i u_i}{\sum f_i}\right) \times h = 35 + \left(\frac{-37}{100}\right) \times 10 = 35 - 3.7 = \mathbf{31.3}$$ [2 Marks]
Answer: $\text{Mean} = 31.3$.
(b) Circle Tangents and Bisectors [3 Marks] Ch 17: Tangents
In the given figure $PQ$ is a tangent to the circle at $A$. $AB$ and $AD$ are bisectors of $\angle CAQ$ and $\angle PAC$. If $\angle BAQ = 30^\circ$, prove that:
(i) $BD$ is a diameter of the circle.
(ii) $ABC$ is an isosceles triangle.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
(i) Prove BD is Diameter
Since $AB$ bisects $\angle CAQ$: $\angle CAB = \angle BAQ = 30^\circ \implies \angle CAQ = 60^\circ$.
Since $PAQ$ is a straight tangent line: $\angle PAC = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ$.
$AD$ bisects $\angle PAC \implies \angle DAC = \frac{120^\circ}{2} = 60^\circ$.
$$\angle DAB = \angle DAC + \angle CAB = 60^\circ + 30^\circ = 90^\circ$$ Since $\angle DAB = 90^\circ$ is an inscribed angle in the circle, chord $BD$ must subtend a right angle at the circumference.
$\therefore \mathbf{BD\text{ is a diameter of the circle}}$. [1.5 Marks]
(ii) Prove ABC is Isosceles
By Alternate Segment Theorem: Angle between tangent $AQ$ and chord $AB$ equals the angle in the alternate segment:
$$\angle ACB = \angle BAQ = 30^\circ$$ In $\triangle ABC$, $\angle CAB = 30^\circ$ and $\angle ACB = 30^\circ$.
Since two angles are equal, opposite sides $AB = BC$.
$\therefore \mathbf{\triangle ABC\text{ is an isosceles triangle}}$. [1.5 Marks]

(c) Trigonometric Identity Proof [3 Marks] Ch 20: Trig Identities
Prove that: $$\frac{\sin \theta - 2\sin^3 \theta}{2\cos^3 \theta - \cos \theta} = \tan \theta$$
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Factor out $\sin \theta$ in numerator and $\cos \theta$ in denominator:
$$\text{LHS} = \frac{\sin \theta(1 - 2\sin^2 \theta)}{\cos \theta(2\cos^2 \theta - 1)}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Express $1$ as $\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta$:
$$1 - 2\sin^2 \theta = \sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta - 2\sin^2 \theta = \cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta$$ $$2\cos^2 \theta - 1 = 2\cos^2 \theta - (\sin^2 \theta + \cos^2 \theta) = \cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Cancel common terms:
$$\text{LHS} = \frac{\sin \theta}{\cos \theta} \times \frac{\cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta}{\cos^2 \theta - \sin^2 \theta} = \mathbf{\tan \theta} = \text{RHS}$$ [1 Mark]
Hence Proved.
Question 10 [10 Marks]

The sum of the ages of Vivek and his younger brother Amit is 47 years. The product of their ages in years is 550. Find their ages.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Let Vivek's age be $x$ years. Then Amit's age $= 47 - x$ years. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Formulate quadratic equation:
$$x(47 - x) = 550 \implies 47x - x^2 = 550$$ $$x^2 - 47x + 550 = 0$$ [1 Mark]

Question 11 [10 Marks]
(a) Heights and Distances - Opposite Ships [4 Marks] Ch 21: Heights & Distances
The angles of depression of two ships A and B as observed from the top of a light house 60 m high are $60^\circ$ and $45^\circ$ respectively. If the two ships are on the opposite sides of the light house, find the distance between the two ships. Give your answer correct to the nearest whole number.
View Stepwise Solution & Rubric
Step 1 Let $CD = 60\text{ m}$ be the lighthouse. $A$ and $B$ are on opposite sides of base $D$.

Angle of depression $45^\circ \implies \angle CBD = 45^\circ$.
Step 2: Distance AD In right $\triangle CDA$ ($\angle D = 90^\circ$):
$$\tan 60^\circ = \frac{CD}{AD} \implies \sqrt{3} = \frac{60}{AD} \implies AD = \frac{60}{\sqrt{3}} = 20\sqrt{3}\text{ m}$$ [1.5 Marks]

$$\tan 45^\circ = \frac{CD}{BD} \implies 1 = \frac{60}{BD} \implies BD = 60\text{ m}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 4: Total Distance AB
$$AB = AD + BD = 20\sqrt{3} + 60 = 20(1.732) + 60 = 34.64 + 60 = 94.64\text{ m}$$ To the nearest whole number: $\mathbf{95\text{ metres}}$ [1.5 Marks]

$PQR$ is a triangle. $S$ is a point on the side $QR$ of $\triangle PQR$ such that $\angle PSR = \angle QPR$. Given $QP = 8\text{ cm}$, $PR = 6\text{ cm}$ and $SR = 3\text{ cm}$.
