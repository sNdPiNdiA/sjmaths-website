19. Mensuration (Cylinder, Cone and Sphere) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: V_cyl = πr²h, V_cone = (1/3)πr²h, V_sph = (4/3)πr³, V_hemi = (2/3)πr³

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Omitting units (cm², cm³, m²) in the final answer costs 1 mark. For composite solids, identify which surfaces are internal/hidden vs exposed.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1(vii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The total surface area of a solid sphere (S₁) and a solid hemisphere (S₂), as shown in the diagram, are equal. The ratio of radii R and r is:
(a) 1 : 1
(b) 2 : 1
(c) √3 : 2
(d) 2 : √3
Sphere S₁ (radius R) and Solid Hemisphere S₂ (radius r)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (c) √3 : 2
Formula Equating Total Surface Area of solid sphere S₁ = 4πR².
Total Surface Area of solid hemisphere S₂ = 3πr² (Curved 2πr² + Base flat πr²).
Given: 4πR² = 3πr² ⇒ R² / r² = 3 / 4.
Taking square root: R / r = √3 / 2.
ICSE 2026 Board Q3(ii) Composite Solid Problem
4 Marks
An eye drop bottle is prepared consisting of a hemisphere, a cylinder and a conical cap, as shown in the given diagram. Height of the cylindrical and conical parts are each equal to the diameter (7 cm). Find the:
(a) minimum height of the cylindrical box required to pack this bottle.
(b) volume of the liquid medicine (shaded part) in the bottle. Give your answer to the nearest whole number. (Use π = 22/7)
Eye Drop Bottle: Conical Cap + Cylinder + Inverted Hemispherical Base (d = 7 cm)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Height Diameter d = 7 cm ⇒ Radius r = 3.5 cm.
Height of cylinder h_cyl = 7 cm; Height of cone h_cone = 7 cm.
The bottom hemisphere is inverted into the cylinder base, so base of the bottle rests on the cylinder rim.
Total height of bottle = h_cyl + h_cone = 7 + 7 = 14 cm. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Liquid Volume Liquid is filled in the cylinder excluding the inverted hemisphere space:
Volume = Volume of cylinder − Volume of hemisphere = πr²h − (2/3)πr³ = πr² [h − (2/3)r]
= (22/7) × (3.5)² × [7 − (2/3 × 3.5)] = 38.5 × [7 − 2.333] = 38.5 × 4.667 ≈ 180 cm³. [2.5 Marks]
ICSE 2026 Board Q9(iii) Composite Solid (Surface Area & Cost)
4 Marks
A solid wooden toy is prepared by joining a cone, a cylinder and a sphere, as shown in the given diagram. The radius of each of the three solids is 7 cm and heights of each of the cone and the cylinder is 24 cm. Find:
(a) the total surface area of the given solid.
(b) the cost of painting the total surface at the rate of ₹0.50 per cm². (Use π = 22/7)
Wooden toy composed of Sphere (r=7 cm) + Cylinder (h=24 cm) + Cone (h=24 cm, r=7 cm)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Slant Height of Cone l = √(r² + h²) = √(7² + 24²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25 cm. [1 Mark]

Total Surface Area
The outer exposed surface consists of:
1. Surface of Sphere = 4πr²
2. Curved Surface of Cylinder = 2πrh
3. Curved Surface of Cone = πrl
TSA = πr [ 4r + 2h + l ] = (22/7) × 7 × [ 4(7) + 2(24) + 25 ]
= 22 × [ 28 + 48 + 25 ] = 22 × 101 = 2,222 cm². [2 Marks]

Cost of Painting Cost = TSA × Rate = 2,222 × 0.50 = ₹1,111. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2025 Board Q5(iii) Mensuration: Separated Solid Capsule
4 Marks
A solid wooden capsule is formed of a cylindrical block and two hemispheres. Find the sum of total surface area of the three parts as shown in Figure 2. Given radius = 3.5 cm and length of cylinder = 14 cm. (Use π = 22/7)
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
For the 3 separated components:
TSA of cylinder = 2πrh + 2πr².
TSA of 2 solid hemispheres = 2 × (3πr²) = 6πr².
Sum of TSAs = 2πrh + 8πr² = 2πr(h + 4r). [2 Marks]
With r = 3.5 = 7/2 cm, h = 14 cm:
2πr = 2 × (22/7) × 3.5 = 22 cm. h + 4r = 14 + 14 = 28 cm.
Total Area = 22 × 28 = 616 cm². [2 Marks]
ICSE 2025 Board Q9(i) Mensuration: Recasting Hollow Sphere into Cone
3 Marks
A hollow sphere of external diameter 10 cm and internal diameter 6 cm is melted and made into a solid right circular cone of height 8 cm. Find radius of cone so formed. [Use π = 22/7]
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
External R = 5 cm, internal r = 3 cm.
Volume of hollow sphere = 4/3 π (5³ − 3³) = 4/3 π (125 − 27) = (392/3)π cm³. [1.5 Marks]
Volume of cone = 1/3 π r_cone² (8) = (8/3)π r_cone².
Equating: (8/3)π r_cone² = (392/3)π ⇒ r_cone² = 49 ⇒ radius of cone = 7 cm. [1.5 Marks]

ICSE 2024 Board Q2(ii) Mensuration • Cylinder Cut Halves
4 Marks
A solid metallic cylinder is cut into two identical halves along its height. Diameter is 7 cm and height is 10 cm. Find:
(a) The total surface area (both the halves).
(b) The total cost of painting the two halves at ₹30 per cm2. (Take π = 22/7)
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
r = 3.5 cm, h = 10 cm.
(a) Original cylinder TSA = 2πr(h+r) = 22 × 13.5 = 297 cm2. [1.5 Marks]
Two new cut rectangular faces = 2 × (7 × 10) = 140 cm2.
Total surface area = 297 + 140 = 437 cm2. [1.5 Marks]
(b) Cost = 437 × ₹30 = ₹13,110. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q8(iii) Mensuration • Spherical Vessel to Cylinder
4 Marks
Oil is stored in a spherical vessel occupying 3/4 of its capacity (radius = 28 cm). This oil is poured into a cylindrical vessel with radius 21 cm. Find the height of the oil in the cylinder to the nearest cm.
View Official Marking Scheme Solution
Volume of oil = 3/4 × (4/3πR3) = πR3 = π(28)3. [1.5 Marks]
In cylinder: πr2h = π(21)2h = π(28)3 [1 Mark]
h = (28 × 28 × 28) / (21 × 21) = 448/9 ≈ 49.78 cm.
To nearest cm: h = 50 cm. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2023 Board Q1(xiii) Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Volume of a cylinder of height 3 cm is $48\pi$. Radius of the cylinder is:
(a) 48 cm
(b) 16 cm
(c) 4 cm
(d) 24 cm
View Verified Solution & Marking
Volume of cylinder $V = \pi r^2 h$.
Given $h = 3\text{ cm}$ and $V = 48\pi$:
$\pi r^2 (3) = 48\pi \implies 3r^2 = 48 \implies r^2 = 16 \implies r = 4\text{ cm}$.
Correct Option: (c) [1 Mark]
ICSE 2023 Board Q7(iii) Solid Mensuration: Hemisphere & Cone in Water Cylinder
4 Marks
A solid is in the shape of a hemisphere of radius 7 cm, surmounted by a cone of height 4 cm. The solid is immersed completely in a cylindrical container filled with water to a certain height. If the radius of the cylinder is 14 cm, find the rise in the water level.
Figure: Immersed solid (hemisphere $r=7\text{ cm}$ + cone $h=4\text{ cm}$) in cylinder ($R=14\text{ cm}$)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Volume of Solid
Solid consists of hemisphere ($r = 7\text{ cm}$) and cone ($r = 7\text{ cm}, h = 4\text{ cm}$):
$V_{\text{hemisphere}} = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{2}{3}\pi (7)^3 = \frac{686\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.1 Mark
$V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (7^2)(4) = \frac{196\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.1 Mark
Total Volume of Solid $V_{\text{solid}} = \frac{686\pi + 196\pi}{3} = \frac{882\pi}{3} = 294\pi\text{ cm}^3$.

Step 2: Water Level Rise
Let the rise in water level in the cylinder be $H$.
Volume of water displaced = Volume of cylinder of radius $R = 14\text{ cm}$ and height $H$:
$\pi R^2 H = V_{\text{solid}}$1 Mark
$\pi (14)^2 H = 294\pi \implies 196 H = 294 \implies H = \frac{294}{196} = \frac{3}{2} = \mathbf{1.5\text{ cm}}$.
Rise in water level = 1.5 cm.1 Mark
