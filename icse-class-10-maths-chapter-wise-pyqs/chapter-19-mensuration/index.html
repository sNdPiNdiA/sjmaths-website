19. Mensuration (Cylinder, Cone and Sphere) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: V_cyl = πr²h, V_cone = (1/3)πr²h, V_sph = (4/3)πr³, V_hemi = (2/3)πr³

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Omitting units (cm², cm³, m²) in the final answer costs 1 mark. For composite solids, identify which surfaces are internal/hidden vs exposed.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The total surface area of a solid sphere (S₁) and a solid hemisphere (S₂), as shown in the diagram, are equal. The ratio of radii R and r is:
(a) 1 : 1
(b) 2 : 1
(c) √3 : 2
(d) 2 : √3
Sphere S₁ (radius R) and Solid Hemisphere S₂ (radius r)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (c) √3 : 2
Formula Equating Total Surface Area of solid sphere S₁ = 4πR².
Total Surface Area of solid hemisphere S₂ = 3πr² (Curved 2πr² + Base flat πr²).
Given: 4πR² = 3πr² ⇒ R² / r² = 3 / 4.
Taking square root: R / r = √3 / 2.
ICSE 2026 Board Q2 Composite Solid Problem
4 Marks
An eye drop bottle is prepared consisting of a hemisphere, a cylinder and a conical cap, as shown in the given diagram. Height of the cylindrical and conical parts are each equal to the diameter (7 cm). Find the:
(a) minimum height of the cylindrical box required to pack this bottle.
(b) volume of the liquid medicine (shaded part) in the bottle. Give your answer to the nearest whole number. (Use π = 22/7)
Eye Drop Bottle: Conical Cap + Cylinder + Inverted Hemispherical Base (d = 7 cm)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Part (a) Height Diameter d = 7 cm ⇒ Radius r = 3.5 cm.
Height of cylinder h_cyl = 7 cm; Height of cone h_cone = 7 cm.
The bottom hemisphere is inverted into the cylinder base, so base of the bottle rests on the cylinder rim.
Total height of bottle = h_cyl + h_cone = 7 + 7 = 14 cm. [1.5 Marks]

Part (b) Liquid Volume Liquid is filled in the cylinder excluding the inverted hemisphere space:
Volume = Volume of cylinder − Volume of hemisphere = πr²h − (2/3)πr³ = πr² [h − (2/3)r]
= (22/7) × (3.5)² × [7 − (2/3 × 3.5)] = 38.5 × [7 − 2.333] = 38.5 × 4.667 ≈ 180 cm³. [2.5 Marks]
ICSE 2026 Board Q3 Composite Solid (Surface Area & Cost)
4 Marks
A solid wooden toy is prepared by joining a cone, a cylinder and a sphere, as shown in the given diagram. The radius of each of the three solids is 7 cm and heights of each of the cone and the cylinder is 24 cm. Find:
(a) the total surface area of the given solid.
(b) the cost of painting the total surface at the rate of ₹0.50 per cm². (Use π = 22/7)
Wooden toy composed of Sphere (r=7 cm) + Cylinder (h=24 cm) + Cone (h=24 cm, r=7 cm)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Slant Height of Cone l = √(r² + h²) = √(7² + 24²) = √(49 + 576) = √625 = 25 cm. [1 Mark]

Total Surface Area
The outer exposed surface consists of:
1. Surface of Sphere = 4πr²
2. Curved Surface of Cylinder = 2πrh
3. Curved Surface of Cone = πrl
TSA = πr [ 4r + 2h + l ] = (22/7) × 7 × [ 4(7) + 2(24) + 25 ]
= 22 × [ 28 + 48 + 25 ] = 22 × 101 = 2,222 cm². [2 Marks]

Cost of Painting Cost = TSA × Rate = 2,222 × 0.50 = ₹1,111. [1 Mark]

ICSE 2023 Board Q11 Solid Mensuration: Hemisphere & Cone in Water Cylinder
4 Marks
A solid is in the shape of a hemisphere of radius 7 cm, surmounted by a cone of height 4 cm. The solid is immersed completely in a cylindrical container filled with water to a certain height. If the radius of the cylinder is 14 cm, find the rise in the water level.
Figure: Immersed solid (hemisphere $r=7\text{ cm}$ + cone $h=4\text{ cm}$) in cylinder ($R=14\text{ cm}$)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Volume of Solid
Solid consists of hemisphere ($r = 7\text{ cm}$) and cone ($r = 7\text{ cm}, h = 4\text{ cm}$):
$V_{\text{hemisphere}} = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{2}{3}\pi (7)^3 = \frac{686\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.1 Mark
$V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (7^2)(4) = \frac{196\pi}{3}\text{ cm}^3$.1 Mark
Total Volume of Solid $V_{\text{solid}} = \frac{686\pi + 196\pi}{3} = \frac{882\pi}{3} = 294\pi\text{ cm}^3$.

Step 2: Water Level Rise
Let the rise in water level in the cylinder be $H$.
Volume of water displaced = Volume of cylinder of radius $R = 14\text{ cm}$ and height $H$:
$\pi R^2 H = V_{\text{solid}}$1 Mark
$\pi (14)^2 H = 294\pi \implies 196 H = 294 \implies H = \frac{294}{196} = \frac{3}{2} = \mathbf{1.5\text{ cm}}$.
Rise in water level = 1.5 cm.1 Mark
ICSE 2020 Board Q12 Solid Mensuration (Melting Sphere into Marbles)
3 Marks
A solid spherical ball of radius 6 cm is melted and recast into 64 identical spherical marbles. Find the radius of each marble.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Radius of big spherical ball $R = 6\text{ cm}$.
Volume of big sphere $V = \frac{4}{3} \pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3} \pi (6)^3\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Let $r$ be the radius of each small spherical marble.
Total volume of 64 marbles $= 64 \times \left(\frac{4}{3} \pi r^3\right)$.
By conservation of volume during recasting:
$$\frac{4}{3} \pi (6)^3 = 64 \times \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 \implies 216 = 64 r^3$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Solving for $r$:
$$r^3 = \frac{216}{64} = \frac{27}{8} \implies r = \sqrt[3]{\frac{27}{8}} = \frac{3}{2} = \mathbf{1.5\text{ cm}}$$
Answer: The radius of each marble is $1.5\text{ cm}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2020 Board Q13 Cylinder Hollowed with Two Conical Cavities
3 Marks
From a solid wooden cylinder of height 28 cm and diameter 6 cm, two conical cavities are hollowed out. The diameters of the cones are also of 6 cm and height 10.5 cm. Taking $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$, find the volume of the remaining solid.
Figure: Wooden cylinder ($H=28\text{ cm}, d=6\text{ cm}$) with two conical cavities hollowed out
View Verified Solution & Marking
Radius of cylinder and cones $r = \frac{6}{2} = 3\text{ cm}$.
Height of cylinder $H = 28\text{ cm}$. Height of each cone $h = 10.5\text{ cm} = \frac{21}{2}\text{ cm}$.
Step 1 Volume of cylinder:
$$V_{\text{cylinder}} = \pi r^2 H = \frac{22}{7} \times 3^2 \times 28 = \frac{22}{7} \times 9 \times 28 = 22 \times 9 \times 4 = \mathbf{792\text{ cm}^3}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 2 Volume of two identical conical cavities:
$$2 \times V_{\text{cone}} = 2 \times \left(\frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h\right) = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 3^2 \times \frac{21}{2} = \frac{2}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 9 \times \frac{21}{2} = 22 \times 9 = \mathbf{198\text{ cm}^3}$$ [1 Mark]
Step 3 Volume of remaining solid:
$$V_{\text{remaining}} = V_{\text{cylinder}} - 2 \times V_{\text{cone}} = 792 - 198 = \mathbf{594\text{ cm}^3}$$
Answer: Volume of remaining solid = $594\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q14 Solid Mensuration: Melting Sphere into Cylinder
4 Marks
A solid metallic sphere of radius $6\text{ cm}$ is melted and made into a solid cylinder of height $32\text{ cm}$. Find the:
  1. radius of the cylinder
  2. curved surface area of the cylinder
Take $\pi = 3.1$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Equate volumes of metallic solids:
Radius of sphere $R = 6\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Volume of sphere} = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (6)^3 = \frac{4}{3}\pi (216) = 288\pi\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 (i) Determine radius of cylinder ($r$):
Height of cylinder $h = 32\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Volume of cylinder} = \pi r^2 h = \pi r^2 (32) = 32\pi r^2$.
Since the sphere is recast into the cylinder:
$32\pi r^2 = 288\pi \implies r^2 = \frac{288}{32} = 9 \implies r = \sqrt{9} = \mathbf{3\text{ cm}}$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 (ii) Calculate Curved Surface Area ($CSA$):
$CSA = 2\pi r h = 2 \times (3.1) \times 3 \times 32$
$CSA = 6 \times 3.1 \times 32 = 18.6 \times 32 = \mathbf{595.2\text{ cm}^2}$. [2 Marks]
ICSE 2019 Board Q15 Solid Mensuration: Scooped Cylinder Volume
4 Marks
A hemispherical and a conical hole is scooped out of a solid wooden cylinder. Find the volume of the remaining solid where the measurements are as follows:
The height of the solid cylinder is $7\text{ cm}$, radius of each of hemisphere, cone and cylinder is $3\text{ cm}$. Height of cone is $3\text{ cm}$.
Give your answer correct to the nearest whole number. Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Vector Diagram: Cylinder with conical hole scooped at top and hemispherical hole scooped at bottom
Step 1 Calculate individual geometric volumes in terms of $\pi$:
Volume of Cylinder: $V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi R^2 H = \pi (3)^2 (7) = 63\pi\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Volume of Conical Hole: $V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3}\pi (3)^2 (3) = 9\pi\text{ cm}^3$.
Volume of Hemispherical Hole: $V_{\text{hemi}} = \frac{2}{3}\pi r^3 = \frac{2}{3}\pi (3)^3 = \frac{2}{3}\pi(27) = 18\pi\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Subtract the scooped volumes from the cylinder:
$V_{\text{remaining}} = V_{\text{cyl}} - (V_{\text{cone}} + V_{\text{hemi}})$
$V_{\text{remaining}} = 63\pi - (9\pi + 18\pi) = 63\pi - 27\pi = 36\pi\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Evaluate numerically using $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$:
$V = 36 \times \frac{22}{7} = \frac{792}{7} = 113.1428\dots\text{ cm}^3$.
Rounding to nearest whole number: $\mathbf{V \approx 113\text{ cm}^3}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q16 Multiple Choice Question

Two right circular cones, $C_1$ and $C_2$, have their heights in the ratio $2 : 1$ and radii in the ratio $1 : 2$. The ratio of their volumes is:
(a) 1 : 4
(b) 1 : 2
(c) 2 : 1
(d) 4 : 1
View Verified Solution & Marking
Solution Given: $\frac{h_1}{h_2} = \frac{2}{1}$ and $\frac{r_1}{r_2} = \frac{1}{2}$.
$\text{Ratio of volumes } \frac{V_1}{V_2} = \frac{\frac{1}{3}\pi r_1^2 h_1}{\frac{1}{3}\pi r_2^2 h_2} = \left(\frac{r_1}{r_2}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{h_1}{h_2}\right) = \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 \times \left(\frac{2}{1}\right) = \frac{1}{4} \times 2 = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}}$.
Correct Answer: (b) 1 : 2 [1 Mark]

ICSE 2026 Imp Q18 Solid Sphere Inscribed in Cylindrical Container
4 Marks
A solid sphere completely fits into a cylindrical container of radius $3.5\text{ cm}$ and height $7\text{ cm}$ as shown in the figure below. Find the volume of water required to fill the remaining (unshaded) space of the container to the nearest of a whole number.
(Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)
Vector Diagram: Solid sphere fitting completely inside cylindrical container of height 7 cm
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 Dimensions of Cylinder:
Radius $r = 3.5\text{ cm} = \frac{7}{2}\text{ cm}$, height $h = 7\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Volume of Cylinder } V_{\text{cyl}} = \pi r^2 h = \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^2 \times 7 = \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{49}{4} \times 7 = \frac{1078}{4} = 269.5\text{ cm}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 Dimensions of Inscribed Sphere:
Since the sphere fits completely into the container, its diameter $= h = 7\text{ cm} \implies$ radius $R = 3.5\text{ cm} = \frac{7}{2}\text{ cm}$.
$\text{Volume of Sphere } V_{\text{sph}} = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{7}{2}\right)^3 = \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \frac{343}{8} = \frac{539}{3} \approx 179.67\text{ cm}^3$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 3 Volume of water to fill remaining space:
$V_{\text{water}} = V_{\text{cyl}} - V_{\text{sph}} = 269.5 - 179.67 = 89.83\text{ cm}^3$.
Rounding to nearest whole number: $\mathbf{V_{\text{water}} \approx 90\text{ cm}^3}$. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2026 Imp Q19 Solid Mensuration: Recasting Sphere into Cones
4 Marks
The surface area of a solid metallic sphere is $616\text{ cm}^2$. It is melted and recast into solid right circular cones of diameter $3.5\text{ cm}$ and height $3.5\text{ cm}$. Find the:
  1. radius of the sphere.
  2. number of cones formed.
(Take $\pi = \frac{22}{7}$)
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1 (a) Find radius of sphere ($R$):
$\text{Surface Area} = 4\pi R^2 = 616 \implies 4 \times \frac{22}{7} \times R^2 = 616$
$R^2 = \frac{616 \times 7}{88} = 7 \times 7 = 49 \implies \mathbf{R = 7\text{ cm}}$. [1.5 Marks]
Step 2 Calculate Volume of Sphere:
$V_{\text{sph}} = \frac{4}{3}\pi R^3 = \frac{4}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times (7)^3 = \frac{4312}{3}\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 3 Calculate Volume of One Cone ($r = \frac{3.5}{2} = \frac{7}{4}\text{ cm}, h = \frac{7}{2}\text{ cm}$):
$V_{\text{cone}} = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times \left(\frac{7}{4}\right)^2 \times \frac{7}{2} = \frac{539}{48}\text{ cm}^3$. [1 Mark]
Step 4 (b) Calculate number of cones formed:
$\text{Number of cones} = \frac{V_{\text{sph}}}{V_{\text{cone}}} = \frac{4312/3}{539/48} = \frac{4312}{3} \times \frac{48}{539} = 8 \times 16 = \mathbf{128\text{ cones}}$. [0.5 Mark]

The following figure represents a solid consisting of a right circular cylinder with a hemisphere at one end and a cone at the other. Their common radius is 7 cm. The height of the cylinder and cone are each of 4 cm. Find the volume of the solid.

Given Common radius $r = 7\text{ cm}$, Cylinder height $h_{\text{cyl}} = 4\text{ cm}$, Cone height $h_{\text{cone}} = 4\text{ cm}$.

ICSE 2017 Board Q22 Area of Shaded Region
4 Marks
In the given figure $ABCD$ is a rectangle. It consists of a circle and two semi circles each of which are of radius 5 cm. Find the area of the shaded region. Give your answer correct to three significant figures.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Rectangle Dimensions Radius $r = 5\text{ cm}$.
Width $AB = 2r = 2 \times 5 = 10\text{ cm}$.
Length $BC = r + 2r + r = 4r = 4 \times 5 = 20\text{ cm}$.
$$\text{Area of Rectangle } ABCD = 20 \times 10 = 200\text{ cm}^2$$ [1.5 Marks]
Step 2: Unshaded Circular Area
One full circle + two semicircles of radius $5\text{ cm}$:
$$\text{Total circular area} = \pi r^2 + 2\left(\frac{1}{2} \pi r^2\right) = 2\pi r^2 = 2 \times 3.1416 \times 25 = 157.08\text{ cm}^2$$ [1.5 Marks]
Step 3: Shaded Region Area
$$\text{Area} = 200 - 157.08 = 42.92\text{ cm}^2$$ To three significant figures: $\mathbf{42.9\text{ cm}^2}$ [1 Mark]
Answer: Area of shaded region $= 42.9\text{ cm}^2$.
ICSE 2017 Board Q23 Conical Tent Volume and Surface Area
3 Marks
A conical tent is to accommodate 77 persons. Each person must have $16\text{ m}^3$ of air to breathe. Given the radius of the tent as $7\text{ m}$ find the height of the tent and also its curved surface area.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Step 1: Volume Total volume of air $= 77 \times 16 = 1232\text{ m}^3$.
Radius $r = 7\text{ m}$.
$$V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h \implies 1232 = \frac{1}{3} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 7^2 \times h$$ $$1232 = \frac{154}{3} h \implies h = \frac{1232 \times 3}{154} = \mathbf{24\text{ m}}$$ [1.5 Marks]
Step 2: Curved Surface Area
Slant height $l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2} = \sqrt{7^2 + 24^2} = \sqrt{49 + 576} = \sqrt{625} = 25\text{ m}$.
$$\text{Curved Surface Area} = \pi r l = \frac{22}{7} \times 7 \times 25 = 22 \times 25 = \mathbf{550\text{ m}^2}$$ [1.5 Marks]
Answers: Height of tent $= 24\text{ m}$; Curved surface area $= 550\text{ m}^2$.
