15. Loci (Locus and Its Constructions) Previous Year Questions

Core Formula: Equidistant from 2 points = Perp Bisector; from 2 lines = Angle Bisector

CISCE Examiner Observation & Common Deduction Pitfall

Do not just sketch. Construction must be done strictly using ruler and compasses, retaining all visible construction arcs.

ICSE 2026 Board Q1 Locus Construction Concept
2 Marks
State the locus of a point P which is equidistant from two fixed points A and B, and state the locus of a point equidistant from two intersecting straight lines AB and AC.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Point Equidistance The locus of a point equidistant from two fixed points A and B is the perpendicular bisector of the line segment AB. [1 Mark]

Line Equidistance The locus of a point equidistant from two intersecting lines AB and AC is the pair of angle bisectors of the angles formed between the lines. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2025 Board Q2 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
Statement 1: The point which is equidistant from three non-collinear points D, E and F is the circumcentre of the ΔDEF.
Statement 2: The incentre of a triangle is the point where the bisector of the angles intersects.
(a) Both statements are true
(b) Both statements are false
(c) Statement 1 true, Statement 2 false
(d) Statement 1 false, Statement 2 true
View Verified Solution & Marking
Circumcentre is equidistant from all vertices of a triangle (intersection of perpendicular bisectors). Incentre is the point of concurrence of interior angle bisectors. Both statements are true. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q3 Multiple Choice Question
1 Mark
The circumcentre of a triangle is the point which is:
(a) at equal distance from the three sides of the triangle.
(b) at equal distance from the three vertices of the triangle.
(c) the point of intersection of the three medians.
(d) the point of intersection of the three altitudes of the triangle.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Correct Option: (b) at equal distance from the three vertices of the triangle.
Circumcentre is the concurrence point of perpendicular bisectors of sides, which are equidistant from triangle vertices. [1 Mark]
ICSE 2024 Board Q4 Geometry • Loci and Constructions
4 Marks
Construct ∠ABC = 90°, where AB = 6 cm, BC = 8 cm.
(a) Locus of points equidistant from B and C.
(b) Locus of points equidistant from A and B.
(c) Mark intersection as O. Construct locus of points at distance OA from O.
(d) Locus of points equidistant from BA and BC.
View Verified Solution & Marking
(a) Perpendicular bisector of BC. [1 Mark]
(b) Perpendicular bisector of AB. [0.5 Mark]
(c) Point O is midpoint of hypotenuse AC. Locus is circumcircle of ΔABC with centre O and radius 5 cm. [1 Mark]
(d) Angle bisector ray of ∠ABC. [1.5 Marks]
ICSE 2020 Board Q5 Construction & Loci on Circle
4 Marks
Use ruler and compass for this question. Construct a circle of radius 4.5 cm. Draw a chord $AB = 6\text{ cm}$.
(i) Find the locus of points equidistant from $A$ and $B$. Mark the point where it meets the circle as $D$.
(ii) Join $AD$ and find the locus of points which are equidistant from $AD$ and $AB$. Mark the point where it meets the circle as $C$.
(iii) Join $BC$ and $CD$. Measure and write down the length of side $CD$ of the quadrilateral $ABCD$.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Figure: Circle construction with perpendicular bisector locus meeting at $D$ and angle bisector locus meeting at $C$
(i) Construct circle of radius $4.5\text{ cm}$. Draw chord $AB = 6\text{ cm}$.
The locus of points equidistant from two points $A$ and $B$ is the perpendicular bisector of $AB$.
Construct the perpendicular bisector of $AB$; it passes through the centre $O$ and intersects the circle at $D$. [1.5 Marks]
(ii) Join $AD$. The locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines $AD$ and $AB$ is the internal angle bisector of $\angle DAB$.
Bisect $\angle DAB$; let the bisector intersect the circle at $C$. [1.5 Marks]
(iii) Join $BC$ and $CD$ to complete cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$.
Measure the side $CD$:
Length of side $CD \approx 5.2\text{ cm}$ (acceptable tolerance: $5.0\text{ cm}$ to $5.4\text{ cm}$). [1 Mark]
ICSE 2019 Board Q6 Loci & Construction: Semicircle & Cyclic Quad
4 Marks
Using ruler and a compass only construct a semi-circle with diameter $BC = 7\text{ cm}$.
Locate a point $A$ on the circumference of the semicircle such that $A$ is equidistant from $B$ and $C$. Complete the cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$, such that $D$ is equidistant from $AB$ and $BC$. Measure $\angle ADC$ and write it down.
View Verified Solution & Marking
Vector Diagram: Semicircle construction with locus perpendicular bisector (A) and angle bisector (D)
Step 1 Draw line segment $BC = 7\text{ cm}$. Bisect $BC$ to find centre $O$, then construct semicircle with radius $OB = 3.5\text{ cm}$. [1 Mark]
Step 2 Locate point $A$ equidistant from $B$ and $C$:
Locus of points equidistant from two fixed points $B$ and $C$ is the **perpendicular bisector of $BC$**.
The perpendicular bisector meets the circumference at point $A$.
(Here, $\Delta ABC$ is isosceles right-angled at $A$ with $\angle ABC = \angle ACB = 45^\circ$). [1 Mark]
Step 3 Locate point $D$ equidistant from $AB$ and $BC$:
Locus of points equidistant from two intersecting lines $AB$ and $BC$ is the **internal angle bisector of $\angle ABC$**.
Construct the bisector of $\angle ABC = 45^\circ$ to intersect the semicircle at point $D$. Join $CD$ and $AD$ to complete cyclic quadrilateral $ABCD$. [1 Mark]
Step 4 Measure $\angle ADC$:
Since $ABCD$ is a cyclic quadrilateral, opposite angles are supplementary:
$\angle ABC + \angle ADC = 180^\circ \implies 45^\circ + \angle ADC = 180^\circ \implies \mathbf{\angle ADC = 135^\circ}$. [1 Mark]
