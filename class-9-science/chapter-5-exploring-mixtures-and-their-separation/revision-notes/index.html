Exploring Mixtures: Revision Notes & Mnemonics

Summary

60-Second Chapter Summary

Master the core of mixtures and separation science in under a minute:

  • Mixture Types: Homogeneous (solutions - uniform phase, size < 1 nm) and Heterogeneous (suspensions - settle out, size > 1000 nm; colloids - uniform scatter, size 1–1000 nm).
  • Concentration Formulas:
    • % m/m = [Solute Mass / Solution Mass] × 100
    • % m/v = [Solute Mass / Solution Volume (mL)] × 100
    • % v/v = [Solute Volume / Solution Volume] × 100
  • Thermal Separation:
    • Crystallization: Purifies solids by slowly cooling hot saturated solutions.
    • Simple Distillation: Separates miscible liquids with BP difference ≥ 25°C.
    • Fractional Distillation: For miscible liquids with BP difference < 25°C.
  • Chromatography: Separates pigments based on their rates of migration ($R_f$) in a rising solvent.
  • Heterogeneous Separations:
    • Separating Funnel: Isolates immiscible liquids by density layers.
    • Sublimation: Extracts vaporizable solids (Camphor, Naphthalene) directly to gas.
    • Centrifugation: Spins samples rapidly to push denser components to the bottom.
    • Coagulation: Addition of Alum clumps fine suspended particles together.
  • Tyndall Effect: Scattering of light by particles in colloids and suspensions, making the beam visible.
Memory Hooks

Custom Mnemonics

Use these quick brain hacks to ensure you never confuse quantities or methods in exams:

Mnemonic 1: S.S.C. (Particle Size Order)
  • Solutions ⇒ Smallest particles (< 1 nm)
  • Colloids ⇒ Centered/middle particles (1–1000 nm)
  • Suspensions ⇒ Supersized particles (> 1000 nm)
Mnemonic 2: H.H. & L.L. (Separating Funnel Densities)
  • Heavier goes Here (bottom) ⇒ Denser liquid drains out first
  • Lighter stays Loft (top) ⇒ Less dense liquid floats on top
