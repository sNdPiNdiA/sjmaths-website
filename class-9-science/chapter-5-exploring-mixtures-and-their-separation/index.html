Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in This Chapter

Separation of mixtures is fundamental to science, technology, and medical diagnostics. In this chapter, we explore how substances are classified based on their homogeneity, the quantitative expression of solution concentrations, and the physical principles underlying separation techniques.

Key Topics Covered

- Differentiating Homogeneous mixtures (solutions) and Heterogeneous mixtures
- Quantifying concentration: Mass by mass (% m/m), Mass by volume (% m/v), Volume by volume (% v/v)
- Solubility curves and temperature dependence of solid vs. gas solutes
- Separation of homogeneous mixtures: Crystallization, Distillation, and Paper Chromatography
- Separation of heterogeneous mixtures: Separating funnels, Sublimation, Centrifugation, and Coagulation (using Alum)
- Properties of Solutions, Suspensions, and Colloids (including Emulsions)
- The Tyndall Effect: Scattering of light in heterogeneous mixtures
