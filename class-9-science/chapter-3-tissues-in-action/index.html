Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in This Chapter

Organisms have groups of cells that cooperate to complete complex physiological operations. In this chapter, we explore how cells differentiate into specialized plant meristems and permanent tissues, and classify animal epithelial, connective, muscular, and nervous systems.

Key Topics Covered

- Definition of Tissues and Division of Labor
- Meristematic Growth Zones (Apical, Lateral, Intercalary)
- Simple Permanent Tissues (Parenchyma, Collenchyma, Sclerenchyma)
- Complex Vascular Tissues (3D Xylem vs Phloem Conduction)
- Animal Epithelial and Connective Networks (Blood, Bone, Ligaments, Tendons)
- Muscular Action (Striated, Smooth, Cardiac) and 3D Neural Action Potentials
