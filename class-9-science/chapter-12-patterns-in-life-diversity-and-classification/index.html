Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in This Chapter

In this chapter, we examine the diversity of living organisms and the patterns scientists use to organise and understand life. Classification provides a systematic way to compare organisms and recognise relationships.

Key Topics Covered

- Scientific Models: Simplifying complex real systems (e.g. cricket shot models, Saha's star ion models).
- Language of Science: Preciseness of symbols (m, v, F, I), values, and standard SI units (avoiding plane fuel mix-ups!).
- Laws, Theories, & Principles: Discovering patterns (laws), explaining mechanisms (theories), and guiding scenarios (principles).
- Making Predictions: Developing testable, evidence-backed hypotheses (e.g., weather variables, eclipse myth-busting).
- Approximation & Estimation: Building physical intuition (estimating daily breathing air volume, family food ratios).
- Multidisciplinary Connections: How biology, physics, chemistry, and math combine to solve real problems (e.g., face mask design).
