Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in This Chapter

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life. In this chapter, you'll journey from the discovery of cells by Robert Hooke in 1665 to understanding the complex organelles that make up both plant and animal cells.

Key Topics Covered

- Discovery of the Cell (Hooke, Leeuwenhoek)
- Cell Theory (Schleiden, Schwann, Virchow)
- Cell Structure & Organelles (3D interactive model)
- Prokaryotic vs Eukaryotic Cells
- Diffusion & Osmosis
- Plant vs Animal Cell Comparison
