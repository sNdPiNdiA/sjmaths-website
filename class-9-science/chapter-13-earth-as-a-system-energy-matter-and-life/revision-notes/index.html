Earth as a System: Energy, Matter, and Life

Master Revision: Earth as a System

Complete chapter recall — concepts, comparisons, mnemonics, traps, cycles and exam reasoning

Core idea: Earth is an interconnected system. Energy flows through the system while matter cycles among the spheres.

1. Five Earth Spheres

SphereRemember

HydrosphereLiquid water: oceans, rivers, lakes, groundwater
CryosphereFrozen water: glaciers, snow, polar ice
AtmosphereAir and gases surrounding Earth
BiosphereLiving organisms and habitats

Mnemonic

G-H-C-A-B — Ground, H2O, Cold water, Air, Biology.

2. Solar Radiation → Heating

  • Sun = main external energy source.
  • EM waves travel through vacuum.
  • Solar energy reaching Earth is mainly UV, visible and IR.
  • Visible light → photosynthesis.
  • IR → surface warming and outgoing terrestrial radiation.
  • Ozone absorbs harmful UV.
  • Greenhouse gases absorb some outgoing infrared radiation.

6. Wind Formation

Uneven heating → temperature difference → pressure difference → wind.

Valley breezeMountain breeze
DayAfter sunset/night
Slopes heatSlopes cool
Warm air risesCool dense air sinks
Valley air moves upslopeAir moves downslope

Rotation: rightward deflection in Northern Hemisphere; leftward in Southern Hemisphere.

7. Ocean Currents

  • Surface currents are strongly driven by planetary winds.
  • Temperature + salinity change density.
  • Cold, dense water can return at depth.
  • Earth’s rotation helps form gyres.
  • Gyres: clockwise in Northern Hemisphere, counter-clockwise in Southern Hemisphere.
  • Currents transport heat and nutrients and regulate climate.

8. Four Biogeochemical Cycles

CycleKey processes
WaterEvaporation → condensation → precipitation → runoff/infiltration
CarbonPhotosynthesis ↔ respiration; decomposition; fossilisation; combustion
NitrogenFixation → assimilation → ammonification → nitrification → denitrification
OxygenPhotosynthesis restores O2; respiration/combustion consume O2

9. Nitrogen Cycle Mnemonic

F-A-A-N-D = Fixation → Assimilation → Ammonification → Nitrification → Denitrification.

Nitrification: ammonia → nitrite → nitrate.

Denitrification: nitrate → atmospheric nitrogen.

11. High-Frequency Traps

  • Weather → troposphere, not stratosphere.
  • Ozone protection → stratosphere; ground-level ozone is harmful.
  • High albedo → more reflection, not more absorption.
  • Wind → high pressure to low pressure.
  • Valley breeze → day; mountain breeze → night.
  • Ocean salinity ↑ → density ↑ → sinking tendency ↑.
  • Carbon is cycled; energy is transferred through the system.
  • Nitrification ≠ denitrification.
  • Eutrophication is linked to excess nutrients and oxygen depletion.
  • Deforestation affects multiple spheres and cycles simultaneously.
