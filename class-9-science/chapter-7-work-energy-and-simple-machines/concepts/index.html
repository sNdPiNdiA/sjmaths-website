How the Ideas Connect

Work is the bridge between force and energy. When a force causes displacement, work is done and energy is transferred. That energy can appear as kinetic energy (energy of motion) or potential energy (stored energy due to position). In an ideal system, mechanical energy is conserved — it merely changes form. Power tells us how quickly that work is performed. Simple machines redistribute force, making tasks feel easier without changing the total work.

Pro Tip

Always check whether force and displacement are in the same direction. If they are perpendicular, no work is done. If they are opposite, work is negative.
