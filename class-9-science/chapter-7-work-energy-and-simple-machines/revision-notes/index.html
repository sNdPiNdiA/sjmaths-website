Key Definitions

Must-Know Concepts

Work Done

Product of force and displacement in the direction of force. W = F × s. SI unit: joule (J).

Positive Work

Force and displacement in the same direction (e.g., lifting a bag upward).

Negative Work

Force and displacement in opposite directions (e.g., friction slowing a moving object).

Zero Work

Occurs when force is zero, displacement is zero, or force is perpendicular to displacement.

Work-Energy Theorem

Work done on an object equals the change in its energy.

Kinetic Energy

Energy due to motion. KE = ½mv². If velocity doubles, KE becomes 4 times.

Potential Energy

Energy due to position. Gravitational PE = mgh. Increases with height.

Conservation of Mechanical Energy

In absence of friction, total mechanical energy (KE + PE) remains constant.

Power

Rate of doing work. P = W/t. SI unit: watt (W). 1 hp = 746 W.

Simple Machines

Devices that make work easier by changing force magnitude or direction. Types: pulley, inclined plane, lever.

Mechanical Advantage

MA = Load / Effort. Always greater than 1 for a machine that provides advantage.

Memory Aids

Mnemonics & Tips

Work Formula Tip

If force and displacement are at an angle, use W = Fs cos(θ). When θ = 0°, cos(0) = 1 (max positive work). When θ = 180°, cos(180) = -1 (max negative work). When θ = 90°, cos(90) = 0 (zero work).

KE Quadruple Rule

Remember: double velocity → quadruple KE. KE ∝ v². This is a favourite exam trick question.

Energy Conservation

In ideal systems (no friction), PE lost = KE gained. Total mechanical energy stays the same. Friction breaks conservation by converting mechanical energy to heat.

Simple Machine Rule

Machines do NOT reduce total work. They trade force for distance (or vice versa). MA > 1 means you apply less force over a longer distance.

Common Mistakes

Exam Trap Avoidance

Mistake 1: Confusing Work with Energy

Work is a process (force causing displacement). Energy is a property (capacity to do work). Work transfers energy.

Mistake 2: Forgetting Perpendicular Force

When a girl carries a box horizontally, the upward force she applies is perpendicular to horizontal displacement. No work is done by that force on the box.

Mistake 3: Using Mass Instead of Weight

In PE = mgh, m is mass (kg), not weight (N). If weight is given, divide by g first.

Mistake 4: Ignoring Sign of Work

Always check direction. Friction does negative work. Gravity does positive work when object falls, negative when it rises.
