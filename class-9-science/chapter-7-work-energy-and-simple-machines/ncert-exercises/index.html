NCERT Exercises

Attempt every question independently. This page intentionally contains questions only; there is no answer/reveal control here.

Q1 True / False
NCERT

State whether the following statements are True or False.

  1. Work is said to be done when a force is applied, even if the object does not move.
  2. Lifting a bucket vertically upward results in positive work done on the bucket.
  3. The SI unit for both work and energy is joule (J).
  4. A motionless stretched rubber band has kinetic energy.
  5. Energy can change from one form to another.
Q2 Fill in the Blanks
NCERT

Fill in the blanks.

  1. Work done = ______ × ______ (in the direction of force).
  2. 1 joule of work is done when a force of ______ newton displaces an object by 1 metre in the direction of the force.
  3. The expression for kinetic energy of a body of mass \(m\) and velocity \(v\) is ______.
  4. The potential energy of an object of mass \(m\) at a small height \(h\) from the Earth's surface is ______.
  5. Power is defined as the ______ at which work is done.
Q3 Multiple Correct
NCERT

When a ball thrown upwards reaches its highest point, tick the correct statement(s).

  1. The force acting on the ball is zero.
  2. The acceleration of the ball is zero.
  3. Its kinetic energy is zero.
  4. Its potential energy is maximum.
Q4 Energy Transformation
NCERT

Identify the energy transformation in each situation: truck moving uphill; unwinding of a watch spring; photosynthesis in green leaves; water flowing from a dam; burning of a matchstick; explosion of a fire cracker; speaking into a microphone; a glowing electric bulb; a solar panel.

Q5 Potential Energy
NCERT

A student is slowly lifted straight up in an elevator from the ground level to the top floor and later climbs the staircase to the same top. Given \(h=72.5\,\mathrm{m}\), \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\), \(m=50\,\mathrm{kg}\):

  1. Find the gain in potential energy when lifted straight up.
  2. Find the gain when the student climbs the stairs.
  3. What do you conclude about dependence of potential energy on path?
Q6 Energy and Power
NCERT

A crane lifts a mass \(m\) to the 10th floor in a certain time and then raises the same mass to the 20th floor in double the time. How much more energy and power are required? Assume equal floor heights.

Q7 Power Application
NCERT

Which factors determine the energy required to raise a flag from the ground to the top of a tall flagpole using a pulley? Does raising it slowly or quickly change the work done? If the speed is doubled, how does the power requirement change? Explain.

Q8 Kinetic Energy Ratio
NCERT

A man of mass \(60\,\mathrm{kg}\) rides a scooter of mass \(100\,\mathrm{kg}\). His \(40\,\mathrm{kg}\) son later joins him. If the scooter reaches the same speed on both days in the same time and fuel supplies all the energy without other losses, find the ratio of fuel used.

Q9 Lever / Seesaw Diagram
NCERT

On a seesaw, an adult weighs twice as much as a child, yet the seesaw is balanced. Draw a figure showing the distances from the fulcrum where the child and adult should sit.

Q10 Work by Gravity
NCERT

A \(2\,\mathrm{kg}\) ball is thrown upward at \(20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).

  1. Identify the sign of work done by gravity during upward and downward motion.
  2. If it reaches \(19.4\,\mathrm{m}\), find the work done by air resistance. Take \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\).

Q15 Energy Dissipation
NCERT

  2. If the average resistive force is \(3000\,\mathrm{N}\) and all its energy is used to create the depression, calculate the depression depth. Take \(g=10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\).
