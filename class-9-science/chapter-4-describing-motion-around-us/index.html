Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in This Chapter

Motion is a change in the position of an object over time with respect to a reference point. In this chapter, we build a mathematical and graphical foundation for describing motion in a straight line and in a plane.

Key Topics Covered

- Position description using reference points (origin) and directional signs (+/-)
- Difference between Distance (scalar) and Displacement (vector)
- Average Speed vs. Average Velocity with rates of change
- Average Acceleration (increasing/decreasing velocities)
- Graphical representation (Position-Time & Velocity-Time slope and area integrations)
- Derivation and applications of the 3 Kinematic Equations of Motion
- Uniform Circular Motion and tangential velocity vector release mechanics
