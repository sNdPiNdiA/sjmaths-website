Basic Cyber Security & Viruses Core Study Notes

This topic is vital for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations as questions regarding malware, computer viruses, worms, trojans, and Information Technology Acts are frequently asked. Candidates must understand the classifications, mechanisms, and preventive measures against cyber threats to clear the Computer Knowledge section with high accuracy.

Fundamentals of Computer Viruses

Understanding the definition, properties, and core behavior of malicious software programs.

  • Definition of Virus: A computer virus is a self-replicating program that attaches itself to clean files or legitimate programs and spreads when those files are executed.
  • Boot Sector Virus: It infects the master boot record (MBR) or volume boot sector of storage media, executing before the operating system boots up completely.
  • Macro Virus: Written in macro languages (such as Visual Basic for Applications) and embedded inside software documents like MS Word, Excel, or PowerPoint.
  • Polymorphic Virus: A complex virus that changes its identifiable signature every time it replicates to evade detection by conventional signature-based antivirus scanners.
  • Stealth Virus: Employs various techniques to actively hide its presence from operating system utilities and antivirus software by intercepting system calls.

Worms, Trojans, and Spyware

Examining non-virus malware categories that pose significant threats to corporate and governmental networks.

  • Computer Worms: Standalone malicious programs that replicate themselves to spread from computer to computer across networks without requiring user interaction.
  • Trojan Horse: Disguises itself as legitimate or useful software but performs covert malicious actions upon execution, named after the Greek mythological wooden horse.
  • Spyware & Adware: Spyware covertly gathers user information (keystrokes, passwords), while Adware automatically delivers unwanted advertisements and pop-ups.
  • Ransomware: Malware that locks computer screens or encrypts user files, demanding a cryptocurrency ransom for providing the decryption key.
  • Rootkits: A collection of software tools designed to enable unauthorized access to a computer or an area of its software while masking its existence.

Cyber Attacks & Social Engineering

Analyzing human-centric vulnerabilities and systemic network attacks.

  • Phishing & Spear Phishing: Fraudulent practice of sending emails purporting to be from reputable companies to induce individuals to reveal personal sensitive data.
  • DDoS Attacks: Distributed Denial of Service floods a targeted server with internet traffic to prevent legitimate users from accessing services.
  • Man-in-the-Middle (MitM): An attack where the cybercriminal secretly intercepts and relays communications between two parties who believe they are directly communicating.
  • Zero-Day Exploit: A cyber attack that targets a software vulnerability that is unknown to the software vendor or hardware manufacturer, leaving zero days for defense.

Security Defenses & Indian Cyber Laws

Understanding protective tools, protocols, and the legal framework in India.

  • Antivirus & Firewall: Antivirus detects and removes malware, while a firewall monitors and filters incoming and outgoing network traffic based on security rules.
  • CERT-In: Indian Computer Emergency Response Team is the national nodal agency for responding to computer security incidents under the Ministry of MeitY.
  • IT Act 2000 Sections: Section 66 deals with computer-related offenses, Section 66C punishes identity theft, and Section 66D covers cheating by personating using computer resources.
  • Digital Signatures: A mathematical scheme for verifying the authenticity of digital messages or documents, governed under Section 3 of the IT Act.
