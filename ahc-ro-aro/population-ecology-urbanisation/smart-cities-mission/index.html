Smart Cities Mission Core Study Notes

The Smart Cities Mission is a flagship urban renewal program launched by the Government of India to promote sustainable cities that provide core infrastructure, clean environment, and 'Smart' solutions. For Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, questions frequently focus on mission objectives, selection rounds, Uttar Pradesh cities included, and specialized urban governance frameworks.

Core Objectives and Strategy

Understanding the fundamental philosophy, pillars, and strategic approaches driving the Smart Cities Mission in India.

  • Mission Vision: To promote cities that provide core infrastructure and give a decent quality of life to its citizens, a clean and sustainable environment and application of 'Smart' Solutions.
  • Core Infrastructure Elements: Adequate water supply, assured electricity supply, sanitation including solid waste management, efficient urban mobility and public transport, affordable housing, robust IT connectivity, and digitalization.
  • Strategic Components: Includes City Improvement (Retrofitting), City Renewal (Redevelopment), City Extension (Greenfield development), and Pan-city initiatives involving the application of smart solutions to existing infrastructure.
  • Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV): Each smart city has a dedicated SPV headed by a full-time CEO, incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, to plan, appraise, release funds, implement, manage, operate, and monitor smart city projects.

Smart Cities in Uttar Pradesh

Detailed breakdown of cities selected from Uttar Pradesh under the Smart Cities Mission and their specialized focus areas.

  • Total Cities from UP: A total of 10 cities from Uttar Pradesh have been selected across various rounds of the Smart Cities Mission competition.
  • Varanasi & Lucknow: Varanasi (Prime Minister's constituency) focuses on heritage conservation, ghat management, and intelligent traffic; Lucknow focuses on e-governance, integrated traffic management, and safe city projects.
  • Agra & Kanpur: Agra focuses on tourism facilitation and solar energy integration; Kanpur emphasizes industrial waste management, river cleaning (Ganga action plan linkage), and modern transport hubs.
  • NCR Region & Other Cities: Ghaziabad, Meerut, Bareilly, Aligarh, Jhansi, and Prayagraj (Allahabad) complete the list of 10 UP cities, prioritizing smart metering, solid waste processing, and digitized civic services.

Financial Framework and Funding

Analysis of budgetary allocation, central-state matching grants, and alternate financing mechanisms.

  • Central Government Assistance: The Central Government contributes financial support of Rs. 48,000 crore over five years, averaging Rs. 100 crore per city per year.
  • Matching State/ULB Share: An equal amount on a matching basis is to be contributed by the respective State Government / Urban Local Body (ULB).
  • Innovative Financing: Encourages Public-Private Partnerships (PPP), convergence with other central schemes like AMRUT, Swachh Bharat Mission, and municipal bonds issuance.

Evaluation and Performance Metrics

Indices and assessment frameworks used to track the progress of Indian smart cities.

  • Ease of Living Index: Assesses the quality of life of urban citizens across institutional, social, economic, and physical pillars administered by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA).
  • Municipal Performance Index (MPI): Evaluates the delivery of civic services, governance, financial performance, planning, and technology adoption by municipal bodies.
  • India Smart Cities Awards (ISCA): Annual awards instituted by MoHUA to recognize and reward cities, states, and partners for innovative and exemplary work under the Smart Cities Mission.
