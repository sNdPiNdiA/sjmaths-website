Population Growth Trends & Census 2011 Core Study Notes

Population Growth Trends and Census 2011 form a high-scoring component for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. This module covers decadal growth rates, demographic dividend, density, sex ratio, and literacy figures for both India and Uttar Pradesh with extreme factual accuracy.

India Population Demographics (Census 2011)

Essential macro-level indicators of India's population as per the 2011 Census report.

  • Total Population: India's total population stood at 1,210.85 million (1.21 billion), accounting for 17.5% of the world's population.
  • Decadal Growth Rate (2001-2011): The growth rate slowed down to 17.70% during 2001-2011 compared to 21.54% in the previous decade (1991-2001).
  • Population Density: National population density increased from 325 persons/sq.km in 2001 to 382 persons/sq.km in 2011.
  • Sex Ratio: Overall sex ratio improved to 943 females per 1,000 males (up from 933 in 2001). Child sex ratio (0-6 years) declined to 918.
  • Literacy Rate: Total literacy rate reached 74.04%, with male literacy at 82.14% and female literacy at 65.46%.

Uttar Pradesh Population Demographics

Crucial UP-specific demographic metrics vital for RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations.

  • UP Total Population: UP houses 19.98 crore people, making up 16.51% of India's total population, ranking 1st among all states.
  • UP Decadal Growth: Decadal growth rate for UP during 2001-2011 stood at 20.23%, higher than the national average of 17.70%.
  • UP Population Density: Population density of UP is 829 persons/sq.km, placing it amongst the densest major states.
  • UP Sex Ratio: Overall sex ratio of UP is 912 females per 1,000 males, while the child sex ratio (0-6 years) is 902.
  • UP Literacy Rate: Total literacy in UP is 67.7% (Male: 77.3%, Female: 57.2%). Gautam Buddha Nagar has the highest literacy (80.1%).

Urbanisation Trends in India & UP

Analysis of urban population distribution, urban growth rates, and municipal classification.

  • India Urban Population: 31.16% of India's population (approx. 377 million) lives in urban areas as per Census 2011.
  • Most Urbanized State/UT: Goa is the most urbanized state (62.1%), whereas Delhi UT has 97.5% urban population. Among major states, Tamil Nadu leads.
  • UP Urbanisation Percentage: Uttar Pradesh has 22.27% urban population, significantly lower than the national average of 31.16%.
  • Most Urbanized District in UP: Ghaziabad is the most urbanized district in UP with 67.6% urban population, followed by Lucknow and Kanpur Nagar.

Demographic Dividend & Theories

Theoretical perspectives on population growth and economic impact of age-structure transformations.

  • Malthusian Theory of Population: Proposed by Thomas Malthus in 1798; states that population grows geometrically while food supply grows arithmetically.
  • Demographic Transition Theory: Explains the transition from high birth/death rates to low birth/death rates across 4 distinct developmental stages.
  • India's Demographic Window: India is experiencing a demographic dividend where over 65% of the population is in the working-age group (15-59 years).
