Literacy Sex Ratio & Demographic Indicators (Census 2011) Core Study Notes

This topic covers critical demographic indicators as per Census 2011, focusing extensively on India and Uttar Pradesh. It is a high-yield area for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, testing precise data regarding literacy rates, sex ratios, child sex ratios, and demographic trends.

India Literacy Profile (Census 2011)

Detailed breakdown of national literacy rates, gender differentials, and state-level rankings.

  • Total Literacy Rate (India): India's overall literacy rate stood at 74.04%, with male literacy at 82.14% and female literacy at 65.46%.
  • Top State in Literacy: Kerala ranked first in India with a literacy rate of 94.00%, followed closely by Mizoram at 91.33%.
  • Lowest State in Literacy: Bihar recorded the lowest literacy rate in India at 61.80%, preceded by Arunachal Pradesh at 65.38%.
  • Gender Gap in Literacy: The absolute gender gap in literacy at the national level narrowed to 16.68 percentage points in 2011 from 21.59 percentage points in 2001.

Sex Ratio Dynamics (Census 2011)

Analysis of overall sex ratio, child sex ratio (0-6 years), and regional variations.

  • Overall Sex Ratio (India): India's sex ratio improved to 943 females per 1,000 males in 2011, compared to 933 in 2001.
  • Child Sex Ratio (0-6 Years): The child sex ratio (0-6 years) dropped sharply from 927 in 2001 to 919 in 2011, raising serious concerns regarding female foeticide.
  • Top State for Sex Ratio: Kerala recorded the highest sex ratio in India at 1,084 females per 1,000 males, followed by Puducherry (1,037 among UTs).
  • Lowest State for Sex Ratio: Haryana recorded the lowest overall sex ratio in India at 879 females per 1,000 males.

Uttar Pradesh Demographic Particulars (Census 2011)

State-specific demographic figures essential for UPPSC and AHC RO/ARO examinations.

  • UP Population & Share: UP is India's most populous state with a population of 19,98,12,341, accounting for roughly 16.51% of India's total population.
  • UP Sex Ratio: The overall sex ratio of Uttar Pradesh is 912 females per 1,000 males, and the child sex ratio (0-6 years) is 902.
  • UP District Extremes in Literacy: Gautam Buddha Nagar has the highest literacy rate in UP (80.12%), while Shravasti has the lowest (46.74%).
  • UP District Extremes in Sex Ratio: Jaunpur has the highest sex ratio in UP (1,024), while Gautam Buddha Nagar records the lowest (851).

Urbanisation and Demographic Dividend

Urban growth trends, definition of urban areas, and demographic structure.

  • India Urban Population Share: India's urban population stood at 31.16% of the total population as per Census 2011, totaling approx. 37.7 crore people.
  • UP Urbanisation Rate: Uttar Pradesh has an urbanisation rate of 22.27%, with Ghaziabad and Lucknow being among the most urbanised districts.
  • Definition of Statutory Town: All places with a municipality, corporation, cantonment board, or notified town area committee are classified as statutory towns.
  • Demographic Dividend Window: India is experiencing a demographic dividend phase where over 50% of its population is below 25 years and over 65% is aged 15-64 years.
