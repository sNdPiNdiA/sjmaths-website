Proportional Paragraph Reduction (Précis Writing for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO) Core Study Notes

Précis writing is a crucial component of the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO Mains descriptive examination, testing a candidate's ability to condense lengthy passages to one-third of their original length while preserving the core essence, formal tone, and logical flow.

Fundamental Rules and Length Proportions

Core principles governing how text must be reduced without losing administrative clarity or legal accuracy.

  • One-Third Rule: The final précis must strictly be around one-third (33%) of the original passage's word count, allowing a permissible deviation of plus or minus 5%.
  • Third-Person Narration: Précis must always be written in the third person (he, she, it, they, the author) and indirect speech, avoiding direct first-person ('I', 'We') or second-person ('You') pronouns.
  • Elimination of Ornamentation: All rhetorical flourishes, metaphors, secondary examples, figures of speech, and repetitive anecdotes must be pruned.
  • Objective Standpoint: Candidates must never incorporate personal opinions, critiques, or external facts not present in the given text.

Methodology of Skimming and Title Formulation

Techniques for grasping the central theme swiftly and deriving an appropriate administrative heading.

  • Skimming Technique: Rapid initial reading focusing on the opening and concluding sentences of paragraphs where core arguments usually reside.
  • Central Theme Isolation: Identifying the single dominant idea that connects all sentences, ensuring subordinate details are discarded.
  • Title Formulation Criteria: Titles must be concise (3 to 6 words), apt, reflective of the core subject, and placed at the top before writing the précis body.
  • Logical Continuity: Sentences in the précis must follow the exact chronological or logical sequence of arguments presented in the original text.
