Maintaining One Third Length in Précis Writing Core Study Notes

Maintaining strictly one-third length of the original passage is a fundamental evaluation criterion in the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC Mains examination. This module covers core strategies for condensing text mathematically while preserving core ideas, tone, and logical coherence without adding personal commentary.

Mathematical Proportion & Word Limit Management

Understanding the strict adherence required for maintaining the one-third proportion in official examinations.

  • The 33% Rule: If the source passage contains 300 words, the précis must strictly fall within 95 to 105 words (approx. 100 words).
  • Acceptable Variance: Examiners generally permit a minor variance of plus-minus 10% around the one-third mark, but exceeding this incurs negative marking.
  • Exclusion of Minor Details: To achieve 1/3rd length, all illustrations, examples, parenthetical remarks, and repetitive adjectives must be eliminated.
  • Word Count Calculation Box: Aspirants must explicitly write the final word count at the end of the answer sheet as mandated in UPPSC/AHC Mains.

Condensation Techniques & Elimination Strategies

Mastering the transformation of lengthy descriptions into concise, high-impact administrative terminology.

  • Substitution of Clauses with Single Words: Convert phrases like 'a person who does not believe in God' into 'Atheist' (नास्तिक) to drastically reduce length.
  • Omission of Direct Speeches and Dialogue: Convert direct quotations or dialogues into indirect narrative format to save space and maintain formal tone.
  • Removal of Redundancies and Tautologies: Eliminate redundant pairs like 'true and accurate' or 'null and void' by retaining only the essential root term.
  • Synthesis of Multiple Sentences: Combine three or four descriptive sentences into a single complex or compound sentence without losing factual essence.

Preserving Logical Flow & Objective Tone

Maintaining structural integrity, chronological sequence, and neutrality in the condensed draft.

  • Third-Person Perspective: Never use 'I', 'We', or 'You'. The précis must be written strictly in the third person (he, she, it, they, or passive voice).
  • Exclusion of Personal Opinions: Avoid phrases like 'I think', 'In my opinion', or adding critical comments on the author's arguments.
  • Logical Transition Markers: Use concise conjunctions like 'consequently', 'however', 'therefore' to maintain smooth flow between compressed ideas.
  • Fidelity to Original Text: The summary must reflect the exact argument of the author without distortion, misinterpretation, or exaggeration.

Title Formulation & Final Polish

Techniques for deriving a precise, catchy, and apt heading for the précis as demanded in examinations.

  • Relevance to Central Theme: The title must capture the core subject in 2 to 5 words and reflect the overarching message of the passage.
  • Placement and Formatting: The title should be placed at the very top of the précis, underlined or highlighted clearly for the examiner.
  • Avoid Interrogative Titles: Do not frame titles as questions (e.g., 'What is democracy?'); instead, use declarative noun phrases (e.g., 'Essence of Democracy').
