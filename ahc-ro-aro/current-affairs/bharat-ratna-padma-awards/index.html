Bharat Ratna and Padma Awards Core Study Notes

A highly critical topic for AHC RO/ARO and UPPSC exams, covering the constitutional validity under Article 18, historical evolution, and the latest 2024 awardees. Questions frequently target legal provisions, UP-specific recipients, and chronological milestones.

Constitutional & Legal Framework

Understanding the constitutional status, legal boundaries, and rules governing India's highest civilian decorations.

  • Article 18(1) and National Awards: Under Article 18(1) of the Constitution of India, these awards are 'decorations' and cannot be used as a prefix or suffix to the recipient's name. Any such use results in forfeiture of the award.
  • Balaji Raghavan v. Union of India (1995): The Supreme Court ruled that National Awards do not amount to 'titles' within the meaning of Article 18(1), as they recognize merit and public service, which does not violate the principle of equality.
  • No Monetary Grant: Unlike Nobel Prizes or other international awards, the Bharat Ratna and Padma Awards carry no monetary grant or cash prize. Recipients receive a Sanad (certificate) signed by the President and a medallion.
  • Annual Limit and Recommendations: By convention, the Bharat Ratna is limited to a maximum of three individuals in a single year (though relaxed to five in 2024). Recommendations are made by the Prime Minister directly to the President.

Bharat Ratna 2024 Laureates

Detailed profiles of the five eminent personalities conferred with the Bharat Ratna in 2024.

  • Karpoori Thakur (Posthumous): Known as 'Jan Nayak', he was a two-time Chief Minister of Bihar. He pioneered reservation for the Other Backward Classes (OBCs) through the Mungeri Lal Commission report implementation.
  • Chaudhary Charan Singh (Posthumous): The 5th Prime Minister of India and a legendary peasant leader from Uttar Pradesh. He played a pivotal role in the abolition of the Zamindari system in UP and championed farmers' rights.
  • P. V. Narasimha Rao (Posthumous): The 9th Prime Minister of India, often called the 'Father of Indian Economic Reforms'. He dismantled the License Raj and introduced LPG (Liberalisation, Privatisation, and Globalisation) reforms in 1991.
  • Dr. M. S. Swaminathan (Posthumous): Renowned agricultural scientist known as the 'Father of India's Green Revolution'. He introduced high-yielding varieties of wheat and rice, transforming India from a food-deficit to a food-surplus nation.
  • Lal Krishna Advani: Veteran statesman and the 7th Deputy Prime Minister of India. He served as the Union Home Minister and was a key architect of the contemporary political landscape of India.

Padma Awards 2024: Analysis & Key Recipients

Statistical breakdown and prominent winners of the Padma Vibhushan, Padma Bhushan, and Padma Shri in 2024.

  • Total Awards Count: For the year 2024, the President approved the conferment of 132 Padma Awards, comprising 5 Padma Vibhushan, 17 Padma Bhushan, and 110 Padma Shri Awards.
  • Demographic Diversity: The list includes 30 women, 8 persons from the category of Foreigners/NRI/PIO/OCI, and 9 posthumous awardees, ensuring wide representation.
  • Padma Vibhushan Awardees: The 5 recipients are: Ms. Vyjayanthimala Bali (Art), Shri Konidela Chiranjeevi (Art), Shri M. Venkaiah Naidu (Public Affairs), Shri Bindeshwar Pathak (Posthumous - Social Work), and Ms. Padma Subrahmanyam (Art).
  • Uttar Pradesh Specific Awardees: Crucial for RO/ARO: Key awardees from UP include Shri Ram Chet Chaudhary (Science & Engineering), Shri Radha Krishan Dhiman (Medicine), and Shri Surendra Prasad Gupta (Medicine) among others.
