India's Foreign Relations Core Study Notes

An exhaustive analysis of India's foreign policy, bilateral relations, multilateral engagements (G20, BRICS, SCO, Quad), and key diplomatic doctrines. This topic is highly critical for the UPPSC and Allahabad High Court RO/ARO exams, focusing on strategic treaties, constitutional provisions, and international summits.

Constitutional Provisions & Foreign Policy Doctrines

India's foreign relations are guided by specific constitutional mandates and evolving strategic doctrines designed to protect national sovereignty and promote global peace.

  • Article 51 (DPSP): Directs the State to promote international peace and security, maintain just and honorable relations between nations, and encourage settlement of international disputes by arbitration.
  • Article 253: Empowers the Parliament of India to make laws for the whole or any part of the territory of India for implementing any treaty, agreement, or convention with any other country.
  • Gujral Doctrine (1996): A set of five principles formulated by I.K. Gujral, advocating non-reciprocal unilateral concessions to India's immediate smaller neighbors to build trust.
  • Neighborhood First Policy: A core component of India's foreign policy focusing on peaceful relations and collaborative synergy with South Asian neighbors (BIMSTEC, SAARC).
  • Act East Policy (2014): Upgraded from the 1991 'Look East Policy' under PM Narendra Modi, focusing on extended security, economic, and cultural integration with the Asia-Pacific region, particularly ASEAN.

Multilateral Engagements & Global Groupings

India actively participates in key multilateral forums to shape global governance, trade, and security architectures.

  • G20 Presidency (2023): Held under the theme 'One Earth, One Family, One Future'. Key outcomes included the New Delhi Leaders' Declaration, launch of the Global Biofuels Alliance, and the India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (IMEC).
  • BRICS Expansion: At the 15th BRICS Summit in Johannesburg (2023), membership was expanded. India supported this expansion to champion the voice of the Global South.
  • The Quad (Quadrilateral Security Dialogue): Consists of India, USA, Japan, and Australia. It aims to ensure a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific region, countering unilateral dominance.
  • Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO): India became a full member in 2017 (alongside Pakistan). India hosted the 23rd SCO Summit virtually in July 2023, adopting the New Delhi Declaration.

Key Bilateral Relations & Strategic Partnerships

India's strategic partnerships with global powers are crucial for defense, technology transfer, and economic growth.

  • India-USA Relations: Elevated to 'Comprehensive Global Strategic Partnership'. Key agreements signed include the four foundational defense pacts: GSOMIA, LEMOA, COMCASA, and BECA. Collaborative initiatives like iCET (Initiative on Critical and Emerging Technology) drive tech cooperation.
  • India-Russia Relations: Characterized as a 'Special and Privileged Strategic Partnership'. Key defense collaborations include the S-400 Triumf air defense system, BrahMos missile joint venture, and Kudankulam Nuclear Power Plant (KKNPP) in Tamil Nadu.
  • India-France Relations: Marked 25 years of Strategic Partnership in 2023. Key areas include defense (Rafale fighter jets, Scorpene submarines under Project-75) and space cooperation between ISRO and CNES.
  • India-Japan Relations: Collaborating on the Mumbai-Ahmedabad High-Speed Rail (MAHSR) project. Joint military exercises include 'Dharma Guardian' (Army) and 'JIMEX' (Navy).

Humanitarian Assistance & Disaster Relief (HADR) Operations

India has emerged as a first responder in global crises, showcasing its soft power and logistical capability through various evacuation and relief operations.

  • Operation Ganga (2022): An evacuation mission to bring back Indian citizens, mostly medical students, from Ukraine during the 2022 Russian invasion via neighboring countries like Poland, Romania, and Hungary.
  • Operation Dost (2023): A search and rescue operation initiated by the Government of India to assist Turkey and Syria after a devastating earthquake, deploying NDRF teams and field hospitals.
  • Operation Kaveri (2023): Conducted by the Indian Armed Forces to evacuate Indian citizens from conflict-torn Sudan, utilizing Indian Navy ships and Indian Air Force aircraft.
  • Operation Ajay (2023): Launched to facilitate the return of Indian citizens from Israel during the 2023 Israel-Hamas conflict.
