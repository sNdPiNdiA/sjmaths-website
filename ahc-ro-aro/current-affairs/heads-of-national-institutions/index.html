Heads Of National Institutions Core Study Notes

This topic covers the key constitutional, statutory, and executive heads of major national institutions in India. It is highly crucial for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC exams, where direct factual questions regarding current appointments, constitutional articles, and institutional tenures are frequently asked.

Constitutional Heads of India

Constitutional posts are established by the Constitution of India and require high-level appointments by the President of India.

  • Chief Justice of India (CJI): Justice Sanjiv Khanna is the 51st Chief Justice of India, succeeding Justice D.Y. Chandrachud. Appointed under Article 124(2) of the Constitution, the CJI holds office until the age of 65 years.
  • Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG): K. Sanjay Murthy is the newly appointed CAG of India, succeeding G.C. Murmu. Appointed under Article 148, the CAG has a tenure of 6 years or up to the age of 65 years, whichever is earlier.
  • Chief Election Commissioner (CEC): Rajiv Kumar is the 25th Chief Election Commissioner of India. Assisted by Election Commissioners Gyanesh Kumar and Sukhbir Singh Sandhu, appointed under Article 324.
  • Attorney General for India: R. Venkataramani is the current Attorney General for India (16th). Appointed by the President under Article 76, he is the chief legal advisor to the Government of India.

Financial and Regulatory Institutions

Regulatory bodies govern the financial, market, and banking sectors of the Indian economy.

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Governor: Shaktikanta Das serves as the 25th Governor of the RBI. Established under the RBI Act, 1934, the central bank regulates the monetary policy of India.
  • Securities and Exchange Board of India (SEBI): Madhabi Puri Buch is the Chairperson of SEBI, making her the first woman to lead the securities market regulator. SEBI was given statutory status in 1992.

Scientific and Defense Organizations

Leaders of India's premier space, defense, and security agencies driving technological advancements.

  • ISRO Chairman: S. Somanath is the 10th Chairman of the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) and Secretary of the Department of Space. He played a pivotal role in the Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1 missions.
  • DRDO Chairman: Dr. Samir V. Kamat is the Chairman of the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), succeeding Dr. G. Satheesh Reddy.
  • Chief of Defence Staff (CDS): General Anil Chauhan is the 2nd Chief of Defence Staff of India, succeeding the late General Bipin Rawat. The CDS acts as the principal military advisor to the Minister of Defence.
  • National Security Advisor (NSA): Ajit Doval is the National Security Advisor of India. He is the primary advisor to the Prime Minister of India on national and international security policies.

Policy and Advisory Bodies

Key figures leading the planning, financial allocation, and law reform advisory bodies of India.

  • NITI Aayog Leadership: The Prime Minister (Narendra Modi) is the Chairperson. Suman Bery is the current Vice-Chairperson, and B.V.R. Subrahmanyam is the Chief Executive Officer (CEO).
  • 16th Finance Commission Chairman: Dr. Arvind Panagariya (former Vice-Chairman of NITI Aayog) is the Chairman of the 16th Finance Commission, constituted under Article 280 of the Constitution to recommend tax devolution for the period 2026-2031.
  • Law Commission of India: Justice (Retd) Ritu Raj Awasthi served as the Chairperson of the 22nd Law Commission of India, which advises the government on complex legal reforms.
