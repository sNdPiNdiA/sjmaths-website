Diseases & Nutrition Core Study Notes

The Diseases and Nutrition module is a high-yield segment for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. It covers essential macro and micronutrients, vitamin deficiency disorders, communicable and non-communicable diseases, and public health initiatives critical for administrative aptitude.

Macronutrients & Micronutrients (Vitamins & Minerals)

Detailed analysis of dietary components required for human metabolism, energy production, and physiological regulation.

  • Fat-Soluble Vitamins: Vitamins A, D, E, and K are soluble in fats, stored in the liver and adipose tissues, and excessive accumulation can lead to hypervitaminosis.
  • Water-Soluble Vitamins: Vitamin C and the B-complex group dissolve in water, cannot be stored significantly in the body, and require regular daily dietary replenishment.
  • Vitamin A (Retinol): Deficiency causes Night Blindness (Nyctalopia), Xerophthalmia, and Bitot's spots. Found abundantly in carrots, cod liver oil, and green leafy vegetables.
  • Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin): Contains Cobalt metal. Its deficiency leads to Pernicious Anemia and neurological impairments. It is synthesized by gut microbiota in animals.
  • Mineral Deficiencies: Iron deficiency causes microcytic anemia; Iodine deficiency leads to goitre and cretinism; Calcium and Phosphorus deficiency results in rickets and osteomalacia.

Communicable & Infectious Diseases

Examination focus on pathogens (bacteria, viruses, protozoa, fungi) transmitting diseases and vector-borne illnesses.

  • Bacterial Diseases: Includes Tuberculosis (Mycobacterium tuberculosis), Typhoid (Salmonella typhi), Cholera (Vibrio cholerae), Diphtheria, and Tetanus.
  • Viral Diseases: Includes Rabies (Rhabdovirus), Dengue (Flavivirus, transmitted by Aedes aegypti), Polio, AIDS (HIV), Influenza, and Smallpox.
  • Protozoan Diseases: Malaria is caused by Plasmodium species (P. vivax, P. falciparum) and transmitted by female Anopheles mosquitoes. Amoebiasis is caused by Entamoeba histolytica.
  • Fungal Infections: Dermatophytes cause ringworm (Tinea infections), athlete's foot, and candidiasis. Common in humid and poorly ventilated conditions.

Nutritional Deficiencies & Malnutrition Disorders

Pathological conditions arising from inadequate caloric or protein intake, prevalent among children in developing nations.

  • Kwashiorkor: Caused strictly by severe protein deficiency while total caloric intake may be adequate. Symptoms include edema (swollen belly), thin hair, and skin pigmentation.
  • Marasmus: Caused by a simultaneous deficiency of both proteins and total calories (energy). Characterized by extreme emaciation, shrunken ribs, and arrested physical growth in infants.
  • Goitre (Galaganda): Enlargement of the thyroid gland due to dietary iodine deficiency, leading to decreased synthesis of thyroxine hormone.

Public Health Schemes & Immunization in India

Key administrative programs and vaccination missions administered by the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare.

  • Mission Indradhanush: Launched in 2014 to achieve full immunization coverage for children up to 2 years of age and pregnant women against 12 vaccine-preventable diseases.
  • National Health Mission (NHM): Encompasses National Rural Health Mission (NRHM) and National Urban Health Mission (NUHM) to provide accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare.
  • Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: World's largest health insurance/assurance scheme fully financed by the government, providing health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care.
