Hindi Varnamala (Alphabet & Phonology) Core Study Notes

Hindi Varnamala forms the absolute foundation of General Hindi for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. Questions routinely test classification based on pronunciation place (Uccharan Sthan), breath effort (Pran), and vocal cord vibration (Ghosh).

Classification of Swar (Vowels)

Vowels are independent sounds uttered without any obstruction in the vocal tract. Modern standard Hindi recognizes 11 distinct vowels.

  • Total Count (Kul Swar): There are 11 written vowels (A, Aa, I, Ee, U, Oo, Ri, E, Ai, O, Au). If Anusvara (AM) and Visarga (Ah) are included as Ayogavaha, the total reaches 13.
  • Hrasva Swar (Short Vowels): Also known as Mool Swar or Ekamatrik swar. They include 4 vowels: A, I, U, and Ri.
  • Deergha Swar (Long Vowels): Formed by the combination of similar short vowels or homorganic vowels. They include 7 vowels: Aa, Ee, Oo, E, Ai, O, Au.
  • Ayogavaha (Anusvara & Visarga): Neither purely vowels nor consonants, they are termed Ayogavaha because they 'carry' (vah) meaning only when attached to a vowel (e.g., Anusvara 'ṁ' and Visarga 'ḥ'). Coined by Acharya Dhirendra Varma.

Classification of Vyanjan (Consonants)

Consonants are sounds that require the help of vowels for articulation and involve some obstruction in the oral cavity.

  • Sparsh Vyanjan (Plosives / Occlusives): Spanning from Ka (क) to Ma (म), divided into 5 groups (Vargas: Kavarg, Chavarg, Tavarg, T[retroflex]varg, Pavarg). Total count is 25.
  • Antastha Vyanjan (Semi-Vowels): Positioned between vowels and consonants. These include 4 letters: Ya (य), Ra (र), La (ल), Va (व).
  • Ushma / Sangharshi Vyanjan (Sibilants / Fricatives): Pronounced with audible friction, producing warm breath. These include 4 letters: Sha (श), Sh[retroflex]a (ष), Sa (स), Ha (ह).
  • Samyukt Vyanjan (Compound Consonants): Formed by joining two distinct consonants: Ksha (क्ष = क् + ष), Tra (त्र = त् + र), Gya (ज्ञ = ज् + ञ), Shra (श्र = श् + र).
  • Uchipta / Tadatadjata Vyanjan (Flaps): Tongue touches the roof of the mouth and flaps down instantly. These include 2 letters: Da (ड़) and Dha (ढ़).

Pronunciation Places (Uccharan Sthan)

Identifying the precise anatomical point of origin in the vocal tract for each letter is heavily tested in AHC RO/ARO exams.

  • Kantha (Velar / Throat): Covered by Sanskrit aphorism 'Kuhvisarjaniyanaman Kanthah': Includes 'A', 'Aa', Kavarg (क, ख, ग, घ, ङ), 'Ha' (ह), and Visarga (ः).
  • Talavya (Palatal): Covered by 'Ichhayshasanam Taluh': Includes 'I', 'Ee', Chavarg (च, छ, ज, झ, ञ), 'Ya' (य), and 'Sha' (श).
  • Murdhanya (Retroflex): Covered by 'Riturasanam Murdha': Includes 'Ri' (ऋ), Tavarg (ट, ठ, ड, ढ, ण), 'Ra' (र), and 'Sha' (ष).
  • Dantya (Dental): Covered by 'Litulasanaman Dantah': Includes Tavarg (त, थ, द, ध, न), 'La' (ल), and 'Sa' (स).
  • Oshthya (Labial): Covered by 'Upupadmaniyanam Oshthau': Includes 'U', 'Oo', and Pavarg (प, फ, ब, भ, म).
  • Dantoshthya & Kanthtalavya: Dantoshthya: 'Va' (व - Teeth + Lips). Kanthtalavya: 'E', 'Ai' (ए, ऐ - Throat + Palate). Kanthoshthya: 'O', 'Au' (ओ, औ - Throat + Lips).

Phonetic Properties: Pran (Breath) & Ghosh (Vibration)

Classification based on the volume of air expelled and the vibration of vocal cords.

  • Alpaprana (Unaspirated Consonants): Consonants requiring lesser breath effort. These include the 1st, 3rd, and 5th letters of each Varga, plus all Antastha (य, र, ल, व). Total: 19 + 4 = 23 (along with all vowels which are inherently Alpaprana).
  • Mahaprana (Aspirated Consonants): Consonants requiring higher breath volume with an audible 'h' sound. These include the 2nd and 4th letters of each Varga, plus all Ushma/Fricative consonants (श, ष, स, ह). Total: 10 + 4 = 14.
  • Aghosh (Unvoiced Consonants): Vocal cords do not vibrate, producing unvoiced sound. These include the 1st and 2nd letters of each Varga, plus three sibilants (श, ष, स). Total: 10 + 3 = 13.
  • Saghosh / Ghosh (Voiced Consonants): Vocal cords vibrate, producing a voiced sound. These include the 3rd, 4th, and 5th letters of each Varga, all Antastha (य, र, ल, व), and 'Ha' (ह). Total: 15 + 4 + 1 = 20 (plus all vowels).
