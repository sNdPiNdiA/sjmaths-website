UP Budget and Economy Core Study Notes

The Uttar Pradesh Budget and Economy section forms a high-yield core for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. It evaluates candidates on state financial allocations, macroeconomic indicators, GSDP contributions, capital expenditure priorities, and flagship welfare schemes.

Macroeconomic Indicators & GSDP Structure

Analysis of Gross State Domestic Product (GSDP), sectoral distribution, and per capita income growth in Uttar Pradesh.

  • GSDP Size and Ranking: Uttar Pradesh stands as the second-largest state economy in India by GSDP, contributing significantly to national GDP with a targeted growth rate exceeding the national average.
  • Sectoral Contributions: The economy exhibits structural transformation where the Services sector holds the largest share (approx. 50+%), followed by Agriculture and allied sectors (~25%), and Industry/Manufacturing (~25%).
  • Per Capita Income Trends: Continuous budgetary focus on direct benefit transfers, rural infrastructure, and MSME clusters has accelerated the upward trajectory of per capita GSDP, though regional disparities remain a challenge.
  • Inflation and Price Stability: Consumer Price Index (CPI-Rural and Urban) for UP closely mirrors national trends, managed through targeted public distribution systems and food security interventions.

State Budget Architecture & Fiscal Parameters

Examination of revenue receipts, capital expenditure, fiscal deficit targets, and tax mobilization strategies in UP Budgets.

  • Capital Outlay Expansion: Recent state budgets allocate a record-high percentage of expenditure toward capital outlays, prioritizing expressways, metro rails, power grids, and medical colleges.
  • Tax and Non-Tax Revenue: State's own tax revenue (SOTR) relies heavily on State GST (SGST), excise duties, stamp & registration fees, and vehicle taxes, supplemented by central tax devolution.
  • Fiscal Deficit Compliance: Uttar Pradesh maintains its fiscal deficit well within the statutory limits prescribed by the FRBM Act (typically capped around 3% to 3.5% of GSDP).
  • Revenue Surplus Maintenance: Unlike several states grappling with revenue deficits, UP consistently targets a revenue surplus, ensuring that borrowed funds are channeled purely into productive capital assets.

Flagship Economic Schemes & Industrial Corridors

Strategic initiatives boosting industrial growth, employment generation, and regional balance in Uttar Pradesh.

  • One District One Product (ODOP): Launched as a pioneering scheme to revive indigenous crafts, MSMEs, and rural artisanal clusters across all 75 districts, serving as a model replicated nationally.
  • UP Expressway Industrial Development (UPEIDA): Development of multi-lane access-controlled expressways including Ganga Expressway, Purvanchal Expressway, Bundelkhand Expressway, and Agra-Lucknow Expressway paired with node industrial manufacturing clusters.
  • UP Defense Industrial Corridor: Spanning 6 nodes (Aligarh, Agra, Kanpur, Chitrakoot, Jhansi, and Lucknow), aimed at indigenizing defense manufacturing, attracting FDI, and establishing aerospace clusters.
  • Vishwakarma Shram Samman Yojana: Financial assistance, modern toolkits, and skill upgradation training provided to traditional artisans like weavers, blacksmiths, potters, and cobblers.

Agriculture, Irrigation & Rural Budgetary Allocations

Financial allocations for augmenting crop productivity, free electricity for tube wells, and rural livelihood programs.

  • Agricultural Subsidies and Power: Substantial budgetary outlays dedicated to free electricity supply for private tube-well owners, subsidized seeds, and fertilizers to lower farming input costs.
  • Major Irrigation Projects: Completion and funding of mega irrigation schemes like the Saryu Nahar National Project, Bansagar Dam, and Ken-Betwa river linking project integration.
  • PM Kisan and Direct Transfers: Seamless implementation of PM-Kisan Samman Nidhi alongside state matching grants, bolstering rural demand and purchasing power.
  • Agri-Export Zones and Cold Chains: Establishment of specialized pack-houses, cold storage grids, and Agri-Export Zones (AEZs) for potatoes, mangoes, and basmati rice to reduce post-harvest losses.
