Folk Dances and Songs of Uttar Pradesh Core Study Notes

Folk dances and songs of Uttar Pradesh constitute an essential segment of the UP Special Knowledge syllabus for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. Mastering regional art forms, accompanying musical instruments, thematic occasions, and district-wise distribution is crucial for securing high scores in the general studies paper.

Major Folk Dances of Western and Braj Regions

The Braj and western regions feature vibrant performance arts rooted in mythological narratives and seasonal changes.

  • Rasleela Dance: Associated deeply with Mathura and Vrindavan, depicting the divine plays (Leelas) of Lord Krishna and Radha.
  • Charkula Dance: Performed in the Braj region where women balance a large multi-tiered circular wooden pyramid holding 108 lighted oil lamps on their heads.
  • Mayur Dance: Also known as Peacock Dance, performed in Braj wearing special peacock-feathered dresses during spring and Janmashtami.
  • Khayal Dance: Performed in the Bundelkhand region during the birth of a child, where women balance colorful bamboo structures representing temples on their heads.

Folk Dances of Bundelkhand Region

Bundelkhand boasts martial and harvest-centric folk dances reflecting its rugged terrain and martial history.

  • Diwari / Dwari Dance: Performed by Ahir community during Deepawali, involving vigorous stick-fighting and acrobatics to rhythmic drum beats.
  • Rai Dance: Beli dance of Bundelkhand performed by women carrying earthen pots or lamps on their heads, resembling the movement of a dancing peacock.
  • Kartal Dance: Performed by cultivators during the wheat harvesting season using wooden clappers (Kartal) with high energetic movements.
  • Baraiya Dance: Performed on auspicious occasions like marriage ceremonies, symbolizing joy and prosperity in the Bundelkhand region.

Folk Dances and Musical Traditions of Purvanchal

Eastern Uttar Pradesh (Purvanchal) is rich in ritualistic, satirical, and agricultural folk art traditions.

  • Katgodi Dance: A dancer wears a dummy horse around the waist and performs mock battles accompanied by Dhol and Shehnai.
  • Chhau / Natwari Dance: Natwari is an acrobatic dance of the Ahir community involving fast footwork on bamboo cylinders or earthen pots.
  • Charkha / Kaharwa Dance: Performed primarily by the Kahar community during marriage and festive occasions.
  • Dadra and Kajri Songs: Kajri is famous from Mirzapur/Varanasi sung during monsoon, while Dadra is a semi-classical light vocal genre.

Traditional Folk Songs of Uttar Pradesh

Folk songs capture the emotional landscape, agricultural cycles, and social milestones of rural communities in UP.

  • Birha: One of the most popular folk song genres of Purvanchal (especially Azamgarh, Ghazipur), depicting pain of separation and social commentary.
  • Alha: Heroic ballad from Bundelkhand detailing the 52 battles fought by legendary warriors Alha and Udal against Prithviraj Chauhan.
  • Chaiti: Seasonal folk songs sung in the Hindu month of Chait (March-April), especially prominent in Varanasi, Gorakhpur, and Mathura.
  • Sohhar: Traditional folk songs sung across UP to celebrate the birth of a male child, performed by women during naming ceremonies.
