One District One Product (ODOP) - Uttar Pradesh Core Study Notes

The One District One Product (ODOP) program is a flagship initiative launched by the Government of Uttar Pradesh to revitalize indigenous crafts, traditional industries, and MSMEs. For Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, questions frequently test specific district-product pairings, financial support schemes, and implementation milestones.

Objectives and Core Components of ODOP

Understand the structural goals aimed at employment generation, GI tagging, and global export promotion.

  • Employment Generation (रोजगार सृजन): Aims to create sustainable employment opportunities at the local level, specifically curbing distress migration from rural and semi-urban areas.
  • Geographical Indication (GI Tagging): Aggressively securing GI tags for ODOP items to protect intellectual property rights and authenticate traditional heritage (e.g., Banarasi Brocade, Lucknow Chikankari).
  • Financial Assistance & Mudra Loans: Provision of capital subsidy through Finance Corporation up to 25% to 50% for project costs under the Margin Money Scheme.
  • Skill Development & Toolkit Distribution: Training traditional artisans under Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) paired with free modern toolkits to boost productivity.

Food Processing and Agricultural ODOP Products

A specialized focus is given to perishables and regional agricultural produce to boost farmers' income under ODOP.

  • Allahabad / Prayagraj (प्रयागराज): Guava (अमरूद) is the designated ODOP product, known for its superior sweetness and local processing potential.
  • Lucknow & Hardoi: Mango (आम) for Lucknow and Handloom/Food processing variants; Malihabadi Dusseheri mango holds global GI prestige.
  • Banda & Hamirpur: Shazar Stone Craft (शजर पत्थर शिल्प) in Banda; indigenous stone craft recognized under ODOP.
  • Kushinagar & Barabanki: Banana fiber products (केला रेशा उत्पाद) for Kushinagar; Handloom textile for Barabanki.

Financial Schemes & Institutional Support

Schemes supporting capital infusion, marketing, and skill enhancement for ODOP beneficiaries.

  • ODOP Margin Money Scheme: Provides financial subsidy through commercial and cooperative banks ranging from 25% for projects up to Rs. 25 Lakhs.
  • Common Facility Centre (CFC) Scheme: Establishes testing labs, design centers, and shared manufacturing units at the cluster level with up to 90% government funding.
  • Marketing Development Assistance (MDA): Financial support for artisans to participate in national and international exhibitions, state fairs like Taj Mahotsav and UP Diwas.
  • Udyam Sarathi App: A digital mobile platform launched by UP Govt to provide comprehensive information on employment, loans, and ODOP schemes.

Impact, Exports and Global Reach

How ODOP has transformed Uttar Pradesh into an export hub and revitalized rural industrial output.

  • Export Surge (निर्यात में वृद्धि): State exports from MSMEs have grown exponentially since ODOP inception, crossing Rs. 1.5 Lakh Crores annually.
  • GI Leadership (जीआई नेतृत्व): Uttar Pradesh holds one of the highest numbers of GI-tagged products in India, largely propelled by the ODOP mapping process.
  • E-Commerce Partnerships: Tie-ups with global and national platforms like Amazon, Flipkart, and GeM to provide direct market access to rural artisans.
