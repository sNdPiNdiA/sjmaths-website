Major Industrial Corridors Core Study Notes

Major Industrial Corridors in Uttar Pradesh are transformative economic catalysts designed to accelerate manufacturing, boost exports, and generate employment. For Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, mastering the route alignments, nodal cities, implementing agencies, and intersections with national corridors (such as DMIC, CBIC, and Expressways) is crucial for cracking factual and statement-based questions.

Amritsar-Kolkata Industrial Corridor (AKIC)

AKIC traverses through the eastern and central parts of Uttar Pradesh, leveraging the Eastern Dedicated Freight Corridor (EDFC).

  • Geographical Alignment: Passes through key districts of Uttar Pradesh including Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Meerut, Hapur, Bulandshahr, Aligarh, Hathras, Mathura, Agra, Firozabad, Etawah, Auraiya, Kanpur, Fatehpur, Prayagraj, Mirzapur, and Chandauli.
  • Integrated Manufacturing Clusters (IMCs): Major IMCs are proposed near Prayagraj and Barh-Bara region to accelerate heavy and medium-scale industries.
  • Logistics Synergy: The corridor integrates multi-modal transport networks connecting National Waterway-1 (River Ganga) and major rail-road grids.
  • Implementing Agency: Executed by National Industrial Corridor Development Corporation (NICDC) in close coordination with UP State Industrial Development Authority (UPSIDA).

Uttar Pradesh Defense Industrial Corridor (UPDIC)

Launched to reduce India's import dependence in defense manufacturing, UPDIC comprises six strategic nodes.

  • Six Strategic Nodes: The six designated nodes are Lucknow, Kanpur, Aligarh, Agra, Jhansi, and Chitrakoot.
  • Aligarh Node: First node where land allotment began; focuses on hardware, small arms, drone manufacturing, and metal products.
  • Jhansi Node: Focuses on heavy equipment, Bharat Dynamics Limited (BDL) plant for anti-guided missile systems, and defense propulsion.
  • Kanpur & Lucknow Nodes: Kanpur focuses on bulletproof jackets, leather gear, and explosives; Lucknow acts as the nodal headquarters for aerospace components and BrahMos missile production.

Expressway-linked Industrial Corridors (UPEIDA)

UPEIDA is spearheading the development of specialized industrial nodes along state expressways to decentralize industrial growth.

  • Purvanchal Expressway Industrial Corridor: Passes through 9 districts (Lucknow to Ghazipur); industrial hubs are being developed in Amethi, Azamgarh, Barabanki, and Mau.
  • Bundelkhand Expressway Industrial Corridor: Connects Chitrakoot to Agra-Lucknow Expressway; industrial development nodes planned in Banda and Jalaun districts.
  • Gorakhpur Link Expressway Corridor: Connects Gorakhpur to Purvanchal Expressway; proposed 3 new industrial corridors/parks in Gorakhpur and Sant Kabir Nagar.
  • Ganga Expressway Industrial Corridor: Spanning 594 km from Meerut to Prayagraj, featuring a major industrial hub in Shahjahanpur with an airstrip for emergency aircraft landing.
