Major Crops and Fruits of Uttar Pradesh Core Study Notes

Uttar Pradesh is an agrarian powerhouse, ranking first in India for the production of food grains, wheat, sugarcane, and potatoes. Understanding the agro-climatic zones, crop distributions, and ODOP (One District One Product) fruit selections is vital for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations.

Agro-Climatic Zones and Major Food Crops

Uttar Pradesh is divided into 9 distinct agro-climatic zones based on rainfall, temperature, and soil type, supporting diverse food and cash crops.

  • Wheat Production Leader: UP ranks 1st in India in total wheat production. The Ganga-Yamuna Doab (Western UP) is the most intensive wheat-cultivating region.
  • Rice (Paddy) Cultivation: Paddy is predominantly grown in the Tarab and Eastern UP regions where rainfall is higher. Major varieties include basmati and indigenous scented rices like Kalanamak.
  • Sugarcane Dominance: UP is the top producer of sugarcane in India. Meerut, Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, and Kheri districts form the core 'Sugar Bowl' of the state.
  • Pulses and Oilseeds: Gram (Chana) and Pigeon Pea (Arhar) are major pulses cultivated mainly in the Vindhyan and Bundelkhand zones. Mustard and Groundnut are primary oilseeds.

Horticulture: Major Fruits and ODOP Integration

Uttar Pradesh has a flourishing horticulture sector with world-renowned varieties of mangoes, guavas, amla, and bananas, heavily supported by the ODOP scheme.

  • Mango Belt & Varieties: Lucknow (Malihabad) is famous for 'Dashawari' mango. Other major varieties include Langra (Varanasi), Chausa, and Safeda. UP exports mangoes under the brand name 'Navab'.
  • Guava (Amrood): Allahabad (Prayagraj) produces the finest quality guavas in India, specifically the 'Allahabad Safeda' and 'Lalit' varieties, known for high vitamin C and fewer seeds.
  • Amla (Indian Gooseberry): Pratapgarh district is recognized as the Amla capital of UP, designated under the ODOP scheme for processing, murabba, and ayurvedic formulations.
  • Banana & Others: Kaushambi, Allahabad, and Gorakhpur are major hubs for banana cultivation. Saharanpur is renowned for high-quality litchis and peaches.

Geographical Indication (GI) Crops & Special Varieties

Several unique agricultural and horticultural produce of UP have earned GI tags, establishing global identity and protective branding.

  • Kalanamak Rice: Known as the 'Buddha's Rice', this scented black-husked rice is cultivated in Siddhartha Nagar and surrounding districts, holding a GI tag.
  • Allahabad Surkha Guava: A unique pink-fleshed guava variety from Prayagraj known for its distinct aroma and sweetness, protected under GI registration.
  • Mahoba Desawari Paan: An indigenous betel vine variety cultivated in Mahoba (Bundelkhand), famous for its exceptional texture and medicinal properties.
  • Barabanki Mentha Oil: UP produces nearly 75-80% of India's total mentha (mint) oil, with Barabanki and Sambhal acting as the primary production and processing hubs.
