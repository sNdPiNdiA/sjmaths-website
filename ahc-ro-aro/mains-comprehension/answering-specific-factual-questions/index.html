Answering Specific Factual Questions in Reading Comprehension Core Study Notes

This module equips Allahabad High Court RO/ARO aspirants with advanced techniques to locate, analyze, and precisely answer explicit factual queries from unseen passages in the descriptive mains examination. Mastery of this skill ensures maximum scoring potential by eliminating vague interpretations and enforcing strict adherence to passage-backed evidence.

Strategies for Locating Specific Facts

Systematic approaches to scanning passages for dates, names, figures, and statutory references without wasting precious examination time.

  • Keyword Spotting (की-वर्ड स्पॉटिंग): Identify unique nouns, numerical figures, acronyms, or specialized legal terms in the question and scan the passage for exact or synonymous matches.
  • Scanning vs. Skimming (स्कैनिंग बनाम स्किमिंग): Use skimming only for the first overall read; strictly use scanning (eye-tracking for target data) when specific factual questions are addressed.
  • Contextual Anchoring (प्रासंगिक एंकरिंग): Pinpoint the paragraph containing the relevant keyword and read two sentences before and after to secure complete factual context.
  • Handling Numerical & Statistical Data (संख्यात्मक और सांख्यिकीय डेटा): Pay meticulous attention to years, percentages, monetary amounts, and legal sections, as examiners frequently alter digits in distractors.

Synthesizing and Drafting Precise Answers

Guidelines for framing crisp, grammatically correct, and legally sound answers in the Allahabad High Court descriptive answer booklet.

  • Conciseness & Precision (संक्षिप्तता और सटीकता): Avoid flowery language and unnecessary introductions; directly address the core factual requirement within the stipulated word limit.
  • Objective Neutrality (वस्तुनिष्ठ तटस्थता): Do not incorporate personal opinions, external news knowledge, or generalized assumptions; rely strictly on passage facts.
  • Grammatical Precision in Hindi/English (व्याकरण की सटीकता): Ensure proper syntax, correct spelling of legal terms, and appropriate use of official administrative vocabulary.
