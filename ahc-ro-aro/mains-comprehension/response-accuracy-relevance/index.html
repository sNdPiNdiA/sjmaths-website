Response Accuracy Relevance in Mains Reading Comprehension Core Study Notes

Response Accuracy and Relevance forms the core evaluative matrix of the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO Mains descriptive comprehension paper. It tests a candidate's ability to extract exact factual data, deduce logical inferences, and formulate precise answers without straying from the author's central premise.

Foundational Principles of Accuracy

Accuracy demands that every word, statistic, and legal or administrative assertion in the answer directly mirrors the provided passage without external distortion.

  • Textual Fidelity (पाठानुकूल निष्ठा): Answers must rely solely on facts explicitly stated or directly implied within the passage; no outside general knowledge should override text data.
  • Data & Numerical Precision (आँकड़ा और संख्यात्मक परिशुद्धता): If a passage specifies dates, statutory sections, or percentages (e.g., Article 226, 15% reservation), copying or paraphrasing them with zero error is mandatory.
  • Elimination of Extraneous Fluff (अतिरिक्त गैर-जरूरी सामग्री का उन्मूलन): Avoid flowery prose, introductory pleasantries, or concluding personal opinions that consume word limits without adding factual value.
  • Contextual Alignment (प्रासंगिक संरेखण): Ensure answers address the specific interrogative pronoun or directive (Why, How, State the provision) of the question prompt directly.

Relevance & Scope Adherence

Relevance governs whether the formulated answer targets the core argument of the passage rather than tangent mentions or secondary arguments.

  • Core Theme Isolation (केंद्रीय विषय पृथक्करण): Identify the primary thesis statement, usually located in the opening or concluding paragraph of the unseen passage.
  • Filtering Secondary Details (माध्यमिक विवरणों को फ़िल्टर करना): Distinguish between primary arguments and illustrative examples; do not elevate an example to the status of a primary theme.
  • Avoiding Anachronisms (काल-दोष से बचना): Do not mix historical timelines or legal amendments unless explicitly contrasted in the passage text.
  • Direct Prompt Mapping (प्रत्येक प्रश्न संकेत का मिलान): Every sentence written in the answer booklet must serve as a direct response to one of the sub-questions asked by the examiner.

Analytical Inference & Vocabulary in Context

Advanced questions in AHC RO/ARO Mains test implicit meanings, synonyms/antonyms of passage terms, and structural intent.

  • Deductive Reasoning (निगमनात्मक तर्क): Inferring the author's unstated intent based on tone (e.g., critical, appreciative, objective, analytical) and logical flow.
  • Contextual Vocabulary Matching (संदर्भित शब्दावली मिलान): Determining the exact meaning of a legal or archaic term based on how it functions within the sentence rather than general dictionary definitions.
  • Synthesizing Summary Titles (सारांश शीर्षक का संश्लेषण): Formulating precise, concise titles that encapsulate the passage's essence within 3 to 6 words without being too broad or too narrow.
