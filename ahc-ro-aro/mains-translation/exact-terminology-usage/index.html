Exact Terminology Usage in Administrative Translation Core Study Notes

Exact terminology usage is critical for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC Mains translation papers. It requires absolute precision in converting legal, judicial, and administrative vocabulary between English and Hindi without altering statutory or official intent.

Judicial and Court Phraseology

Mastering high-frequency legal and judicial terms used in Allahabad High Court judgments, orders, and petitions.

  • Affidavit: A written statement confirmed by oath or affirmation, for use as evidence in court proceedings under CPC.
  • Injunction: A judicial order restraining a person from beginning or continuing an threatened action (Temporary/Permanent).
  • Suo Motu: An action taken by a court of its own accord without any formal petition being filed by a party.
  • Writ Petition: A formal written order issued by a superior court (under Article 226/32) directing a lower court or government official to do or stop doing something.

Administrative Correspondence and Noting

Standard terms used in official letters, office memorandums, notifications, and file noting in UP government departments.

  • Office Memorandum: A formal inter-departmental communication method that is generally less formal than an official letter.
  • Endorsement: A formal note written on a document or letter, usually forwarding it to other subordinate authorities for necessary action.
  • Gazette Notification: An official public announcement published in the official state gazette carrying legal validity.
  • Sanction: Official permission or approval for expenditure, prosecution, or administrative actions.

Financial and Budgetary Terminology

Precise translation of audit, treasury, and public finance terms essential for administrative efficiency in RO/ARO roles.

  • Appropriation: The setting apart of public funds for a specific expenditure authorized by the legislature.
  • Consolidated Fund: All revenues received, loans raised, and receipts by the government form the Consolidated Fund of India/State (Article 266).
  • Audit Objection: Discrepancy or irregularity pointed out by the Accountant General (AG) during financial audits.
  • Emoluments: Salary, allowances, and financial benefits attached to a public office or official position.

Statutory and Constitutional Lexicon

Fundamental constitutional and legislative terms that form the bedrock of High Court legal translation tasks.

  • Ultra Vires: Acting beyond one's legal power or constitutional authority; opposite of Intra Vires.
  • Quash: To officially reject or invalidate a legal order, proceeding, or lower court judgment.
  • Mandamus: A judicial writ issued as a command to an inferior court or public authority to perform a mandatory duty.
  • Prerogative: A special right or privilege exercised by virtue of constitutional or official office.
