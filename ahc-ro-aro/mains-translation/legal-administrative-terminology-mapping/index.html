Legal Administrative Terminology Mapping Core Study Notes

Legal Administrative Translation forms a cornerstone of the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO Mains Examination, requiring precise bilingual conversion between English and Hindi. Mastery over official legal terminology, court phraseology, and administrative vocabulary ensures precision in drafting judgments, orders, and official circulars.

Core Legal & Judicial Terminology

High-frequency English legal terms and their exact standardized Hindi equivalents used in High Court pleadings and administrative orders.

  • Affidavit / Deponent: A written statement confirmed by oath or affirmation, for use as evidence in court proceedings. Hindi: शपथ पत्र (Affidavit) and शपथकर्ता या अभिसाक्षी (Deponent).
  • Injunction (Interim / Permanent): A judicial order restraining a person from beginning or continuing an threatened action. Hindi: व्यदेश या निषेधाज्ञा; Interim is अंतरिम निषेधाज्ञा.
  • Writ Jurisdiction (Mandamus, Certiorari): Extraordinary constitutional remedies under Articles 32 and 226. Mandamus translates to 'परमादेश' (We Command), and Certiorari translates to 'उत्प्रेषण' (To be certified).
  • Suo Motu / Amicus Curiae: Suo Motu means an action taken by a court of its own accord ('स्वतः संज्ञान'). Amicus Curiae means a friend of the court ('न्याय मित्र').

Administrative & Secretariat Terminology

Mastery over bureaucratic and secretarial expressions required for translating administrative notifications, Government Orders (GOs), and noting-drafting.

  • Government Order (GO) / Notification: Official executive communication issued by state departments. Translated as 'शासनादेश' for GO and 'अधिसूचना' for Gazette notifications.
  • Endorsement / Memorandum: Endorsement refers to writing or signing on the back of a document ('पृष्ठांकन'), while Memorandum refers to an administrative note or memo ('ज्ञापन' or 'ज्ञाप').
  • Sanction / Ex-post facto Sanction: Official approval or permission for expenditure. Ex-post facto sanction translates to retrospective approval ('उत्तरभावी' or 'पूर्वव्यापी संस्वीकृति').
  • Quorum / Adjournment: Minimum members required for a meeting is 'गणपूर्ति'. Temporary suspension of a sitting to a future date is 'स्थगन'.

Latin Maxims in Indian Judicial System

Frequently tested Latin legal maxims and their precise semantic rendering into Hindi and English for High Court judgments.

  • Audi Alteram Partem: Fundamental rule of natural justice meaning 'let the other side be heard as well'. Hindi: 'दूसरे पक्ष को भी सुना जाए' या 'न्याय का नैसर्गिक सिद्धांत'.
  • Nemo Judex in Causa Sua: Rule against bias; no person can be a judge in their own cause. Hindi: 'कोई भी व्यक्ति अपने स्वयं के मामले में न्यायाधीश नहीं हो सकता' (पक्षपात का निषेध).
  • Res Judicata: A matter that has been adjudicated by a competent court and cannot be pursued further. Hindi: 'प्रांग न्याय' (Civil Procedure Code Section 11).
  • De Facto / De Jure: De facto means in fact or practice ('वस्तुतः' / 'तथ्यतः'). De jure means by law or rightfully ('विधितः' / 'कानूनी रूप से').

Drafting Rules and Sentence Structure Conventions

Syntactic rules governing official correspondence, ensuring absence of ambiguity, passive voice preferences, and formal terminology usage.

  • Impersonality and Passive Voice: Official correspondence avoids personal pronouns like 'I' or 'We'. It utilizes impersonal passive constructions such as 'It is hereby ordered' ('इसके द्वारा आदेश दिया जाता है').
  • Precision and Uniformity: Synonyms should not be interchanged arbitrarily within the same document. A statutory term like 'shall' must translate mandatorily to 'shall' / 'करेगा' and 'may' to 'सकेगा'.
  • Avoidance of Archaic Expressions: Modern administrative translation discards archaic words like 'Hereinbefore' (पूर्वोक्त), 'Thereunder' (तदधीन), unless strictly required in statutory drafting.
