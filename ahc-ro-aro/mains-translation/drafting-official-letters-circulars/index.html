Drafting Official Letters and Circulars Core Study Notes

Official Letter and Circular drafting and translation is a high-weightage component of the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC Mains examinations. Mastery over precise administrative terminology, standard letter layouts, and formal bilingual equivalence is essential for scoring high marks.

Structure and Anatomy of an Official Letter

An Official Letter (शासकीय पत्र) follows a rigid formal layout designed to maintain administrative hierarchy, clarity, and accountability.

  • Sender Identification (प्रेषक): Placed at the top left or center depending on state manual, containing the name, designation, and department of the issuing officer.
  • Dispatch Number and Date (संख्या एवं दिनांक): Mandatory alphanumeric dispatch reference number (पत्रांक संख्या) followed by the exact date of issue on the right or left margin.
  • Addressee Details (सेवा में): Addressed formally to 'To, The Secretary, Government of Uttar Pradesh' (सेवा में, सचिव, उत्तर प्रदेश शासन) starting on the left margin.
  • Subject and Reference (विषय एवं संदर्भ): A concise summary statement capturing the core intent of the communication, prefixed with 'Subject:' (विषय:) and 'Reference:' (संदर्भ:).
  • Salutation and Body (संबोधन एवं मुख्य भाग): Begins with 'Sir/Madam' (महोदय/महोदया) followed by paragraphs in third person, passive voice, organized logically into introductory, core, and concluding paras.
  • Subscription and Signature (भवदीय / हस्ताक्षर): Concludes with 'Yours faithfully' (भवदीय) on the right side, accompanied by signature, full name in block letters, and official designation.

Drafting Circulars (परिपत्र) - Principles and Format

A circular is an administrative instrument used to communicate policies, rules, or instructions simultaneously to a large number of subordinate offices or employees.

  • Definition and Scope (परिभाषा और दायरा): Used when identical instructions need to reach multiple branches, districts, or courts without issuing separate individual letters.
  • Heading and Numbering (शीर्षक एवं संख्यांकन): Prominently displays 'CIRCULAR' (परिपत्र) centered at the top, accompanied by a serial circular number and year of issue.
  • Universal Addressee Block (सार्वभौमिक प्राप्तकर्ता ब्लॉक): Addressed collectively such as 'All District Judges subordinate to the High Court' (उच्च न्यायालय के अधीन सभी जिला जज) or 'All Heads of Departments'.
  • Execution and Compliance (कार्यान्वयन एवं अनुपालन): Must include a strict compliance deadline or reporting date by which subordinate offices must acknowledge receipt and action taken.
  • Endorsement and Distribution (पृष्ठांकन एवं वितरण): Ends with 'Endorsement No. & Date' (पृष्ठांकन संख्या एवं दिनांक) listing copies forwarded to related departments for information and necessary action.

Key Legal & Administrative Vocabulary for Translation

Accurate bilingual translation in AHC RO/ARO exams requires precise equivalents authorized by the Official Language Commission, UP.

  • Administrative Term Pairs (प्रशासनिक शब्द युग्म): Endorsement = पृष्ठांकन, Circular = परिपत्र, Notification = अधिसूचना, Resolution = संकल्प, Memorandum = ज्ञापन, Gazette = राजपत्र.
  • Financial & Budgetary Terms (वित्तीय एवं बजटीय शब्द): Sanction = संस्वीकृति / मंजूरी, Appropriation = विनियोजन, Audit = लेखापरीक्षा, Expenditure = व्यय, Emoluments = परिलब्धियां.
  • Judicial & Court Terminology (न्यायिक शब्दावली): In the High Court = उच्च न्यायालय में, Writ Petition = रिट याचिका, Affidavit = शपथ पत्र, Contempt of Court = न्यायालय की अवमानना, Jurisdiction = अधिकारिता.
  • Phrasal Equivalents (वाक्यांश समतुल्य): 'With reference to' = के संदर्भ में, 'In continuation of' = के निरंतरता में, 'As directed' = निर्देशानुसार, 'Under the provisions' = प्रावधानों के अंतर्गत.

Common Errors and Drafting Etiquette

Avoiding procedural and grammatical pitfalls ensures formal acceptability in government secretariats and judicial registries.

  • Avoidance of First Person (प्रथम पुरुष से परहेज): Official letters must never use 'I think' or 'We decided'. Instead, use passive constructions like 'It has been decided' (यह निर्णय लिया गया है).
  • Consistency in Numbering (संख्यांकन में एकरूपता): Paragraphs should be numbered sequentially with Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3) and sub-paragraphs with letters or Roman numerals.
  • Date and Place Formatting (दिनांक और स्थान प्रारूप): Dates must follow standard day-month-year format in Hindi (e.g., 25 अक्टूबर, 2023) and appropriate formal style in English.
