Major Exports Imports Core Study Notes

This topic covers India's and Uttar Pradesh's major agricultural and commercial export-import dynamics, trade policies, and balance of payments. It is crucial for Allahabad High Court RO/ARO aspirants due to frequent factual questions on foreign trade statistics, GI-tagged goods, and domestic trade corridors.

India's Major Export Commodities & Destinations

Analysis of top merchandise and agricultural commodities exported globally from India.

  • Engineering Goods: Constitutes the single largest share of India's total merchandise exports, including transport equipment, machinery, and iron/steel products.
  • Petroleum Products: Refined petroleum items form a major chunk of exports due to India's massive refining capacity centered in Jamnagar and Paradip.
  • Agricultural & Allied Products: Includes basmati rice, non-basmati rice, marine products (shrimp/prawns), spices, buffalo meat, and tea/coffee. India is the leading exporter of rice globally.
  • Gems and Jewellery: High-value export sector involving cut and polished diamonds, gold jewellery, and colored gemstones, primarily processed in Surat and Mumbai.
  • Top Export Destinations: The USA remains India's largest export destination, followed by the UAE, China, Bangladesh, and the Netherlands.

India's Major Import Commodities & Sources

Examination of essential imports required for energy security, industrial manufacturing, and domestic consumption.

  • Crude Petroleum & Natural Gas: India imports over 85% of its crude oil requirements, making petroleum the single largest imported item by value.
  • Electronic Goods: Includes telecom instruments, semiconductor devices, integrated circuits, and computer hardware sourced heavily from China and East Asia.
  • Gold & Silver: High-value precious metals imported to meet domestic jewellery demand and as an investment hedge, largely sourced from Switzerland and UAE.
  • Coal, Coke & Briquettes: Coking coal and thermal coal imported extensively for steel plants and thermal power stations from Australia, Indonesia, and South Africa.
  • Top Import Sources: China remains India's largest import partner, followed by Russia, the UAE, the USA, and Saudi Arabia.

Uttar Pradesh Export Profile & ODOP Initiative

Focus on UP's growing export ecosystem, special economic zones, and the One District One Product scheme.

  • One District One Product (ODOP): UP's flagship scheme launched to promote indigenous crafts and specialized products (e.g., Moradabad brassware, Bhadohi carpets, Agra leather goods) for global markets.
  • UP Export Statistics & Corridors: UP has crossed significant milestones in merchandise exports, driven by agro-processing, leather, readymade garments, and engineering goods along the UP Defence Industrial Corridor.
  • Major Export Hubs in UP: Noida and Greater Noida (IT, Electronics, Readymade garments), Kanpur (Leather), Varanasi (Handloom & Silk sarees), Aligarh (Hardware).
  • Agri-Export Zones (AEZs) in UP: Established for specific agricultural commodities such as Mangoes (Lucknow, Saharanpur), Potatoes (Agra, Farrukhabad), and Basmati Rice (Western UP).

Foreign Trade Policy (FTP 2023) & Institutional Framework

Key operational pillars and institutions governing India's export-import ecosystem.

  • FTP 2023 Four Pillars: Incentive to Remission, Export promotion through collaboration (Exporters, States, Districts), Ease of doing business (reduction in transaction cost), and Emerging Areas (E-commerce export hubs).
  • DGFT (Directorate General of Foreign Trade): An attached office of the Ministry of Commerce and Industry responsible for formulating and implementing the Foreign Trade Policy of India.
  • SEZs and EOUs: Special Economic Zones and Export Oriented Units enjoy tax holidays, duty-free imports of raw materials, and streamlined customs clearance to boost exports.
  • FIEO & Export Promotion Councils: Federation of Indian Export Organisations (FIEO) and various commodity-specific EPCs act as the apex bodies linking exporters with global markets.
