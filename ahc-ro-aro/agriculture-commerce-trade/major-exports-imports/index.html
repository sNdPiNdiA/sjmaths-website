1. Basics of International Trade & India's Context

Core Concepts & Definitions

  • Exports: Goods and services sold by India to other countries.
  • Imports: Goods and services bought by India from other countries.
  • Balance of Trade (BoT): The difference between the value of a country's exports and imports of goods.
    • Trade Surplus: Exports > Imports.
    • Trade Deficit: Imports > Exports (India typically has a merchandise trade deficit).
  • Balance of Payments (BoP): A broader record of all economic transactions between a country and the rest of the world.

High-Yield Facts / Exam Tips

  • India generally experiences a **merchandise trade deficit** (goods) but often a **services trade surplus**.
  • **Agriculture** is a vital sector contributing significantly to India's export earnings and rural economy.

Important Landmark Facts / Rules

  • Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT): The primary government body responsible for implementing India's Foreign Trade Policy.

2. India's Major Agricultural Exports

Core Concepts

  • Key agricultural products that India sells to the global market, earning foreign exchange.

Important Comparison/Data Table (Top Agri-Exports - Recent Trends)

Rank Major Agri-Export Category Key Destinations/Significance
1 Marine Products USA, China, Japan, Vietnam. India is a major exporter of shrimp.
2 Basmati Rice Middle East, Europe. India is the world's largest exporter of Basmati rice.
3 Spices USA, China, Vietnam, UAE. India is the "Spice Bowl of the World."
4 Non-Basmati Rice African and Asian countries. India is a leading global supplier.
5 Sugar & Sugar Confectionery Indonesia, Bangladesh, Sudan. India has emerged as a significant sugar exporter.

  • Marine Products
  • Basmati Rice
  • Spices
  • Non-Basmati Rice

High-Yield Facts / Exam Tips

  • India is a **net exporter of agricultural products**.
  • **Basmati Rice** and **Marine Products** consistently rank high in value.

3. India's Major Agricultural Imports

Core Concepts

  • Key agricultural products that India buys from other countries to meet domestic demand.

Important Comparison/Data Table (Top Agri-Imports - Recent Trends)

Rank Major Agri-Import Category Key Origin Countries/Significance
1 Edible Oils (Palm, Soybean, Sunflower) Indonesia, Malaysia (Palm); Argentina, Brazil (Soybean); Ukraine, Russia (Sunflower). Largest import bill.
2 Pulses Canada, Myanmar, Australia, Russia. To bridge domestic production gaps.
3 Fruits & Vegetables Afghanistan (dry fruits), China, USA. Specific varieties or off-season supply.
4 Spices (specific types like Pepper, Cloves) Vietnam, China, Indonesia. To meet industrial demand or specific varieties.
5 Cotton USA, Australia, Brazil. For textile industry, especially long-staple varieties.

High-Yield Facts / Exam Tips

  • **Edible Oils** are consistently India's largest agricultural import by value, driven by high domestic consumption and production shortfalls.
  • India is the **world's largest importer of edible oils**.
  • Government initiatives like the **National Mission on Edible Oils – Oil Palm (NMEO-OP)** aim to reduce import dependence.

4. Overall Merchandise Trade & Policy Highlights

Core Concepts

  • Beyond agriculture, the broader categories of goods and services India trades globally.

Important Comparison/Data Table (Overall Top Exports/Imports - Goods & Services)

Category Top 3 Exports (Overall Goods) Top 3 Imports (Overall Goods)
Goods Petroleum Products, Gems & Jewellery, Engineering Goods Crude Petroleum, Gold, Electronic Goods
Services Software Services, Business Services, Travel Business Services, Travel, Transport

Important Landmark Facts / Rules

  • Foreign Trade Policy (FTP): A comprehensive document by the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, outlining strategies and incentives for exports and imports. The latest FTP 2023 aims to achieve $2 trillion in exports by 2030.
  • Special Economic Zones (SEZs): Geographically delineated duty-free enclaves treated as foreign territory for trade operations, designed to boost exports.
  • Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Schemes: Government initiatives to boost domestic manufacturing and make Indian products globally competitive, thereby increasing exports and reducing imports.

High-Yield Facts / Exam Tips

  • **Crude Petroleum** is consistently India's single largest import item, significantly impacting the trade deficit.
  • India's **services exports** have shown robust growth, often helping to offset the merchandise trade deficit.
  • **USA** and **UAE** are consistently among India's top trading partners for goods.
