Heavy Industries & Industrial Infrastructure Core Study Notes

Heavy industries form the backbone of capital goods production, infrastructure development, and industrial growth in Uttar Pradesh and India. For the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, questions frequently test establishment years, public sector undertakings (PSUs), collaborations, mineral linkages, and industrial corridors.

Iron & Steel and Metallurgical Industries

Iron and steel form the foundational heavy industry, supplying raw materials to machine tools, construction, and automobile sectors.

  • Bhilai Steel Plant (BSP): Established in 1955 in Chhattisgarh with Soviet Union (USSR) collaboration during the Second Five Year Plan. Known for producing rail tracks and heavy structural steel.
  • Rourkela Steel Plant (RSP): Established in 1955 in Odisha with West German (FRG) collaboration. It was the first public sector integrated steel plant in India and specializes in flat-rolled products.
  • Durgapur Steel Plant (DSP): Established in 1956 in West Bengal with British collaboration. It produces alloy steels, wheel sets, and axles for Indian Railways.
  • Bokaro Steel Plant: Established in 1964 in Jharkhand with Soviet assistance, becoming the fourth public sector steel plant and later integrated with SAIL.
  • Hindalco Industries (Renukoot): Located in Sonbhadra district, Uttar Pradesh, Hindalco (Aditya Birla Group) is a premier producer of primary aluminum and heavy rolled products, leveraging local bauxite and Rihand hydel power.

Heavy Engineering & Manufacturing PSUs

Heavy engineering industries manufacture capital goods, power generation equipment, heavy electrical machinery, and transport vehicles.

  • Bharat Heavy Electricals Limited (BHEL): India's largest engineering and manufacturing enterprise in the energy and infrastructure sector, operating major units including Hardwar, Bhopal, Tiruchirappalli, and Jhansi (UP).
  • Heavy Engineering Corporation (HEC): Established in Ranchi, Jharkhand, to manufacture heavy capital equipment for steel plants, mining equipment, and heavy machine tools with Czech and Soviet assistance.
  • Diesel Locomotive Works (DLW / BLW): Located in Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh. Originally manufacturing diesel locomotives, it has now transitioned to producing high-horsepower electric locomotives for Indian Railways.
  • Scooters India Limited (SIL): Headquartered in Sarojini Nagar, Lucknow (UP), established in 1972. Historically known for manufacturing 'Vijai Super' scooters and three-wheelers ('Vikram').

Cement, Chemical & Fertilizer Heavy Industries

Process-based heavy industries reliant on limestone, coal, petroleum feedstocks, and sulfur/phosphates.

  • Cement Industry & Raw Material Linkage: Cement manufacturing requires heavy bulk inputs: limestone and gypsum. In UP, Mirzapur and Sonbhadra districts are prominent hubs due to rich Vindhyan limestone deposits.
  • Fertilizer Manufacturing Hubs in UP: Major heavy fertilizer plants in Uttar Pradesh include Gorakhpur, Phulpur (Prayagraj), Aonla (Bareilly), and Jagdishpur (Amethi), largely utilizing gas via HBJ pipeline.
  • Mathura Refinery (IOCL): Commissioned in 1982 by Indian Oil Corporation, it is the sixth refinery in India, strategically located to supply petroleum products to north-west India including Delhi-NCR.

Industrial Corridors, Policy & Infrastructure in UP

Modern planning integrates heavy industries into designated manufacturing zones, expressways, and defense corridors.

  • UP Defence Industrial Corridor: Spanning 6 nodes: Agra, Aligarh, Kanpur, Chitrakoot, Jhansi, and Lucknow. Designed to boost indigenous defense manufacturing, aerospace components, and heavy military hardware.
  • Gorakhpur Link Expressway & Purvanchal Expressway: Designed to establish industrial clusters along major transit routes, reducing logistics costs for heavy manufacturing and promoting backward regions of Uttar Pradesh.
  • Udyog Bandhu: An apex organization of the Government of Uttar Pradesh dedicated to facilitating single-window clearances, investment promotion, and resolving grievances for mega and heavy industrial projects.
