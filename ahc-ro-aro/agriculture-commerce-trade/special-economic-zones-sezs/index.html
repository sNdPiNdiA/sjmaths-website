Special Economic Zones (SEZs) Core Study Notes

Special Economic Zones (SEZs) are specifically delineated duty-free enclaves treated as foreign territory for trade operations, duties, and tariffs. For the AHC RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, mastering SEZ frameworks, Uttar Pradesh specific export hubs, legal acts, and fiscal incentives is crucial for scoring high in the Agriculture, Commerce & Trade section.

Concept, Objectives, and Legal Framework

Understanding the fundamental definitions, statutory backing, and primary objectives governing SEZs under Indian law.

  • Definition & Status: An SEZ is a specially demarcated territory treated as being outside the customs territory of India for the purpose of undertaking authorized operations, manufacturing, and trading.
  • Statutory Backing: Governed by the SEZ Act, 2005 and SEZ Rules, 2006, replacing the earlier administrative-order-based EPZ regime.
  • Primary Objectives: Generation of additional economic activity, promotion of exports of goods and services, promotion of investment from domestic and foreign sources, and creation of employment opportunities.
  • Single Window Clearance: The Board of Approval (BoA) at the central level and Unit Approval Committees at the zone level provide speedy clearances for setting up units.

Fiscal Incentives, Taxation, and Foreign Trade Benefits

Exploration of duty exemptions, tax holidays, and financial relaxations provided to developers and units within SEZs.

  • Income Tax Holidays: 100% deduction of export profits under Section 10AA of the Income Tax Act for the first 5 years, 50% for the next 5 years, and 50% of ploughed-back export profit for the subsequent 5 years.
  • Duty-Free Goods Procurement: Exemption from customs duty and integrated GST (IGST) on import or domestic procurement of capital goods, raw materials, and consumables for authorized operations.
  • Indirect Tax Exemptions: Exemption from Central Sales Tax (CST), Service Tax (now subsumed in GST), and state-level levies like electricity duty and stamp duty within the zone.
  • External Commercial Borrowings (ECB): SEZ units are permitted external commercial borrowings up to $500 million per year without any maturity restriction through recognized banking channels.

UP-Specific SEZs, Agro-Exports, and Industrial Corridors

Highlighting prominent operational Special Economic Zones, IT parks, and agricultural export hubs located within Uttar Pradesh.

  • Noida Special Economic Zone (NSEZ): One of the prominent central government-owned SEZs, managed by the Ministry of Commerce & Industry, catering largely to IT/ITeS, gems, jewelry, and engineering goods.
  • Kanpur & Lucknow SEZs: Specialized zones focusing on leather, footwear, biotechnology, and precision engineering exports, bolstering the state's commercial backbone.
  • UP Agro-Climatic Export Zones: Integration of SEZ frameworks with agricultural export clusters for mangoes (Lucknow/Saharanpur), potatoes (Agra), and basmati rice (Western UP) under APEDA initiatives.
  • Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC) Linkage: DADRI-Noida-Ghaziabad investment region acts as a multimodal transport hub boosting SEZ-based manufacturing and logistics.

Governance, Administration, and Exit Mechanisms

Examining the institutional hierarchy, approval boards, development commissioners, and de-notification procedures.

  • Board of Approval (BoA): The apex inter-ministerial body headed by the Commerce Secretary (Government of India) responsible for granting approval for the establishment of SEZs.
  • Development Commissioner (DC): Each SEZ is administered by a Development Commissioner who exercises administrative and quasi-judicial powers under the SEZ Act.
  • Customs Clearance & Operations: Zones have designated customs officers and automated clearing mechanisms, operating under self-certification schemes in many instances.
  • Exit Policy / De-notification: SEZ developers or units may exit or de-notify subject to clearance of outstanding dues, repayment of proportional tax benefits enjoyed, and approval from the Board of Approval.
