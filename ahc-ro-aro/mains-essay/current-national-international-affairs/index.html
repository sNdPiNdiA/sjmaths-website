Current National International Affairs Core Study Notes

Current National and International Affairs form a critical component of the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC Mains examinations. This topic evaluates an aspirant's analytical ability to connect socio-economic, geopolitical, and environmental developments with constitutional and administrative frameworks.

Constitutional, Legal, and Judicial Reforms

Analysis of recent legislative frameworks, judicial independence, and administrative accountability crucial for judicial officers and review officers.

  • Criminal Law Transformation: Replacement of IPC, CrPC, and Indian Evidence Act with BNS, BNSS, and BSA respectively, focusing on justice over punishment, incorporating community service, and setting timelines for trials.
  • Digital Judiciary & E-Courts: Integration of Artificial Intelligence in legal translation (SUVAS), live streaming of constitutional bench hearings, and implementation of the Inter-operable Criminal Justice System (ICJS).
  • Judicial Appointments & Collegium Debate: Ongoing discourse regarding transparency in High Court and Supreme Court judge appointments, Memorandum of Procedure (MoP), and balancing judicial independence with executive consultation.
  • Alternative Dispute Resolution (ADR): Mediation Act, 2023 institutionalization of pre-litigation mediation to reduce the humongous backlog of cases pending across Indian courts, especially in subordinate courts.

Socio-Economic Challenges & Inclusive Development

Evaluation of poverty alleviation schemes, employment generation models, and welfare administration in Uttar Pradesh and India.

  • Multidimensional Poverty Index (MPI): NITI Aayog reports indicate a steep decline in poverty from 24.85% to 11.28% between 2015-16 and 2019-21, lifting 13.5 crore people out of poverty, with Uttar Pradesh recording the highest absolute decline.
  • Employment & Skill Development: Implementation of PM Vishwakarma Yojana, Stand-Up India, and MGNREGS convergence with asset creation to tackle rural-urban disparity and youth unemployment.
  • Uttar Pradesh Growth Engine: UP's target to become a $1 trillion economy, driven by Global Investors Summit, Expressway networks (Purvanchal, Bundelkhand, Ganga Expressways), and Defence Industrial Corridor.
  • Women Empowerment Initiatives: Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam (106th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2023) providing 33% reservation to women in Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.

Science, Technology, and Environmental Degradation

Critical assessment of India's space missions, artificial intelligence governance, climate change mitigation, and biodiversity protection.

  • Space Exploration Milestones: Chandrayaan-3 success making India the first country to land on the lunar south pole; Aditya-L1 successfully placed in the Lagrange point 1 halo orbit for solar corona study.
  • Artificial Intelligence Governance: IndiaAI Mission approved with a ₹10,372 crore outlay to build computing infrastructure, ethical frameworks, and sovereign AI models, alongside the Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence (GPAI) leadership.
  • Climate Action & Renewable Energy: International Solar Alliance (ISA) expansion, National Green Hydrogen Mission targeting 5 MMT annual production by 2030, and aggressive adoption of Electric Vehicles (FAME / PM E-Drive schemes).
  • Biodiversity & Wildlife Conservation: Project Tiger completing 50 years with India hosting over 75% of the world's wild tiger population; launch of Project Cheetah, Project Dolphin, and expansion of Ramsar wetland sites.

Contemporary International Relations & Geopolitics

Strategic positioning of India in multilateral platforms, neighborhood first policy, and maritime security frameworks.

  • Global South Leadership & G20: Voice of Global South Summits hosted by India, amplifying developing nation concerns on debt distress, food security, and climate justice on global platforms.
  • Indo-Pacific & Quad Architecture: Strengthening cooperation among India, USA, Japan, and Australia (Quad) to ensure a free, open, and inclusive Indo-Pacific, countering unilateral maritime hegemony.
  • IMEC Corridor Initiative: India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor announced during the New Delhi G20 Summit, connecting Europe, Middle East, and India via shipping and railway networks.
  • Strategic Autonomy & Multialignment: India's independent foreign policy stance balancing relations with Western democracies and traditional partners like Russia, while managing complex ties with China.
