Seating Arrangement Core Study Notes

Seating Arrangement is a high-scoring and critical component of the General Intelligence and Reasoning section in the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. It evaluates a candidate's analytical capability, spatial orientation, and ability to process complex multi-variable conditional data under strict time constraints.

Linear Seating Arrangements

Involves persons sitting in a single or parallel rows facing North, South, or both.

Circular Seating Arrangements

Deals with individuals seated around a circular table facing towards the center or outside.

Rectangular & Square Table Arrangements

Advanced arrangements featuring corners and sides of geometric polygons.

Conditional Elimination & Shortcut Techniques

Methodologies to solve complex arrangements within 90 seconds.
