Mineral Resources Coal Iron Bauxite Core Study Notes

Mineral resources, particularly coal, iron ore, and bauxite, form the bedrock of India's industrial economy and energy security. For the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations, questions frequently focus on geographical distribution, geological epochs (Gondwana vs. Tertiary), leading producing states, and specific mining belts in India and Uttar Pradesh.

Coal Resources in India

Coal is India's primary source of commercial energy, classified chiefly into Gondwana and Tertiary coal formations.

  • Gondwana Coal Reserves: Accounts for 98% of total coal reserves and 99% of total coal production in India; found primarily in river valleys like Damodar, Mahanadi, and Godavari.
  • Anthracite Coal: Highest grade coal with 80-95% carbon content; hard, black, and burns with a short blue flame; limited reserves found only in Jammu and Kashmir in India.
  • Bituminous Coal: Most widely used commercial coal in India with 60-80% carbon content; densely deposited in Gondwana formations across Jharkhand, Odisha, and Chhattisgarh.
  • Lignite Coal: Low-grade brown coal containing 40-55% carbon; major deposits located in Neyveli (Tamil Nadu), Palana (Rajasthan), and J&K.
  • Tertiary Coal: Younger coal deposits (55 million years old) found in northeastern states (Assam, Meghalaya, Arunachal Pradesh) with high sulfur content.

Iron Ore Resources in India

Iron ore is foundational to the heavy engineering and metallurgical industries, found largely in Dharwar rock systems.

  • Hematite Ore: Red ore containing 60-70% metallic iron; highest reserves in India, found extensively in Odisha, Jharkhand, Chhattisgarh, and Karnataka.
  • Magnetite Ore: Black ore with highest iron content (70-72%) and strong magnetic properties; found in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, and Tamil Nadu.
  • Odisha Iron Belt: Leading producer of iron ore in India; major mines located in Sundargarh, Mayurbhanj, and Keonjhar districts (Badampahar and Gurumahisani).
  • Chhattisgarh-Jharkhand Belt: Dalli-Rajhara in Durg district (Chhattisgarh) supplies iron ore to Bhilai Steel Plant; Bailadila in Dantewada is famous for high-grade hematite export to Japan.
  • Karnataka Belt: Kudremukh (Bellary-Hospet-Chitradurga belt) deposits are processed as slurry and transported through pipelines to Mangaluru port.

Bauxite Resources in India

Bauxite is the primary ore of aluminum, formed by the decomposition of rocks rich in aluminum silicates under tropical conditions.

  • Nature and Chemical Composition: Hydrated aluminum oxide (Al2O3.2H2O); clay-like substance varying from pink to white or red in color, closely associated with laterite rocks.
  • Odisha Bauxite Leadership: Odisha is the top producer of bauxite in India, accounting for over 50% of total national output; Koraput, Kalahandi, and Sundargarh are core districts.
  • Gujarat and Maharashtra Reserves: Gujarat ranks high with deposits in Kutch, Jamnagar, and Junagadh; Maharashtra has major reserves in Kolhapur, Ratnagiri, and Thane districts.
  • Jharkhand and Chhattisgarh: Lohardaga in Jharkhand and Mainpat plateau in Surguja district (Chhattisgarh) are prominent bauxite-yielding regions supporting the aluminum industry.

Mineral Distribution in Uttar Pradesh

Uttar Pradesh is primarily an agricultural state with limited mineral wealth, concentrated mainly in the southern plateau (Bundelkhand and Vindhyan region).

  • Mineral Policy of UP: First mineral policy of UP was announced in 1998, declaring mining as an industry to attract investments and generate employment in backward regions.
  • Limestone and Dolomite: Limestone is found in Dalla-Guruma (Sonbhadra) and Kajrahat (Mirzapur) supplying cement plants; dolomite is found in Banda and Sonbhadra.
  • Coal and Non-Metallic Minerals: Coal occurs in the Singrauli region of Sonbhadra district; glass sand (silica) is prominently found in the river beds of Shankargarh (Prayagraj) and Banda.
  • Banda and Lalitpur Minerals: Lalitpur district holds unique mineral deposits including uranium, copper, platinum, and diaspore; Banda is known for pyrophyllite, bauxite, and china clay.
