Major Rivers and Lakes of India Core Study Notes

The study of major rivers and lakes is pivotal for the Allahabad High Court RO/ARO and UPPSC examinations. Questions frequently target drainage systems, origins, right and left-bank tributaries, multi-purpose river valley projects, and tectonic or oxbow lakes specific to Uttar Pradesh and India.

Himalayan Drainage System

Perennial river systems originating from the snow-clad Himalayas, featuring antecedent drainage and massive gorges.

  • Ganga River System: Originates as Bhagirathi from Gangotri Glacier; joins Alaknanda at Devprayag to form the Ganga. Total length is approx. 2,525 km, with maximum length flowing through Uttar Pradesh.
  • Yamuna River System: Longest and westernmost tributary of the Ganga, originating from Yamunotri Glacier on Bandarpunch peak. Meets Ganga at Triveni Sangam in Prayagraj.
  • Brahmaputra River System: Originates from Chemayungdung glacier near Mansarovar lake in Tibet (known as Yarlung Tsangpo). Enters India in Arunachal Pradesh as Dihang.
  • Indus River System: Flows west-northwest between the Ladakh and Zanskar ranges. Major tributaries include Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, and Sutlej governed by the Indus Waters Treaty (1960).
  • Major Left Bank Tributaries of Ganga in UP: Includes Ramganga, Gomti, Ghaghra, Gandak, and Kosi. Gomti originates from Gomat Taal (Fulhar Lake) in Pilibhit district, Uttar Pradesh.

Peninsular Drainage System

Older river systems characterized by broad, shallow valleys, non-perennial (seasonal) flow, and graded profiles.

  • Godavari River: Largest river of Peninsular India, often called 'Dakshina Ganga', originating from Trimbakeshwar in Nashik district, Maharashtra.
  • Krishna River: Second largest east-flowing peninsular river, originating near Mahabaleshwar in the Sahyadri range. Major tributaries include Tungabhadra, Ghatprabha, and Musi.
  • Narmada River: Flows westward through a rift valley between Vindhya and Satpura ranges, originating from Amarkantak plateau and draining into the Gulf of Khambhat.
  • Tapi (Tapti) River: Another major west-flowing river originating from Multai in Betul district, Madhya Pradesh, flowing through a rift valley parallel to Narmada.
  • Chambal River (UP-MP Border): Important right-bank tributary of Yamuna originating from Janapav hill (Mhow, MP). Famous for extensive badland topography (ravines) along the UP-MP border.

Major Lakes of India and Uttar Pradesh

Inland water bodies categorized by origin including tectonic, glacial, oxbow, lagoon, and artificial reservoirs.

  • Wular Lake (Jammu & Kashmir): Largest freshwater lake in India, formed due to tectonic activity. Fed by the Jhelum River.
  • Chilika Lake (Odisha): Largest coastal lagoon in India and second largest in the world. Designated as India's first Ramsar wetland site.
  • Fulhar Lake (Pilibhit, UP): A prominent oxbow lake located in Madhotand, Pilibhit district, serving as the primary source of the Gomti River.
  • Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar: India's largest artificial man-made lake/reservoir, formed behind the Rihand Dam on the Rihand River in Sonbhadra district, UP.
  • Loktak Lake (Manipur): Largest freshwater lake in Northeast India, famous for floating decomposed plant materials called 'phumdis' and Keibul Lamjao National Park.

River Interlinking & Major Water Projects

Strategic engineering interventions designed to transfer surplus water from flood-prone basins to water-deficit regions.

  • Ken-Betwa Link Project: First project under the National Perspective Plan for river interlinking. Transfers water from Ken River to Betwa River, benefiting Bundelkhand region across UP and MP.
  • Tehri Dam Project: Highest dam in India (260.5 meters) built on Bhagirathi River in Uttarakhand, serving multi-purpose irrigation and hydroelectric power generation.
  • Sardar Sarovar Project: Massive concrete gravity dam on the Narmada River in Gujarat, supplying drinking water and irrigation to Gujarat, MP, Maharashtra, and Rajasthan.
  • Matatila Dam: Constructed on the Betwa River in Lalitpur district, Uttar Pradesh, serving joint irrigation interests of Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh.
