## Verdict

The collection is not yet sufficient as a complete, reliable standalone mastery resource. AA SL has the strongest lesson depth. AA HL has substantial extension lessons but uncovered syllabus areas and accuracy defects. AI SL has incomplete exercise/test sections and missing core coverage. AI HL has only five focused extension lessons and substantial course gaps.

A page being long, having four tabs, or listing syllabus codes is not evidence of mastery. Judge complete concept coverage, mathematical correctness, worked variations, independent transfer, feedback, technology competence and delayed retrieval. Hubs are assessed as navigation/coverage maps, not as full textbooks.
