Who We Are

SJMaths is an independent educational initiative launched in January 2026 in Uttar Pradesh, India, dedicated to making high-quality, concept-driven learning freely accessible to school students and competitive exam aspirants across India. We bridge the gap between textbook theory and practical problem-solving through meticulously curated study notes, verified step-by-step NCERT solutions, chapter-wise previous year questions (PYQs), and authentic UPSC Prelims & Mains practice modules.

Our Editorial & Quality Standards

Every piece of educational content published on SJMaths undergoes a strict multi-tier review cycle before reaching students:

  • Curriculum Verification: All formulas, proofs, and definitions are cross-referenced with official NCERT and CBSE publication standards.
  • Authentic Distractor Design: In competitive exam quizzes (such as UPSC and SSC), multiple-choice options and statement-based questions are crafted using standard reference texts (e.g., M. Laxmikanth, Ramesh Singh, Bipan Chandra, Spectrum) to accurately simulate real exam conditions.
  • Bilingual Accessibility: Key microtopics feature authentic Hindi translations alongside English so Hindi-medium students have equal access to top-tier preparation material.
  • Continuous User Feedback: Our analytics and feedback channels are actively monitored to promptly clarify solutions, update recent year questions, and refine explanations.
