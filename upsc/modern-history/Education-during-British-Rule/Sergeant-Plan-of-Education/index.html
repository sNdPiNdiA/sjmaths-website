Sergeant Plan Of Education

The Sergeant Plan of Education, officially titled 'Post-War Educational Development in India', was a comprehensive 1944 blueprint prepared by the Central Advisory Board of Education (CABE) to reconstruct and universalize the Indian educational system over a 40-year period.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) focusing on British colonial policy, evolution of modern education, and nationalist responses. Also connects with Post-Independence educational policy roots (Kothari Commission, Radhakrishnan Commission) tested in Mains GS-1/GS-2.

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Sergeant Plan Of Education.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
