Orientalist Anglicist Controversy

A fierce 19th-century policy debate within the British East India Company administration regarding the medium of instruction, content, and objectives of education to be promoted in British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) covering British administrative policies, socio-cultural impacts, and the evolution of modern Indian education from the Charter Act of 1813 to Macaulay's Minute of 1835.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of the Orientalist Anglicist Controversy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Charter Act of 1813
  • Early British administrative policies in India
  • Introduction of Western education in colonial India
