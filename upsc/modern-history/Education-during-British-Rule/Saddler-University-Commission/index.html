Saddler University Commission

Officially known as the Calcutta University Commission, the Saddler Commission was appointed in 1917 by the Government of India to investigate the condition and potential reforms of Calcutta University, serving as a comprehensive blueprint for higher education overhaul across British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding its timeline, key Indian members, and structural recommendations (like the separation of intermediate education and establishment of a Board of Secondary Education). In Mains GS-1 (History) and GS-2 (Education/Governance), it serves as a critical policy benchmark for the evolution of Indian educational federalism and institutional autonomy.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, chronology, and structural mechanisms of the Saddler University Commission.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions of British Indian education.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
