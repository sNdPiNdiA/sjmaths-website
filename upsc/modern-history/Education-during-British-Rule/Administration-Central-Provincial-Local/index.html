Administration Central Provincial Local

The multi-tiered structural evolution of British-Indian administration encompassing central autocratic control, provincial decentralization experiments, and local self-government institutions from 1773 to 1947.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims through statutory evolution (Regulating Act to Government of India Act 1935) and in Mains GS-1/GS-2 for tracing the roots of Indian federalism, local governance, and bureaucratic machinery.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the chronological evolution of central, provincial, and local administrative tiers under British rule.
  • Evaluate financial devolution schemes (Mayo, Lytton, Ripon) and statutory reforms (1861, 1892, 1909, 1919, 1935).
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to tackle complex match-the-following and chronological Prelims questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • British expansion and consolidation in India
  • Constitutional history of India
