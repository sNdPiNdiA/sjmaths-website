Development Of Vernacular Education

The historical evolution and policy shifts regarding the promotion, funding, and regulation of primary and secondary education in Indian regional languages (vernaculars) under British colonial rule.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History in Prelims (acts, committees, chronologies) and Mains GS-1 (social reform, socio-cultural impact of British rule, intelligentsia formation).
