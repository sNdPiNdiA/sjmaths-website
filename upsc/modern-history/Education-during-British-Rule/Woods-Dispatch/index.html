Woods Dispatch

Issued in July 1854 by Sir Charles Wood, President of the Board of Control, the Wood's Dispatch is hailed as the 'Magna Carta of English Education in India', laying the comprehensive blueprint for a structured system of public education from primary schools to universities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding historical acts, educational milestones, and institutional developments. In Mains (GS-1 Modern Indian History / GS-2 Governance), it forms the basis for analyzing colonial policy, socio-cultural awakening, and the anglicist-classicist controversy resolution.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, objectives, and administrative mechanisms of the Wood's Dispatch of 1854.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, university establishment models, and the grant-in-aid system.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding colonial education policy.

  • Downward Filtration Theory
  • Charter Act of 1853
