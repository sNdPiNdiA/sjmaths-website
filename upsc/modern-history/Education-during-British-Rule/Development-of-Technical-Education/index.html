Development Of Technical Education

The historical evolution and institutionalisation of vocational, engineering, and technical training under British colonial rule in India, transitioning from military-driven surveying needs to industrial manpower creation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Modern Indian History (GS-1) focusing on British economic policies, educational evolution, and socio-economic impact, as well as providing historical backdrop for contemporary skill development policies in GS-2/GS-3.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and chronological milestones of technical education under British rule.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution of pioneering engineering and technical institutions across British India.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky UPSC Prelims chronology and committee questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Charter Act of 1813 and Woods Despatch (1854)
  • Basic understanding of British economic drain theory and industrial policy
