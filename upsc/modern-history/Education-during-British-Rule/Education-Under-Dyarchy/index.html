Education Under Dyarchy

Education Under Dyarchy refers to the period between 1921 and 1937 when the administration of education in British India was transferred to Indian ministers under the system of dyarchy established by the Government of India Act, 1919, while overall financial control and reserved subjects remained with the British executive.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Modern Indian History (GS-1), specifically the evolution of Indian education policy, constitutional experiments, and the friction between nationalist aspirations and colonial financial constraints.
