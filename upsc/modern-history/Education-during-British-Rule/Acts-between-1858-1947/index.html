Acts Between 1858 1947

The constitutional and statutory enactments passed by the British Parliament governing the administration of British India from the assumption of direct Crown rule in 1858 until India's independence in 1947.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic forms the backbone of Indian Polity and Modern History in UPSC CSE. Prelims frequently tests specific institutional changes, franchise expansions, and executive veto powers, while Mains requires analyzing the evolution of responsible government, devolution of powers, and communal electorates.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
