Indian Universities Act 1904

A landmark colonial educational legislation enacted during the Viceroyalty of Lord Curzon that officially tightened official control over Indian universities, reduced the size of senates, and emphasized research and higher standards over mere expansion.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly critical microtopic for Modern Indian History (GS-1) focusing on British educational policies, institutional centralization under Lord Curzon, and the growth of Indian nationalism. Frequently tested in Prelims regarding specific provisions, committees, and Governor-General association.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, legislative mechanisms, and context of the Indian Universities Act 1904.
  • Evaluate the institutional framework, centralization measures, and financial grants introduced by Lord Curzon.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to differentiate between Raleigh Commission recommendations and the 1904 Act provisions for Prelims.
