Hartog Committee

The Hartog Committee (officially the Auxiliary Committee of the Indian Statutory Commission) was appointed in 1929 under the chairmanship of Sir Philip Hartog to survey the growth of education in British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination (GS Paper I - Modern Indian History and GS Paper II - Education/Governance dimensions), particularly regarding British colonial education policy, quantitative expansion versus qualitative degradation, and rural primary education.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of the Hartog Committee report of 1929.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions regarding primary and secondary education under British rule.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Wood's Despatch (1854)
  • Hunter Commission (1882)
  • Lord Curzon's Educational Policy (1904)
  • Sadler University Commission (1917-19)
