Hunter Education Commission

The Hunter Education Commission, officially known as the Indian Education Commission (1882-83), was appointed by Viceroy Lord Ripon to review the implementation of Wood's Despatch of 1854 and assess the state of primary and secondary education in British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding chronology, specific recommendations (mass primary education, private enterprise), and often confused with the Hunter Committee of 1919 (Jallianwala Bagh).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, objectives, and historical context of the Hunter Commission.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions regarding primary vs. secondary education.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions without confusing it with the 1919 Hunter Committee.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Wood's Despatch of 1854
  • Evolution of British educational policy in India
