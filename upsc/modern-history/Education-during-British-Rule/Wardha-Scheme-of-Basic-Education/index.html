Wardha Scheme Of Basic Education

Also known as Basic Education or Nai Talim, the Wardha Scheme was an educational plan formulated in 1937 under the guidance of Mahatma Gandhi, advocating for free, compulsory, and craft-centered primary education through the mother tongue.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) and Social Sector/Governance (GS-2), testing evolution of national education policy, Gandhian ideology, and self-reliance models.
