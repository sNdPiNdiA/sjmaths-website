Kothari Education Commission

The Indian Education Commission (1964–1966), popularly known as the Kothari Commission, was a national commission appointed by the Government of India to formulate a comprehensive, national pattern of education and advise on the national policy of education principles for development at all levels.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (historical chronology, committee chairpersons, key quantitative targets like 6% GDP) and Mains GS Paper 1 & 2 (evolution of Indian education policy, post-independence institution building, and structural reforms).
