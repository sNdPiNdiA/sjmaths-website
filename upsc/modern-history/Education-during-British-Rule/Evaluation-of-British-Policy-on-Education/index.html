Evaluation Of British Policy On Education

A comprehensive critical assessment of how colonial educational interventions in British India evolved from orientalist preservation to Anglicist imposition, mass neglect, and eventual utilitarian vocational framing, serving imperial administrative and economic goals.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History in both Prelims (chronology, acts, committee reports) and Mains GS-1 (impact of British policies on society, rise of nationalism, socio-religious reforms, and the roots of modern Indian intelligentsia).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, chronological evolution, and imperial motives behind British educational policies.
  • Evaluate structural outcomes, class filtration theory, regional disparities, and long-term socio-political impacts.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to retain committee names, acts, and chronological milestones for Prelims.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Charter Acts of 1793, 1813, and 1833
  • Macaulay's Minute of 1835
  • Wood's Despatch of 1854
