Syed Ahmad Khan

A 19th-century Indian Muslim reformer, jurist, and educationist who pioneered the Aligarh Movement, advocating for rationalism, scientific temper, and reconciliation between Islamic theology and modern Western education.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the socio-religious reform movements, the evolution of Muslim political consciousness, and the roots of the Two-Nation Theory in Modern Indian History.
