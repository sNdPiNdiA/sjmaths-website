Mahadev Govind Ranade (1842–1901)

A pioneering Indian social reformer, economist, and judge, often called the 'Father of Indian Economics' and the 'Prince of Graduates', who provided the intellectual foundation for the Indian National Congress.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Ranade is a perennial favorite in Modern History (Social Reform Movements) and GS Paper I (Indian Society). Questions focus on his institutional building, economic critique of British rule, and his role as a mentor to Gokhale.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
