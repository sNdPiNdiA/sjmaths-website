Annie Besant & Theosophical Society

The Theosophical Society was an international organization founded in 1875 to promote the study of comparative religion, philosophy, and science, with Annie Besant serving as its President (1907–1933) and transforming it into a vehicle for Indian cultural revival and political nationalism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Cultural-Nationalist' phase of the Indian Freedom Struggle, the synthesis of Western occultism with Eastern philosophy, and the evolution of the Home Rule Movement.
