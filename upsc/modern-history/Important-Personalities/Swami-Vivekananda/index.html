Swami Vivekananda

A 19th-century Indian Hindu monk, philosopher, and chief disciple of Ramakrishna Paramahansa who introduced Indian philosophies of Vedanta and Yoga to the Western world and spearheaded a cultural renaissance in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Modern Indian History (Socio-Religious Reform Movements) and Ethics (GS-IV). Frequently tested for his role in nationalism, education, and synthesis of East-West thought.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
