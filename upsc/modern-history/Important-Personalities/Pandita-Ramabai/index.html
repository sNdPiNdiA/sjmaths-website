Pandita Ramabai

A pioneering Indian social reformer, feminist, and scholar who challenged the patriarchal structures of 19th-century Hindu society through education, widow empowerment, and institutional reform.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Modern Indian History (Social Reform Movements) and Women's Studies in GS Paper I; frequently tested for her unique synthesis of traditional Sanskrit scholarship and radical social critique.
