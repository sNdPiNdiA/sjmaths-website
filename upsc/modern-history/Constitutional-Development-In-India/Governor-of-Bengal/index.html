Governor Of Bengal

The Governor of Bengal was a premier executive office established by the British East India Company following the Battle of Plassey (1757) and Battle of Buxar (1764), which later evolved into the Governor-General of Bengal under the Regulating Act of 1773.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial foundational microtopic for Modern Indian History (GS Paper 1) and Constitutional Development (GS Paper 2), tracing the administrative evolution from a purely commercial trading post to a centralized imperial government.
