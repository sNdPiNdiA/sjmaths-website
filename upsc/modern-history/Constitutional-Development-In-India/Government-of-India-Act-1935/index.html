Government Of India Act 1935

A landmark constitutional enactment by the British Parliament that provided a detailed blueprint for an All-India Federation, introduced provincial autonomy, and served as the primary structural template for the modern Constitution of India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (factual provisions, division of subjects, bicameralism) and Mains (evolution of Indian federalism, administrative continuity, legacy in the present Constitution).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
