Charter Act Of 1813

The East India Company Act 1813, commonly known as the Charter Act of 1813, was an Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom which renewed the charter of the British East India Company (EIC) for another 20 years, while decisively curtailing its commercial monopoly in India and asserting absolute British Crown sovereignty.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (Modern History, Constitutional Evolution) and Mains GS-1/GS-2 (British policies, socio-religious awakening, economic impact of colonial rule). Frequently tested for shifts in trade monopoly, Christian missionary permissions, and educational grants.
