Government Of India Act 1919

Also known as the Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms, the Government of India Act 1919 was an Act of the British Parliament enacted to expand the participation of Indians in the administration of their country.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC CSE Prelims for specific structural changes (Dyarchy, bicameralism, direct elections) and in Mains GS Paper 2 for the evolution of the Indian Constitution and the transition from responsible governance to provincial autonomy.
