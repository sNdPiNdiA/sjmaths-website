The Indian Councils Act 1861

A landmark British Parliamentary statute that transformed the executive-dominated governance of India into a consultative framework, initiating the policy of association of Indians with legislation and decentralizing legislative powers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the Indian legislature, constitutional decentralization, portfolio system origins, and the historical seeds of representative government under British rule.
