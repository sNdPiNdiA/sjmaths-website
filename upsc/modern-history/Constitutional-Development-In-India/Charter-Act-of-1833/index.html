Charter Act Of 1833

Also known as the Saint Helena Act 1833 or the Government of India Act 1833, this landmark legislation marked the final step towards the centralization of British administration in India and ended the East India Company's commercial privileges, converting it into a purely administrative body for the British Crown.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (evolution of central legislature, governor-general powers, legal codification) and Mains GS Paper II (historical underpinnings of the Indian Constitution, evolution of administrative machinery).
