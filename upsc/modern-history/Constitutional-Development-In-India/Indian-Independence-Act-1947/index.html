Indian Independence Act 1947

An Act of the Parliament of the United Kingdom that partitioned British India into two new independent dominions of India and Pakistan, giving effect to the Mountbatten Plan of June 3, 1947.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (dates, provisions, sovereignty transfers) and Mains (constitutional evolution, partition trauma, nation-building, integration of princely states).
