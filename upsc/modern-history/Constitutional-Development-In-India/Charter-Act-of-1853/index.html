Charter Act Of 1853

The Charter Act of 1853 was the last of the series of Charter Acts passed by the British Parliament between 1793 and 1853, significantly altering the constitutional machinery of British India by separating the legislative and executive functions of the Governor-General's Council and introducing open competition for civil services.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Exam (Prelims & Mains GS Paper I & II) under Modern Indian History and Indian Polity, as it marks the foundational shift toward a parliamentary-type local legislature, open merit-based recruitment, and the beginning of representative governance in India.
