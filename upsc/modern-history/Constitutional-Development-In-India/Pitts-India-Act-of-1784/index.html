Pitts India Act Of 1784

Enacted by the British Parliament under Prime Minister William Pitt the Younger, the Pitt's India Act of 1784 established a system of double government by distinguishing between the commercial and political functions of the East India Company, placing Indian affairs under the direct control of the British government.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of British paramountcy in India, the transition from a trading corporation to a territorial sovereign power, and the historical foundation of the dual control system tested frequently in Prelims and Modern Indian History Mains.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the structural architecture and dual control mechanism introduced by the Pitt's India Act of 1784.
  • Evaluate the comparative differences between the Regulating Act of 1773 and the Pitt's India Act of 1784.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to differentiate between the Board of Control and Court of Directors.
