Indian Councils Act 1892

An enactment of the British Parliament that made incremental changes to the composition and functions of the legislative councils in British India, introducing the principle of indirect election and the right to discuss the annual budget and pose questions to the executive.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Prelims (evolution of legislative devolution, representative elements) and Mains GS-1/GS-2 (Constitutional Development, nationalist movement pressure on colonial administration).
