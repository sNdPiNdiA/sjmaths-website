Evolution Of Police 1791

The formal reorganization of the police system in Bengal in 1791 under Lord Cornwallis, which established a regular district police force headed by a Daroga, replacing the traditional feudal Zamindari police system.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under Modern Indian History (Evolution of Administrative and Police Services). It highlights British colonial state-building, centralization of executive authority, and the structural shift from indigenous decentralized policing to a bureaucratic colonial security apparatus.
