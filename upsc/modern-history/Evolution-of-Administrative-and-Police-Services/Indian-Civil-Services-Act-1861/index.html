Indian Civil Services Act 1861

Enacted by the British Parliament, the Indian Civil Services Act of 1861 aimed to validate the irregular past appointments made to high-level civil service posts in India and reserve specific high-ranking offices exclusively for members of the covenanted civil service.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic is critical for UPSC CSE Prelims (history of British legislation, administrative evolution) and Mains GS-1 (British administrative policies, institutional legacy, and structural evolution of public administration in India).
