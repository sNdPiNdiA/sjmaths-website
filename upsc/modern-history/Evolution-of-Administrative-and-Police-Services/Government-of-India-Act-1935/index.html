Government Of India Act 1935

A landmark constitutional enactment by the British Parliament that provided a blueprint for a federal system in India, established provincial autonomy, and served as the primary constitutional prototype for independent India's Constitution.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (direct statutory features, division of powers, electoral provisions) and Mains GS-1 & GS-2 (evolution of constitutional framework, administrative legacy, national movement milestones).
