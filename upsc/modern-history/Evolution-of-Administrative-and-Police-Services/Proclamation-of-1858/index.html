Proclamation Of 1858

The Queen's Proclamation of 1858, issued on November 1, 1858, by Queen Victoria, transferred the governance of India directly from the British East India Company to the British Crown, signaling a major paradigm shift in Indian administrative history.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (historical chronology, constitutional milestones, transfer of power) and Mains GS-1 (British administrative policies post-1857, evolution of civil services, policy of 'divide and rule', and treatment of princely states).
