William Bentinck's Contribution

Lord William Bentinck served as the Governor-General of India from 1828 to 1835, marking an era of benevolent liberalism, rigorous administrative consolidation, socio-religious reforms, and financial retrenchment.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) focusing on British administrative policies, socio-religious reform movements of the 19th century, and the transition from a purely mercantile corporation to a structured administrative state.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of William Bentinck's administrative, judicial, and social reforms.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions including the suppression of Sati and Thuggee.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on Charter Act 1833 and judicial reorganizations.
