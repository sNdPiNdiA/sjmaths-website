Islington Commission

The Royal Commission on the Public Services in India, appointed in 1912 under the chairmanship of Lord Islington, tasked with examining and making recommendations on the recruitment, conditions of service, salary, and the extent of employment of Indians in the higher public services of British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) under the evolution of civil services, administrative reforms, British imperial policy, and the growing pressure of Indian nationalism for 'Indianisation' of services.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and historical context of the Islington Commission (1912-1915).
  • Evaluate structural recommendations regarding Imperial vs. Provincial services and the concept of 'Indianisation'.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics and distinguish it from other historic public service commissions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Evolution of Civil Services in British India
  • Indian Councils Act 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms)
  • Rise of Indian Nationalism
