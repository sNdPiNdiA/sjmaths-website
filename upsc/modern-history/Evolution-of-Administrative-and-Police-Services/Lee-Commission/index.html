Lee Commission

The Lee Commission, officially known as the Superior Civil Services in India Commission, was established in 1923 under the chairmanship of Lord Lee of Fareham to investigate the structure, recruitment, and racial composition of the all-India services under British rule.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

A high-yielding microtopic in Modern History (Evolution of Administrative and Police Services). UPSC frequently tests its recommendations, particularly regarding the 'Indianization' of the civil services, the creation of the Public Service Commission, and the division between all-India and transferred services.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the background, terms of reference, and key recommendations of the Lee Commission.
  • Evaluate the impact of the commission on Indianization and administrative devolution under the Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms.
  • Analyze Examiner Traps regarding statutory bodies versus advisory boards established following the commission.
