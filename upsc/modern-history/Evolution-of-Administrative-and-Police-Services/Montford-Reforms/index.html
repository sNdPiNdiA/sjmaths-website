Montford Reforms

The Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms, embodied in the Government of India Act 1919, introduced the system of 'dyarchy' in the provinces and expanded legislative representation in British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the constitutional evolution of British India, the growth of representative institutions, and the roots of federalism in GS-1 (Modern Indian History) and GS-2 (Indian Constitution and Polity).
