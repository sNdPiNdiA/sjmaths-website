Aitchison Committee On Public Services

The Public Service Commission appointed in 1886 under the chairmanship of Sir Charles Aitchison to consider the claims of Indians to higher employment in the public services of India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under Modern Indian History (Evolution of Administrative Services) frequently tested in Prelims regarding administrative reforms, colonial policy shifts, and the nomenclature of civil services.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the historical context, terms of reference, and recommendations of the Aitchison Committee.
  • Understand the transition from covenanted to provincial/imperial/subordinate civil service classifications.
  • Analyze the colonial intent behind the committee's reforms and Indian nationalist reactions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Charter Act of 1853 (End of company patronage)
  • Queen Victoria's Proclamation of 1858
  • Indian Civil Service Act of 1861
