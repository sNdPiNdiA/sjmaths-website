Land Revenue Mahalwari

The Mahalwari system was a major land revenue system introduced by the British in India, where the unit of assessment was the 'Mahal' (estate or village as a whole), making the village community jointly and severally responsible for the payment of revenue.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding its geographical coverage, architects, and comparison with Permanent Settlement and Ryotwari. In Mains GS-1, it is essential for analyzing British economic policies, commercialization of agriculture, and the roots of rural indebtedness.
