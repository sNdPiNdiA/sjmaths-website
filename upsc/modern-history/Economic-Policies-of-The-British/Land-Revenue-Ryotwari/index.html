Land Revenue Ryotwari

A land revenue system introduced by the British in India where direct settlement was made between the government and the ryot (cultivator), establishing individual property rights in land without intermediaries like Zamindars.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Extensively tested in UPSC Prelims regarding its geographical spread, architects, and differences from Permanent Settlement, and in Mains (GS-1/GS-3) regarding agrarian distress, commercialization of agriculture, and colonial economic exploitation.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, mechanisms, and historical evolution of the Ryotwari System.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution across Madras, Bombay, and Assam, and its comparison with Zamindari and Mahalwari systems.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of British economic policies in 18th and 19th century India
  • Overview of Land Revenue Settlements (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari)
