Colonialism Phase Of Free Trade

The second phase of British colonialism in India (approx. 1813 to 1858), characterized by the transformation of India into a consumer of British manufactured goods and a supplier of raw materials, driven by the industrial bourgeoisie in Britain.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) and Economy/Post-Independence (GS-3). Tests the structural shift from mercantile loot to industrial-capitalist exploitation, deindustrialization, commercialization of agriculture, and drain of wealth.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
