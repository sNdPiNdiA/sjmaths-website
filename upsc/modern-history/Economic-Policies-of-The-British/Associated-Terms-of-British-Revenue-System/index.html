Associated Terms Of British Revenue System

The specialized vernacular and administrative terminology associated with the three major British land revenue settlements (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, and Mahalwari) implemented in colonial India to maximize agrarian surplus extraction.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (matching terms with meanings or matching terms to regions/systems) and Mains (GS-1 Modern Indian History: Land revenue impacts, peasant movements, and rural impoverishment).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational vernacular and administrative terms of the British revenue apparatus.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions of colonial land extraction.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims match-the-column questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic chronology of British conquest of India
  • Understanding of Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, and Mahalwari systems
