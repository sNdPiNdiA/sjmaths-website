Land Revenue Permanent Settlement

The Permanent Settlement, introduced in 1793 by Lord Cornwallis, was a land revenue system that fixed the tax liability of land in perpetuity with the zamindars, converting them into hereditary landowners while reducing cultivators to mere tenants.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (chronology, geographical spread, key figures like John Shore and Cornwallis) and Mains (GS-1 Modern Indian History, impact of British economic policies on Indian agriculture, commercialization, rural indebtedness, and peasant uprisings).
