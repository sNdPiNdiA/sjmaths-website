Colonialism Phase Of Finance Imperialism

The third and final phase of British colonialism in India (late 19th to mid-20th century), characterized by the export of surplus British capital to India, direct investment in infrastructure (railways, ports, tea plantations, mining), and financial control through banking, insurance, and the managing agency system.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the structural transformation of India's economy into a captive market for British surplus capital, laying the foundation for modern economic nationalism, and framing answers on the 'Drain of Wealth' in GS Paper 1 (Modern Indian History) and GS Paper 3 (Indian Economy/Imperialism impacts).
