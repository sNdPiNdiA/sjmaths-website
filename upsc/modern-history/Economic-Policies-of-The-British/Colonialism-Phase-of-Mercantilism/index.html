Colonialism Phase Of Mercantilism

The first phase of British colonialism in India (approx. 1757-1813), characterized by a monopoly of trade, direct plunder of Indian wealth, and the forced purchase of Indian manufactured goods using revenues extracted directly from India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition of the East India Company from a trading corporation to a territorial sovereign power, explaining the 'Drain of Wealth', and forming the foundation of modern economic history questions in both Prelims and Mains (GS Paper I).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
