Drain Of Wealth Theory

The Drain of Wealth Theory refers to the systematic transfer of India's wealth, resources, and capital to Britain without any adequate economic, commercial, or material return, which formed the core mechanism of British colonial exploitation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is foundational for Modern Indian History in UPSC CSE. It frequently appears in Prelims regarding pioneering economists (Dadabhai Naoroji, R.C. Dutt) and literary works, and is indispensable for Mains (GS-1 Modern History, GS-3 Indian Economy) to analyze the deindustrialization of India and the impoverishment of the peasantry.
