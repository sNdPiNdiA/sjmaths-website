Local Government Resolution Of May And Dyarchy

The 1882 Resolution on Local Self-Government (Lord Ripon) aimed to decentralize administration by creating local boards, while Dyarchy (introduced by the Government of India Act 1919) established a dual system of government in provinces, transferring 'Local Self-Government' to Indian ministers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the evolution of administrative decentralization, the transition from colonial autocracy to representative government, and the constitutional history of India.
