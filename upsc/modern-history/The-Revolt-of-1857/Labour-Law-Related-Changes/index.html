Labour Law Related Changes

The evolution of colonial labour legislation in India, primarily post-1857, aimed at regulating the industrial workforce to serve British economic interests, ensuring supply, and maintaining order in plantations, mines, and factories.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from mercantilist exploitation to industrial capitalism in colonial India; frequently linked to the rise of the Indian working-class movement and nationalist mobilization.
