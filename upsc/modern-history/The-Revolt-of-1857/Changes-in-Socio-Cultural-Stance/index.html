Changes In Socio Cultural Stance

The post-1857 shift in British colonial policy from active social interventionism (liberalism) to a stance of 'non-interference' and 'conservative paternalism' to prevent further native uprisings.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from the 'Civilizing Mission' to the 'Policy of Association', which explains the British alliance with conservative/feudal elements in India.
