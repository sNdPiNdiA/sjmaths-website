Critique Economic Drain

The systematic transfer of India's wealth and economic resources to Britain without any adequate economic, commercial, or material return, which formed the core of British colonial exploitation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding Indian nationalism, economic nationalism of early nationalists (Moderates), Modern History (GS-1), and colonial economic history (GS-3 Economy/Growth).
