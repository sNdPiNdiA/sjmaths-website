Commercialization Of Indian Agriculture

The structural transformation of Indian agriculture under British colonial rule, where agricultural production shifted from meeting domestic subsistence needs to producing cash crops for national and global industrial markets.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the 'Drain of Wealth', the impoverishment of the Indian peasantry, rural indebtedness, and structural backwardness of Indian agriculture, frequently tested in Modern History (GS-1) and Indian Economy linkages (GS-3).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Commercialization Of Indian Agriculture.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
