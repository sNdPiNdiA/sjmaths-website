Stagnation And Deterioration Of Agriculture

The systematic decline, technological backwardness, and commercial stagnation of traditional Indian agriculture under British colonial rule driven by exploitative land revenue settlements and forced commercialization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) and Indian Economy/Post-Independence (GS-3), exploring how British economic policies transformed a self-sufficient agrarian economy into a stagnant, impoverished sector.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the structural causes of agrarian backwardness under colonial rule.
  • Evaluate the impact of land revenue systems (Zamindari, Ryotwari, Mahalwari) on peasant indebtedness.
  • Understand the forced commercialization of agriculture and its vulnerability to global market shocks.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • British economic policies in India
  • Deindustrialization and ruin of artisans
