IndustrializationRuin Of Artisans And Handicrafts Men

The systematic dismantling and pauperization of traditional Indian artisans, handloom weavers, and handicraft producers during British rule driven by unilateral free trade, industrial revolution in Britain, and discriminatory colonial economic policies.

Crucial for GS Paper 1 (Modern Indian History - British impact on Indian economy) and GS Paper 3 (Inclusive growth, manufacturing sector evolution). Frequently tested in Prelims via economic history chronology and Mains through the critical evaluation of deindustrialization theories.
