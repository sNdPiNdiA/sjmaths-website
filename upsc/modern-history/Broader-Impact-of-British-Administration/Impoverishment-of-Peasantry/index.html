Impoverishment Of Peasantry

The systematic economic ruin, destitution, and loss of land rights of the Indian agricultural population resulting from British colonial land revenue systems, commercialization of agriculture, and destruction of traditional village self-sufficiency.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern History (GS-1) covering the socio-economic impact of British rule, land revenue settlements (Permanent, Ryotwari, Mahalwari), peasant movements (Indigo, Deccan Riots, Moplah, Bardoli), and Mains analytical questions regarding the de-peasantization of India.
