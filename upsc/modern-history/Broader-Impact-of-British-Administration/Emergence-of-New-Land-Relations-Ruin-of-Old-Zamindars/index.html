Emergence Of New Land Relations Ruin Of Old Zamindars

The systematic dismantling of traditional agrarian hierarchies by British colonial land revenue settlements (Permanent Settlement of 1793, Ryotwari, and Mahalwari), which converted land into a private, alienable, and marketable commodity, leading to the economic displacement of hereditary elites and the rise of a new class of absentee landlords, urban speculators, and moneylenders.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition of Indian agrarian structure under colonial rule, the commercialization of agriculture, rural indebtedness, peasant uprisings, and socio-economic changes tested in both GS Paper 1 (Modern Indian History) and GS Paper 3 (Land Reforms, Agriculture).
