Development Of Modern Industry

The asymmetric growth of factory-based manufacturing, extractive industries, and infrastructure (railways/plantations) in British India, characterized by colonial subordination, late emergence of Indian-owned capital, and lack of capital-goods manufacturing.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper 1 (Modern Indian History - Economic impact of British rule, Deindustrialization vs. Industrialization) and GS Paper 3 (Industrial policy, colonial legacy). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding chronology of key factories, managing agency systems, and tariff policies.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Development Of Modern Industry under British colonialism.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks (Managing Agencies), and tariff policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on industrial chronology.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Drain of Wealth theory
  • Deindustrialization of traditional handicrafts
  • Land revenue settlements and commercialization of agriculture
