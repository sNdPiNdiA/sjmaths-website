Critique Of The Colonial Economy

The intellectual and economic dismantling of British imperial policies in India by 19th-century Indian nationalist thinkers who exposed the systematic exploitation, deindustrialization, and drain of wealth.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central pillar of Modern Indian History in General Studies Paper-1 (History of India) and General Studies Paper-2/3 (Economic development, colonial legacy), frequently tested in both Prelims via statements and Mains via analytical questions on the Drain Theory.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Critique Of The Colonial Economy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • British expansion and consolidation in India
  • Land revenue systems (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari)
  • Charter Acts and free trade policy shifts
