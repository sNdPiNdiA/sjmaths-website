Abolition Of The Dual System

The Dual System of administration in Bengal, established by Robert Clive in 1765, separated the 'Diwani' (revenue collection) and 'Nizamat' (police and judicial administration) functions. The abolition of this system in 1772 by Warren Hastings marked the direct assumption of administrative responsibility by the British East India Company.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic for Modern Indian History (GS Paper I). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding the chronologies of Robert Clive and Warren Hastings, and in Mains regarding the systematic exploitation, economic drain, and structural transformation of Indian administration under Company rule.
