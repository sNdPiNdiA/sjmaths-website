Famine And Poverty

The systematic destitution of Indian peasantry and artisans under British colonial rule, culminating in catastrophic man-made famines due to the deliberate destruction of indigenous industries, extractive land revenue systems, and laissez-faire economic policies.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper 1 (Modern Indian History - Impact of British Rule on India) and GS Paper 3 (Economy - Inclusive Growth and Poverty), frequently tested through both analytical Mains questions on deindustrialization/drain of wealth and factual Prelims questions on famine commissions and chronologies.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of colonial famines and systematic poverty.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions of British economic exploitation.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on famine commissions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Land Revenue Systems (Permanent Settlement, Ryotwari, Mahalwari)
  • Drain of Wealth Theory by Dadabhai Naoroji
  • Deindustrialization of traditional Indian handicrafts
