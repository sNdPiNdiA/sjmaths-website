Chauri Chaura Incident 5th Feb 1922

A violent confrontation in the Gorakhpur district of United Provinces where a mob of protesters set fire to a police station, leading to the death of 22-23 policemen, which served as the immediate catalyst for Gandhi's withdrawal of the Non-Cooperation Movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the critical turning point in the Gandhian phase of the freedom struggle, testing the candidate's understanding of non-violence (Ahimsa) vs. mass mobilization, and the internal ideological rift within the Congress (Pro-changers vs. No-changers).
