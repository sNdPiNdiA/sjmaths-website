Rowlatt Satyagraha And Jallianwala Bagh Massacre April 13 1919

The Rowlatt Satyagraha was the first mass-based political movement led by Gandhi against the repressive Anarchical and Revolutionary Crimes Act (1919), which culminated in the brutal Jallianwala Bagh massacre, marking a turning point in the Indian freedom struggle.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the transition from moderate to mass-based nationalism, the radicalization of the Indian youth, and the subsequent launch of the Non-Cooperation Movement.
