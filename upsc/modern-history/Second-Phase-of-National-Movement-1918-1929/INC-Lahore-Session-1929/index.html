INC Lahore Session 1929

The Lahore Session of the Indian National Congress (INC) held in December 1929, presided over by Jawaharlal Nehru, marked a paradigm shift from 'Dominion Status' to 'Purna Swaraj' (Complete Independence) as the official goal of the national movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a watershed moment in Indian history, marking the transition from constitutional agitation to mass civil disobedience, frequently tested for its ideological shift and the subsequent launch of the Civil Disobedience Movement.
