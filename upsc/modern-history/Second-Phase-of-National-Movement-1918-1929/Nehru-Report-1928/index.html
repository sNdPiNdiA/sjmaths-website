Nehru Report 1928

The Nehru Report was the first major attempt by Indians to draft a constitutional framework for India, formulated by an All-Parties Conference in response to Lord Birkenhead's challenge to produce a consensus constitution.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the shift from 'Dominion Status' to 'Purna Swaraj' and highlights the growing communal divide in the Indian National Movement.
