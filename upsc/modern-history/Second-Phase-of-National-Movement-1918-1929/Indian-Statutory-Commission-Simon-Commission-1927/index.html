Indian Statutory Commission Simon Commission 1927

A seven-member all-British commission appointed by the British Conservative government under Sir John Simon to report on the working of the Government of India Act 1919 and suggest further constitutional reforms.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It serves as a critical turning point in the Indian National Movement, marking the transition from moderate constitutional agitation to mass-based radical nationalism and the demand for Purna Swaraj.
