Jinnahs Fourteen Points Demand 1929

A charter of demands issued by Muhammad Ali Jinnah in March 1929 as a counter-proposal to the Nehru Report (1928), outlining the minimum political safeguards required by the Muslim League for participation in a future constitutional framework.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Parting of Ways' between the Congress and the Muslim League, the evolution of communal politics, and the transition from constitutional agitation to the demand for separate electorates.
