Highlight Champaran Satyagraha 1917

The Champaran Satyagraha was the first civil disobedience movement led by Mahatma Gandhi in India, aimed at addressing the grievances of indigo cultivators against the exploitative 'Tinkathia' system imposed by European planters.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition of the Indian National Movement from elite-led constitutional agitation to mass-based grassroots mobilization, serving as a template for future Gandhian movements.
