INC Allahabad Address 1930

The Allahabad Address refers to the Presidential Address delivered by Sir Muhammad Iqbal at the 21st session of the All-India Muslim League in Allahabad, 1930, which provided the intellectual and political blueprint for a separate Muslim state in Northwest India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the Two-Nation Theory, the ideological shift in the Muslim League, and the precursor to the Pakistan Resolution (1940).
