Highlight Kheda Satyagraha 1918

The Kheda Satyagraha was the first non-cooperation movement in India, organized by Mahatma Gandhi in the Kheda district of Gujarat to protest against the British government's refusal to remit land revenue despite crop failure.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition of Gandhi from a local agitator to a national leader and demonstrates the application of Satyagraha in a peasant-led agrarian crisis.
