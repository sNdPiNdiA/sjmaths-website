Khilafat Movement 1919 20

A pan-Islamic political protest campaign launched by Muslims in British India to influence the British government and protect the Ottoman Caliphate during the aftermath of World War I.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the first major instance of Hindu-Muslim unity in the national movement and serves as the precursor to the Non-Cooperation Movement (NCM).
