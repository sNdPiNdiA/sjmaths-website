Highlight Ahmedabad Mill Strike 1918

The Ahmedabad Mill Strike (1918) was the first hunger strike led by Mahatma Gandhi in India, representing a pivotal shift in the nationalist movement toward organized labor activism and the application of Satyagraha in industrial disputes.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition of Gandhi from a regional leader (Champaran) to a national figure, demonstrating the efficacy of non-violent resistance in urban industrial settings and the birth of the Ahmedabad Textile Labour Association (Majoor Mahajan).
