Developments Of Civil Services

The evolution of the British administrative machinery in India from a commercial agency (EIC) to a professional, merit-based, and hierarchical bureaucratic apparatus designed to consolidate colonial rule.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Steel Frame' of British India, the transition from patronage to meritocracy, and the administrative legacy influencing modern Indian governance.
