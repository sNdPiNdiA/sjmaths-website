The Charter Acts

A series of legislative enactments by the British Parliament between 1793 and 1853 that renewed the East India Company's (EIC) charter, gradually curtailing its commercial monopoly and centralizing administrative control in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These acts form the constitutional bedrock of British rule in India, marking the transition from a trading body to a sovereign administrative entity, frequently tested for their impact on legislative, executive, and judicial centralization.
