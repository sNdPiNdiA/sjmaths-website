Impact Of British Administration

The systematic transformation of Indian socio-economic, legal, and political structures by the East India Company (EIC) to facilitate colonial extraction, market integration, and administrative control.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic forms the bedrock of Modern Indian History, frequently appearing in GS Paper I (History) and GS Paper II (Governance/Polity) to explain the genesis of modern Indian institutions.
