Pitts India Act, 1784

A landmark constitutional reform enacted by the British Parliament to rectify the defects of the Regulating Act of 1773, establishing a system of 'Dual Control' (Diarchy) over the East India Company's affairs in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the beginning of British government control over the Company's administration, a recurring theme in the evolution of the Indian Constitution and administrative history.
