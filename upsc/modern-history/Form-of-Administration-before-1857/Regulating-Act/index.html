Regulating Act of 1773

The first legislative step taken by the British Government to control and regulate the affairs of the East India Company (EIC) in India, marking the transition from a commercial body to a political-administrative entity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is the foundational act of the British constitutional history in India, frequently tested for its role in centralizing administration and establishing the Supreme Court.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
