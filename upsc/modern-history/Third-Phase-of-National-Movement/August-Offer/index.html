August Offer

A proposal made by the British Government in August 1940, through Viceroy Lord Linlithgow, to secure Indian cooperation in the Second World War by promising Dominion Status and the expansion of the Viceroy's Executive Council.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks a critical shift in British policy from 'no concession' to 'limited concession' and serves as the immediate precursor to the Individual Satyagraha and the Cripps Mission.
