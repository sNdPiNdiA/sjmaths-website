Mountbatten Plan

The Mountbatten Plan, also known as the 'June 3rd Plan' (1947), was the final blueprint for the partition of British India into two independent dominions, India and Pakistan, and the transfer of power.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a pivotal topic in Modern Indian History, frequently tested for its role in the end of British Raj, the mechanism of partition, and the constitutional transition via the Indian Independence Act, 1947.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
