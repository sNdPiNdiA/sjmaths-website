Pakistan Resolution

The Pakistan Resolution, also known as the Lahore Resolution, was a formal political statement adopted by the All-India Muslim League (AIML) on March 23, 1940, demanding autonomous and sovereign states for Muslims in the North-Western and Eastern zones of British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the formal shift of the Muslim League from a demand for 'safeguards' to the demand for 'partition', serving as the ideological bedrock for the creation of Pakistan.
