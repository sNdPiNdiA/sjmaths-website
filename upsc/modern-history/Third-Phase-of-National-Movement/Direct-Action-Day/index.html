Direct Action Day

A call for mass protest and communal mobilization issued by the All India Muslim League (AIML) on August 16, 1946, to demand the creation of a separate sovereign state of Pakistan.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the point of no return in the partition process, leading to the 'Great Calcutta Killings' and the collapse of the Interim Government's communal harmony, essential for understanding the final phase of the Indian National Movement.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Direct Action Day.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
