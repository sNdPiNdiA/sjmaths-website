Parallel Governments

Self-governing administrative structures established by Indian nationalists during the Quit India Movement (1942) to challenge British colonial authority by exercising sovereign functions like law enforcement, taxation, and judicial arbitration.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the radicalization of the freedom struggle, the decentralization of nationalist power, and the transition from protest politics to governance-based resistance.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Parallel Governments.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Quit India Movement (1942) context
  • Evolution of Gandhian mass movements
