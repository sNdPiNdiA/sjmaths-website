Individual Satyagraha

A limited, symbolic protest movement launched by the Indian National Congress in 1940 to affirm the right to free speech against the British war effort, rather than seeking to embarrass the government during WWII.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents a critical bridge between the August Offer and the Quit India Movement, testing the candidate's understanding of Gandhian strategy and the Congress's nuanced stance on WWII.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
