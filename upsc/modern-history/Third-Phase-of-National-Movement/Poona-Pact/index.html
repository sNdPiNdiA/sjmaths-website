Poona Pact

An agreement signed on September 24, 1932, between B.R. Ambedkar and M.K. Gandhi (on behalf of the upper-caste Hindus) to abandon the Communal Award's provision of separate electorates for the Depressed Classes in favor of reserved seats within a joint electorate.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a pivotal event in the history of Dalit politics and the Indian National Movement, testing the candidate's understanding of the conflict between nationalist unity and identity-based political representation.
