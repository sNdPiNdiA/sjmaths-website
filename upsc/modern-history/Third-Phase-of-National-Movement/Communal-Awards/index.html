Communal Awards

The Communal Award, announced by British PM Ramsay MacDonald in August 1932, was a scheme of representation that extended the system of separate electorates to the Depressed Classes (Dalits) and other minorities, aiming to institutionalize communal divisions in Indian politics.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a pivotal event in the Third Phase of the National Movement, marking the climax of the British 'Divide and Rule' policy and triggering the historic Poona Pact between Gandhi and Ambedkar.
