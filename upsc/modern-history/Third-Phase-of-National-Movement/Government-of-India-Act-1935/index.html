Government Of India Act 1935

The Government of India Act 1935 was the longest and most complex piece of British legislation for India, providing a framework for an All-India Federation and provincial autonomy, serving as the primary blueprint for the Constitution of India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is the most significant constitutional precursor to the Indian Constitution; questions frequently focus on the division of powers, the federal structure, and the transition from dyarchy to provincial autonomy.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
