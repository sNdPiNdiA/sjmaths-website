Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose And INA

The Indian National Army (INA) or Azad Hind Fauj was an armed force formed by Indian nationalists in 1942 in Southeast Asia, with the objective of securing Indian independence from British rule through military action, led by Subhash Chandra Bose.

This topic is a perennial favorite in UPSC Prelims (chronology, leadership, locations) and Mains (impact on British withdrawal, communal harmony, and the role of radical nationalism).
