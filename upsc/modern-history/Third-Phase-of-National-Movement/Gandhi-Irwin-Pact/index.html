Gandhi Irwin Pact

A political agreement signed on March 5, 1931, between Mahatma Gandhi and Lord Irwin, representing the British Government, which marked the end of the Civil Disobedience Movement and paved the way for Congress participation in the Second Round Table Conference.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a pivotal turning point in the Indian National Movement, testing the candidate's understanding of the 'Truce-Struggle-Truce' strategy and the complex negotiations between colonial authority and nationalist leadership.
