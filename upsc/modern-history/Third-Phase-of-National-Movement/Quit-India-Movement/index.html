Quit India Movement

A mass civil disobedience movement launched by the Indian National Congress on August 8, 1942, demanding an orderly British withdrawal from India, characterized by the slogan 'Do or Die'.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the final major mass struggle before independence, testing candidates on the shift from constitutional agitation to spontaneous mass uprising and the role of underground activities.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Quit India Movement.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
