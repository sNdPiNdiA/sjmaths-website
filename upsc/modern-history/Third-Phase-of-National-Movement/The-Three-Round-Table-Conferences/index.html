The Three Round Table Conferences

A series of three peace conferences organized by the British Government between 1930 and 1932 to discuss constitutional reforms in India, following the failure of the Simon Commission.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These conferences are central to understanding the transition from the Civil Disobedience Movement to the Government of India Act, 1935, and the evolution of the communal award.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
