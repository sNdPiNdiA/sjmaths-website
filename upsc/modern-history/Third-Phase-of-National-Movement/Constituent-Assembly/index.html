Constituent Assembly

A sovereign body of elected representatives tasked with drafting the Constitution of India, functioning as both a constitution-making body and a provisional parliament.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to understanding the constitutional evolution of India, the legitimacy of the Indian state, and the transition from colonial rule to a sovereign republic.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Constituent Assembly.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Government of India Act 1935
  • Cabinet Mission Plan 1946
  • Indian Independence Act 1947
