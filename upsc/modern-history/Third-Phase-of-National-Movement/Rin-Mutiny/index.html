Rin Mutiny

The Royal Indian Navy (RIN) Mutiny, also known as the Bombay Mutiny, was a widespread revolt by Indian sailors of the Royal Indian Navy on ships and shore establishments in February 1946 against the British colonial administration.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical turning point in the 'Third Phase of the National Movement' (1945-47), signaling the collapse of the British Raj's control over the armed forces, which ultimately accelerated the decision to transfer power.
