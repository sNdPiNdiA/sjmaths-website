Desai Liaquat Pact

An informal, abortive agreement reached in January 1945 between Bhulabhai Desai (Leader of Congress Party in Central Legislative Assembly) and Liaquat Ali Khan (Deputy Leader of Muslim League) to form an interim government at the Centre.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents a critical phase of 'Constitutional Deadlock' during WWII, highlighting the failed attempts at Congress-League cooperation before the Wavell Plan.
