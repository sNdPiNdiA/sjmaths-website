Cabinet Mission Plan (1946)

A high-level mission sent by the British Labour Government to India in March 1946 to discuss and plan the transfer of power from British rule to Indian leadership, aiming to preserve Indian unity while addressing communal demands.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is the final constitutional blueprint before the Mountbatten Plan and the Indian Independence Act, frequently tested for its complex federal structure and the failure of the 'Grouping' clause.
