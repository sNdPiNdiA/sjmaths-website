Provisional Government Of Free India Singapore

The Arzi Hukumat-e-Azad Hind was a militant provisional government established in Singapore on October 21, 1943, by Subhas Chandra Bose to lead the Indian independence movement from abroad and command the Indian National Army (INA).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the radical phase of the freedom struggle, testing the candidate's understanding of the intersection between international diplomacy, military strategy, and anti-colonial nationalism.
