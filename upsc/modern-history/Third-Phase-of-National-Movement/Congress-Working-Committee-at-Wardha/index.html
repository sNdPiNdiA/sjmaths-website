Congress Working Committee At Wardha

The Wardha sessions of the Congress Working Committee (CWC) served as the strategic nerve center for the Indian National Congress during the critical transition period of 1939-1942, specifically shaping the trajectory of the Quit India Movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the shift from constitutional agitation to mass civil disobedience, the ideological rift between Gandhi and Bose, and the internal decision-making process of the INC during WWII.
