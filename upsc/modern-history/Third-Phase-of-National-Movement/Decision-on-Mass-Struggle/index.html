Decision On Mass Struggle

The strategic shift in the Indian National Congress (INC) from constitutional agitation and petitioning to the adoption of 'Mass Struggle' (Satyagraha/Non-Cooperation) as the primary instrument for achieving Swaraj.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the transition from the Moderate phase to the Gandhian era, testing the evolution of political ideology, mass mobilization techniques, and the internal debates within the INC.
