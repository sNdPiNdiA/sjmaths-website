Atlees Declaration And Transfer Of Power

The formal announcement by British PM Clement Attlee on February 20, 1947, setting a definitive deadline (June 1948) for the British withdrawal from India, marking the final phase of the decolonization process.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the constitutional transition, the role of the Labour Party in post-WWII British policy, and the geopolitical compulsions leading to the Mountbatten Plan.
