Tokyo Conference March 1942

A pivotal diplomatic gathering held in Tokyo (March 28–30, 1942) convened by Rash Behari Bose to formalize the formation of the Indian Independence League (IIL) and coordinate the struggle for India's liberation from British rule with Japanese support.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of the Azad Hind Fauj (INA), the role of the Indian diaspora in the freedom struggle, and the complex geopolitical alignment of Indian revolutionaries during WWII.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
