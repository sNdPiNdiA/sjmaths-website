Civil Disobedience Movement

A mass-based anti-colonial movement (1930-1934) launched by the Indian National Congress under Gandhi, characterized by the deliberate violation of unjust laws, specifically the Salt Laws, to achieve Purna Swaraj.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the shift from constitutional agitation to mass mobilization, testing candidates on the evolution of Gandhian strategy, regional variations, and the transition from the 1930 movement to the 1932 revival.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
