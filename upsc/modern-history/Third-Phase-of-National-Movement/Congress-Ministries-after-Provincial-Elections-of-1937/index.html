Congress Ministries After Provincial Elections Of 1937

The period (1937-1939) marking the governance of Indian National Congress in 8 out of 11 provinces under the Government of India Act, 1935, representing a shift from 'non-cooperation' to 'office acceptance' as a strategy to utilize state power for mass mobilization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the freedom struggle from agitation to administration, the internal ideological debates within Congress (Left vs. Right), and the eventual resignation leading to the 'Deliverance Day' and the Quit India Movement.
