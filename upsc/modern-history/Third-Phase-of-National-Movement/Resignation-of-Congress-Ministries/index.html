Resignation Of Congress Ministries

The collective resignation of Congress ministries in October-November 1939 across eight provinces as a protest against the British government's unilateral decision to involve India in World War II without consulting the Indian people.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This event marks a critical shift from 'Constitutional Agitation' to 'Mass Struggle' (Individual Satyagraha and Quit India Movement), testing the candidate's understanding of the transition in Gandhian strategy.
