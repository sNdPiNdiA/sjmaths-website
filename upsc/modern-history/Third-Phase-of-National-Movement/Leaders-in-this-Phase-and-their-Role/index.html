Leaders In This Phase And Their Role

The Third Phase of the Indian National Movement (1919-1947), often termed the 'Gandhian Era', represents the transition from elite-led constitutional agitation to mass-based, non-violent, and revolutionary resistance against British colonial rule.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This phase is the highest-weightage area in Modern History. UPSC frequently tests the ideological nuances, inter-leader dynamics, and the evolution of strategies (Satyagraha, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India).
