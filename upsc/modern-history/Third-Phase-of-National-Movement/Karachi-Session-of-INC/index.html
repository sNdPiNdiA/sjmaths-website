Karachi Session Of INC

The 1931 Karachi Session of the Indian National Congress was a landmark event held under the presidency of Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, primarily convened to endorse the Gandhi-Irwin Pact and define the socio-economic vision of independent India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a high-yield topic for UPSC Prelims (Resolution on Fundamental Rights) and Mains (Evolution of Congress ideology from political to socio-economic freedom).
