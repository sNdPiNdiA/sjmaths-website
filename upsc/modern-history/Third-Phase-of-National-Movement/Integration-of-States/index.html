Integration Of States

The process of merging 565 Princely States into the Indian Union post-Independence (1947-1950), orchestrated by the States Department under Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel and V.P. Menon.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the consolidation of India's territorial integrity, federal structure, and the evolution of the Indian Constitution.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Integration Of States.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
