MLCongress Response To Desai Liaquat

The Desai-Liaquat Pact (1945) was an informal, unauthorized proposal for an interim government between Bhulabhai Desai (Congress) and Liaquat Ali Khan (Muslim League), which ultimately failed due to lack of official endorsement from both parties' high commands.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the critical 'failed bridge' phase of the 1945 political negotiations, highlighting the deep-seated mistrust between the INC and ML, and serves as a precursor to the Wavell Plan.
