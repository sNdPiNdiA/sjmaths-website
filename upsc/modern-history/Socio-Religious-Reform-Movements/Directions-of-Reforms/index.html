Directions Of Reforms

The 'Directions of Reforms' refers to the ideological and methodological trajectories adopted by 19th and 20th-century Indian reformers to modernize society, categorized primarily into Reformist and Revivalist movements.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian nationalism, the synthesis of Western rationalism with indigenous traditions, and the socio-political underpinnings of the Indian Renaissance.
