Significance Of Reform Movements

The 19th and 20th-century socio-religious reform movements were organized efforts by the Indian intelligentsia to modernize society by purging irrational, superstitious, and oppressive practices, while simultaneously fostering a sense of national identity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These movements form the bedrock of modern Indian nationalism, social justice discourse, and the evolution of the Indian Constitution's secular and egalitarian values.
