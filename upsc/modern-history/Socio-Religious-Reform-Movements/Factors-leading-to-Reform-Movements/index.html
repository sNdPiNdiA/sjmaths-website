Factors Leading To Reform Movements

The 19th-century Socio-Religious Reform Movements (SRRM) were a response to the stagnation of Indian society, triggered by the collision between traditional Indian values and modern Western rationalism introduced by British colonial rule.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic forms the bedrock of Modern Indian History. UPSC frequently tests the causal link between colonial policies, intellectual awakening, and the rise of Indian Nationalism.
