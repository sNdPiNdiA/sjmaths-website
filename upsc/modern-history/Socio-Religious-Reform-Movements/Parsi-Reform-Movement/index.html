Parsi Reform Movement

A 19th-century socio-religious reform movement among the Zoroastrian community in India, primarily centered in Bombay, aimed at modernizing Parsi social customs and purging superstitious practices to align with Western education and liberal values.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the 'Westernized' segment of the Indian Renaissance, highlighting the role of urban, mercantile elites in social reform and the tension between traditional orthodoxy and modern rationalism.
