Impact Of Reform Movements

The 19th and 20th-century socio-religious reform movements were a transformative process of 'self-correction' within Indian society, aimed at reconciling traditional values with modern rationalist and humanist ideals to facilitate national regeneration.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian Nationalism, the transition from medieval to modern social structures, and the intellectual roots of the Indian Constitution (Equality, Liberty, Fraternity).
