Press Inquiry Committee 1947

The Press Inquiry Committee (1947) was a post-independence government initiative tasked with reviewing the existing restrictive press laws inherited from the British colonial administration to align them with the democratic aspirations of a free India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It serves as a critical bridge between colonial-era press censorship and the constitutional guarantee of Freedom of Speech and Expression (Article 19(1)(a)), often tested in the context of the evolution of media laws in India.
