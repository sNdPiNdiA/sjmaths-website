Vernacular Press Act 1878

A draconian legislative measure enacted by Lord Lytton to curtail the freedom of the Indian-language press by empowering the government to confiscate printing presses and demand security deposits for 'seditious' content.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a pivotal topic in Modern Indian History, illustrating the colonial state's repressive policy towards nationalist discourse and the subsequent rise of organized political agitation in India.
