Licensing Act 1857

An emergency legislative measure enacted by Lord Canning during the Revolt of 1857 to impose strict state control over the press by mandating a government license for the publication and circulation of all printed materials.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the first major legislative attempt by the British to curb the 'freedom of the press' in response to political instability, serving as a precursor to the more draconian Vernacular Press Act of 1878.
