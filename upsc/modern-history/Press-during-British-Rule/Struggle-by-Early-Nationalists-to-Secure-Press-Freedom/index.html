Struggle By Early Nationalists To Secure Press Freedom

The systematic campaign by the Indian National Congress (INC) and early nationalist leaders (1885-1905) to challenge colonial legislative curbs on the press, viewing it as the 'fourth estate' for political education and public opinion mobilization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian nationalism, the role of the press as a tool for mass mobilization, and the colonial state's repressive legislative apparatus.
