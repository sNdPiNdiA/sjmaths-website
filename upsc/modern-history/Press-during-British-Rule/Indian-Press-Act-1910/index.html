Indian Press Act 1910

A draconian legislative measure enacted by the British Government of India to curb the rising tide of nationalist sentiment and revolutionary activities by empowering local authorities to demand security deposits from printing presses and newspapers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the peak of colonial censorship, illustrating the shift from 'regulation' to 'suppression' of the Indian nationalist press, frequently appearing in questions regarding the evolution of civil liberties and colonial administrative policy.
