Press During And After The First World War

A period of intense state-sponsored censorship and repressive legislation (1914-1920) followed by a transition toward nationalist mobilization through the press, marking the shift from colonial control to the Gandhian era of journalism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian nationalism, the colonial state's 'security' apparatus, and the role of media in mass mobilization during the Home Rule and Non-Cooperation movements.
