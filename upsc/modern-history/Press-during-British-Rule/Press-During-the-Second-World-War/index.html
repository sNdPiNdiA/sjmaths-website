Press During The Second World War

A period of extreme state-imposed censorship and restrictive legislative control over the Indian press, driven by the British colonial government's need to suppress nationalist sentiment and manage war-time propaganda during 1939-1945.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the evolution of civil liberties, the intersection of colonial security laws with nationalist movements, and the role of the press as a tool for political mobilization during the Quit India Movement.
