Newspaper Incitement To Offences Act 1908

A repressive legislative measure enacted by the British colonial government to empower magistrates to confiscate printing presses and property used for publishing material deemed to incite murder or acts of violence.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of colonial press censorship, the suppression of the Swadeshi movement, and the trajectory of Indian nationalism.
