Press ActMetcalfe Act 1835

The Press Act of 1835, also known as the Metcalfe Act, was a landmark legislative measure that repealed the restrictive Licensing Regulations of 1823, earning Charles Metcalfe the title 'Liberator of the Indian Press'.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents a critical shift in British colonial policy from 'repressive control' to 'liberal tolerance' regarding the vernacular and English press, serving as a precursor to the rise of Indian nationalism.
