Press Post Independence

The evolution of the Indian press from a tool of anti-colonial struggle to a 'Fourth Estate' in a democratic republic, governed by constitutional provisions and statutory regulations.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of democratic institutions, freedom of speech (Article 19), and the tension between state security and media autonomy.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Press Post Independence.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Article 19(1)(a) of the Constitution
  • History of Press during British Rule (Vernacular Press Act, etc.)
