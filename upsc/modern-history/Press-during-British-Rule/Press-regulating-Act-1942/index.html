Press Regulating Act 1942

The Press Regulating Act of 1942 was a wartime emergency measure promulgated by the British colonial government under the Defence of India Rules to suppress nationalist reporting during the Quit India Movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the peak of colonial censorship during the Second World War, illustrating the conflict between imperial security and the freedom of the press in the context of the Indian National Movement.
