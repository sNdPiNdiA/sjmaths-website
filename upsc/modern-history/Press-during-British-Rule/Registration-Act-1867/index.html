Registration Act 1867

The Press and Registration of Books Act, 1867, was a regulatory statute enacted by the British colonial government to maintain a systematic record of all books and newspapers published in India, primarily to monitor and control the burgeoning nationalist press.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical component of the 'Press during British Rule' module, illustrating the colonial strategy of 'legalized surveillance' and the evolution of censorship mechanisms from direct bans to bureaucratic regulation.
