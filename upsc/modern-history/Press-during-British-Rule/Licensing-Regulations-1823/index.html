Licensing Regulations 1823

A restrictive press policy introduced by Acting Governor-General John Adam, mandating that every printer and publisher obtain a government license to operate a press, effectively curbing the burgeoning Indian-language press.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the first major legislative attempt by the British to institutionalize censorship, serving as a precursor to the more draconian Vernacular Press Act of 1878.
