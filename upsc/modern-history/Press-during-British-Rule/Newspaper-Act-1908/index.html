Newspaper Act 1908

The Newspaper (Incitement to Offences) Act, 1908, was a draconian legislative measure enacted by the British colonial government to suppress the burgeoning nationalist press, specifically targeting publications that encouraged violence or acts of terrorism during the Swadeshi Movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical component of the 'Press during British Rule' module, frequently tested to evaluate the candidate's understanding of colonial repressive mechanisms and the evolution of the Indian freedom struggle.
