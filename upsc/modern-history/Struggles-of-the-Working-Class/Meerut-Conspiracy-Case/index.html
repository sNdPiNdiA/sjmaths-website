Meerut Conspiracy Case

A landmark 1929 legal proceeding initiated by the British colonial government against 31 trade union leaders, communists, and activists, aimed at suppressing the rising influence of the Communist Party of India (CPI) and the organized labor movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the colonial state's repressive legislative response to the growth of socialist and communist ideologies in India, serving as a critical case study for the evolution of labor laws and civil liberties under the British Raj.
