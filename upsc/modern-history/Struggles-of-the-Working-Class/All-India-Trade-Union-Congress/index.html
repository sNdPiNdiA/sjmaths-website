All India Trade Union Congress (AITUC)

The AITUC is the oldest trade union federation in India, established in 1920 to provide a unified platform for the Indian working class to voice grievances against colonial exploitation and participate in the national freedom struggle.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

AITUC represents the intersection of the Indian National Movement and the rise of organized labor, frequently appearing in Prelims regarding its founding leadership and ideological shifts, and in Mains regarding the evolution of labor rights and socialist influence.
