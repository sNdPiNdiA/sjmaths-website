Trade Union Act 1926

The Trade Union Act 1926 is the primary legislation that provides for the registration of trade unions and defines the law relating to registered trade unions in British India, granting them legal status and immunity from certain civil and criminal liabilities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of labor rights, the institutionalization of the working-class movement, and the colonial state's strategy of 'controlled recognition' to mitigate radical industrial unrest.
