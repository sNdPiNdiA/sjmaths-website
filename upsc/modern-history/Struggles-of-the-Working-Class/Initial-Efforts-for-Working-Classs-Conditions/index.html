Initial Efforts For Working Classs Conditions

The nascent phase of labor organization in colonial India (mid-19th to early 20th century), characterized by philanthropic, humanitarian, and nationalist-led initiatives to address the exploitative conditions of industrial workers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of the Indian National Movement, the intersection of social reform and labor rights, and the transition from 'philanthropy' to 'organized trade unionism'.
