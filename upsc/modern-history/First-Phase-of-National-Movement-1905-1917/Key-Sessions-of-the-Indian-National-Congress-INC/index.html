Key Sessions Of The Indian National Congress INC

The chronological milestones and resolutions of the Indian National Congress from 1885 to 1917, reflecting the evolution of Indian nationalism from moderate petitions to assertive extremism and constitutional reunion.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (chronology, presidents, locations, key resolutions) and Mains GS-1 (evolution of national movement, extremist vs moderate dynamics).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational chronology, presidents, and landmark resolutions of key INC sessions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, ideological shifts, and friction points between Moderates and Extremists.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains historical questions.
