Pre INC Organisations

Political associations formed in 19th-century India, primarily by the Western-educated intelligentsia, to articulate grievances, demand administrative reforms, and foster a nascent sense of national consciousness before the formal establishment of the Indian National Congress in 1885.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These organizations represent the 'seed phase' of Indian nationalism. UPSC frequently tests the chronology, founders, objectives, and regional focus of these bodies to assess the candidate's understanding of the evolution of political consciousness.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Pre INC Organisations.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
