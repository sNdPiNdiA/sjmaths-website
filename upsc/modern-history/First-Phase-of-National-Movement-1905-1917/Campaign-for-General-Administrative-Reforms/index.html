Campaign For General Administrative Reforms

The systematic critique, demands, and agitations led by early Indian nationalists (Moderates) between 1905 and 1917 targeting the bureaucratic machinery, police administration, judiciary, and civil-military expenditure of the British Indian government.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of economic and administrative nationalism in Modern Indian History (GS-1), constitutional growth, civil services reforms, and the roots of public accountability.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the core administrative grievances raised by early nationalists against the British Raj.
  • Evaluate the nationalist demands regarding separation of powers, judicial reforms, and curtailment of police authoritarianism.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to retain key administrative reform demands for Prelims and Mains.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Rise of Indian Nationalism
  • Moderate Phase of the Indian National Congress (1885-1905)
