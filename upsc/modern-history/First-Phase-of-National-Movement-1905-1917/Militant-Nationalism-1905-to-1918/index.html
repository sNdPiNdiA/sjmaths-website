Militant Nationalism 1905 To 1918

The phase of the Indian freedom struggle characterized by aggressive assertion of national pride, rejection of British mendicancy (prayer and petition), mass mobilization, and the advent of extremist leadership alongside revolutionary terrorism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims due to specific facts on associations, newspapers, acts, and extremist leaders. Essential for GS-1 Mains to analyze the ideological shift from Moderates to Extremists, the Surat Split, Swadeshi and Boycott movements, and the rise of revolutionary extremism.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, ideology, and mechanisms of Militant Nationalism from 1905 to 1918.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, press censorship acts, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding extremist leaders, revolutionary secret societies, and the Home Rule Leagues.
