Government Repression (1905–1917)

Government Repression during the First Phase of the Indian National Movement (1905–1917) refers to the systematic legislative, executive, and physical measures deployed by the British colonial administration to suppress the Swadeshi and Boycott Movement, curb the rise of revolutionary nationalism, and break the extremist leadership.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the Indian freedom struggle from moderate petitioning to militant nationalism and the ultimate Surat Split (1907), as well as the colonial state's authoritarian character tested in Prelims (chronology of repressive acts) and Mains (impact of repression on the trajectory of nationalism).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the chronology, provisions, and direct impacts of colonial repressive legislation passed between 1905 and 1917.
  • Evaluate the British policy of 'Rally the Moderates' combined with a heavy-handed suppression of Extremists.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to accurately identify repressive acts and ordinances in UPSC Prelims.

  • The Swadeshi and Boycott Movement (1905)
  • Surat Split of 1907
