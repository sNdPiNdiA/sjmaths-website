Moderate Campaign For Constitutional Reforms

The Moderate phase of the Indian National Movement (1885-1905) pursued a systematic and peaceful campaign for constitutional reforms, demanding greater representation in legislative councils, extension of the sphere of council activities, and the ultimate realization of self-government within the British Empire through petition, prayer, and protest.
