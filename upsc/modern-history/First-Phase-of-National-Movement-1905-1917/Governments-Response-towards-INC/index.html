Governments Response Towards INC

The official policy, attitudes, and counter-strategies adopted by the British colonial administration in India toward the Indian National Congress (INC) from its inception in 1885 through the Moderates and Extremists phases up to 1917, shifting from initial benign tolerance to systematic hostility and repression.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Prelims (chronology of British Viceroyalties, repressive acts, safety valve theory) and Mains GS-1 (evolution of Indian nationalism, colonial counter-strategies, divide and rule policies).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
