The Moderate Congress 1885 1905

The Moderate phase represents the initial period of the Indian National Congress (INC) characterized by a belief in British justice, constitutional agitation, and the '3P' method (Prayer, Petition, Protest).

This topic is foundational for understanding the evolution of Indian nationalism, the ideological divide between Moderates and Extremists, and the economic critique of colonialism.
