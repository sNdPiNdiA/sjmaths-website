Hindustan Republican Association

A revolutionary underground organization established in October 1924 in Kanpur by prominent freedom fighters like Sachindra Nath Sanyal, Ram Prasad Bismil, and Jogesh Chandra Chatterjee to overthrow British colonial rule through armed struggle and establish a Federal Republic of the United States of India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS Paper I), tracing the evolution of militant nationalism, socialist ideologies in the freedom struggle, and the transition from individual terrorism to organized mass-mobilization revolutionary movements.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, ideology, and organizational structure of the Hindustan Republican Association.
  • Evaluate the ideological shift from spiritual-nationalism to socialist-republicanism within Indian revolutionary history.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to accurately trace key figures, trials, and organizational evolution for Prelims and Mains.
