Reasons Of Muslim League Pact With Congress

The Lucknow Pact (1916) was a landmark agreement between the Indian National Congress (INC) and the All-India Muslim League (AIML) to present a united front to the British government, characterized by the Congress's acceptance of separate electorates for Muslims.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the evolution of communal politics, the shift in Congress policy towards minority representation, and the temporary phase of Hindu-Muslim unity during the Home Rule Movement era.
