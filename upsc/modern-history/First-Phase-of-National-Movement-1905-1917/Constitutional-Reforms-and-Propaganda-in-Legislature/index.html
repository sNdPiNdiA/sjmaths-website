Constitutional Reforms And Propaganda In Legislature

The systematic campaign by early nationalists (Moderates) demanding expansion and reform of legislative councils (1905–1917), using the legislature as a platform for political education, exposure of colonial misrule, and economic critique.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1), covering the Moderates' political methods, the evolution of constitutional reforms (Morley-Minto Reforms 1909), and the transformation of legislative chambers into sounding boards for Indian public opinion.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts of Moderate legislative politics and the demand for self-government.
  • Evaluate the provisions, limitations, and communal implications of the Indian Councils Act of 1909 (Morley-Minto Reforms).
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to retain legislative reforms and nationalist responses for Prelims and Mains.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Rise of Indian Nationalism
  • Moderate Phase of INC (1885-1905)
  • Economic Critique of British Rule (Drain Theory)
