Early Phase Indian National Congress

The formative period of the Indian National Congress (1885–1905), characterized by the leadership of Moderate nationalists who relied on constitutional methods, prayer, petition, and protest.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (chronology, founders, early sessions, economic critique) and Mains (GS Paper 1: Modern Indian History - Freedom Struggle, contributions and limitations of Moderates).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, objectives, and methods of the early Indian National Congress.
  • Evaluate the Moderate ideology, economic critique of British rule, and constitutional demands.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on early Congress leaders and sessions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Rise of Nationalism in 19th Century India
  • Socio-Religious Reform Movements
  • Pre-Congress Political Associations
