Annulment Of Partition Of Bengal

The official revocation of the 1905 Partition of Bengal announced by King George V at the Delhi Durbar in December 1911, which reunited the Bengali-speaking regions while creating new administrative provinces.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Modern Indian History (GS-1) as it marks a major tactical retreat by the British Raj under intense nationalist pressure, the shift of the imperial capital to Delhi, and the emergence of distinct communal alienation among certain Muslim elites.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, geopolitical drivers, and mechanisms of the Annulment of Partition of Bengal.
  • Evaluate spatial reorganization, administrative redistribution of provinces, and nationalist impacts.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding the 1911 Delhi Durbar and aftermath.
