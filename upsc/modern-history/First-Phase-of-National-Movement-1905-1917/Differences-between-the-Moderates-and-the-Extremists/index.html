Differences Between The Moderates And The Extremists

The ideological and tactical divergence within the Indian National Congress (INC) between 1905 and 1917, characterized by Moderates who favored constitutional agitation, petitions, and faith in British justice, versus Extremists who advocated radical methods, mass mobilization, swadeshi, and absolute self-government (Swaraj).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is a permanent fixture in UPSC CSE Prelims (chronology, leaders, association with events like the Surat Split) and GS Paper 1 Mains (evolution of nationalist ideology, nature of freedom struggle, critique of early methods).
