Lucknow Session Of INC 1916 Lucknow Pact

The 1916 Lucknow Session of the Indian National Congress (INC) marked the historic reunion of the Moderates and Extremists after the Surat Split of 1907, and witnessed the formalizing of the Lucknow Pact between the INC and the All-India Muslim League.
