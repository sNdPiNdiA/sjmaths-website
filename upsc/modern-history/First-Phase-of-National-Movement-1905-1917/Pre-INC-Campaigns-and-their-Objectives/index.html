Pre INC Campaigns And Their Objectives

Organized political activities and associations formed between 1820s and 1885 that sought to represent Indian interests to the British colonial administration through petitions, press, and constitutional agitation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These associations represent the 'seed phase' of Indian nationalism, testing the evolution from regional/class-based grievances to national political consciousness.
