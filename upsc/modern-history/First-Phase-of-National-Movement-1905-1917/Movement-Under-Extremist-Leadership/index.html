Movement Under Extremist Leadership

A radical phase of the Indian National Movement (1905-1917) characterized by the shift from 'mendicancy' (petitions/prayers) to 'Swaraj' (self-rule) through mass mobilization, boycott, and swadeshi.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of nationalist ideology, the Surat Split, and the transition from moderate constitutionalism to militant mass-based agitation.
