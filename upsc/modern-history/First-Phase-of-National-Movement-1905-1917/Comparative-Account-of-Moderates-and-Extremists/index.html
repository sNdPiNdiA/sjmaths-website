Comparative Account Of Moderates And Extremists

A systematic ideological and methodological divergence within the Indian National Congress (INC) from 1905 to 1917, contrasting the early nationalists who relied on constitutional agitation with the militant nationalists who demanded immediate self-government (Swaraj) through direct mass action.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC GS Paper I (Modern Indian History - Indian National Movement - phases, ideologies, Surat Split of 1907) and GS Paper II (evolution of political thought, constitutionalism vs direct action). Frequently tested in both Prelims (statement-based questions on leaders, methods, and sessions) and Mains (analytical evaluation of their contributions and limitations).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the foundational ideological, methodological, and social base differences between Moderates and Extremists.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions of early nationalism.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding leaders, sessions, and trajectories.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Foundation of the Indian National Congress (1885)
  • Safety Valve Theory
  • Curzonian policies and Partition of Bengal (1905)
