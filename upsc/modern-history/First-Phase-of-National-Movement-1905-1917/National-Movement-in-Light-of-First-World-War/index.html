National Movement In Light Of First World War

The period (1914-1918) marked a paradigm shift in the Indian National Movement, characterized by the transition from moderate constitutional agitation to mass mobilization, fueled by wartime economic distress and global political shifts.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This period serves as the bridge between the 'Moderate-Extremist' era and the 'Gandhian' era, frequently tested for its impact on revolutionary activities, constitutional reforms, and the rise of mass nationalism.
