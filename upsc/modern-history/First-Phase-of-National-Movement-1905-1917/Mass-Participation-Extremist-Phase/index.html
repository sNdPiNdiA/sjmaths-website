Mass Participation Extremist Phase

The phase of the Indian National Movement roughly spanning 1905 to 1917, characterized by the transition from elite petitioning politics to militant nationalism, widespread mass mobilization, and the adoption of aggressive methods like Swadeshi, Boycott, and National Education.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the Indian freedom struggle from moderate liberalism to militant mass action, testing socio-economic impacts of Swadeshi, ideological shifts (Extremists vs. Moderates), and the emergence of revolutionary terrorism.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
