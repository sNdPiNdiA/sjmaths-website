Debate Over INC Being A Safety Valve

The historical and historiographical debate surrounding the foundation of the Indian National Congress (INC) in 1885, questioning whether it was engineered primarily as a 'safety valve' by British officials to defuse growing indigenous discontent or emerged organically from Indian national awakening.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (Modern Indian History chronology and historical interpretations) and Mains GS-1 (Freedom Struggle - its various stages and crucial contributors/controversies).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the core arguments of the Safety Valve Theory and its proponents.
  • Understand the counter-arguments, specifically the Conspiracy Theory and Lightning Conductor Theory.
  • Evaluate the nationalist interpretation championed by early nationalists and Marxist historians.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Foundational understanding of 19th-century socio-religious reform movements
  • Rise of regional political associations prior to 1885
