Montague Statement Of August 1917

A historic pronouncement made by Edwin Samuel Montagu, the Secretary of State for India, on August 20, 1917, in the British House of Commons, promising the gradual introduction of responsible government in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition of British imperial policy from autocracy to progressive constitutional devolution, laying the groundwork for the Government of India Act 1919 (Montagu-Chelmsford Reforms), and altering the nature of the Indian National Movement.
