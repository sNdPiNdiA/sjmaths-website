Limitations With Home Rule Leagues

The inherent organizational, ideological, and geographical constraints of the Home Rule Movement (1916–1918) that restricted its mass reach and prevented it from transforming into a sustained, pan-India revolutionary campaign.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolutionary trajectory of the Indian National Movement between the Surat Split and the emergence of Gandhian mass politics, frequently tested in both Prelims statements and Mains analytical evaluations.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and operational mechanisms of Home Rule League limitations.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution bottlenecks, socio-political exclusions, and leadership contradictions.
  • Apply high-yield analytical frameworks to distinguish Home Rule limitations from later Gandhian movements.
