Swadeshi Movement And Associated Leaders

The Swadeshi Movement (1905-1908) was the first major mass movement in modern India, triggered by the Partition of Bengal, aimed at economic self-reliance (Swadeshi) and political autonomy (Swaraj) through the boycott of British goods.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition from 'Moderate' to 'Extremist' politics, introduces mass mobilization techniques, and serves as a precursor to the Gandhian era of non-cooperation.
