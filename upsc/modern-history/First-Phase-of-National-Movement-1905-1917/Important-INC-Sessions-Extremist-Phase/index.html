Important INC Sessions Extremist Phase

The Indian National Congress sessions held during the Extremist Phase (1905–1917) marked a profound ideological shift from Moderate constitutional petitioning to Assertive Nationalism, witnessing intense intra-party friction, the historic Surat Split, and the eventual reunification at Lucknow.
