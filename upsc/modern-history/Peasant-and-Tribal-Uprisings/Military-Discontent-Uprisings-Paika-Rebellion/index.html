Military Discontent Uprisings Paika Rebellion

The Paika Rebellion (1817) was a militant uprising of the traditional landed militia (Paikas) of Odisha against the British East India Company's oppressive land revenue policies and administrative displacement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical topic in Modern Indian History, often cited as a precursor to the 1857 revolt, testing the candidate's understanding of socio-economic grievances and colonial administrative impact on traditional military classes.
