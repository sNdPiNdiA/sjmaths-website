Tribal Movements Tana Bhagat Movement

The Tana Bhagat Movement was a socio-religious and political tribal uprising (1914) led by the Oraon tribe in the Chota Nagpur region, characterized by a synthesis of Sanskritization, monotheism, and anti-colonial nationalism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the transition of tribal movements from localized agrarian resistance to integration with the mainstream Indian National Movement (INC).
