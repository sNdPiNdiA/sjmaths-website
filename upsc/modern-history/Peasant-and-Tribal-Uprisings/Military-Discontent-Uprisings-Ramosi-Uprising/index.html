Military Discontent Uprisings Ramosi Uprising

The Ramosi Uprising was a series of agrarian-military revolts in the Western Ghats (Maharashtra) during the 19th century, triggered by the displacement of the Ramosis from their traditional role as hill police and the subsequent economic distress caused by British land revenue policies.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the transition of traditional martial classes into rebels due to colonial administrative restructuring. It highlights the intersection of tribal identity, economic dispossession, and anti-colonial resistance.
