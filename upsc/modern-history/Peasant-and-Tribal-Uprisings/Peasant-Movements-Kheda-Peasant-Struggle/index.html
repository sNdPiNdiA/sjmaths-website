Peasant Movements Kheda Peasant Struggle

The Kheda Satyagraha (1918) was a non-violent agrarian protest led by Mahatma Gandhi and Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel against the British colonial government's insistence on full revenue collection despite crop failure in the Kheda district of Gujarat.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the first major 'Peasant Satyagraha' in India, demonstrating the efficacy of Gandhian methods in rural mobilization and the rise of Sardar Patel as a national leader.
