Tribal Movements Chuar Uprising

The Chuar Uprising was a series of agrarian-tribal revolts (1766–1816) led by the Bhumij and other tribal groups against the British East India Company's oppressive land revenue policies and the displacement of traditional tribal landholders in the Jungle Mahals region.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the earliest phase of tribal resistance against colonial land revenue systems (Permanent Settlement) and the erosion of traditional tribal autonomy, frequently appearing in Prelims regarding chronology and causes.
