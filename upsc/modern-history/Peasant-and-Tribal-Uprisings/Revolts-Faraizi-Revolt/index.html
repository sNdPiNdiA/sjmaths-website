Revolts Faraizi Revolt

The Faraizi movement was a 19th-century socio-religious reform movement turned agrarian revolt, initiated by Haji Shariatullah in Eastern Bengal, aimed at purging Islamic practices of un-Islamic accretions and resisting the exploitation of peasants by British indigo planters and zamindars.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the transition of religious reform movements into militant peasant resistance, a recurring theme in UPSC Modern History questions regarding the nature of 19th-century anti-colonial struggles.
