Peasant Movements Tebhaga Movement

A militant sharecroppers' movement in Bengal (1946-47) demanding the reduction of the landlord's share of the produce from one-half to one-third (Tebhaga).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the transition from Gandhian non-violence to radical, organized peasant mobilization, highlighting the role of the Communist Party and the Kisan Sabha in the pre-independence era.
