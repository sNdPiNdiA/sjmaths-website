Tribal Movements Singphos Rebellion

The Singphos Rebellion refers to the series of armed uprisings by the Singpho tribe of Assam and Arunachal Pradesh against British colonial expansion and administrative encroachment in the early 19th century.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Resistance to British Rule' module in Modern Indian History, specifically the tribal resistance in the North-East frontier region.
