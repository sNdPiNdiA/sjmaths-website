Tribal Movements Bhil Uprising

The Bhil Uprisings were a series of armed revolts by the Bhil tribe against the British East India Company and local princely states, primarily triggered by colonial interference in their traditional socio-economic structures and forest rights.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tribal movements are a staple in Modern Indian History (GS-I). UPSC frequently tests the causes, leadership, and the distinction between Bhil revolts and other contemporary tribal uprisings like the Santhal or Munda rebellions.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
