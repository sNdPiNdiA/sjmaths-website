Peasant Movements Champaran Satyagraha

The 1917 Champaran Satyagraha was the first civil disobedience movement in India led by Mahatma Gandhi, aimed at ending the exploitative 'Tinkathia' system imposed by European indigo planters on the peasantry of Bihar.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition of the Indian National Movement from elite-led constitutional agitation to mass-based Gandhian struggle, frequently tested for its methodology and socio-economic impact.
