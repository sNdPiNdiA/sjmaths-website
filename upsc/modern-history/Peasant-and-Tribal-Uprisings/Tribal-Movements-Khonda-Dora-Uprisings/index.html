Tribal Movements Khonda Dora Uprisings

The Khonda Dora uprisings were localized tribal revolts in the Visakhapatnam Agency area of Andhra Pradesh, primarily directed against the exploitation by 'Muttadars' (local landlords) and the restrictive forest policies of the British colonial administration.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests regional tribal movements to assess the candidate's understanding of colonial land revenue systems, tribal agency, and the socio-economic impact of British administrative integration in the Madras Presidency.
