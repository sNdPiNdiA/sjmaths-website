Tribal Movements Santhal Rebellion

The Santhal Rebellion (Hul) of 1855-56 was a massive armed uprising by the Santhal community against the oppressive colonial administration, zamindars, and moneylenders (dikus) in the Damin-i-koh region.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a seminal event in Indian history, representing the transition from localized tribal resistance to organized anti-colonial struggle, frequently tested for its socio-economic causes and administrative aftermath.
