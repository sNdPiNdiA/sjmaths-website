Tribal Movements Jaintia And Garo Rebellion

The Jaintia and Garo rebellions were 19th-century anti-colonial tribal uprisings in the North-East Frontier region, primarily triggered by British administrative encroachment, taxation, and interference in traditional socio-political autonomy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These movements are critical for understanding the 'Peripheral Resistance' against British expansionism, the transition from traditional tribal governance to colonial administration, and the socio-economic impact of the House Tax and Income Tax in the North-East.
