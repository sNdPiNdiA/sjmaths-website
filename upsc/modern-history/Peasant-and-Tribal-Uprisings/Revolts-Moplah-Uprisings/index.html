Revolts Moplah Uprisings

A series of agrarian revolts by the Moplah (Mapilla) Muslim peasantry in the Malabar region of Kerala against the oppressive Hindu Jenmi (landlord) class and the British colonial administration, peaking in 1921.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the intersection of agrarian distress, anti-colonial struggle, and the complex communal dynamics during the Khilafat-Non-Cooperation Movement era.
