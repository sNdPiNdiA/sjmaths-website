Revolts Kuka Movement

The Kuka Movement (Namdhari Movement) was a socio-religious reform movement in mid-19th century Punjab that evolved into a militant anti-British political struggle, emphasizing self-rule, swadeshi, and non-cooperation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical precursor to the Gandhian Non-Cooperation Movement and represents the intersection of religious revivalism and anti-colonial nationalism in North-West India.
