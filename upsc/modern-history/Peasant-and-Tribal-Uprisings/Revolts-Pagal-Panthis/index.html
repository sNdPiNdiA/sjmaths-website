Revolts Pagal Panthis

A semi-religious, peasant-led agrarian uprising in the Mymensingh district of Bengal (1825–1835) against the oppressive taxation and exploitation by the British colonial administration and local zamindars.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from religious-reformist movements to militant peasant resistance in early 19th-century colonial India.
