Other Important Commissions

Ad-hoc or statutory bodies constituted by the British colonial administration to investigate, report, or reform specific socio-economic, political, or administrative issues in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

These commissions are frequently tested in Prelims (matching commissions with their objectives/years) and provide critical context for Mains answers on colonial administrative evolution.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Other Important Commissions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Chronology of British Rule
  • Evolution of Constitutional Acts
  • Socio-Religious Reform Movements
