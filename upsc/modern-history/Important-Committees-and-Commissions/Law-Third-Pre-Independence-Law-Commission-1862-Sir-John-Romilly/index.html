Law Third Pre Independence Law Commission 1862 Sir John Romilly

The Third Law Commission of India, established in 1862 under the chairmanship of Sir John Romilly, was a British-appointed body tasked with codifying substantive law for India, primarily focusing on the law of succession and contract.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of the Indian Legal System, the process of codification during the British Raj, and the historical roots of modern Indian civil laws.
