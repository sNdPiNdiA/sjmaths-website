Famine Lyall Commission

The Lyall Commission (1898) was a British-appointed commission tasked with investigating the causes and management of the severe Indian famine of 1896-97, following the recommendations of the earlier Strachey Commission.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the evolution of British famine policy in India. The Lyall Commission represents the transition from 'laissez-faire' relief to more structured, albeit colonial-centric, administrative intervention.
