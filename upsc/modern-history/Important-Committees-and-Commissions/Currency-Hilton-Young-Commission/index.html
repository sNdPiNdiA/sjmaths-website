Currency Hilton Young Commission

The Royal Commission on Indian Currency and Finance (1926), chaired by Edward Hilton Young, was a pivotal body tasked with investigating the Indian currency system and recommending the establishment of a central bank to stabilize the economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is the foundational bedrock for the evolution of India's monetary policy, the establishment of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI), and the transition from the Gold Exchange Standard to a managed currency system.
