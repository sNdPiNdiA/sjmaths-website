Famine MacDonnell Commission

The MacDonnell Commission (1900-1901) was a high-level committee appointed by the British Government under Lord Curzon to investigate the causes of the devastating 1899-1900 famine and to formulate a comprehensive famine relief policy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents the culmination of British famine policy in India, marking a shift from reactive relief to a more structured, preventive administrative framework, frequently appearing in Modern History questions regarding colonial economic exploitation and administrative evolution.
