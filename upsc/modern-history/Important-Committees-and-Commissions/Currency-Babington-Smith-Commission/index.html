Currency Babington Smith Commission

The Babington Smith Committee (1919) was a British-appointed commission tasked with recommending the future of Indian currency and exchange rates in the post-WWI economic landscape.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical component of the 'Economic History of India' under British rule, specifically regarding the evolution of the Gold Exchange Standard and the impact of colonial monetary policy on Indian inflation and trade.
