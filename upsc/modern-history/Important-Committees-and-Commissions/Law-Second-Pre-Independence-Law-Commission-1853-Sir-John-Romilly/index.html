Law Second Pre Independence Law Commission 1853 Sir John Romilly

The Second Law Commission of India was established under the Charter Act of 1853 to examine the recommendations of the First Law Commission and to suggest reforms in the judicial administration and legal procedures of British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It represents a critical phase in the codification of Indian law, bridging the gap between the Macaulay-led First Commission and the eventual enactment of major codes like the IPC and CrPC.
