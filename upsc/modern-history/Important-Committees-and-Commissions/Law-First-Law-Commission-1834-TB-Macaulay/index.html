Law First Law Commission 1834 TB Macaulay

The First Law Commission of India was established under the Charter Act of 1833 to codify Indian laws, ensuring uniformity and simplicity in the legal system under the chairmanship of Thomas Babington Macaulay.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the genesis of the codification of Indian laws, transitioning from personal/customary laws to a unified British-Indian legal framework, frequently tested in the context of constitutional history and administrative evolution.
