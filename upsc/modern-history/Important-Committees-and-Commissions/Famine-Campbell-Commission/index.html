Famine Campbell Commission

The Campbell Commission (1866) was the first-ever famine commission appointed by the British Government in India, specifically tasked to investigate the causes and suggest relief measures following the devastating Orissa Famine of 1866.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the shift from 'laissez-faire' indifference to the formalization of 'Famine Codes' and state responsibility in disaster management, a recurring theme in Modern Indian History.
