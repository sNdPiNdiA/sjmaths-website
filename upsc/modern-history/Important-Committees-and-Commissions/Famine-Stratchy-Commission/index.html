Famine Strachey Commission (1880)

The first Famine Commission appointed by the British Government in 1880 under the chairmanship of Sir Richard Strachey to formulate a systematic policy for famine prevention and relief in British India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks the transition from 'ad-hoc' relief measures to a 'codified' administrative framework, reflecting the colonial state's evolving approach to agrarian distress and state responsibility.
