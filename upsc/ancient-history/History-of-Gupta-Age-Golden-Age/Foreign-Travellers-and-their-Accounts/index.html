Foreign Accounts: Gupta Age

Comprehensive analysis of Chinese travelogues, specifically Faxian, providing a contemporary lens into the socio-economic and religious fabric of the Gupta Empire.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Centralized around Magadha and Pataliputra.
  • Description of the Ganges valley civilization.

Personalities

  • Faxian: Buddhist monk and explorer.
  • Chandragupta II: The ruling emperor.

Mindmap: Key Observations

  • Socio-Economic State
    • 'Chandalas' existence outside city gates (untouchability).
    • Mild administrative justice system.
    • General prosperity and use of Cowrie shells for trade.
  • Religious Landscape
    • Flourishing of Mahayana and Hinayana Buddhism.
    • Presence of free hospitals and charity inns.
