Kumaragupta I: The Golden Zenith

A comprehensive study of Kumaragupta I, the longest-reigning Gupta monarch, focusing on administrative stability and the founding of Nalanda University.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Empire stretched from Saurashtra to Bengal.
  • Controlled the Narmada valley region.

Key Titles

  • Mahendraditya
  • Shakraditya
  • Ashwamedhapara

Mindmap: The Reign of Kumaragupta I

  • Religious Policy
    • Devotee of Lord Kartikeya (Skanda).
    • Introduced Peacock-type coins depicting the deity.
  • Cultural & Educational
    • Founder of Nalanda University (Mahavihara).
    • Promotion of Sanskrit literature.
  • Military Defense
    • Repelled the Pushyamitra attack toward the end of his reign.
    • Succession struggle mentioned in the Bhitari Stone Pillar inscription.
