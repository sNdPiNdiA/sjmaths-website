Samudragupta: The Napoleon of India

The greatest ruler of the Gupta dynasty, known for his relentless military conquests and multifaceted personality as a warrior-poet.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Core: Gangetic Plain
  • Dakshinapatha: Reached Kanchipuram
  • Frontier: Assam, Bengal, Nepal

Personalities

  • Harisena: Court Poet
  • Chandragupta I: Predecessor
  • Chandragupta II: Successor
  • Military Strategy
    • Aryavarta: Annexation (Direct control)
    • Dakshinapatha: Tributary (Indirect control)
    • Atavika Rajyas: Forest kingdoms subdued
  • Cultural Patronage
    • Kaviraja: Title for musical skill
    • Coinage: Veena-player type coins
    • Religious: Devotee of Vishnu but allowed Buddhist stupas
