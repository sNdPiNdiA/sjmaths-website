The Mauryan Economic Engine

An analysis of the state-controlled economy, agrarian expansion, and trade networks that sustained the Mauryan Empire.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Control over Magadhan iron mines.
  • Control of trade routes (Uttarapatha).

Officials

  • Samaharta: Revenue Collector.
  • Sannidhata: Treasurer.
  • Adhyakshas: Superintendents of trade/industry.

Economic Structure Mindmap

  • Agrarian Economy
    • Land ownership: State control (Sita).
    • Irrigation: Sudarshana Lake (Pushyagupta).
  • Trade & Commerce
    • Punch-marked coins (Pana).
    • Standardization of weights and measures.
