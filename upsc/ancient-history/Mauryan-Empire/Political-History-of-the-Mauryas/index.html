Mauryan Political History: Foundation to Decline

An analytical overview of the first pan-Indian empire that established the template for ancient Indian statecraft and administration.

1. Core Study Notes

Core Geography

  • Pataliputra: The nerve center.
  • Taxila & Ujjain: Provincial seats of power.
  • Kalinga: Strategic coastal expansion.

Personalities

  • Chandragupta: The Architect.
  • Chanakya: The Strategist.
  • Ashoka: The Philosopher-King.

Mauryan Power Structure

  • Centralization: Strong bureaucracy with the King as the supreme authority.
  • Provincial Administration: Divided into 5 provinces (Pradesh) governed by Kumara/Aryaputra.
  • Municipal governance: Detailed by Megasthenes in Indica.
