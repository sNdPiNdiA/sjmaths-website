Mauryan Polity: Centralized Autocracy

An exploration of the highly centralized bureaucratic machinery and administrative innovations that established the first pan-Indian empire.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

1. Core Study Notes

Core Structure

  • Centralized Autocracy
  • Divisions: Pataliputra, Taxila, Ujjain, Tosali, Suvarnagiri

Key Officials

  • Tirthas (18 high-ranking officials)
  • Adhyakshas (Superintendents)
  • Rajukas (Rural officials)
  • Administrative Tiers:
    • Central: King assisted by Mantri Parishad.
    • Provincial: Governed by Kumara/Aryaputra (Princes).
    • District: Governed by Rajukas.
    • Village: Governed by Gramika.
  • Revenue System:
    • Bhaga: 1/6th share of produce.
    • Bali: Religious tax/tribute.

मुख्य संरचना

प्रमुख अधिकारी

  • तीर्थ (18 उच्च पदस्थ अधिकारी)
  • अध्यक्ष (विभागों के प्रमुख)
  • राजुक (ग्रामीण अधिकारी)
  • प्रशासनिक स्तर:
    • केंद्रीय: राजा और मंत्री परिषद।

    • जिला: राजुकों द्वारा शासित।
    • ग्राम: ग्रामिक द्वारा शासित।
  • राजस्व प्रणाली:
    • भाग: उपज का 1/6 हिस्सा।
