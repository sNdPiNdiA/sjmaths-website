Mauryan Imperial Administrative Structure

An analytical study of the highly centralized bureaucratic machinery established by Chandragupta Maurya and refined by Ashoka.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

Master the key concepts and dimensions of this topic.

1. Core Study Notes

Centralized Core

  • King as the fountainhead of justice.
  • Mantri-Parishad (Council of Ministers).
  • Secretariat (Adhyakshas).

Grassroots

  • Sthanika (Regional).
  • Gopa (Village headman/accountant).
  • Gramika (Village official).

Administrative Hierarchy (Mindmap)

  • Central Administration:
    • Tirthas (18 Departments).
    • Adhyakshas (Superintendents of various economic departments).
  • Provincial Administration:
    • Kumaras/Aryaputras (Royal Princes as Governors).
    • Provinces divided into Vishayas.
  • Municipal Administration:
    • 6 Committees of 5 members each (as noted by Megasthenes).

प्रशासनिक पदानुक्रम (माइंडमैप)

  • केंद्रीय प्रशासन:
    • तीर्थ (18 विभाग)।
    • अध्यक्ष (विभिन्न आर्थिक विभागों के अधीक्षक)।

    • कुमार/आर्यपुत्र (गवर्नर के रूप में शाही राजकुमार)।
    • प्रांतों का विषयों में विभाजन।

    • 5 सदस्यों वाली 6 समितियां (मेगस्थनीज के अनुसार)।
