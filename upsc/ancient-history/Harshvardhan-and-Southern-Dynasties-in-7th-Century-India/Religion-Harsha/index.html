Religious Evolution under Harshavardhana

An analysis of King Harsha's religious transition from Shaivism to Mahayana Buddhism and his role as a patron of religious pluralism in 7th-century India.

Study Notes

1. Core Study Notes

Core Beliefs

  • Shaivism & Sun Worship (Early)
  • Mahayana Buddhism (Late)
  • Royal Religious Tolerance

Key Figures

  • Hiuen Tsang (Xuanzang)
  • Rajyashri (Buddhist Influence)
  • Divakaramitra (Monk)

Religious Transformation Mindmap

  • Early Religious Inclination
    • Devotion to Shiva and Surya (Solar worship).
    • Maintenance of Hindu Brahmanical traditions.
  • The Buddhist Pivot
    • Influence of his sister Rajyashri.
    • Deep interactions with the Chinese pilgrim Hiuen Tsang.
    • Shift towards the Mahayana school of Buddhism.
  • Religious Assemblies
    • Kannauj Assembly (643 CE): Purpose was to popularize Mahayana tenets; Hiuen Tsang presided over the debate.
    • Prayag Assembly: Held every 5 years; focused on Daan (charity) to monks, Brahmins, and the poor.
