1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Harsha: Thanesar/Kannauj (North)
  • Chalukyas: Badami/Vatapi (Deccan)
  • Pallavas: Kanchipuram (South)

Personalities

  • Harshavardhana
  • Pulakeshin II
  • Mahendravarman I

Administrative Hierarchies

  • Central Level: King as divine authority, Council of Ministers (Mantri-parishad).
  • Provincial Level: Bhuktis (Harsha) / Rashtras (Chalukyas) / Vishayas (Districts).
  • Village Level: Sabha/Ur (Pallavas) acting as autonomous bodies.
