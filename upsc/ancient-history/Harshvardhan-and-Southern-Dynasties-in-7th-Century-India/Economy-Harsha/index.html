Economy of Harsha's Empire

An analytical study of the agrarian, mercantile, and fiscal structures during the 7th-century reign of Emperor Harshavardhana.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Kannauj as the nodal hub
  • Gangetic plain as the agrarian base
  • Ports like Tamralipti losing importance

Personalities

  • Harshavardhana: Central Authority
  • Hiuen Tsang: Primary Chronicler
  • Samantas: Rising Feudal Landlords

Economic Mindmap

  • Agrarian Economy
    • Land grants (Agrahara) increased.
    • Shift towards self-sufficient village units.
    • Dominance of the landlord-tenant relationship.
  • Trade and Commerce
    • Decline of long-distance trade.
    • Local markets (Mandis) flourished.
    • Limited use of metallic currency (debasement).
  • Fiscal Policy
    • State revenue divided into four parts: Admin, Charity, Scholarship, and Religious grants.
    • Taxation: 1/6th of produce as the standard land tax.
