1. Core Study Notes

Core Regions

  • Thanesar (Core)
  • Kannauj (Capital)
  • Gangetic Plains

Officials

  • Mahasandhivigrahaka
  • Akshapatalika
  • Baladhikrita

Administrative Hierarchy

  • Central Government: King as the absolute head, assisted by a council of ministers (Mantriparishad).
  • Provincial Administration: Divided into Bhuktis (provinces) governed by Uparikas.
  • District Administration: Divided into Vishayas, governed by Vishayapatis.
  • Local/Village: Grama, headed by Gramika, assisted by elders.
