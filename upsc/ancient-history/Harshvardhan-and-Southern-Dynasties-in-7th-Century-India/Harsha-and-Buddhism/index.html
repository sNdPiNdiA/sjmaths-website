Harsha: Patronage and Buddhist Evolution

An analysis of Emperor Harshavardhana's transition from Shaivism to Buddhism and its socio-religious impact in 7th-century India.

Geography

  • Centered in Kannauj
  • Control over Gangetic plains
  • Influence extended to Malwa/Orissa

Personalities

  • Harshavardhana
  • Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang)
  • Divakaramitra

Mindmap: Harsha and Buddhism

  • Religious Evolution
    • Early phase: Worshipper of Shiva and Surya
    • Later phase: Deep leaning toward Mahayana Buddhism
    • Influencers: Sister Rajyashri and Monk Xuanzang
  • Institutional Support
    • Kannauj Assembly: Mahayana exposition
    • Prayag Assembly: Quintennial 'Moksha Parishad'
    • Monastery building: Construction at Nalanda
