1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Pallavas: Tondaimandalam (Coromandel Coast).
  • Chalukyas: Karnataka plateau and Western Deccan.

Personalities

  • Pallavas: Mahendravarman I, Narasimhavarman I.
  • Chalukyas: Pulakeshin II, Vikramaditya I.

Administrative Hierarchy

  • Pallava System:
    • Mandalam (Province) overseen by Rajakumara.
    • Kottams (Districts) managing local groups.
    • Nadus (Village clusters) governed by Nattar.
    • Sabha/Ur: Autonomous village assemblies (Brahmadeya vs. non-Brahmadeya).
  • Chalukyan System:
    • Vishayas/Rashtras: Provinces under Vishayapati.
    • Bhoga: Smaller territorial units.
    • Village level: Governed by Gramabhojaka.
