Society & Culture: Pallavas & Chalukyas

An analytical study of the cultural synthesis, religious transitions, and social structure of the Deccan and Southern kingdoms in the 7th Century.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Pallavas: Kanchi-centric, Coromandel coast.
  • Chalukyas: Vatapi (Badami), Krishna-Tungabhadra doab.

Personalities

  • Mahendravarman I: Poet, Dramatist (Mattavilasa Prahasana).
  • Ravikirti: Court poet of Pulakeshin II (Aihole Inscription).

Cultural Synthesis Mindmap

  • Social Stratification
    • Emergence of Brahmana-dominated land grants (Brahmadeya).
    • The Bhakti Movement: Alvars (Vaishnava) & Nayanars (Shaiva).
  • Religious Milieu
    • Decline of Buddhism.
    • Growth of Jainism in the Chalukyan heartland.
    • Rise of Vedic Brahmanism supported by Royal patronage.
  • Architectural Evolution
    • Pallava: Transition from rock-cut shrines to monolithic Ratha temples at Mahabalipuram.
    • Chalukya: Aihole (Temple of experiments) and Pattadakal (Synthesis of Nagara/Dravida).
