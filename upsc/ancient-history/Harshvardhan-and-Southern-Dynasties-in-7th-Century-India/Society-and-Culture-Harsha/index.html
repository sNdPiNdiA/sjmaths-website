Society and Culture under Harsha

An analytical study of social stratification, religious pluralism, and literary advancements during the reign of Harshavardhana (606-647 CE).

1. Core Study Notes

Social Stratification

  • Four-fold Varna system continued.
  • Shudras were primarily agriculturists.
  • Untouchability gained ground; 'Antyajas' lived outside cities.

Cultural Milieu

  • Sanskrit as the language of the elite.
  • Buddhism and Brahmanism existed in harmony.
  • Kannauj emerged as the intellectual capital.

Mindmap: Harsha's Society

  • Social Structure
    • Brahmins held high status; exemption from taxes.
    • Kshatriyas as rulers; Vaishyas as merchants/traders.
    • Status of Women: Decline in remarriage, Sati presence mentioned.
  • Religious Synthesis
    • Harsha was a 'Paramabhattaraka'.
    • The Prayag Assembly (Moksha Parishad) held every five years.
    • Patronage to Nalanda University.
