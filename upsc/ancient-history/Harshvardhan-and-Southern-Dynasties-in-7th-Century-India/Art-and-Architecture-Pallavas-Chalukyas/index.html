An analytical study of the evolutionary transition from rock-cut shrines to structural temples in Peninsular India.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Pallavas: Kanchipuram, Mahabalipuram
  • Chalukyas: Badami, Aihole, Pattadakal

Personalities

  • Mahendravarman I: Founder of cave style
  • Pulakeshin II: Patron of Badami caves
  • Narasimhavarman I: Mamallapuram sculptor

Architectural Evolution Mindmap

  • Pallava Phase
    • Mahendra Style: Pillar-based cave temples.
    • Mamalla Style: Monolithic Rathas (Five Rathas).
    • Rajasimha Style: Structural temples (Kailasanatha).
  • Chalukyan Phase
    • Badami Caves: Synthesis of Nagara and Dravida elements.
    • Aihole: "Cradle of Temple Architecture" (Lad Khan temple).
    • Pattadakal: UNESCO site, blend of North and South styles.
