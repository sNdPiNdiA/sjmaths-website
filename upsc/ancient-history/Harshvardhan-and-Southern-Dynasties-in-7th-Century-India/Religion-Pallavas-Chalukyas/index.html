Religion in 7th Century India

An analysis of the coexistence of Buddhism, Shaivism, and Vaishnavism under the patronage of Harshavardhana, Pallavas, and Chalukyas.

1. Core Study Notes

Core Centers

  • Kannauj (Harsha)
  • Kanchi (Pallavas)
  • Badami/Aihole (Chalukyas)

Key Figures

  • Hiuen Tsang (Witness)
  • Appar (Shaiva Saint)
  • Ravikirti (Jain Poet)

Religious Dynamics

  • Harshavardhana: Transition from Surya/Shiva worship to Mahayana Buddhism, climaxing in the Prayag Assembly.
  • Pallavas: Shift from Jainism (Mahendravarman) to Nayanar-led Shaivism. Rock-cut temples as propaganda.
  • Chalukyas: Vedic sacrificial culture combined with Puranic temple construction; religious pluralism (Jainism flourished at Aihole).
