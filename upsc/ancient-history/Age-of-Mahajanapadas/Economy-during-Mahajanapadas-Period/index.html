The Second Urbanization: Mahajanapada Economy

An exploration of the socio-economic metamorphosis of the Gangetic plains during the 6th century BCE, marking the rise of urban centers and a money economy.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

Master the key concepts and dimensions of this topic.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Gangetic Plains: Fertile alluvium
  • Iron deposits: Bihar/Jharkhand
  • Uttarapatha & Dakshinapatha connectivity

Economic Pillars

  • Shrenis: Guilds of merchants/artisans
  • Bali: The earliest voluntary/mandatory tax
  • Punch-marked Coins: Silver/Copper

Economic Mindmap

  • Agriculture
    • Iron ploughshare (Phala) utilization
    • Surplus paddy cultivation (Transplantation method)
  • Urbanization
    • Craft centers: Artisans residing in specific areas
    • Cities as nodes of trade and power
  • Trade & Currency
    • Barter to Coinage (Punch-marked coins)
    • Rise of merchant class (Setthi/Gahapati)
TermMeaning
BaliEarly tax collected by Bali-sadhakas
ShreniOrganized guilds of craftsmen
Nishka/SatmanaEarly Vedic currency units
