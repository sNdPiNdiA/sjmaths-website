The Nanda Dynasty: Imperial Architects

Explore the rise of the Nandas, the first 'non-Kshatriya' dynasty of Magadha that unified North India.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Centered in Pataliputra
  • Expanded across the Indo-Gangetic plain
  • Control over fertile mineral-rich regions

Personalities

  • Mahapadma Nanda (Sarvakshatrantaka)
  • Dhana Nanda (The last ruler)
  • Chanakya (The primary adversary)

Empire Structure & Expansion

  • Mahapadma Nanda: Known as 'Ekarat' and 'Sarvakshatrantaka' (destroyer of all Kshatriyas) in Puranas.
  • Military Might: Pliny and Curtius mention 200,000 infantry and 80,000 cavalry.
  • Economic Policy: Introduction of standardized currency (punch-marked coins) and systematic taxation.
