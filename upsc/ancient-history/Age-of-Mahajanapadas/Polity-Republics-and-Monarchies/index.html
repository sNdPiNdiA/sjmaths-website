The Age of Mahajanapadas

An analysis of the transition from tribal polities to territorial states, categorised into monarchies and republics.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Centered in Indo-Gangetic plains
  • Iron-rich soil encouraged expansion
  • Rivers like Ganga, Yamuna aided trade

Polity Types

  • Monarchies: Hereditary, autocratic
  • Republics: Gana-Sanghas, tribal assemblies
FeatureMonarchy (Rajya)Republic (Gana-Sangha)
Source of PowerKing (Divinity/Heritage)Gana (Assembly/Council)
ExamplesMagadha, Kosala, VatsaVajji, Malla, Shakya
TaxationRegular/SystematicIrregular/Levied by consensus
  • Key Characteristics:
    • Rise of the 'Varna' system
    • Standing armies development
    • Administrative bureaucracy: Bali, Bhaga, Sulka
