The Shishunaga Dynasty: Hegemony Consolidation

An analysis of the transition from Haryanka to Shishunaga rule, focusing on the tactical expansion of Magadha and the decisive defeat of the Avanti Kingdom.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

Master the key concepts and dimensions of this topic.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Control over the Gangetic trade route.
  • Strategic buffer at Vaishali.
  • Annexation of the fertile Avanti region.

Personalities

  • Shishunaga: Founder, defeated Pradyota dynasty.
  • Kalashoka: Patron of the 2nd Buddhist Council.
  • Kakasavarna: Alternate name for Kalashoka.
  • Political Consolidation
    • End of the 100-year rivalry with Avanti.
    • Administrative stability replacing Haryanka chaos.
  • Religious Significance
    • Second Buddhist Council (383 BCE).
    • Emergence of Sthaviravada and Mahasamghika schools.
RulerKey Achievement
