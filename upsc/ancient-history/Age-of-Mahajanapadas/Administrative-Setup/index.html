Administrative Evolution: Mahajanapada Era

A study of the transition from tribal polities to territorial states (Janapadas to Mahajanapadas) marked by professional bureaucracy.

Syllabus Core Study Notes (Deep-Dive)

Master the key concepts and dimensions of this topic.

1. Core Study Notes

Geography

  • Centered in Gangetic Plains
  • Strategic use of Iron
  • Control over fertile land

Personalities

  • Rajanya (Ruling class)
  • Bali-sadhakas (Tax officers)
  • Gramini (Village head)

Administrative Hierarchy

  • Central Administration:
    • Monarchy (Rajan/Samrat): Hereditary kingship.
    • Parishad/Sabha: Advisory councils.
  • Provincial/Local:
    • Gramini: Managed rural administration.
    • Bali-sadhaka: Assigned to collect 'Bali'.
