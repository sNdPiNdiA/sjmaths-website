Changed Names Of Some Cities States And Countries

The official alteration of geographical, political, or administrative nomenclature by sovereign states or sub-national entities to reflect decolonization, linguistic reorganization, cultural reclamation, or geopolitical shifts.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 Geography & Polity) via direct matching questions of former versus current names, constitutional procedures under Article 3 for states, and global geopolitical toponymic changes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and constitutional mechanisms of changing names of cities, states, and countries.
  • Evaluate global and domestic spatial distribution of toponymic changes driven by decolonization and linguistic re-articulation.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding name changes.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Indian Polity: Parliament and State Reorganization (Articles 1-4)
  • World Regional Geography: Colonial history and sovereign re-identifications
