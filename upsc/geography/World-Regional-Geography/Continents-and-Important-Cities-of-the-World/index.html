Continents And Important Cities Of The World

Comprehensive study of continental landmasses, their structural geology, major physiographic divisions, and the spatial distribution of strategically, economically, and historically important global cities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (map-based questions on cities near rivers, seas, deserts, straits) and Mains GS-1 (World Geography, urbanization trends, geomorphological features).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
