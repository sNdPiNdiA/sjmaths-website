Ganga River System

The Ganga River System is the largest river basin in India, draining an area of over 8,61,404 sq km across India, Nepal, China, and Bangladesh, originating from the Gangotri Glacier as Bhagirathi and merging into the Bay of Bengal.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims for right-bank vs. left-bank tributaries, confluence points (Prayags), and geopolitical dimensions. In Mains GS Paper I (Geography) and GS Paper III (Environment/Pollution), questions focus on Namami Gange, ecological flows, and interlinking of rivers.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
