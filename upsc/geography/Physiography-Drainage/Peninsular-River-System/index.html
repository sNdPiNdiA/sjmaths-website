Peninsular River System

The Peninsular drainage system is older than the Himalayan system, characterized by broad, shallow, mature valleys, fixed courses with little meandering, and a non-perennial (rain-fed) flow regime draining primarily into the Bay of Bengal and the Arabian Sea.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (map-based locations of tributaries, right/left bank flows, estuary vs delta formations) and Mains GS-1 (inter-linking of rivers, water resource conflicts, physiographic impacts on settlement and irrigation).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of the Peninsular River System.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, east-flowing vs. west-flowing drainage, and institutional frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
