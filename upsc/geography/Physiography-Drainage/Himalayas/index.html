Himalayas

The Himalayas are the world's youngest, highest, and loftiest fold mountain system forming a massive arc stretching over 2,400 km across South Asia, created by the ongoing tectonic collision between the Indian and Eurasian plates.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial core physiographic unit tested in Prelims via longitudinal/latitudinal classifications, pass locations, river origins, and in Mains GS-1 (Geophysical phenomena, geomorphological changes, disaster vulnerability) and GS-3 (Environmental conservation, climate change).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and tectonic mechanisms of Himalayan orogeny.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, regional classifications, and transverse river divisions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
