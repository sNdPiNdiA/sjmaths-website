Lakes

A lake is a body of relatively still water of considerable size, localized in a basin, surrounded by land, and distinct from any river or other outlet that serves to feed or drain the lake. Lakes form via various tectonic, glacial, fluvial, volcanic, and anthropogenic processes.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Geography (Physical Geography, Drainage System, Geomorphology) and Environment (Wetlands, Ramsar Sites, Eutrophication) in UPSC Prelims and GS Paper I/III Mains.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution of major Indian and global lakes, institutional frameworks, and conservation policies.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics and resolve examiner traps for Prelims and Mains questions.
