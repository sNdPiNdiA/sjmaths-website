Himalayan Drainage

The Himalayan drainage system comprises perennial rivers fed by both melting glaciers and monsoon rains, cutting across the young fold mountains through antecedent and epigenetic valleys to form extensive alluvial plains.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for UPSC CSE Prelims (river origins, tributaries, right/left bank arrangements, wildlife sanctuaries along river basins) and Mains GS-1 (Physiography, water resource management, inter-linking of rivers, geo-political implications).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
