Indus River System

The Indus River System is one of the world's major transboundary river basins, originating in the Tibetan Plateau, flowing through India and Pakistan, and draining into the Arabian Sea. It encompasses the mighty Indus River along with its major Himalayan and trans-Himalayan tributaries.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Geography (Drainage System, Physiography, Himalayan rivers vs Peninsular rivers) in Prelims, and International Relations / Geopolitics (Indus Waters Treaty, hydro-diplomacy) in Mains GS-2.
