Indian Coasts And Islands

India's coastline spans approximately 7,516.6 km (including island territories), bordered by the Arabian Sea to the west, the Bay of Bengal to the east, and the Indian Ocean to the south, featuring distinct emerged and submerged coastal landforms alongside two major island groups (Andaman & Nicobar and Lakshadweep).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (map-based questions, channels, passes, coastal landforms, mangrove distribution) and Mains GS-1 (physiography, coastal erosion, vulnerability to climate change, sea-level rise, geopolitics of the Indo-Pacific).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
