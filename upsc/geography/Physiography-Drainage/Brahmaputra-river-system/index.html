Brahmaputra River System

The Brahmaputra is one of Asia's major trans-boundary rivers, originating from the Chemayungdung glacier in the Kailash range, traversing Tibet (Yarlung Tsangpo), entering India through Arunachal Pradesh (Siang/Dihang), flowing through the Assam valley, and merging with the Padma in Bangladesh before emptying into the Bay of Bengal.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly critical for UPSC CSE Prelims (river origins, left/right bank tributaries, geographical alignments, islands) and Mains GS Paper 1 & 3 (Geomorphology, floods, erosion, geopolitics of water, inter-state/international river disputes, and infrastructure development in Northeast India).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master the complete course, basin characteristics, and major left and right bank tributaries of the Brahmaputra system.
  • Analyze the geomorphological challenges including braided channels, annual devastating floods, bank erosion, and Majuli river island dynamics.
  • Evaluate trans-boundary water sharing, geopolitical dimensions with China, and institutional frameworks like the Brahmaputra Board.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • General Physiography of India
  • Himalayan Drainage System versus Peninsular Drainage System
  • Basic Climatology of the Indian Monsoon
