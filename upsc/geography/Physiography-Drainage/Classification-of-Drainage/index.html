Classification Of Drainage

The systematic categorization of river systems and drainage networks based on their origin, geological history, structural control, and spatial patterns of water flow across a landscape.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental core topic for UPSC Prelims (Geography - Indian & World Physical Geography) and Mains GS-1 (Geomorphology, Drainage Systems, and Physiography), frequently tested through conceptual matching and map-based questions.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Classification Of Drainage.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
