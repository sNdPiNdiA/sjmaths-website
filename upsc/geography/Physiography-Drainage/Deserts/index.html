Deserts

A desert is a barren area of landscape where little precipitation occurs and, consequently, living conditions hostile for plant and animal life, defined primarily by an aridity index of less than 0.05.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (geographical distribution of major world deserts, climatic factors like cold ocean currents and rain-shadow effects) and Mains GS-1 (Geomorphology, physical geography features, landform evolution) and GS-3 (Environmental degradation, desertification, and land restoration).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Deserts.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Climatology (Global Pressure Belts, Trade Winds)
  • Geomorphology (Erosional and Depositional Landforms)
  • Oceanography (Ocean Currents)
