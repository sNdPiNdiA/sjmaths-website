Water On The Surface Of The Earth

Water on the surface of the Earth encompasses all liquid and solid water bodies residing above the continental and oceanic crusts, representing a vital dynamic interface in the global hydrological cycle. It includes oceans, seas, lakes, rivers, glaciers, ice caps, and atmospheric moisture components that interact constantly via evaporation, precipitation, and runoff.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Geography (GS-1: Climatology, Oceanography, Geomorphology) and Environment/Economy (GS-3: Water Resource Management, Climate Change). UPSC frequently tests hydrological distribution percentages, cryogenic reserves, salinity gradients, and institutional frameworks like river basin management.
