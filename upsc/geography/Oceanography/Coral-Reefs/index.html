Coral Reefs

Coral reefs are diverse underwater ecosystems built by carbonate structures secreted by colonial marine invertebrates known as polyps, forming symbiotic relationships with microscopic algae called zooxanthellae.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims (geographical distribution, environmental degradation, bleaching, biological interactions) and Mains GS-3 (biodiversity loss, climate change impacts, coastal ecosystem management).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, physiological mechanisms, and ecological requirements of Coral Reefs.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, global coral hotspots, and institutional frameworks in India.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Climatic zones and ocean currents
