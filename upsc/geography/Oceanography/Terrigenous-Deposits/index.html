Terrigenous Deposits

Terrigenous deposits are sediments derived from the land (lithogenous origin) that are transported to the oceans via rivers, winds, glaciers, and coastal erosion, constituting the bulk of continental margin sediments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Geomorphology and Oceanography (marine sediments, pelagic vs. neritic deposits) and in Mains GS-1 regarding ocean topography and resource geography.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Terrigenous Deposits.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, transport mechanisms, and sediment classification.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic geomorphology
  • Submarine relief features
  • Continental shelf and slope mechanics
