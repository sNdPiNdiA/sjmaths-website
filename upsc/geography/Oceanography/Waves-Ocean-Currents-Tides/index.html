Waves Ocean Currents Tides

Oceanic movements encompassing surface waves driven by wind, continuous horizontal and vertical fluid streams known as ocean currents driven by density and atmospheric forces, and periodic vertical sea level variations called tides generated by gravitational forces of the moon and sun.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (mapping currents, tidal ranges, fishing grounds like Grand Banks) and Mains GS Paper 1 (Physical Geography phenomena, global climate regulation, impact on human activities and navigation).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
