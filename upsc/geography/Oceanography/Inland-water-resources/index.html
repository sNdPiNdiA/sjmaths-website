Inland Water Resources

Inland water resources encompass all surface freshwater bodies—including rivers, streams, lakes, ponds, tanks, and man-made reservoirs—along with substantial groundwater aquifers contained within a landmass. They are vital hydrological assets that sustain ecological balance, agriculture, domestic consumption, and industrial activities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic is essential for UPSC CSE Prelims (mapping of river basins, lakes, Ramsar sites, wetlands, inland waterways) and Mains GS-1 (Geographical physical features) as well as GS-3 (Water resource management, conservation, irrigation, and infrastructure development).
