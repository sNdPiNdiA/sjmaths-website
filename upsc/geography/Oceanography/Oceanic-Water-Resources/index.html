Oceanic Water Resources

Oceanic water resources encompass the vast expanse of saline water covering approximately 71% of the Earth's surface, serving as the ultimate reservoir of moisture, thermal energy, biological assets, and mineral wealth essential for the global hydrological cycle and socio-economic sustenance.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (mapping of maritime zones, UNCLOS, mineral wealth, desalination technologies) and Mains GS-1 (physical geography, ocean currents) and GS-3 (blue economy, resource security, marine pollution).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Oceanic Water Resources.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Physical Oceanography fundamentals
