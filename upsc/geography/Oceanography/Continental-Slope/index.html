Continental Slope

The continental slope is the steep descent connecting the outer edge of the continental shelf to the deep ocean floor (abyssal plain), marking the true boundary between the continental crust and oceanic crust.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Geography (GS-1) under Oceanography, covering submarine relief features, plate tectonics, sediment dynamics, and international maritime boundaries under UNCLOS.
