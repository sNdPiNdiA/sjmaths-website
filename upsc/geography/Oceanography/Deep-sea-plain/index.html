Deep Sea Plain

Also known as abyssal plains, deep sea plains are vast, flat, or gently sloping regions of the deep ocean floor lying between depths of 3,000 and 6,000 meters.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding ocean floor morphology, pelagic sedimentation, and mineral resource distribution (e.g., polymetallic nodules). Relevant for Mains GS-1 (Geomorphology & Oceanography) and GS-3 (Deep Ocean Mission, Marine Resources).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Deep Sea Plain formation.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, bathymetric features, and international legal frameworks (ISA).
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
