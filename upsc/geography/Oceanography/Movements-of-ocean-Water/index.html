Movements Of Ocean Water

Movements of ocean water encompass the horizontal and vertical motions of marine masses driven by solar energy, wind, gravity, Coriolis force, and density differences, categorized into waves, tides, and ocean currents.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination (GS Paper I - Geography: Oceanography), testing foundational physical geography, global climate regulation, fishery zones, and geopolitical implications of oceanic routes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Waves, Tides, and Ocean Currents.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, thermohaline circulation, and climatic impacts.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Climatology basics (Pressure belts, Winds, Coriolis force)
  • Basic Earth-Sun geometry and gravitational mechanics
