Biotic Deposits

Biotic deposits (pelagic oozes) are deep-sea marine sediments composed primarily of the skeletal remains and calcareous or siliceous hard parts of microscopic marine organisms (plankton).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Oceanography (marine resources, floor topography, and sedimentology) and connects to climate change, ocean acidification, and paleoceanography in Mains GS-I/III.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Biotic Deposits.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
