Surface Water Resources

Surface water resources refer to the renewable water found on the Earth's surface in rivers, lakes, streams, wetlands, and artificial reservoirs, primarily sustained by precipitation and snowmelt.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper I (Geographical distribution of water resources, rivers) and Paper III (Water resource management, irrigation, inter-state disputes, and environmental impact assessment).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, hydrological cycles, and basin characteristics of India's surface water.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution disparities, river basin potentials, and institutional frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding water allocation and river systems.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Physiography of India
  • Climatology (Monsoon Mechanism)
  • Basic Drainage Systems
