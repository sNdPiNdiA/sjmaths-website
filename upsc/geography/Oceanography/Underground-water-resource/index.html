Underground Water Resource

Underground water resources encompass all water found beneath the land surface in the pore spaces of soils and in the fractures of lithologic formations (groundwater), sustained by hydrological recharge and serving as the largest accessible reservoir of fresh water on Earth.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (geomorphology, hydrogeology, government schemes like Atal Bhujal Yojana, CGWB reports) and Mains GS-1 (water resource geography) & GS-3 (irrigation, agricultural crises, environmental degradation, water security).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
