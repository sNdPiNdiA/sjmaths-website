Conservation Of Water Resources

The systematic management, protection, and sustainable utilization of fresh water resources in surface and subsurface reservoirs to prevent depletion, contamination, and ecological degradation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper I (Geography - water resource distribution, scarcity) and GS Paper III (Environment, Agriculture, Irrigation management, and Disaster Management related to floods/droughts).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Conservation Of Water Resources.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Hydrological cycle fundamentals
  • Basic climatology and monsoon patterns in India
  • Physiography of India
