Techniques Of Water Conservation

Water conservation encompasses all policies, strategies, and engineering techniques to sustainably manage fresh water as a scarce resource, protect the hydrosphere, and meet human and environmental demand. It involves demand management, supply augmentation, and resource recycling.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper I (Geographical physical resource distribution), GS Paper II (Government policies, inter-state water disputes, institutional frameworks), and GS Paper III (Disaster management, agriculture pricing, irrigation efficiency, and sustainable development goals).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
