Hydrological Cycle

The continuous circulation of water within the Earth-atmosphere system, driven by solar energy and gravity, involving phase changes among liquid, vapor, and ice.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to understanding physical geography, climate systems, monsoon mechanisms, water resource management, and environmental degradation issues in both Prelims (GS-1) and Mains (GS-1/GS-3).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Hydrological Cycle.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
