Density Of Ocean Waters

Density is the mass per unit volume of seawater, primarily governed by the interplay of temperature, salinity, and pressure. It is the fundamental driver of thermohaline circulation and vertical stratification in the global ocean.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding Ocean Currents (Thermohaline Circulation), Climate Change (Global Conveyor Belt), and Marine Ecosystem distribution. Frequently tested in Prelims regarding the relationship between density, temperature, and salinity.
