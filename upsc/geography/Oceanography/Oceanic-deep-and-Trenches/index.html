Oceanic Deep And Trenches

Oceanic trenches are long, narrow, steep-sided depressions in the ocean floor that represent the deepest parts of the oceans, formed predominantly by the subduction of one tectonic plate beneath another.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (mapping of deepest trenches, Pacific Ring of Fire, plate tectonics) and Mains GS-1 (Geomorphology, distribution of major landforms, geodynamic processes).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
