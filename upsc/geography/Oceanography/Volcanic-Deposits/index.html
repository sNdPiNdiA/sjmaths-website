Volcanic Deposits

Volcanic deposits refer to all materials—solid, liquid, and gaseous—ejected from volcanic vents or fissures and subsequently accumulated on the Earth's surface or ocean floor, forming distinctive geological strata and landforms.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (geomorphology, oceanography, mineral resources) and Mains GS Paper 1 (geographical features, plate tectonics, distribution of key natural resources).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Volcanic Deposits.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
