Great Barrier Reef

The world's largest coral reef system composed of over 2,900 individual reefs and 900 islands stretching for over 2,300 kilometres over an area of approximately 344,400 square kilometres in the Coral Sea off the coast of Queensland, Australia.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under Geography (Oceanography, Marine Biodiversity, Climate Change impacts) and General Studies Paper III (Environment, Conservation, Pollution). Frequently targeted for conceptual questions on coral bleaching, biogeography, and UNESCO heritage designations.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of coral reef formation and the Great Barrier Reef ecosystem.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, management policies, and climate vulnerabilities.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding marine ecology and threats.

  • Marine ecosystems
  • Global climate patterns and El Nino Southern Oscillation
