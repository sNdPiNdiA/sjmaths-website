Abiotic Deposits

Abiotic deposits refer to non-biogenic mineral resources accumulated on the ocean floor through chemical precipitation, hydrothermal activity, continental weathering, and extraterrestrial fallout, forming critical deep-sea mineral reserves.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly relevant for UPSC CSE Prelims (Geography/Environment: ocean bottom relief, mineral resources, UNCLOS) and Mains GS-3 (Resources, Economic Geography, Geopolitics of critical minerals).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, classification, and chemical formation mechanisms of Abiotic Deposits.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, deep-sea mining technologies, and international legal frameworks (ISA/UNCLOS).
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
