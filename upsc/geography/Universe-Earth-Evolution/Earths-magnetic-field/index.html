Earths Magnetic Field

Earth's magnetic field (geomagnetic field) is a dynamic dipolar shield extending from the Earth's interior out into space, where it interacts with the solar wind, protecting the biosphere from harmful cosmic and solar radiation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC CSE Prelims through conceptual questions on magnetic poles vs. geographic poles, inclination, declination, and dynamo theory. In Mains (GS-1 Geography), it relates to Earth's internal structure, atmospheric evolution, and space weather dynamics.
