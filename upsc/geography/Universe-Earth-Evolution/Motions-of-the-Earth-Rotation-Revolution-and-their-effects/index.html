Motions Of The Earth Rotation Revolution And Their Effects

The Earth exhibits two primary continuous motions: Rotation (spinning on its tilted axis leading to day and night) and Revolution (orbiting the Sun in an elliptical path causing changing seasons and varying lengths of day and night).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental core topic in physical geography, frequently tested in UPSC CSE Prelims for conceptual clarity on insolation, equinoxes, solstices, and time calculations, while serving as a baseline for climatology and geomorphology in Mains GS-1.
