Earths Geological Time Scale

The Geological Time Scale (GTS) is a chronological system of chronological dating that classifies geological strata in time, serving as the international standard for depicting Earth's 4.54-billion-year history.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper 1 (Geography: Geomorphology, Earth History, Evolution of Life) and acts as foundational knowledge for understanding mineral resource distributions, fossil fuels, and paleoclimatology.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and hierarchical divisions of Earths Geological Time Scale.
  • Evaluate eons, eras, periods, epochs, and ages alongside major evolutionary and tectonic milestones.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims chronology questions.
