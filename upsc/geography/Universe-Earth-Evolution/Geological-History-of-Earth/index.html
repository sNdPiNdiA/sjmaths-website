Geological History Of Earth

The chronological chronicle of Earth's physical evolution and biological development spanning approximately 4.54 billion years, organized hierarchically into Eons, Eras, Periods, Epochs, and Ages.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental bedrock topic for Geography and Environment in UPSC CSE. Directly tested in Prelims via chronological ordering of geological eras, fossil markers, mass extinction events, and India's physiographic evolution (Peninsular block, Himalayas, Gondwana coal belts). In Mains (GS-1), it forms the baseline for understanding natural resource distribution, geomorphology, and past climate changes.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and evolutionary milestones.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
