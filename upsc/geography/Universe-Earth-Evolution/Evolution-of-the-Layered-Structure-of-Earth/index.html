Evolution Of The Layered Structure Of Earth

The geological and chemical process through which the homogeneous primordial Earth differentiated into distinct concentric zones—crust, mantle, outer core, and inner core—primarily driven by planetary heating and gravitational segregation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding geodynamics, plate tectonics, internal heat sources, and earthquake wave propagation, frequently tested in Prelims (direct statements on density stratification and seismic discontinuities) and Mains GS-1 (Geomorphology).
