Origin Of Life On Earth

The scientific study of how life emerged spontaneously from non-living inorganic matter on early Earth approximately 3.8 to 4.2 billion years ago, transitioning from chemical evolution to biological evolution.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 Geography/Science integration regarding Hadean and Archaean eons, Miller-Urey experiment, Panspermia, hydrothermal vents) and provides interdisciplinary analytical value for Mains GS-3 (Science & Technology, biotechnology, astrobiology).
