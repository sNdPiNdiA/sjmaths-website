Time And Costs Overruns Of Infrastructure Projects

Time overrun refers to the delay in completing an infrastructure project beyond its scheduled completion date, while cost overrun represents the excess expenditure incurred over the originally approved estimated budget.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Infrastructure: Energy, Ports, Roads, Airports, Railways; Investment Models like PPP) and Prelims (economic indicators, MoSPI reports, and governance mechanisms).
