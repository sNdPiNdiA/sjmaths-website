Textile Industry

The textile industry is a labor-intensive manufacturing sector encompassing the entire value chain from fiber production and spinning to weaving, dyeing, and garment manufacturing.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy & Manufacturing Sector) and Geography Optional, focusing on location factors, employment generation, and export competitiveness.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Understand Weberian and modern locational factors of the textile industry.
  • Analyze the structural shift from the decentralized handloom/powerloom sector to organized mills.
  • Evaluate government schemes like PM MITRA and Tech Textiles.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Economic Geography
  • Agro-based industries
  • Raw material linkages
