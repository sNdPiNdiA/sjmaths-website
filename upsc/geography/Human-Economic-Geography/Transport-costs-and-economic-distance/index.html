Transport Costs And Economic Distance

Transport costs refer to the direct and indirect expenses incurred in moving goods, passengers, and information from origin to destination, while economic distance translates physical distance into cost, time, and effort metrics that dictate spatial interaction and market reach.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper 1 (Human Geography, location of industries, world economic geography) and GS Paper 3 (Infrastructure, logistics, economic development, supply chain resilience).
