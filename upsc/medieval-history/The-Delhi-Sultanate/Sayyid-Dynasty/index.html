Sayyid Dynasty

The Sayyid Dynasty (1414–1451 CE) was the fourth dynasty of the Delhi Sultanate, established by Khizr Khan following the collapse of the Tughlaq dynasty after Timur's invasion.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested for its transitional role between the Tughlaqs and Lodis, the nature of its 'vassal' status to Timurids, and the shrinking territorial extent of the Sultanate.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Sayyid Dynasty.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
