Slave Dynasty Iltutmish

Shams-ud-din Iltutmish (1211–1236 AD) was the third ruler of the Mamluk dynasty, widely regarded as the 'Real Founder' of the Delhi Sultanate for consolidating the empire and securing legal recognition from the Caliphate.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Iltutmish is a perennial favorite for UPSC due to his institutional innovations (Iqta, Chahalgani) and his role in transitioning the Sultanate from a military camp to a stable administrative state.
