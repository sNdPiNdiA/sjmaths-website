Tughlaq Dynasty Firuz Shah Tughlaq

Firuz Shah Tughlaq (1351–1388) was the third ruler of the Tughlaq dynasty, known for his extensive public works, administrative reforms, and a policy of appeasement toward the Ulema and nobility.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested for his unique administrative innovations (Iqta system, taxation), public welfare initiatives (canals, hospitals), and his role in the decline of the Delhi Sultanate.
