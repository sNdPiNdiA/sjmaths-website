Slave Dynasty Rajia Sultan

Razia Sultan (r. 1236–1240) was the first and only female Muslim ruler of the Delhi Sultanate, daughter of Iltutmish, who challenged the patriarchal norms of the Turkic nobility.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tests the candidate's understanding of gender dynamics in medieval politics, the power struggle between the Sultan and the 'Chahalgani' (Corps of Forty), and the administrative challenges of the early Delhi Sultanate.
