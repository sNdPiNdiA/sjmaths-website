Tughlaq Dynasty Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq

Ghiyasuddin Tughlaq (reigned 1320–1325 AD) was the founder of the Tughlaq dynasty, marking the transition from the Khalji dynasty to a more centralized, agrarian-focused administration in the Delhi Sultanate.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the shift in administrative policy, agrarian reforms, and the consolidation of the Sultanate's frontiers against Mongol incursions.
