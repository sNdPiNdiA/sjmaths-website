Decline Of The Sultanate

The systemic disintegration of the Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526) caused by the erosion of central authority, economic exhaustion, and the failure of the Iqta system under the Lodi dynasty.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from the Sultanate to the Mughal Empire and the evolution of administrative structures in Medieval India.
