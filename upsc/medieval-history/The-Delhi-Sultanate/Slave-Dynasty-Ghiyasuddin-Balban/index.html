Slave Dynasty Ghiyasuddin Balban

Ghiyasuddin Balban (1266–1287 AD) was the ninth Sultan of the Mamluk dynasty who transformed the Delhi Sultanate from a loose confederation of Turkish nobles into a centralized, absolute monarchy based on the theory of Divine Kingship.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Balban is a perennial favorite for UPSC due to his administrative innovations, the 'Theory of Kingship', and his role in curbing the power of the 'Chahalgani' (The Forty), which serves as a case study for state centralization.
