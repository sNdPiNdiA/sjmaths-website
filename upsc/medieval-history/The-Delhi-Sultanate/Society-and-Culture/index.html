Society And Culture

The socio-cultural landscape of the Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526) represents a synthesis of Perso-Arabic Islamic traditions and indigenous Indian social structures, characterized by the emergence of a composite Indo-Islamic culture.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian syncretism, the impact of the Bhakti-Sufi movements, and the structural changes in the caste and urban systems during the medieval period.
