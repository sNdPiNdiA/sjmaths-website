Various Aspects Of Rule Under Khiljis

The Khilji Dynasty (1290–1320 AD) marked the 'Imperialist Phase' of the Delhi Sultanate, characterized by the transition from a tribal-military oligarchy to a centralized, autocratic, and expansionist state.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested for its radical economic reforms (Market Regulations), military centralization, and the shift in the nature of kingship from 'First among equals' to 'Absolute Despot'.
