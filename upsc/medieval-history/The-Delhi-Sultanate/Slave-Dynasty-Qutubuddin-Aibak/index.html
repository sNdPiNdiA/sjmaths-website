Slave Dynasty Qutubuddin Aibak

Qutubuddin Aibak (1206–1210 AD) was the founder of the Mamluk (Slave) Dynasty and the first independent Muslim ruler of the Delhi Sultanate, establishing the foundation of Turko-Afghan rule in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from Rajput polity to Islamic Sultanate, the evolution of Indo-Islamic architecture, and the consolidation of military-feudal structures in medieval India.
