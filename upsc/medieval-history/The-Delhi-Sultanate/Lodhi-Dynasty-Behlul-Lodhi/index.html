Lodhi Dynasty Behlul Lodhi

The Lodhi Dynasty (1451–1526) was the final ruling house of the Delhi Sultanate, representing the first Afghan dynasty in India, founded by Behlul Lodhi after the decline of the Sayyid Dynasty.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition from Turkic-Persian to Afghan political culture, the shift in the nature of kingship (from 'Sultan' to 'First among equals'), and the geopolitical precursors to the Mughal conquest.
