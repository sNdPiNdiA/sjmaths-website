The Cheras

The Cheras (or Keralaputras) were a major South Indian dynasty that ruled over parts of modern-day Kerala and Tamil Nadu, evolving from an early Sangam-era power into the influential Mahodayapuram Cheras (Kulasekharas) during the 9th–12th centuries.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the political, cultural, and maritime history of South India; frequently tested in the context of the 'Tripartite Struggle' era and the evolution of the Malayalam language.
