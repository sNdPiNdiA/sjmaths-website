The Rajputs

A group of warrior clans emerging in North and Western India (c. 7th–12th century) claiming Kshatriya status, characterized by a feudal political structure, chivalric code, and intense regionalism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the political fragmentation of India before the Ghurid invasions and the transition from the Gupta-post-Gupta era to the Sultanate period.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
