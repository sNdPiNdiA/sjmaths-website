Cholas Dynasty

The Imperial Cholas (c. 850–1279 CE) were a thalassocratic Tamil dynasty that established a centralized bureaucratic state, renowned for its sophisticated local self-government, maritime supremacy in the Indian Ocean, and monumental Dravidian temple architecture.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

A perennial favorite for Prelims (Art & Culture, Administration) and Mains (Governance, Maritime History, Temple Architecture).
