Indian Feudalism

A socio-economic and political structure characterized by the decentralization of state power, the rise of landed intermediaries (samantas), and the emergence of a self-sufficient manorial economy between 750-1200 CE.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from the centralized Gupta/Harsha empires to the fragmented regional kingdoms of the Early Medieval period; frequently tested in both Prelims (terminology) and Mains (socio-economic impact).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Indian Feudalism.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
