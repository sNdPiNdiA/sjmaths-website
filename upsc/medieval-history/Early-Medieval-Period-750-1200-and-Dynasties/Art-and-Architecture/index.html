Art And Architecture: Early Medieval Period (750-1200 AD)

The transition from classical Gupta-Vakataka traditions to regional styles characterized by the rise of temple architecture, complex iconography, and the evolution of Nagara, Dravida, and Vesara styles.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This period marks the crystallization of regional identities in Indian art, frequently tested through architectural features, temple patronage, and stylistic evolution in Prelims and Mains.
