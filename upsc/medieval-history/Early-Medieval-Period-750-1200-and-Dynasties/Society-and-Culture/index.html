Society And Culture (750-1200 AD)

The Early Medieval period represents a transition from the classical Gupta-Vakataka model to a decentralized, feudal, and regionalized socio-cultural structure characterized by the proliferation of castes and the rise of temple-centric urbanism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of Indian feudalism, the 'Kalivarjya' concept, and the synthesis of regional identities that define modern Indian cultural geography.
