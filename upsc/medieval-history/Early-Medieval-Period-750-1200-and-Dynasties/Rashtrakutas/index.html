Rashtrakutas

A powerful imperial dynasty that ruled the Deccan and parts of Central India from the 8th to the 10th century, acting as a bridge between North and South Indian politics.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequent source of questions regarding the Tripartite Struggle, temple architecture (Ellora/Elephanta), and administrative decentralization in the Early Medieval period.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Rashtrakutas.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Tripartite Struggle (Palas, Pratiharas, Rashtrakutas)
  • Early Medieval Deccan political geography
