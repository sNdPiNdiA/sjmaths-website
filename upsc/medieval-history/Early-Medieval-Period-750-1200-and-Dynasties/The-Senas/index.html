The Senas

The Sena dynasty was a Hindu dynasty that ruled from Bengal through the 11th and 12th centuries, succeeding the Palas and marking a period of significant cultural and religious revival in Eastern India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

The Senas are critical for understanding the transition from the Pala period to the Islamic conquest of Bengal, the evolution of Brahmanical orthodoxy, and the development of the Gauda style of art and literature.
