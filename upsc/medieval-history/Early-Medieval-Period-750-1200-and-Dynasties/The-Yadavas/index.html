The Yadavas of Devagiri

A prominent medieval Indian dynasty (c. 850–1334 CE) that ruled the Deccan region, primarily modern-day Maharashtra, northern Karnataka, and parts of Madhya Pradesh, with their capital at Devagiri (Daulatabad).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the political transition in the Deccan between the decline of the Chalukyas/Rashtrakutas and the advent of the Delhi Sultanate (Khaljis).
