Vijayanagar Economy

A highly monetized, agrarian-based economy characterized by the Nayankara system, extensive maritime trade, and a sophisticated taxation structure that facilitated the empire's role as a global commercial hub.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding administrative terminology (Nayankara, Ayagar) and in Mains for understanding medieval state formation and trade-urbanization linkages.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Vijayanagar Economy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
