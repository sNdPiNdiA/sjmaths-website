Vijayanagar Dynasties Aravidu

The Aravidu dynasty was the fourth and final ruling house of the Vijayanagara Empire (1570–1646), established by Tirumala Deva Raya following the catastrophic Battle of Talikota (1565). It marked the empire's shift from Hampi to Penukonda and Chandragiri, characterized by decentralization and regional fragmentation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the post-Talikota political landscape, the decline of centralized authority, and the rise of the Nayaka kingdoms which shaped South Indian history until the colonial era.
