Vijayanagar Administration

A highly centralized yet segmentary state structure characterized by the Nayankara system, military-feudal bureaucracy, and a sophisticated revenue administration that integrated agrarian expansion with temple-centric socio-economic control.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding administrative terminology (Nayaka, Ayagar) and in Mains for its unique military-feudal synthesis and impact on South Indian socio-economic history.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Vijayanagar Administration.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Understanding of the Feudalism debate
