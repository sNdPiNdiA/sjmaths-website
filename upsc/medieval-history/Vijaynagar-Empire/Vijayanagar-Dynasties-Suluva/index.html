Vijayanagar Dynasties Suluva

The Saluva Dynasty (1485–1505 CE) was the second ruling house of the Vijayanagara Empire, established by Saluva Narasimha Deva Raya following the decline of the Sangama dynasty.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition of power in medieval South India, the rise of military commanders (Nayakas), and the stabilization of the empire against Bahmani and Gajapati threats.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Vijayanagar Dynasties Suluva.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Bahmani-Vijayanagara rivalry
  • Geography of the Deccan
