Sufi Orders Silsila Naqshbandi

The Naqshbandi Silsila is a prominent Sunni Sufi order known for its strict adherence to Sharia, emphasis on silent dhikr (remembrance of God), and significant political influence in the Mughal Empire.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Medieval History for its role in the socio-political landscape of the Mughal era, specifically regarding the reaction against Akbar's liberal policies.
