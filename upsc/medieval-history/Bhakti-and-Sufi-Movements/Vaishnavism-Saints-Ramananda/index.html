Vaishnavism Saints Ramananda

Ramananda was a 14th-15th century Vaishnava devotional poet-saint who bridged the gap between South Indian Bhakti (Ramanuja's Vishishtadvaita) and North Indian vernacular devotionalism, emphasizing Rama-bhakti and social inclusivity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Ramananda is a pivotal figure in the 'Bhakti Movement' syllabus. UPSC frequently tests his role in democratizing religion, his lineage of disciples, and his departure from rigid caste-based Sanskrit orthodoxy.
