Bhakti Movement Groups Nirgun

The Nirgun Bhakti tradition refers to the worship of a formless, abstract, and omnipresent God (Nirguna Brahman), rejecting idol worship, rituals, and the caste-based hierarchy of the Brahmanical order.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims (Art & Culture/History) regarding philosophy, key saints, and their literary contributions; Mains requires analysis of their role in social reform and egalitarianism.
