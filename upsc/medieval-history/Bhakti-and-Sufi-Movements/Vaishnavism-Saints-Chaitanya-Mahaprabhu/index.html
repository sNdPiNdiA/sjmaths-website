Vaishnavism Saints Chaitanya Mahaprabhu

Chaitanya Mahaprabhu (1486–1534) was a 16th-century Bengali saint and social reformer who popularized Gaudiya Vaishnavism, emphasizing 'Bhakti' through 'Sankirtana' (congregational chanting) as the primary path to salvation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the socio-religious evolution of Medieval India, the democratization of Bhakti, and the cultural synthesis in Bengal and Odisha.
