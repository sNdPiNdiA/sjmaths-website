Vaishnavism Saints: Dnyaneshwar (Dhaneswar)

Sant Dnyaneshwar (1275–1296) was a 13th-century Marathi saint, poet, and philosopher of the Varkari tradition, best known for his commentary on the Bhagavad Gita, the 'Dnyaneshwari'.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the Bhakti movement's regional variations; Dnyaneshwar is central to understanding the socio-religious evolution of Maharashtra and the democratization of knowledge through vernacular literature.
