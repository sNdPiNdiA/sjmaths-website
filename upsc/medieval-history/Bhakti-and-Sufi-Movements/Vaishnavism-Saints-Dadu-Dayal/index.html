Vaishnavism Saints Dadu Dayal

Dadu Dayal (1544–1603) was a prominent 16th-century Nirguna Bhakti saint from Rajasthan, who founded the Dadu Panth, emphasizing monotheism, social equality, and the rejection of caste-based rituals.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the Bhakti movement's regional variations, the distinction between Saguna/Nirguna traditions, and the socio-religious impact of saints like Dadu Dayal on medieval Indian society.
