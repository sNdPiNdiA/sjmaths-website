Sufi Orders Silsila Chisti

The Chisti Silsila is the most influential Sufi order in the Indian subcontinent, characterized by its emphasis on asceticism, poverty, love for humanity, and the use of music (Sama) to induce spiritual ecstasy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Art & Culture and Medieval History for its syncretic impact on Indian society, architecture (Dargahs), and its relationship with the Delhi Sultanate and Mughal rulers.
