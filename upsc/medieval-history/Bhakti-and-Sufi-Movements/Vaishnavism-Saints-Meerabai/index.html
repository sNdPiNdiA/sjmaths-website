Vaishnavism Saints Meerabai

Meerabai (c. 1498–1546) was a 16th-century Rajput princess and mystic poet-saint of the Bhakti movement, renowned for her radical devotion (Bhakti) to Lord Krishna, transcending caste, gender, and social hierarchies.
