Bhakti Saints Shankaracharya

Adi Shankaracharya (788–820 CE) was a 8th-century Indian philosopher and theologian who consolidated the doctrine of Advaita Vedanta, revitalizing Vedic tradition against the rising influence of Buddhism and Jainism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the intellectual transition from post-Gupta Buddhism to the revival of Brahmanical Hinduism, forming the bedrock of the Bhakti movement.
