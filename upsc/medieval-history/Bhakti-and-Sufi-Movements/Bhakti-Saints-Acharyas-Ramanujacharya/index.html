Bhakti Saints Acharyas Ramanujacharya

Ramanujacharya (1017–1137 CE) was a seminal 11th-century philosopher and theologian who founded the Vishishtadvaita (Qualified Non-dualism) school of Vedanta, bridging the gap between Vedic orthodoxy and the Bhakti movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Ramanujacharya is a perennial favorite in UPSC Prelims (Art & Culture/History) due to his role in the Bhakti movement's evolution, his philosophical opposition to Shankara's Advaita, and his influence on later Vaishnava saints.
