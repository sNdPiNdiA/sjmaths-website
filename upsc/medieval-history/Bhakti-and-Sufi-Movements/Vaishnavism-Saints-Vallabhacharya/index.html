Vaishnavism Saints Vallabhacharya

Vallabhacharya (1479–1531) was a prominent Telugu philosopher and the founder of the Pushtimarg (Path of Grace) sect of Vaishnavism, emphasizing pure non-dualism (Shuddhadvaita) and devotion to Lord Krishna.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the Bhakti movement's philosophical nuances, specifically the distinction between various schools of Vedanta (Advaita, Vishishtadvaita, Shuddhadvaita) and the regional spread of Krishna-bhakti.
