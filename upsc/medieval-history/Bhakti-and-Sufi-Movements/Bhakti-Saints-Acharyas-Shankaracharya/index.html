Bhakti Saints Acharyas Shankaracharya

Adi Shankaracharya (788–820 CE) was the pre-eminent philosopher-theologian who revived Vedic Hinduism through the Advaita Vedanta school, establishing a pan-Indian institutional framework to counter the decline of Buddhism and Jainism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the intellectual transition from the post-Gupta period to the medieval Bhakti movement; frequently tested in Art & Culture and Medieval History sections.
