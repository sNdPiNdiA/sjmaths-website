Impact Of Bhakti Movement

A socio-religious reform movement spanning the 7th to 17th centuries that emphasized personal devotion (Bhakti) to a deity, challenging the rigid caste hierarchy and ritualistic dominance of the Brahmanical orthodoxy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims (Art & Culture/History) and Mains (GS-I: Indian Culture/Society) for its role in shaping regional identities, vernacular literature, and social egalitarianism.
