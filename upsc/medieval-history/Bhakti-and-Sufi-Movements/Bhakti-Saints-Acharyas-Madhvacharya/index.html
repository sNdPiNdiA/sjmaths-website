Bhakti Saints Acharyas Madhvacharya

Madhvacharya (1238–1317 CE) was a 13th-century philosopher and the chief proponent of the Dvaita (Dualism) school of Vedanta, emphasizing the eternal distinction between the individual soul (Jiva) and the Supreme Being (Brahman/Vishnu).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the philosophical distinctions between Advaita (Shankara), Vishishtadvaita (Ramanuja), and Dvaita (Madhva) to assess conceptual clarity in Medieval Indian intellectual history.
