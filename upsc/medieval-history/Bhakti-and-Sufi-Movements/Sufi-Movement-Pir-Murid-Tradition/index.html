Sufi Movement Pir Murid Tradition

The Pir-Murid tradition is the foundational hierarchical and spiritual relationship in Sufism, where the 'Pir' (spiritual guide/master) initiates and mentors the 'Murid' (disciple/seeker) on the path of 'Tasawwuf' (mysticism) to attain 'Marifat' (divine knowledge).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the socio-religious fabric of Medieval India, the evolution of syncretic culture, and the institutionalization of Sufi Silsilas which influenced political legitimacy and social integration.
