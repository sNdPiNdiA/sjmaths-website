Vaishnavism Saints Tukaram

Sant Tukaram (1608–1650) was a preeminent 17th-century Marathi poet-saint of the Varkari Sampradaya, central to the Bhakti movement in Maharashtra, known for his devotional 'Abhangas' that challenged caste hierarchies and ritualistic orthodoxy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tukaram is a perennial favorite in UPSC History (Prelims/Mains) due to his role in socio-religious reform, the democratization of Bhakti, and his influence on Maratha identity and the rise of the Maratha Empire.
