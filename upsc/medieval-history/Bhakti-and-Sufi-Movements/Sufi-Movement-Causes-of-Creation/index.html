Sufi Movement Causes Of Creation

The Sufi movement was a mystical dimension of Islam (Tasawwuf) that emerged as a reaction against the growing materialism, legalism, and political centralization of the Umayyad and Abbasid Caliphates, emphasizing direct personal experience of God.
