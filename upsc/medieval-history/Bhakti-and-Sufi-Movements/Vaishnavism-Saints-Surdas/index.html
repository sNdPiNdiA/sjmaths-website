Vaishnavism Saints Surdas

Surdas (c. 1478–1583) was a blind saint-poet of the Saguna Bhakti tradition, a central figure in the Pushtimarg sect, and a devotee of Krishna whose works revolutionized Braj Bhasha literature.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Surdas represents the zenith of the Krishna-bhakti movement. UPSC tests his philosophical alignment with Vallabhacharya, his linguistic contribution to Braj Bhasha, and his role in the cultural synthesis of Medieval India.
