Vaishnavism Saints Namdev

Namdev (1270–1350 CE) was a seminal Marathi saint-poet of the Varkari tradition, known for his egalitarian devotion to Vithoba (a form of Vishnu/Krishna) and his significant contribution to the Guru Granth Sahib.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Namdev represents the bridge between the Marathi Bhakti movement and the North Indian Sant tradition, frequently appearing in questions regarding the evolution of vernacular literature and socio-religious reform.
