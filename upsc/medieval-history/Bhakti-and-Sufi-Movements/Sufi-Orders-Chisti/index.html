Sufi Orders Chisti

The Chisti Silsila is the most influential Sufi order in the Indian subcontinent, characterized by its emphasis on simplicity, poverty, music (Sama), and a syncretic approach to local traditions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Medieval History for its socio-cultural impact, interaction with the Delhi Sultanate, and its role in the spread of Islam in India.
