Vaishnavism Saints Guru Ghasidas

Guru Ghasidas was a 19th-century social reformer and spiritual leader from Chhattisgarh who founded the Satnami sect, emphasizing monotheism, social equality, and the rejection of the caste system, often contextualized within the broader Bhakti tradition's egalitarian ethos.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests regional socio-religious reform movements that challenged the caste hierarchy. Guru Ghasidas is critical for understanding the 'Bhakti-reformist' continuum in Central India and the evolution of subaltern movements.
