Vaishnavism Saints Shankardeva

Srimanta Shankardeva (1449–1568) was a polymath, saint-scholar, and social reformer who pioneered the Ekasarana Dharma (Neo-Vaishnavite movement) in Assam, emphasizing monotheistic devotion to Lord Krishna.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Art & Culture (Bhakti movement) and Medieval History; questions focus on his literary contributions, institutional innovations (Sattras), and socio-cultural impact on Assamese identity.
