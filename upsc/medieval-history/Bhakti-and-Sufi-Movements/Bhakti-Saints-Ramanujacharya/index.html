Bhakti Saints Ramanujacharya

Ramanujacharya (1017–1137 CE) was a seminal 11th-century philosopher and theologian who founded the Vishishtadvaita (Qualified Non-dualism) school of Vedanta, bridging the gap between Vedic orthodoxy and the devotional Bhakti movement.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Ramanujacharya is a perennial favorite in UPSC Prelims (Art & Culture/History) due to his philosophical contributions, influence on the Vaishnava tradition, and the recent installation of the 'Statue of Equality'.
