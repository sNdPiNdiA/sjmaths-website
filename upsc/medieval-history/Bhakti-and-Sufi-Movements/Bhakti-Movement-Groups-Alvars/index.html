Bhakti Movement Groups Alvars

The Alvars were a group of 12 Tamil poet-saints who lived between the 6th and 9th centuries CE, dedicated to the path of 'Bhakti' (devotion) towards Lord Vishnu (Vishnu-bhakti). They were instrumental in the revival of Hinduism in South India, challenging the dominance of Jainism and Buddhism through emotional, vernacular poetry.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the distinction between Alvars (Vaishnavite) and Nayanars (Shaivite), their social inclusivity (caste/gender), and their literary contributions (Divya Prabandham).
