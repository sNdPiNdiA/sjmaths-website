Bhakti Movement Groups Sagun

The Sagun Bhakti tradition emphasizes devotion to a personal God (Ishwara) with attributes (Guna), form (Rupa), and name (Nama), rejecting the abstract, formless Brahman of the Nirgun school.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the distinction between Sagun and Nirgun schools, regional variations, and the socio-religious impact of Bhakti saints on medieval Indian society.
