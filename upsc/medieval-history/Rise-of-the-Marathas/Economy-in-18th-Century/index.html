Economy In 18th Century

A period of profound transition characterized by the decline of the Mughal centralized fiscal system, the rise of regional successor states, and the gradual integration of the Indian economy into the global mercantile network via the East India Company.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Great Divergence', the transition from medieval to colonial economy, and the socio-economic roots of the 1857 revolt.
