The Sindhias

The Sindhias were a prominent Maratha dynasty of the Kunbi caste that established a powerful princely state centered in Gwalior, playing a decisive role in 18th-century Indian geopolitics.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from Maratha Confederacy to British Paramountcy, the Anglo-Maratha Wars, and the evolution of the Subsidiary Alliance system.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of The Sindhias.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
