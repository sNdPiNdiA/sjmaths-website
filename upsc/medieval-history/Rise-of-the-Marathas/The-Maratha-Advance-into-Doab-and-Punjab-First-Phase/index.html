The Maratha Advance Into Doab And Punjab First Phase

The expansionist phase (1750s) where the Maratha Empire, under the Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao, extended its military influence from the Deccan into the Gangetic Doab and Punjab, challenging the declining Mughal authority and the rising Afghan power of Ahmad Shah Abdali.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the geopolitical vacuum in 18th-century India, the shift from regional to imperial ambitions of the Marathas, and the direct precursor to the Third Battle of Panipat (1761).
