The Maratha Advance Into Doab And Punjab Second Phase

The second phase of Maratha expansion (1757–1758) represents the zenith of Maratha territorial ambition under the Peshwa Balaji Baji Rao, characterized by the expulsion of Afghan garrisons from Punjab and the establishment of Maratha suzerainty over the Indus region.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the geopolitical vacuum in 18th-century India, the shift from regional power to imperial contenders, and the direct causal link to the Third Battle of Panipat (1761).
