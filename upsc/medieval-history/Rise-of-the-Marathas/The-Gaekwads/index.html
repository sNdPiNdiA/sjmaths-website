The Gaekwads

The Gaekwads were a prominent Maratha dynasty that established the Baroda State, emerging as one of the most powerful members of the Maratha Confederacy under the Peshwas.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the decentralization of the Maratha Empire, the evolution of Princely States, and the nature of British paramountcy in Western India.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
