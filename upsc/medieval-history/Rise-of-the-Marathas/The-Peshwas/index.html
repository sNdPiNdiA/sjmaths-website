The Peshwas

The Peshwas were the hereditary Prime Ministers of the Maratha Empire who transformed the state from a monarchy into a confederacy, shifting the power center from the Chhatrapati to the Peshwa office in Pune.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition of power in 18th-century India, the Maratha-British conflicts, and the administrative evolution of the Maratha Confederacy.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
