The Marathas And Nizam Ul Mulk

The geopolitical struggle between the rising Maratha Confederacy and the autonomous Hyderabad state established by Nizam-ul-Mulk Asaf Jah I, defining the power vacuum in the Deccan post-Aurangzeb.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the decline of the Mughal Empire, the shift from centralized authority to regional successor states, and the Maratha expansionist policy (Chauth and Sardeshmukhi).
