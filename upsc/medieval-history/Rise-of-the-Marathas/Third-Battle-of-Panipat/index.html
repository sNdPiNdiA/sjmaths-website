Third Battle Of Panipat

A decisive military conflict fought on 14 January 1761 between the Maratha Empire and the invading forces of the Afghan King Ahmad Shah Abdali, supported by Indian allies.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It marks a watershed moment in Indian history, signaling the end of Maratha hegemony in North India and creating a power vacuum that facilitated British colonial expansion.
