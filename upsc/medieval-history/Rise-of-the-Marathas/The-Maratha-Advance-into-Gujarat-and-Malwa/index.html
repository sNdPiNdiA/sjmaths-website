The Maratha Advance Into Gujarat And Malwa

The strategic expansion of the Maratha Empire under the Peshwas (primarily Baji Rao I) into the Mughal provinces of Gujarat and Malwa during the early 18th century, transitioning from predatory raids to formal administrative control.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the decline of the Mughal Empire, the shift in power dynamics from the Deccan to North India, and the evolution of Maratha fiscal systems (Chauth and Sardeshmukhi).
