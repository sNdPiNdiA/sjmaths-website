The Marathas And Their Policy Of Expansion

The Maratha expansion policy refers to the strategic transition from 'Swarajya' (sovereignty over the homeland) to 'Samrajya' (imperial hegemony), characterized by the institutionalization of Chauth and Sardeshmukhi to finance a mobile military state.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the power vacuum post-Aurangzeb, the transition from Mughal decline to British ascendancy, and the socio-political structure of the 18th-century Indian polity.
