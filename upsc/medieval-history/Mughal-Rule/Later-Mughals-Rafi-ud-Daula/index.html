Later Mughals Rafi Ud Daula

Rafi-ud-Daula, also known by his regnal title Shah Jahan II, was a puppet Mughal Emperor who reigned briefly in 1719 under the control of the Sayyid Brothers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Era of Kingmakers' (1713–1720) and the rapid decline of central Mughal authority post-Aurangzeb.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
