Akbar Conflict With The Afghans Hemu

The conflict refers to the struggle for the throne of Delhi between the nascent Mughal Empire under Akbar (represented by Bairam Khan) and the Suri-Afghan forces led by Hemu, culminating in the Second Battle of Panipat (1556).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This event marks the definitive restoration of Mughal rule in India, ending the Suri interregnum and establishing the political legitimacy of the Mughal dynasty.
