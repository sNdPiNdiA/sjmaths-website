Mughal Economy Trend Of Indias Economy In First Half Of 18th Century

A period of transition characterized by the systemic decline of the centralized Mughal fiscal apparatus (Jagirdari crisis) and the emergence of regional successor states, leading to a shift from a centralized imperial economy to decentralized, localized agrarian and commercial networks.
