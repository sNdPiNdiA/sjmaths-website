Mughal Society The Middle Strata

The middle strata in Mughal society comprised a diverse, non-homogenous group of professionals, merchants, lower-level officials, and artisans who bridged the gap between the ruling elite (Mansabdars) and the agrarian peasantry.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the socio-economic structure of the Mughal period to assess the candidate's understanding of urban growth, commercialization, and the rise of the 'middle class' in pre-colonial India.
