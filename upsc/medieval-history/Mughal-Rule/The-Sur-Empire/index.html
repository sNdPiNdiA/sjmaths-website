The Sur Empire

The Sur Empire (1540–1555) was an Afghan dynasty founded by Sher Shah Suri after defeating Humayun, representing a brief but transformative interregnum in Mughal rule characterized by centralized administrative, agrarian, and military reforms.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It serves as the bridge between the Sultanate and Mughal administrative systems; Sher Shah's reforms (land revenue, currency, communication) became the blueprint for Akbar's governance.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
