Mughal Society The Commercial Classes

The commercial classes in Mughal India comprised a diverse hierarchy of merchants, bankers, and brokers who facilitated the monetization of the agrarian economy and the expansion of internal and external trade.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Proto-Industrialization' debate, the role of the 'Middle Class' in the Mughal economy, and the transition from medieval to colonial economic structures.
