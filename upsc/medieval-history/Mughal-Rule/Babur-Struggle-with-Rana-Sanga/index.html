Babur Struggle With Rana Sanga

The military and political conflict between the Mughal founder Babur and the Rajput confederacy led by Rana Sanga of Mewar, culminating in the Battle of Khanwa (1527), which solidified Mughal hegemony in North India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the transition from the Delhi Sultanate to the Mughal Empire, the shift in military technology (gunpowder), and the nature of Rajput-Mughal political relations.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
