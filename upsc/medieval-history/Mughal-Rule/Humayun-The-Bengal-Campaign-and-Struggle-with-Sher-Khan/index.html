Humayun The Bengal Campaign And Struggle With Sher Khan

A critical phase in the early Mughal period (1537-1540) characterized by Humayun's strategic miscalculation in Bengal and the rise of Sher Khan Suri, leading to the temporary collapse of Mughal authority in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is essential for understanding the transition from the Mughal to the Sur Empire, the strategic importance of the Chunar and Gaur forts, and the military tactical superiority of Sher Khan over Humayun.
