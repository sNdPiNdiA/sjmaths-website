Mughal Economy The Mughal State And Commerce

The Mughal economy was a centralized agrarian-based system characterized by high revenue extraction, a sophisticated monetary network, and an expanding commercial sector integrated into global trade routes.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from medieval to early modern India, the impact of European mercantilism, and the structural reasons for the Mughal decline.
