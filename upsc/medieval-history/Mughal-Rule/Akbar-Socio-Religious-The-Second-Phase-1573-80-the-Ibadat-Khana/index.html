Akbar Socio Religious The Second Phase 1573 80 The Ibadat Khana

The second phase of Akbar's religious policy (1573-1580) marks the transition from orthodox Islamic adherence to a policy of 'Sulh-i-kul' (universal peace), institutionalized by the establishment of the Ibadat Khana (House of Worship) at Fatehpur Sikri in 1575.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the evolution of Mughal secularism, the role of the Ulema in state affairs, and the intellectual synthesis of Akbar's reign, frequently appearing in Prelims (chronology/concepts) and Mains (state-religion relations).
