Aurangzeb Marathas And The Deccan

The protracted military and political struggle between the Mughal Empire under Aurangzeb and the rising Maratha power, exacerbated by the annexation of Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur and Golconda).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the 'Deccan Ulcer' theory, the decline of the Mughal Empire, and the rise of regional powers in the 18th century.
