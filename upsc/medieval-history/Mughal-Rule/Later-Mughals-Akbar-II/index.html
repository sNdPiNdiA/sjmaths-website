Later Mughals Akbar II

Akbar II (reigned 1806–1837) was the 17th Mughal Emperor, serving as a titular sovereign under the de facto control of the British East India Company (EIC). His reign marks the transition of the Mughal throne from a political power to a mere pensionary institution.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the decline of Mughal authority, the evolution of British paramountcy, and the socio-political context of the early 19th-century Indian resistance.
