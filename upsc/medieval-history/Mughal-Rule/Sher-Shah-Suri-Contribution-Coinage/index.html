Sher Shah Suri Contribution Coinage

Sher Shah Suri (1540-1545) revolutionized the Indian monetary system by abolishing debased currency and introducing a standardized tri-metallic currency system based on fixed weights and purity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is a perennial favorite in UPSC Prelims (History) due to its role in economic integration, administrative centralization, and its direct influence on the Mughal and British currency systems.
