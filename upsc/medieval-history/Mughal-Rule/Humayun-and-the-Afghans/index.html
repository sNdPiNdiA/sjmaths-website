Humayun And The Afghans

The struggle between the early Mughal state under Humayun and the resurgent Afghan nobility, primarily led by Sher Shah Suri, representing the conflict between the nascent Mughal centralized authority and the traditional Afghan tribal confederacy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This period marks the critical 'interregnum' in Mughal history, testing the candidate's understanding of political instability, military strategy, and the transition from the Lodi-Afghan legacy to the Suri administration.
