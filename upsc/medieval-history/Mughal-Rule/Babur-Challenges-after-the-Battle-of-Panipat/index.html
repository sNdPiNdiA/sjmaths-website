Babur Challenges After The Battle Of Panipat

The period immediately following the First Battle of Panipat (1526) where Babur transitioned from a nomadic conqueror to a sedentary ruler, facing existential threats from the Afghan nobility and the Rajput Confederacy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the consolidation phase of the Mughal Empire, the shift in military tactics from Central Asian to Indian warfare, and the socio-political resistance of the Lodi remnants and Rajput powers.
