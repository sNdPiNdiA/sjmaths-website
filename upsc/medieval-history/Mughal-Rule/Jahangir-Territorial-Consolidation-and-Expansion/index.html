Jahangir Territorial Consolidation And Expansion

The period (1605–1627) characterized by the stabilization of Mughal frontiers, the culmination of the Deccan policy, and the strategic management of the North-Western borderlands.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the transition from Akbar's expansionist phase to the consolidation phase, and the geopolitical challenges of the Mughal Empire in the 17th century.
