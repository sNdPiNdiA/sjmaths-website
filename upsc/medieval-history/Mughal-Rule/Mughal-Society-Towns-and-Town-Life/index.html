Mughal Society Towns And Town Life

The Mughal urban landscape was a complex network of administrative, military, and commercial centers (qasbas and shahrs) that facilitated the extraction of surplus and the circulation of commodities within a centralized imperial framework.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC frequently tests the socio-economic dynamics of Mughal urbanization, the distinction between rural-urban linkages, and the role of the 'Camp-City' phenomenon in shaping the economy.
