Later Mughals Rafi Ud Darajat

Rafi-ud-Darajat was the 10th Mughal Emperor who reigned for a brief period in 1719, serving as a puppet ruler under the control of the Sayyid Brothers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Era of Puppet Emperors' and the peak of the Sayyid Brothers' influence (Kingmakers) in the decline of the Mughal Empire.
