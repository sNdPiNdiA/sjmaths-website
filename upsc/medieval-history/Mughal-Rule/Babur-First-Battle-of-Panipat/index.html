Babur First Battle Of Panipat

The First Battle of Panipat (April 21, 1526) was a decisive military engagement between the forces of Zahir-ud-din Muhammad Babur and the Lodi Empire under Ibrahim Lodi, marking the end of the Delhi Sultanate and the establishment of the Mughal Empire in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is a cornerstone of Medieval Indian History, frequently tested for its tactical innovations (Gunpowder/Artillery), the transition from Sultanate to Mughal rule, and the geopolitical shifts in Central and South Asia.
