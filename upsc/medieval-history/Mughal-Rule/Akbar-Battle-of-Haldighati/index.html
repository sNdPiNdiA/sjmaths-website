Akbar Battle Of Haldighati

A decisive military engagement fought on June 18, 1576, between the Mughal Empire, led by Raja Man Singh I, and the forces of Mewar, commanded by Maharana Pratap, in the narrow mountain pass of Haldighati, Rajasthan.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tests the understanding of Mughal expansionist policies, Rajput-Mughal relations, and the historiography of regional resistance against imperial hegemony.
