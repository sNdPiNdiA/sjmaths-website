Later Mughals Jahandar Shah

Jahandar Shah (1712–1713) was the second ruler of the Later Mughal period, whose short reign marked the beginning of the 'Kingmaker' era and the institutionalization of administrative decay.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

UPSC tests the transition from the centralized Mughal state to the era of court factions, the rise of the Sayyid Brothers, and the shift in fiscal policies like Ijarah.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
