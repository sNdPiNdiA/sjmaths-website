Baburs Contribution And Significance Of His Advent

The advent of Zahir-ud-din Muhammad Babur in 1526 marks the transition from the Delhi Sultanate to the Mughal Empire, introducing advanced gunpowder warfare and a new concept of centralized sovereignty in India.
