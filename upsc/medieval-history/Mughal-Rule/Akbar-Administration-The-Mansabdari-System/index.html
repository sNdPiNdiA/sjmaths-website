Akbar Administration The Mansabdari System

A unique, integrated military-cum-civil administrative grading system introduced by Akbar (c. 1571) to organize the Mughal bureaucracy and military hierarchy, replacing the traditional iqta system.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a perennial favorite for Prelims (conceptual mechanics) and Mains (administrative evolution, centralization, and socio-economic impact).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
