Akbar Administration Concept Of Navaratnas

The 'Navaratnas' (Nine Gems) refers to a select group of nine highly accomplished courtiers, scholars, and administrators who formed the intellectual and executive backbone of Emperor Akbar’s imperial court.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Composite Culture' (Ganga-Jamuni Tehzeeb) of the Mughal era, administrative decentralization, and the patronage of arts/sciences under Akbar.
