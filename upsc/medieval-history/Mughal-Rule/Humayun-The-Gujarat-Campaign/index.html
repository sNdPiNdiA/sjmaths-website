Humayun The Gujarat Campaign

A strategic military expedition (1535-1536) led by Mughal Emperor Humayun against Bahadur Shah of Gujarat to curb the latter's growing power, expansionist ambitions, and support for Afghan rebels.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tests the candidate's understanding of Mughal consolidation, the geopolitical rivalry between the Mughals and the Sultanate of Gujarat, and the strategic failures that led to the temporary loss of Mughal control.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Humayun The Gujarat Campaign.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
