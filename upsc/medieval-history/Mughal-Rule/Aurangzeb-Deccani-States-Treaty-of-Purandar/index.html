Aurangzeb Deccani States Treaty Of Purandar

The Treaty of Purandar (1665) was a strategic pact between the Mughal Empire, represented by Mirza Raja Jai Singh, and the Maratha Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, aimed at curbing Maratha expansion and securing Mughal hegemony in the Deccan.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the 'Deccan Ulcer' policy of Aurangzeb, the evolution of Maratha-Mughal diplomacy, and the geopolitical shift in 17th-century India.
