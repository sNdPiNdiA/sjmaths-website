Aurangzeb And The Deccani States

The systematic expansionist policy of Aurangzeb (1658–1707) aimed at the total annexation of the Deccan Sultanates (Bijapur and Golconda) and the containment of the rising Maratha power, marking the final phase of Mughal territorial consolidation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is critical for understanding the 'Deccan Ulcer' theory, the decline of the Mughal Empire, and the shift in the center of gravity of Indian politics from Delhi to the Deccan.
