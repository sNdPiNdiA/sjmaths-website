Aurangzeb Expansion Of Empire

The expansionist phase of the Mughal Empire under Aurangzeb (1658–1707) marked the territorial zenith of the empire, characterized by the annexation of the Deccan Sultanates and prolonged military engagement in the South.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the 'Deccan Ulcer', the decline of the Mughal Empire, and the shift in administrative focus from North to South India.
