Later Mughals Farrukhsiyar

Farrukhsiyar (reigned 1713–1719) was the ninth Mughal Emperor, whose reign marked the consolidation of the 'Kingmaker' era and the irreversible decline of central Mughal authority.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the transition from Mughal hegemony to British colonial dominance, specifically the 1717 Farman and the rise of the Sayyid Brothers.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
