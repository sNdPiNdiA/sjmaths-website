Later Mughals Rise Of Regional States And Foreign Invasions

The period from 1707 (death of Aurangzeb) to 1857 (end of Mughal Empire) characterized by the systemic decline of central authority, the emergence of autonomous successor states, and the destabilizing impact of foreign invasions.

This topic is critical for understanding the transition from Medieval to Modern India, the power vacuum that facilitated British colonial expansion, and the socio-political fragmentation of the 18th century.
