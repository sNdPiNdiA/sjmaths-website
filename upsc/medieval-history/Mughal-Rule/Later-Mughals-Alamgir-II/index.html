Later Mughals Alamgir II

Alamgir II (reigned 1754–1759) was the 15th Mughal Emperor, a puppet ruler placed on the throne by the Wazir Imad-ul-Mulk, marking a period of extreme political instability and the decline of central authority.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the power vacuum in North India, the rise of the Marathas, the Afghan invasions of Ahmad Shah Abdali, and the prelude to the Battle of Plassey (1757).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
