Akbar Socio Religious Initiatives Relations With The Ulama And Social Reforms

Akbar's socio-religious policy was a transition from orthodox Islamic governance to 'Sulh-i-kul' (universal peace), aimed at integrating a diverse populace through institutionalized religious discourse and social liberalization.

This topic is central to understanding the 'Mughal Synthesis' and the evolution of secular statecraft in India, frequently appearing in Prelims (policy chronology) and Mains (secularism/national integration).
