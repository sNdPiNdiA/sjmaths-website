Conflicts Of The Bahmani Successor States With Vijayanagar

The geopolitical struggle between the five Bahmani successor states (Deccan Sultanates) and the Vijayanagara Empire, primarily driven by control over the Raichur Doab, the Krishna-Godavari delta, and the Marathwada region.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is central to understanding the shifting power dynamics of Medieval India, the evolution of military technology (artillery), and the eventual decline of the Vijayanagara Empire post-1565.
