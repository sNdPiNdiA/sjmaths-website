Bahmani Military

The Bahmani military was a centralized, multi-ethnic, and gunpowder-reliant force that evolved from a feudal-cavalry structure into a sophisticated administrative-military apparatus, serving as the primary instrument for the Sultanate's expansion and defense against the Vijayanagara Empire.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the evolution of medieval Indian warfare, the introduction of gunpowder, and the socio-political tensions between 'Afaqis' (foreigners) and 'Dakhinis' (locals) within the military hierarchy.
