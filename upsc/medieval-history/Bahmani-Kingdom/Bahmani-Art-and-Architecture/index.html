Bahmani Art And Architecture

The Bahmani architectural style represents a distinct synthesis of Persian, Central Asian, and indigenous Deccan traditions, characterized by massive masonry, heavy domes, and the evolution of the 'Deccani' arch.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Art & Culture (Prelims) and Medieval History (Mains) due to its unique fusion of Islamic aesthetics with local Deccan craftsmanship, serving as a precursor to the Bijapur style.
