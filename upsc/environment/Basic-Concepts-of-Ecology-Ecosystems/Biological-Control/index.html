Biological Control

Biological control (Biocontrol) is a method of controlling pests, weeds, and plant diseases using other living organisms (natural enemies) such as predators, parasitoids, or pathogens. It is a cornerstone of Integrated Pest Management (IPM) aimed at reducing reliance on synthetic chemical pesticides.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Environment (GS-III) and Agriculture (GS-III). UPSC frequently tests the ecological balance, invasive species management, and the shift from chemical to sustainable agriculture.
