Seasonal And Diurnal Fluctuation

Seasonal and diurnal fluctuations refer to the periodic, predictable variations in environmental factors—primarily temperature, light (photoperiod), and moisture—occurring over annual (seasonal) and daily (diurnal) cycles, which dictate biological rhythms and ecosystem productivity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding Biomes, Phenology, Climate Change impacts on biodiversity, and adaptation strategies in Ecology (GS Paper III).
