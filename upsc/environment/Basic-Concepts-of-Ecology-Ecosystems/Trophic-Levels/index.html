Trophic Levels

Trophic levels represent the hierarchical feeding positions in a food chain or web, defined by the number of energy transfer steps from the primary source of energy (sunlight).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding ecosystem dynamics, bioaccumulation, biomagnification, and energy flow efficiency, which are recurring themes in Environment & Ecology Prelims and Mains.
