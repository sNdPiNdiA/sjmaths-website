Flow Of Energy In Ecosystem

The unidirectional transfer of energy through trophic levels in an ecosystem, governed by the laws of thermodynamics, starting from solar radiation and dissipating as metabolic heat.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to Environment & Ecology (GS-III). Frequently tested via conceptual MCQs on trophic efficiency, bioaccumulation, and ecological pyramids.
