Food Web

A food web is a complex, interconnected network of multiple food chains within an ecosystem, representing the actual feeding relationships and energy flow pathways among various organisms.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding ecosystem stability, trophic cascades, bioaccumulation, and climate change impacts on biodiversity.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
