Turnover

Turnover in ecology refers to the seasonal or periodic mixing of water layers in a lake or reservoir, driven by temperature-induced density changes, which redistributes nutrients and dissolved oxygen.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding aquatic ecosystem dynamics, eutrophication, and the impact of climate change on freshwater biodiversity.
