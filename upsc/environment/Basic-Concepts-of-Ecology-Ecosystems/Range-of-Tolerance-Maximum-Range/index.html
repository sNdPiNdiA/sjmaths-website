Range Of Tolerance Maximum Range

The Range of Tolerance is the span of environmental conditions (temperature, salinity, pH, etc.) within which an organism can survive, grow, and reproduce, with the 'Maximum Range' representing the absolute physiological limits beyond which the organism faces mortality.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding species distribution, climate change impacts, and biodiversity loss, frequently appearing in Prelims (Environment/Ecology) and Mains (GS Paper III: Conservation).
