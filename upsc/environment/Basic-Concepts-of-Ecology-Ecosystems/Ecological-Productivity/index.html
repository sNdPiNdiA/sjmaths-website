Ecological Productivity

Ecological productivity is the rate at which biomass is generated by an ecosystem, primarily through the conversion of solar energy into chemical energy by autotrophs (Primary Productivity) and subsequent energy transfer to heterotrophs (Secondary Productivity).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to understanding energy flow, trophic dynamics, climate change impacts, and food security; frequently tested in Prelims (Environment/Geography) and Mains (GS Paper III).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
