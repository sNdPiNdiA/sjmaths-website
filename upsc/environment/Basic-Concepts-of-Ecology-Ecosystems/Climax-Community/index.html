Climax Community

The final, stable, and self-perpetuating stage of ecological succession in an ecosystem, where the community composition remains relatively constant until disturbed by external environmental factors.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding ecosystem stability, biodiversity conservation, and forest management policies; frequently tested in the context of ecological succession and biome resilience.
