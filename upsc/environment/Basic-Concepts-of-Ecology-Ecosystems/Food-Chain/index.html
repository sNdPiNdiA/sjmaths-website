Food Chain

A linear sequence of organisms through which nutrients and energy pass as one organism eats another, representing the unidirectional flow of energy in an ecosystem.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to understanding ecosystem services, bioaccumulation, biomagnification, and environmental impact assessments (EIA).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Food Chain.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
