Population Ecology

The study of how biotic populations interact with their environment, focusing on factors that influence population size, density, distribution, and age structure over time.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding biodiversity loss, conservation biology, sustainable development, and demographic dividend questions in both Prelims and Mains (GS Paper III).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Population Ecology.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of Ecology
  • Basic Biology (NCERT Class 12)
