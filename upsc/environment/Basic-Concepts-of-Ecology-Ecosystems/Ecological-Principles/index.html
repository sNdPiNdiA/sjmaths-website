Ecological Principles

Ecological principles are the fundamental laws governing the interactions between organisms and their environment, focusing on energy flow, nutrient cycling, and population dynamics. It encompasses the study of biotic and abiotic components and their systemic integration within the biosphere.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Environment & Ecology (GS Paper III). It forms the base for understanding climate change, biodiversity loss, and sustainable development goals (SDGs).
