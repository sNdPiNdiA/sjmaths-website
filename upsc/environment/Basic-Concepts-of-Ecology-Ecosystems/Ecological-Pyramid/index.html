Ecological Pyramid

A graphical representation of the relationship between different organisms at different trophic levels in an ecosystem, depicting the flow of energy, biomass, or population numbers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims to assess conceptual clarity on ecosystem dynamics, energy flow, and trophic efficiency; essential for Mains answers on biodiversity and climate change.
