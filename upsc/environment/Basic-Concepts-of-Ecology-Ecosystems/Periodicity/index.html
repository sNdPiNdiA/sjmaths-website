Periodicity

Periodicity in ecology refers to the rhythmic, recurring, and predictable biological activities of organisms in response to environmental cues, primarily driven by solar and lunar cycles.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding ecosystem dynamics, climate change impacts on phenology, and biodiversity conservation strategies.
