Habitat Ecological Niche

Habitat is the physical 'address' where an organism lives, while an Ecological Niche is the functional 'profession' or role an organism plays within its ecosystem, encompassing its interactions with biotic and abiotic factors.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to Environment & Ecology (GS Paper III). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding species interactions, competitive exclusion, and ecosystem stability.
