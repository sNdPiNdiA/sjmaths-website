Ecotone

An ecotone is a transitional area of vegetation between two different plant communities, such as forest and grassland, characterized by a high degree of species diversity and density.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Ecotones are critical for understanding biodiversity hotspots, climate change impacts, and ecological sensitivity, frequently appearing in Prelims (Environment/Ecology) and Mains (GS Paper III).
