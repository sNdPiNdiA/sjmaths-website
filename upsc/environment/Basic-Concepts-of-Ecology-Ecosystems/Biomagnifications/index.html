Biomagnifications

The progressive increase in the concentration of non-biodegradable, persistent, and fat-soluble substances (like heavy metals or pesticides) at successive trophic levels of a food chain.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Environment & Ecology (Prelims) and Disaster Management/Pollution (Mains). It links toxicology, food security, and biodiversity loss.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Biomagnifications.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
