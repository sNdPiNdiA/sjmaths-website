Ecological Succession

The gradual, predictable, and directional process of change in the species structure of an ecological community over time, leading to a stable climax community.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims (Environment/Ecology) regarding community dynamics, biodiversity patterns, and ecosystem restoration; essential for Mains answers on climate change and habitat loss.
