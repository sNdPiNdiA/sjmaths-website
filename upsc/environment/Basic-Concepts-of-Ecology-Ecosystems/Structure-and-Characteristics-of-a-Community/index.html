Structure And Characteristics Of A Community

An ecological community is an assemblage of populations of different species occupying the same geographical area at a specific time, interacting through trophic, competitive, and symbiotic relationships.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding ecosystem dynamics, biodiversity conservation, and climate change impact assessments in both Prelims (Environment/Ecology) and Mains (GS Paper III).
