Environmental Protection Act (EPA), 1986

An umbrella legislation enacted under Article 253 of the Constitution to provide a framework for the coordination of central and state authorities, established in the wake of the Bhopal Gas Tragedy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

EPA is the 'parent' act for various rules (EIA, CRZ, Hazardous Waste). It is frequently tested for its constitutional basis, delegated legislation powers, and its role as an 'Umbrella Act'.
