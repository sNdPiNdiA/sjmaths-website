International Conventions Protocols Their Objectives

International environmental law comprises a set of multilateral agreements, treaties, and protocols designed to regulate human impact on the biosphere, manage transboundary pollution, and promote sustainable development through collective global governance.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is a perennial favorite in UPSC Prelims (Environment section) and provides the legal/institutional framework for Mains answers on Climate Change, Biodiversity, and Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
