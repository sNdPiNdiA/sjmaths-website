Benefits Of River Ganga

The Ganga is a transboundary river of the Indian subcontinent, serving as a vital hydrological, socio-economic, and ecological lifeline for the Indo-Gangetic Plain, supporting one-third of India's population.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested under GS Paper I (Geography), GS Paper III (Environment/Economy), and Essay papers due to its role in food security, cultural heritage, and climate resilience.
