NAPCC National Mission For Sustaining Himalayan Ecosystems

NMSHE is one of the eight missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), specifically designed to evolve management measures for sustaining and safeguarding the Himalayan glacier and mountain ecosystem.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

The Himalayas are the 'Third Pole' and a climate-sensitive zone. UPSC frequently tests the intersection of climate change, biodiversity, and transboundary water security.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
