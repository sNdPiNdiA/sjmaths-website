Indian Climate Change Assessment

The systematic scientific evaluation of climate change impacts, vulnerabilities, and future projections across the Indian subcontinent, primarily synthesized in the 'Assessment of Climate Change over the Indian Region' report by the Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Environment & Ecology) and GS Paper I (Geography). It links climate science with policy frameworks like NAPCC and India's NDCs under the Paris Agreement.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Indian Climate Change Assessment.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of Atmospheric Science
  • Basics of Indian Monsoon dynamics
  • Understanding of IPCC Assessment Reports
