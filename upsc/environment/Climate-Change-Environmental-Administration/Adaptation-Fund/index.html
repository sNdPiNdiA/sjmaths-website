Adaptation Fund

A dedicated international fund established under the Kyoto Protocol to finance concrete adaptation projects and programmes in developing countries that are particularly vulnerable to the adverse effects of climate change.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Critical for Environment (GS-III) and International Relations (GS-II). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding institutional linkages (UNFCCC/Kyoto/Paris) and funding mechanisms.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Adaptation Fund.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • UNFCCC framework
  • Kyoto Protocol mechanisms
  • Climate Finance basics
