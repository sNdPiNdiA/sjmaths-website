Carbon Footprint

The total amount of greenhouse gases (GHGs), primarily carbon dioxide and methane, generated by the actions of an individual, event, organization, service, place, or product, expressed as carbon dioxide equivalent (CO2e).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to climate change mitigation strategies, India's NDC commitments, and the transition to a Net-Zero economy by 2070.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Carbon Footprint.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
