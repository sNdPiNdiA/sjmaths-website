Botanical Survey Of India

The Botanical Survey of India (BSI) is the apex research organization under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), mandated for taxonomic and floristic studies of wild plant resources of India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

BSI is critical for questions related to biodiversity conservation, environmental governance, and institutional frameworks in India. It frequently appears in Prelims regarding its mandate, publications, and role in the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Botanical Survey Of India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of Biodiversity
  • Biological Diversity Act, 2002
  • Environmental Governance in India
