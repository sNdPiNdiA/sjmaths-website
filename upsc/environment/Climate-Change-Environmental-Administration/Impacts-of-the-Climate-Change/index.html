Impacts Of The Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, primarily driven by anthropogenic greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions, resulting in systemic disruptions to the Earth's biosphere, cryosphere, and socio-economic stability.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This is a core theme in GS Paper III (Environment & Ecology) and GS Paper I (Geography). It is frequently linked to disaster management, food security, and international relations (COP summits).
