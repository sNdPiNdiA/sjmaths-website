Central Zoo Authority (CZA)

A statutory body established under the Wildlife (Protection) Act, 1972, mandated to oversee, regulate, and provide technical/financial assistance to zoos across India to ensure high standards of animal welfare and conservation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Environment Prelims regarding statutory status, composition, and its role in ex-situ conservation. Mains relevance lies in biodiversity management and animal welfare ethics.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Central Zoo Authority.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
