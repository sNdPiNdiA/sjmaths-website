Agriculture Increases Carbon Dioxide Emissions

Agriculture contributes to CO2 emissions primarily through land-use change (deforestation for cultivation), soil organic matter oxidation, and the energy-intensive production of inputs like fertilizers and machinery.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Environment/Agriculture) and GS Paper I (Geography). UPSC frequently tests the distinction between CO2 (land use) and CH4/N2O (biological processes) in agriculture.
