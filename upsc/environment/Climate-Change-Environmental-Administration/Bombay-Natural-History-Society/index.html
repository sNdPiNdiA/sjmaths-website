Bombay Natural History Society (BNHS)

A pan-India wildlife research organization founded in 1883, functioning as an autonomous NGO engaged in conservation research, advocacy, and public awareness.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Environment Prelims regarding biodiversity conservation, bird migration studies, and institutional roles in national policy.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Bombay Natural History Society.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Knowledge of Wildlife Protection Act, 1972
