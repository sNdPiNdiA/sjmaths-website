NAPCC National Water Mission

The National Water Mission (NWM) is one of the eight core missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), aimed at ensuring integrated water resource management to conserve water, minimize wastage, and ensure more equitable distribution across India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical component of GS Paper III (Environment/Climate Change) and GS Paper II (Governance/Policy). UPSC frequently tests the intersection of climate adaptation, water security, and sustainable development goals.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of NAPCC National Water Mission.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of NAPCC
  • Integrated Water Resources Management (IWRM) concepts
