Factors Affecting Climate Change

Climate change refers to long-term shifts in temperatures and weather patterns, driven by both natural internal processes and external forcings, as well as persistent anthropogenic changes in the composition of the atmosphere.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This is a core pillar of the Environment syllabus (GS Paper III). It links directly to international climate governance (UNFCCC, Paris Agreement), disaster management, and sustainable development goals.
