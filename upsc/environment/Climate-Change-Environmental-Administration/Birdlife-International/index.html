Birdlife International

A global partnership of non-governmental organizations (NGOs) that strives to conserve birds, their habitats, and global biodiversity by working with people towards sustainability in the use of natural resources.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently linked to Environment and Ecology (GS-III), specifically regarding biodiversity conservation, international environmental governance, and indicator species for climate change.
