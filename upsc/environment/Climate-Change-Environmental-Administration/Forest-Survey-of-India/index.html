Forest Survey Of India

The Forest Survey of India (FSI) is a premier national organization under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC), responsible for conducting surveys and assessments of the country's forest resources.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

FSI reports (ISFR) are the primary data source for Environment questions in Prelims and provide critical evidence for Mains answers on climate change, biodiversity, and sustainable development.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Forest Survey Of India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Indian Geography
  • Knowledge of National Forest Policy 1988
