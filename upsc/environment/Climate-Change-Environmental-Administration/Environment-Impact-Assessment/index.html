Environment Impact Assessment

A statutory process under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, designed to identify, predict, and evaluate the environmental, social, and health consequences of proposed developmental projects before decision-making.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

EIA is a perennial favorite in GS Paper III (Environment/Development) and GS Paper II (Governance/Policy). It tests the balance between 'Ease of Doing Business' and 'Sustainable Development'.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Environment Impact Assessment.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Environment (Protection) Act, 1986
  • Sustainable Development Goals
  • Principles of Environmental Law (Precautionary/Polluter Pays)
