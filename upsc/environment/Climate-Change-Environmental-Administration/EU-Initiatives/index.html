EU Initiatives

The European Union's strategic policy framework aimed at achieving climate neutrality by 2050 through legislative, economic, and technological interventions, primarily anchored by the European Green Deal.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

EU initiatives serve as global benchmarks for environmental governance, carbon pricing, and circular economy models, frequently appearing in Prelims (Environment/IR) and Mains (GS-III Environment/Economy).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of EU Initiatives.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • UNFCCC framework
  • Paris Agreement basics
  • Carbon markets
