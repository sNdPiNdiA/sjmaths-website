Climate Change Governance

Climate Change Governance refers to the multi-level, multi-actor institutional frameworks, policies, and mechanisms designed to mitigate greenhouse gas emissions and facilitate adaptation to climate impacts. It encompasses international treaties, national legislative mandates, and sub-national administrative actions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a high-priority area in GS Paper III (Environment/Economy) and GS Paper II (International Relations). UPSC frequently tests the evolution from Kyoto to Paris, India's NDCs, and the institutional architecture of climate finance.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Climate Change Governance.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of International Law
