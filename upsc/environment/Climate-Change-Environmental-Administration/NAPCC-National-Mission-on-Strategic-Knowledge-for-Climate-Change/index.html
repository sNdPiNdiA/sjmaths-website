NAPCC National Mission On Strategic Knowledge For Climate Change

One of the eight core missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), aimed at identifying challenges, fostering research, and building a robust knowledge system to address climate change impacts in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in the context of India's climate policy architecture, institutional capacity building, and the intersection of science, technology, and policy-making.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें
