NAPCC National Mission On Sustainable Habitat

One of the eight core missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), aimed at making habitats sustainable through improvements in energy efficiency in buildings, management of solid waste, and a shift to public transport.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Environment/Urbanization) and GS Paper I (Geography/Urbanization). Frequently linked with Smart Cities Mission, AMRUT, and Swachh Bharat Mission.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of NAPCC National Mission On Sustainable Habitat.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
