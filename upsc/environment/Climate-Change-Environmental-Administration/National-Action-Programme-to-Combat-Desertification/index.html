National Action Programme To Combat Desertification

The National Action Programme (NAP) is India's strategic policy framework formulated to implement the United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification (UNCCD), aimed at addressing land degradation and desertification through sustainable land management and community-based interventions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Environment & Ecology) and GS Paper I (Geography). Frequently linked to Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 15.3) and India's commitment to Land Degradation Neutrality (LDN).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
