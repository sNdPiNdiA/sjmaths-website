Biocarbon Fund

The BioCarbon Fund (BioCF) is a public-private initiative managed by the World Bank that mobilizes finance for projects that sequester or conserve carbon in forest and agricultural ecosystems (Land Use, Land-Use Change, and Forestry - LULUCF).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a critical component of Climate Finance and Sustainable Development, frequently linked to REDD+ mechanisms, sustainable agriculture, and international climate negotiations (UNFCCC).
