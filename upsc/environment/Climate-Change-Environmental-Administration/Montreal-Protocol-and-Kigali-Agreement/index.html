Montreal Protocol And Kigali Agreement

The Montreal Protocol is an international treaty designed to protect the ozone layer by phasing out the production of ozone-depleting substances (ODS), while the Kigali Amendment is its 2016 legally binding update to phase down hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs) to mitigate global warming.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequent topic in Environment & Ecology (Prelims) and Climate Change Governance (Mains). It represents the most successful multilateral environmental agreement (MEA) and a model for climate diplomacy.
