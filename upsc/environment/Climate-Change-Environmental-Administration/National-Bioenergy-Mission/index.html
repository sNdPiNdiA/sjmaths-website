National Bioenergy Mission

A strategic policy framework under the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) aimed at promoting the generation of energy from biomass, agricultural residues, and organic waste to achieve energy security and decarbonization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Environment/Energy) and GS Paper II (Governance). It links directly to India's Net Zero 2070 target, circular economy, and farmers' income enhancement.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Bioenergy Mission.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of Renewable Energy
  • National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)
  • Biomass vs Biofuel distinction
