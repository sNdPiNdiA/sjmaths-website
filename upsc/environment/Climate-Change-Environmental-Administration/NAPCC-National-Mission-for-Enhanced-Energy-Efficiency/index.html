NAPCC National Mission For Enhanced Energy Efficiency

One of the eight core missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), aimed at strengthening the market for energy efficiency through innovative policy instruments and regulatory frameworks.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Environment and Economy sections due to its intersection with India's NDCs (Nationally Determined Contributions) and the Energy Conservation Act.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of NAPCC National Mission For Enhanced Energy Efficiency.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) functions
  • Basics of Carbon Markets
