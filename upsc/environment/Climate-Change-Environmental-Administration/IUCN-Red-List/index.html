IUCN Red List

The IUCN Red List of Threatened Species is the world's most comprehensive inventory of the global conservation status of biological species, utilizing a set of quantitative criteria to evaluate the extinction risk of thousands of species and subspecies.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a perennial favorite in Environment & Ecology. Questions focus on the criteria for classification, the distinction between 'Extinct in the Wild' vs 'Extinct', and the role of IUCN in global biodiversity governance (e.g., CITES, CBD).

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of IUCN Red List.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
