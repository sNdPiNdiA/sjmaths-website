Green Rating For Integrated Habitat Assessment

GRIHA is India's national rating system for green buildings, developed by TERI (The Energy and Resources Institute) and endorsed by the Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) to evaluate the environmental performance of a building holistically over its entire life cycle.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

GRIHA is a critical component of India's climate change mitigation strategy, specifically under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC) - National Mission on Sustainable Habitat (NMSH). It is frequently tested in the context of sustainable urban development, energy efficiency, and environmental governance.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
