IPCC: Full Form and Key FactsIPCC: पूरा नाम और प्रमुख तथ्य

  • Full form: Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change.पूरा नाम: जलवायु परिवर्तन पर अंतरसरकारी पैनल।
  • Established: 1988 by the World Meteorological Organization (WMO) and United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP).स्थापना: 1988 में विश्व मौसम विज्ञान संगठन (WMO) और संयुक्त राष्ट्र पर्यावरण कार्यक्रम (UNEP) द्वारा।
  • Role: Assesses published climate research; it does not conduct its own research. Its reports inform climate policy.भूमिका: प्रकाशित जलवायु शोध का आकलन करता है; स्वयं शोध नहीं करता। इसकी रिपोर्टें जलवायु नीति के लिए जानकारी देती हैं।

Intergovernmental Panel On Climate Change

The IPCC is the United Nations body for assessing the science related to climate change, providing policymakers with regular scientific assessments on climate change, its implications, and potential future risks, as well as options for adaptation and mitigation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is the primary scientific authority underpinning international climate negotiations (UNFCCC/Paris Agreement). UPSC frequently tests its structure, reporting mechanisms, and the distinction between its assessment reports and policy prescriptions.
