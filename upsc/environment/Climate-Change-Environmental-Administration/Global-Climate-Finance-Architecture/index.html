Global Climate Finance Architecture

The complex system of public, private, and alternative sources of financing—supported by bilateral, multilateral, and domestic mechanisms—designed to support mitigation and adaptation actions to address climate change.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to GS Paper III (Environment/Economy) and GS Paper II (International Relations). Frequently tested via COP outcomes, institutional mandates (GCF, GEF), and the 'Common But Differentiated Responsibilities' (CBDR) principle.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Global Climate Finance Architecture.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • UNFCCC basics
  • Paris Agreement
  • Kyoto Protocol
  • Basic Macroeconomics
