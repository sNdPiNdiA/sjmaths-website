Drawback In EIA Process

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) is a statutory process under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986, designed to predict environmental consequences of developmental projects; 'Drawbacks' refer to systemic, procedural, and institutional failures that undermine its efficacy in balancing economic growth with ecological sustainability.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

EIA is a perennial favorite in GS Paper III (Environment/Development) and GS Paper II (Governance). It tests the candidate's ability to critique policy implementation and understand the conflict between 'Ease of Doing Business' and 'Environmental Rule of Law'.
