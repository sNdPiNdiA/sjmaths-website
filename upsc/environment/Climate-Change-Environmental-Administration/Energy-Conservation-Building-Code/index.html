Energy Conservation Building Code (ECBC)

A regulatory framework under the Energy Conservation Act, 2001, that sets minimum energy performance standards for commercial buildings with a connected load of 100kW or greater or a contract demand of 120kVA or more.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

ECBC is a critical component of India's Nationally Determined Contributions (NDCs) under the Paris Agreement, focusing on 'Energy Efficiency' as a key pillar of climate mitigation.
