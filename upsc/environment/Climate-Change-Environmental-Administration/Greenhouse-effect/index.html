Greenhouse Effect

A natural process where atmospheric gases trap infrared radiation emitted by the Earth's surface, maintaining the planet's temperature within a range suitable for life. The 'Enhanced Greenhouse Effect' refers to the anthropogenic increase in these gases leading to global warming.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to Environment and Ecology (GS Paper III). It forms the basis for understanding Climate Change, Global Warming, Carbon Sequestration, and international climate negotiations (UNFCCC, Paris Agreement).
