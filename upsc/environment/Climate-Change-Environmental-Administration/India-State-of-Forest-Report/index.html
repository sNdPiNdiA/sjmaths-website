India State Of Forest Report

A biennial publication by the Forest Survey of India (FSI) under the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MoEFCC) that assesses the forest and tree cover of India using remote sensing technology.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Environment (Prelims) and Geography/Disaster Management (Mains). Frequently tested on trends, definitions of forest types, and state-wise rankings.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of India State Of Forest Report.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Remote Sensing
  • National Forest Policy 1988
  • Indian Forest Act 1927
