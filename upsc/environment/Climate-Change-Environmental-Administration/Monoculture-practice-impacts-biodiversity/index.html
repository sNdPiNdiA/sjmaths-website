Monoculture Practice Impacts Biodiversity

Monoculture is the agricultural practice of growing a single crop, plant, or livestock species, variety, or breed in a field or farming system at a time. It leads to ecological simplification, reducing genetic diversity and ecosystem resilience.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly linked to GS Paper III (Environment/Agriculture), specifically 'Conservation, environmental pollution and degradation'. Frequently tested in the context of food security vs. ecological sustainability.
