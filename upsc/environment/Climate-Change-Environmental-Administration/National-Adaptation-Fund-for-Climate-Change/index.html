Understand NAFCC's 2015 launch, climate-adaptation purpose, project support for vulnerable States and UTs, and NABARD's role as the National Implementing Entity. NAFCC की 2015 में स्थापना, जलवायु-अनुकूलन का उद्देश्य, संवेदनशील राज्यों और केंद्रशासित प्रदेशों के लिए परियोजना सहायता तथा National Implementing Entity के रूप में NABARD की भूमिका जानें।

National Adaptation Fund For Climate Change

A central sector scheme established in 2015 to support concrete adaptation activities in states and union territories that are particularly vulnerable to the adverse effects of climate change.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Environment/Climate Change) and GS Paper II (Governance/Policy). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding funding mechanisms and institutional hierarchy.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Adaptation Fund For Climate Change.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC)
  • State Action Plan on Climate Change (SAPCC)
  • Climate Finance basics
