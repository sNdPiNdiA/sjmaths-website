NAPCC National Mission On Sustainable Agriculture

NMSA is one of the eight missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), designed to enhance agricultural productivity, focus on integrated farming, water use efficiency, and soil health management to make Indian agriculture climate-resilient.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

NMSA is a high-yield topic for GS Paper III (Agriculture/Environment) and Prelims, focusing on climate adaptation strategies, resource efficiency, and government schemes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of NAPCC National Mission On Sustainable Agriculture.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of NAPCC
  • Knowledge of Indian Agriculture sector challenges
