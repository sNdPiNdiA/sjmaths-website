Eco Mark Scheme

A voluntary labeling scheme introduced by the Government of India in 1991 to identify consumer products that meet specific environmental criteria while maintaining quality standards.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Relevant for Environment (GS-III) under sustainable consumption, pollution control, and government policy initiatives. Frequently tested in the context of 'Green Economy' and 'Sustainable Development Goals'.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Eco Mark Scheme.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Environmental Protection Act, 1986
  • Concepts of Sustainable Consumption and Production (SCP)
