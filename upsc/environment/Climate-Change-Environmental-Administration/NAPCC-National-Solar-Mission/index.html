NAPCC National Solar Mission

The Jawaharlal Nehru National Solar Mission (JNNSM) is one of the eight core missions under the National Action Plan on Climate Change (NAPCC), launched in 2010 to establish India as a global leader in solar energy by creating policy conditions for its diffusion across the country.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a cornerstone of India's Intended Nationally Determined Contributions (INDCs) under the Paris Agreement and a critical component of India's energy security and decarbonization strategy.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
