Least Developed Countries Fund (LDCF)

A dedicated climate fund established under the UNFCCC (2001) to support the Least Developed Countries (LDCs) in preparing and implementing National Adaptation Programmes of Action (NAPAs) and National Adaptation Plans (NAPs).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in the context of Climate Finance, UNFCCC mechanisms, and North-South cooperation; essential for Mains answers on 'Climate Justice' and 'Adaptation Finance'.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Least Developed Countries Fund.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
