Global Warming Health

The study of how anthropogenic climate change, driven by greenhouse gas emissions, alters environmental determinants of health, leading to direct and indirect morbidity and mortality.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

High relevance in GS Paper III (Environment/Disaster Management) and GS Paper II (Social Justice/Health Policy). Frequently linked to SDGs (Goal 3 and 13).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Global Warming Health.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts
  • Basic understanding of Greenhouse Effect
  • Epidemiological basics
