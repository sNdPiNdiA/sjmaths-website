National Action Plan For Climate Change

The NAPCC is a comprehensive policy framework launched in 2008 by the Prime Minister's Council on Climate Change to address India's climate mitigation and adaptation challenges through eight national missions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

It is a cornerstone of India's environmental governance, frequently appearing in Prelims (mission objectives) and Mains (climate policy, sustainable development, and international commitments).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Action Plan For Climate Change.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of UNFCCC and Paris Agreement
  • Basic understanding of India's NDCs
