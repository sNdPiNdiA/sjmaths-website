FAME India Programme

Faster Adoption and Manufacturing of (Hybrid &) Electric Vehicles in India (FAME) is a flagship incentive scheme under the National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP) 2020, aimed at promoting the adoption of electric and hybrid vehicles to reduce vehicular emissions and fossil fuel dependency.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Critical for GS Paper III (Environment, Infrastructure, Economy) and GS Paper II (Government Policies). It links India's COP26 Panchamrit goals, Net Zero 2070 targets, and the 'Make in India' initiative.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of FAME India Programme.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • National Electric Mobility Mission Plan (NEMMP)
  • Paris Agreement/COP26 commitments
  • Basic understanding of Battery Electric Vehicles (BEVs) vs Hybrid Electric Vehicles (HEVs)
