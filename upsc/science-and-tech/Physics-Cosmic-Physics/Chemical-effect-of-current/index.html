Chemical Effect Of Current

The chemical effect of electric current refers to the chemical changes, notably electrolysis and ion migration, produced when an electric current is passed through a conducting liquid (electrolyte).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (General Science - Physics/Chemistry overlap: electroplating, Faraday's laws, conductivity of liquids) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Industrial applications, green hydrogen generation, battery technologies).
