Looming

Looming is an uncommon optical illusion and superior optical mirage caused by the refraction of light through atmospheric layers of varying temperature and density, making distant objects appear elevated, distorted, and floating above their actual position.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested in UPSC CSE Prelims (Science & Technology - Physics/Optics) through direct conceptual questions or applications of atmospheric refraction, total internal reflection, and optical phenomena distinct from inferior mirages.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and optical physics mechanisms of Looming.
  • Distinguish between superior mirages (looming), inferior mirages, and towering.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding atmospheric phenomena.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic geometric optics (refraction, refractive index, Snell's law)
  • Atmospheric stratification and lapse rate
