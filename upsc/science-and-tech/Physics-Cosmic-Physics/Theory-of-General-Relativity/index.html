Theory Of General Relativity

Albert Einstein's geometric theory of gravitation published in 1915, which posits that the phenomena of gravitation is not a classical force, but rather a curvature of spacetime caused by mass and energy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (Science & Tech / Physics applications like GPS, gravitational waves, black holes) and Mains GS-3 (Space technology, scientific discoveries, applications of modern physics).
