Waves

A wave is a disturbance or oscillation that travels through space and matter, transferring energy from one place to another without transporting the medium itself.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (General Science - Physics) via direct property-based questions, electromagnetic spectrum, seismic waves, and communication technologies. Mains GS-3 applications include telecommunications, remote sensing, and disaster management (tsunamis/earthquakes).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Mechanical, Electromagnetic, and Matter waves.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and technological applications.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Electromagnetism fundamentals
  • Simple harmonic motion
