Meteoroids Meteors Meteorites

The cosmic continuum of interplanetary rocky debris categorized by its phase and location: Meteoroids (in space), Meteors (burning through Earth's atmosphere, colloquially called shooting stars), and Meteorites (surviving impact on Earth's surface).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 Geography & Science/Tech) regarding cosmic phenomena, atmospheric layers, and space exploration. Essential for understanding Earth's bombardment history, solar system formation, and asteroid deflection missions.
