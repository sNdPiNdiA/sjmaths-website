Gravitational Lensing

Gravitational lensing is a cosmic phenomenon where a massive celestial body (such as a galaxy cluster or black hole) causes a sufficient gravitational field to bend and distort the path of light traveling from a distant background source to an observer. It acts as a natural cosmic telescope, magnifying, distorting, or multiple-imaging distant objects.
