Cohesive Force

Cohesive force is the intermolecular force of attraction acting between identical molecules of the same substance, primarily responsible for phenomena like surface tension, droplet formation, and high tensile strength in liquids such as water.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Civil Services Prelims (General Science / Physics) through applied questions on capillary action, plant water transport, surface tension, and physical properties of matter.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Cohesive Force versus Adhesive Force.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, biological transport mechanisms, and physical applications.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • States of matter and surface chemistry fundamentals
