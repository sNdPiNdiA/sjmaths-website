Fuse

An electrical safety device consisting of a metallic wire or strip that melts and breaks the circuit when excess current flows through it, protecting appliances from damage and preventing electrical fires.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Physics/Everyday Science) focusing on household circuitry, electrical safety, materials science, and thermal properties of matter.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Electrical Fuses.
  • Evaluate material properties, ratings, and comparative parameters of different fuse types.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding circuit protection.
