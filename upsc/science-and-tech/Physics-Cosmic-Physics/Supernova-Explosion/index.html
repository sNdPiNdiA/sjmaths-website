Supernova Explosion

A supernova is a powerful and luminous stellar explosion that occurs during the last evolutionary stages of a massive star or through the triggered detonation of a white dwarf, briefly outshining entire galaxies and dispersing heavy elements into the cosmos.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Space Physics, Cosmic phenomena, Astronomy) and connects to GS-3 Mains regarding space exploration, astrophysics advancements, and cosmic nucleosynthesis.
