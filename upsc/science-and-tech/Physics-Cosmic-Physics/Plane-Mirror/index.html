Plane Mirror

A plane mirror is a flat, smooth reflective surface—typically formed by depositing a thin layer of silver or aluminium on one side of a glass plate—that reflects light according to the laws of reflection to form virtual, upright, and laterally inverted images.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential microtopic under General Science (Physics - Optics) for UPSC Prelims testing foundational laws of reflection, image characteristics, magnification, and field of view, while serving as a baseline concept for advanced optical instruments and defence applications like periscopes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and technological applications in optical systems.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding lateral inversion and magnification.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें
