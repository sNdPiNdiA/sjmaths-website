Gravitation

Gravitation is the universal attractive force existing between any two masses in the universe, described classically by Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation and geometrically by Einstein's General Theory of Relativity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for UPSC Civil Services Prelims (GS-1 Physics, Space Technology applications, satellite mechanics, weightlessness) and Mains GS-3 (Science & Technology - indigenous space missions, LIGO-India gravitational wave observatory).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Gravitation.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
