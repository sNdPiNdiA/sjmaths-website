Latent Heat

Latent heat is the heat energy absorbed or released by a substance during a constant-temperature phase change (e.g., solid to liquid, liquid to gas), wherein the added energy alters molecular potential energy rather than kinetic energy or temperature.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper I (Climatology, thermodynamics of the atmosphere, tropical cyclones, and monsoon energetics) and General Studies Paper III (Science & Technology - fundamental physics and industrial applications).
