Light

Light is electromagnetic radiation within a certain portion of the electromagnetic spectrum that can be perceived by the human eye, exhibiting both wave and particle (photon) dual nature.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Light is a core topic in General Science (Prelims) and Science & Technology (Mains). UPSC regularly tests foundational physics principles, optical instruments, modern communication applications like fiber optics, and atmospheric phenomena.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Light including reflection, refraction, and diffraction.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, technological applications, and institutional frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Physics
  • Electromagnetic Spectrum concepts
