Rayleigh Scattering

Rayleigh Scattering is the elastic scattering of light or other electromagnetic radiation by particles much smaller than the wavelength of the light, named after the 19th-century British physicist Lord Rayleigh.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology Prelims (physics phenomena applied to everyday occurrences like blue sky, red sunsets) and conceptual grounding for GS-3 remote sensing and atmospheric optics.
