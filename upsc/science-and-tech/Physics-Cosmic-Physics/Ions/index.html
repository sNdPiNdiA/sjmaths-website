Ions

An ion is an atom or molecule in which the total number of electrons is not equal to the total number of protons, giving it a net positive or negative electrical charge.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Physics and Chemistry modules under General Science in UPSC Prelims (concepts of electrochemistry, ionization, cosmic rays, ionosphere) and Science & Technology applications (lithium-ion batteries, plasma physics).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
