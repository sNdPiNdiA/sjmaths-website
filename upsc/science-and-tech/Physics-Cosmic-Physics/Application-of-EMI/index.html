Application Of EMI

Electromagnetic Induction (EMI) is the phenomenon where an electromotive force (EMF) is induced in a closed circuit by a changing magnetic flux, serving as the foundational principle for modern electrical power generation and conversion technologies.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC CSE Prelims (Science & Tech applications, daily life physics) and GS Paper III (technology missions, infrastructure, energy systems), requiring a deep understanding of induction-based devices and principles.
