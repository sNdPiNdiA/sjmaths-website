Chrome Plating

Chrome plating (electroplating using chromium) is an advanced surface finishing technique that deposits a thin layer of chromium onto a metal or plastic substrate to enhance corrosion resistance, wear properties, and aesthetic appeal.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested under Science and Technology (Industrial Chemistry, Materials Science, Environmental Pollution & Toxicology), involving heavy metal toxicity (Hexavalent Chromium), industrial safety regulations, and manufacturing value chains for GS-3 and Prelims.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational electrochemical mechanisms and classifications of chrome plating.
  • Evaluate environmental hazards, spatial distribution of industrial clusters, and regulatory frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding heavy metals.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic electrochemistry (Anode, Cathode, Electrolyte)
  • Oxidation states of transition metals
