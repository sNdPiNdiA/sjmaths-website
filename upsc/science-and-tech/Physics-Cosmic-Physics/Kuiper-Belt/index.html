Kuiper Belt

The Kuiper Belt is a circumstellar disc in the outer Solar System, extending from the orbit of Neptune (at 30 AU) to approximately 50 AU from the Sun, containing vast numbers of minor planetoids, remnants from the Solar System's formation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology and Geography (Cosmic Physics, Solar System evolution, space missions like New Horizons) and serves as vital content for understanding planetary dynamics and celestial body classifications.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, dynamics, and structural divisions of the Kuiper Belt.
  • Evaluate comparative characteristics of Kuiper Belt Objects (KBOs), Oort Cloud, and Asteroid Belt.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding outer space bodies.

  • Kepler's laws of planetary motion
