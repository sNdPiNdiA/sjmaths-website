Myopia Correction Concave Lens

Myopia (nearsightedness) is a refractive error where distant objects appear blurred because light focuses in front of the retina, which is optically corrected using a diverging concave lens of negative focal power.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested frequently in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Physics, Human Physiology, Optical Instruments) and holds indirect relevance for Mains GS-2 (Public Health policies regarding childhood blindness and visual impairment in India).
