Adhesive Force

Adhesive force refers to the intermolecular attraction between unlike molecules, causing them to cling to one another. It is fundamental to surface chemistry, fluid mechanics, and material science.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested frequently in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 Science & Tech / Physics) through daily life phenomena, capillary action, meniscus formation, and biomedical/industrial applications.
