Comet

Comets are icy, small solar system bodies that, when passing close to the Sun, warm up and begin to release gases, a process known as outgassing, producing a visible atmosphere or coma, and sometimes a tail.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Cosmic Physics, Space Missions) regarding astronomical terminology, orbital characteristics, origin of the solar system, and interplanetary space probes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Comet structures, orbits, and origins.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution like the Kuiper Belt and Oort Cloud, alongside space mission interventions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding comet tails and classifications.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Solar System formation
  • Kepler's laws of planetary motion
  • Basic astrophysics
