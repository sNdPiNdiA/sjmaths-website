Rainbow

An optical and meteorological phenomenon caused by reflection, refraction, and dispersion of light in water droplets resulting in a continuous spectrum of light appearing in the sky.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under General Science (Physics - Optics, Wave phenomena, and atmospheric optics) to check conceptual clarity of light dispersion and geometrical optics.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of primary and secondary rainbows.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of light reflection, refraction, dispersion, and total internal reflection.
