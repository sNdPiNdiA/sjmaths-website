Electromagnetic Induction

The phenomenon where a changing magnetic field through a conductor induces an electromotive force (EMF) or electric current, governed primarily by Faraday's Laws of Induction.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental to understanding power generation (generators, transformers), wireless charging, and modern electrical infrastructure, frequently appearing in Science & Tech Prelims.
