Asteroids

Asteroids are rocky, airless remnants left over from the early formation of our solar system about 4.6 billion years ago, primarily orbiting the Sun between Mars and Jupiter in the Main Asteroid Belt.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology (Space exploration, planetary defense) and Geography (Cosmic physics, solar system evolution) in both Prelims and GS-3 Mains.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Asteroids.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Gravity and orbital dynamics
