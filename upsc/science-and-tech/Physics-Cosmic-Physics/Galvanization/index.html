Galvanization

Galvanization is the industrial metallurgical process of applying a protective zinc coating to iron or steel to prevent premature rusting and corrosion.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested extensively in UPSC CSE Prelims (under Science and Tech: Materials Science, Chemistry of Corrosion, Sacrificial Protection) and Mains GS-3 (Infrastructure, Industrial Manufacturing, and Resource Efficiency).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and electrochemical mechanisms of Galvanization.
  • Evaluate industrial processes, spatial distribution, and policy dimensions of steel manufacturing.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on corrosion prevention.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic electrochemistry
  • Properties of metals and alloys
  • Oxidation and reduction reactions
