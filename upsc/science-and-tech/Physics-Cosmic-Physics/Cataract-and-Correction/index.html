Cataract And Correction

A cataract is the medical condition characterized by the progressive clouding and opacity of the eye's natural crystalline lens, leading to decreased visual acuity; correction is primarily achieved via surgical replacement of the opaque lens with an artificial Intraocular Lens (IOL).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व
