Escape Velocity

Escape velocity is the minimum speed needed for a free, non-propelled object to escape from the gravitational influence of a primary body, reaching an infinite distance from it.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested frequently in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 / Science & Technology) for conceptual clarity on planetary atmospheres, orbital mechanics, and satellite launches; relevant for Mains GS-3 regarding space technology advancements.
