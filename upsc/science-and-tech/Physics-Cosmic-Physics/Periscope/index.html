Periscope

An optical instrument that utilizes reflection via plane mirrors or total internal reflection through prisms to allow observation of objects from a concealed, obstructed, or elevated position.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested in UPSC Prelims GS-1 (Science & Technology - Physics/Optics) through direct applications of laws of reflection, critical angles, and military/naval hardware mechanics.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational ray-optics principles governing periscopes including reflection and prism alignment.
  • Evaluate structural differences between simple tube periscopes and advanced submarine optical/photonic periscopes.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims optical and physics application questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Laws of Reflection
  • Total Internal Reflection (TIR)
  • Ray Optics and Wavefronts
