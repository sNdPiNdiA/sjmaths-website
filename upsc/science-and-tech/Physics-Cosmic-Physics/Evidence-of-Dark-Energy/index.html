Evidence Of Dark Energy

Dark Energy is a hypothetical, pervasive form of energy that exerts a negative pressure, acting as a repulsive force to drive the accelerated expansion of the universe. It constitutes approximately 68-70% of the total mass-energy density of the cosmos.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Science & Tech (Space Physics) due to its role in the Standard Cosmological Model (Lambda-CDM) and its connection to major international space missions like Euclid and James Webb.
