Wireless Charging

Wireless charging (or inductive charging) is a technology that transfers electrical energy from a power source to a receiving device without a physical cable, typically utilizing electromagnetic fields and resonant inductive coupling.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science and Technology (GS Paper III) covering emerging technologies, energy efficiency, Internet of Things (IoT), Electric Vehicles (EVs), and telecommunications standards.
