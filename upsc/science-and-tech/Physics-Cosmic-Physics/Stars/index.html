Stars

A star is a massive, luminous celestial body composed of plasma held together by its own gravity, generating energy through nuclear fusion in its core.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (GS-1) under Cosmic Physics, stellar evolution, and basic astronomy; relevant for Mains (GS-3 Science & Technology) regarding space exploration and space missions.
