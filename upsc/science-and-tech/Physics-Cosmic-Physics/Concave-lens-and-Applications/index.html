Concave Lens And Applications

A concave lens is a diverging lens thinner at its center than at its edges, causing parallel light rays to refract outward and diverge away from a common virtual focal point.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims General Science under optics, covering image formation, ray diagrams, sign conventions, and medical applications like correcting myopia (nearsightedness).
