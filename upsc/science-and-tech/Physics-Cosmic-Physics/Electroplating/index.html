Electroplating

Electroplating is an electrolytic process in which a thin layer of metal is deposited onto the surface of a conductive object (substrate) by passing a direct electrical current through an aqueous solution containing metal ions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Physics/Chemistry interface, everyday applications, industrial chemistry) and Mains (GS-3 Industrial Pollution, Circular Economy, and Green Technologies).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
