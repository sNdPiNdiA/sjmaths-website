Hypermetropia Correction Convex Lens

Hypermetropia, or long-sightedness, is a common optical defect of the eye where distant objects are seen clearly but near objects appear blurred, corrected optically using a converging (convex) lens.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (General Science - Physics optics, human eye defects) and relevant for public health governance discussions under National Programme for Control of Blindness and Visual Impairment (NPCBVI) in Mains GS-2.
