Interstellar

Interstellar refers to the physical space, matter, and radiation existing between star systems within a galaxy, comprised of the Interstellar Medium (ISM) made of gas and cosmic dust.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested under Science and Technology (Space Physics, Cosmic Phenomena, Astronomy) in Prelims through conceptual questions on cosmic rays, nebulae, and stellar lifecycle, and in Mains regarding India's advancements in astronomy and astrophysics.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Interstellar space and the Interstellar Medium (ISM).
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, classification of ISM phases, and institutional frameworks of space exploration.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on cosmic physics.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Astrophysics
  • Stellar Evolution
  • Cosmology Fundamentals
