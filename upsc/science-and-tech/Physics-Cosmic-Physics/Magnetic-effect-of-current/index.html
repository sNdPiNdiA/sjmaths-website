Magnetic Effect Of Current

The Magnetic Effect of Current refers to the phenomenon where an electric current flowing through a conductor produces a magnetic field around it, first observed by Hans Christian Oersted in 1820.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic forms the foundational physics base for understanding electromagnetic induction, electric motors, generators, MRI machines, and particle accelerators, frequently tested in Prelims through direct concept-application questions.
