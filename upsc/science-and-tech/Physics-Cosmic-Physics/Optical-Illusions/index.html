Optical Illusions

An optical illusion is characterized by visually perceived images that differ from objective reality, arising due to physiological mechanisms of the human visual system or cognitive misinterpretations of sensory inputs.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested frequently in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (Physics/Biology overlapping concepts like total internal reflection, refraction, and human eye anatomy) and in Mains GS-3 for applications of science in daily life and cognitive phenomena.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Optical Illusions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Human eye anatomy and neural processing
