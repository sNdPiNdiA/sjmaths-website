Hypermetropia Farsightedness Correction Convex Lens

Hypermetropia (Long-sightedness) is a refractive error where light rays from nearby objects focus behind the retina instead of on it, causing blurred near vision. Correction is achieved using a convex (converging) lens to increase the refractive power of the eye.

Frequently tested in General Science (Physics) section of Prelims; relates to human physiology, optics, and public health initiatives like the National Programme for Control of Blindness.
