White Dwarf

A white dwarf is the extremely dense stellar remnant left over by the collapse of a low-to-intermediate-mass star (below approximately 8 solar masses) after it has exhausted its nuclear fuel.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested in UPSC CSE Prelims under Science & Technology (Space Physics, Astrophysics) through concepts like electron degeneracy pressure, Chandrasekhar limit, and cosmological distance indicators.
