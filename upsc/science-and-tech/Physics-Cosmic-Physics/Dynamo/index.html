Dynamo

A dynamo is an electrical generator that creates direct current (DC) using a commutator, converting mechanical rotation into electrical energy through electromagnetic induction.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested extensively in Prelims under General Science (physics principles, electromagnetic induction) and in Mains GS Paper 3 (Science and Tech - indigenous technology, energy infrastructure applications).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
