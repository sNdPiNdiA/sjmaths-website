Mirage

A naturally occurring optical phenomenon in which light rays are bent via refraction to produce a displaced or distorted image of distant objects or the sky, caused by varying refractive indices in layers of air of different temperatures.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (GS-1 Science & Tech / Physics) via direct or applied questions on atmospheric refraction, total internal reflection, and optical illusions.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
