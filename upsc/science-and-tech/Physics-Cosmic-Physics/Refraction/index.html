Refraction

Refraction is the bending of light or wave propagation when it passes obliquely from one transparent medium into another due to a change in its phase velocity caused by differing optical densities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested extensively in UPSC CSE Prelims via everyday optical phenomena (twinkling of stars, mirages, advanced sunrise and delayed sunset) and applied Science & Tech frameworks.
