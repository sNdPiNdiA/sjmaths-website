Motor

An electromechanical device that converts electrical energy into mechanical energy, operating primarily on the fundamental principles of electromagnetism, specifically Lorentz force and electromagnetic induction.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for Science & Technology (GS-3: Applications of Science, Emerging Technologies, Energy Infrastructure, Electric Mobility, and Industrial Automation). Frequently tested in Prelims via core physics principles (Fleming's rules, induction) and application-based tech (BLDC motors, EV propulsion).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of AC, DC, and BLDC motors.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, manufacturing ecosystems, and national policy dimensions like FAME and PLI schemes.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding motor types and electromagnetic rules.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic electromagnetism
  • Faraday's laws of induction
  • Lorentz force equation
