Astigmatism

A common and generally treatable imperfection in the eye's curvature that causes blurred, distorted, or doubled vision at all distances due to uneven focusing of light rays.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested under General Science (Physics in Biology context / Human Eye optics) in UPSC Prelims, and relevant for public health policies in Mains GS-2 (Health infrastructure).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and optical mechanisms of Astigmatism.
  • Evaluate clinical classifications, diagnostic methods, and corrective measures.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic geometric optics (reflection, refraction, lenses)
  • Anatomy of the human eye (cornea, lens, retina)
