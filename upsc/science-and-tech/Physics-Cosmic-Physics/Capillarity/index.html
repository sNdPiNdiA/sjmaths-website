Capillarity

Capillarity is the spontaneous rise or fall of a liquid in a narrow tube (capillary) due to the interplay of cohesive and adhesive forces, governed by surface tension.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims under General Science (Physics) to evaluate conceptual clarity on fluid mechanics, surface tension, and real-world natural phenomena.
