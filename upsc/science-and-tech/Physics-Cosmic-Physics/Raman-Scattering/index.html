Raman Scattering

Raman scattering is the inelastic scattering of a photon by molecules, resulting in a change in frequency and energy of the scattered photons, discovered by Indian physicist Sir C.V. Raman in 1928.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for UPSC CSE Science & Technology syllabus under physics, space/cosmic physics, and applications in nanotechnology, forensics, pharmaceuticals, and industrial material characterization.
