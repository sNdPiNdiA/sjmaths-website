Basics Of Nanoscience

Nanoscience is the study of phenomena and manipulation of materials at atomic, molecular, and macromolecular scales, where properties differ significantly from those at a bulk scale. The nanoscale typically ranges from 1 to 100 nanometers.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC GS Paper III (Science and Technology - Awareness in the fields of IT, Space, Computers, robotics, nano-technology, bio-technology). Evaluated through direct factual Prelims MCQs on quantum confinement and macroscopic applications, and analytical Mains questions on governance, toxicity, and industrial transformation.
