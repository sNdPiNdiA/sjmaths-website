Big Data Initiative And Privacy

Big Data refers to datasets characterized by extreme volume, velocity, variety, veracity, and value that require advanced computational architectures for processing, while privacy entails the governance of personal data collection, processing, storage, and cross-border flows under strict legal frameworks.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services GS Paper III (Science & Technology - IT, Cyber Security, AI, Governance) and GS Paper II (Polity & Constitution - Right to Privacy, State Surveillance, Digital Governance). Frequently tested in Prelims via institutional mechanisms and Mains via ethical dilemmas of data monetization vs. citizen privacy.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
