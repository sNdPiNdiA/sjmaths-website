Nanomaterials

Nanomaterials are materials with structural features at least in one dimension falling in the nanoscale range, typically between 1 and 100 nanometers, exhibiting novel physical, chemical, and biological properties distinct from their bulk counterparts.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology syllabus (Prelims: direct applications, quantum dots, carbon nanotubes; Mains: GS-3 internal security, industrial manufacturing, environmental impact, ethical challenges, and indigenous technological capabilities under National Nanotechnology Mission).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Nanomaterials.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Understanding of solid-state structures and quantum mechanics principles
