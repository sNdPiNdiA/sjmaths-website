Government Initiatives In ICT

Government initiatives in Information and Communication Technology (ICT) encompass policy frameworks, digital infrastructure missions, and e-governance platforms launched by the state to bridge the digital divide, ensure transparent service delivery, and foster a knowledge-based economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper-II (Governance, E-governance applications, transparency, accountability) and General Studies Paper-III (Science & Technology - awareness in IT, computers, robotics, AI, cyber security, and indigenous technology development). Frequently tested in Prelims via specific portal launches, nodal ministries, and scheme objectives.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
