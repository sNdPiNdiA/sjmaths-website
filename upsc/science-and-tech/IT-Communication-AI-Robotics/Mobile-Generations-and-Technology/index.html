Mobile Generations And Technology

Mobile generations refer to the successive evolutionary stages of wireless cellular communication technology, categorized by distinct spectrum bands, data transfer speeds, switching techniques, and access technologies (from analog 1G to ultra-low latency 5G and future 6G).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services GS Paper III (Science & Technology - IT, Computers, Telecommunications, Robotics, AI integration) and Prelims regarding spectrum allocation, indigenous telecom manufacturing (Bharat 6G, 5G test beds), and digital inclusion.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Mobile Generations And Technology from 1G to 5G/6G.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, spectrum auctions, institutional frameworks (TRAI, DoT), and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding speeds, frequencies, and standards.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of electromagnetic spectrum, analog vs digital signals, and packet switching.
