Types And Applications Of LASERS

LASER stands for Light Amplification by Stimulated Emission of Radiation, producing a highly directional, monochromatic, and coherent beam of light.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (advanced physics, defense applications, and communication tech) and in Mains GS-3 (achievements of Indians in science & technology, indigenous tech development, and security applications).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, emission mechanisms, and properties of LASERs.
  • Evaluate different classifications of LASERs (Gas, Solid-state, Liquid, Semiconductor) and their specific industrial, medical, and defense applications.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements and structure Mains answers on cutting-edge optical technologies.
