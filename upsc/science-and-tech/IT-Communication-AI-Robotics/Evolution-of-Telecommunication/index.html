Evolution Of Telecommunication

The historical and technological transition of transmitting information over significant distances via electromagnetic signals, shifting from analog wire-based telegraphy and telephony to modern digital, wireless, broadband, and 5G/6G communication systems.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology (GS-3) under IT, Communication, and Digital Infrastructure. Evaluated through the lens of digital inclusion, socio-economic transformation, broadband penetration, national security (telecom spectrum management), and critical infrastructure reforms.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Evolution Of Telecommunication from 1G to 5G/6G.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, regulatory bodies (TRAI, DoT), and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding generations, spectrum bands, and policy acts.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of electromagnetic spectrum, waves, and digital data transmission.
