Applications Of Robotics

The deployment of programmable, autonomous, or semi-autonomous mechanical systems integrated with artificial intelligence, sensors, and actuators to perform complex physical tasks across industrial, medical, agricultural, and security domains.

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper 3 (Science & Technology - Awareness in the fields of IT, Space, Computers, Robotics, Nanotechnology, Bio-technology) and ethical dimensions in GS Paper 4.
