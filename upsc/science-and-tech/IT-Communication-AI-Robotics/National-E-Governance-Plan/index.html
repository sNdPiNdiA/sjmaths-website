National E Governance Plan

The National e-Governance Plan (NeGP), formulated by the Department of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) and Department of Administrative Reforms and Public Grievances (DARPG), is a comprehensive initiative aiming to make all government services accessible to the common man in his locality, through common service delivery outlets and ensure efficiency, transparency, and reliability of such services at affordable costs.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Exam across Prelims (Government schemes, Digital India ecosystem, institutional bodies like NeGD) and Mains GS Paper II (Governance, e-governance applications, transparency, accountability, citizen-centric administration) and GS Paper III (IT, cyber security, digital infrastructure).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Basic understanding of IT infrastructure and digital public goods
