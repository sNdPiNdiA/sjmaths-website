Computer Terminology And Fundamental

Computers are programmable electronic devices that process input data into meaningful output information through a sequence of arithmetic and logical operations governed by stored instructions (software). The fundamental architecture revolves around the von Neumann model, featuring a Central Processing Unit (CPU), memory, input/output mechanisms, and secondary storage.
