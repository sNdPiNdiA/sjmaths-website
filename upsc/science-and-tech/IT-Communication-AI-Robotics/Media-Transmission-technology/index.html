Media Transmission Technology

The systematic process of sending, propagating, and receiving analog or digital information signals (audio, video, data) across physical or wireless communication channels over geographic distances.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly links to Science & Technology (IT, Communication, Space tech), national security, digital divide, public broadcasting reforms (Prasar Bharati), spectrum management, and digital infrastructure expansion under GS Paper III.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Media Transmission Technology.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of Electromagnetic Spectrum
  • Digital vs. Analog Signals
  • Telecommunication Networks
