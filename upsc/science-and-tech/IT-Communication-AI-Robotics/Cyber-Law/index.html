Cyber Law

Cyber Law encompasses the legal framework, regulations, and statutes governing the digital sphere, internet usage, electronic commerce, software, and information security.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (Information Technology Acts, Data Protection laws, CERT-In guidelines) and Mains GS-3 (Internal Security, Cyber Warfare, Critical Infrastructure Protection, and Policy Frameworks).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Cyber Law.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of computer networks and internet protocols
  • Familiarity with Indian polity and legislative process
