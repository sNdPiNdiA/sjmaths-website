What Is Nanoscience And Nanotechnology

Nanoscience is the study of structures and materials on the scale of 1 to 100 nanometers (nm), where unique physical, chemical, and biological properties emerge. Nanotechnology is the application of this science to design, produce, and manipulate structures at the nanoscale for specific functional purposes.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व
