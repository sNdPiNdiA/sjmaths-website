Types Of Computers

Classification of computational systems based on their data handling methods (analog, digital, hybrid), size, architecture, and processing capacity (supercomputers, mainframes, minicomputers, microcomputers).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly tested in UPSC Prelims under Science and Technology (IT & Computing advancements) and relevant for Mains GS-3 applications in national supercomputing missions, artificial intelligence data processing, and digital governance architecture.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and operational mechanisms of all types of computers.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution of India's supercomputing infrastructure and policy frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on computer classification.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Binary number system fundamentals
