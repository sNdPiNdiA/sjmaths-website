Ministry Of Communication And Information Technology

The Ministry of Communications and Information Technology was a former Indian government ministry that was bifurcated in July 2016 into the Ministry of Communications and the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY). It served as the nodal agency for formulating policies, promoting manufacturing, delivering digital infrastructure, and regulating telecommunications, postal services, and IT governance.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper-3 (Science & Technology - IT, Computers, Robotics, Digital Infrastructure, e-Governance) and GS Paper-2 (Governance, Government Policies & Interventions), testing institutional evolution, digital public infrastructure, and policy frameworks.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational administrative evolution and bifurcation of the erstwhile Ministry of Communications and IT.
  • Evaluate institutional frameworks including Department of Telecommunications (DoT), Department of Posts (DoP), and MeitY.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding digital India initiatives and statutory bodies.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Indian administrative machinery and government departments
  • Fundamental awareness of India's telecom and IT sectors
