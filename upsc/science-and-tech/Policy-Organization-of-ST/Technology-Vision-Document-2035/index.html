Technology Vision Document 2035

A comprehensive strategic roadmap released by TIFAC (Technology Information, Forecasting and Assessment Council) under the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, charting out technology trajectories across 12 crucial national domains to transform India into a developed, self-reliant nation by 2035.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly relevant for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper III (Science and Technology - Policy, Planning, and Indigenous Technology Development) as well as Prelims regarding national science policies, foresight exercises, and strategic developmental goals.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
