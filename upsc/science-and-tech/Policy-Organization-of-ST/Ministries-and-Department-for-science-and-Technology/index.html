Ministries And Department For Science And Technology

The institutional framework comprising the Ministry of Science and Technology along with its core departments (DST, DSIR, and DBT) mandated to formulate, implement, and coordinate science, technology, and innovation policies in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Prelims (factual questions on autonomous bodies, statutory mandates, and nodal ministries) and Mains GS-3 (Science & Technology - indigenization of technology, policy, and awareness in fields of IT, Space, Biotech, Nanotech).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
