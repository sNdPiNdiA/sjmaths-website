National Intellectual Property Rights Policy

Adopted in May 2016, India's National Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) Policy is a comprehensive institutional framework titled 'Creative India; Innovative India' designed to stimulate innovation, protect intellectual property, and foster entrepreneurship across economic sectors.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper-3 (Science and Technology, Economic Development, Intellectual Property Rights) and Prelims regarding institutional bodies, international treaties (TRIPS), and domestic statutory frameworks.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Intellectual Property Rights Policy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
