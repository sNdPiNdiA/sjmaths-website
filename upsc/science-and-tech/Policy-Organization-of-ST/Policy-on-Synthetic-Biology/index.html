Policy On Synthetic Biology

Synthetic biology is a multidisciplinary field of science and engineering that combines biology and engineering to design and construct new biological parts, devices, and systems, or redesign existing natural biological systems for useful purposes. India's evolving policy landscape aims to harness this transformative technology while ensuring rigorous biosafety, ethical oversight, and biosecurity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Exam under General Studies Paper-3 (Science and Technology - Awareness in the fields of IT, Space, Computers, robotics, Nano-technology, bio-technology and issues relating to intellectual property rights). It intersects with national security (biosecurity), international relations (UN Convention on Biological Diversity), ethics (bioethics), and economic development (bioeconomy).
