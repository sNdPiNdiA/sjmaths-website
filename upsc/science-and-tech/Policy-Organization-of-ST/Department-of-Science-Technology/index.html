Department Of Science Technology

The Department of Science and Technology (DST), operating under the Ministry of Science and Technology, Government of India, is the nodal agency for formulating policies, promoting, funding, and coordinating frontier research, science popularization, and technology development in the country.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (factual questions on autonomous bodies, autonomous research institutes, national portals, and flagship schemes) and Mains GS Paper III (Science & Technology - indigenization of technology, capacity building, R&D funding, policy frameworks, and applications of S&T for national development).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Department Of Science Technology.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Overview of Ministry of Science and Technology components
