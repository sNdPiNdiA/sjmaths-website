Awards Related To Science

National and international honors, medals, and monetary prizes conferred to recognize outstanding contributions by scientists, researchers, and technologists in advancing scientific knowledge, innovation, and technological development.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims through factual matching of awards, ministries, and categories (e.g., Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize, Rashtriya Vigyan Puraskar). In Mains (GS Paper III), understanding these awards highlights India's institutional capacity, incentives for R&D, and promotion of indigenous scientific temper.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Science and Technology Awards in India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions of national science honors.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding eligibility and categories.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of India's Science, Technology and Innovation (STI) ecosystem
  • Structure of Council of Scientific and Industrial Research (CSIR) and Department of Science and Technology (DST)
