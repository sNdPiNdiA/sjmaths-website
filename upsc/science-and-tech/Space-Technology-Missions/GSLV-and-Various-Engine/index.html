GSLV And Various Engine

Geosynchronous Satellite Launch Vehicle (GSLV) is a three-stage heavy-lift launch vehicle developed by ISRO designed to place heavier payloads like communication satellites into Geosynchronous Transfer Orbit (GTO), characterized by its critical indigenous cryogenic upper stage.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology syllabus under Space Technology, achievements of Indians in science & tech, and technology indigenization. Frequently tested in Prelims regarding rocket stages, cryogenic fuels, and engine types (S139, Vikas, CUD/CE-7.5, CE-20), and in Mains for strategic self-reliance.
