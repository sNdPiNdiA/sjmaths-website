Cryogenic Rockets

A cryogenic rocket utilizes a cryogenic rocket engine that operates with propellants stored at extremely low temperatures (liquefied gases), providing exceptionally high specific impulse and enabling deep-space missions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology (GS-III) covering achievements in Space Technology, indigenous technology development, geopolitical denials (MTCR context), and heavier payload capabilities for GSLV and LVM3 missions.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and thermodynamic mechanisms of Cryogenic Rockets.
  • Evaluate ISRO's technological evolution, indigenous development, and spatial research infrastructure.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics and avoid common UPSC examiner traps regarding propellants and engine cycles.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Newtonian physics
  • Rocket propulsion fundamentals
  • Chemical propellants
