Earth Observation Systems

Earth Observation Systems (EOS) comprise space-borne and airborne satellite payloads designed to monitor, map, and collect quantitative and qualitative data about Earth's physical, chemical, and biological systems.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Prelims (Science & Tech, ISRO missions, remote sensing orbits) and Mains GS-3 (Space Technology, Disaster Management, Agriculture, and Internal Security applications).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Earth Observation Systems.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Physics (electromagnetic spectrum)
  • Orbital Mechanics (LEO, SSO, GEO)
  • Remote Sensing Principles
