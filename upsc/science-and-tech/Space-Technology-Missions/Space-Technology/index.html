Space Technology

Space technology encompasses the hardware, software, and operational methodologies used for space exploration, satellite deployment, orbital mechanics, and remote sensing applications. It is a critical enabler of socio-economic development, national security, and scientific discovery.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Space Technology features consistently in UPSC Prelims (satellites, launch vehicles, orbits, international treaties) and Mains GS Paper-3 (Achievements of Indians in science & technology, indigenization of technology, and developing new technology).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Space Technology.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Physics (Gravity, Momentum, Thermodynamics)
  • General Science (Electromagnetic Spectrum, Orbital Mechanics)
