Orbits Type

An orbit is the curved path of a celestial object or spacecraft around a star, planet, or moon, dictated by gravitational forces and centrifugal balance. In space technology missions, orbit types determine a satellite's coverage, resolution, latency, and application scope ranging from Earth observation to deep-space navigation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC CSE Prelims via direct factual matching (e.g., altitude, inclination, application of LEO, MEO, GEO, SSO, and Lagrange points) and in Mains (GS Paper III: Space Technology advancements, dual-use capabilities, satellite communication security, and debris mitigation).
