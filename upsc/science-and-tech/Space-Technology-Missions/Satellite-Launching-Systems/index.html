Satellite Launching Systems

Satellite Launching Systems comprise multi-stage rocket vehicles powered by chemical, cryogenic, or electric propulsion designed to overcome Earth's gravitational pull and place payloads into specific orbital trajectories.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology syllabus under GS Paper III (Space Technology, indigenous capabilities, commercial space sector reforms), frequently tested for technical propulsion mechanisms, orbital mechanics, and ISRO's launch vehicle evolution.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Satellite Launching Systems.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Newtonian physics
  • Orbital mechanics basics
  • General awareness of ISRO missions
