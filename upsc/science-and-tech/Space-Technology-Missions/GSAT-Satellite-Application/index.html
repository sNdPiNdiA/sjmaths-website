GSAT Satellite Application

GSAT (Geostationary Satellite) series comprises India's indigenous, advanced communication satellites developed by ISRO to achieve self-reliance in broadcasting, telecommunications, meteorological observations, and strategic defense applications.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly tested in Prelims (specific satellite names, launch vehicles, orbital parameters, band frequencies) and Mains GS-3 (Science & Technology - achievements of Indians in science & technology; indigenous technology and development of new technology).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of GSAT Satellite Application.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Orbital mechanics basics (LEO, MEO, GEO)
  • Electromagnetic spectrum and frequency bands (C, Ku, Ka, S-band)
