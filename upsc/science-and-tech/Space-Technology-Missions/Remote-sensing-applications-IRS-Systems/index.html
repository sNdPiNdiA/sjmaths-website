Remote Sensing Applications IRS Systems

India's indigenous Remote Sensing (IRS) satellite series, managed by ISRO, constitutes one of the largest civilian remote sensing satellite constellations in the world, providing multi-spectral, multi-resolution data for Earth observation, resource management, and disaster governance.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly critical for Prelims (ISRO missions, sensor types, orbits, applications) and Mains GS-3 (Science & Tech - space applications, disaster management, agriculture, security, and GIS governance).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Remote Sensing Applications IRS Systems.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic physics of electromagnetic spectrum
  • Fundamentals of orbits (Sun-Synchronous vs Geostationary)
  • Basic Indian geography and resource mapping
