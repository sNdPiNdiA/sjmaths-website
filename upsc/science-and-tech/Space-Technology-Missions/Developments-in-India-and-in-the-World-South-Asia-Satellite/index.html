Developments In India And In The World South Asia Satellite

The South Asia Satellite (officially designated as GSAT-9) is a geostationary communication and meteorology satellite launched by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) to provide various communication, broadcasting, and disaster management applications to South Asian Association for Regional Cooperation (SAARC) member nations.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science and Technology (GS Paper III - Space Technology, India's Space Diplomacy, Soft Power, International Relations in South Asia, and Neighborhood First Policy).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational technical specifications and orbital dynamics of GSAT-9.
  • Evaluate geopolitical implications of India's space diplomacy and the 'Neighborhood First' policy.
  • Analyze regional benefits including tele-education, telemedicine, and disaster warning systems.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Geostationary orbits
  • India's foreign policy framework in South Asia
