INSAT Satellite Application

The Indian National Satellite (INSAT) system is a series of multipurpose geostationary satellites launched by ISRO to satisfy the country's telecommunications, broadcasting, meteorology, and search-and-rescue needs.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology (Space Technology, indigenous satellite programs, socio-economic applications, disaster management) in GS Paper III and analytical essay topics.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
