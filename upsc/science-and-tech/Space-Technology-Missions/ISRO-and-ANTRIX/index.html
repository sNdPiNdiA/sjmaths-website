ISRO And ANTRIX

ISRO (Indian Space Research Organisation) is India's national space agency under the Department of Space (DoS), while Antrix Corporation Limited is its wholly-owned commercial arm incorporated to market space products and services globally.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper III (Science and Technology - Space Technology, Achievements of Indians in Science & Tech, Commercialization of Space, and Institutional Frameworks). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding corporate entities of DoS and international commercial agreements.
