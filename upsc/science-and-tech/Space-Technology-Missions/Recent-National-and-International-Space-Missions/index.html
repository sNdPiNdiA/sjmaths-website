Recent National And International Space Missions

Recent national and international space missions encompass state-led and commercial ventures launched by global space agencies (ISRO, NASA, ESA, CNSA, JAXA) focusing on lunar exploration, deep-space probes, Earth observation, and sustainable space utilization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (factual identification of launch vehicles, orbits, mission objectives, instruments) and Mains GS Paper III (Science & Technology - achievements of Indians in science & technology, space technology applications, international cooperation, and commercialization).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Recent National And International Space Missions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic orbital mechanics
  • Launch vehicle evolution (PSLV, GSLV, LVM3, Artemis Accords)
  • Basic astronomy
