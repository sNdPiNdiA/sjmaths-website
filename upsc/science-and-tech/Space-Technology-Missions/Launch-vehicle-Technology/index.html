Launch Vehicle Technology

Launch vehicle technology encompasses the engineering systems, propulsion mechanisms, and guidance frameworks utilized to propel payloads—such as satellites and crewed spacecraft—from Earth's surface into designated orbits against gravitational forces.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Science & Technology (GS Paper III) covering space missions, indigenous capabilities, commercial space policy, and strategic dual-use aerospace technologies.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Launch Vehicle Technology.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic physics of motion and gravity
  • Understanding of orbital mechanics (LEO, MEO, GEO)
