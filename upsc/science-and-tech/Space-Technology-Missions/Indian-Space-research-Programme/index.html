Indian Space Research Programme

The Indian Space Research Programme is a state-sponsored, application-driven national space framework steered by the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) under the Department of Space (DoS), aimed at socio-economic development through indigenous space technology.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Science & Technology - Awareness in Space Technology, achievements of Indians in science & tech, technology indigenization) and Prelims (ISRO centers, launch vehicles, satellite applications, and recent space reforms).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, milestones, and propulsion mechanisms of the Indian Space Programme.
  • Evaluate institutional frameworks, spatial distribution of centers, and recent private sector space reforms.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding launch vehicles and orbits.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Physics
  • Orbital Mechanics
  • Basic Geography of India
