Developments In India And In The World Solar Mission ADITYA

Aditya-L1 is India's maiden dedicated solar space mission designed to place a spacecraft in a halo orbit around the Lagrangian Point 1 (L1) of the Sun-Earth system to continuously observe the Sun without occultation or eclipses.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Science and Technology - Space Technology, indigenous space capabilities, space exploration, and solar physics). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding Lagrange points, orbits, payload objectives, and global solar missions.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and orbital mechanics of the Aditya-L1 mission and global solar observatories.
  • Evaluate spatial positioning at Lagrangian Point 1 (L1) and institutional frameworks of ISRO and international space agencies.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding payloads and solar atmospheric layers.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic orbital mechanics
  • Solar atmosphere structure (Photosphere, Chromosphere, Corona)
  • ISRO launch vehicles (PSLV)
