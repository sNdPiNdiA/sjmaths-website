Money Function And Classification

Money is any universally accepted medium of exchange that standardizes value, acts as a deferred payment standard, and stores wealth over time. Its classification ranges from commodity and fiat money to narrow and broad money aggregates.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding monetary aggregates (M1, M2, M3, M4), liquidity positions, and RBI's structural framework; highly relevant for Mains GS-3 (Indian Economy, Monetary Policy, and Inflation).
