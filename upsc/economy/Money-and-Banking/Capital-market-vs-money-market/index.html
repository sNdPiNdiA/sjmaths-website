Capital Market Vs Money Market

The financial system is bifurcated into the money market, which handles short-term funding and liquidity needs (up to one year), and the capital market, which channels medium to long-term funds for permanent capital formation and investments exceeding one year.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding instruments, regulators (RBI vs SEBI), and liquidity management, as well as in Mains (GS-3 Mobilization of Resources, Growth, and Development).
