Money Multiplier

The money multiplier is the ratio of the total money supply (M) to the monetary base or reserve money (H), illustrating how initial currency injections by the central bank expand through commercial bank lending.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding RBI's monetary policy transmission, liquidity management, and components of money supply (M1, M2, M3, M4). Useful in Mains GS-3 for questions on inflation, banking sector health, credit growth, and economic stimulus.
