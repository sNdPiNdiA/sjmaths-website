Digital Money

Digital money refers to monetary value that is stored, managed, and exchanged exclusively through electronic computer systems, networks, and digital wallets, possessing no mandatory physical counterpart unless converted into fiat cash. It spans centralized commercial bank money, decentralized cryptocurrencies, and state-backed Central Bank Digital Currencies (CBDCs).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This topic is vital for UPSC CSE Prelims (monetary policy, RBI instruments, cryptocurrency regulations, and fintech indicators) and Mains GS Paper III (financial inclusion, mobilization of resources, digital economy, and macroeconomic stability).
