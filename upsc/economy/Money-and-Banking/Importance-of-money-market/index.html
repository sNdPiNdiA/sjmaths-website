Importance Of Money Market

The money market is the financial market where high-liquidity, short-term financial instruments with maturities ranging from overnight up to one year are traded, serving as the core engine for equilibrating short-term liquidity deficits and surpluses for banks, corporate houses, and the government.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding short-term instruments (Treasury Bills, Commercial Paper, Certificate of Deposit, Call Money) and RBI monetary policy transmission; crucial for Mains GS Paper III under 'Indian Economy and issues relating to Mobilisation of Resources'.
