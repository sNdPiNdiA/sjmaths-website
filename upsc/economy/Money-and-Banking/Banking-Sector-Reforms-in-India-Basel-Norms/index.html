Banking Sector Reforms In India Basel Norms

Banking sector reforms in India encompass structural, operational, and regulatory changes initiated post-1991 to enhance financial stability, efficiency, and competitiveness, heavily anchored in international capital adequacy standards known as Basel Accords formulated by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision (BCBS).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (RBI guidelines, Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio [CRAR], Basel III parameters, Prompt Corrective Action [PCA]) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, mobilization of resources, growth, development, and banking sector vulnerability/NPAs).
