Regional Rural Banks

Regional Rural Banks (RRBs) are state-level government sponsored banking institutions in India operating at regional levels in different States, established to provide credit and other facilities to small and marginal farmers, agricultural laborers, artisans, and small entrepreneurs.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (institutional framework, equity shareholding structure, statutory backing under RRB Act 1976) and Mains GS Paper III (financial inclusion, rural credit delivery, priority sector lending, banking sector reforms).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, statutory origins, and mechanisms of Regional Rural Banks.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, equity holding patterns, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on banking inclusion.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Indian Banking Structure
  • Priority Sector Lending
  • Financial Inclusion Policies
