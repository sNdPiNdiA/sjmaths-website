Banking Ombudsman

The Banking Ombudsman Scheme is a quasi-judicial apex grievance redressal mechanism established under Section 35A of the Banking Regulation Act, 1949, enabling bank customers to resolve complaints relating to deficiency in banking services.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding jurisdiction, appeal mechanism, and institutional integration under the Reserve Bank-Integrated Ombudsman Scheme (RB-IOS). Crucial for Mains GS Paper III (Inclusive Growth, Financial Inclusion, and Consumer Protection).
