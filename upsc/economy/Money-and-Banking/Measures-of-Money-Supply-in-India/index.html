Measures Of Money Supply In India

Money supply refers to the total volume of money held by the public at a particular point of time in an economy, categorized by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) using traditional (M1, M2, M3, M4) and monetary aggregate (NM0, NM1, NM2, NM3) frameworks.

Crucial core microtopic for UPSC Prelims (direct MCQs on liquidity order, components of M1/M3, RBI working group recommendations) and Mains GS-3 (Monetary policy transmission, inflation control, liquidity management).
