Financial Market And Its Instruments Concept And Functions

A financial market is a marketplace where buyers and sellers trade financial assets such as equities, bonds, currencies, and derivatives, facilitating capital formation, price discovery, and liquidity. It is broadly divided into Money Market (short-term funds up to one year) and Capital Market (long-term funds exceeding one year).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (direct questions on money market instruments, SEBI/RBI regulations, G-Secs, T-Bills) and Mains GS Paper III (mobilization of resources, growth, development, and inclusive finance).
