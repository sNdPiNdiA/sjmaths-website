Banking In India Scheduled Commercial Banks

Scheduled Commercial Banks (SCBs) in India comprise all banks included in the Second Schedule of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) Act, 1934, satisfying criteria of minimum paid-up capital and reserves, and ensuring operations do not adversely affect depositors' interests.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (RBI Act provisions, bank categorization, priority sector lending, capital adequacy norms) and GS Paper III Mains (financial inclusion, banking sector reforms, NPAs, bad bank, digital banking).
