Broad And Narrow Money

Narrow money ($M_1$) represents the most liquid forms of money easily usable for transactions, whereas Broad money ($M_3$) includes both liquid components and less liquid financial assets like time deposits held by the public in the banking system.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding RBI's monetary aggregates, liquidity management, and structural shifts in financial savings; highly relevant for Mains GS-3 questions on monetary policy transmission, inflation control, and financial inclusion.
