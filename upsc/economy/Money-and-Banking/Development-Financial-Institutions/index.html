Development Financial Institutions

Development Financial Institutions (DFIs) are specialized financial intermediaries established to provide long-term risk capital, medium-to-long-term project finance, and advisory services for sectors requiring massive capital outlays with long gestation periods where commercial banks hesitate to lend.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Infrastructure, Financing Models, Investment); frequently tested in Prelims regarding the difference between commercial banks and DFIs, NABEX/NaBFID structures, and historical evolution of IFCI, IDBI, and ICICI.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Development Financial Institutions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of banking, monetary policy, and infrastructure financing.
