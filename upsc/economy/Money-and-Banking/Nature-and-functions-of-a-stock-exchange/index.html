Nature And Functions Of A Stock Exchange

A stock exchange is an organized and regulated financial marketplace where buyers and sellers trade existing shares, debentures, and other securities, providing vital liquidity, capital formation, and price discovery to the economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding regulatory bodies (SEBI), capital market instruments, and secondary market mechanisms. In Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy), it forms the bedrock of resource mobilization, corporate governance, and inclusive growth.
