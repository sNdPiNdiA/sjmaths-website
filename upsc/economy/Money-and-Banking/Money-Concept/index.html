Money Concept

Money is any item or verifiable record that is generally accepted as payment for goods and services and repayment of debts in a given socio-economic context or country. It serves simultaneously as a medium of exchange, a unit of account, a store of value, and a standard of deferred payment.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (monetary aggregates, legal tender, cryptocurrency, CBDC) and Mains GS-3 (monetary policy transmission, financial inclusion, digital economy, inflation control).
