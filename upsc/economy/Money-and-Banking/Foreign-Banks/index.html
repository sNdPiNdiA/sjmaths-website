Foreign Banks

Foreign banks are banking institutions headquartered in a foreign country that operate branches, subsidiaries, or representative offices within India under the regulatory framework of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) and the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding RBI regulations, WTO commitments, Priority Sector Lending (PSL) norms, and Wholly Owned Subsidiary (WOS) models; relevant in Mains for understanding financial globalization, capital flows, and foreign direct investment in the banking sector.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Foreign Banks.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Structure of Indian Banking System
  • Reserve Bank of India Act, 1934
  • Banking Regulation Act, 1949
