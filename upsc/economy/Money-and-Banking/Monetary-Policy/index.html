Monetary Policy

Monetary policy refers to the macroeconomic policy implemented by the central bank (RBI in India) to manage the money supply, interest rates, and overall liquidity in the economy to achieve price stability while keeping in mind economic growth.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (direct policy rates, tools, MPC composition, liquidity management) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, inflation control, central bank autonomy, financial inclusion).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Monetary Policy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
