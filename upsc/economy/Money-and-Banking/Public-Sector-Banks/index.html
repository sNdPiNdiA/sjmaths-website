Public Sector Banks

Public Sector Banks (PSBs) are commercial banks in India where the central government holds a majority stake of more than 50% in the paid-up capital, acting as the backbone of India's financial inclusion, priority sector lending, and monetary transmission mechanism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Banking, Inclusive Growth, Mobilization of Resources). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding banking sector reforms, ownership thresholds, capital adequacy norms, and amalgamation history.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and operational mechanisms of Public Sector Banks.
  • Evaluate historical evolution, spatial distribution, governance frameworks, and consolidation phases.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics and navigate tricky examiner traps in Prelims and Mains.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of Indian Financial System
  • Central Bank (RBI) functions and monetary policy
