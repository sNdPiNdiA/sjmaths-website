Private Banks

Private sector banks are banking financial institutions where the majority stake (more than 50%) is held by private shareholders and individuals rather than the government, operating under the regulatory purview of the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) via the Banking Regulation Act, 1949.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic for UPSC CSE Prelims (RBI guidelines, capital adequacy, differential banking, prompt corrective action) and Mains GS Paper III (financial inclusion, NPAs, consolidation of banking sector, and role of private capital in economic growth).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Private Banks.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Structure of Indian Banking System
  • Role of Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
  • Basics of Monetary Policy and Basel Norms
