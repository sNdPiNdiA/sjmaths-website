Insurance Companies

Insurance companies are specialized financial intermediaries that pool risk from individuals and corporate entities, collecting periodic premiums to indemnify them against specified future losses while mobilizing long-term domestic savings for capital markets.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under Economy (Money and Banking / Financial Sector). Tested in Prelims via institutional mandates (IRDAI, FDI limits, statutory bodies) and in Mains GS-3 regarding financial inclusion, social security schemes, long-term infrastructure financing, and risk mitigation.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Insurance Companies.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
