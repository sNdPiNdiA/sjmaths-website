Nationalized Banks

Commercial banks whose majority ownership (51% or more) and control have been acquired by the Central Government of India through specific legislative enactments to subserve national socio-economic objectives.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding historical phases of nationalization, statutory frameworks, and post-2019 mega-mergers. In Mains (GS Paper III - Economy), nationalized banks form the bedrock of financial inclusion, priority sector lending (PSL), and credit delivery to agriculture and MSMEs.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Nationalized Banks.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Structure of Indian Banking System
  • Reserve Bank of India functions
  • History of planned economic development in India
