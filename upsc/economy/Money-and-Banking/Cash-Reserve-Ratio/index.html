Cash Reserve Ratio

Cash Reserve Ratio (CRR) is the specified minimum fraction of the total deposits of customers that commercial banks have to hold as reserves either in cash with themselves or, more strictly in the Indian context, with the central bank (RBI).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (monetary policy instruments, banking operations, RBI acts) and Mains GS Paper 3 (mobilization of resources, monetary transmission, economic growth vs inflation control).
