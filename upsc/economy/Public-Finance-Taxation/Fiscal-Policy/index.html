Fiscal Policy

Fiscal policy is the use of government taxation and public expenditure to influence macroeconomic conditions, including aggregate demand, employment, inflation, and economic growth.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to General Studies Paper III (Indian Economy & Issues relating to Planning, Mobilization of Resources, Growth, Development, and Employment). Tested heavily in both Prelims (concepts like deficits, FRBM Act, fiscal stimulus) and Mains (fiscal consolidation, capital vs. revenue expenditure, and cooperative federalism).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Fiscal Policy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
