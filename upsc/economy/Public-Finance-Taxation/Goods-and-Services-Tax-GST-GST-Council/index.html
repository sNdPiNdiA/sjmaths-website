Goods And Services Tax GST GST Council

The Goods and Services Tax (GST) is a comprehensive, multi-stage, destination-based indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services, underpinned by the constitutional framework of Article 279A which establishes the GST Council as a federal apex body.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to Indian fiscal federalism, cooperative federalism, tax reforms, macroeconomic stability, and ease of doing business; heavily tested in Prelims (articles, constitutional amendments, tax rates, exemption lists) and Mains (fiscal devolution, compensation cess, implementation challenges, structural reforms).
