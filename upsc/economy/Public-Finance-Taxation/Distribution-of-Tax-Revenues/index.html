Distribution Of Tax Revenues

The constitutional and statutory mechanism through which tax revenues collected by the Union (Central) government are allocated between the Union and the States, and further sub-allocated among individual states based on recommendations of the Finance Commission under Article 280.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to Indian Federalism, Fiscal Federalism, and Centre-State relations. Frequently tested in Prelims (Articles, Finance Commission mandates, tax types) and Mains GS Paper II (Cooperative and Competitive Federalism, vertical and horizontal imbalances).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Distribution Of Tax Revenues under Part XII of the Constitution.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, vertical/horizontal devolution formulas, and institutional frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding Finance Commission criteria.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Constitutional Framework of India (Part XI and XII)
  • Basics of Taxation (Direct vs Indirect Taxes)
  • Role and Function of the Finance Commission
