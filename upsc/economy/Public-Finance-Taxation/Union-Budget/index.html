Union Budget

The Annual Financial Statement mandated under Article 112 of the Indian Constitution, detailing the estimated receipts and expenditures of the Government of India for a financial year.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central microtopic for Economy (GS-3 Public Finance, Fiscal Policy, Taxation) and Polity (Parliamentary Procedure, Constitutional Provisions). Heavily tested via conceptual mechanics of deficits, budget enactment stages, and structural reforms.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Union Budget under Article 112.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic macroeconomic aggregates
  • Constitutional framework of Parliament and executive
