Types Of Taxes

Taxes are compulsory unrequited payments made by individuals and corporations to the government to finance public goods and services. They are broadly classified based on incidence (Direct vs Indirect), economic impact (Progressive, Regressive, Proportional), and constitutional assignment.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental pillar of Indian Economy (GS Paper III - Indian Economy and issues relating to mobilization of resources). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding the incidence of indirect taxes, GST mechanisms, progressive vs regressive taxation, and constitutional distribution of taxing powers under Schedule VII.
