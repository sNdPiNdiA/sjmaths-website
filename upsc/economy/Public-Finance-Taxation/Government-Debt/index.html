Government Debt

Government debt (also known as public debt, national debt, or sovereign debt) represents the total cumulative amount borrowed by the central and state governments through the issuance of securities to meet fiscal deficits, fund public infrastructure, and manage temporary cash flow mismatches.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (FRBM Act, Treasury Bills, G-secs, External vs. Internal Debt, Ways and Means Advances) and Mains (Fiscal federalism, crowding-out effect, debt sustainability, capital vs. revenue expenditure, and macroeconomic stability under GS Paper III).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, classification, and institutional mechanisms of Government Debt in India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, internal vs. external borrowing limits, and institutional frameworks like the FRBM Act.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements and structure comprehensive Mains answers.
