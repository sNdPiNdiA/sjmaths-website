Methods Of Taxation

Methods of taxation refer to the structural ways in which tax liability is distributed across different income groups or economic bases, fundamentally categorized into progressive, regressive, proportional, and digressive systems.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper III (Indian Economy & mobilization of resources, equity vs. efficiency, tax reforms) and Prelims (conceptual clarity on tax incidence, fiscal policy, and structural inequality).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Methods Of Taxation.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
