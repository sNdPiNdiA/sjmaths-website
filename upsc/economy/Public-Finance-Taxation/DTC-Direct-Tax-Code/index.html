DTC Direct Tax Code

The Direct Taxes Code (DTC) was proposed as a comprehensive legislation to replace the archaic Income Tax Act of 1961, aiming to simplify direct tax laws, widen the tax base, reduce litigation, and phase out exemptions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (Taxation, Economic Reforms, Budget) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Indian Economy and issues relating to resource mobilization, tax reforms, and black money).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and historical evolution of the Direct Taxes Code.
  • Evaluate structural differences between the Income Tax Act 1961 and proposed DTC reforms.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding tax administration.
