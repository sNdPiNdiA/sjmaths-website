Revenue Receipts Non Tax Revenue Receipts

Non-tax revenue receipts comprise government earnings from sources other than taxes, such as interest on loans, dividends from public sector undertakings, profits of the Reserve Bank of India, fees, fines, and external grants, which do not create liability or reduce assets.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding government fiscal architecture, fiscal deficit management, and alternative resource mobilization strategies for sustainable public finance in UPSC Prelims and Mains GS Paper III.
