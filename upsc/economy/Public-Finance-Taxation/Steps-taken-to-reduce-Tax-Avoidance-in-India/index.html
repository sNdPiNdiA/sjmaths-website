Steps Taken To Reduce Tax Avoidance In India

Tax avoidance refers to the use of legal methods to minimize the amount of income tax owed, typically by exploiting loopholes, ambiguities, or gaps in domestic and international tax laws. India has instituted robust legislative, administrative, and international treaty mechanisms to counter aggressive tax planning and base erosion.
