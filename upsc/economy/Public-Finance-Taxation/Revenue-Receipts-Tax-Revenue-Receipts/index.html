Revenue Receipts Tax Revenue Receipts

Revenue receipts are those government receipts which neither create any liability nor cause any reduction in assets. Tax revenue receipts comprise compulsory payments made by individuals and corporations to the government without direct quid pro quo benefits, forming the backbone of the Union and State consolidated funds.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both UPSC Prelims (distinguishing between revenue and capital receipts, direct vs indirect taxes, tax buoyancy) and Mains GS Paper III (Fiscal federalism, tax reforms, GST, and resource mobilization).
