Centre State Distribution Finance Commission

A constitutional mechanism established under Article 280 of the Indian Constitution to define financial relations and tax revenue sharing between the Central Government and State Governments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central pillar of Indian Federalism (GS Paper II - Polity & Governance; GS Paper III - Indian Economy & Fiscal Policy). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding vertical vs horizontal devolution formulas and constitutional mandates.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Centre State Distribution Finance Commission.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
