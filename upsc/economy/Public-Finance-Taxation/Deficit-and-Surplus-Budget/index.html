Deficit And Surplus Budget

A budget deficit occurs when government expenditures exceed its total revenues, requiring borrowings or asset sales, whereas a surplus budget arises when revenues surpass expenditures, allowing for debt reduction or reserve accumulation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to Indian Economy syllabus under Public Finance, Fiscal Policy, Macroeconomic stability, and federal fiscal relations; frequently tested in both Prelims (budget metrics, FRBM Act) and Mains (fiscal consolidation, capital expenditure quality).
