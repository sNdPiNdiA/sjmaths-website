Fiscal Responsibility And Budget Management FRBM Act 2003

An enactment of the Parliament of India institutionalized to institutionalize financial discipline, reduce fiscal deficit, macro-economic stability, and mandate prudent management of public funds by the Central Government.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (fiscal targets, statutory definitions, escape clauses) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, budgetary trends, fiscal federalism, cooperative federalism, and macroeconomic stability).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Fiscal Responsibility And Budget Management FRBM Act 2003.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of Indian Public Finance
  • Components of Union Budget (Revenue and Capital accounts)
  • Role of Reserve Bank of India (RBI)
