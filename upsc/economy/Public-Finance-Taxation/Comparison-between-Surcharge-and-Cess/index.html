Comparison Between Surcharge And Cess

A surcharge is an additional tax levied on the existing tax liability (income or corporate tax) of individuals and corporations, while a cess is a special-purpose tax levied on top of basic tax rates to fund specific government welfare schemes.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व
