Service Tax

Service Tax was an indirect tax levied by the Central Government of India on specific services provided or agreed to be provided, which was eventually subsumed under the Goods and Services Tax (GST) regime on July 1, 2017.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding the evolution of India's indirect taxation structure, cooperative federalism, constitutional amendment acts (101st Constitutional Amendment Act, 2016), and fiscal federalism disputes.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, historical evolution, and collection mechanisms of Service Tax.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, constitutional provisions (Article 268A, Residuary powers), and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding indirect tax reform.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of Taxation
  • Indirect Taxes
  • Seventh Schedule of the Indian Constitution
