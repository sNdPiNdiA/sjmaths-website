WTO And Services

The WTO framework governing international trade in services is institutionalized under the General Agreement on Trade in Services (GATS), which came into force in 1995 as a result of the Uruguay Round negotiations.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper III (Economy - Service Sector, Global Trade, International Institutions) and Prelims (GATS modes, plurilateral negotiations, and India's stance at WTO).
