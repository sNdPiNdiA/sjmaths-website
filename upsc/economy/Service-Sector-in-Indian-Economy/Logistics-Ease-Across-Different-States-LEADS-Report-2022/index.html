Logistics Ease Across Different States LEADS Report 2022

The LEADS (Logistics Ease Across Different States) Report 2022 is the 4th edition of an annual evaluation index released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry, Government of India, designed to assess the logistics performance, efficiency of supply chain infrastructure, and regulatory environment across all Indian states and Union Territories.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (government indices, socio-economic indicators, logistics policy) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Infrastructure, Energy, Investment, Supply Chain, and Regional Development).
