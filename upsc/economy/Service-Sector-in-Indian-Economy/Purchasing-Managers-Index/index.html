Purchasing Managers Index

The Purchasing Managers Index (PMI) is an economic indicator based on monthly surveys of private sector companies designed to provide an advance indication of economic trends in the manufacturing and services sectors.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (economic indicators, data sources) and Mains GS Paper III (growth, development, industrial output, and monetary policy transmission).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
