National Investment And Infrastructure Fund

NIIF is India's first quasi-sovereign wealth fund set up by the Government of India in 2015 to maximize economic impact through infrastructure investments in commercially viable projects, both greenfield and brownfield.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Infrastructure, Investment Models, Mobilization of Resources) and Prelims (Economic Governance, Financial Institutions).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Investment And Infrastructure Fund.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें
