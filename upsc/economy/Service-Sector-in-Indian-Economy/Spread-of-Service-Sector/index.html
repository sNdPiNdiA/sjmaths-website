Spread Of Service Sector

The multi-directional, spatial, and structural expansion of tertiary economic activities across various geographical tiers, urban-rural divides, and industrial sub-sectors in India, contributing over 54% to India's Gross Value Added (GVA).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (economic data, sub-sector classification, spatial trends) and Mains GS-3 (inclusive growth, employment elasticity, regional disparities, and digital infrastructure integration).
