IT And Telecom Services

The Information Technology (IT) and Telecommunications sector comprises software development, IT-enabled services (ITeS), business process management (BPM), and wired/wireless telecommunication infrastructure that acts as the digital backbone of India's modern service economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (Digital India, 5G spectrum, AGR, USOF, international tech pacts) and Mains GS-3 (Economy: growth drivers, employment, digital divide, infrastructure, inclusive growth).
