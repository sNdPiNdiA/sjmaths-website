Logistics Performance Index

The Logistics Performance Index (LPI) is an interactive benchmarking tool created by the World Bank that helps countries identify the challenges and opportunities they face in the performance of trade logistics.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC GS Paper III (Indian Economy - Infrastructure, Transport, Investment Models, Competitiveness) and Prelims (International Reports and Indices).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, pillars, and mechanisms of the Logistics Performance Index.
  • Evaluate India's spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions like PM GatiShakti and National Logistics Policy.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on logistics pillars and rankings.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of India's transport infrastructure (rail, road, port, air)
  • Core macroeconomic concepts of trade competitiveness and supply chains
