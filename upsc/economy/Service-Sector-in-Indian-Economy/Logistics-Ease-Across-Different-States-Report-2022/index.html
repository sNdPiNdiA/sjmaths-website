Logistics Ease Across Different States Report 2022

The Logistics Ease Across Different States (LEADS) Report 2022 is a comprehensive evaluation framework released by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry to assess logistics infrastructure, services, and human resources across Indian states and union territories.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper III (Economy - Infrastructure, Ports, Roads, Airports, Railways, Investment Models, and Competitiveness) and Prelims regarding national logistics performance metrics.
