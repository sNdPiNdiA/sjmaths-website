Services Economic Survey 2021 22

The Economic Survey 2021-22 provides a comprehensive macroeconomic assessment of India's services sector, analyzing its performance, GVA contribution, FDI inflows, sub-sector resilience during the COVID-19 pandemic, and strategic roadmap for post-pandemic recovery.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (services sector share, GVA growth data, FDI equity inflows) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Economy, inclusive growth, employment generation, digital infrastructure, and infrastructure development).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, growth rates, and structural trends of the services sector highlighted in the Economic Survey 2021-22.
  • Evaluate sub-sector performance including IT-BPM, tourism, real estate, financial services, and telecommunications.
  • Apply high-yield data points and high-impact mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements and construct value-added Mains answers.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of National Income Accounting
  • Components of Gross Value Added (GVA)
  • Structure of India's Balance of Payments
