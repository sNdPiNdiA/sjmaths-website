World Bank Human Capital Project

A global accelerated effort by the World Bank launched in 2018 to bolster investments in people through health, education, and social protection to build human capital and eliminate extreme poverty.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly relevant for GS Paper III (Economy - Human Development, Inclusive Growth, Poverty & Employment) and Prelims (International Organizations, Global Indices like Human Capital Index).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of World Bank Human Capital Project.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
