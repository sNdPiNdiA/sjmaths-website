Poverty Eradication And Employment Related Programs

Multi-pronged socio-economic policy frameworks and state-driven interventions designed to alleviate absolute deprivation, generate sustainable livelihoods, enhance human capital, and ensure social protection in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper II (Welfare schemes, governance, vulnerable sections) and GS Paper III (Inclusive growth, employment, poverty estimation, agrarian crisis). Frequently tested via direct factual schemes in Prelims and analytical governance questions in Mains.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, classification, and implementation mechanisms of India's poverty alleviation and employment schemes.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, fiscal architectures, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements on wage employment vs. self-employment programs.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Indian Economy
  • Five-Year Plans
  • Constitutional Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP)
