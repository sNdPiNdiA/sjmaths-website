Poverty In India

Poverty in India is defined as a condition where individuals lack the financial resources and essentials for a minimum standard of living, historically measured through caloric intake thresholds and currently via multi-dimensional deprivations.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Inclusive Growth, Poverty, Development) and Prelims (Economic and Social Development - committees, MPI indices, welfare schemes).
