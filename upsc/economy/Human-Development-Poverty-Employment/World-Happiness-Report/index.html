World Happiness Report

The World Happiness Report is a landmark publication of global data that ranks countries by how happy their citizens perceive themselves to be, using extensive survey metrics and institutional indicators.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding publishing agencies, ranking indices, and performance parameters, and utilized in Mains GS-2 and GS-3 for arguments on inclusive growth, human development, and well-being beyond GDP.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of World Happiness Report.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें
