Expert Groups For Estimating Poverty

The formal institutional mechanisms and expert committees constituted by the Government of India to define poverty lines, establish measurement methodologies, and estimate poverty incidence across rural and urban sectors.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to Indian Economy syllabus (Human Development, Poverty, Employment). Frequently tested in Prelims through chronological ordering of committees, methodology shifts (Calorie intake vs. Multi-dimensional), and specific data points, while Mains requires evaluating inclusive growth and policy targeting.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Expert Groups For Estimating Poverty.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) surveys
  • Difference between absolute and relative poverty
