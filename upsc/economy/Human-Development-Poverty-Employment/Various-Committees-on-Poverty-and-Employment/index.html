Various Committees On Poverty And Employment

A systematic evolution of expert groups and committees appointed by the Government of India since independence to define, measure, and estimate poverty lines, alongside tracking employment patterns.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (Committee names, chairpersons, calorie norms, expenditure metrics) and Mains GS-3 (Inclusive growth, poverty alleviation strategies, employment dynamics, inequality).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master chronological timeline, chairpersons, and methodologies of all major poverty and employment committees.
  • Compare nutritional versus comprehensive consumption expenditure approaches.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to eliminate incorrect Prelims options effortlessly.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of National Sample Survey Office (NSSO) consumer expenditure surveys.
