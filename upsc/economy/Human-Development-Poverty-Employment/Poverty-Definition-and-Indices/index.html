Poverty Definition And Indices

Poverty is a multidimensional state of deprivation encompassing lack of basic necessities of life (income/consumption) and capabilities (health, education, living standards).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Inclusive Growth, Human Development) and GS Paper II (Welfare Schemes, Social Justice). Frequently tested via committees (Tendulkar, Rangarajan), global indices (MPI), and conceptual differences (Absolute vs. Relative Poverty).
