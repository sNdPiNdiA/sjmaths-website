Multidimensional Poverty

Multidimensional poverty measures deprivation across multiple simultaneous indicators—such as health, education, and standard of living—rather than relying solely on income or consumption expenditure.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper II (Welfare schemes, poverty, development issues) and Paper III (Inclusive growth, economic development), as well as Prelims questions on global indices and NITI Aayog reports.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
