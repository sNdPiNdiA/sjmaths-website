Human Development Concepts And Approaches

Human development is defined as the process of enlarging people's choices, enhancing human capabilities, and expanding freedom so that they can live a long, healthy, and creative life. It shifts the primary objective of development from mere economic growth to human well-being.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper 1 (Population and associated issues, developmental disparities), GS Paper 2 (Poverty, hunger, health, education, human resource policies, welfare schemes), and GS Paper 3 (Inclusive growth, employment, and economic development). Frequently tested in Prelims via UNDP indices, methodologies, and socio-economic parameters.
