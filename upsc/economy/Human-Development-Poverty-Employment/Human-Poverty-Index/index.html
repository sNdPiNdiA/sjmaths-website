Human Poverty Index

The Human Poverty Index (HPI) is a composite index introduced by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in 1997 in its Human Development Report to measure the deprivation in the basic dimensions of human development in developing (HPI-1) and developed (HPI-2) countries.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under UPSC GS Paper III (Economy - Inclusive Growth, Poverty, Human Development) and GS Paper I (Social Issues). Essential for understanding alternative paradigms of poverty measurement beyond mere income deprivation.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
