Socio Economic And Caste Census

The Socio Economic and Caste Census (SECC) is a comprehensive study of the socioeconomic status of rural and urban households in India, enabling the ranking of households based on specific parameters and capturing caste data.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper II (Welfare schemes, governance, vulnerable sections) and Paper III (Inclusive growth, poverty estimation, economy of India). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding administrative jurisdiction, distinction from the decennial Census, and use of data for targeted welfare delivery.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Socio Economic And Caste Census.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Decennial Census of India
  • Poverty estimation methodologies (Lakdawala, Tendulkar, Rangarajan committees)
  • Targeted Public Distribution System (TPDS)
