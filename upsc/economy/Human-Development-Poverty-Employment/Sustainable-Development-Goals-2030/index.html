Sustainable Development Goals 2030

An intergovernmental blueprint adopted by all United Nations Member States in 2015 through Agenda 2030, comprising 17 interconnected global goals designed to end poverty, protect the planet, and ensure peace and prosperity for all by 2030.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (direct questions on specific goal targets, NITI Aayog SDG India Index) and Mains GS Papers II (International relations, governance, welfare schemes) and III (Inclusive growth, environment, resource mobilization, economic development).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Sustainable Development Goals 2030.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Millennium Development Goals (MDGs) evolution
  • Basic concepts of inclusive growth and sustainable development
  • Functioning of UN bodies and NITI Aayog
