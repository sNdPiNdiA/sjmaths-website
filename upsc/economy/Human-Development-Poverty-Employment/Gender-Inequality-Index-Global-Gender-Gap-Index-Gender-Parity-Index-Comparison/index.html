Gender Inequality Index Global Gender Gap Index Gender Parity Index Comparison

A comprehensive comparative study of three premier global indices—Gender Inequality Index (GII by UNDP), Global Gender Gap Index (GGGI by WEF), and Gender Parity Index (GPI by UNESCO)—evaluating gender disparities across reproductive health, empowerment, economic participation, and education.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (factual criteria, publishing agencies, indicator sets) and Mains GS Paper-1 (Women's role, population, developmental issues) and GS Paper-2 (Inclusive governance, social sector policies, international reports).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, indicator methodologies, and publishing agencies of GII, GGGI, and GPI.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and India's latest rankings and performance trajectories.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements and construct value-added Mains arguments.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic concepts of Human Development Index (HDI)
  • Understanding of socio-economic gender disparities in India
