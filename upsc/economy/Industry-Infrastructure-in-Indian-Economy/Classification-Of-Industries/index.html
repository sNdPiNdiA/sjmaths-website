Classification Of Industries

The systematic categorization of manufacturing and economic enterprises based on raw materials used, capital investment, ownership structure, output nature, and market orientation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding structural transformation of the Indian economy, industrial policy evolution (IDRA 1951, MSME definitions), regional imbalances, and inclusive growth strategies in Prelims and GS Paper III (Mains).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
