Economic Reforms Related To Industries

A comprehensive shift in India's industrial policy initiated in July 1991, dismantling the 'License-Permit-Quota Raj' and transitioning the economy toward Liberalization, Privatization, and Globalization (LPG).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to General Studies Paper 3 (Indian Economy and issues relating to planning, mobilization of resources, growth, development, and employment). Highly tested in Prelims regarding Industrial Policy Resolutions, MRTP Act, FERA/FEMA, delicensing, and MSME definitions.
