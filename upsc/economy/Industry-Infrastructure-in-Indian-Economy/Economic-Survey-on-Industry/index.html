Economic Survey On Industry

The Economic Survey of India's annual assessment of the industrial sector's performance, growth trends, structural shifts, policy impacts, and macro-economic contributions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination as it integrates core economic theory with real-time data, assessing manufacturing sector dynamism, MSME resilience, index of industrial production (IIP) trends, capital expenditure (CapEx), and policy interventions like Production Linked Incentive (PLI) schemes in Prelims and GS Paper III Mains.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of the Economic Survey On Industry.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core macroeconomic indicators
  • Index of Industrial Production (IIP)
  • National Industrial Classification (NIC)
