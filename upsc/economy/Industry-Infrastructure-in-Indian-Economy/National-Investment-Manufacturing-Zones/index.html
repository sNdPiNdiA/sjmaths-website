National Investment Manufacturing Zones

National Investment and Manufacturing Zones (NIMZs) are mega-scale integrated industrial townships established under the National Policy on Manufacturing (NPM), 2011, designed to promote world-class infrastructure, clean technologies, and administrative ease for manufacturing growth.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy - Industrial Growth, Manufacturing Sector, Make in India, Infrastructure, Employment Generation) and Prelims (Policy provisions, minimum land area requirements, institutional mechanisms).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of National Investment Manufacturing Zones.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें
