Disinvestment Of PSEs

Disinvestment refers to the dilution or sale of the Central Government's equity stake in Public Sector Enterprises (PSEs) to private entities or the public, aimed at unlocking capital, improving operational efficiency, and reducing fiscal deficits.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Economic Reforms, Privatization, Infrastructure, and Government Budgeting). Tested frequently in Prelims regarding structural classifications (Maharatna, Navratna, Miniratna), institutional mechanisms (DIPAM), and policy shifts.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
