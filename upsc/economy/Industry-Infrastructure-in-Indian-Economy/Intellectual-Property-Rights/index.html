Intellectual Property Rights

Intellectual Property Rights (IPR) are legal rights that protect creations of the mind—such as inventions, literary and artistic works, designs, and symbols—granting creators exclusive rights to use and commercialize their intellectual assets for a specified period.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (TRIPS agreement, GI tags, WIPO treaties, statutory bodies) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Innovation, R&D, Make in India, compulsory licensing, pharmaceutical pricing, technology transfer).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
