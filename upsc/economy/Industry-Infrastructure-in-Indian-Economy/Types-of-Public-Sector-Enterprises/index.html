Types Of Public Sector Enterprises

Public Sector Enterprises (PSEs) in India are business undertakings owned, managed, and controlled by the Central Government, State Governments, or both, aimed at socio-economic development, infrastructure creation, and strategic control of the economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims (criteria for Maharatna, Navratna, Miniratna status, financial autonomy) and Mains GS Paper III (role of PSUs in economic development, privatization, disinvestment policies, and corporate governance).
