Index Of Industrial Production

The Index of Industrial Production (IIP) is a composite indicator that measures the short-term changes in the volume of production of a basket of industrial products during a given period with respect to a chosen base year.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims due to its composition, base year, data-releasing agency, and core sectors. Essential for Mains GS Paper 3 under 'Indian Economy and issues relating to planning, mobilization of resources, growth, development and employment.'
