Eight Core Sectors

The Eight Core Sectors represent the foundational pillars of the Indian economy, comprising Electricity, Steel, Refinery Products, Crude Oil, Coal, Natural Gas, Cement, and Fertilizers, which collectively constitute 40.27% of the Index of Industrial Production (IIP).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims regarding exact composition, weightage hierarchies, and release authorities, while Mains GS-3 examines their role in infrastructure bottlenecks, capital investments, and industrial growth linkages.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, relative weightages, and institutional compilation mechanisms of the Eight Core Sectors.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, structural constraints, and policy reforms impacting these industries.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims sorting and weightage-based MCQs.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of macroeconomics and industrial output indices (IIP).
