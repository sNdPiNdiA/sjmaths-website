Industrial Corridors

An Industrial Corridor is a packaged cluster of world-class infrastructure—comprising high-speed rail/freight corridors, ports, airports, power grids, and specialized manufacturing nodes—designed to stimulate rapid economic growth and manufacturing competitiveness along a specific geographical axis.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Infrastructure, Manufacturing, and Regional Development) and Prelims (mapping of corridors, PM GatiShakti, NICDC, and special economic zones).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Industrial Corridors.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
