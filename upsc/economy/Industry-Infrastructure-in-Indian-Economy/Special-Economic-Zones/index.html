Special Economic Zones

Special Economic Zones (SEZs) are specifically delineated duty-free enclaves treated as foreign territory for the purpose of industrial, service, and trade operations, aiming to boost exports, foreign direct investment (FDI), and employment generation with minimal bureaucratic hurdles.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy & Infrastructure, Export Competitiveness, Manufacturing, Land Reforms, Employment) and Prelims (Statutory provisions, SEZ Act 2005, Baba Kalyani Committee, tax incentives, WTO compliance).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, legislative framework, and institutional mechanisms of SEZs in India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, export performance, land-acquisition issues, and WTO implications.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basics of international trade, balance of payments, export promotion measures, and industrial policy.
