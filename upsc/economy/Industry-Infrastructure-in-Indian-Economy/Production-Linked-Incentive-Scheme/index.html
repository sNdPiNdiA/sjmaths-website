Production Linked Incentive Scheme

The Production Linked Incentive (PLI) Scheme is an outcome-based flagship financial stimulus policy introduced by the Government of India to incentivize incremental sales of products manufactured in domestic units, aiming to boost domestic manufacturing, reduce import dependence, and integrate India into global value chains (GVCs).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper III (Indian Economy - Industrial Growth, Make in India, Export Competitiveness, Technology, and Budgeting) and Prelims (Government Schemes, Economic Policy, Subsidies).
