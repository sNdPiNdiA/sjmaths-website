Micro Small And Medium Enterprises

MSMEs are non-subsidiary, independent enterprises that employ fewer than a given number of employees and have annual turnover below certain thresholds, serving as the backbone of India's manufacturing, exports, and employment generation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper-3 (Indian Economy: Growth, Development, Employment, Industrial Policy) and Prelims (data-driven questions on classification, definitions, and schemes like Udyam, CGTMSE, and PMEGP).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
