Strategic And Critical Minerals

Strategic and critical minerals are non-fuel metallic or non-metallic minerals that are essential for the economic development and national security of a country, the supply chain of which is vulnerable to disruption.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly relevant for GS Paper 3 (Economy - Industry, Infrastructure, Security, Environment) and Prelims (Geography, Economy, Science & Technology), focusing on India's net-zero transition, supply chain resilience, and Atmanirbhar Bharat.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
