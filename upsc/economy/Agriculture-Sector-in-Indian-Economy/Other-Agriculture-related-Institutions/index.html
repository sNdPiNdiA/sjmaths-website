Other Agriculture Related Institutions

A comprehensive network of autonomous bodies, boards, research councils, statutory authorities, and development corporations functioning under the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare and allied ministries to support research, regulation, pricing, export promotion, and cooperative development in Indian agriculture.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (factual questions on statutory status, parent ministries, and headquarters) and Mains GS Paper-3 (institutional reforms, supply chain bottlenecks, price support mechanisms, and agricultural governance).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Other Agriculture Related Institutions.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Indian Economy
  • Structure of Indian Agriculture
  • NABARD and institutional credit basics
