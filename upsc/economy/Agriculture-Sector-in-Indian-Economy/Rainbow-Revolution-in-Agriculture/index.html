Rainbow Revolution In Agriculture

The Rainbow Revolution in Indian agriculture represents an inclusive, multi-faceted strategy aimed at holistically developing agriculture, animal husbandry, forestry, fisheries, poultry, and food processing by integrating all preceding specialized color revolutions into a single, coordinated framework.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (Environment, Agriculture Schemes, Economic Survey data) and Mains GS Paper-3 (Indian Economy, Agriculture, Food Security, Inclusive Growth, Doubling Farmers' Income).
