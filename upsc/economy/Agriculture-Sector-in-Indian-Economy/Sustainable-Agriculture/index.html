Sustainable Agriculture

Agricultural practices that meet current societal food and fiber needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet theirs, by integrating environmental health, economic profitability, and social equity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper 3 (Agriculture, Food Security, Environment, Land Degradation) and Prelims (schemes, practices like ZBNF/Natural Farming, missions, and bodies like NMSA).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Sustainable Agriculture.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Indian Agriculture and Cropping Patterns
  • Green Revolution impacts and environmental externalities
