Animal Husbandry

Animal husbandry is a branch of agriculture concerned with the breeding, rearing, and management of livestock for meat, fibre, milk, eggs, and other products, serving as a critical pillar of rural livelihood and nutritional security in India.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly relevant for UPSC Prelims (schemes, livestock census data, breed geography) and Mains GS-3 (Agriculture: economics of animal rearing, doubling farmers' income, food security, white and pink revolutions).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
