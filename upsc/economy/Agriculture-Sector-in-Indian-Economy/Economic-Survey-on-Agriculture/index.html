Economic Survey On Agriculture

The Economic Survey of Agriculture is an exhaustive annual flagship document presented by the Ministry of Finance, Government of India, detailing the performance, structural shifts, policy interventions, challenges, and medium-term outlook of the primary sector. It serves as the official macroeconomic diagnostic report on Indian agriculture, allied sectors, food security, capital formation, and rural economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (direct data questions on GVA share, credit growth, institutional schemes, food grain production) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Agriculture: Cropping patterns, MSP, subsidies, e-NAM, digital agriculture, institutional credit, and supply chain management).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
