Union Budgets On Agriculture

The systematic allocation of fiscal resources, capital expenditures, subsidies, and policy incentives directed by the Government of India through the annual Union Budget toward the primary sector to ensure food security, enhance rural livelihoods, and boost agricultural productivity.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Agriculture, Resource Mobilization, Inclusive Growth) and Prelims (schemes, budget allocations, institutional credit, and capital formation trends).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
