Important Agriculture And Income Support Schemes

Comprehensive overview of direct benefit transfers, price support mechanisms, and financial cushioning policies designed by the state to mitigate agrarian distress, ensure minimum guaranteed returns, and alleviate rural poverty in India.

Directly relevant for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Agriculture, Subsidies, Minimum Support Prices, and Food Security) as well as Prelims factual questions regarding scheme eligibility, funding patterns, and nodal ministries.
