NITI Aayog

National Institution for Transforming India (NITI) Aayog is the apex public policy think tank of the Government of India, established on January 1, 2015, replacing the erstwhile Planning Commission to foster cooperative federalism and bottom-up planning.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper 2 (Statutory, regulatory, and various quasi-judicial bodies, cooperative federalism) and GS Paper 3 (Inclusive growth, economic planning, government budgeting, policy interventions). Regularly tested in Prelims regarding its composition, statutory status, and indices.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of NITI Aayog.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core foundational concepts of Indian Economy and Planning
  • Evolution of the Planning Commission vs. NITI Aayog
