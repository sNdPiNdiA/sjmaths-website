Decentralized Planning

Decentralized planning is a development paradigm where the formulation of economic plans, resource allocation, and project implementation are driven from the grassroots level (villages, blocks, and districts) upward rather than imposed by central authorities. It operationalizes participatory governance and democratic federalism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper II (Polity and Governance - devolution of powers, Panchayati Raj Institutions) and GS Paper III (Indian Economy - inclusive growth, regional disparities, planning mechanisms). Frequently tested via constitutional amendments, committee reports, and spatial economic disparities.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
