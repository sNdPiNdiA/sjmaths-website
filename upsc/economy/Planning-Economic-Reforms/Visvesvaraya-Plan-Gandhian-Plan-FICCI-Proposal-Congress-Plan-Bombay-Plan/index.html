Visvesvaraya Plan Gandhian Plan FICCI Proposal Congress Plan Bombay Plan

The pre-independence economic planning blueprints developed by Indian technocrats, industrialists, Gandhians, and political leaders between 1934 and 1944 to transition colonial India into a modern, self-reliant industrialized economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in Prelims via chronological ordering, authorship matching, and ideological inclinations, and serves as foundational GS Mains content for tracing the evolution of Indian economic planning and state capitalism.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, chronology, and authorships of the pre-independence economic plans.
  • Evaluate the ideological divide between heavy industrialization (Bombay Plan, Visvesvaraya) and decentralized agrarian/cottage development (Gandhian Plan).
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky chronological and matching-based Prelims questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic Indian History (National Movement 1930-1947)
  • Foundations of Indian Economy and Planning
