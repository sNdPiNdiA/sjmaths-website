Harrod Domar Strategy

A classical macroeconomic growth model emphasizing that economic growth depends on the quantity of labor and capital, specifically highlighting capital accumulation through savings and investment as the primary driver of development.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for understanding the foundational economic strategy of India's early Five-Year Plans (specifically the First and Second Plans), evaluating the evolution of Indian planning, and addressing structural constraints like the savings-investment gap in Mains GS Paper III.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mathematical mechanisms of the Harrod Domar Strategy.
  • Evaluate historical application in India's Five-Year Plans and post-independence economic trajectories.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding growth models.

  • Basic Macroeconomics
  • Capital-Output Ratio
  • Savings and Investment Concepts
