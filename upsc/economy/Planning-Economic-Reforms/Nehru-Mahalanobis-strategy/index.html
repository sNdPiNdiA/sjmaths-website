Nehru Mahalanobis Strategy

An economic development model adopted in India's Second Five-Year Plan (1956-61) emphasizing heavy industrialization, public sector dominance, and a capital-goods bias to achieve long-term self-reliance.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

A foundational topic for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Planning, Growth & Development) and frequently tested in Prelims regarding economic history, industrial policy resolutions, and planning models.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Nehru Mahalanobis Strategy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of Indian economic history post-independence
  • Overview of Five-Year Plans in India
