Planning Commission And National Development Council NDC History

The Planning Commission was a non-constitutional, extra-constitutional, and non-statutory body established in March 1950 by an executive resolution of the Government of India to formulate India's Five-Year Plans, while the National Development Council (NDC), established in August 1952, served as the apex body for policy decision-making and deliberations on development in India, securing cooperation of states in execution of plans.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper III (Indian Economy, Planning, Mobilization of Resources, Inclusive Growth) and General Studies Paper II (Federal Polity, Centre-State Relations, Inter-State Council vs NDC). Tested extensively in Prelims regarding statutory status, composition, and historical evolution leading up to NITI Aayog.
