Economic Planning Meaning Objectives Strategies

Economic planning refers to the conscious and deliberate utilization of a country's available resources by a central authority to achieve predetermined socio-economic objectives over a specified timeframe. In the Indian context, it evolved from centralized directive planning under the Planning Commission to cooperative and competitive federalism under NITI Aayog.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for General Studies Paper III (Indian Economy and issues relating to planning, mobilization of resources, growth, development, and employment). UPSC frequently tests the structural shifts from Five-Year Plans to perspective planning, the role of indicative planning, and the evolution of federal planning mechanisms.
