Gandhian Strategy

The Gandhian Strategy of economic development prioritizes a decentralized, self-reliant village economy (Gram Swaraj) centered on human dignity, cottage industries, trusteeship, and moral growth over material hedonism.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Economy - Planning, Inclusive Growth, Decentralization) and GS Paper IV (Ethics - Gandhian Ethics, Integrity, Altruism). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding economic thinkers, Sarvodaya, and decentralized planning.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Gandhian Strategy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Philosophical foundations of the Indian National Movement
