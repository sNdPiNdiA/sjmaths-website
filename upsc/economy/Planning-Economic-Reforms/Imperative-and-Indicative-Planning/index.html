Imperative And Indicative Planning

Imperative planning is a centralized, command-economy system where resources, production targets, and prices are rigidly controlled by the state, whereas indicative planning operates in a mixed economy where the state uses policy incentives, forecasts, and guidelines to steer private sector behavior without direct coercion.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper 3 (Indian Economy and planning, resource mobilization, growth, development), tracing India's structural shift from socialist-leaning Soviet-style imperative elements in early Five-Year Plans to market-friendly indicative planning post-1991 LPG reforms.
