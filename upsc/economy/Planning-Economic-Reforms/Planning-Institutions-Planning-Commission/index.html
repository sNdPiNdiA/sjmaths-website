Planning Institutions Planning Commission

The Planning Commission was a non-constitutional, extra-constitutional, and non-statutory body established in March 1950 by an executive resolution of the Government of India, tasked with formulating India's Five-Year Plans and charting the nation's socio-economic development trajectory.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for understanding India's transition from centralized command planning (1950-2014) to cooperative and competitive federalism under NITI Aayog. Extensively tested in GS Paper 2 (Polity - Federalism, Executive bodies) and GS Paper 3 (Economy - Planning, Inclusive growth, Resource mobilization).
