Central Sector And Centrally Sponsored Schemes

Central Sector Schemes (CSS) are 100% funded and implemented by the Central Government, whereas Centrally Sponsored Schemes (CSS-sponsored) are jointly funded by the Centre and States with specific sharing patterns to execute national development priorities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (budgetary allocations, funding patterns, nodal ministries) and Mains GS Paper II & III (Centre-State fiscal relations, cooperative federalism, implementation efficacy of welfare policies).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Central Sector And Centrally Sponsored Schemes.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
