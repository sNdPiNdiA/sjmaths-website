Regional And National Planning

National planning encompasses macroeconomic goal-setting and resource allocation at the central government level, whereas regional planning addresses spatial imbalances, localized socio-economic disparities, and sub-national resource optimization.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Inclusive Growth, Regional Disparities) and GS Paper I (Geography - Regional Planning, Urbanization, Location of Industries). Frequently tested in Prelims via institutional mechanisms, statutory bodies, and decentralization provisions.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Regional And National Planning.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Understanding of India's Five-Year Plan history
  • Basics of federal fiscal architecture (Finance Commission, NITI Aayog)
