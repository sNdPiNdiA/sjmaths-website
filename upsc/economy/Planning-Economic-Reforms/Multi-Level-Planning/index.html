Multi Level Planning

Multi Level Planning is a decentralized planning framework where the planning process is disaggregated across various spatial and administrative tiers—National, State, District, Block, and Village—to ensure bottom-up resource allocation, participatory governance, and optimal utilization of local endowments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for GS Paper III (Inclusive Growth, Decentralization, Planning) and GS Paper II (Devolution of Powers, Local Governance under 73rd and 74th Constitutional Amendments). Tested via structural bottlenecks, fiscal federalism, and district planning committees.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, spatial tiers, and mechanisms of Multi Level Planning.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and fiscal federalism dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Panchayati Raj Institutions (PRIs)
