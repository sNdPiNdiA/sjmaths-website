LPG Strategy

The LPG (Liberalisation, Privatisation, and Globalisation) Strategy refers to the sweeping structural economic reforms introduced in India in July 1991 to dismantle the License-Permit-Quota Raj, resolve the acute Balance of Payments (BoP) crisis, and integrate the domestic economy with the global market.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic is a foundational pillar of the Indian Economy syllabus for both Prelims (direct policy questions, committees, and reform chronological sequences) and Mains GS-3 (evaluation of economic reforms, inclusive growth, industrial policy, and globalization impacts).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of the 1991 LPG Strategy.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, structural changes, and institutional frameworks across different economic sectors.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on economic reforms.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Pre-1991 Indian socialist economic model
  • Balance of Payments (BoP) crisis basics
  • Macroeconomic indicators (Fiscal Deficit, Inflation, Forex Reserves)
