Methods Of GDP Calculations

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) represents the monetary value of all final goods and services produced within a country's geographic borders over a specific period, computed primarily through the Output (Value Added), Income, and Expenditure approaches.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Fundamental core microtopic in Indian Economy tested repeatedly in Prelims (concept clarity on GVA, Factor Cost vs. Market Price, base year revisions) and Mains GS-3 (economic growth measurement, structural reforms, statistical adequacy).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
