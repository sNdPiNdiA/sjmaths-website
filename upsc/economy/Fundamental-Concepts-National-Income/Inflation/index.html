Inflation

Inflation refers to a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy over a period of time, leading to a corresponding fall in the purchasing power of money.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Central to Indian macroeconomic governance (RBI Act, MPC framework), inflation directly affects monetary policy formulation, poverty assessment, wage indexing, and bond yields. Frequently tested in Prelims (indices measurement, base year effects) and Mains (growth-inflation trade-off, structural bottlenecks, food inflation management).
