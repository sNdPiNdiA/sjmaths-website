Concepts And Types Of Inflation

Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy over a period of time, leading to a corresponding fall in the purchasing power of money.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (direct MCQs on CPI vs WPI, core vs headline inflation, and MPC targets) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, growth, mobilization of resources, and inclusive growth issues).
