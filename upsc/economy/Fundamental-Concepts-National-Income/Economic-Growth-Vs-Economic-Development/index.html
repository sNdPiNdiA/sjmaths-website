Economic Growth Vs Economic Development

Economic growth refers to the quantitative increase in a country's real output (GDP/GNP) over time, whereas economic development is a multi-dimensional qualitative process that includes growth along with structural changes, reduction in poverty, inequality, and unemployment, and enhancement of human well-being.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic forms the foundational bedrock of Indian Economy in GS Paper III (Inclusive Growth, Mobilization of Resources, Development & Employment) and frequently appears in Prelims regarding the limitations of GDP, indices like HDI, and qualitative welfare metrics.
