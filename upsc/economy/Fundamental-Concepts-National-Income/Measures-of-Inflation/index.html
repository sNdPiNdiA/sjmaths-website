Measures Of Inflation

Measures of inflation are quantitative indices used to track the average change in prices of a basket of goods and services over time in an economy, serving as critical gauges of purchasing power and macroeconomic stability.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding CPI vs. WPI, base years, institutional mandates (RBI Monetary Policy Committee), and in Mains GS-3 under 'Inclusive Growth and Mobilization of Resources'.
