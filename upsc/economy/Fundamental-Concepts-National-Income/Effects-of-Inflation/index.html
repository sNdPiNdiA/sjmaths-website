Effects Of Inflation

Inflation refers to the general increase in prices and fall in the purchasing value of money over time, producing wide-ranging redistribution effects across wealth, income, savings, and international trade.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Tested extensively in UPSC Prelims (impact on purchasing power, debtors/creditors, bond yields, real vs. nominal returns) and Mains GS-3 (economic growth vs. price stability, welfare implications, monetary policy transmission).
