Measures Of Economic Growth

Measures of economic growth quantify the quantitative increase in a nation's production of goods and services over a specific period, primarily tracked through metrics like Gross Domestic Product (GDP), Gross National Product (GNP), and their real versus nominal adjustments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

This microtopic forms the bedrock of the Indian Economy syllabus for UPSC CSE Prelims (standard definitions, base year revisions, real vs. nominal GDP) and Mains GS-3 (inclusive growth, resource allocation, and structural transformation).
