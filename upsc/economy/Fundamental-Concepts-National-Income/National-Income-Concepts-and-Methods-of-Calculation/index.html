National Income Concepts And Methods Of Calculation

National income measures the total monetary value of all final goods and services produced within an economy or by its residents over a specific period, typically one fiscal year. It serves as the primary macroeconomic indicator for economic activity, welfare assessment, and structural planning.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (concepts like GDP, GNP, NNP at Factor Cost/Market Price, deflation, real vs. nominal GDP) and Mains GS Paper III (inclusive growth, mobilization of resources, economic development, and structural reforms).
