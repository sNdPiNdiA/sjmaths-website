Causes Of Inflation

Inflation is the sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services in an economy over a period, fundamentally driven by structural imbalances between aggregate demand and aggregate supply, monetary expansion, and cost structures.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial core concept frequently tested in UPSC Prelims (distinguishing demand-pull vs cost-push, monetary policy instruments) and Mains GS-3 (Mobilization of Resources, Growth, Development, and Inflation management).
