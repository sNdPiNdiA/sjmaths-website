Microeconomic Concepts

Microeconomics is the branch of economics that studies the behavior of individual economic agents—such as households, firms, and industries—in decision-making and allocation of limited resources. It analyzes how prices of goods and services are determined through the interplay of individual demand and supply.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for Prelims (Conceptual clarity on demand-supply shifts, market structures, elasticity, and welfare economics) and Mains GS Paper III (Inclusive growth, industrial policy, pricing mechanisms, subsidies, and market failures).
