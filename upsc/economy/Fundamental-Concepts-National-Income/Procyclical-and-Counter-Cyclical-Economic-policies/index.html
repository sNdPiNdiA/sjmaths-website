Procyclical And Counter Cyclical Economic Policies

Procyclical economic policies amplify macroeconomic fluctuations by expanding during booms and contracting during recessions, whereas countercyclical policies stabilize the business cycle by dampening excesses through fiscal and monetary interventions.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Directly tested in Prelims (Economy, Fiscal Policy, Monetary Policy instruments like Countercyclical Capital Buffers) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Economic Development, Growth, Inflation, Macroeconomic stability, and management of economic shocks).
