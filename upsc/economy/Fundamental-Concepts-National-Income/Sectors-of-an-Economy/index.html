Sectors Of An Economy

Sectors of an economy represent the macro-level division of economic activities based on the nature of operations, ownership, working conditions, and use of natural resources. They provide the fundamental architecture for measuring National Income (GDP), employment distribution, and structural transformation.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (distinguishing between primary, secondary, tertiary sectors, organized vs. unorganized labor, and public vs. private assets) and GS Paper III (Structural transformation, employment elasticity, jobless growth, and manufacturing/service sector policies).
