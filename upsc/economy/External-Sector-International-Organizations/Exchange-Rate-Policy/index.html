Exchange Rate Policy

Exchange rate policy refers to the strategy and framework adopted by a central bank and government to manage their currency's value relative to other foreign currencies, balancing macroeconomic stability, trade competitiveness, and capital account openness.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Prelims (questions on RBI interventions, Managed Float, REER vs. NEER, Capital Convertibility) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Economy - External Sector, Export Competitiveness, Forex Reserves management).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Exchange Rate Policy in India.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution of forex reserves, RBI intervention tools, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions on REER/NEER and exchange rate regimes.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Balance of Payments (BoP)
  • Monetary Policy Framework
  • Foreign Exchange Reserves (FER)
