Universal Postal Union

The Universal Postal Union (UPU), established by the Treaty of Bern in 1874, is a specialized agency of the United Nations that coordinates postal policies among member nations, forming the single postal territory for the reciprocal exchange of letter mail.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under Prelims (International Organizations, UN Specialized Agencies) and Mains GS Paper II (Global Governance, Multilateral Institutions affecting India's trade and logistics).
