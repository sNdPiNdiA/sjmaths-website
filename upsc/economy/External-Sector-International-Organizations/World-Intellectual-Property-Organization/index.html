World Intellectual Property Organization

The World Intellectual Property Organization (WIPO) is a self-funding specialized agency of the United Nations, created in 1967 to promote and protect intellectual property (IP) across the world through cooperation among states.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (International organizations, UN specialized agencies, global IP indices, treaties like PCT and Madrid system) and Mains GS Paper II (Global governance, multilateral bodies) and GS Paper III (IPR issues, technology transfer, innovation economy).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
