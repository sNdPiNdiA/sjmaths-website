FERA FEMA

The Foreign Exchange Regulation Act (FERA), 1973 and its successor the Foreign Exchange Management Act (FEMA), 1999 represent India's historic legislative transition from a stringent, criminal-based foreign exchange control regime to a liberalized, civil-based management regime aimed at facilitating external trade and payments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under Economy (External Sector). Tested in Prelims for statutory differences, criminal vs. civil liabilities, and enforcement mechanisms (ED), and in Mains for India's balance of payments liberalization, capital account convertibility, and ease of doing business.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
