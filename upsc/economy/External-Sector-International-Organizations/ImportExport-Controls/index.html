ImportExport Controls

ImportExport Controls refer to the legal, tariff, and non-tariff mechanisms implemented by sovereign governments and regulatory bodies to regulate, restrict, or promote the cross-border movement of goods and services. In India, these controls are governed primarily by the Foreign Trade (Development and Regulation) Act, 1992, and administered by the Directorate General of Foreign Trade (DGFT) under the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper 3 (Indian Economy and issues relating to mobilization of resources, growth, development, and external sector). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding trade policy instruments, export-import regulations, and trade facilitation, and in Mains regarding export competitiveness, protectionism, and current account management.
