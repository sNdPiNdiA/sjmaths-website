Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank

The Asian Infrastructure Investment Bank (AIIB) is a multilateral development bank whose mission is to finance infrastructure in the Asia-Pacific region and beyond, addressing the massive infrastructure funding gap.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (International financial institutions, voting shares, headquarters, founding members) and Mains GS Paper II (Multilateral groupings, geopolitics of international finance, India's economic diplomacy and strategic positioning).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, voting structures, and operational mechanisms of the AIIB.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution of projects, regional hubs, and geo-economic dimensions involving India and China.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding multilateral development banks.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of international financial architecture (World Bank, IMF, ADB)
  • Concepts of multilateral development banking and sovereign borrowing
