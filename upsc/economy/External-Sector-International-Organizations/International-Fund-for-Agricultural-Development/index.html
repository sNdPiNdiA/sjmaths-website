International Fund For Agricultural Development

The International Fund for Agricultural Development (IFAD) is a specialized international financial institution of the United Nations established in 1977 as a major outcome of the 1974 World Food Conference, dedicated to eradicating rural poverty and hunger in developing countries.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Highly relevant for UPSC Civil Services Exam under GS Paper III (Economy - Inclusive Growth, Agriculture, Mobilization of Resources) and Prelims (International Organizations, UN bodies, specialized agencies). Questions frequently test institutional membership, funding architecture, and India-IFAD relations.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of International Fund For Agricultural Development.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Core concepts of rural development and agricultural economics
