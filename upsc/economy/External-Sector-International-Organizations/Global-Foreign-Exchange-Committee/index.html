Global Foreign Exchange Committee

The Global Foreign Exchange Committee (GFXC) is a forum of central banks and private sector foreign exchange committees established in 2017 to promote a robust, liquid, open, and appropriately transparent global foreign exchange market.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Prelims (GS-1/Economy regarding international financial architecture, BIS, RBI) and Mains GS Paper III (External Sector, International Financial Institutions, Global Governance, and Financial Stability).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Global Foreign Exchange Committee.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
