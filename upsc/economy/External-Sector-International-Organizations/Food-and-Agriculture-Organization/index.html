Food And Agriculture Organization

A specialized agency of the United Nations that leads international efforts to defeat hunger, eliminate poverty, and achieve food security for all through sustainable agricultural development.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for UPSC CSE Prelims (International Organizations, UN bodies, specialized agencies, headquarters) and Mains GS-2 (International Relations, global governance, UN bodies) and GS-3 (Agriculture, Food Security, PDS, Economics of Agriculture).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts, history, and functional mechanisms of the Food and Agriculture Organization (FAO).
  • Evaluate institutional frameworks, global reports, and India's partnership with the FAO.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims statements regarding specialized UN agencies.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of the United Nations system and specialized agencies.
  • Core concepts of global food security, nutrition, and sustainable agriculture.
