International Bank For Reconstruction And Development

The International Bank for Reconstruction and Development (IBRD) is an international financial institution established in 1944, functioning as the original lending arm of the World Bank Group to promote sustainable development, poverty reduction, and institution building in middle-income and creditworthy low-income countries.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding international financial architectures, voting rights, and World Bank Group components; essential for Mains GS Paper III (Global economic governance, international institutions, and developmental financing).
