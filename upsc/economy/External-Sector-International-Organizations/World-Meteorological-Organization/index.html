World Meteorological Organization

The World Meteorological Organization (WMO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations dedicated to international cooperation on meteorology (weather and climate), operational hydrology, and related geophysical sciences.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper II (International Organizations, UN Agencies) and GS Paper III (Environment, Disaster Management, Climate Change, Economic development via early warning systems).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and institutional mechanisms of the World Meteorological Organization.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, UN system integration, and global climate monitoring frameworks.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding WMO reports and specialized bodies.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Basic understanding of United Nations specialized agencies
  • Core concepts of global climate change and atmospheric science
