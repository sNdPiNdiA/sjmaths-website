International Maritime Organization

The International Maritime Organization (IMO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for regulating international shipping safety, security, and the prevention of marine and atmospheric pollution by ships.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (international organizations, UN specialized agencies, headquarters, conventions) and Mains GS Paper II (international relations, global governance) & GS Paper III (environment, marine pollution, infrastructure, blue economy).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of International Maritime Organization.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
