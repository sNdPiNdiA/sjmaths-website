Balance Of Payments

The Balance of Payments (BoP) is a systematic and comprehensive accounting statement of all economic transactions between the residents of a country and the rest of the world during a specific period, typically a year.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (direct conceptual MCQs on Current Account, Capital Account, Convertibility) and Mains GS Paper III (Indian Economy, external sector stability, exchange rate management, and export competitiveness).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of Balance Of Payments.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

Prerequisitesआवश्यक शर्तें

  • Macroeconomic aggregates
  • Foreign exchange markets
  • National income accounting
