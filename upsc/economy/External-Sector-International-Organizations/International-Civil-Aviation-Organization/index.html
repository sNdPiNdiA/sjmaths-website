International Civil Aviation Organization

The International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations established by the Chicago Convention of 1944 to manage the administration and governance of the Convention on International Civil Aviation (Chicago Convention).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Examination under GS Paper II (International Organizations, UN agencies) and GS Paper III (Infrastructure: Aviation sector, External Sector, Global Governance). Tests knowledge of specialized UN bodies, international treaties, and aviation safety standards.
