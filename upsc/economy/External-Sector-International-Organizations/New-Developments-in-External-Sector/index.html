New Developments In External Sector

Recent structural transformations, policy interventions, trade pacts, and regulatory updates governing India's balance of payments, merchandise and services trade, capital flows, and international economic integration.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Civil Services Exam (Prelims & Mains GS-3 Economy) as it directly impacts macroeconomic stability, export-led growth strategies, foreign exchange reserves management, and geopolitical positioning in global value chains.

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
