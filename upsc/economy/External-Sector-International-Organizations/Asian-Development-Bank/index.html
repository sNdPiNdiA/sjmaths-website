Asian Development Bank

The Asian Development Bank (ADB) is a regional development bank established on December 19, 1966, dedicated to promoting social and economic development in Asia and the Pacific through loans, technical assistance, grants, and equity investments.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding voting shares, membership structure, headquarters location, and India's founding role. In Mains GS Paper II and III, it is relevant for discussions on multilateral financing, infrastructure development in South Asia, regional cooperation, and India's bilateral-multilateral economic diplomacy.
