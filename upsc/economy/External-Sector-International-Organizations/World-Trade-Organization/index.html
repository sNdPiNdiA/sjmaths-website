World Trade Organization

The World Trade Organization (WTO) is the only global international organization dealing with the rules of trade between nations, established on January 1, 1995, by the Marrakesh Agreement replacing the General Agreement on Tariffs and Trade (GATT).

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE Prelims (core international institutions, agreements, dispute settlement mechanism, ministerial conferences) and Mains GS Paper II & III (bilateral/multilateral groupings, global economic governance, agriculture subsidies, and India's trade policy stands).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of World Trade Organization.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
