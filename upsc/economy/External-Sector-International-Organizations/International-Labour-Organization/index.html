International Labour Organization

The International Labour Organization (ILO) is a specialized agency of the United Nations mandated to advance social and economic justice by setting international labour standards.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding UN specialized agencies, core conventions, and India's ratification status. In Mains (GS Paper II/III), it is crucial for questions on labour reforms, informal economy, social security, and international governance.

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of International Labour Organization.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
