Foreign Exchange, Exchange Rate, NEER, REER, and ETF

Foreign exchange encompasses foreign currencies and assets held by a central bank, while exchange rates dictate the relative value of currencies. NEER (Nominal Effective Exchange Rate) and REER (Real Effective Exchange Rate) measure a currency's trade-weighted value against a basket of foreign currencies without and with inflation adjustments, respectively, whereas an Exchange Traded Fund (ETF) is an investment fund traded on stock exchanges holding assets like commodities or equities.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Essential for UPSC CSE Prelims (conceptual questions on currency valuation, inflation differentials, and financial instruments) and Mains GS Paper III (External Sector, Balance of Payments, Export Competitiveness, and Monetary Policy).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of foreign exchange reserves, exchange rate regimes, NEER, REER, and ETFs.
  • Evaluate trade competitiveness, inflation adjustments, and institutional frameworks governing external stability.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions regarding currency appreciation vs. depreciation.
