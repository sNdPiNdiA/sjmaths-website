International Finance Corporation

The International Finance Corporation (IFC) is an international financial institution established in 1956 that offers investment, advisory, and asset-management services to encourage private-sector development in developing countries. As a member of the World Bank Group, it focuses exclusively on the private sector in emerging markets.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC CSE GS Paper II (International Institutions, Global Economic Governance) and GS Paper III (Indian Economy, Mobilization of Resources, Growth & Development, Infrastructure Financing). Frequently tested for its distinct mandate compared to IBRD and IDA, its mobilization of private capital, and India's position as one of its largest client countries.
