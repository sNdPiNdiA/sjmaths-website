International Telecommunication Union

The International Telecommunication Union (ITU) is the specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for issues concerning information and communication technologies (ICTs), allocating global radio spectrum and satellite orbits, and developing technical standards.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding UN specialized agencies, headquarters, specialized bodies, and India's membership/elections. In Mains GS Paper II and III, it relates to international organizations, global governance of emerging technologies (AI, 5G/6G, cybersecurity), and digital inclusion.
