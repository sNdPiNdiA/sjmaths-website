African Development Bank

The African Development Bank (AfDB) Group is a multilateral development finance institution established in 1964 to spur sustainable economic development and social progress in African countries, contributing to poverty reduction.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding international financial institutions, India's membership status, voting shares, and concessional lending windows like the African Development Fund. Highly relevant for Mains GS Paper II (International Relations - Multilateral Groupings) and GS Paper III (Global Economy).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of African Development Bank.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
