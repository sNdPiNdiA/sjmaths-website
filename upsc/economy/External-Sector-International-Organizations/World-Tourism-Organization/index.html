World Tourism Organization

The World Tourism Organization (UN Tourism, formerly UNWTO) is the specialized agency of the United Nations responsible for the promotion of responsible, sustainable, and universally accessible tourism worldwide.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Frequently tested in UPSC Prelims regarding its UN status, headquarters, and global indices, while relevant in Mains GS-3 (Economy, Services Sector, Employment generation) and GS-1 (Geography, Sustainable Tourism).
