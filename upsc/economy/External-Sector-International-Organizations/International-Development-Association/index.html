International Development Association

The International Development Association (IDA) is the concessional financing arm of the World Bank Group that helps the world’s poorest countries by providing zero- to low-interest loans (called credits) and grants for programs that boost economic growth, reduce poverty, and improve people’s lives.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic under Economy (External Sector & International Organizations). Frequently tested in Prelims regarding World Bank Group membership, concessional lending windows, eligibility criteria (GNI per capita thresholds), and India's historical and current status (both as a former major borrower and now as a donor).
