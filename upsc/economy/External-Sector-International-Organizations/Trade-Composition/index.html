Trade Composition

Trade composition refers to the structural break-up and relative share of various commodity groups, value-added products, and services in a nation's total exports and imports. It reflects the underlying structural sophistication, industrial capacity, and global integration of an economy.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for UPSC Prelims (trend-based questions on India's top export/import items and partner countries) and Mains GS Paper 3 (Industrial policy, export-led growth, balance of payments, and integration into Global Value Chains).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
