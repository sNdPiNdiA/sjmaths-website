Indias External Debt

India's external debt refers to the total stock of residents' liabilities to non-residents, repayable in foreign currency, goods, or services, comprising both sovereign and non-sovereign obligations.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial for both Prelims (data points, composition, sustainability metrics like Debt-to-GDP ratio) and Mains (GS Paper III - Indian Economy, macroeconomic stability, capital account convertibility, and vulnerability to global shocks).

Learning Outcomesसीखने के परिणाम

  • Master foundational concepts and mechanisms of India's External Debt.
  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.

  • Balance of Payments
  • Capital Account
  • Forex Reserves
  • Monetary Policy of RBI
