Foreign Capital FDI FPI FII QFI

Foreign Capital encompasses all financial flows from abroad into a domestic economy, categorized fundamentally into Foreign Direct Investment (FDI) involving managerial control and Foreign Portfolio Investment (FPI) involving financial asset ownership without direct control.

Importance in UPSCUPSC में महत्व

Crucial microtopic for UPSC CSE Prelims (RBI regulations, FEMA, capital account convertibility, shareholding limits) and Mains GS Paper 3 (mobilization of resources, inclusive growth, macroeconomic stability, impact of foreign capital on domestic manufacturing and exchange rate).

  • Evaluate spatial distribution, institutional frameworks, and policy dimensions.
  • Apply high-yield mnemonics to solve tricky Prelims and Mains questions.
