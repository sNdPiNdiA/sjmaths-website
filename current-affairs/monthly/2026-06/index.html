The Zojila Tunnel

Context: Union Minister for Road Transport and Highways witnessed the historic final breakthrough blast of the Zojila Tunnel at Meenamarg in Kargil. Location: Connects Sonamarg (Baltal) in Jammu and Kashmir with Minamarg (Drass/Kargil) in Ladakh. Constructed By: National Highways and Infrastructure Development Corporation Limited (NHIDCL). Aim: The primary aim of the tunnel is to eliminate winter isolation for the Ladakh region. Historically, heavy snowfall, avalanches, and landslides shut down the Zojila Pass for up to six months each year, cutting off Ladakh completely.
