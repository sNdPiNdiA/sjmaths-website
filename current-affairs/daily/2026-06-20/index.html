Building a Nutritionally Secure India

This editorial is based on "NFHS-6 reveals progress amid nutrition challenges" published in The Hindu on 19/06/2026. The article emphasizes that healthcare gains mask deep feeding failures, maternal time poverty, and affordability gaps, requiring AI-driven PDS, crèches, and decentralized governance for true nutritional security. For Prelims: National Family Health Survey (NFHS)-6 2023-24, Maternal and Infant Mortality, Poshan Tracker, Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (PMGKAY), Minimum Support Price (MSP), Non-communicable Diseases (NCDs), Carbon Credit, Bio-fortified, Anemia. For Mains: Key achievements of India in nutritional security, Key factors hindering nutritional security in India and interventions required to enhance nutritional security. The National Family Health Survey (NFHS)-6 2023-24 report outlines a dual reality where robust institutional healthcare delivery and expanded immunization have successfully reduced child stunting. However, these programmatic gains are severely capped by a cereal-centric food system, rising adult obesity, and the high cost of micronutrient-dense diets. Addressing this requires shifting from mere caloric security to precise, multisectoral interventions that target maternal time poverty and gut health.
