Indocolea devendrae: Newly Discovered Bryophyte Genus from Kerala

Subject: Environment & Ecology / Biodiversity (Bryophytes, Plant Taxonomy, Western Ghats Biodiversity) Researchers from Kerala have discovered a new genus of bryophyte named Indocolea devendrae in the forests of northern Kerala. The discovery is significant because entirely new plant genera are rarely identified in India. Bryophytes are the simplest land plants and are often called the “amphibians of the plant kingdom” because they require water for reproduction.
