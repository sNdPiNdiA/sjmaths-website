The Ethanol Imperative for Viksit Bharat

This editorial is based on “India’s ethanol push requires a relook” published in The Hindu Business Line on 15/06/2026. The article emphasizes that India must shift from volume-driven ethanol targets to a sustainable framework, prioritizing 2G technology, water-use efficiency, and balanced resource allocation to mitigate environmental and economic risks. For Prelims: Ethanol Blending Programme, National Policy on Biofuels (2018), Flex-Fuel Vehicles (FFVs), Oilseeds, Pulses, Food Inflation, Carbon Footprint, Production-Linked Incentives (PLI), Circular Bio-economy, Viability Gap Funding (VGF). For Mains: Key facts regarding ethanol blending, its benefits and associated challenges, Steps needed to accelerate India’s ethanol blending program. India’s ethanol blending programme has successfully scaled production to achieve E20 targets, significantly aiding energy security and farm incomes. However, the sector now faces challenges regarding surplus capacity, water intensity, and food-fuel competition. Future policy must prioritize 2G technology, sustainable water management, and technological upgrades to ensure long-term viability.
