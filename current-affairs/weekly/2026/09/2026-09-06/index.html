National Affairs: BRICS, Arunachal Talks, WDFC and Major Policy Updates

NationalHigh importance6–12 September 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • The 18th BRICS Summit began in New Delhi on 12 September 2026; India held the 2026 chairship under the theme “Building for Resilience, Innovation, Cooperation and Sustainability”.
  • The summit highlighted expanded BRICS membership, partner countries, political and security cooperation, economic and financial cooperation, and cultural and people-to-people cooperation.
  • India and China held their first Corps Commander-level meeting in the Arunachal Pradesh sector, linked to the Wacha and Damai border meeting points and continuing LAC dialogue.
  • The final sections of the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor were dedicated to the nation, completing the JNPT/Navi Mumbai–Dadri connectivity; related projects were valued at about ₹20,700 crore.
  • The 7th Global Fintech Fest 2026 was held in Mumbai under the theme “Potential to Impact: Agentic AI, Tokenisation, Quantum – Trusted, Connected, Global Systems for Inclusive Finance”.
  • The 9th Rashtriya Poshan Maah was launched for 9 September–8 October 2026 under the theme “Hamari Anganwadi Hamari Jimmedari”, with focus on nutrition, protein, fresh meals, community ownership and early childhood development.
  • PM-SETU approved strategic investments of ₹735.70 crore for ITI clusters at Bhiwadi, Medchal and Meerut; cumulative sanctioned investment reached about ₹2,171 crore across nine clusters.
  • DILRMP 3.0 received an outlay of ₹565.50 crore for 2026–31, including a GIS-based integrated Land Stack and 14-digit Bhu-Aadhaar/ULPIN for land parcels.
  • Proposed Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules amendments require clearer sponsored-listing disclosure, search-result fairness and current-versus-previous price transparency; the new rules are to apply from 1 January 2027.
  • Digital Bharat Nidhi and Jharkhand signed an agreement under the Amended BharatNet Programme involving about ₹1,684 crore, 4,395 Gram Panchayats and nearly 26,777 villages.
  • NSO released district-level estimates for the unincorporated non-agricultural sector using ASUSE 2025 data for January–December 2025, covering establishments, workers and GVA.

The week combined major diplomatic and infrastructure developments with nutrition, land-record modernisation, digital connectivity, e-commerce regulation and district-level economic data.

Exam angle: BRICS chairship and theme, LAC dialogue, dedicated freight corridors, POSHAN, PM-SETU, ULPIN, BharatNet and ASUSE are relevant for UPSC, UPPSC, SSC, Banking and CDS/NDA.

Remember: BRICS New Delhi; WDFC JNPT–Dadri; DILRMP 3.0 ₹565.50 crore; PM-SETU ₹735.70 crore; BharatNet Jharkhand ₹1,684 crore.

International Affairs: BRICS, Correct the Map and Health Cooperation

InternationalHigh importance6–12 September 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • India hosted the 18th BRICS Summit in New Delhi with resilience, innovation, sustainability and Global South cooperation as key themes.
  • The UN General Assembly adopted the “Correct the Map” resolution by 164–1; the initiative was led by Initiative Africa and Togo and promoted projections that show relative continental size more accurately.
  • The 79th session of the WHO South-East Asia Regional Committee was held in Dili, Timor-Leste, from 7–10 September and produced the Dili Declaration on Human Resources for Health: Equity in Specialized Care.
  • The WHO session covered medical devices, diagnostics, health-facility resilience, patient and blood safety, TB elimination, and viral hepatitis, HIV and STI responses; India received recognition for maternal and neonatal tetanus elimination and NCD screening.
  • The UN General Assembly declared 27 November the International Day for the Elimination of Child, Early and Forced Marriage; Sierra Leone led the resolution and about 70 countries co-sponsored it.
  • MAHE, IIT Madras and IISc Bengaluru were selected as host institutions for the 2026–27 US–India Fellowship covering AI, quantum computing, semiconductors, biotechnology, energy, defence and space technology.

International developments focused on multilateral reform, fair map representation, specialised healthcare, child-marriage prevention and India–US academic and technology cooperation.

Exam angle: UN resolutions, WHO regional committees, international days, BRICS and India–US technology partnerships are useful for UPSC GS-2, UPPSC, SSC and Banking.

Remember: Correct the Map vote: 164–1; WHO RC79: Dili; Child Marriage Day: 27 November; Fellowship hosts: MAHE, IIT Madras and IISc.

Economy, Banking and Business: Forex, Ratings, MSME Finance and Partnerships

EconomyHigh importance6–12 September 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • India’s foreign-exchange reserves reached a record high of about US$785.7 billion in the week ending 4 September, rising by nearly US$45 billion in one week; RBI inflows between 5 June and 31 August were about US$136.3 billion.
  • Japan Credit Rating Agency upgraded India’s sovereign rating by one notch to A, citing growth, consumption, investment, financial-sector strength and a lower NPA ratio.
  • The Ministry of Finance secured about US$1 billion in IFC-linked long-term credit support for MSMEs; US$500 million was routed to SIDBI and the balance is to come through a lender syndicate.
  • ADB signed its first Transaction Advisory Services mandate in India with Madhya Pradesh for renewable-energy PPPs, solar and battery storage, with potential private investment of about US$1 billion.
  • RBI added Coastal Local Area Bank, Vijayawada, to the Second Schedule of the RBI Act, 1934; it was described as India’s first Local Area Bank and gained better access to RBI liquidity and money-market facilities.
  • NPCI International and Singapore-based FOMO Pay partnered to enable UPI QR acceptance for Singapore merchants, allowing Indian tourists to pay through UPI while merchants receive SGD settlement.
  • ONGC announced an Alternative Investment Fund of about ₹2,000 crore for deep-tech energy startups, covering AI subsurface intelligence, drilling, hydrogen, mobility, CBG, refinery technology and digital asset management.
  • JSW Group and Volkswagen Group signed a proposed 51:49 India passenger-vehicle JV focused on EVs, hybrids, ICE vehicles, localisation, manufacturing and R&D, with possible capacity of four lakh vehicles annually.
  • Qualcomm and AWS partnered on customised AI chips, AI inference and high-speed optical connectivity for AWS data centres; the stated optical capability was about 1.6 Tbps.
  • Bandhan Bank and Mastercard launched four credit-card variants: Sparks, Ignite, Flare and Lumina.

The week brought record forex reserves, a sovereign-rating upgrade, large MSME and renewable-energy financing arrangements, new payment connectivity and technology/manufacturing partnerships.

Exam angle: Remember the forex figure, JCR rating, IFC–MSME funding, Coastal Local Area Bank, UPI–Singapore, ONGC AIF and proposed JSW–Volkswagen ratio for economy and banking questions.

Science and Technology: CE20, Ocean Research, AI, Chips and Space Technology

Science & TechnologyHigh importance6–12 September 2026

  • ISRO conducted a successful hot test of the CE20 cryogenic engine at Mahendragiri, Tamil Nadu, at 220 kN thrust; the engine uses LOX and LH2 and is linked to the upgraded LVM3 C32 stage and Gaganyaan-related LTPM demonstration.

  • The India Photonics Association was launched as an ecosystem-led industry body focused on industry–academia collaboration, research, policy support and photonics innovation; the market was estimated at US$78.4 billion in 2025 and about US$155 billion by 2034.
