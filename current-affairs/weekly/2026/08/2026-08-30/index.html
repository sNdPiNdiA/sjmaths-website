InternationalHigh importanceSeptember 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • UN General Assembly endorsed the adoption of the Equal Earth map projection
  • Accurately represents relative land mass sizes of continents compared to the traditional Mercator projection
  • Provides true area representation for Africa and South America
  • India voted in favour of the UN resolution

The UN General Assembly backed the transition towards the Equal Earth projection to eliminate the spatial distortion inherent in the Mercator map, accurately depicting true continent sizes like Africa. India supported the resolution.

Exam angle: Equal Earth projection vs Mercator projection, cartography, UNGA resolutions (UPSC GS-1 Geography, GS-2 International Affairs).

Remember: Equal Earth Map Projection adopted by UN; fixes Mercator distortion of Africa & continents; India voted YES.
