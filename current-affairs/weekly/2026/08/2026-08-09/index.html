Assent to Prevention of Insults to National Honour (Amendment) Act, 2026

NationalHigh importanceAugust 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • President Droupadi Murmu gave assent to the amendment
  • Amends Section 3 of the 1971 Act
  • Criminalizes intentional disruption of singing the National Song (Vande Mataram)

President Droupadi Murmu gave assent to legislation amending Section 3 of the Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act, 1971. Draft National Food Security (Amendment) Bill, 2026

NationalHigh importanceAugust 2026

Key Facts & Highlights

  • Proposed amendment to Section 3(1) of the NFSA, 2013
  • AAY allocations shift from uniform household entitlement to individual entitlement
  • Individual allocation set at 7 kg per person/month, capped at 35 kg per household

The Union Government proposed an amendment to Section 3(1) of the National Food Security Act (NFSA), 2013. Under the proposal, Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) allocations shift from a uniform household entitlement (35 kg/household) to an individual entitlement of 7 kg per person per month, capped at a maximum ceiling of 35 kg per household, eliminating intra-category disparities across different family sizes.

Exam angle: UPSC GS-2/GS-3 (Welfare Schemes & Food Security), UPSSSC/Banking GA.

Remember: NFSA Draft Bill 2026: Shift to 7 kg/person monthly allocation for AAY households.
