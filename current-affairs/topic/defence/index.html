India’s Northeast Transformation

This editorial is based on “From borderland to India’s strategic resource frontier” published in The Hindu on 08/06/2026. It emphasizes that this strategic reimagining must prioritize inclusive development and local institutional trust to avoid replicating past tensions between national resource ambitions and indigenous rights. For Prelims: Critical Mineral, Lithium, Rare Earth Elements, India-Myanmar-Thailand (IMT) Trilateral Highway, Siliguri Corridor, Kaladan Multi-Modal Transit Transport Project, Subansiri Lower, Akhaura–Agartala Rail Link, PM-DevINE, PM Gati Shakti, BharatNet, Small-scale Hydro Project, Carbon Credit, Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR). For Mains: Strategic and geopolitical importance of northeast India, associated concerns, government initiatives for development and stability of northeast India, and steps needed for sustainable development of northeast India. The Indian government is strategically transforming the Northeast from a security-centric borderland into a critical mineral and industrial powerhouse. marking a pivotal shift in national development priorities. By integrating infrastructure, border management, and peace-building, the "Ashtalakshmi" region is evolving into the essential anchor of India’s Act East Policy, 2014 and long-term resource security framework.
