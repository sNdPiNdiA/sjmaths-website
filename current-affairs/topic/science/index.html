PIB Hyderabad Organises VARTALAP Media Workshop at Mahabubnagar

The Press Information Bureau (PIB), Hyderabad, organised a Special VARTALAP Media Workshop at Mahabubnagar as part of the nationwide campaign “12 Saal Vishwas Ke, Vikas Ke, Jan Kalyan Ke”. The workshop was inaugurated by Shri P. Srinivas, District Public Relations Officer (DPRO), Mahabubnagar, in the presence of Smt. Varganti Gayathri, Media & Communication Officer, PIB Hyderabad. Around 140 journalists from Mahabubnagar participated in the workshop. Addressing the gathering, Shri P. Srinivas called upon journalists to play a proactive role in creating awareness about various Government welfare schemes among citizens. He stressed the importance of fact-checking and responsible journalism in the era of artificial intelligence and digital communication. He urged media professionals to verify information before publication and contribute towards building an informed society through ethical reporting. Speaking on the occasion, Smt. Varganti Gayathri highlighted the objectives of the VARTALAP initiative and emphasised the importance of effective communication in disseminating information about Government programmes and welfare schemes.She also elaborated on the role of PIB in bridging the gap between Government initiatives and the public through credible and transparent communication. The workshop featured sessions by eminent journalist Shri Muddam Narasimha Swamy who delivered a presentation on the achievements of the Central Government during the last 12 years, highlighting major developmental initiatives, welfare programmes and governance reforms of the Central Government. The session provided participants with an overview of key milestones of Central government achievements in this 12 years. Ms. B.N. Satya Priya conducted a session on fact-checking and combating misinformation in the digital age. She highlighted the importance of verifying information through reliable sources, identifying misleading content and adopting best practices for responsible reporting. The interactive session encouraged participants to strengthen their fact-checking capabilities and uphold journalistic credibility in dessiminating government policies.

BRICS Nations Call for Stronger MSME Collaboration, Sustainability and Global Integration at Inaugural BRICS MSME Forum 2026

The Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises (MSME), Government of India successfully convened the Inaugural BRICS MSME Forum 2026 in Agra, bringing together government representatives, policymakers, industry leaders, and private-sector stakeholders from BRICS Member and Partner Countries to deliberate on strengthening MSME ecosystems, fostering sustainability, and enhancing global competitiveness. On the occasion, Shri Jitan Ram Manjhi, Minister of MSME, Government of India, emphasized the importance of collective action in shaping the future of MSMEs across BRICS nations. "MSMEs are central to achieving inclusive and sustainable economic development. The challenges facing enterprises today whether related to financing, technology adoption, sustainability or market access require collaborative solutions. BRICS countries possess immense strengths and complementary capabilities. Through continued dialogue and cooperation, we can build a robust framework that empowers MSMEs to become drivers of innovation, exports and employment," he noted.

NIXI Marks 23rd Foundation Day

The National Internet Exchange of India (NIXI) today celebrated its 23rd Foundation Day, bringing together policymakers, industry leaders, members of the internet community, academia, and technology experts to reflect on India's digital journey and discuss the future of internet-led growth. A key highlight of the celebrations was the launch of four new digital initiatives by the Secretary, Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India, and Chairman, NIXI. These included the IX Portal, myIRINN Portal, .IN Auction Portal, and an AI-Powered WHOIS Screening Platform. The portals are aimed at enhancing user experience, improving operational efficiency, bringing transparency and strengthening monitoring and verification processes across India's internet ecosystem. The AI-powered WHOIS screening platform will help enhance domain name security and strengthen the detection of suspicious websites hence strengthening India's .in domain space. The event also recognised and felicitated organisations and stakeholders whose contributions have helped strengthen India's internet ecosystem and accelerate the growth of the country's digital infrastructure.

National-Level Hackathon “STATATHON – A Data Journey for Viksit Bharat” Successfully Concludes, Showcasing Innovative Data Solutions by Young Talent

The national-level hackathon, “STATATHON – A Data Journey for Viksit Bharat”, concluded successfully, bringing together young innovators from across the country to develop data-driven solutions to challenges in official statistics. This was organised by NSO, India in collaboration with the Innovation Cell of the Ministry of Education. The Grand Finale had participation from 25 teams. Participants worked on five challenges, representing different domains of official statistics. Two prizes were awarded in each category. In the first category, a Start-Up named Airavata Technologies from Maharashtra secured the top prize, followed by Sri Sairam Engineering College, Tamil Nadu. Bharat Institute of Engineering & Technology, Telangana and Sri Ramakrishna College of Arts and Science, Tamil Nadu claimed the first and second prizes, respectively, in the second category. In the third category, the Institute of Technical Education & Research (Faculty of Engineering and Technology), Odisha emerged victorious, with R.M.K. Engineering College, Tamil Nadu, as the runner-up. Bhagwan Parshuram Institute of Technology, Delhi and Chennai Institute of Technology, Tamil Nadu, secured the winner and runner-up positions, respectively, in the fourth category. In the fifth category, Jai Parkash Mukand Lal Innovative Engineering and Technology Institute, Haryana won the first prize, while Kongu Engineering College and Matrix Labs, a start-up, both from Tamil Nadu, jointly finished as runner-up.
