Limited manufacturing depth, financialisation, and historically commerce-oriented business practices have reduced incentives for innovation. As a result, India’s R&D intensity remains below the level needed to support its long-term economic and technological ambitions. The Long Shadow of Colonial Deindustrialisation Empirical Evidence from Developed Markets (Lazonick, HBR 2014 – US S&P 500 companies, 2003-2012): Public vs. Private Companies (Asker, Farre-Mensa, Ljungqvist, 2015): Democracy, Uncertainty, and the Discount Rate Indian firms underinvest in R&D due to interacting structural and institutional factors.
