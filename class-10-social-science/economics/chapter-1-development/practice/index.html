NCERT-based practice

Different goals

Explain how a landless worker, a prosperous farmer, an unemployed youth and a rural woman may define development differently.

Conflict case

A dam brings electricity but submerges tribal land. Identify beneficiaries, affected groups and a fair way to decide.

Income calculation

Country income is ₹12,00,000 and population is 200. Calculate per capita income.

Average versus equality

Country A incomes: 9,500, 10,500, 9,800, 10,000, 10,200. Country B: 500, 500, 500, 500, 48,000. Calculate both averages and explain which is more equitable.

State indicators

Use the Haryana, Kerala and Bihar data to explain why higher per capita income does not automatically mean better development.

Public facilities

Give three examples where collective provision is cheaper or better than individual provision.

Human development

Compare India with Sri Lanka, Bangladesh and Nepal using income, life expectancy and schooling data.

Sustainability

Suggest local actions to reduce groundwater overuse and dependence on fossil fuels.

Activities from the chapter

Development goals table

Complete goals for rural labourers, farmers dependent on rain, urban unemployed youth, children from rich families and an adivasi affected by a dam.

National development debate

List goals for your locality. Mark which benefit many people, which conflict, and how a democratic decision can be fair.

Education data

Use the Uttar Pradesh rural data: literacy — male 76%, female 54%; child literacy — male 90%, female 87%; attendance — male 85%, female 82%.

BMI activity

BMI = weight in kg ÷ height in metres². Discuss nutrition, food and exercise sensitively; never use results to shame anyone.
