1. Per capita income is
A.Total population ÷ total income
B.Total income ÷ total population
C.Income × population
D.Only government income
Answer: B
2. Which is a non-income goal²
A.Respect and security
B.Only high consumption
C.Only exports
D.Only total income
Answer: A
3. IMR measures deaths before the age of
A.One year
B.Five years
C.Ten years
D.Fifteen years
Answer: A
4. HDI combines
A.Health, education and income
B.Only income
C.Only population
D.Only trade
Answer: A
5. Why can average income mislead²

B.It shows every person's income

D.It measures health
Answer: A
6. Groundwater is overused when
A.Use exceeds recharge
B.Rainfall occurs
C.Wells are covered
D.Crops grow
Answer: A
7. Crude oil is
A.Non-renewable
B.Renewable every year
C.A public facility
D.An HDI indicator
Answer: A
8. Public facilities include
A.Schools and healthcare
B.Only private cars
C.Only luxury goods
D.Only exports
Answer: A
