NCERT exercise answers
1. Development of a country can generally be determined by
Answer: (iv) all the above — income, literacy and health together indicate development.
2. Neighbouring country with better human development than India
Answer: (ii) Sri Lanka.
3. Fourth family's income
Answer: Average ₹5,000 for four families gives total income ₹20,000. Known incomes total ₹14,000, so the fourth family earns ₹6,000: option (iv).
4. World Bank criterion and limitation
Answer: The World Bank mainly uses per capita income. It hides inequality and ignores health, education, security, equality and environmental quality.
5. UNDP and World Bank
Answer: World Bank focuses mainly on per capita income. UNDP uses health, education and income in the Human Development Index.
6. Why averages are used
Answer: Averages make comparison possible across different-sized groups. They can hide unequal distribution; the same average can coexist with widespread poverty.
7. Kerala and Haryana
Answer: Per capita income is useful but incomplete. Kerala's better health and education outcomes show that it must be used with other indicators.
8. Present and future energy sources
Answer: India uses coal, petroleum, natural gas, hydropower, nuclear power, solar, wind and biomass. Future choices should expand renewables, storage and energy efficiency.
9. Why sustainability matters
Answer: Overuse of water, forests, soil and fossil fuels damages present life and leaves fewer resources for future generations.
10. Earth, needs and greed
Answer: Resources can meet essential needs when shared and used carefully. Unlimited consumption causes inequality, depletion and environmental degradation.
11. Examples of environmental degradation
Answer: Air pollution, plastic waste, contaminated water, loss of trees, groundwater decline, noise and soil damage are suitable local examples.
12. Table 1.6 comparison
Answer: Use each column separately: compare GNI per capita, life expectancy, mean years of schooling and HDI rank; state the highest and lowest country from the table.
13. BMI and undernourishment
Answer: Kerala has lower undernourishment than Madhya Pradesh. Food availability alone is insufficient; access, poverty, health, sanitation, care and distribution also affect nutrition.
