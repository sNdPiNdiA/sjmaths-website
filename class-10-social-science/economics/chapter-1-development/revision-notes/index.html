Development

  • Means progress towards a better life.
  • Different people have different goals.
  • One group's development can conflict with another's.

Income and non-income goals

  • Income helps meet material needs.
  • Freedom, equality, security, respect and safe surroundings are equally important.
  • A good life needs a mix of goals.

National development

  • Consider many groups and conflicts.
  • Choose a fair path that benefits a large number of people.
  • Democratic discussion matters.

Per capita income

Total income ÷ total population

Useful for comparison; does not show distribution.

Public facilities

  • Collective schools, hospitals, water, sanitation, security and PDS improve life.
  • Money alone cannot buy clean air, disease prevention or community safety.

Indicators

  • IMR: deaths before age one per 1,000 live births.
  • Literacy: literate people aged 7+.
  • Net attendance ratio: 15–17-year-olds attending school as a percentage of that age group.
  • Life expectancy: expected years of life at birth.

Human development

  • UNDP uses health, education and income.
  • HDI means Human Development Index.
  • PPP makes international income comparisons more meaningful.

Sustainability

  • Use renewable resources no faster than they are replenished.
  • Non-renewables have limited stocks.
  • Protect resources for future generations.

Data recall

  • Kerala had lower IMR and higher literacy/attendance than Haryana and Bihar.
