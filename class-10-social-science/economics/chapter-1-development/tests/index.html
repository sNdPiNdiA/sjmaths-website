Write point-wise answers. Use NCERT terms, indicators, examples and a balanced conclusion.

5. Explain two limitations of average income.

7. Define IMR, literacy rate and net attendance ratio.
8. Explain why Kerala can have better human development outcomes than Haryana despite lower per capita income.
9. What are public facilities² Give four examples.
10. How does UNDP measure development differently from the World Bank²
11. What is PPP and why is it used in international income comparisons²

13. Use groundwater to explain sustainable development.

15. Explain the statement: resources should meet needs, not greed.
