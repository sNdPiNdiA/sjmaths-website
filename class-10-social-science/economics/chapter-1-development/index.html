Chapter Overview

What You'll Learn in Development

Development is more than a rise in income. It is about meeting people's different aspirations while ensuring security, dignity, equal opportunity and a healthy environment for the future.

Key Topics Covered

- Different people, different development goals.
- Income and other criteria for comparison between countries and states.
- Per capita income, public facilities and the Human Development Report.
- Sustainable development and responsible use of natural resources.
