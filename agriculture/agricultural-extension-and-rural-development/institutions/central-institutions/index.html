Evolution and Framework of Agricultural Research and Extension in India

Agricultural research and extension in India have evolved from rudimentary colonial administrative units into a sophisticated, multi-tiered national network. The foundation of modern agricultural science began with the establishment of the Imperial Agricultural Research Institute (IARI) at Pusa, Bihar, in 1905, which was later relocated to New Delhi following the 1934 earthquake. Following the Royal Commission on Agriculture (1928), the Imperial Council of Agricultural Research was established on July 16, 1929, which was subsequently renamed the Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR).

  • Apex Body: ICAR functions as the apex body for coordinating, guiding, and managing research and education in agriculture and animal husbandry across India.
  • Union Minister Role: The Union Minister of Agriculture serves ex-officio as the President of the ICAR Society.
  • Administrative Head: The Secretary, Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India, serves as the Director-General of ICAR.

Structure of Central Agricultural Institutions under ICAR

The institutional framework governed by ICAR is vast, decentralized, and highly specialized, encompassing Deemed Universities, National Institutes, Central Agricultural Universities, National Research Centres (NRCs), National Bureaus, and All India Coordinated Research Projects (AICRPs).

  • Deemed Universities: Prominent institutions possessing university status include the Indian Agricultural Research Institute (IARI, New Delhi), Indian Veterinary Research Institute (IVRI, Izatnagar), National Dairy Research Institute (NDRI, Karnal), and Central Institute of Fisheries Education (CIFE, Mumbai).
  • National Institutes: Specialized establishments like the National Academy of Agricultural Research Management (NAARM) in Hyderabad focus on agricultural management capacity building and leadership development.
  • Central Agricultural Universities: Institutions like Dr. Rajendra Prasad Central Agricultural University (Pusa, Bihar), Central Agricultural University (Imphal, Manipur), and Rani Lakshmi Bai Central Agricultural University (Jhansi, Uttar Pradesh) cater to regional and national educational demands.

National Bureaus and Commodity Research Institutes

Conservation of genetic resources and intensive commodity-specific research are spearheaded by specialized national bureaus and central research institutes distributed across diverse agro-climatic zones of India.

  • NBPGR: The National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources (New Delhi) is mandated with the acquisition, introduction, exchange, evaluation, and cryopreservation of plant genetic resources.
  • Commodity Institutes: Examples include the Central Rice Research Institute (CRRI, Cuttack), Central Potato Research Institute (CPRI, Shimla), Central Tobacco Research Institute (CTRI, Rajahmundry), and Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research (IISR, Lucknow).
  • Horticultural and Commercial Crops: Central Institute for Subtropical Horticulture (CISH, Lucknow) and Indian Institute of Vegetable Research (IIVR, Varanasi) play pivotal roles in horticultural advancement in Uttar Pradesh.

Extension Systems: KVKs and ATARI Network

Frontline agricultural extension in India is driven by the Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) system, operationalized under the administrative control of ICAR agricultural universities, ICAR institutes, and non-governmental organizations.

  • Inception: The first KVK was established in 1974 at Puducherry (Pondicherry) under the administrative control of TNAU, following the recommendations of the Mohan Singh Mehta Committee (1974).
  • Mandate: Technology assessment and refinement through On-Farm Testing (OFT), Frontline Demonstrations (FLD), and capacity building of farmers and extension personnel.
  • ATARI: Agricultural Technology Application Research Institutes (ATARIs) monitor, coordinate, and review the functioning of KVKs across distinct geographical zones. Uttar Pradesh falls under ATARI Zone III (Kanpur) and Zone IV (Bareilly).

Recent Innovations and Institutional Reforms in Indian Agriculture

To bridge the digital and productivity divide, contemporary central institutions have integrated advanced information technology, precision agriculture tools, and startup incubation ecosystems.

  • DARE/ICAR Initiatives: Implementation of Farmer FIRST, ARYA (Attracting and Retaining Youth in Agriculture), and Mera Gaon Mera Gaurav (MGMG) to establish direct institutional linkages with farming communities.
  • Agri-Tech Infrastructure: National Agricultural Science Fund (NASF) and National Agricultural Higher Education Project (NAHEP) designed to upgrade academic rigor and frontier research in SAUs and ICAR institutes.

⚡ Chapter Master Summary: Central Agricultural Institutions and Extension Organizations in India

Systematic concept review designed for quick retention and last-minute pre-exam revision.

Concept 1

Apex Governance of Agricultural Research

ICAR operates as an autonomous organization under the Department of Agricultural Research and Education (DARE), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare.

  • DARE-ICAR Integration: The Secretary of DARE concurrently functions as the Director-General of ICAR, ensuring administrative synchronization.
Concept 2

Evolution and Genesis of KVKs

KVKs act as district-level science and technology dissemination centers, translating laboratory research into actionable on-farm applications.

  • First KVK: Established in 1974 at Puducherry under the administrative umbrella of Tamil Nadu Agricultural University (TNAU).
Concept 3

Role of National Bureaus

Preservation of biodiversity is institutionalized through specialized bureaus for plants, animals, agriculturally important microorganisms, and fish genetic resources.

  • NBPGR Mandate: Introduction, quarantine, evaluation, and long-term cold storage conservation of plant genetic material.
Concept 4

Commodity-Specific Research Stations

Targeted research on staple foods, commercial cash crops, and horticultural species is concentrated in specialized national institutes.

  • IISR Lucknow: Focuses exclusively on sugarcane agronomy, varietal improvement, and crop protection in subtropical India.

📚 Technical Terms Glossary

ICAR (Indian Council of Agricultural Research)
The apex apex organization coordinating agricultural research and education in India, established in 1929.
KVK (Krishi Vigyan Kendra)
District-level agricultural science center designed for frontline extension and technology assessment.
ATARI (Agricultural Technology Application Research Institute)
An institutional unit that monitors, evaluates, and guides KVK operations within designated geographical zones.
IARI (Indian Agricultural Research Institute)
Premier agricultural deemed university located in New Delhi, historically known as Pusa Institute.
NBPGR (National Bureau of Plant Genetic Resources)
Central institution responsible for plant exploration, exchange, quarantine, and germplasm conservation.
IVRI (Indian Veterinary Research Institute)
Apex institution for veterinary science and animal disease research located in Izatnagar, Uttar Pradesh.
NDRI (National Dairy Research Institute)
Premier research and educational institution for dairying, located in Karnal, Haryana.
NAARM (National Academy of Agricultural Research Management)
ICAR institution focused on capacity building in agricultural research and educational management in Hyderabad.
CPRI (Central Potato Research Institute)
Central commodity research institute dedicated to potato science, located in Shimla.
IISR (Indian Institute of Sugarcane Research)
Specialized ICAR institute focused on sugarcane production technology, located in Lucknow.
DARE (Department of Agricultural Research and Education)
Government department under the Ministry of Agriculture coordinating central agricultural administration.
OFT (On-Farm Testing)
A KVK trial methodology designed to assess technological feasibility and economic viability under local farming conditions.

⚖️ Concepts Often Confused in Examinations

IARI VS ICAR

IARI is a premier research institute and Deemed University, whereas ICAR is the apex governing council for all agricultural research institutions in India.

KVK VS ATARI

KVK is the district-level frontline extension center, while ATARI is the zonal institute that monitors and coordinates KVK activities.
