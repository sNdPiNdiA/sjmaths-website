Khasra: Legal Village Land Record in Indian Agricultural Administration

Khasra is a crucial legal revenue document in India containing comprehensive plot-wise details of land ownership, area, crop cultivation, soil classification, and irrigation sources. Essential for Uttar Pradesh TGT and PGT Agriculture examinations, it forms the foundation of agricultural statistics and rural land administration.

Introduction and Definition of Khasra

Khasra is a Persian-derived legal term used in the revenue administration of North Indian states, including Uttar Pradesh, Haryana, Punjab, and Bihar. It serves as a plot-wise register of agricultural lands maintained at the village level.

  • Maintained by the village revenue official known as the Lekhpal (formerly Patwari).
  • Contains exact numerical survey numbers (Khasra numbers) assigned to every distinct parcel of agricultural land within a revenue village (Mauza).
  • Acts as a primary document for identifying land parcels during agricultural census, crop cutting experiments, and agricultural taxation.

Contents and Information Recorded in Khasra

The Khasra document contains exhaustive details regarding land use, agronomic practices, and ownership parameters for each agricultural season across the agricultural year (fasli year).

  • Plot Number (Khasra Number): Unique identification number assigned to every land parcel during revenue settlement surveys.
  • Area and Ownership: Total geographical area, cultivated area, uncultivated area, name of the recorded landowner (Khatedar), and tenant particulars.
  • Crop Details: Specific crops grown during different agricultural seasons—Kharif (Autumn), Rabi (Spring), and Zaid (Summer).
  • Soil Type and Irrigation: Classification of soil texture/fertility status and the primary source of irrigation (e.g., tube well, canal, open well).

Agricultural Seasons (Fasli Years) and Khasra Entries

In Uttar Pradesh, agricultural land revenue and crop inspections (Girdawari) are tied to the Fasli calendar year, which begins on July 1st and ends on June 30th.

  • Kharif Season (Fasli Quarter 1): Crop inspection conducted by the Lekhpal starting around August 10th (locally known as Padtal).
  • Rabi Season (Fasli Quarter 2): Crop inspection initiated around January 1st to record winter crops like wheat (Triticum aestivum), barley (Hordeum vulgare), and chickpea (Cicer arietinum).
  • Zaid Season (Fasli Quarter 3): Inspection conducted around May 1st covering summer catch crops, green manures, and cucurbitaceous vegetables.

Significance of Khasra in Agricultural Economics and Policy

Khasra data is indispensable for macro-level agricultural planning, crop estimation surveys, and disaster relief disbursements.

  • Provides empirical baseline data for calculating crop yields via Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs).
  • Enables governments to disburse agricultural input subsidies, minimum support price (MSP) procurement passes, and crop insurance claims (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana).
  • Serves as legally admissible evidence in civil and revenue courts regarding land possession and boundary disputes.

Digitization of Revenue Records: Computerized Khasra in Uttar Pradesh

Under modern e-governance initiatives by the Board of Revenue, Uttar Pradesh, manual Khasra registers have been computerized under portals like Bhulekh UP.

  • Farmers can access real-time Khasra status, plot area, and mutation records online using unique plot identification numbers.
  • Reduces human discretion, minimizes land litigation, and ensures transparency in rural land transactions.
  • Integrates geospatial data with textual revenue records for precise remote sensing-based crop acreage estimation.

⚡ High-Yield Exam Points (UP TGT / PGT Focus)
  • Khasra is a plot-wise legal revenue document maintained by the village Lekhpal in Uttar Pradesh.
  • The agricultural crop inspection recorded in the Khasra register is legally designated as Girdawari or Padtal.
  • The agricultural revenue year in Uttar Pradesh starts on 1st July and ends on 30th June (Fasli Year).
  • Khasra contains 22 standard columns detailing soil class, irrigation source, crop grown, seed rate/area, and yield status in traditional UP revenue manuals.
  • Khatauni is the legal record of rights (ROR) showing ownership holdings, whereas Khasra is primarily a field register of land utilization and crop tracking.
⚠️ Common Mistakes & Exam Pitfalls
  • Students often confuse Khasra with Khatauni; remember that Khasra is plot-centric (survey number wise), while Khatauni is owner-centric (account holder wise).
  • Many confuse the Lekhpal with the Tehsildar; the Lekhpal prepares the Khasra at the village level, while the Tehsildar exercises judicial and administrative control at the tehsil level.

⚡ Chapter Master Summary: Khasra: Legal Village Land Record in Indian Agricultural Administration

Systematic concept review designed for quick retention and last-minute pre-exam revision.

Concept 1

Definition and Purpose of Khasra

Khasra functions as the primary field book of agricultural statistics at the village level.

  • Khasra Register: A comprehensive plot-wise legal document containing physical, agricultural, and ownership particulars.
  • Lekhpal: The grassroots revenue functionary responsible for seasonal field verification and updating entries.
Concept 2

Girdawari and Crop Inspections

Girdawari is the systematic process of physical field inspection for recording crop status.

  • Three Seasons: Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid crop cycles inspected tri-annually.
  • Padtal: Field verification by village officials to prevent fraudulent crop declarations.
Concept 3

Columns and Data Structure

The layout of the Khasra register is standardized under state revenue codes.

  • 22 Columns: Traditional UP format capturing plot numbers, area, irrigation sources, tree counts, and ownership.
Concept 4

Digitization of Revenue Records

Modern agricultural administration utilizes digital land records for transparency.

  • Bhulekh UP: Online portal providing instant access to computerized Khasra and Khatauni documents.

📚 Technical Terms Glossary

Khasra (Plot-wise Agricultural Field Register)
A legal revenue document maintained at the village level recording plot-wise crop cultivation, soil type, and land use.
Khatauni (Record of Rights (ROR))
A legal revenue register showing land ownership accounts, holding sizes, and inheritance details of cultivators.
Girdawari (Seasonal Crop Inspection)
The physical field verification and recording of crops grown on every agricultural plot by the village Lekhpal.
Lekhpal (Village Revenue Accountant / Patwari)
The grassroots administrative official responsible for maintaining village revenue records, Khasra, and Khatauni.
Fasli Year (Agricultural Revenue Year)
The financial year for agricultural revenue in India, running from July 1st to June 30th.
Mauza (Revenue Village)
A distinct geographical revenue unit for which a separate Khasra and survey map is maintained.
Khatedar (Recorded Landholder / Tenant)
A farmer or landowner holding legal occupancy rights over a specific agricultural holding.
Shajra (Village Cadastral Map)
The scaled map of a revenue village showing boundaries and spatial layout corresponding to Khasra numbers.
Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij)
The official transfer of land ownership title in revenue records following sale, gift, or inheritance.
Tehsildar (Executive Revenue Officer)
The administrative head of a tehsil responsible for supervising revenue administration and land records.
Crop Cutting Experiment (CCE)
A scientific field method conducted on randomly selected Khasra plots to estimate average crop yields.

🎯 Must-Remember High-Frequency Facts

  • Fasli year runs from July 1 to June 30.
  • Traditional Khasra in UP contains 22 columns.
  • Online verification of Khasra in UP is done via Bhulekh portal.

📌 Quick Recap Bullet Points

  • Khasra is a plot-wise agricultural land record maintained by the Lekhpal in Uttar Pradesh.
  • It records crop cultivation across three agricultural seasons: Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid.
  • Girdawari is the official term for seasonal physical field inspections.
  • Khatauni is owner-centric, whereas Khasra is plot-centric.

⚖️ Concepts Often Confused in Examinations

Khasra VS Khatauni

Khasra records physical plot details, crops, and land use, while Khatauni records legal ownership and shares of landholders.

Question 1 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What primary information does a Khasra register provide in agricultural administration?

Explanation: Khasra is a plot-wise land record register that captures physical parameters such as crop grown, irrigation source, soil type, and area for every survey number.

Question 2 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Which village-level official is officially responsible for preparing and maintaining the Khasra register in Uttar Pradesh?

Explanation: The Lekhpal is the primary revenue official at the village level tasked with maintaining land records, including Khasra and Khatauni.

Question 3 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What is the term used for the seasonal crop inspection conducted by revenue officials to update the Khasra?

Explanation: Girdawari or Padtal refers to the periodical field inspection carried out by the Lekhpal to record crops and land use changes in the Khasra.

Question 4 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

During which months are the three crop inspections (Girdawari) typically conducted for Khasra updates in Uttar Pradesh?

Explanation: The Girdawari inspections for Khasra take place around August for Kharif, January for Rabi, and May for Zaid crops.

Question 5 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What is the duration of the Fasli year used in Indian revenue administration and Khasra maintenance?

Explanation: The Fasli year begins on July 1st and ends on June 30th, spanning across agricultural seasons from Kharif to Zaid.

Question 6 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

How does Khasra differ fundamentally from Khatauni?

Explanation: Khasra provides plot-by-plot physical details and crop data, whereas Khatauni is a record of rights (ROR) detailing ownership holdings and shares of cultivators.

Question 7 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What is the traditional number of columns found in the standard Khasra register in Uttar Pradesh?

Explanation: The traditional Khasra register in UP contains 22 columns detailing plot numbers, area, soil classification, irrigation source, cultivator name, and crop details.

Question 8 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What is the legal name of the village cadastral map that corresponds directly with Khasra plot numbers?

Explanation: Shajra is the village cadastral map drawn to scale, where every numbered polygon corresponds to a specific Khasra number in the textual register.

Question 9 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Which online portal is established by the Government of Uttar Pradesh for public access to computerized Khasra and Khatauni records?

Explanation: Bhulekh UP (upbhulekh.gov.in) is the official portal for viewing computerized land records, Khasra details, and ownership records in Uttar Pradesh.

Question 10 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Why is Khasra data essential for conducting Crop Cutting Experiments (CCEs)?

Explanation: Khasra data provides the sampling frame of cultivated plots, enabling statistical agencies to select random plots for conducting CCEs to estimate state crop production.

Question 11 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

If a farmer wants to prove legal ownership of a specific land parcel in a court of law, which document among revenue records carries primary legal evidentiary weight regarding ownership?

Explanation: While Khasra shows physical possession and seasonal cultivation, Khatauni is the legal Record of Rights (ROR) that establishes ownership title.

Question 12 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What term is used for the administrative process of updating land ownership in revenue records after a property sale or inheritance?

Explanation: Mutation (Dakhil-Kharij) is the process whereby ownership changes are officially recorded in the Khatauni and Khasra records.

Question 13 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Which officer serves as the immediate supervisory authority over the village Lekhpal in revenue administration?

Explanation: The Supervisor Kanoongo (Revenue Inspector) directly supervises the work of Lekhpals within a designated circle.

Question 14 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Under which administrative department does the maintenance of Khasra and land records fall in Uttar Pradesh?

Explanation: The Board of Revenue, Uttar Pradesh, is the supreme body governing land administration, revenue courts, and land records like Khasra and Khatauni.

Question 15 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What agricultural activity is directly verified during the Zaid season inspection recorded in the Khasra?

Explanation: Zaid season crops include cucurbits, green fodder, vegetables, and green manure crops grown during summer, which are inspected around May.

Question 16 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

In the context of agricultural census operations in India, what role does the Khasra play?

Explanation: The Agricultural Census utilizes village Khasra and Khatauni records as the foundational frame for tabulating operational land holdings and land use patterns.

Question 17 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What does an entry indicating 'Banjar' in a Khasra register signify?

Explanation: Banjar refers to cultivable wasteland or barren land recorded in land revenue registers that is not currently under active agricultural production.

Question 18 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

Why are changes in cropping patterns recorded seasonally in the Khasra rather than annually?

Explanation: Seasonal recording is necessary because different crops are grown on the same plot across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons within the same agricultural year.

Question 19 of 19 Conceptual MCQ

What is the primary significance of the unique plot number (Khasra number) assigned to agricultural land?

Explanation: The Khasra number serves as a unique cadastral identifier linking the physical land parcel on the Shajra map to its entries in revenue records.

Test Question 1 of 10 TGT Standard

In UP revenue administration, if a dispute arises regarding the actual physical cultivation of wheat on a specific plot during the winter season, which record is consulted first by revenue authorities?

Solution & Rationale: Khasra records seasonal crop cultivation (Girdawari), making it the primary document for verifying which crop was actually grown on a plot during a specific season.

Test Question 2 of 10 PGT Standard

Consider the following statements regarding Khasra: 1. It is a plot-wise register. 2. It records legal ownership shares. 3. It is maintained by the Lekhpal. Which of the above statements are correct?

Solution & Rationale: Khasra is plot-wise and maintained by the Lekhpal (Statements 1 and 3 are correct). Legal ownership shares are recorded in the Khatauni, not the Khasra.

Test Question 3 of 10 PGT Standard

What is the specific administrative term for the official verification of village land records and crop inspection conducted by revenue inspectors (Supervisor Kanoongo)?

Solution & Rationale: Supervisor Kanoongo conducts test checks of field inspections (Girdawari/Padtal) performed by Lekhpals to ensure accuracy in Khasra records.

Test Question 4 of 10 TGT Standard

Which of the following land classification categories is typically NOT recorded as part of active agricultural cropping columns in a traditional Khasra?

Solution & Rationale: Residential or built-up areas (Ghar or Abadi) are non-agricultural land uses, whereas Chahi, Nahri, and Barani represent agricultural soil-irrigation categories recorded in Khasra.

Test Question 5 of 10 TGT Standard

How does computerization of Khasra records under programs like Bhulekh impact rural land administration?

Solution & Rationale: Computerization ensures transparent public access, prevents unauthorized manual alterations, and simplifies land verification for credit and subsidies.

Test Question 6 of 10 PGT Standard

In the structural layout of a standard UP Khasra document, what type of information is typically entered in the final concluding columns?

Solution & Rationale: The final columns of the Khasra register in UP record auxiliary details such as numbers of trees, rental values, and remarks regarding crop damage or natural calamities.

Test Question 7 of 10 PGT Standard

If a hailstorm damages a wheat crop in Uttar Pradesh, which official report derived from Khasra data is used to initiate relief distribution to affected farmers?

Solution & Rationale: Anawari reports, derived from seasonal Khasra/Girdawari assessments of crop condition, determine the extent of crop damage (in annas) required for disaster relief.

Test Question 8 of 10 TGT Standard

What is the relationship between the Shajra map and the Khasra register?

Solution & Rationale: The Shajra is the cadastral map of the village with spatial boundaries, and every polygon on this map bears a Khasra number matching the Khasra register.

Test Question 9 of 10 TGT Standard

Which of the following correctly describes the administrative hierarchy for rural land records in Uttar Pradesh from village level upward?

Solution & Rationale: The correct revenue administrative hierarchy is Lekhpal at the village level, supervised by Kanoongo, under the jurisdiction of the Tehsildar and SDM at the tehsil level.

Test Question 10 of 10 PGT Standard

Why is the Fasli year (July to June) adopted for agricultural revenue and Khasra recording instead of the calendar year (January to December)?

Solution & Rationale: The Fasli year was institutionalized during the Mughal and British periods to align with the agricultural cycle, starting with Kharif land preparation in July, which fits the natural crop calendar.
