The subject-wise Syllabus for SSC CGL Tier 1 is qualifying in nature and consists of four sections: Quantitative Aptitude, Reasoning, English, and General Awareness.

Quantitative Aptitude

Number System & Basic Arithmetic
0/0
  • Whole Numbers: Classification, prime numbers, composite numbers, factors
  • Divisibility Rules & Remainder Theorem
  • Decimals and Fractions: Recurring decimals, additions, comparisons
  • LCM and HCF: Core concepts and word problems
  • Square Roots & Cube Roots: Calculation techniques, surds & indices
Commercial Arithmetic
0/0
  • Percentage: Basic concepts, change, population, election problems
  • Ratio & Proportion: Proportional division, duplicate ratio, variation
  • Averages: Consecutive numbers, weighted average, speed average
  • Profit & Loss: Cost price/Selling price, dishonest dealer, successive discount
  • Simple Interest & Compound Interest: Installments, difference formulas
  • Partnership: Time and investment ratios
  • Mixture & Alligation: Multi-vessel mixing, replacement rules
Speed, Time & Work
0/0
  • Time and Work: Efficiency, wages, alternate days, group work
  • Pipes and Cisterns: Inlet/outlet pipes, leakage, filling times
  • Time, Speed & Distance: Relative speed, average speed, sound speed
  • Problems on Trains: Crossing poles, platforms, opposite/same direction
  • Boats & Streams: Upstream, downstream, still water speed
  • Races & Circular Motion: Linear races, circular tracks
Algebra
0/0
  • Basic Algebraic Identities: linear and quadratic equations
  • Elementary Surds: rationalization, surds comparison
  • Graphs of Linear Equations: slopes, intercepts, intersections
Geometry
0/0
  • Triangle & its Centres: Incentre, circumcentre, orthocentre, centroid
  • Congruence & Similarity of Triangles: Theorems (SAS, ASA, SSS)
  • Quadrilaterals: Parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, trapezium
  • Regular Polygons: Interior/exterior angles, diagonals calculation
  • Circles: Chords, tangent theorems, angles subtended, common tangents
Mensuration
0/0
  • 2D Mensuration: Areas, perimeters of triangles, quadrilaterals, circles
  • 3D Figures (Prism, Cone, Cylinder): Volume, CSA, TSA
  • 3D Figures (Sphere, Hemisphere, Pyramids): Volume, surface area
Trigonometry
0/0
  • Trigonometric Ratios: Sine, cosine, tangent values, conversions
  • Standard Identities: sin2 + cos2 = 1, complementary angles
  • Heights and Distances: Angles of elevation and depression
Data Interpretation
0/0
  • Histogram and Frequency Polygon: reading and analyzing intervals
  • Bar Diagrams & Pie Charts: percentage sharing, absolute values
