Data Collection & Presentation

  • Primary and Secondary data
  • Methods of data collection
  • Tabulation of data
  • Graphs and charts diagrammatic representation
  • Frequency distributions: construction and presentation

Central Tendency & Dispersion

  • Mean, Median, Mode calculation
  • Quartiles, Deciles, Percentiles
  • Range, Quartile deviation, Mean deviation
  • Standard deviation & relative dispersion

Moments, Correlation & Probability

  • Moments: Raw moments, Central moments, Sheppard's correction
  • Meaning of skewness, kurtosis, and measures
  • Scatter diagram, Simple correlation coefficient, Regression lines
  • Spearman's rank correlation & Association of attributes
  • Probability: Classical, empirical, conditional, Bayes' theorem

Distributions & Sampling

  • Random variable: expectation, variance, joint distribution
  • Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential distributions
  • Population vs Sample & Parameter vs Statistic
  • Sampling & non-sampling errors
  • Simple random, stratified, systematic, cluster sampling
  • Multistage, multiphase, purposive, convenience sampling

Inference & Analysis

  • Point & Interval estimation: Maximum likelihood, least squares
  • Hypothesis testing: Small and large sample tests
  • Z-test, t-test, Chi-square test, F-test, ANOVA
  • Time Series Analysis: components, measurement of trends
  • Index Numbers: construction, formulae, cost of living index
