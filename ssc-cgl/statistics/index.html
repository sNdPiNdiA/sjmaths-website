Basic Descriptive Statistics

  • Primary and Secondary data & Collection methods
  • Tabulation, Graphs & Diagrammatic presentation
  • Mean, Median, Mode, Quartiles, Deciles, Percentiles
  • Range, Quartile/Mean/Standard deviation, Relative dispersion
  • Moments, Skewness and Kurtosis and measures

Correlation, Regression & Probability

  • Scatter diagram & Simple correlation coefficient
  • Regression lines & Spearman's rank correlation
  • Association of attributes & Multiple regression/correlation
  • Probability: Conditional, Compound, Bayes' theorem
  • Random Variable & Probability Distributions (Binomial, Poisson, Normal, Exponential)

Sampling, Inference & Applied Stats

  • Sampling Theory: Random, Stratified, Cluster, Quota sampling
  • Statistical Inference: Estimation, Hypothesis testing, Z/t/Chi-square/F-tests
  • Analysis of Variance (ANOVA): One-way & Two-way classified data
  • Time Series Analysis: Components & Trend measurement
  • Index Numbers: Formulae, Construction, Base shifting & Cost of living index
