Vocabulary & Words

  • Synonyms and Antonyms: Core lists and contextual meanings
  • One Word Substitution: Categories (professions, studies, phobias)
  • Idioms and Phrases: meanings and usage in sentences
  • Spelling Correction: Commonly misspelled exam terms
  • Fill in the Blanks: Single/double blanks, vocabulary/grammar fits

Reading & Logic

  • Cloze Test: Passage context reading, option elimination
  • Sentence Rearrangement (Para-jumbles): Link finding, transition words
  • Reading Comprehension: Factual and inference questions
