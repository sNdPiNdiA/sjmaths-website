Analytical Reasoning Syllabus & Topics Guide

Comprehensive breakdown of the Analytical Reasoning syllabus for SSC CGL Tier 1 & 2, UPSC CSAT, and Banking exams with topic weightage, frameworks, and preparation strategies.

What is Analytical Reasoning?

Analytical Reasoning assesses a candidate's ability to examine complex information, identify underlying patterns, deduce logical conclusions, and evaluate arguments. The standard syllabus across SSC CGL, UPSC CSAT, and Banking exams is divided into two broad domains: Structural Logic/Puzzles (Seating Arrangements, Blood Relations, Direction Tests) and Critical Verbal Reasoning (Statement & Assumptions, Syllogisms, Course of Action, Cause & Effect, Data Sufficiency).

Complete Analytical Reasoning Syllabus Breakdown

6. Data Sufficiency Evaluating if Statement (1) alone or Statement (2) alone or both together suffice High (2–4 Questions) Moderate to High

4 Pro Strategies to Master Analytical Reasoning

  1. Draw Systematic Diagrams: Never solve seating or puzzle questions in your head. Always represent clues visually with arrows and positive/negative constraint markers.
  2. Never Assume Outside Knowledge: In critical verbal reasoning (Assumptions & Course of Action), restrict your logic strictly to what is provided in the given statement.
  3. Master Venn Diagrams for Syllogisms: Standardize your Venn diagram notation for "Some", "All", "No", and "Only a few" to eliminate ambiguity.
  4. Practice Elimination: In Data Sufficiency, check each statement independently first before combining them.
