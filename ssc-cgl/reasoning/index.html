Tips, Tricks & Core Concepts

Boost your speed and accuracy in the General Intelligence & Reasoning section with these exam-proven shortcuts and logical frameworks.

Alphabet Shortcuts

  • EJOTY Rule: Memorize letters at multiples of 5 (E=5, J=10, O=15, T=20, Y=25).
  • Reverse Pairs (Sum = 27): Opposite letters sum to 27 (A+Z=27, B+Y=27). Remember via words like AZadi, BYe, CuX, LOVE.

Number & Series Patterns

  • Multi-level Differences: Calculate first and second-level differences to expose hidden arithmetic progressions.
  • Prime & Power Relations: Keep a lookout for prime numbers and squares/cubes offsets (e.g., x^2 ± 1, x^3 ± x).

Venn & Blood Relations

  • Standard Symbols: Use + for male, - for female, double lines for couples, and horizontal lines for siblings.
  • Syllogism Venn: Draw minimal overlapping circles. Check "Either-Or" conditions when individual conclusions are doubtful.
