Paper 3: Finance & Economics (AAO Post)

Topic-wise syllabus of Paper 3 (General Studies: Finance & Economics) for candidates preparing for the Assistant Audit Officer (AAO) post in SSC CGL.

Part A: Finance & Accounts (80 Marks)

  • Nature, scope and limitations of Financial Accounting
  • Basic concepts, Conventions & GAAP principles
  • Single and double entry & Books of Original Entry
  • Bank Reconciliation statement & Trial Balance
  • Journal, ledgers & Rectification of Errors
  • Trading, Profit & Loss A/c, Balance Sheet & Appropriations
  • Capital vs Revenue Expenditure & Depreciation Accounting
  • Valuation of Inventories
  • Non-profit organisations Accounts & Bills of Exchange
  • Self Balancing Ledgers

Part B: Economics & Governance (120 Marks)

  • CAG of India (Constitutional provisions, role, duties)
  • Finance Commission (Role and functions)
  • Basic Concepts of Economics & Micro Economics (PPC, study methods)
  • Theory of Demand and Supply & Consumer behavior
  • Theory of Production and Cost (laws of returns)
  • Forms of Market & Price Determination
  • Indian Economy: National Income, Poverty, Unemployment, Agri/Services
  • Economic Reforms in India since 1991 (LPG reforms)
  • Money and Banking: RBI, Commercial banks, Monetary/Fiscal policy
  • Role of Information Technology in Governance
