Accounting Principles & Concepts

  • Nature, scope, and limitations of Financial Accounting
  • Accounting Concepts, Conventions, and GAAP
  • Single and Double Entry & Books of original entry

Accounting Process & Final Accounts

  • Journal, Ledgers, Trial Balance, Rectification of errors
  • Bank Reconciliation Statement (BRS)
  • Manufacturing, Trading, Profit & Loss Accounts
  • Balance Sheet & Capital vs Revenue expenditure
  • Depreciation accounting & Valuation of inventories
  • Non-profit accounts, receipts-payments, bills of exchange

Governance & Micro-economics

  • CAG: Constitutional role, duties, and powers
  • Finance Commission: role and functions
  • Micro-economics: theory of demand, supply, consumer behavior
  • Theory of Production and Cost & forms of market

Indian Economy & Reforms

  • Indian Economy: National Income concepts and measurement
  • Poverty, unemployment, economic infrastructure
  • Economic reforms in India since 1991 (LPG policies)
  • Money and Banking: RBI, monetary policy, FRBM Act 2003
  • Role of IT in Governance and administration
