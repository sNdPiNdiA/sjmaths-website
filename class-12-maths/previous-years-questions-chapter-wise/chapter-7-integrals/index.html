CBSE Board Evaluation & Answer Presentation Strategy:

Step-wise marking is strictly enforced across all 2-mark, 3-mark, and 5-mark subjective questions. Always quote standard algebraic identities or theorem statements before carrying out algebraic substitutions. For matrix operations and vector cross-products, clearly tabulate intermediate matrix rows and components to guarantee full partial credit even if a minor arithmetic slip occurs in the final step.

CBSE Class 12 Chapter 7: Integrals Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit III: Calculus (Contributes 8 to 10 Marks, the highest single-chapter weightage). Question blueprint: Includes 2 MCQs (1 Mark each), one 2-Mark or 3-Mark indefinite integral (using substitution, partial fractions, or integration by parts), and one 3-Mark or 5-Mark definite integral evaluated using properties (like King property: integral from 0 to a of f(x)dx = integral from 0 to a of f(a-x)dx).

Examiner Advice: Always write the constant of integration (+ C) for indefinite integrals. In integration by parts (ILATE rule), be careful with negative signs in consecutive integration steps. For definite integrals involving modulus functions |x - c|, split the limits of integration at the critical point c.
