CBSE Class 12 Chapter 9: Differential Equations Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit III: Calculus (Contributes 4 to 6 Marks). Question blueprint: Regularly features 1 MCQ on determining order and degree of a differential equation, and one 3-Mark or 5-Mark problem solving either a Homogeneous differential equation (put y = vx) or a Linear Differential Equation (dy/dx + Py = Q).

Examiner Advice: Remember that degree is only defined when the differential equation is a polynomial in its derivatives (e.g. dy/dx inside sin, cos, or e^(dy/dx) means degree is not defined). For linear differential equations, write the standard form first, calculate Integrating Factor IF = e^(integral P dx), and write the solution y * IF = integral (Q * IF) dx + C.
