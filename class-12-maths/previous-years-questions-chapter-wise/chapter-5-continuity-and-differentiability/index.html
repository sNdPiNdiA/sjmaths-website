CBSE Board Evaluation & Answer Presentation Strategy:

Step-wise marking is strictly enforced across all 2-mark, 3-mark, and 5-mark subjective questions. Always quote standard algebraic identities or theorem statements before carrying out algebraic substitutions. For matrix operations and vector cross-products, clearly tabulate intermediate matrix rows and components to guarantee full partial credit even if a minor arithmetic slip occurs in the final step.

CBSE Class 12 Chapter 5: Continuity and Differentiability Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit III: Calculus (Total 35 Marks across Chapters 5 through 9). Question blueprint: Usually comprises 1-2 MCQs (1 Mark), one 2-Mark or 3-Mark problem on finding constants (k, a, b) for a continuous function, and one 3-Mark or 5-Mark problem on logarithmic or second-order parametric differentiation.

Examiner Advice: For continuity at x = c, explicitly show that Left Hand Limit equals Right Hand Limit and equals f(c). When performing logarithmic differentiation for functions of form u(x)^v(x), remember to apply the product rule carefully after taking log on both sides. In second-order derivatives d^2y/dx^2 with parametric equations, do not forget the chain rule factor dt/dx.
