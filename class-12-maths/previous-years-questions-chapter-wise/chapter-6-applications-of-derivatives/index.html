CBSE Board Evaluation & Answer Presentation Strategy:

Step-wise marking is strictly enforced across all 2-mark, 3-mark, and 5-mark subjective questions. Always quote standard algebraic identities or theorem statements before carrying out algebraic substitutions. For matrix operations and vector cross-products, clearly tabulate intermediate matrix rows and components to guarantee full partial credit even if a minor arithmetic slip occurs in the final step.

CBSE Class 12 Chapter 6: Applications of Derivatives Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit III: Calculus (Contributes 6 to 8 Marks). Question blueprint: Features 1 MCQ on strictly increasing/decreasing intervals or rate of change, and a high-yield 4-Mark Case-Based Question (Section E) or 5-Mark optimization problem testing geometric maxima and minima (e.g. cylinder inscribed in a sphere, open cardboard box).

Examiner Advice: Always state the domain when finding intervals where a function is increasing or decreasing. In word optimization problems, express the quantity to be maximized or minimized in terms of a single variable, take the first derivative, set it to zero, and verify using the Second Derivative Test (d^2y/dx^2 < 0 for maximum, > 0 for minimum).
