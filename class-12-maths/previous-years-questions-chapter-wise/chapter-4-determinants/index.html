Always quote standard algebraic identities or theorem statements before carrying out algebraic substitutions. For matrix operations and vector cross-products, clearly tabulate intermediate matrix rows and components to guarantee full partial credit even if a minor arithmetic slip occurs in the final step.

CBSE Class 12 Chapter 4: Determinants Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit II: Algebra (Matrices & Determinants carry 10 Marks combined). Question blueprint: Typically 1 MCQ/Assertion-Reason (1 Mark) on determinant properties or singular matrices, and a high-yield 5-Mark long answer problem in Section D on solving a 3x3 system of linear equations using the Matrix Inversion Method (AX = B).

Examiner Advice: Always check if det(A) != 0 before proceeding to adj(A). When evaluating minors and cofactors, take extra care with alternating signs: A_ij = (-1)^(i+j) * M_ij. Remember the determinant relations: |kA| = k^n * |A| for an n x n matrix, and |adj(A)| = |A|^(n-1).
