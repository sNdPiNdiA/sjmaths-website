CBSE Class 12 Chapter 10: Vector Algebra Blueprint & Exam Strategy

Marks Weightage: Unit IV: Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry (14 Marks combined). Question blueprint: Typically includes 1-2 MCQs (1 Mark each) on dot product, unit vectors, or direction cosines, and one 2-Mark or 3-Mark problem on cross products, vector projection, or finding vectors perpendicular to given vectors.

Examiner Advice: Projection of vector a on vector b is (a . b) / |b|. Two non-zero vectors are perpendicular if a . b = 0, and parallel/collinear if a x b = 0 (or a = lambda * b). Area of a triangle formed by vectors a and b is (1/2)|a x b|, while area of a parallelogram with diagonals d1 and d2 is (1/2)|d1 x d2|.
