Atomic structure describes the organization of subatomic particles within atoms and is governed by quantum mechanical laws and wave equations. Quantum mechanics replaces classical trajectories with probability distributions defined by wavefunctions and quantum numbers. परमाणु संरचना परमाणुओं के भीतर उप-परमाण्विक कणों के संगठन का वर्णन करती है और यह क्वांटम यांत्रिकीय नियमों तथा तरंग समीकरणों द्वारा शासित होती है। क्वांटम यांत्रिकी (Quantum mechanics) शास्त्रीय प्रक्षेप पथों को तरंग फलनों (wavefunctions) और क्वांटम संख्याओं द्वारा परिभाषित प्रायिकता वितरण (probability distributions) से प्रतिस्थापित करती है। परमाणु संरचना परमाणुओं के भीतर उप-परमाण्विक कणों के संगठन का वर्णन करती है और यह क्वांटम यांत्रिकीय नियमों तथा तरंग समीकरणों द्वारा शासित होती है। क्वांटम यांत्रिकी (Quantum mechanics) शास्त्रीय प्रक्षेप पथों को तरंग फलनों (wavefunctions) और क्वांटम संख्याओं द्वारा परिभाषित प्रायिकता वितरण (probability distributions) से प्रतिस्थापित करती है।

Quantum Numbers and Orbital Characteristics क्वांटम संख्याएँ और कक्षक विशेषताएँ क्वांटम संख्याएँ और कक्षक विशेषताएँ

  • The principal quantum number (n) determines the main energy level and average distance of the electron from the nucleus.
  • The azimuthal quantum number (l) defines the orbital angular momentum and three-dimensional shape, ranging from 0 to (n - 1).
  • The magnetic quantum number (ml) specifies the spatial orientation of the orbital in a magnetic field, taking values from -l to +l.
  • The spin quantum number (s or ms) describes the intrinsic angular momentum of the electron, taking values of +1/2 or -1/2.
  • मुख्य क्वांटम संख्या (principal quantum number, n) मुख्य ऊर्जा स्तर और नाभिक से इलेक्ट्रॉन की औसत दूरी निर्धारित करती है।
  • दिगंशीय क्वांटम संख्या (azimuthal quantum number, l) कक्षक कोणीय संवेग और त्रिविमीय आकृति को परिभाषित करती है, जिसका मान 0 से (n - 1) तक होता है।
  • चुंबकीय क्वांटम संख्या (magnetic quantum number, ml) चुंबकीय क्षेत्र में कक्षक के त्रिविमीय अभिविन्यास को निर्दिष्ट करती है, जिसका मान -l से +l तक होता है।
  • चक्रण क्वांटम संख्या (spin quantum number, s या ms) इलेक्ट्रॉन के आंतरिक कोणीय संवेग का वर्णन करती है, जिसका मान +1/2 या -1/2 होता है।

Wavefunction and Radial-Angular Nodes तरंग फलन और त्रिज्यीय-कोणीय नोड तरंग फलन और त्रिज्यीय-कोणीय नोड

  • A radial node occurs where the radial probability density function equals zero, calculated as (n - l - 1).
  • An angular node corresponds to a planar or conical surface where the angular wavefunction is zero, equal to the azumithal quantum number l.
  • The total number of nodes in any given atomic orbital is equal to (n - 1).
  • The square of the wavefunction, ψ2, represents the probability density of finding an electron at a specific spatial point.
  • त्रिज्यीय नोड (radial node) वहाँ होता है जहाँ त्रिज्यीय प्रायिकता घनत्व फलन शून्य होता है, जिसकी गणना (n - l - 1) द्वारा की जाती है।
  • कोणीय नोड (angular node) एक समतलीय या शंक्वाकार सतह के अनुरूप होता है जहाँ कोणीय तरंग फलन शून्य होता है, जो दिगंशीय क्वांटम संख्या l के बराबर होता है।
  • किसी भी परमाणु कक्षक में नोड्स की कुल संख्या (n - 1) के बराबर होती है।
  • तरंग फलन का वर्ग, ψ², किसी विशिष्ट स्थानिक बिंदु पर इलेक्ट्रॉन के पाए जाने के प्रायिकता घनत्व (probability density) को दर्शाता है।

Filling Rules and Electronic Configuration इलेक्ट्रॉन भरने के नियम और इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास इलेक्ट्रॉन भरने के नियम और इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास

  • The Aufbau principle dictates that electrons fill orbitals in order of increasing energy, determined by the (n + l) rule.
  • Pauli exclusion principle states that no two electrons in the same atom can have identical sets of all four quantum numbers.
  • Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity requires that electron pairing in degenerate orbitals does not occur until each orbital is singly occupied with parallel spins.
  • Exchanges energy stabilization makes half-filled and completely filled subshells exceptionally stable due to symmetrical charge distribution.
  • आफबाऊ सिद्धांत (Aufbau principle) यह निर्धारित करता है कि इलेक्ट्रॉन ऊर्जा के बढ़ते क्रम में कक्षकों को भरते हैं, जो (n + l) नियम द्वारा तय होता है।
  • पाउली का अपवर्जन सिद्धांत (Pauli exclusion principle) बताता है कि एक ही परमाणु में किन्हीं दो इलेक्ट्रॉनों के लिए चारों क्वांटम संख्याओं का सेट समान नहीं हो सकता।
  • हुंड का अधिकतम बहुलता का नियम (Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity) यह अनिवार्य करता है कि समभ्रंश कक्षकों (degenerate orbitals) में इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्मन तब तक नहीं होता जब तक कि प्रत्येक कक्षक में समानांतर चक्रण के साथ एक-एक इलेक्ट्रॉन न भर जाए।
  • विनिमय ऊर्जा स्थिरीकरण (exchange energy stabilization) के कारण सममित रूप से अर्ध-पूरित और पूर्ण-पूरित उपकोश असाधारण रूप से स्थिर होते हैं।

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • The de Broglie wavelength is inversely proportional to the momentum of the moving particle.
  • Radial probability density curves show that 1s has maximum probability at the Bohr radius (52.9 pm).
  • The energy of orbitals in hydrogen depends solely on 'n', whereas in multi-electron systems it depends on both 'n' and 'l'.
  • Degenerate orbitals possess identical energies and belong to the same subshell in the absence of external magnetic or electric fields.
  • An f-subshell contains a maximum of 14 electrons distributed across 7 degenerate orbitals.
  • The total number of orbitals associated with a principal quantum number 'n' is given by n2.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing angular nodes (equal to l) with radial nodes (equal to n - l - 1).
  • Assuming multi-electron energy levels depend only on 'n' like hydrogen atom.
  • Forgetting that magnetic quantum number ml values include zero (-l to +l).
  • Writing incorrect electronic configurations for transition metal ions by removing electrons from the wrong subshell (e.g., removing from 3d instead of 4s first).

⚡ Quick Revision: Atomic Structure and Quantum Mechanics

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Wave Mechanical Model

Treats electrons as standing waves described by the Schrödinger equation, yielding quantized energy levels naturally.

Quantum Numbers

A set of four integers (n, l, ml, ms) that uniquely specify the energy, shape, orientation, and spin of an electron.

Orbital Geometries

Visualized via boundary surface diagrams enclosing 90% total electron probability density for s, p, d, and f orbitals.

Electronic Configuration Principles

Governed by Aufbau, Pauli, and Hund rules to establish ground-state electron distributions across atoms.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Principal Quantum Number (n)
Specifies main energy shell. Unitless integer (≥1).
Azimuthal Quantum Number (l)
Specifies subshell shape. Integer from 0 to n-1.
Magnetic Quantum Number (ml)
Specifies spatial orientation. Integer from -l to +l.
Spin Quantum Number (ms)
Specifies electron spin direction. Values +1/2 or -1/2.
Node (Wavefunction Node)
Region of zero probability of finding an electron.
Bohr Magneton (μB)
Unit of magnetic dipole moment, equal to eℏ/(2me).

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Total orbitals in shell n = n2
  • Total maximum electrons in shell n = 2n2
  • Radial nodes = n - l - 1
  • Angular nodes = l
  • Spin-only magnetic moment μ = √[n(n+2)] BM

📌 Quick Recall

  • Quantum mechanics replaces classical orbits with probability waves.
  • Four quantum numbers define every electron state.
  • Aufbau, Pauli, and Hund principles dictate ground state arrangements.
  • Anomalous configurations arise from exchange energy and stability of half/full subshells.

⚖️ Key Differences

Radial Node VS Angular Node

Radial node is a spherical shell of zero probability; angular node is a plane or cone passing through the nucleus.

Question 1 of 18

Which of the following sets of quantum numbers is not allowed?

Explanation: For n = 2, the maximum value of l is n - 1 = 1. Therefore, l = 2 is impossible.

Question 2 of 18

What is the total number of nodes present in a 4s orbital?

Explanation: Total nodes = n - 1. For 4s, n = 4, so total nodes = 4 - 1 = 3 (all radial nodes since l = 0).

Question 3 of 18

What is the de Broglie wavelength of an electron moving with a velocity of 3 × 106 m/s?

Explanation: λ = h / (m · v) = (6.626 × 10-34 J s) / ((9.1 × 10-31 kg) × (3 × 106 m/s)) ≈ 2.42 × 10-10 m.

Question 4 of 18

The ground state electronic configuration of Chromium (Z = 24) is:

Explanation: Chromium exhibits an exception to stability where an electron shifts from 4s to 3d to achieve a stable half-filled 3d5 configuration.

Question 5 of 18

Which orbital has three radial nodes and one angular node?

Explanation: Angular node = l = 1 (p-orbital). Radial nodes = n - l - 1 = n - 1 - 1 = n - 2. Given radial nodes = 3, n - 2 = 3 → n = 5. Thus, it is 5p.

Question 6 of 18

According to Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle, if the uncertainty in position is zero, the uncertainty in momentum is:

Explanation: Δx · Δp ≥ h / 4π. If Δx = 0, then Δp must approach infinity.

Question 7 of 18

What is the maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in a subshell with l = 3?

Explanation: For l = 3 (f-subshell), the number of orbitals is (2l + 1) = 7. Maximum electrons = 2 × 7 = 14.

Question 8 of 18

The spin-only magnetic moment of Fe2+ ion (Z = 26) is:

Explanation: Fe2+ has [Ar] 3d6 configuration with 4 unpaired electrons. μ = √[4(4+2)] = √24 ≈ 4.90 BM.

Question 9 of 18

Which of the following sets of quantum numbers describes an electron in a 3dz2 orbital?

Explanation: For 3d, n = 3 and l = 2. The ml value corresponding to dz2 is 0.

Question 10 of 18

The energy of an electron in the second Bohr orbit of hydrogen atom is:

Explanation: En = -13.6 × (Z2 / n2) = -13.6 × (1 / 22) = -13.6 / 4 = -3.4 eV.

Question 11 of 18

Which of the following transitions in hydrogen spectrum emits a photon of the highest frequency?

Explanation: Transition from n = 5 to n = 1 involves the largest energy drop, yielding the highest frequency and shortest wavelength (Lyman series limit).

Question 12 of 18

The number of radial nodes in a 3s orbital is:

Explanation: Radial nodes = n - l - 1 = 3 - 0 - 1 = 2.

Question 13 of 18

Which species is isoelectronic with Ne (Z = 10)?

Explanation: Na+, Mg2+, and F- all possess 10 electrons, exactly like Neon.

Question 14 of 18

The correct set of four quantum numbers for the outermost electron of Sodium (Z = 11) is:

Explanation: Sodium configuration is [Ne] 3s1. Outermost electron is in 3s: n = 3, l = 0, ml = 0, ms = +1/2.

Question 15 of 18

The orbital having zero angular momentum is:

Explanation: Angular momentum of an orbital is given by √[l(l+1)]ℏ. For s-orbital, l = 0, hence angular momentum is zero.

Question 16 of 18

Radial wave function distribution for 1s orbital shows maximum probability at:

Explanation: The maximum radial probability density for 1s in hydrogen occurs at r = 52.9 pm (Bohr radius).

Question 17 of 18

Pauli's Exclusion Principle restricts maximum electrons in any single orbital to:

Explanation: An orbital can hold a maximum of two electrons with opposite spins.

Question 18 of 18

What is the wavelength of a photon emitted when an electron drops from n = 4 to n = 2 in hydrogen?

Explanation: Using Rydberg formula: 1/λ = RH (1/22 - 1/42) = RH (1/4 - 1/16) = 3RH/16, yielding ≈ 486 nm (Balmer series, H-beta).
