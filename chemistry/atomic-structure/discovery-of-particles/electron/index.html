Electron इलेक्ट्रॉन (Electron)

The electron is a subatomic particle with a negative elementary electric charge of approximately 1.602 × 10-19 Coulombs. It constitutes the outer regions of the atom and determines the chemical reactivity of elements. इलेक्ट्रॉन एक उप-परमाण्विक कण (subatomic particle) है जिस पर लगभग 1.602 × 10-19 कूलॉम का ऋणात्मक प्राथमिक विद्युत आवेश होता है। यह परमाणु के बाहरी क्षेत्रों का निर्माण करता है और तत्वों की रासायनिक अभिक्रियाशीलता (chemical reactivity) को निर्धारित करता है।

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Discovery and Fundamental Properties खोज और मूलभूत गुण

  • The electron was discovered by J.J. Thomson in 1897 through cathode ray discharge tube experiments.
  • Cathode rays travel from the cathode to the anode and are deflected by both electric and magnetic fields towards the positive plate, confirming their negative charge.
  • The specific charge (e/m) of an electron is independent of the nature of the gas used and the material of the electrodes in the discharge tube.
  • The absolute charge of an electron was precisely measured by R.A. Millikan using the oil drop experiment as 1.602 × 10-19 C.
  • The rest mass of an electron is 9.109 × 10-31 kg, which is approximately 1/1836th of the mass of a hydrogen atom.
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन की खोज 1897 में जे.जे. थॉमसन (J.J. Thomson) द्वारा कैथोड किरण विसर्जन नलिका (cathode ray discharge tube) प्रयोगों के माध्यम से की गई थी।
  • कैथोड किरणें कैथोड से एनोड की ओर गमन करती हैं और विद्युत तथा चुंबकीय क्षेत्रों द्वारा धनात्मक प्लेट की ओर विक्षेपित (deflected) हो जाती हैं, जो उनके ऋणात्मक आवेश (negative charge) की पुष्टि करता है।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन का विशिष्ट आवेश (e/m) विसर्जन नलिका में प्रयुक्त गैस की प्रकृति और इलेक्ट्रोड की सामग्री से स्वतंत्र होता है।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन के निरपेक्ष आवेश को आर.ए. मिलिकन (R.A. Millikan) द्वारा तेल बूंद प्रयोग (oil drop experiment) का उपयोग करके 1.602 × 10-19 C के रूप में सटीक रूप से मापा गया था।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन का विराम द्रव्यमान (rest mass) 9.109 × 10-31 kg है, जो हाइड्रोजन परमाणु के द्रव्यमान का लगभग 1/1836 वां भाग है।

Wave-Particle Duality and Quantum Mechanics तरंग-कण द्वैतता और क्वांटम यांत्रिकी

  • Louis de Broglie proposed that electrons exhibit dual behavior, acting both as particles and as waves.
  • The Davisson and Germer experiment experimentally verified the wave nature of electrons through nickel crystal diffraction patterns.
  • Werner Heisenberg established that it is impossible to determine simultaneously both the exact position and exact momentum of an electron.
  • Erwin Schrödinger formulated the wave equation Hψ = Eψ, treating the electron as a three-dimensional standing wave in an electrostatic potential.
  • The square of the wave function, |ψ|2, represents the probability density of finding an electron at a given point in space.
  • लुई डी ब्रोग्ली (Louis de Broglie) ने प्रस्तावित किया कि इलेक्ट्रॉन द्वैत व्यवहार प्रदर्शित करते हैं, जो कण और तरंग दोनों के रूप में कार्य करते हैं।
  • डेविसन और जर्मर प्रयोग (Davisson and Germer experiment) ने निकल क्रिस्टल विवर्तन पैटर्न के माध्यम से इलेक्ट्रॉनों की तरंग प्रकृति को प्रयोगात्मक रूप से सत्यापित किया।
  • वर्नर हाइजेनबर्ग ने स्थापित किया कि किसी इलेक्ट्रॉन की सटीक स्थिति और सटीक संवेग (exact position and exact momentum) दोनों को एक साथ निर्धारित करना असंभव है।
  • इरविन श्रोडिंगर ने तरंग समीकरण Hψ = Eψ तैयार किया, जिसमें इलेक्ट्रॉन को स्थिरवैद्युत विभव (electrostatic potential) में एक त्रि-आयामी स्थिर तरंग के रूप में माना गया है।
  • तरंग फलन (wave function) का वर्ग, |ψ|2, अंतरिक्ष में किसी दिए गए बिंदु पर इलेक्ट्रॉन के पाए जाने की प्रायिकता घनत्व (probability density) को दर्शाता है।

Electronic Configurations and Spin इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास और चक्रण

  • Electrons fill atomic orbitals in order of increasing energy according to the Aufbau principle and the (n + l) rule.
  • The Pauli exclusion principle states that no two electrons in the same atom can have identical sets of all four quantum numbers.
  • Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity dictates that electron pairing in degenerate orbitals does not occur until each orbital is singly occupied with parallel spins.
  • The intrinsic angular momentum of an electron is characterized by the spin quantum number s = ±1/2.
  • Anomalous electronic configurations like those of Cr ([Ar] 3d5 4s1) and Cu ([Ar] 3d10 4s1) arise due to the extra stability of half-filled and fully-filled subshells.
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन आफबाऊ सिद्धांत (Aufbau principle) और (n + l) नियम के अनुसार बढ़ती ऊर्जा के क्रम में परमाणु कक्षकों (atomic orbitals) को भरते हैं।
  • पाउली का अपवर्जन सिद्धांत (Pauli exclusion principle) बताता है कि एक ही परमाणु में किन्हीं दो इलेक्ट्रॉनों के चारों क्वांटम संख्याओं के सेट समान नहीं हो सकते।
  • हुंड का अधिकतम बहुलता का नियम (Hund's rule of maximum multiplicity) यह निर्धारित करता है कि अपभ्रष्ट कक्षकों (degenerate orbitals) में इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्मन तब तक नहीं होता जब तक कि प्रत्येक कक्षक समानांतर चक्रण (parallel spins) के साथ एकल रूप से भर न जाए।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन का आंतरिक कोणीय संवेग (intrinsic angular momentum) चक्रण क्वांटम संख्या s = ±1/2 द्वारा अभिलक्षित होता है।
  • Cr ([Ar] 3d5 4s1) और Cu ([Ar] 3d10 4s1) जैसे असामान्य इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास अर्ध-पूरित और पूर्ण-पूरित उपकोशों की अतिरिक्त स्थिरता के कारण उत्पन्न होते हैं।
📐 Key Formulas & Equations
Planck-Einstein Relation प्लांक-आइंस्टीन संबंध (Planck-Einstein Relation)
E = hν = hc / λ
Conditions: Applies to photon emission or absorption involving electronic transitions.
Units: E in Joules, h in J s, ν in s-1, c in m s-1, λ in m
De Broglie Wavelength डी ब्रोग्ली तरंगदैर्ध्य (De Broglie Wavelength)
λ = h / p = h / (mv)
Conditions: Applies to wave-particle duality of electrons and other moving particles.
Units: λ in meters, h in J s, m in kg, v in m s-1
Bohr Angular Momentum Quantization बोर कोणीय संवेग क्वांटीकरण (Bohr Angular Momentum Quantization)
mvr = n(h / 2π)
Conditions: Stationary orbits where n = 1, 2, 3...
Units: m in kg, v in m s-1, r in m, h in J s
Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle हाइजेनबर्ग अनिश्चितता सिद्धांत (Heisenberg Uncertainty Principle)
Δx · Δpx ≥ h / (4π)
Conditions: Simultaneous measurement of position and momentum.
Units: Δx in meters, Δpx in kg m s-1, h in J s
Specific Charge (Charge to Mass Ratio) विशिष्ट आवेश (Specific Charge)
e / m = 1.7588 × 1011 C kg-1
Conditions: Determined via J.J. Thomson's cathode ray experiment using crossed electric and magnetic fields.
Units: C kg-1
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Aufbau Principle filling order (4s before 3d)आफबाऊ सिद्धांत भरने का क्रम (3d से पहले 4s)

Observation: Transition metals fill 3d before 4s upon ionization, and chromium/copper exhibit shifted configurations. — Electron-electron repulsion and exchange energy stabilize degenerate d-orbitals when symmetrically half-filled or fully-filled. अवलोकन: संक्रमण धातुएं आयनीकरण पर 4s से पहले 3d भरती हैं, और क्रोमियम/कॉपर स्थानांतरित विन्यास प्रदर्शित करते हैं। इलेक्ट्रॉन-इलेक्ट्रॉन प्रतिकर्षण और विनिमय ऊर्जा (exchange energy) सममित रूप से अर्ध-पूरित या पूर्ण-पूरित होने पर अपभ्रष्ट d-कक्षकों को स्थिर करती है।

Exception Pauli Exclusion Principle strictly limits orbital occupancy to two electronsपाउली अपवर्जन सिद्धांत कक्षक अधिभोग को दो इलेक्ट्रॉनों तक सीमित करता है

Observation: In plasma states or extreme gravitational/pressure regimes, degeneracy pressure alters standard fermionic behavior. — Extreme environmental conditions override normal atomic electrostatic and quantum constraints. अवलोकन: प्लाज्मा अवस्थाओं या अत्यधिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण/दबाव वाले क्षेत्रों में, अपभ्रष्टता दबाव (degeneracy pressure) मानक फर्मिऑनिक व्यवहार को बदल देता है। अत्यधिक पर्यावरणीय स्थितियां सामान्य परमाणु स्थिरवैद्युत और क्वांटम बाधाओं को निष्प्रभावी कर देती हैं।

🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Assuming cathode rays are electromagnetic radiation; they are actually streams of negatively charged material particles (electrons).
  • Confusing the mass of an electron in kg (9.1 × 10-31 kg) with that of a proton.
  • Forgetting that the (n + l) rule uses principal quantum number n to break ties when (n + l) values are identical.
  • Treating the orbit in Bohr's model as a flat classical circular path instead of a probabilistic wave function.

⚡ Quick Revision: Electron

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Corpuscular Nature of Electrons

Cathode rays consist of fundamental negative corpuscles named electrons, characterized by constant e/m ratio irrespective of origin.

Wave-Particle Duality

Moving electrons possess a wavelength inversely proportional to their momentum, governed by the de Broglie equation.

Quantum Mechanical Model

The spatial distribution of electrons is described by atomic orbitals derived from solutions to the Schrödinger wave equation.

Orbital Filling Rules

Electronic configurations follow the Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, and Hund's rule.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Cathode Rays (Stream of e-)
Streams of fast-moving electrons emitted from the cathode in a discharge tube.
Specific Charge (e / m)
Ratio of electric charge to mass of a particle, expressed in C kg-1.
De Broglie Wavelength (λ = h/mv)
The wavelength associated with a moving material particle.
Wave Function (ψ)
Mathematical function representing the quantum state of a system.
Node (Probability = 0)
Region in space where the probability density of finding an electron is zero.
Spin Quantum Number (s = ±1/2)
Quantum number describing the intrinsic angular momentum of an electron.

⚖️ Key Differences

Radial Node VS Angular Node

Radial nodes are spherical surfaces where probability is zero (given by n - l - 1), whereas angular nodes are planar/conical surfaces (given by l).

Question 1 of 19

Who measured the e/m ratio of an electron?

Explanation: J.J. Thomson measured the charge-to-mass ratio of cathode ray particles using a discharge tube with electric and magnetic fields.

Question 2 of 19

What is the rest mass of an electron in kg?

Explanation: The rest mass of an electron is precisely 9.109 × 10-31 kg.

Question 3 of 19

Which experiment determined the charge of an electron?

Explanation: R.A. Millikan performed the oil drop experiment to determine the absolute charge on an electron.

Question 4 of 19

The wave nature of the electron was experimentally established by:

Explanation: Davisson and Germer demonstrated electron diffraction using a nickel crystal, verifying de Broglie's wave hypothesis.

Question 5 of 19

According to Heisenberg's uncertainty principle, which pair of variables cannot be measured simultaneously with absolute precision?

Explanation: Position and momentum are conjugate variables bound by Δx · Δp ≥ h / (4π).

Question 6 of 19

What is the correct electronic configuration of Chromium (Cr, Z = 24)?

Explanation: Chromium adopts [Ar] 3d5 4s1 to achieve symmetrical half-filled d-subshell stability.

Question 7 of 19

The maximum number of electrons in a shell with principal quantum number n is:

Explanation: Each principal shell n contains n subshells and can hold a maximum of 2n2 electrons.

Question 8 of 19

Which quantum number specifies the orientation of an orbital in space?

Explanation: The magnetic quantum number ml determines the spatial orientation of an atomic orbital.

Question 9 of 19

The number of radial nodes for a 4s orbital is:

Explanation: Radial nodes = n - l - 1 = 4 - 0 - 1 = 3.

Question 10 of 19

Cathode rays consist of:

Explanation: Cathode rays are streams of high-speed electrons emitted from the cathode.

Question 11 of 19

De Broglie wavelength λ of a particle of mass m and velocity v is given by:

Explanation: De Broglie's equation relates wavelength to momentum: λ = h / p = h / mv.

Question 12 of 19

Which rule states that no two electrons in an atom can have the same set of four quantum numbers?

Explanation: Pauli exclusion principle restricts any two electrons from sharing identical quantum numbers.

Question 13 of 19

What is the value of charge to mass ratio (e/m) for an electron?

Explanation: J.J. Thomson determined e/m = 1.7588 × 1011 C kg-1.

Question 14 of 19

An orbital with l = 2 is designated as:

Explanation: Azimuthal quantum number l values 0, 1, 2, 3 correspond to s, p, d, f orbitals respectively.

Question 15 of 19

Total number of angular nodes for a d orbital (l = 2) is:

Explanation: Number of angular nodes equals the azimuthal quantum number l, which is 2 for a d orbital.

Question 16 of 19

The ground state electronic configuration of Copper (Cu, Z = 29) is:

Explanation: Copper adopts [Ar] 3d10 4s1 to attain a fully filled, highly stable d-subshell.

Question 17 of 19

The unit of Planck's constant (h) is:

Explanation: Planck's constant has dimensions of energy × time, with SI units Joule second (J s).

Question 18 of 19

According to Hund's rule, pairing of electrons in orbitals belonging to the same subshell does not occur until:

Explanation: Hund's rule requires single occupancy with parallel spins across all degenerate orbitals before pairing begins.

Question 19 of 19

Which subatomic particle was discovered last?

Explanation: The neutron was discovered by James Chadwick in 1932, long after electrons and protons.

Question 1 of 10
What is the de Broglie wavelength of an electron accelerated through a potential difference of 100 V?

Solution: Using λ = 12.27 / √V Å, for V = 100 V, λ = 12.27 / 10 = 1.227 Å.

Question 2 of 10
The total number of nodes (radial + angular) in a 4d orbital is:

Solution: Total nodes = n - 1 = 4 - 1 = 3 (2 radial and 1 angular).

Question 3 of 10
Which species is isoelectronic with Ne?

Solution: Ne (10 e-), Na+ (11 - 1 = 10), Mg2+ (12 - 2 = 10), and Al3+ (13 - 3 = 10) are all isoelectronic.

Question 4 of 10
The maximum number of electrons in an atom that can have n = 4 and ms = -1/2 is:

Solution: Total electrons in n = 4 is 2n2 = 32. Exactly half of these will have spin ms = -1/2, giving 16.

Question 5 of 10
Davisson and Germer experiment proved:

Solution: The diffraction pattern observed in the Davisson-Germer experiment confirmed the wave nature of electrons.

Question 6 of 10
Which of the following electronic configurations represents an excited state?

Solution: 1s2 2s2 2p5 3s1 represents an electron promoted from 2p to 3s, signifying an excited state.

Question 7 of 10
The orbital angular momentum of an electron in a p-orbital is:

Solution: Orbital angular momentum = √[l(l + 1)] (h / 2π). For p-orbital, l = 1, giving √(2) (h / 2π).

Question 8 of 10
The correct set of four quantum numbers for the outermost electron of Sodium (Na, Z = 11) is:

Solution: Outer configuration is 3s1: n = 3, l = 0 (s), ml = 0, s = +1/2.

Question 9 of 10
An electron has a velocity of 500 m s-1 accurate up to 0.004%. What is the uncertainty in its position?

Solution: Using Δx · m Δv ≥ h / (4π), with Δv = 500 × (0.0004) = 0.02 m s-1, calculation yields Δx ≈ 0.058 m.

Question 10 of 10
Which quantum number is not derived from the Schrödinger wave equation?

Solution: The spin quantum number was introduced empirically and via relativistic quantum mechanics (Dirac equation), not directly from the spatial Schrödinger wave equation.
