Discovery of Fundamental Subatomic Particles मूल उप-परमाण्विक कणों की खोज (Discovery of Fundamental Subatomic Particles)

Subatomic particles were identified through high-voltage electrical discharge experiments in gases at low pressure and radioactivity studies. These discoveries established the composite nature of atoms consisting of electrons, protons, and neutrons. उप-परमाण्विक कणों (subatomic particles) की पहचान कम दाब पर गैसों में उच्च-वोल्टेज विद्युत विसर्जन प्रयोगों और रेडियोधर्मिता अध्ययनों के माध्यम से की गई थी। इन खोजों ने परमाणुओं की संयुक्त प्रकृति को स्थापित किया, जो इलेक्ट्रॉन, प्रोटॉन और न्यूट्रॉन से मिलकर बने होते हैं।

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Cathode Rays and Discovery of Electron कैथोड किरणें और इलेक्ट्रॉन की खोज

  • Cathode rays are generated in a discharge tube at low pressure (≈0.01 mm Hg) and high voltage (≈10,000 V).
  • J.J. Thomson determined the charge-to-mass ratio (e/m) of electrons as 1.758820 × 1011 C kg-1.
  • Cathode rays cast shadows, possess kinetic energy, and are deflected towards the positive plate in an electric field confirming their negative charge.
  • The nature of cathode rays is independent of the nature of the gas and the cathode material used inside the discharge tube.
  • कैथोड किरणें कम दाब (≈0.01 mm Hg) और उच्च वोल्टेज (≈10,000 V) पर विसर्जन नली (discharge tube) में उत्पन्न होती हैं।
  • जे.जे. थॉमसन ने इलेक्ट्रॉनों के आवेश-द्रव्यमान अनुपात (charge-to-mass ratio (e/m)) को 1.758820 × 10^11 C kg^-1 निर्धारित किया।
  • कैथोड किरणें छाया बनाती हैं, इनमें गतिज ऊर्जा (kinetic energy) होती है, और विद्युत क्षेत्र में धनात्मक प्लेट की ओर विक्षेपित होती हैं, जो इनके ऋणात्मक आवेश (negative charge) की पुष्टि करता है।
  • कैथोड किरणों की प्रकृति विसर्जन नली में उपयोग की गई गैस और कैथोड सामग्री की प्रकृति से स्वतंत्र (independent) होती है।

Anode Rays and Discovery of Proton एनोड किरणें और प्रोटॉन की खोज

  • Anode rays (canal rays) were discovered by E. Goldstein using a perforated cathode in a discharge tube.
  • Unlike cathode rays, the e/m ratio of positive particles depends on the nature of the residual gas inside the tube.
  • The lightest positive ion is obtained from hydrogen gas and is termed the proton, possessing a unit positive charge.
  • Wien and Thomson further confirmed the particulate nature of positive ions through parabolic mass spectrometry.
  • एनोड किरणों (कैनाल किरणें) की खोज ई. गोल्डस्टीन ने विसर्जन नली में छिद्रित कैथोड का उपयोग करके की थी।
  • कैथोड किरणों के विपरीत, धनात्मक कणों का e/m अनुपात नली के अंदर मौजूद अवशिष्ट गैस की प्रकृति पर निर्भर करता है।
  • सबसे हल्का धनात्मक आयन हाइड्रोजन गैस से प्राप्त होता है और इसे प्रोटॉन कहा जाता है, जिसमें इकाई धनात्मक आवेश होता है।
  • वीन और थॉमसन ने पैराबोलिक मास स्पेक्ट्रोमेट्री के माध्यम से धनात्मक आयनों की कणिका प्रकृति (particulate nature) की पुष्टि की।

Discovery of Neutron and Radioactivity न्यूट्रॉन की खोज और रेडियोधर्मिता

  • Neutrons were discovered by James Chadwick in 1932 by bombarding beryllium-9 with alpha particles.
  • The reaction is represented as 4Be9 + 2He4 → 6C12 + 0n1.
  • Neutrons are electrically neutral subatomic particles with a mass slightly greater than that of a proton (1.6749 × 10-27 kg).
  • Radioactive decay studies by Rutherford and Soddy provided direct evidence for the transmutation of elements and complex nuclear architecture.
  • न्यूट्रॉन की खोज जेम्स चैडविक ने 1932 में बेरिलियम-9 पर अल्फा कणों की बमबारी करके की थी।
  • अभिक्रिया को 4Be9 + 2He4 -> 6C12 + 0n1 के रूप में दर्शाया गया है।
  • न्यूट्रॉन विद्युत रूप से उदासीन उप-परमाण्विक कण हैं जिनका द्रव्यमान प्रोटॉन के द्रव्यमान से थोड़ा अधिक (1.6749 × 10^-27 kg) होता है।
  • रदरफोर्ड और सोडी द्वारा रेडियोधर्मी क्षय (radioactive decay) अध्ययनों ने तत्वों के रूपांतरण और जटिल परमाणु वास्तुकला के लिए प्रत्यक्ष प्रमाण प्रदान किए।
📐 Key Formulas & Equations
Millikan's Oil Drop Charge Quantization मिलिकन का तेल बूंद आवेश क्वांटीकरण (Millikan's Oil Drop Charge Quantization)
q = n · e
Conditions: Equilibrium of gravitational, electric, and viscous drag forces on charged oil droplets
Units: q in Coulombs, n is an integer, e = 1.602 × 10-19 C
Rutherford Alpha Particle Scattering Distance of Closest Approach रदरफोर्ड अल्फा कण प्रकीर्णन निकटतम पहुँच की दूरी (Rutherford Alpha Particle Scattering Distance of Closest Approach)
r0 = (1 / 4πε0) · (2 Z e2 / ½ m v2)
Conditions: Kinetic energy of alpha particle fully converted to electrostatic potential energy at closest distance
Units: r0 in meters, Z is atomic number, v in m s-1
Planck-Einstein Relation for Discharged Radiations विकिरणों के लिए प्लांक-आइंस्टीन संबंध (Planck-Einstein Relation for Discharged Radiations)
E = h ν
Conditions: General quantum relation applied to excitation and ionization in discharge tubes
Units: E in Joules, h in J s, ν in s-1
Specific Charge of Electron (Thomson's Ratio) इलेक्ट्रॉन का विशिष्ट आवेश (थॉमसन का अनुपात) (Specific Charge of Electron)
e / m = V / (B2 · r2)
Conditions: Perpendicular electric and magnetic fields applied to cathode rays
Units: e/m in C kg-1, V in Volts, B in Tesla, r in meters
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Anode ray particles have constant e/m ratio regardless of gasएनोड किरण कणों का e/m अनुपात गैस से स्वतंत्र होता है

Observation: Anode rays e/m ratio varies with the gas used — Positive ions are formed by stripping electrons from neutral gas atoms; hence their mass depends directly on the molar mass of the gas. अवलोकन: एनोड किरणों का e/m अनुपात उपयोग की गई गैस के साथ बदलता है — धनात्मक आयन उदासीन गैस परमाणुओं से इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालकर बनते हैं; इसलिए उनका द्रव्यमान सीधे गैस के मोलर द्रव्यमान पर निर्भर करता है।

Exception All atomic nuclei contain neutronsसभी परमाणु नाभिक में न्यूट्रॉन होते हैं

Observation: The normal hydrogen isotope (protium, 1H1) contains no neutrons — Protium consists of a single proton and a single electron, making it the only stable nuclide without a neutron. अवलोकन: सामान्य हाइड्रोजन समस्थानिक (प्रोटियम, 1H1) में कोई न्यूट्रॉन नहीं होता है — प्रोटियम में केवल एक प्रोटॉन और एक इलेक्ट्रॉन होता है, जो इसे बिना न्यूट्रॉन वाला एकमात्र स्थिर न्यूक्लाइड बनाता है।

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Cathode rays originate from the cathode and travel towards the anode independently of gas composition.
  • The e/m value for an electron was measured by J.J. Thomson using crossed electric and magnetic fields.
  • Charge of an electron (-1.602 × 10-19 C) was precisely determined by R.A. Millikan via oil drop experiment.
  • Anode rays are formed by ionization of residual gas molecules by high-speed cathode rays.
  • Neutron is slightly heavier than a proton, with a rest mass of approximately 1.6749 × 10-27 kg.
  • Chadwick discovered the neutron by bombarding 4Be9 with α-particles, releasing penetrating neutral radiation.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Assuming anode rays are emitted directly from the anode; they are actually produced by gas ionization in the inter-electrode space.
  • Confusing the specific charge (e/m) constancy of cathode rays with the variable e/m of anode rays.
  • Forgetting that protium (hydrogen-1) lacks a neutron, leading to errors in atomic mass calculations.
  • Misinterpreting deflection directions in electric and magnetic fields for oppositely charged particles.

⚡ Quick Revision: Discovery of Fundamental Subatomic Particles

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Corpuscular Nature of Electricity

Cathode ray experiments established that electricity consists of discrete negative corpuscles later named electrons by Stoney and Thomson.

Positive Ionization Process

Canal rays confirmed the existence of positive subatomic units whose mass varies depending on the parent atom in the discharge tube.

Nuclear Neutral Component

The discovery of the neutron resolved the discrepancy between atomic number and atomic mass, explaining nuclear stability and isotopes.

Fundamental Constants

Precise measurements of charge and mass ratios established baseline physical constants essential for modern quantum mechanics.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Cathode Rays (Stream of electrons)
High-velocity electron beams emitted from cathode in low-pressure discharge tubes.
Anode Rays (Canal rays)
Positive ion streams moving towards perforated cathode in discharge tubes.
Specific Charge (e/m ratio)
Ratio of electric charge to mass of a subatomic particle, measured in C kg-1.
Neutron (0n1)
Neutral subatomic particle with mass 1.6749 × 10-27 kg.
Proton (1H1 ion)
Fundamental positively charged subatomic particle present in all atomic nuclei.
Radioactivity (Spontaneous disintegration)
Spontaneous emission of radiation from unstable atomic nuclei.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Electron mass: 9.109 × 10-31 kg
  • Proton mass: 1.6726 × 10-27 kg
  • Neutron mass: 1.6749 × 10-27 kg
  • Elementary charge (e): 1.602 × 10-19 C
  • Specific charge of electron: 1.7588 × 1011 C kg-1

📌 Quick Recall

  • Atoms are divisible and composed of electrons, protons, and neutrons.
  • Cathode rays are universal; anode rays depend on the gas identity.
  • Millikan's experiment proved charge quantization.
  • Chadwick completed the fundamental triad by discovering the neutron.

⚖️ Key Differences

Cathode Ray e/m ratio VS Anode Ray e/m ratio

Cathode ray e/m is constant; anode ray e/m changes with the nature of the gas.

Question 6 of 19

Which subatomic particle has zero electrical charge?

Cathode rays consist of:

Question 8 of 19

Which isotope of hydrogen contains no neutrons?

Question 10 of 19

The mass of a neutron is approximately equal to the mass of which other particle?

Explanation: Neutrons and protons have nearly equal masses, with the neutron being slightly heavier.

Question 11 of 19

In a discharge tube, pressure is kept at what approximate value to observe cathode rays?

Explanation: Extremely low pressure around 0.01 mm Hg is maintained to allow gas ionization and cathode ray formation.

Question 12 of 19

The specific charge of anode rays is maximum when the gas filled in the tube is:

Explanation: Since hydrogen has the lowest molar mass, the e/m ratio (specific charge) of its positive ion (proton) is the maximum.

Question 1 of 10
What is the charge-to-mass ratio unit for an electron in SI units?

Solution: Specific charge is charge divided by mass, expressed in Coulombs per kilogram (C kg-1).

Question 2 of 10
Which of the following statements about anode rays is incorrect?

Solution: Anode rays do not originate from the anode itself; they are formed by ionization of gas molecules between the electrodes.

Question 3 of 10
Calculate the specific charge of an alpha particle given its charge is +2e and mass is 4 amu.

Solution: Alpha specific charge = (2e) / (4m) = e / 2m, which is half of the proton's specific charge (e/m).

Question 4 of 10
The nuclear reaction 4Be9 + 2He4 → 6C12 + X demonstrates the discovery of:

Solution: Particle X is 0n1, representing the neutron.

Question 5 of 10
Millikan's oil drop experiment established that electric charge is:

Solution: Measured charges on oil droplets were integral multiples of a fundamental elementary charge, proving quantization.

Question 6 of 10
Which gas produces anode rays with the highest e/m ratio?

Solution: Hydrogen gives protons, which possess the smallest mass and hence the highest e/m ratio among positive ions.

Question 7 of 10
What is the rest mass of a neutron in kg?

Solution: The rest mass of a neutron is 1.6749 × 10-27 kg.

Question 8 of 10
Who performed the electrical discharge tube experiments that led to electron discovery?

Solution: J.J. Thomson is credited with the definitive discovery and measurement of electron properties using discharge tubes.

Question 9 of 10
Why are neutrons difficult to detect directly in electric and magnetic deflection experiments?

Solution: Neutrons lack electric charge, so Lorentz force equations yield zero deflection in electric and magnetic fields.

Question 10 of 10
The discovery of subatomic particles fundamentally proved that:

Solution: The identification of electrons, protons, and neutrons proved that atoms possess an intricate internal subatomic structure.
