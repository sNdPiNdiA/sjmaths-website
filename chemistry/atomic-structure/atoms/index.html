Atomic Structure परमाणु संरचना (Atomic Structure) परमाणु संरचना (Atomic Structure)

Atoms consist of a dense central nucleus composed of protons and neutrons, surrounded by electrons distributed in discrete quantum energy levels. The quantum mechanical model describes electron behavior using wave functions governed by the Schrödinger wave equation. परमाणु एक सघन केंद्रीय नाभिक से बने होते हैं जिसमें प्रोटॉन और न्यूट्रॉन होते हैं, जो अलग-अलग क्वांटम ऊर्जा स्तरों में वितरित इलेक्ट्रॉनों से घिरे होते हैं। क्वांटम यांत्रिक मॉडल श्रोडिंगर तरंग समीकरण (Schrödinger wave equation) द्वारा शासित तरंग फलनों (wave functions) का उपयोग करके इलेक्ट्रॉन के व्यवहार का वर्णन करता है। परमाणु एक सघन केंद्रीय नाभिक से बने होते हैं जिसमें प्रोटॉन और न्यूट्रॉन होते हैं, जो अलग-अलग क्वांटम ऊर्जा स्तरों में वितरित इलेक्ट्रॉनों से घिरे होते हैं। क्वांटम यांत्रिक मॉडल श्रोडिंगर तरंग समीकरण (Schrödinger wave equation) द्वारा शासित तरंग फलनों (wave functions) का उपयोग करके इलेक्ट्रॉन के व्यवहार का वर्णन करता है।

Quantum Numbers and Orbitals क्वांटम संख्याएँ और कक्षक (Quantum Numbers and Orbitals) क्वांटम संख्याएँ और कक्षक (Quantum Numbers and Orbitals)

  • The Principal Quantum Number (n) determines the main energy level, average distance of the electron from the nucleus, and primary size of the orbital.
  • The Azimuthal Quantum Number (l) defines the angular momentum and the 3D shape of the orbital, taking integer values from 0 to (n-1).
  • The Magnetic Quantum Number (ml) specifies the spatial orientation of an orbital in an external magnetic field, ranging from -l to +l.
  • The Spin Quantum Number (ms) describes the intrinsic angular momentum of the electron, having values of +1/2 or -1/2.
  • मुख्य क्वांटम संख्या (Principal Quantum Number, n) मुख्य ऊर्जा स्तर, नाभिक से इलेक्ट्रॉन की औसत दूरी और कक्षक के प्राथमिक आकार को निर्धारित करती है।
  • दिगंशीय क्वांटम संख्या (Azimuthal Quantum Number, l) कोणीय संवेग और कक्षक की त्रिविमीय (3D) आकृति को परिभाषित करती है, जिसका मान 0 से (n-1) तक पूर्णांक होता है।
  • चुंबकीय क्वांटम संख्या (Magnetic Quantum Number, ml) बाह्य चुंबकीय क्षेत्र में कक्षक के त्रिविमीय अभिविन्यास को निर्दिष्ट करती है, जिसका मान -l से +l तक होता है।
  • चक्रण क्वांटम संख्या (Spin Quantum Number, ms) इलेक्ट्रॉन के आंतरिक कोणीय संवेग का वर्णन करती है, जिसका मान +1/2 या -1/2 होता है।

Wave-Particle Duality and Mechanics तरंग-कण द्वैत और यांत्रिकी (Wave-Particle Duality and Mechanics) तरंग-कण द्वैत और यांत्रिकी (Wave-Particle Duality and Mechanics)

  • De Broglie hypothesis establishes that moving matter exhibits dual wave-particle characteristics, with wavelength inversely proportional to momentum.
  • Schrödinger wave equation Hψ = Eψ uses the Hamiltonian operator H to describe allowed energy states and wave functions ψ for electrons.
  • The square of the wave function (ψ2) represents the probability density of finding an electron at a specific spatial coordinate.
  • A radial node represents a spherical region where electron probability density drops to zero, calculated as (n - l - 1).
  • डी ब्रोग्ली परिकल्पना (De Broglie hypothesis) यह स्थापित करती है कि गतिशील द्रव्य तरंग-कण द्वैत प्रकृति प्रदर्शित करता है, जिसमें तरंगदैर्घ्य संवेग के व्युत्क्रमानुपाती होता है।
  • श्रोडिंगर तरंग समीकरण Hψ = Eψ, हैमिल्टनियन ऑपरेटर H का उपयोग करके इलेक्ट्रॉनों के लिए अनुमत ऊर्जा अवस्थाओं और तरंग फलनों (ψ) का वर्णन करता है।
  • तरंग फलन का वर्ग (ψ²) किसी विशिष्ट स्थानिक निर्देशांक पर इलेक्ट्रॉन के पाए जाने की प्रायिकता घनत्व (probability density) को दर्शाता है।
  • एक त्रिज्यीय नोड (radial node) उस गोलाकार क्षेत्र को दर्शाता है जहाँ इलेक्ट्रॉन प्रायिकता घनत्व शून्य हो जाता है, जिसकी गणना (n - l - 1) के रूप में की जाती है।

Filling Rules and Electronic Configuration इलेक्ट्रॉन भरने के नियम और इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास इलेक्ट्रॉन भरने के नियम और इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास

  • The Aufbau Principle requires electrons to fill orbitals in order of increasing energy, governed by the (n + l) rule.
  • Pauli Exclusion Principle states that no two electrons in the same atom can possess identical sets of all four quantum numbers.
  • Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity mandates that electron pairing in degenerate orbitals does not occur until each orbital is singly occupied with parallel spin.
  • Half-filled and completely filled subshells exhibit extra exchange energy and symmetrical charge distribution, imparting high stability.
  • आफबाऊ सिद्धांत (Aufbau Principle) के अनुसार इलेक्ट्रॉनों को ऊर्जा के बढ़ते क्रम में कक्षकों में भरा जाना चाहिए, जो (n + l) नियम द्वारा शासित होता है।
  • पाउली का अपवर्जन सिद्धांत (Pauli Exclusion Principle) यह बताता है कि एक ही परमाणु में किन्हीं दो इलेक्ट्रॉनों के लिए चारों क्वांटम संख्याओं का सेट समान नहीं हो सकता।
  • हुंड का अधिकतम बहुलता का नियम (Hund's Rule of Maximum Multiplicity) यह अनिवार्य करता है कि समभ्रंश कक्षकों (degenerate orbitals) में इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्मन तब तक नहीं होता जब तक कि प्रत्येक कक्षक में एक-एक इलेक्ट्रॉन समान चक्रण के साथ न भर जाए।
  • अर्ध-पूरित और पूर्ण-पूरित उपकोश अतिरिक्त विनिमय ऊर्जा (extra exchange energy) और सममित आवेश वितरण प्रदर्शित करते हैं, जो उन्हें उच्च स्थिरता प्रदान करते हैं।

Exception Radial nodes equal (n - l - 1) for all hydrogenic orbitals.सभी हाइड्रोजनिक कक्षकों के लिए त्रिज्यीय नोड्स (n - l - 1) के बराबर होते हैं।सभी हाइड्रोजनिक कक्षकों के लिए त्रिज्यीय नोड्स (n - l - 1) के बराबर होते हैं।

Observation: 1s orbital has zero radial nodes (1 - 0 - 1 = 0) — The fundamental boundary condition sets the probability maximum at the nucleus for the 1s state without intersecting zero within r > 0. अवलोकन: 1s कक्षक में शून्य त्रिज्यीय नोड होते हैं (1 - 0 - 1 = 0) — मौलिक सीमा स्थिति यह निर्धारित करती है कि 1s अवस्था के लिए नाभिक पर प्रायिकता अधिकतम होती है और r > 0 के भीतर यह शून्य को नहीं काटती है।

Exception Bohr model is universally applicable to multi-electron atomic systems.बोहर मॉडल बहु-इलेक्ट्रॉन परमाणु प्रणालियों पर सार्वभौमिक रूप से लागू होता है।बोहर मॉडल बहु-इलेक्ट्रॉन परमाणु प्रणालियों पर सार्वभौमिक रूप से लागू होता है।

Observation: Fails for multi-electron atoms (He, Li, etc.) — Bohr's derivation ignores inter-electronic repulsion and simultaneous relativistic/quantum wave interference effects present in systems with two or more electrons. अवलोकन: बहु-इलेक्ट्रॉन परमाणुओं (He, Li, आदि) के लिए विफल रहता है — बोहर का व्युत्पन्न अंतर-इलेक्ट्रॉनिक प्रतिकर्षण और दो या अधिक इलेक्ट्रॉनों वाली प्रणालियों में मौजूद सापेक्षिक/क्वांटम तरंग हस्तक्षेप प्रभावों की उपेक्षा करता है।

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • The energy of an electron in a hydrogen atom depends solely on the principal quantum number (n).
  • For multi-electron atoms, orbital energy depends on both (n + l) values; lower value fills first.
  • The magnetic quantum number (ml) designates the orientation of an orbital and has 2l + 1 values for a given l.
  • Energy of a photon emitted is given by ΔE = E2 - E1 = hν.
  • Radial distribution functions give the probability of finding an electron within a spherical shell of thickness dr at radius r.
  • The Heisenberg uncertainty principle highlights the limitation of simultaneous deterministic trajectory tracking for subatomic particles.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing angular nodes (which equal l) with radial nodes (which equal n - l - 1).
  • Assuming multi-electron energy levels depend only on n, forgetting that 4s fills before 3d due to (n + l) comparison.
  • Omitting the negative sign in electron energy equations (En = -13.6 Z2/n2 eV).
  • Treating ms as having more than two states (+1/2 and -1/2).

⚡ Quick Revision: Atomic Structure

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Bohr Model Foundations

The Bohr model introduced quantized angular momentum (mvr = nh/2π) for circular electron orbits, successfully explaining the atomic spectrum of hydrogen.

Quantum Mechanical Model

Replaces fixed circular paths with probabilistic wave functions (ψ), treating electrons as three-dimensional standing waves governed by the Schrödinger equation.

Quantum Numbers Framework

A set of four numbers (n, l, ml, ms) completely specifies the energy, shape, orientation, and spin orientation of any electron in an atom.

Electronic Configuration Laws

Atoms populate atomic orbitals following the Aufbau principle, Pauli exclusion principle, and Hund's rule, minimizing total system energy.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Defines the main shell and average size of the orbital.

Defines the subshell and angular shape of the orbital.
Magnetic Quantum Number (ml)
Defines the spatial orientation of an orbital.
Spin Quantum Number (ms)
Defines the spin angular momentum of the electron.
Node (ψ2 = 0)
Region in space where electron probability density is zero.
Photoelectric Work Function (W0 or hν0)
Minimum energy required to eject an electron from a metal surface.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Total number of orbitals in shell n = n2
  • Total number of electrons in shell n = 2n2
  • Angular nodes = l
  • Radial nodes = n - l - 1
  • Ground state energy of hydrogen atom = -13.6 eV

📌 Quick Recall

  • Atomic structure transitions from classical Bohr orbits to probabilistic quantum wave mechanics.
  • Four quantum numbers categorize every electron in an atom uniquely.
  • Aufbau, Pauli, and Hund principles dictate ground state electron configurations.
  • Exceptions like Cr and Cu stem from exchange energy stabilization of half/full d-subshells.

⚖️ Key Differences

Orbits VS Orbitals

Orbits are circular planar paths (Bohr model); orbitals are 3D regions of space with maximum electron probability (Quantum model).

Question 1 of 18

What is the maximum number of electrons that can be accommodated in a subshell with l = 3?

Explanation: For l = 3 (f-subshell), the number of orbitals is 2l + 1 = 7. Each orbital holds 2 electrons, giving 7 × 2 = 14 electrons.

Question 2 of 18

The number of radial nodes in a 4s orbital is:

Explanation: Radial nodes = n - l - 1. For 4s, n = 4, l = 0. Therefore, radial nodes = 4 - 0 - 1 = 3.

Question 3 of 18

Which of the following sets of quantum numbers is not permissible?

Explanation: For n = 1, the maximum value of l is n - 1 = 0. Thus, l cannot equal 1 when n = 1.

Question 4 of 18

De Broglie wavelength of a particle is:

Explanation: According to de Broglie relation, λ = h/p = h/(mv), showing inverse proportionality with momentum p.

Question 5 of 18

The energy of the second Bohr orbit of the hydrogen atom is:

Explanation: En = -13.6 × (Z2/n2). For H (Z=1) and n=2, E2 = -13.6 / 4 = -3.4 eV.

Question 6 of 18

Hund's rule deals with the distribution of electrons in:

Explanation: Hund's rule states that electron pairing in degenerate (equal energy) orbitals does not take place until each is singly occupied.

Question 7 of 18

Which transition in the hydrogen spectrum would give the same wavelength as Balmer line n=4 to n=2?

Explanation: Wavelength scales with Z2; scaling transitions across hydrogen-like ions permits matching specific lines.

Question 8 of 18

What is the total number of angular nodes for a 5d orbital?

Explanation: Angular nodes equal the azimuthal quantum number l. For d orbitals, l = 2.

Question 9 of 18

Heisenberg's uncertainty principle is significant for:

Explanation: Planck's constant h is extremely small, making the uncertainty noticeable only for subatomic, microscopic particles.

Question 10 of 18

The correct ground-state electronic configuration of Chromium (Z=24) is:

Explanation: Chromium adopts [Ar] 3d5 4s1 to achieve extra stability from a half-filled 3d subshell.

Question 11 of 18

The orbital with spherical symmetry is:

Explanation: s-orbitals have l = 0 and are spherically symmetrical around the nucleus.

Question 12 of 18

An electron has a velocity of 500 m/s accurate up to 0.02%. What is the uncertainty in its position?

Explanation: Applying Δx ≥ h / (4π m Δv) with mass of electron m = 9.1 × 10-31 kg yields the uncertainty value.

Question 13 of 18

Which of the following sets of quantum numbers describes an electron in a 4f orbital?

Explanation: For 4f, n = 4, l = 3 (since f corresponds to l = 3), and ml ranges from -3 to +3.

Question 14 of 18

The threshold frequency for a metal is 5 × 1014 s-1. If light of frequency 8 × 1014 s-1 strikes the metal, what is the kinetic energy of the ejected electron?

Explanation: KE = h(ν - ν0) = 6.626 × 10-34 × (8 - 5) × 1014 = 1.988 × 10-19 J.

Question 15 of 18

Which quantum number does not follow from the solution of the Schrödinger wave equation?

Explanation: The spin quantum number arises from relativistic quantum mechanics and Dirac's equation, not from the spatial Schrödinger wave equation.

Question 16 of 18

The ratio of radii of the first three Bohr orbits of hydrogen atom is:

Explanation: Radius rn ∝ n2. Thus, r1 : r2 : r3 = 12 : 22 : 32 = 1 : 4 : 9.

Question 17 of 18

What is the total number of nodal planes in a dxy orbital?

Explanation: The dxy orbital lies between the x and y axes, having nodal planes along the xz and yz planes (total of 2 nodal planes).

Question 18 of 18

The wave number of the spectral line in the Paschen series when n2 = 4 is (RH = 109677 cm-1):

Explanation: Paschen series has n1 = 3. For n2 = 4, ν̂ = RH (1/32 - 1/42) = 109677 × (1/9 - 1/16) ≈ 5332 cm-1.

Question 1 of 10
Calculate the energy required to excite an electron in hydrogen atom from n=1 to n=2.

Solution: ΔE = E2 - E1 = -3.4 eV - (-13.6 eV) = 10.2 eV.

Question 2 of 10
The orbital angular momentum of an electron in a 2p orbital is:

Solution: Orbital angular momentum = √[l(l+1)] (h / 2π). For p orbital, l = 1, so √[1(2)] (h / 2π) = √2 (h / 2π).

Question 3 of 10
Which ion has the highest magnetic moment (spin-only)?

Solution: Mn2+ has [Ar] 3d5 configuration with 5 unpaired electrons, giving the highest unpaired count and highest magnetic moment.

Question 4 of 10
The ratio of frequencies of the series limit of Lyman and Balmer series in hydrogen spectrum is:

Solution: Series limit frequency corresponds to n2 → ∞. Lyman limit n1=1 (νL = RHc), Balmer limit n1=2 (νB = RHc/4). Ratio is 4 : 1.

Question 5 of 10
If uncertainty in position and momentum are equal, the uncertainty in velocity is:

Solution: Set Δx = Δp / m = mΔv / m. Since Δx · mΔv ≥ h/4π, substituting gives (Δv)2 = h / (4πm2), solving yields Δv = (1/2m)√(h/π).

Question 6 of 10
The total number of electrons with ms = -1/2 in an atom with atomic number Z = 12 is:

Solution: Mg (Z=12) has 12 electrons total. Exactly half of them ($12 / 2 = 6$) will possess spin ms = -1/2.

Question 7 of 10
Which transition emits a photon of lowest frequency in the hydrogen atomic spectrum?

Solution: Lowest energy difference corresponds to the smallest gap between adjacent high principal quantum numbers, i.e., n = 6 to n = 5.

Question 8 of 10
The quantum number that cannot be determined from the Bohr model of hydrogen atom is:

Solution: The Bohr model only utilized principal quantization, whereas azimuthal, magnetic, and spin quantum numbers arose from wave mechanics.

Question 9 of 10
The de Broglie wavelength of a bullet of mass 10 g traveling at 300 m/s is:

Solution: λ = h / (mv) = 6.626 × 10-34 / (0.01 kg × 300 m/s) = 2.21 × 10-34 / 1 = 2.21 × 10-34 m.

Question 10 of 10
The wave function ψ for an s orbital has spherical symmetry because:

Solution: s-orbitals lack angular dependence (θ and φ terms are absent), and l = 0 means zero angular nodes, rendering it completely spherically symmetric.
