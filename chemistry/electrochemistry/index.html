Electrochemistry and Redox Processes विद्युत रसायन और रेडॉक्स प्रक्रम (Electrochemistry and Redox Processes)

Electrochemistry investigates the interconversion of chemical and electrical energy through oxidation-reduction reactions occurring in galvanic and electrolytic cells. Quantitative relationships are governed by the Nernst equation, Faraday's laws of electrolysis, and Kohlrausch's law of independent migration of ions. विद्युत रसायन (Electrochemistry) गैल्वेनिक और विद्युत-अपघटनी सेलों में होने वाली ऑक्सीकरण-अपचयन (oxidation-reduction) अभिक्रियाओं के माध्यम से रासायनिक और विद्युत ऊर्जा के अंतःपरिवर्तन का अध्ययन करता है। मात्रात्मक संबंध नर्नस्ट समीकरण (Nernst equation), फैराडे के विद्युत-अपघटन के नियम (Faraday's laws of electrolysis) और आयनों के स्वतंत्र अभिगमन के कोलराउस नियम (Kohlrausch's law) द्वारा नियंत्रित होते हैं।

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Electrochemical Cells and Standard Electrode Potentials विद्युत-रासायनिक सेल और मानक इलेक्ट्रोड विभव

  • A galvanic cell spontaneously converts chemical energy into electrical energy, featuring oxidation at the anode (negative polarity) and reduction at the cathode (positive polarity).
  • Standard reduction potential (E°) values measured against the Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE) determine the oxidizing and reducing strengths of species.
  • The cell potential is calculated as E°cell = E°cathode - E°anode using IUPAC standard reduction potentials.
  • A positive value of E°cell corresponds to a spontaneous reaction with a negative Gibbs free energy change (ΔG < 0).
  • एक गैल्वेनिक सेल (galvanic cell) स्वतः ही रासायनिक ऊर्जा को विद्युत ऊर्जा में परिवर्तित करता है, जिसमें एनोड पर ऑक्सीकरण (ऋणात्मक ध्रुवता) और कैथोड पर अपचयन (धनात्मक ध्रुवता) होता है।
  • मानक हाइड्रोजन इलेक्ट्रोड (SHE) के सापेक्ष मापे गए मानक अपचयन विभव (E°) मान प्रजातियों की ऑक्सीकरण और अपचयन क्षमता निर्धारित करते हैं।
  • सेल विभव (cell potential) की गणना IUPAC मानक अपचयन विभव का उपयोग करके E°_cell = E°_cathode - E°_anode के रूप में की जाती है।
  • E°_cell का धनात्मक मान ऋणात्मक गिब्स मुक्त ऊर्जा परिवर्तन (ΔG < 0) के साथ एक स्वतः प्रवर्तित अभिक्रिया (spontaneous reaction) को दर्शाता है।

Electrolytic Conductance and Kohlrausch's Law विद्युत-अपघटनी चालकत्व और कोलराउस का नियम

  • Molar conductivity (Λm) increases with dilution for weak electrolytes due to an increase in the degree of dissociation (α).
  • For strong electrolytes, molar conductivity increases slightly with dilution following the Debye-Hückel-Onsager equation: Λm = Λ∞m - A√c.
  • Kohlrausch's law enables the calculation of limiting molar conductivity for weak electrolytes by algebraic combination of strong electrolyte values.
  • Transference number (transport number) represents the fraction of total current carried by a specific cation or anion in solution.
  • दुर्बल विद्युत-अपघट्यों (weak electrolytes) के लिए तनुता (dilution) के साथ मोलर चालकता (Λm) बढ़ती है, जिसका कारण वियोजन की मात्रा (degree of dissociation, α) में वृद्धि है।
  • प्रबल विद्युत-अपघट्यों के लिए, मोलर चालकता डिबाई-ह्यूकेल-ओनसेगर समीकरण (Debye-Hückel-Onsager equation): Λm = Λ°m - A√c के अनुसार तनुता के साथ थोड़ी बढ़ती है।
  • कोलराउस का नियम (Kohlrausch's law) प्रबल विद्युत-अपघट्यों के मानों के बीजगणितीय संयोजन द्वारा दुर्बल विद्युत-अपघट्यों के लिए सीमांत मोलर चालकता की गणना को सक्षम बनाता है।
  • अभिगमन संख्या (transference number) विलयन में किसी विशिष्ट धनायन या ऋणायन द्वारा ले जाए गए कुल विद्युत धारा के अंश को दर्शाती है।

Electrolysis and Commercial Batteries विद्युत-अपघटन और व्यावसायिक बैटरियाँ

  • During electrolysis, preferential discharge of ions at electrodes depends on their reduction potentials, overpotential, and the nature of electrodes.
  • Faraday's laws of electrolysis state that the amount of chemical change during electrolysis is directly proportional to the total charge passed.
  • The Leclanché dry cell uses a zinc container as anode and a carbon rod surrounded by MnO2 and NH4Cl paste as cathode.
  • Secondary cells such as the lead-acid storage battery can be recharged by applying an external potential in the reverse direction.
  • विद्युत-अपघटन के दौरान, इलेक्ट्रोड पर आयनों का अधिमान्य विसर्जन (preferential discharge) उनके अपचयन विभव, अतिविभव (overpotential) और इलेक्ट्रोड की प्रकृति पर निर्भर करता है।
  • फैराडे के विद्युत-अपघटन के नियम बताते हैं कि विद्युत-अपघटन के दौरान होने वाला रासायनिक परिवर्तन प्रवाहित कुल आवेश के सीधे समानुपाती होता है।
  • लेक्लांशे शुष्क सेल (Leclanché dry cell) में जिंक पात्र का उपयोग एनोड के रूप में और MnO2 तथा NH4Cl के पेस्ट से घिरी कार्बन छड़ का उपयोग कैथोड के रूप में किया जाता है।
  • द्वितीयक सेल जैसे लेड-एसिड स्टोरेज बैटरी (lead-acid storage battery) को विपरीत दिशा में बाह्य विभव लगाकर पुनः आवेशित किया जा सकता है।
📐 Key Formulas & Equations
Gibbs Free Energy and Cell Potential गिब्स मुक्त ऊर्जा और सेल विभव (Gibbs Free Energy and Cell Potential)
ΔG = -nFE°cell
Conditions: Standard state conditions (1 bar, 298 K, 1 M activity)
Units: ΔG in Joules (J), n is moles of electrons, F ≈ 96485 C mol-1
Kohlrausch's Law of Independent Migration स्वतंत्र अभिगमन का कोलराउस नियम (Kohlrausch's Law of Independent Migration)
Λ∞m = ν+λ°+ + ν-λ°-
Conditions: Infinite dilution where interionic interactions are negligible
Units: Λm in S cm2 mol-1
Faraday's First Law of Electrolysis फैराडे का विद्युत-अपघटन का प्रथम नियम (Faraday's First Law of Electrolysis)
w = Z × I × t = (M / nF) × I × t
Conditions: Constant current passing through an electrolyte solution or melt
Units: w in grams, I in amperes (A), t in seconds (s)
Degree of Dissociation for Weak Electrolytes दुर्बल विद्युत-अपघट्यों के लिए वियोजन की मात्रा (Degree of Dissociation for Weak Electrolytes)
α = Λm / Λ∞m
Conditions: Weak electrolytes at moderate to low concentrations
Units: α is dimensionless
Nernst Equation नर्नस्ट समीकरण (Nernst Equation)
E = E° - (RT / nF) ln Q
Conditions: Applicable for any temperature at non-standard concentrations
Units: E in volts (V), R in J K-1 mol-1, T in Kelvin (K), F in C mol-1
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Fluorine is expected to be a weaker oxidizing agent than heavier halogens based on standard size trends.मानक आकार प्रवृत्तियों के आधार पर फ्लोरीन को भारी हैलोजन की तुलना में दुर्बल ऑक्सीकारक होना चाहिए।

Observation: Fluorine is the strongest known oxidizing agent (E° = +2.87 V). — Extremely low enthalpy of dissociation of F2 and high hydration enthalpy of F- ions compensate for its bond energy. अवलोकन: फ्लोरीन सबसे शक्तिशाली ज्ञात ऑक्सीकारक है (E° = +2.87 V)। F2 की अत्यंत कम वियोजन एन्थैल्पी और F- आयनों की उच्च जलयोजन एन्थैल्पी इसकी बंध ऊर्जा की भरपाई कर देती है।

Exception Electrolytic conductance of all substances increases continuously upon increasing temperature.तापमान बढ़ाने पर सभी पदार्थों का विद्युत-अपघटनी चालकत्व निरंतर बढ़ता है।

Observation: Conductivity of metallic conductors decreases with increasing temperature. — Thermal agitation of metal lattice cores increases resistance, whereas increased ionic mobility lowers solution resistance. अवलोकन: धात्विक चालकों की चालकता तापमान बढ़ने के साथ घटती है। धातु जालक क्रोडों में तापीय विक्षोभ प्रतिरोध बढ़ाता है, जबकि आयनिक गतिशीलता में वृद्धि विलयन के प्रतिरोध को कम करती है।

Exception Concentration cells always have a standard cell potential (E°cell) different from zero.सांद्रता सेलों (concentration cells) का मानक सेल विभव (E°_cell) हमेशा शून्य से भिन्न होता है।

Observation: Standard cell potential of a concentration cell is always zero (E°cell = 0). — Both electrodes are composed of the same half-cell material, making standard reduction potentials identical. अवलोकन: सांद्रता सेल का मानक सेल विभव हमेशा शून्य होता है (E°_cell = 0)। दोनों इलेक्ट्रोड एक ही अर्ध-सेल पदार्थ से बने होते हैं, जिससे मानक अपचयन विभव समान हो जाते हैं।

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE) has an assigned potential of exactly 0.00 V at all temperatures.
  • The unit of electrical resistance is the ohm (Ω) and electrical conductance is measured in Siemens (S).
  • Cell constant (G*) is defined as the ratio of distance between electrodes (l) to the cross-sectional area (A), having units cm-1.
  • Molar conductivity unit is S cm2 mol-1, which equals 10-4 S m2 mol-1.
  • During the discharge of a lead-acid battery, sulfuric acid is consumed and the density of the electrolyte decreases.
  • Rusting of iron is an electrochemical process where oxygen reduction occurs at cathodic regions and iron oxidation occurs at anodic regions.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing anode and cathode polarities between galvanic and electrolytic cells; remember anode is always the site of oxidation.
  • Omitting temperature conversion to Kelvin when applying the Nernst equation or thermodynamic relations.
  • Miscalculating the value of 'n' (moles of electrons transferred) in complex balanced redox reactions.
  • Forgetting that specific conductivity (k) decreases with dilution while molar conductivity (Λm) increases.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Cell Constant (G* = l / A)
Ratio of distance between electrodes to their active surface area. Unit: cm-1.
Specific Conductance (k (kappa))
Conductance of a 1 cm cube of electrolyte solution. Unit: S cm-1.
Molar Conductivity (Λm = k / c)
Conducting power of all ions produced by one mole of electrolyte in solution.
Electrochemical Equivalent (Z)
Mass of a substance deposited by passing 1 coulomb of electricity. Unit: g C-1.
Overpotential (η)
Potential difference between thermodynamically determined reduction potential and experimental potential.
Transport Number (t+ / t-)
Fraction of total electric current carried by an individual ionic species.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Faraday Constant F ≈ 96485 C mol-1
  • Standard temperature for electrochemical standard states is 298.15 K
  • Standard pressure for gases in electrochemical cells is 1 bar (100 kPa)
  • Standard concentration for aqueous ionic species is 1 mol L-1
  • Gibbs energy change and cell potential relationship: ΔG° = -nFE°cell

📌 Quick Recall

  • Galvanic cells generate electrical work from spontaneous chemical reactions.
  • Electrolytic cells drive non-spontaneous reactions using external electrical sources.
  • Nernst equation connects cell potential to reactant and product concentrations.
  • Kohlrausch law allows determination of limiting molar conductivity for weak electrolytes.

⚖️ Key Differences

Specific Conductance (k) VS Molar Conductivity (Λm)

Specific conductance decreases on dilution due to fewer ions per unit volume, whereas molar conductivity increases because volume containing 1 mole increases.

Question 1 of 18

What is the standard reduction potential of the Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE)?

Explanation: By international convention, the standard reduction potential of the SHE is arbitrarily assigned a value of exactly 0.00 V at all temperatures.

Question 2 of 18

Which of the following expressions represents the Nernst equation for a general redox reaction at 298 K?

Explanation: The Nernst equation at 298 K is expressed as E = E° - (0.0591 / n) log Q, where Q is the reaction quotient.

Question 3 of 18

What happens to the molar conductivity of a weak electrolyte on infinite dilution?

Explanation: At infinite dilution, weak electrolytes undergo complete dissociation (degree of dissociation approaches 1), and molar conductivity reaches a maximum limiting value (Λ∞m).

Question 4 of 18

In a galvanic cell, the anode is:

Explanation: In galvanic cells, the anode is the negative electrode where oxidation takes place (loss of electrons).

Question 5 of 18

The unit of specific conductance (kappa) is:

Explanation: Specific conductance is the reciprocal of specific resistivity (ρ), having units of ohm-1 cm-1 or S cm-1.

Question 6 of 18

How many coulombs of charge are required to deposit 1 mole of aluminium from an Al3+ solution?

Explanation: Deposition of 1 mole of Al3+ requires 3 moles of electrons: 3 × 96500 C = 289500 C.

Question 7 of 18

Which relation correctly links standard Gibbs free energy change (ΔG°) and standard cell potential (E°cell)?

Explanation: The standard Gibbs free energy change is related to standard cell potential by the equation ΔG° = -nFE°cell.

Question 8 of 18

What is the primary function of a salt bridge in a galvanic cell?

Explanation: A salt bridge contains an inert electrolyte (like KCl or NH4NO3) whose ions migrate to half-cells to prevent accumulation of charge, maintaining electrical neutrality.

Question 9 of 18

Kohlrausch's law is used to calculate:

Explanation: Kohlrausch's law of independent migration of ions allows the determination of Λ∞m for weak electrolytes by summing limiting ionic conductivities.

Question 10 of 18

In the electrolysis of aqueous NaCl using inert electrodes, the product formed at the cathode is:

Explanation: Due to lower reduction potential of water / H+ compared to Na+, hydrogen gas is liberated at the cathode.

Question 11 of 18

Which of the following is a secondary electrochemical cell?

Explanation: Lead-acid storage batteries are rechargeable secondary cells, whereas dry cells and mercury cells are primary cells.

Question 12 of 18

The cell constant G* is expressed in terms of electrode separation (l) and area (A) as:

Explanation: Cell constant G* = l / A, where l is distance between electrodes and A is effective cross-sectional area.

Question 13 of 18

If the standard cell potential (E°cell) is positive, the equilibrium constant (K) for the cell reaction is:

Explanation: Since E°cell = (RT / nF) ln K, a positive E°cell results in ln K > 0, meaning K > 1.

Question 14 of 18

During the charging of a lead-acid battery, which reaction occurs at the anode?

Explanation: During charging, the process is reversed; PbSO4 at the positive electrode is oxidized to PbO2.

Question 15 of 18

What is the unit of molar conductivity (Λm)?

Explanation: Molar conductivity is defined as k / c, yielding units of S cm2 mol-1.

Question 16 of 18

Which substance is used as the electrolyte in a Leclanché dry cell?

Explanation: A paste of ammonium chloride (NH4Cl) and zinc chloride (ZnCl2) serves as the acidic electrolyte medium in a dry cell.

Question 17 of 18

Which metal can displace copper from a CuSO4 solution?

Explanation: Zinc has a lower (more negative) reduction potential than copper (-0.76 V vs +0.34 V), allowing zinc to spontaneously displace copper.

Question 18 of 18

What is the value of Faraday constant (F) approximately equal to?

Explanation: The Faraday constant F is approximately 96,485 C mol-1, commonly rounded to 96,500 C mol-1.

The electrical resistance of a 0.05 mol L-1 NaOH solution is 50 Ω. If its cell constant is 0.5 cm-1, what is its molar conductivity?

Question 1 of 10
What is the cell reaction for a standard hydrogen electrode acting as a cathode?

Solution: At the cathode, reduction occurs: 2H+(aq) + 2e- → H2(g).

Question 2 of 10
Which of the following aqueous solutions exhibits the lowest electrical conductivity?

Solution: Acetic acid (CH3COOH) is a weak electrolyte with low degree of dissociation, resulting in fewer ions and lower conductivity compared to strong acids and salts.

Question 3 of 10
Calculate the EMF of the cell: Zn | Zn2+ (0.01 M) || Cu2+ (0.1 M) | Cu, given E°cell = 1.10 V at 298 K.

Solution: Using Nernst equation: Ecell = 1.10 - (0.0591 / 2) log (0.01 / 0.1) = 1.10 - 0.02955 (-1) = 1.1295 V.

Question 4 of 10
During the electrolysis of molten NaCl, the product formed at the anode is:

Solution: At the anode, chloride ions undergo oxidation to yield chlorine gas: 2Cl- → Cl2(g) + 2e-.

Question 5 of 10
Which property decreases upon dilution of a weak electrolyte solution?

Solution: Specific conductance decreases on dilution because the number of current-carrying ions per unit volume decreases.

Question 6 of 10
What is the oxidation state of chromium in K2Cr2O7?

Solution: Let oxidation state of Cr be x. 2(+1) + 2(x) + 7(-2) = 0 ⇒ 2 + 2x - 14 = 0 ⇒ 2x = 12 ⇒ x = +6.

Question 7 of 10
Which metal cannot be obtained by the electrolysis of an aqueous solution of its salt?

Solution: Sodium has a very negative reduction potential (-2.71 V), so water is reduced preferentially at the cathode during aqueous electrolysis.

Question 8 of 10
The equivalent conductance of a 0.1 N solution of an electrolyte is 200 S cm2 eq-1. What is its specific conductance?

Solution: k = (Λeq × Normality) / 1000 = (200 × 0.1) / 1000 = 0.02 S cm-1.

Question 9 of 10
In a hydrogen-oxygen fuel cell, the reaction taking place at the cathode is:

Solution: In alkaline fuel cells, oxygen is reduced at the cathode: O2(g) + 2H2O(l) + 4e- → 4OH-(aq).

Question 10 of 10
What is the equilibrium constant (Kc) for a cell reaction with E°cell = 0.295 V at 298 K (with n = 2)?

Solution: E°cell = (0.0591 / n) log Kc ⇒ 0.295 = (0.0591 / 2) log Kc ⇒ log Kc = 10 ⇒ Kc = 1010.
