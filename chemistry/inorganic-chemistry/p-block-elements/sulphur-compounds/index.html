Inorganic Chemistry

Sulphur Compounds

Sulphur forms an extensive series of hydrides, halides, oxides, and oxoacids exhibiting variable oxidation states ranging from -2 to +6. These compounds play fundamental roles in industrial synthesis, including the manufacture of sulphuric acid via the contact process.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Oxides and Oxoacids of Sulphur

  • Sulphur dioxide (SO2) is a gas with acidic character, forming sulphites in aqueous alkaline solutions.
  • Sulphur trioxide (SO3) exists in multiple modifications including cyclic trimer (γ-form) and chain polymers (α-form).
  • Sulphuric acid (H2SO4) is a strong diprotic acid characterized by high boiling point and strong dehydrating properties.
  • Peroxodisulphuric acid (H2S2O8), known as Marshall's acid, contains a peroxide linkage (-O-O-) and sulphur in the +6 oxidation state.

Halides and Hydrides of Sulphur

  • Sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) is an extremely inert, octahedral gas due to steric hindrance and thermodynamic stability.
  • Sulphur dichloride (SCl2) and disulphur dichloride (S2Cl2) act as chlorinating agents and solvents.
  • Hydrogen sulphide (H2S) is a toxic, flammable gas with a characteristic rotten-egg odour and functions as a reducing agent.

Industrial Production and Applications

  • The Contact Process involves sulphur combustion, catalytic oxidation of SO2 to SO3, and absorption in H2SO4.
  • Sodium thiosulphate pentahydrate (Na2S2O3·5H2O) is applied in photography as a fixing agent.
  • Dithionic and polythionic acids feature direct sulphur-sulphur covalent bonding networks.

⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Group 16 hydrides decrease in thermal stability down the group.

Observation: Hydrogen sulphide has lower thermal stability and higher acidity than water. — Larger atomic radius of sulphur and weaker S-H bond energy compared to O-H.

Exception Hexafluorides of group 16 elements are reactive towards hydrolysis.

Observation: SF6 is kinetically inert to hydrolysis even at elevated temperatures. — Steric crowding of six fluorine atoms prevents nucleophilic attack of water at the central sulphur atom.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Sulphur dioxide turns acidified potassium dichromate paper from orange to green due to formation of Cr3+.
  • Concentrated sulphuric acid acts as a powerful dehydrating agent, removing water from carbohydrates like sucrose.
  • Oleum is produced by dissolving sulphur trioxide in 100% sulphuric acid.
  • SF6 has an octahedral geometry involving sp3d2 hybridization of the central sulphur atom.
  • Sodium thiosulphate is widely used in iodometric titrations to estimate iodine concentrations quantitatively.
  • Thiosulphuric acid (H2S2O3) features one terminal sulphur bonded via a double bond and one sulphide-like sulphur.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Assuming SF6 undergoes rapid hydrolysis like SF4 due to similar stoichiometry.
  • Confusing the oxidation state of sulphur in Caro's acid (+6) with normal lower-state sulphites.
  • Forgetting that concentrated H2SO4 cannot dry hydrogen sulphide because H2SO4 oxidizes H2S to sulphur.
  • Incorrectly assigning all sulphur atoms in sodium tetrathionate the same oxidation state.

⚡ Quick Revision: Sulphur Compounds

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Hydrides and Halides

Sulphur forms covalent hydrides and halides where higher oxidation states are stabilized by electronegative substituents like fluorine.

Oxides of Sulphur

SO2 and SO3 are principal acidic oxides forming sulphites and sulphates upon hydration.

Oxoacids Architecture

Sulphur oxoacids contain tetrahedral sulphur centres coordinated to hydroxyl groups, oxo groups, and occasionally peroxy or sulphur linkages.

Industrial Synthesis

Sulphuric acid is manufactured via the Contact Process, relying on catalytic heterogeneous oxidation of sulphur dioxide.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Caro's Acid (H2SO5)
Peroxomonosulphuric acid containing one -O-O- linkage.
Marshall's Acid (H2S2O8)
Peroxodisulphuric acid containing a central peroxy bridge.
Oleum (H2S2O7)
Fuming sulphuric acid formed by dissolving SO3 in H2SO4.
Hyposulphite (S2O42-)
Dithionite ion exhibiting strong reducing properties.
Thiosulphate (S2O32-)
Anion derived from sulphuric acid by replacing one oxygen with sulphur.
Hypo (Na2S2O3·5H2O)
Sodium thiosulphate pentahydrate used in photographic fixing.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • V2O5 is the standard catalyst for industrial SO2 oxidation.
  • SF6 is chemically inert due to steric hindrance around sulphur.
  • Concentrated H2SO4 has high affinity for water, liberating significant heat upon dilution.
  • Thiosulphate reacts with iodine to form tetrathionate and iodide.
  • Oxidation state of sulphur in H2S2O8 includes +6 with peroxy oxygen atoms.

📌 Quick Recall

  • Sulphur exhibits variable oxidation states from -2 to +6 in compounds.
  • Oxoacids feature tetrahedral structural units around sulphur atoms.
  • Contact process is the core industrial pathway for sulphuric acid manufacture.
  • Sulphur halides demonstrate varied reactivity based on steric and electronic factors.

⚖️ Key Differences

Caro's Acid (H2SO5) VS Marshall's Acid (H2S2O8)

Caro's acid is monoperoxy sulphurous/sulphuric acid with one sulphur, whereas Marshall's acid is peroxodisuphuric acid containing two sulphur atoms.

Question 1 of 19

What is the catalyst used in the Contact process for the conversion of SO2 to SO3?

Explanation: Vanadium pentoxide (V2O5) is the standard industrial catalyst employed at 450-500°C.

Question 2 of 19

Which of the following oxoacids of sulphur contains a peroxide linkage?

Explanation: Peroxodisulphuric acid (H2S2O8, Marshall's acid) contains a -O-O- peroxide bond.

Question 3 of 19

What is the hybridization of sulphur in SF6?

Explanation: Sulphur forms six bonds with fluorine, utilizing six orbitals resulting in sp3d2 octahedral hybridization.

Question 4 of 19

Concentrated sulphuric acid cannot be used to dry hydrogen sulphide because:

Explanation: Concentrated H2SO4 acts as an oxidizing agent and oxidizes H2S to free sulphur while reducing itself to SO2.

Question 5 of 19

What is the oxidation state of sulphur in sodium thiosulphate (Na2S2O3)?

Explanation: Sodium thiosulphate has two sulphur atoms with average oxidation state +2, but structurally one is -2 (sulphide-like) and the other is +6 (sulphate-like).

Question 6 of 19

Which gas turns acidified potassium dichromate paper green?

Explanation: Sulphur dioxide reduces dichromate (orange) to Cr3+ (green).

Question 7 of 19

Oleum is chemically defined as:

Explanation: Oleum (H2S2O7) is formed by dissolving sulphur trioxide in concentrated sulphuric acid.

Question 8 of 19

Which hydrides of group 16 is a liquid at room temperature?

Explanation: Water (H2O) is liquid due to extensive intermolecular hydrogen bonding, whereas others are gases.

Question 9 of 19

The geometry of sulphur dioxide molecule is:

Explanation: SO2 has a lone pair on the sulphur atom, resulting in a bent or angular molecular geometry.

Question 10 of 19

In iodometric titrations, sodium thiosulphate reacts with iodine to produce:

Explanation: I2 + 2S2O3^2- → S4O6^2- + 2I^- produces sodium tetrathionate.

Question 11 of 19

Which sulphur halide is extremely stable and inert to hydrolysis?

Explanation: SF6 is kinetically inert due to full coordination shielding of the central sulphur atom by fluorine atoms.

Question 12 of 19

The number of S=O bonds in sulphuric acid is:

Explanation: Sulphuric acid contains two terminal S=O double bonds and two S-OH single bonds.

Question 13 of 19

What is the product formed when excess chlorine reacts with cold, dilute sodium hydroxide containing sulphur compounds?

Explanation: Chlorine-alkali reactions do not directly involve sulphur unless specific sulphide ores are oxidized.

Question 14 of 19

Which acid is known as Marshall's acid?

Explanation: Peroxodisulphuric acid is commonly known as Marshall's acid.

Question 15 of 19

What is the basicity of orthophosphoric acid? (Control question for sulphur context)

Explanation: Orthophosphoric acid is tribasic (H3PO4), while standard sulphuric acid is dibasic.

Question 16 of 19

Which property of concentrated sulphuric acid accounts for its charring action on sugar?

Explanation: Concentrated H2SO4 extracts water molecules from carbohydrates (like C12H22O11), leaving behind elemental carbon.

Question 17 of 19

Which sulphur species acts exclusively as a reducing agent?

Explanation: In H2S, sulphur is in its lowest oxidation state (-2) and can only undergo oxidation, acting as a reducing agent.

Question 18 of 19

What is the colour of solid sulphur dioxide at low temperatures?

Explanation: Solid and gaseous sulphur dioxide are colourless.

Question 19 of 19

Which method is used for large-scale production of sulphur dioxide?

Explanation: S(s) + O2(g) → SO2(g) is the primary industrial and laboratory preparation route.

Which oxoacid of sulphur contains a direct sulphur-sulphur bond?

Solution: Dithionic acid (H2S2O6) contains a direct single covalent bond between the two sulphur atoms.

Question 2 of 10
The shape of sulphur hexafluoride (SF6) is:

Solution: Six bond pairs and zero lone pairs yield a regular octahedral geometry.

Question 3 of 10
Sulphur dioxide acts as a bleaching agent due to:

Solution: SO2 bleaches substances via temporary reduction in the presence of moisture.

Question 4 of 10
Which of the following has the highest boiling point?

Solution: Water possesses strong intermolecular hydrogen bonding, raising its boiling point drastically compared to heavier group 16 hydrides.

Question 5 of 10
What is the basicity of H2SO3 (sulphurous acid)?

Solution: H2SO3 has two ionizable hydrogen atoms attached to oxygen, making it diprotic (basicity 2).

Question 6 of 10
Which reagent is used to detect sulphate ions in aqueous solution?

Solution: Barium chloride reacts with sulphate ions to precipitate insoluble white barium sulphate (BaSO4).

Question 7 of 10
What is the oxidation state of sulphur in H2S?

Solution: Hydrogen has +1, hence sulphur must be -2 to balance the neutral molecule.

Question 8 of 10
Which compound is known as 'hypo' used in photography?

Solution: Sodium thiosulphate pentahydrate is commonly called hypo.

Question 9 of 10
During the contact process, why is SO3 absorbed in concentrated H2SO4 instead of water directly?

Solution: Direct dissolution of SO3 in water is highly exothermic and forms a stable mist of fine sulphuric acid droplets that are difficult to condense.

Question 10 of 10
What is the coordination number of sulphur in SF6?

Solution: Sulphur is bonded to six fluorine atoms, giving a coordination number of six.
