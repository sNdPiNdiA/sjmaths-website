Inorganic Chemistry

Boranes

Boranes are binary hydrides of boron characterized by electron-deficient multi-center two-electron bonds. They adopt polyhedral skeletal structures governed by Wade's rules based on skeletal electron pairs.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Structure and Bonding in Diborane

  • Diborane (B2H6) contains 12 valence electrons and possesses a bridged structure involving three-center two-electron (3c-2e) B-H-B bonds.
  • The molecule contains four terminal B-H bonds that lie in a single plane along with the two boron atoms.
  • The two bridging hydrogen atoms lie symmetrically above and below this nodal plane, forming two banana bonds.
  • The hybridization of each boron atom in diborane is sp3, utilizing three hybrid orbitals for normal bonds and one for the bridge.

Wade's Rules and Structural Classification

  • Closoboranes have the general formula [BnHn]2- and correspond to (n + 1) skeletal electron pairs.
  • Nidoboranes follow the formula BnHn+4 and possess (n + 2) skeletal electron pairs, representing a deltahedron with one vertex missing.
  • Arachnaboranes have the formula BnHn+6 and require (n + 3) skeletal electron pairs, corresponding to a framework missing two vertices.
  • Hypoboranes possess (n + 4) skeletal electron pairs with the empirical formula BnHn+8.

Preparation and Chemical Reactions of Boranes

  • Diborane is synthesized industrially by treating boron trifluoride with lithium aluminum hydride: 4BF3 + 3LiAlH4 → 2B2H6 + 3LiF + 3AlF3.
  • In the laboratory, diborane is prepared by oxidizing sodium borohydride with iodine: 2NaBH4 + I2 → B2H6 + 2NaI + H2.
  • Boranes are extremely reactive and undergo spontaneous ignition in air releasing large amounts of heat: B2H6 + 3O2 → B2O3 + 3H2O.
  • Reaction with Lewis bases like ammonia initially yields an addition compound [H2B(NH3)2]+[BH4]-, which upon pyrolysis forms borazine (B3N3H6).

⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Simple hydrides of group 13 elements form polymeric or covalent molecules with standard 2c-2e bonds.

Observation: Boron forms electron-deficient hydrides (boranes) with 3c-2e bridge bonds. — Small size and high electronegativity of boron compared to other group 13 elements favor multi-center bonding to achieve stable electron configurations.

Exception Hydrides of non-metals are typically stable toward moisture and air at room temperature.

Observation: Lower boranes like B2H6 are pyrophoric and catch fire spontaneously in moist air. — High thermodynamic instability combined with severe electron deficiency makes them extremely susceptible to nucleophilic attack by oxygen and water.

Exception Reaction of B2H6 with excess ammonia yields simple coordination complexes.

Observation: Pyrolysis of the diammoniate of diborane yields borazine (inorganic benzene) rather than simple adducts. — Elimination of hydrogen gas at elevated temperatures drives the condensation of the ionic intermediate into a cyclic B-N aromatic ring.

Closo = n+1, Nido = n+2, Arachno = n+3, Hypo = n+4

Application: Count skeletal electron pairs by taking total valence electrons of (B + H + negative charge - 2n) and dividing by 2.

[BnHn]2- → n+4 → n+6 → n+8

Application: Remember the sequence Closo (n, charge -2), Nido (n+4), Arachno (n+6), Hypo (n+8) for neutral borane series.

Diborane Structure Mnemonic
4 + 2 = 6

Application: 4 terminal H atoms and 2 bridging H atoms make up the 6 total hydrogens in B2H6.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Diborane (B2H6) contains two 3c-2e B-H-B bridge bonds and four terminal 2c-2e B-H bonds.
  • Boron exhibits sp3 hybridization in diborane.
  • Wade's rules are used to predict the skeletal geometries of boranes and heteroboranes based on skeletal electron pairs.
  • Borazine (B3N3H6) is isoelectronic and structurally analogous to benzene.
  • Lower boranes react vigorously with water to yield boric acid and hydrogen gas: B2H6 + 6H2O → 2H3BO3 + 6H2.
  • The reaction of diborane with asymmetric cleavage occurs with tertiary amines, yielding [H2B(NR3)2]+[BH4]-.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Assuming diborane has a planar structure similar to ethane; it is actually a bridged non-planar structure.
  • Confusing the number of terminal and bridging hydrogen atoms in B2H6.
  • Miscalculating skeletal electron pairs by forgetting to account for negative charges in closo-borane anions.
  • Confusing borazine with benzene in terms of chemical reactivity, as borazine is much more reactive due to polarity in B-N bonds.

⚡ Quick Revision: Boranes

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Electron Deficiency and Three-Center Bonding

Boranes are electron-deficient hydrides that cannot satisfy octet rules with normal covalent bonds, resulting in the formation of 3c-2e banana bonds.

Wade's Structural Taxonomy

Polyhedral skeletal electron pair theory (Wade's rules) classifies boranes into closo, nido, arachno, and hypo structures based on skeletal electron pair counts.

Synthesis and Reactivity

Diborane is prepared via reduction of boron halides with metal hydrides and exhibits high reactivity, acting as a strong reducing agent and Lewis acid.

Borazine and Inorganic Aromaticity

Reaction of diborane with ammonia yields borazine, a heterocyclic compound exhibiting aromatic character due to pπ-pπ back-bonding between boron and nitrogen.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Diborane (B2H6)
Simplest stable neutral borane containing bridging hydrogen atoms.
Banana Bond (3c-2e Bond)
A three-center two-electron bond holding three atoms together with two electrons.
Wade's Rules (SEP Theory)
Empirical rules relating skeletal electron pair count to polyhedral geometry.
Closoborane ([BnHn]2-)
Closed cage polyhedral borane anion with n+1 skeletal pairs.
Nidoborane (BnHn+4)
Nest-like open borane structure missing one vertex.
Borazine (B3N3H6)
Inorganic benzene synthesized from diborane and ammonia.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • B2H6 has 12 valence electrons total.
  • Bridging B-H bond length is longer than terminal B-H bond length.
  • Closo = n + 1 skeletal pairs.
  • Nido = n + 2 skeletal pairs.
  • Arachno = n + 3 skeletal pairs.

📌 Quick Recall

  • Boranes are electron-deficient hydrides requiring multi-center bonding.
  • Diborane features two 3c-2e B-H-B bridges and four terminal B-H bonds.
  • Wade's rules successfully rationalize skeletal geometries of cluster hydrides.
  • Boranes react with Lewis bases and undergo spontaneous combustion in air.

⚖️ Key Differences

Terminal B-H bond VS Bridging B-H bond

Terminal bonds are normal 2c-2e covalent bonds, whereas bridging bonds are electron-deficient 3c-2e bonds.
