Organic Chemistry

Polymerization

Polymerization is the chemical process of combining many small molecules known as monomers into a covalently bonded macromolecular chain. It proceeds primarily through either chain-growth (addition) or step-growth (condensation) mechanisms.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Chain-Growth Polymerization Mechanisms

  • Free-radical polymerization involves initiation, propagation, and termination steps using initiators like benzoyl peroxide.
  • Ionic polymerization proceeds via cationic (Lewis acid catalysts) or anionic (organometallic catalysts) active centers.
  • Ziegler-Natta catalysts (e.g., TiCl4 + Al(C2H5)3) yield stereoregular, high-density polymers.

Step-Growth and Condensation Reactions

  • Monomers must possess two or more functional groups to allow continuous chain extension.
  • Elimination of small by-products like H2O, HCl, or CH3OH typically accompanies the reaction.
  • High molecular mass is achieved only at very high fractional conversions (p > 0.98).

Copolymerization and Tacticity

  • Copolymers are synthesized from two or more distinct monomers, classified as statistical, alternating, block, or graft.
  • Tacticity defines the stereochemical arrangement of chiral centers along the polymer backbone (isotactic, syndiotactic, atactic).
  • Cross-linking forms three-dimensional networks, transforming thermoplastics into thermosetting plastics upon curing.
📐 Key Formulas & Equations
Number-Average Molecular Mass
Mn = ∑(NiMi) / ∑Ni
Conditions: Based on number of molecules of each mass
Units: g mol-1
Weight-Average Molecular Mass
Mw = ∑(NiMi2) / ∑(NiMi)
Conditions: Based on mass fraction of molecules
Units: g mol-1
Polydispersity Index (PDI)
PDI = Mw / Mn
Conditions: Measures molecular mass distribution width
Units: Dimensionless (≥ 1)
Degree of Polymerization (Carothers Equation)
Xn = 1 / (1 - p)
Conditions: For step-growth polymerization where p is fractional conversion
Units: Dimensionless number
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Step-growth polymers always eliminate small molecules like water or alcohol.

Observation: Polyurethanes formed from diisocyanates and diols eliminate no by-products. — The reaction proceeds via an addition mechanism across the isocyanate double bond (-N=C=O).

Exception Free-radical polymerization proceeds via random head-to-tail addition.

Observation: Head-to-head or tail-to-tail defects occur at a low percentage depending on steric factors. — Radical stability and steric hindrance during propagation dictate the addition orientation.

Exception Polymers are high molecular weight solids with definite melting points.

Observation: Atactic polymers are generally amorphous and lack a sharp melting point. — Random spatial orientation prevents efficient crystalline packing of chains.

💡 Shortcuts & Memory Aids
Carothers PDI Rule for Monodisperse Systems
PDI = 1.0

Application: Living polymers and natural proteins have uniform chain lengths where Mw equals Mn.

Monomer to Polymer Conversion Shortcut
n(Monomer) → (-M-)

Application: For addition polymers, the empirical formula of the repeating unit is identical to that of the monomer.

Ziegler-Natta Catalyst Identification
Transition Metal Halide + Alkyl Aluminum

Application: Look for TiCl4 paired with Al(C2H5)3 to immediately identify low-pressure HDPE synthesis.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Terylene (Dacron) is a polyester formed from ethylene glycol and terephthalic acid.
  • Nylon-6,6 is synthesized via condensation of hexamethylenediamine and adipic acid.
  • Buna-S is a copolymer of 1,3-butadiene and styrene in a 3:1 ratio.
  • Natural rubber is a cis-1,4-polyisoprene polymer.
  • Bakelite is a thermosetting polymer formed from phenol and formaldehyde.
  • Low-density polythene (LDPE) is produced by free-radical polymerization under high pressure and temperature.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing the repeating unit of Nylon-6 with Nylon-6,6 (Nylon-6 has 6 carbons from caprolactam).
  • Assuming condensation polymers always eliminate water (some eliminate HCl or alcohols).
  • Forgetting that PDI can never be less than 1.0 for synthetic polymers.
  • Assuming Mn and Mw represent the same average molecular mass.

⚡ Quick Revision: Polymerization

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Mechanism Types

Polymerization is broadly divided into chain-growth (requiring initiation, propagation, and termination) and step-growth (involving functional group condensation).

Molecular Mass Averages

Polymers exhibit a distribution of chain lengths, characterized quantitatively by number-average (Mn) and weight-average (Mw) molecular masses.

Stereochemistry and Tacticity

The spatial orientation of substituent groups along the polymer chain dictates physical properties such as crystallinity, tensile strength, and melting point.

Commercial Polymers

Industrial polymers include addition elastomers, thermoplastics, and cross-linked thermosetting resins tailored for specific applications.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Monomer (M)
Small reactive molecule that reacts to form a polymer chain.
Polymer ((M)n)
Macromolecule composed of multiple repeating monomer units.
Degree of Polymerization (Xn)
Number of monomer units incorporated in a polymer chain.
Polydispersity Index (PDI)
Ratio of weight-average to number-average molecular mass.
Tacticity (Stereoarrangement)
Stereochemical arrangement of chiral centers in a polymer backbone.
Copolymer (Mixed Polymer)
Polymer derived from two or more different monomer species.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Xn = 1 / (1 - p)
  • PDI = Mw / Mn
  • Natural rubber is cis-1,4-polyisoprene
  • Ziegler-Natta catalyst: TiCl4 + Al(C2H5)3
  • Nylon-6,6 monomers: Hexamethylenediamine and Adipic acid

📌 Quick Recall

  • Chain-growth involves active species and rapid propagation.
  • Step-growth relies on condensation between functional groups.
  • PDI measures the breadth of molecular mass distribution.
  • Copolymerization alters the physical and mechanical properties of materials.

⚖️ Key Differences

Thermoplastic VS Thermosetting

Thermoplastics soften on heating and can be remolded; thermosets undergo cross-linking and decompose upon heating.

The Polydispersity Index (PDI) for a completely monodisperse polymer is:

Explanation: Nylon-6,6 is a polyamide containing repeating -CONH- amide linkages.

Question 6 of 18

Explanation: Buna-S is synthesized by the copolymerization of 1,3-butadiene and styrene in a 3:1 ratio.

Question 7 of 18

Which of the following is a thermosetting polymer?

Explanation: Bakelite is formed from phenol and formaldehyde and forms an extensive cross-linked network that hardens permanently upon heating.

Question 8 of 18

What is the repeating unit in Nylon-6?

Explanation: Nylon-6 is polymerized from caprolactam and contains a five-carbon methylene chain with an amide group.

Question 9 of 18

Which species acts as the initiator in free-radical addition polymerization?

Explanation: Benzoyl peroxide undergoes homolytic cleavage to generate free radicals that initiate chain-growth.

Question 10 of 18

Which type of tacticity describes polymer chains where substituent groups are placed randomly?

Explanation: Atactic polymers have random placement of substituent groups along the polymer backbone.

Question 11 of 18

What is the value of fractional conversion (p) required to achieve a degree of polymerization (Xn) of 100 in step-growth polymerization?

Explanation: Using Xn = 1 / (1 - p), 100 = 1 / (1 - p), which gives 1 - p = 0.01, so p = 0.99.

Question 12 of 18

Neoprene is a synthetic rubber formed by the polymerization of:

Explanation: Neoprene is polychloroprene, formed by the addition polymerization of 2-chloro-1,3-butadiene (chloroprene).

Question 13 of 18

Explanation: Polyhydroxybutyrate-co-β-hydroxyvalerate (PHBV) is a biodegradable copolymer used in specialty packaging.

Question 14 of 18

Explanation: Melamine-formaldehyde resin (Melmac) is formed by the condensation of melamine and formaldehyde.

Question 15 of 18

Which property is measured by weight-average molecular mass (Mw)?

Explanation: Mw is typically determined by methods sensitive to molecular size, such as light scattering and ultracentrifugation.

Question 16 of 18

Which monomer is used to prepare Orlon (polyacrylonitrile)?

Explanation: Orlon is polyacrylonitrile, where acrylonitrile is also known as vinyl cyanide.

Question 17 of 18

Question 1 of 10
Calculate the degree of polymerization for a step-growth polymer at 95% monomer conversion.

Solution: Using Xn = 1 / (1 - p), where p = 0.95, Xn = 1 / (1 - 0.95) = 1 / 0.05 = 20.

Question 2 of 10
Which of the following is a homopolymer?

Solution: PVC (polyvinyl chloride) is formed from a single monomer (vinyl chloride), whereas the others are copolymers.

Question 3 of 10
Which monomer is polymerized to obtain Orlon?

Solution: Orlon is polyacrylonitrile, produced by addition polymerization of acrylonitrile.

Question 4 of 10
In free-radical polymerization, which step destroys the radical centers without forming new ones?

Solution: Termination occurs when two radical chains combine or disproportionate, eliminating active radicals.

Question 5 of 10
Which polymer is used for making non-stick cookware coatings?

Solution: Teflon (polytetrafluoroethylene, PTFE) provides high chemical inertness and a non-stick surface.

Question 6 of 10
What structural feature characterizes isotactic polypropylene?

Solution: In isotactic polymers, all substituent groups are located on the same side of the polymer backbone.

Question 7 of 10
Which of the following pairs represents addition polymers?

Solution: PVC and polystyrene are both formed by chain-growth addition polymerization without by-products.

Question 8 of 10
What is the function of sulfur in vulcanization of rubber?

Solution: Vulcanization introduces sulfur cross-links between polyisoprene chains, improving elasticity and tensile strength.

Question 9 of 10
Which functional groups react to form a polyester linkage in step-growth polymerization?

Solution: Esters are formed via condensation between carboxylic acid (-COOH) and hydroxyl (-OH) groups.

Question 10 of 10
If Mn = 50,000 g mol-1 and Mw = 75,000 g mol-1, what is the Polydispersity Index?

Solution: PDI = Mw / Mn = 75,000 / 50,000 = 1.50.
