Functions of Steroid Hormones

Steroid hormones are tetracyclic lipid-soluble derivatives of cholesterol containing the cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene ring system. They diffuse freely across cellular membranes to bind intracellular receptors, regulating gene transcription and protein synthesis.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Structural Classification and Biosynthesis

  • All steroid hormones are synthesized from cholesterol via the cleavage of the side chain to form pregnenolone in the mitochondria.
  • The core structural framework is the cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene ring system, comprising three cyclohexane rings (A, B, C) and one cyclopentane ring (D).
  • Carbon counts strictly define classes: Estrogens (C18) possess an aromatic A-ring, Androgens (C19) lack a C-17 side chain, and Corticosteroids/Progestins (C21) retain the two-carbon side chain.
  • Hydroxylation and dehydrogenation reactions catalyzed by cytochrome P450 enzymes dictate functional specificity across endocrine tissues.

Glucocorticoids and Mineralocorticoids Function

  • Cortisol acts as the primary glucocorticoid, stimulating gluconeogenesis, proteolysis, and lipolysis to maintain blood glucose levels during stress.
  • Glucocorticoids exert potent anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive actions by inhibiting phospholipase A2 via annexin induction.
  • Aldosterone functions as the principal mineralocorticoid, promoting sodium reabsorption and potassium excretion in the distal convoluted tubules of nephrons.
  • Mineralocorticoid action is mediated via cytosolic mineralocorticoid receptors, regulating blood volume and maintaining systemic electrolyte balance.

Sex Steroids and Receptor Mechanism

  • Testosterone and its potent derivative dihydrotestosterone (DHT) regulate the development of male secondary sex characteristics and spermatogenesis.
  • Estradiol and progesterone coordinate the female reproductive cycle, endometrial proliferation, and maintenance of pregnancy.
  • Because of their lipophilic nature, steroid hormones traverse the plasma membrane unassisted and bind to specific intracellular receptors in the cytoplasm or nucleus.
  • Hormone-receptor complexes act as transcription factors, binding to hormone response elements (HREs) on DNA to modulate specific gene expression.

⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Most steroid hormones contain 21 or more carbon atoms.

Observation: Estrogens possess only 18 carbon atoms. — Oxidative loss of the C-19 angular methyl group and aromatization of the A-ring during biosynthesis from androgens.

Exception Aldosterone exists predominantly as an open-chain keto-steroid.

Observation: Aldosterone exists primarily as a cyclic hemiacetal in aqueous physiological solution. — Spontaneous intramolecular reaction between the C-18 aldehyde group and the C-11 hydroxyl group.

Exception Steroid hormones act exclusively through slow genomic mechanisms.

Observation: Certain steroids trigger rapid non-genomic cellular responses within seconds to minutes. — Binding to specialized membrane-associated receptors coupled to second messenger cascades rather than intracellular nuclear receptors.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • The precursor for all steroid hormones is cholesterol, containing 27 carbon atoms.
  • The fundamental ring system of steroids is cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene (phenanthrene ring fused to a cyclopentane ring).
  • Estrogens are unique among steroid hormones because they possess an aromatic A-ring and 18 carbon atoms.
  • Aldosterone regulates blood pressure and electrolyte balance by acting on the distal tubules of the kidneys.
  • Glucocorticoids suppress the immune response by inhibiting the enzyme phospholipase A2.
  • Steroid hormone receptors function as ligand-activated transcription factors interacting with DNA response elements.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing the carbon count of androgens (C19) with estrogens (C18).
  • Assuming steroid hormones bind to cell-surface transmembrane G-protein coupled receptors exclusively, ignoring their primary intracellular nuclear receptor mechanism.
  • Confusing progesterone (C21) with testosterone (C19) during structural identification questions.
  • Forgetting that aldosterone contains an active cyclic hemiacetal form in aqueous solution due to C-18 and C-11 interaction.

⚡ Quick Revision: Functions of Steroid Hormones

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Steroid Core and Nomenclature

Steroids are derivatives of the saturated tetracyclic hydrocarbon sterane (cyclopentanoperhydrophenanthrene). Substituents and double bonds follow strict IUPAC stereochemical numbering conventions.

Adrenocortical Hormones

Glucocorticoids (cortisol) regulate carbohydrate metabolism and suppress inflammation, while mineralocorticoids (aldosterone) control sodium and water retention in renal tubules.

Sex Hormones

Androgens, estrogens, and progestins govern sexual differentiation, gametogenesis, and the maintenance of secondary sex characteristics via genomic regulation.

Mechanism of Action

Lipophilic steroids cross the phospholipid bilayer, bind intracellular receptors, dimerize, and recruit coregulators to modulate specific gene transcription.

📚 Terms & Units Glossary

Pregnane (C21H36)
Parent hydrocarbon structure for progestins, glucocorticoids, and mineralocorticoids.
Androstane (C19H32)
Parent hydrocarbon structure for androgens such as testosterone.
Estrane (C18H30)
Parent hydrocarbon structure for estrogens lacking a C-19 methyl group.
Cortisol (C21H30O5)
Primary human glucocorticoid regulating energy metabolism.
Aldosterone (C21H28O5)
Primary mineralocorticoid controlling sodium homeostasis.
HRE (Hormone Response Element)
Specific DNA sequence where steroid-receptor complexes bind.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Cholesterol has 27 carbon atoms and serves as the universal precursor.
  • Estrogens have 18 carbons and an aromatic A-ring.
  • Androgens have 19 carbons and lack the C-17 side chain.
  • Corticosteroids and progestins have 21 carbons.
  • Steroid receptors function as ligand-activated transcription factors.

📌 Quick Recall

  • Steroids are lipid-soluble hormones derived from cholesterol.
  • Classified by carbon count into C18, C19, and C21 families.
  • Exert biological effects by binding intracellular receptors and altering gene transcription.
  • Crucial for electrolyte balance, metabolism, and reproduction.

⚖️ Key Differences

Glucocorticoids VS Mineralocorticoids

Glucocorticoids regulate glucose metabolism and stress response, whereas mineralocorticoids regulate electrolyte balance and blood volume.
