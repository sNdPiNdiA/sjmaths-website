Preparation of Benzoic Acid

Benzoic acid is prepared industrially and in the laboratory via oxidation of alkylbenzenes, hydrolysis of nitriles or benzotrichlorides, and carbonation of Grignard reagents. The aromatic carboxylic acid serves as a fundamental intermediate in organic synthesis.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Oxidation of Alkylbenzenes

  • All alkylbenzenes (toluene, ethylbenzene, isopropylbenzene) with at least one benzylic hydrogen yield benzoic acid upon vigorous oxidation with alkaline or acidic KMnO4.
  • The alkyl chain is progressively cleaved down to a single carboxyl group (-COOH) attached directly to the aromatic ring.
  • Tertiary alkylbenzenes (e.g., tert-butylbenzene) do not react under these conditions due to the absence of benzylic hydrogen atoms.

Hydrolysis of Derivatives and Nitriles

  • Benzonitrile undergoes hydrolysis when treated with aqueous acids or bases to yield benzoic acid and ammonia/ammonium salts.
  • Benzotrichloride (trichloromethylbenzene) undergoes exhaustive nucleophilic substitution of chlorine atoms by hydroxyl groups to form benzoic acid upon heating with aqueous calcium hydroxide.
  • Cyanobenzene derivatives can be synthesized from primary aromatic amines via Sandmeyer reaction (CuCN/KCN) prior to hydrolysis.

Grignard Reagent Carbonation

  • Phenylmagnesium bromide reacts with solid carbon dioxide (dry ice) to yield a magnesium salt of benzoic acid.
  • Subsequent acid hydrolysis with dilute mineral acids (HCl or H2SO4) liberates free benzoic acid.
  • This method is particularly useful for extending the carbon chain by one carbon atom from an aryl halide precursor.

⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Alkylbenzenes yield benzoic acid on KMnO4 oxidation

Observation: Tert-butylbenzene does not yield benzoic acid — Complete lack of benzylic C-H bonds prevents the initial free-radical or oxidative hydrogen abstraction step.

Exception Primary alkyl groups yield benzoic acid regardless of chain length

Observation: Longer-chain alkylbenzenes undergo cleavage of all extra carbons — Oxidative cleavage attacks the activated benzylic carbon and breaks adjacent C-C bonds, eventually leaving only the single carbon attached to the ring.

💡 Shortcuts & Memory Aids
Benzylic Hydrogen Rule
n(Benzylic H) ≥ 1 → Benzoic Acid

Application: Check for at least one hydrogen on the alpha-carbon of an alkylbenzene to confirm successful oxidation to benzoic acid.

Grignard Carbon Addition
Ar-X + CO2 → Ar-COOH

Application: Use this method instantly when an organic synthesis problem requires increasing the carbon count by one unit to form an aromatic acid.

Trichloride Hydrolysis Shortcut

Application: Recall that gem-trihalides on a terminal carbon hydrolyze to carboxylic acids after losing water equivalents.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Toluene on oxidation with acidic KMnO4 gives benzoic acid.
  • Tert-butylbenzene is resistant to oxidation by alkaline KMnO4.
  • Grignard reagent synthesis of benzoic acid requires dry ice (solid CO2).
  • Benzonitrile yields benzoic acid upon acid-catalyzed hydrolysis.
  • Benzotrichloride is prepared by exhaustive side-chain chlorination of toluene.
  • Benzoic acid is a weaker acid than formic acid but stronger than acetic acid.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Assuming tert-butylbenzene can be oxidized to benzoic acid by KMnO4.
  • Forgetting to add the acid hydrolysis step (H3O+) after Grignard carbonation.
  • Using wet ether instead of dry ether during the preparation of the Grignard reagent.
  • Confusing ring substitution with side-chain substitution during toluene chlorination.

⚡ Quick Revision: Preparation of Benzoic Acid

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Oxidative Routes

Oxidation of alkylbenzenes targets the benzylic carbon. Any alkyl substituent with at least one benzylic hydrogen is oxidized completely to a -COOH group.

Organometallic Carbonation

Aryl Grignard reagents function as strong nucleophiles that attack electrophilic carbon dioxide, inserting a carbonyl carbon to form benzoate salts.

Hydrolysis Pathways

Both benzonitrile and benzotrichloride undergo stepwise hydrolysis where nitrogen and chlorine atoms are replaced by oxygen to furnish the carboxylic acid functionality.

Synthetic Utility

These versatile pathways allow aromatic carboxylic acids to be accessed from petroleum-derived hydrocarbons, aryl halides, and coal-tar derivatives.

Grignard Reagent (R-Mg-X)

Aromatic nitrile yielding benzoic acid on hydrolysis.
Benzotrichloride (C6H5CCl3)
(Trichloromethyl)benzene used to prepare benzoic acid via hydrolysis.
Dry Ice (Solid CO2)
Reagent providing the carbonyl carbon in Grignard carbonation.
Alkaline KMnO4 (KMnO4 / KOH)
Strong oxidizing agent used for side-chain oxidation of alkylbenzenes.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Only alkylbenzenes with ≥1 benzylic H yield benzoic acid on oxidation.
  • Grignard carbonation extends the carbon chain by one carbon.
  • Benzotrichloride hydrolysis requires calcium hydroxide and heat.
  • Oxidation of toluene breaks all side-chain carbons beyond the alpha carbon.
  • Nitrile hydrolysis proceeds through an amide intermediate.

📌 Quick Recall

  • Alkylbenzenes oxidize to benzoic acid using KMnO4.
  • Grignard reagents react with CO2 to form benzoic acid salts.
  • Nitriles and benzotrichloride hydrolyze to benzoic acid.
  • Benzylic hydrogen presence is mandatory for side-chain oxidation.

⚖️ Key Differences

Toluene Oxidation VS Tert-butylbenzene Oxidation

Toluene has benzylic hydrogens and oxidizes; tert-butylbenzene lacks benzylic hydrogens and does not oxidize.
