Preparation of Benzaldehyde

Benzaldehyde is an aromatic aldehyde prepared via oxidation, reduction, or formylation of toluene and benzene derivatives. Specific synthetic routes dictate whether the intermediate can be protected from over-oxidation to benzoic acid.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Oxidation of Toluene Derivatives

  • Etard Reaction utilizes chromyl chloride (CrO2Cl2) to selectively oxidize the methyl group of toluene to a chromium complex that hydrolyzes to benzaldehyde.
  • Use of chromic anhydride (CrO3) in acetic anhydride converts toluene into benzylidene diacetate, which yields benzaldehyde upon acid hydrolysis.
  • Side-chain chlorination of toluene yields benzal chloride (C6H5CHCl2), which undergoes nucleophilic substitution and hydrolysis to form benzaldehyde.

Formylation of Benzene Rings

  • Gattermann-Koch Reaction treats benzene with carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride in the presence of anhydrous AlCl3 and CuCl to yield benzaldehyde.
  • The active electrophile in the Gattermann-Koch reaction is formyl cation or a protonated carbon monoxide species generated via Lewis acid coordination.
  • Gattermann Reaction substitutes hydrogen cyanide (HCN) and HCl in place of CO and HCl, yielding an imine intermediate that hydrolyzes to benzaldehyde.

Reduction of Benzoyl Chloride

  • Rosenmund Reduction involves the catalytic hydrogenation of benzoyl chloride over palladium poisoned with barium sulfate (BaSO4).
  • The barium sulfate surface acts as a catalytic poison to decrease palladium activity, preventing the over-reduction of benzaldehyde to benzyl alcohol.
  • Alternative selective reductions use lithium tri-tert-butoxyaluminohydride [LiAlH(O-t-Bu)3] to reduce acid chlorides directly to aldehydes at low temperatures.
📐 Key Formulas & Equations
Gattermann-Koch Reaction
C6H6 + CO + HCl → [HCOCl] → C6H5CHO
Conditions: Anhydrous AlCl3 and CuCl catalyst under pressure
Units: Gas phase reactants under anhydrous Lewis acid catalysis
Rosenmund Reduction
C6H5COCl + H2 → C6H5CHO + HCl
Conditions: Catalytic hydrogenation over Pd supported on BaSO4 poisoned with quinoline or sulfur
Units: Gas-liquid heterogeneous catalytic reduction
Side-chain Chlorination followed by Hydrolysis
C6H5CH3 + 2 Cl2 → C6H5CHCl2 + 2 HCl; C6H5CHCl2 + H2O → C6H5CHO + 2 HCl
Conditions: UV light for free radical halogenation, followed by aqueous base or acid hydrolysis at 373 K
Units: Molar ratios: 1 toluene : 2 chlorine gas
Etard Reaction
C6H5CH3 + 2 CrO2Cl2 → C6H5CH3•2CrO2Cl2 → C6H5CHO
Conditions: Chromyl chloride in CS2 or CCl4 solvent followed by acidic hydrolysis
Units: Stoichiometric molar ratio: 1 toluene : 2 chromyl chloride
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Strong oxidizing agents like KMnO4 or HNO3 oxidize alkylbenzenes directly to benzoic acid.

Observation: Etard reaction (CrO2Cl2) and CrO3 in acetic anhydride stop specifically at the aldehyde stage. — Chromium complexes formed isolate the intermediate oxidation state and prevent further oxidation to carboxylic acids.

Exception Rosenmund reduction successfully converts acid chlorides to aldehydes using palladium catalysts.

Observation: Formaldehyde cannot be prepared by this method because formyl chloride is extremely unstable. — H-C(=O)Cl decomposes rapidly into carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride under ambient conditions.

Exception Friedel-Crafts acylation and formylation normally require Lewis acid catalysts like AlCl3.

Observation: Gattermann-Koch reaction does not work with phenol or aniline derivatives. — Lewis acid catalysts coordinate strongly with the heteroatom lone pairs of phenols and amines, deactivating the aromatic ring.

💡 Shortcuts & Memory Aids

Toluene + 2 CrO2Cl2

Application: Remember that 2 moles of chromyl chloride are required per 1 mole of toluene to form the brown addition compound.

Rosenmund Catalyst Poison Mnemonic
Pd/BaSO4 + Poison

Application: Associate BaSO4 with 'Brake' (it acts as a brake on Pd activity) to prevent reduction past the aldehyde stage.

Gattermann vs Gattermann-Koch
Koch uses CO + HCl; Standard uses HCN + HCl

Application: Use the presence of 'Koch' as a cue for Carbon Monoxide (CO) as the reactant.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Chromyl chloride (CrO2Cl2) oxidizes the methyl group of toluene via an Etard complex.
  • Rosenmund reduction employs Pd/BaSO4 to prevent the reduction of benzaldehyde to benzyl alcohol.
  • Gattermann-Koch reaction fails for phenol due to catalyst deactivation by coordination.
  • Benzal chloride (C6H5CHCl2) on alkaline hydrolysis yields benzaldehyde.
  • Lithium tri-tert-butoxyaluminohydride reduces acid chlorides selectively to aldehydes.
  • Formyl chloride cannot be isolated, requiring in-situ generation in the Gattermann-Koch reaction.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing the Etard reagent (CrO2Cl2) with standard chromic acid (H2CrO4) which gives benzoic acid.
  • Omitting barium sulfate in Rosenmund reduction, leading to complete reduction to benzyl alcohol.
  • Assuming Gattermann-Koch works on aniline and phenol without ring protection.
  • Forgetting that side-chain chlorination requires UV light or radical initiators.

⚡ Quick Revision: Preparation of Benzaldehyde

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Oxidative Methods

Toluene is selectively converted to benzaldehyde using mild oxidants like chromyl chloride or chromium trioxide in acetic anhydride, avoiding over-oxidation.

Formylation Routes

Benzene undergoes electrophilic aromatic substitution via the Gattermann-Koch or Gattermann reactions using carbon monoxide/HCN and HCl under Lewis acid catalysis.

Reduction of Acyl Chlorides

Benzoyl chloride is catalytically hydrogenated using poisoned palladium catalysts in the Rosenmund reduction to yield benzaldehyde.

Hydrolysis of Gem-Dihalides

Exhaustive side-chain halogenation of toluene produces benzal chloride, which undergoes nucleophilic substitution and hydrolysis to form benzaldehyde.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Etard reagent formula: CrO2Cl2
  • Rosenmund catalyst: Pd supported on BaSO4
  • Gattermann-Koch catalysts: Anhydrous AlCl3 and CuCl
  • Hydrolysis product of benzal chloride: Benzaldehyde
  • Active species in Gattermann-Koch: Protonated CO or formyl cation

📌 Quick Recall

  • Benzaldehyde is synthesized primarily from toluene and benzene derivatives.
  • Selective oxidation or poisoned reduction prevents over-oxidation to benzoic acid.
  • Lewis acid catalysis is vital for aromatic formylation.
  • Side-chain halogenation followed by hydrolysis provides an alternative alkylbenzene route.

⚖️ Key Differences

Etard Reaction VS Complete Oxidation

Etard uses CrO2Cl2 to stop at the aldehyde; complete oxidation uses KMnO4 to form benzoic acid.

Question 8 of 18

Explanation: Reaction with CrO3 in acetic anhydride converts toluene into benzylidene diacetate, which yields benzaldehyde upon acid hydrolysis.

Which catalyst is used in the Gattermann-Koch reaction along with CuCl?

Explanation: Anhydrous aluminum chloride (AlCl3) is the primary Lewis acid catalyst used alongside copper(I) chloride in the Gattermann-Koch reaction.

Question 11 of 18

Which of the following methods involves free-radical halogenation followed by hydrolysis?

Explanation: Chlorination of toluene in the side-chain using Cl2 and UV light produces benzal chloride, which on hydrolysis gives benzaldehyde.

Question 12 of 18

Why does Rosenmund reduction stop at the aldehyde stage?

Explanation: The poisoning of palladium by barium sulfate reduces its hydrogenation capacity so that aldehydes are not reduced further to alcohols.

Which reducing agent can convert benzoyl chloride directly into benzaldehyde without using hydrogen gas?

Explanation: Lithium tri-tert-butoxyaluminohydride [LiAlH(O-t-Bu)3] is a sterically hindered reducing agent that converts acid chlorides to aldehydes.

Question 14 of 18

What is the chemical formula of chromyl chloride?

Explanation: Chromyl chloride has the chemical formula CrO2Cl2 and is a dark red liquid used in the Etard reaction.

Question 16 of 18

In side-chain chlorination of toluene to prepare benzal chloride, how many moles of Cl2 are consumed per mole of toluene?

Explanation: Two moles of chlorine gas react with one mole of toluene, replacing two alpha-hydrogens to form benzal chloride (C6H5CHCl2).

Question 17 of 18

Which functional group is converted to -CHO in the Rosenmund reduction?

Explanation: The Rosenmund reduction specifically reduces acyl chlorides (-COCl) to aldehydes (-CHO).

Question 18 of 18

Benzaldehyde can be prepared industrially by the oxidation of toluene with air in the presence of which catalyst?

Explanation: Vapor-phase oxidation of toluene with air or oxygen over vanadium pentoxide (V2O5) yields benzaldehyde.
