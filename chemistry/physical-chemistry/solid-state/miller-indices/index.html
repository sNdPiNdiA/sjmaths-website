Miller Indices

Miller indices are a system of notation used in crystallography to define crystal planes and directions in Bravais lattices. They are represented by three integers (hkl) for planes and [uvw] for directions, derived from the reciprocals of the fractional intercepts made by the plane with the crystallographic axes.

UP PGT Chemistry Subject Code 02 B.Sc. / M.Sc. Level

Derivation and Determination of Miller Indices

  • Find the intercepts of the crystal plane along the crystallographic axes a, b, and c in terms of unit cell dimensions.
  • Take the reciprocals of these fractional intercepts.
  • Clear fractions to obtain the smallest set of integers (hkl) enclosed in parentheses.
  • If a plane is parallel to an axis, its intercept is at infinity (∞), making its Miller index zero.

Crystallographic Planes and Directions

  • Planes are denoted by round brackets (hkl), while a family of symmetry-related planes is denoted by curly brackets {hkl}.
  • Directions are represented by square brackets [uvw] and form orthogonal sets to corresponding planes in cubic systems where h=u, k=v, l=w.
  • Negative intercepts are denoted by a bar over the integer (e.g., h̄ written as h̄kl).
  • Interplanar spacing dhkl decreases as the sum of squares of Miller indices (h2 + k2 + l2) increases.

Symmetry and Cubic Lattice Spacing

  • In cubic crystals, a plane with indices (100) is perpendicular to the x-axis.
  • The spacing between adjacent planes decreases for higher order indices, affecting the X-ray diffraction peak positions.
  • Permutation of indices such as (110), (101), and (011) belong to the same family of planes {110} in a cubic system.
  • The density of lattice points in a plane increases with lower Miller index values.

Interplanar Spacing for Cubic Systems
dhkl = a / √(h2 + k2 + l2)
Conditions: Cubic crystal system where a = b = c
Units: dhkl and a in meters or Angstroms (∠)
Angle Between Two Planes
cos(θ) = (h1h2 + k1k2 + l1l2) / [√(h12 + k12 + l12) √(h22 + k22 + l22)]
Conditions: Cubic crystal system
Units: Dimensionless ratio yielding angle in degrees or radians
Bragg's Law for Interplanar Spacing
λ = 2dhkl sin(θ)
Conditions: X-ray diffraction from (hkl) planes
Units: λ and d in meters or Angstroms, θ in degrees
Reciprocal Intercepts Rule
h : k : l = (a / x) : (b / y) : (c / z)
Conditions: Interception of crystal plane along unit cell axes a, b, and c
Units: Dimensionless integers
⚠️ Important Exceptions & Anomalies
Exception Planes passing through the origin must have their coordinates shifted.

Observation: Origin translation — If a plane passes through the origin (0,0,0), a parallel plane must be constructed or the origin shifted to determine finite intercepts.

Exception Equivalence of planes and directions in non-cubic systems.

Observation: Anisotropy in tetragonal, orthorhombic, and hexagonal systems — Unequal axial lengths (a ≠ b ≠ c) mean that orthogonal directions and planes do not share identical numerical indices.

🎯 Key Exam Points
  • Miller indices are always expressed as the smallest possible integers.
  • A plane parallel to an axis has a Miller index of 0.
  • For cubic crystals, the direction [hkl] is perpendicular to the plane (hkl).
  • The interplanar spacing formula for cubic systems is d = a / √(h2 + k2 + l2).
  • Negative intercepts are denoted with a overline, e.g., (1̄00).
  • The sum of squares of Miller indices determines the relative density of planes.
🚫 Common Errors & Confusions
  • Confusing plane notation (hkl) with direction notation [uvw].
  • Failing to reduce fractions to the lowest integers (e.g., writing (222) instead of (111)).
  • Forgetting to place a negative sign as an overline for negative axis intercepts.
  • Applying non-cubic interplanar formulas to cubic crystal systems.

⚡ Quick Revision: Miller Indices

Core formulas, rapid recall points, and distinguishing criteria at a glance.

Intercepts and Reciprocals

Miller indices are determined by taking the reciprocals of fractional intercepts made by a plane with the unit cell axes and clearing fractions to the lowest integers.

Planes vs Directions

Crystallographic planes are designated by (hkl) brackets, whereas crystallographic directions are represented by [uvw] brackets based on vector coordinates.

Interplanar Spacing

The perpendicular distance between adjacent parallel planes (dhkl) depends on the unit cell parameter 'a' and the sum of the squares of the Miller indices.

Symmetry Families

Equivalent crystallographic planes due to crystal symmetry are grouped into families denoted by {hkl} curly brackets.

🎯 Key Takeaways

  • Plane parallel to axis → Index is 0
  • Cubic system direction [hkl] is perpendicular to plane (hkl)
  • dhkl = a / √(h2 + k2 + l2)
  • Smallest integers rule for Miller indices
  • Negative intercept denoted with a bar

📌 Quick Recall

  • Reciprocals of fractional axis intercepts give Miller indices.
  • Planes use (hkl), directions use [uvw].
  • Interplanar spacing decreases as h2 + k2 + l2 increases.
  • Cubic systems simplify vector-plane orthogonality relationships.

⚖️ Key Differences

(hkl) Planes VS [uvw] Directions

(hkl) represents crystallographic planes using reciprocals, while [uvw] represents vector directions without reciprocal conversion.

Question 1 of 20

What are the Miller indices of a plane that cuts the x and y axes at unit intercepts and is parallel to the z-axis?

Explanation: Intercepts are x=1, y=1, z=∞. Reciprocals are 1/1=1, 1/1=1, 1/∞=0. Thus, the indices are (110).

Question 2 of 20

If the intercepts of a plane on the axes are a, 2b, and 3c, what are its Miller indices?

Explanation: Intercepts are 1, 2, 3. Reciprocals are 1, 1/2, 1/3. Multiply by LCM (6) to clear fractions: 6, 3, 2. Thus, (632).

Question 3 of 20

In a cubic crystal, what is the angle between the [100] direction and the (100) plane?

Explanation: In a cubic crystal system, the direction [hkl] is perpendicular to the plane (hkl), meaning the [100] direction is normal to (100), making it parallel (0 degrees) to the normal of the plane, or the direction is perpendicular to the plane itself.

Question 4 of 20

A plane is parallel to the x and y axes and cuts the z-axis at 1 unit. Its Miller indices are:

Explanation: Intercepts: x=∞, y=∞, z=1. Reciprocals: 0, 0, 1. Miller indices: (001).

Question 5 of 20

Which brackets are used to denote a family of equivalent crystallographic planes?

Explanation: Curly brackets {hkl} denote a family of symmetry-equivalent planes, while denotes a family of equivalent directions.

Question 6 of 20

What is the interplanar spacing d for (110) planes in a simple cubic lattice with edge length 'a'?

Explanation: d = a / √(12 + 12 + 02) = a / √2.

Question 7 of 20

If a crystal plane has indices (2, -1, 1), how is it conventionally written?

Explanation: Negative indices are written with a bar over the number, so -1 becomes 1̄, yielding (21̄1).

Question 8 of 20

What does a Miller index of zero (0) for an axis indicate?

Explanation: A zero index arises because the intercept is at infinity (∞), meaning the plane is parallel to that axis.

Question 9 of 20

What is the Miller index of a plane passing through the origin (0,0,0)?

Explanation: Planes passing through the origin yield infinite or zero intercepts that cannot be directly indexed, requiring a parallel plane shift.

Question 10 of 20

For a cubic crystal, which set of planes has the highest interplanar spacing?

Explanation: Interplanar spacing d = a / √(h2 + k2 + l2). Lower sum of squares (1 for {100}) yields the largest d.

Question 11 of 20

Which of the following represents a direction rather than a plane?

Explanation: Square brackets [uvw] designate crystallographic directions, whereas parentheses designate planes.

Question 12 of 20

Determine the Miller indices if intercepts along axes are 3a, 4b, and 2c.

Explanation: Reciprocals: 1/3, 1/4, 1/2. Multiply by LCM (12): 4, 3, 6. Indices: (436).

Question 13 of 20

What is the zone axis condition for a direction [uvw] to lie in a plane (hkl)?

Explanation: A direction [uvw] lies in plane (hkl) if the Weiss zone law is satisfied: hu + kv + lw = 0.

Question 14 of 20

In a face-centered cubic (FCC) lattice, which planes are allowed in X-ray diffraction according to systematic absences?

Explanation: For FCC lattices, reflections occur only when Miller indices h, k, and l are all even or all odd.

Question 15 of 20

What is the ratio of d100 : d110 : d111 for a cubic crystal?

Explanation: d100 = a/1, d110 = a/√2, d111 = a/√3. Ratio is 1 : 1/√2 : 1/√&3;.

Question 16 of 20

How many equivalent planes belong to the {100} family in a cubic unit cell?

Explanation: The {100} family includes (100), (010), (001), (1̄00), (01̄0), and (001̄), totaling 6 faces.

Question 17 of 20

What are the Miller indices of a plane intersecting axes at a/2, b/3, and c/4?

Explanation: Intercepts are 1/2, 1/3, 1/4. Reciprocals: 2, 3, 4. Indices: (234).

Question 18 of 20

Which crystal system has equal axial angles (α = β = γ = 90°) but unequal axes (a ≠ b ≠ c)?

Explanation: Orthorhombic systems feature a ≠ b ≠ c and α = β = γ = 90°.

Question 19 of 20

What is the value of h2 + k2 + l2 for the (221) plane?

Explanation: 22 + 22 + 12 = 4 + 4 + 1 = 9.

Question 20 of 20

Why are Miller indices preferred over actual axis intercepts in crystallography?

Explanation: Miller indices provide a compact, scale-independent notation that handles parallel planes and intercepts at infinity uniformly.

Question 1 of 10
Calculate the interplanar spacing for (220) planes in a cubic lattice with a = 4.0 ∠.

Solution: d = 4.0 / √(22 + 22 + 02) = 4.0 / √8 = 4.0 / 2.828 = 1.414 ∠.

Question 2 of 10
A crystal plane cuts intercepts of 3a, 3b, and 3c. What are its Miller indices?

Solution: Reciprocals of 3, 3, 3 are 1/3, 1/3, 1/3. Reducing to smallest integers yields (111).

Question 3 of 10
Which of the following families of planes has the highest atomic packing density in a simple cubic unit cell?

Solution: {100} planes contain the highest relative area density of lattice points in a simple cubic lattice.

Question 4 of 10
If direction [211] is perpendicular to a plane (hkl) in a cubic system, what are h, k, and l?

Solution: In cubic systems, the direction [uvw] is perpendicular to the plane (hkl) when h = u, k = v, and l = w, resulting in (211).

Question 5 of 10
What is the sum of h2 + k2 + l2 for the plane (321)?

Solution: 32 + 22 + 12 = 9 + 4 + 1 = 14.

Question 6 of 10
A plane has intercepts ∞a, b, ∞c. Its Miller indices are:

Solution: Intercepts: x=∞, y=1, z=∞. Reciprocals: 0, 1, 0. Indices: (010).

Question 7 of 10
In X-ray diffraction, which Miller indices correspond to the second-order reflection of (100)?

Solution: Higher-order reflections from (hkl) planes correspond to (nh nk nl), so second-order of (100) is (200).

Question 8 of 10
What are the Miller indices of a plane making intercepts of a/3, b/2, and ∞c?

Solution: Reciprocals of 1/3, 1/2, ∞ are 3, 2, 0. Indices: (320).

Question 9 of 10
Which condition must be met by a direction [uvw] to lie in the plane (111)?

Solution: Using hu + kv + lw = 0 with h=1, k=1, l=1 gives 1(u) + 1(v) + 1(w) = 0, so u + v + w = 0.

Question 10 of 10
How many distinct planes are in the {110} family of a cubic crystal?

Solution: The {110} family includes permutations of (±1, ±1, 0), totaling 12 equivalent planes in a cubic unit cell.
