1. Discovery Milestones & Historiographical Timeline 1. खोज के महत्वपूर्ण पड़ाव एवं ऐतिहासिक कालक्रम

1826 Charles Masson First European explorer to visit Harappa mounds; documented ruins in 'Narrative of Various Journeys in Balochistan, Afghanistan and the Panjab'. हड़प्पा के टीलों का दौरा करने वाले प्रथम यूरोपीय यात्री; अपनी पुस्तक में खंडहरों का प्रथम अभिलेखीकरण किया।
1853 & 1856 Alexander Cunningham Father of Indian Archaeology; surveyed Harappa; acquired first Indus seal (hump-less bull motif) but failed to realize its antiquity. भारतीय पुरातत्व के जनक; हड़प्पा का सर्वेक्षण किया; प्रथम सिन्धु मुहर (बिना कूबड़ वाला वृषभ) प्राप्त की।
1856 John & William Brunton Brunton brothers used ancient Harappan baked bricks as track ballast while laying the Lahore-Karachi railway line. लाहौर-कराची रेलवे लाइन बिछाते समय ब्रंटन बंधुओं ने गिट्टी के रूप में प्राचीन हड़प्पा कालीन पकी ईंटों का उपयोग किया।
1921 Daya Ram Sahni First scientific excavation at Harappa (Montgomery/Sahiwal, Punjab, Pakistan) under ASI Director Sir John Marshall. सर जॉन मार्शल के निर्देशन में हड़प्पा का प्रथम वैज्ञानिक उत्खनन प्रारम्भ किया।
1922 Rakhaldas (R.D.) Banerjee Discovered Mohenjo-daro (Larkana, Sindh) while investigating a 2nd century CE Kushana Buddhist stupa mound. कुषाण कालीन बौद्ध स्तूप टीले की खोज करते हुए मोहनजोदड़ो की खोज की।
1924 Sir John Marshall Officially announced the discovery of a new Bronze Age civilization to the world in 'The Illustrated London News'; coined term 'Indus Civilization'. 'द इलस्ट्रेटेड लंदन न्यूज' में विश्व के समक्ष सिन्धु घाटी सभ्यता की ऐतिहासिक घोषणा की; 'सिन्धु सभ्यता' नामकरण किया।

2. Geographical Extent & Boundary Extremes 2. भौगोलिक विस्तार एवं चारों छोर

The Harappan civilization occupied a roughly triangular area of ~1.3 million sq. km (12,99,600 sq. km), exceeding contemporary Egypt and Mesopotamia combined. हड़प्पा सभ्यता लगभग 13 लाख वर्ग किमी (12,99,600 वर्ग किमी) के त्रिभुजाकार क्षेत्र में फैली थी, जो समकालीन मिस्र और मेसोपोटामिया दोनों के संयुक्त क्षेत्रफल से भी बड़ा था।

Master Mnemonic: M-S-D-A (चारों दिशाओं के छोर)

M Manda (मांडा): Northernmost limit on Chenab River (Akhnoor, J&K).

S Sutkagan Dor (सुत्कागेंडोर): Westernmost limit on Dasht River (Makran Coast, Balochistan).

D Daimabad (दैमाबाद): Southernmost limit on Pravara River (Godavari basin, Maharashtra).

A Alamgirpur (आलमगीरपुर): Easternmost limit on Hindon River (Yamuna basin, Meerut, UP).

Special Northern Trading Outposts (अफगानिस्तान की व्यापारिक चौकियां):
  • Shortugai (शोर्टुघई, Badakhshan): Situated on Oxus (Amu Darya) river; established specifically for mining and trading Lapis Lazuli. Only site with direct evidence of canal irrigation.
  • Mundigak (मुंडीगाक, Kandahar): Northern transit trading station on the overland caravan route connecting Baluchistan with Central Asia.

6. Pointwise Revision Grids: Town Planning, Economy, Religion & Script 6. त्वरित पुनरीक्षण ग्रिड: नगर नियोजन, अर्थव्यवस्था, धर्म एवं लिपि (बिन्दुवार)

Town Planning & Drainage (नगर नियोजन व जलनिकासी)

  • Gridiron System: Chessboard plan; streets intersected at strict 90° right angles running North-South and East-West.
  • Dual Division: Raised Western Citadel (administration & granary) + Eastern Lower Town (residential). Exceptions: Dholavira (3 parts), Chanhudaro (no citadel), Lothal (common wall).
  • Standard Bricks: Kiln-baked bricks in uniform 1 : 2 : 4 ratio (thickness : width : length) using English bond masonry.
  • Underground Drainage: Waste water flowed into covered street sewers with inspection sumps and soak pits; covered with removable limestone slabs. (Absent at Kalibangan and Banawali).
  • Introverted Domestic Design: Room entrances opened into side lanes, not main roads, for privacy and noise reduction. No ground-floor windows.

Agriculture & Domestication (कृषि एवं पशुपालन)

  • Primary Staple Crops: Wheat (club & dwarf wheat) and Barley (two-row & six-row barley).
  • Cotton Priority: First in the world to grow, spin and weave cotton (Akkadian: 'Sindhu', Greek: 'Sindon').
  • Rice Evidence: Rice husks embedded in pottery found at Lothal and Rangpur (Gujarat).
  • Ploughed Field: Criss-cross furrows at Kalibangan (mixed cropping of mustard & chickpea). Terracotta plough at Banawali.
  • Animals: Humped Bull (Zebu) was sacred; Buffalo, sheep, goat, pig, dog, cat, elephant, camel (Kalibangan).
  • Animal Traps: NO LION on seals; NO COW on seals; HORSE not central (debated bones at Surkotada, toy at Lothal).

Social & Religious Life (सामाजिक-धार्मिक जीवन)

  • Mother Goddess (मातृदेवी): Terracotta figurines with fan-shaped headdresses at Harappa & Mohenjo-daro (absent at Lothal/Kalibangan).
  • Pashupati Mahadeva: Proto-Shiva seal in yogic posture with 3 faces and horned crown, surrounded by ETRB (Elephant, Tiger, Rhino, Buffalo) + 2 deers.
  • Tree & Nature Worship: Sacred Pipal tree (Ficus religiosa), linga-yoni stone worship, humped bull and unicorn.
  • Fire Altars: Rows of fire altars (Yajna-kundas) excavated at Kalibangan and Lothal.
  • NO TEMPLES: Completely aniconic public worship; no monumental temples or priestly palaces found.
  • Burials: North-South orientation. Cemetery R-37 & Coffin at Harappa; Joint burial at Lothal; Pot burials at Surkotada.

Trade, Weights, Seals & Script (व्यापार, बाट, मुहर व लिपि)

  • Mesopotamian Trade: Sargon of Akkad inscription (c. 2350 BCE) mentions trade with Meluhha (IVC), Dilmun (Bahrain), Magan (Oman).
  • Barter & Weights: No metallic coins. Binary weights (1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64) for small goods and decimal (160, 200, 320) for bulk goods. Base unit: 16 (13.63 g).
  • Steatite Seals: Square (2x2 cm) soapstone seals. Dominant motif: Mythical Unicorn (>70%), followed by humped bull, elephant, tiger.
  • Boustrophedon Script: Written from Right to Left in line 1, Left to Right in line 2. Undeciphered logo-syllabic script with 400–450 signs; 'Fish' symbol is most frequent.
  • Longest Inscription: 26 signs at Dholavira; 10-sign giant Harappan signboard at Dholavira North Gate.

8. Critical Exam Traps & Common Candidate Pitfalls 8. परीक्षार्थियों के सामान्य भ्रम एवं बचने योग्य गलतियाँ

Examiners frequently formulate trick statements around these 8 specific facts. Memorize them thoroughly: आयोग के प्रश्नपत्रों में अक्सर इन्हीं 8 विशिष्ट तथ्यों पर भ्रम पैदा करने वाले कथन बनाए जाते हैं:

Iron Knowledge Trap (लोहे का ज्ञान)
Harappans were Chalcolithic / Bronze Age people. Iron was COMPLETELY UNKNOWN to them. Iron first appeared in India during the Later Vedic period (~1000 BCE) at Atranjikhera (Etah, UP) and Gandhara.
Lion vs Tiger Seal Trap (शेर बनाम बाघ)
Tiger is frequently depicted on Indus seals (including horned deities fighting tigers and human-tiger hybrids). LION WAS NEVER DEPICTED on any Indus seal or terracotta object.
Cow Representation Trap (गाय का अंकन)
Humped Bull (Zebu) was the most sacred animal and Unicorn was the most frequent motif (>70%). COW WAS NEVER DEPICTED on Indus seals or stone seals (though domesticated biologically).
Temple & Public Religion Trap (मंदिर एवं पूजा स्थल)
NO MONUMENTAL TEMPLE or dedicated public worship building has ever been discovered in the entire IVC (in stark contrast to contemporary Sumerian Ziggurats and Egyptian stone temples).
Coinage & Currency Trap (सिक्के एवं मुद्रा)
NO METALLIC COINS existed in IVC. Trade was conducted strictly through barter and regulated by standardized cubical chert weights based on binary and decimal ratios. First punched-marked coins (Aahat) appeared much later in ~6th century BCE.
Cotton Priority Trap (कपास का प्रथम उत्पादन)
Harappans were the FIRST PEOPLE IN THE WORLD to cultivate, spin and weave cotton (called 'Sindhu' in Akkadian and 'Sindon' in Greek derived from Sindh). It did NOT originate in Egypt.
Script Direction Trap (लिपि लेखन दिशा)
Harappan script is Boustrophedon (written from Right to Left in the first line, and Left to Right in the second line). It is logo-syllabic with ~400-450 signs (most common is the Fish symbol).
Largest Site Trap (सबसे बड़ा स्थल)
Rakhigarhi (Haryana, >350–550 hectares) is now officially confirmed as the LARGEST Harappan site in the world, surpassing Mohenjo-daro (~300 hectares). Largest site in Gujarat is Dholavira.

Q1. Highlight the distinctive salient features of the town planning and underground drainage system of the Indus Valley Civilization. प्रश्न 1. सिन्धु घाटी सभ्यता के नगर नियोजन एवं भूमिगत जल निकासी प्रणाली की प्रमुख विशेषताओं को रेखांकित कीजिए।
Model Pointwise Framework:

The Harappan town planning showcased unprecedented municipal engineering: (1) Gridiron / Chessboard Layout: Streets intersected at strict right angles (90 degrees), dividing cities into rectangular blocks oriented along cardinal directions. (2) Dual Division: Raised Western Citadel housed administrative and granary complexes; Eastern Lower Town accommodated common residential quarters (Dholavira uniquely had 3 tiers). (3) Standardized Bricks: Kiln-baked bricks in uniform 1:2:4 ratio laid in English bond masonry with gypsum mortar. (4) Underground Drainage: Waste water from house bathrooms flowed into covered street sewers equipped with inspection sumps and terracotta soak jars, covered with removable limestone slabs. (5) Introverted Architecture: House entrances opened into side lanes rather than main streets to ensure privacy and eliminate noise.

हड़प्पा सभ्यता का नगर नियोजन समकालीन विश्व में अद्वितीय था: (1) ग्रिड पद्धति: सड़कें समकोण (90°) पर काटती हुई नगर को आयताकार खंडों में बांटती थीं। (2) द्वि-विभाजन: पश्चिमी गढ़ी (दुर्ग) प्रशासनिक व अन्नागार हेतु तथा पूर्वी निचला नगर आवासीय बस्ती था (धौलावीरा में 3 स्तर)। (3) मानकीकृत ईंटें: 1:2:4 अनुपात की पकी ईंटें जिप्सम गारे के साथ प्रयुक्त हुईं। (4) भूमिगत जल निकासी: घरों की नालियां ढकी हुई मुख्य नालियों में मिलती थीं, जिनमें गाद रोकने हेतु सोक-पिट व निरीक्षण गड्ढे थे। (5) आवासीय निजता: मुख्य सड़क पर कोई खिड़की या सीधा प्रवेश द्वार नहीं खुलता था, द्वार गलियों में खुलते थे।

Q2. Discuss the archaeological significance of Harappan sites discovered in Uttar Pradesh with special reference to Sanauli and Alamgirpur. प्रश्न 2. सनौली एवं आलमगीरपुर के विशेष संदर्भ में उत्तर प्रदेश में खोजे गए हड़प्पा स्थलों के पुरातात्विक महत्व की विवेचना कीजिए।
Model Pointwise Framework:

Harappan sites in western Uttar Pradesh demonstrate the eastern expansion and transitional evolution of the civilization into the Ganga-Yamuna Doab: (1) Alamgirpur (Meerut): Discovered on Hindon River, marking the easternmost geographical boundary of IVC; yielded textile impressions on ceramic troughs. (3) Mandi (Muzaffarnagar): Unearthed an immense hoard of Harappan gold jewellery and micro-beads. Q3. Critically examine the major theories regarding the decline of the Indus Valley Civilization. Was it a sudden catastrophic collapse or a gradual transformation? प्रश्न 3. सिन्धु घाटी सभ्यता के पतन से संबंधित प्रमुख सिद्धांतों का आलोचनात्मक परीक्षण कीजिए। क्या यह आकस्मिक विनाश था अथवा क्रमिक परिवर्तन?
Model Pointwise Framework:

The decline of the Indus Valley Civilization (c. 1900 BCE) represents one of the most debated questions in Indian proto-history: (1) Aryan Invasion Theory: Proposed by Wheeler and Childe citing Rigvedic 'Purandara' and Mohenjo-daro skeletons. This has been decisively refuted by Kennedy and Dales as skeletons show anemia/malaria, not trauma, and a 300-year gap separates IVC decline from Vedic migration. (2) Ecological & Climatic Shift: Fairservis emphasized deforestation from over-firing baked bricks. Recent speleothem data confirms the 4.2 kiloyear BP global megadrought that weakened monsoons across South Asia. (3) Hydrological Instability: Raikes and Sahni posited tectonic uplifts damming the Indus; Agrawal and Lal highlighted the desiccation of the sacred Saraswati (Ghaggar-Hakra) river system as Sutlej and Yamuna shifted away. (4) Economic Disruption: Ratnagar demonstrated that the collapse of Mesopotamian trade disrupted urban craft networks. Conclusion: Modern consensus rejects a single apocalyptic collapse. Instead, climate aridification and hydrological shifts caused gradual de-urbanization, decentralization, and eastward migration of populations into agrarian settlements of Punjab, Haryana, and western UP.

हड़प्पा सभ्यता का पतन (लगभग 1900 ई.पू.) आकस्मिक विनाश न होकर क्रमिक वि-नगरीकरण एवं रूपांतरण था: (1) आर्य आक्रमण सिद्धांत: व्हीलर व चाइल्ड ने ऋग्वेद में इंद्र को 'पुरंदर' कहे जाने व मोहनजोदड़ो के कंकालों के आधार पर यह सिद्धांत दिया। केनेडी व डेल्स ने इसे पूर्णतः खंडित किया क्योंकि कंकालों पर युद्ध के घाव नहीं बल्कि मलेरिया/एनीमिया के साक्ष्य हैं तथा कालक्रम में 300 वर्षों का अंतर है। (2) जलवायु एवं पारिस्थितिक क्षरण: फेयरसर्विस के अनुसार ईंट पकाने हेतु वनों के अंधाधुंध कटान से पर्यावरण असंतुलन हुआ। 4.2 किलोवर्ष महासूखे ने मानसूनी वर्षा को अत्यधिक कमजोर कर दिया। (3) भूगर्भीय व जलप्लावन परिवर्तन: रेइक्स के अनुसार विवर्तनिक हलचलों से सिन्धु में बाढ़ आई; अग्रवाल व लाल के अनुसार घग्घर-हाकरा (सरस्वती) नदी सूख गई क्योंकि सतलज सिन्धु की ओर और यमुना गंगा की ओर मुड़ गई। (4) व्यापारिक पतन: रत्नागर के अनुसार मेसोपोटामियाई व्यापार टूटने से नगरीय शिल्प आधार ढह गया। निष्कर्ष: यह आकस्मिक पतन नहीं बल्कि जलवायु परिवर्तन व नदी मार्ग बदलाव के कारण पूर्व (गंगा-यमुना दोआब) व दक्षिण की ओर हुआ क्रमिक ग्रामीण विस्थापन था।

Q4. Analyze the socio-religious practices and craft specialization of Harappan people based on archaeological artifacts recovered from major sites. प्रश्न 4. प्रमुख स्थलों से प्राप्त पुरातात्विक पुरावशेषों के आधार पर हड़प्पा कालीन सामाजिक-धार्मिक मान्यताओं एवं शिल्प विशेषज्ञता का विश्लेषण कीजिए।
Model Pointwise Framework:

Archaeological artifacts reveal a sophisticated, stratified, and non-theocratic socio-religious structure: (1) Religious Practices: (a) Female Fertility Cult: Terracotta Mother Goddess figurines with fan-shaped headdresses prominent at Harappa and Mohenjo-daro (absent at Lothal/Kalibangan).
